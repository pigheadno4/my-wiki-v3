# Send money to multiple recipients with Payouts

Pay vendors and send disbursements in multiple currencies

​Need to pay multiple recipients? PayPal offers everything from basic payouts to enterprise-level solutions.

**info**
Not a developer? Send Payouts within your PayPal account. [Get Started](https://www.paypal.com/payoutsweb/landing)

## Choose a payouts solution

### Standard Payouts

Easy, powerful payouts

Send money immediately to multiple recipients with their emails, phone numbers, or PayPal IDs.

### Advanced Payouts

Enterprise payouts solution.

Enterprise-level payout solution to streamline your payments at a global scale.
