# Download reports from your dashboard

The PayPal dashboard provides a user-friendly way to generate and download reports without any coding requirements. This no-code solution is perfect for merchants who need occasional reports or want to get started quickly.

## Prerequisites

To download reports from your dashboard, you need to have:

- PayPal Business account login access.
- Report viewing permissions.
- A clear understanding of required date ranges and filters.

## Steps to download reports

1. **Log into PayPal Business Account**
   - Navigate to your PayPal Business dashboard.
   - Ensure you have the necessary permissions.

2. **Access reports Ssection**
   - Click on "Reports" in the main navigation.
   - Select "Activity Download" or relevant report type.

3. **Configure report parameters**
   - Set date range for the data you need.
   - Choose transaction types to include.
   - Select file format, such as CSV or PDF.

4. **Generate report**
   - Click "Create Report" or similar action button.
   - Wait for processing to complete.

5. **Download file**
   - Download the generated report file.
   - Save to your preferred location.

## Warnings

- Large date ranges may take longer to process.
- Some reports have daily download limits.
- Historical data availability may be limited.

## Best practices

- Use specific date ranges to reduce file size.
- Download reports regularly to avoid data limits.
- Keep downloaded files secure and organized.
