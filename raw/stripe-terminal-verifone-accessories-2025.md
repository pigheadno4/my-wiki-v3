# Verifone accessories

Learn about accessories for all Stripe supported Verifone readers.

> #### Verifone reader support
>
> Verifone reader support is in public preview for the United States and Canada. Some verifone readers are in private preview for Ireland and the United Kingdom (V660p, UX700, P630) and for Singapore (V660p, P630). To join the preview, you must [contact the Sales team to order the applicable reader](https://stripe.com/contact/sales).

These are the accessories available for each Verifone reader Stripe supports.

## V660p

The V660p requires a power supply (adapter included), and USB-C cable. You can request to add the optional Full Feature Base to the V660p reader. The Full Feature Base adds the ability to support Ethernet while also charging the device.
![](assets/stripe-terminal-v660p-accessories.png)

V660p Full Feature Base

## UX700

The UX700 requires a power supply (adapter included). This reader has a built-in connection for Ethernet.
![](assets/stripe-terminal-ux700-ethernet.png)

UX700 Ethernet connection

## P630

The P630 requires a power supply and the DO Orange Dongle (only supports Ethernet) and the 2M Orange cable. When you order this reader, we include the power adapter, D0 Orange dongle, and cable.
![](assets/stripe-terminal-p630-orange-dongle.png)

P630 dongle and cable

## M425

The M425 requires a power supply and the DO Orange Dongle (only supports Ethernet) and the 1M Orange cable. The D0 Orange dongle only supports Ethernet. When you order this reader, we include the power adapter, D0 Orange dongle and cable.
![](assets/stripe-terminal-m425-accessories.png)

M425 dongle and cable
