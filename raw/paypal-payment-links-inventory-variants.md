# Manage inventory, variants, and taxes

Use the Payment Links and Buttons dashboard to build flexible purchase experiences while configuring pricing, variants, taxes, inventory behavior, and alerts to fit your platform's needs.

## Prerequisites

1. Log in to your [PayPal Business Account](https://www.paypal.com/business/open-business-account?_ga=2.49411041.673139926.1760916697-1259758161.1760473483).
2. From the navigation menu, select **Pay & Get Paid** > **Create Payment Links and Buttons** or **Create Shopping Cart Buttons**.

## Manage inventory

Keep product quantities accurate with inventory tracking and alerts.

1. Log in to your PayPal business account.
2. From the left navigation, select **Sales** > **Payment Links and Buttons**.
3. Set the maximum number of units a customer can purchase in a single transaction in the **Quantity** field. This setting only applies when you use the **One set price** pricing option, and is useful when you want to limit bulk purchases.
4. Enter **Item ID**, **Quantity in stock**, and **Alert quantity** to receive email notifications when inventory is low or out of stock.
5. Choose whether customers can purchase out-of-stock items.

## Variants

Variants let you offer up to three variations per item, such as color, size, or type.

1. In the dashboard, select **Show more options** and turn on **Variants**.
2. Enter a variant name, and list up to 10 options for that variant.
3. Add images and prices for your item’s primary variant.
4. To price each option individually, select **Add price per variant**.

## Taxes, discounts, and fees

Use the **Checkout** tab to configure taxes, discounts, and fees for each item so customers see accurate pricing during checkout.

| Type         | Description                                                                                                                                         | How to set it                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| :----------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Taxes        | Set tax rates for each item, including custom rates. If your country requires tax-inclusive pricing, make sure the item price already includes tax. | In the **Checkout** tab, turn on **Taxes**. Then select **Use tax from my settings** or **Use a specific tax rate**, and choose **Percentage**, **Flat amount**, or **Taxes not applicable**. |
| Discounts    | Apply automatic discounts to each item and variant.                                                                                                 | In the **Checkout** tab, turn on **Discounts**. Then choose **Amount Off** (fixed amount), **Percentage** (percentage off the price), or **No discount** from the drop-down menu.             |
| Handling fee | Add a single handling fee for this item, regardless of how many units a customer buys.                                                              | In the **Checkout** tab, turn on the **Handling fee** and enter the fee amount.                                                                                                               |
| Shipping fee | Set the shipping fee for this item, including free shipping.                                                                                        | In the **Checkout tab**, turn on the **Shipping fee**, choose a setting from the **Shipping fee** dropdown, and enter the fee amount.                                                         |
