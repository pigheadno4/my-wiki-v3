# Activating FPA for direct merchants

This flow is for existing merchants who have notsigned up for Fraud Protection Advanced (FPA). You can activate FPA through one of the following:

- Business Tools
- Account Settings


## Activating FPA through Business Tools
- In **Business Tools** , navigate to the **Manage Risk** section. Select the **Fraud Tools** tile highlighted in the following screenshot. On the next screen, select **Get Started.**
![FPA discovery in Business Tools](assets/FPA_Home_Discovery_BusinessTools.png)

![FPA Get Started screen](assets/Get_startedFPA.png)

- Select **Do it yourself with PayPal's fraud tool** and then select **Next** . If you select **comparison chart** , you'll see a detailed comparison of features for [Fraud Protection](https://developer.paypal.com/docs/checkout/advanced/customize/fraud-protection/) (FP), Fraud Protection Advanced (FPA), and Chargeback protection, as shown in the following screenshots:
![FPA DIY selection screen](assets/FPA_Home_DIY_FPA_Recommended.png)

![FPA at a glance comparison chart](assets/FPA_glance.png)

- A recommended solution will be preselected for you based on your business metrics. However, you can modify the selection if you want. Make sure the **Fraud Protection Advanced Tool** is selected and select **Next** .
![Select FPA tool screen](assets/Activate_Dir_Mer_Select_FPA.png)

- Confirm your details to set up automatic bank payments for your fraud tool, and select **Next** .
![Set up auto debit screen](assets/Activate_Dir_Mer_Set_Up_auto_debit.png)

- Select **Let's Go** to open the Fraud Protection Advanced tool and begin customizing your fraud tool.
![FPA activation end screen](assets/Activate_DirMer_EndOfFlow_.png)


## Activating FPA through Account Settings
Go to your **Account Settings** , select **Payment preferences** . Next to the **Manage fraud** section, select **Choose a fraud tool** .

![Manage Fraud tool in Account Settings](assets/Manage_Fraud_tool.png)

Follow steps 2 through 5 in Activating FPA through Business Tools to complete the activation process.
