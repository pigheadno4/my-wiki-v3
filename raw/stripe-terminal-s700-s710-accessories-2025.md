## S700 and S710 Case

The S700/S710 Case protects your Stripe Reader S700/S710 against damage from drops at countertop height. Its compact design allows your customers to access all payment methods (tap, chip, and swipe). You can access the headphone jack, USB port, and volume buttons without removing the case.

The S700/S710 Case is also compatible with the S700/S710 Dock. This allows you to protect your reader if you transition from docked to mobile applications.

## S700 and S710 Hub

The S700/S710 Hub provides drop-in I/O expansion to your POS configuration. The 10/100M LAN port provides physical ethernet connectivity. Additionally, if you need USB connectivity between your POS and peripheral devices such as cash drawers, printers, or barcode scanners, the S700/S710 Hub has 2x USB-A ports. The communication ports on the hub are activated when the Stripe Reader S700/S710 is connected to the hub. Use of alternative hubs might result in unreliable communication or charging of the Stripe Reader S700/S710.

> The S700/S710 Hub doesn’t connect Stripe Reader S700/S710 to peripheral devices, such as a barcode scanner or printer. It connects peripheral devices to your POS device, such as a tablet or cash register.

The S700/S710 Hub provides power to Stripe Reader S700/S710 and connected peripherals through a built-in 6 foot USB-C cable. We recommend using the power adapter supplied with Stripe Reader S700/S710 for full power capabilities. If you need an additional or replacement power adapter, order the S700/S710 Charger in the [Stripe Dashboard](https://dashboard.stripe.com/terminal/shop).

The S700/S710 Hub can provide a total of 1A shared between the two USB-A ports. Accessories that require high power, such as printers, must have their own power source. Different devices such as cell phones might require different charging protocols that aren’t supported by the S700/S710 Hub.

## S700 and S710 Dock

Use the S700/S710 Dock to display the Stripe Reader S700/S710 for customers on a countertop. The dock is compatible with the Stripe Reader S700/S710 with and without a case. The Stripe Reader S700/S710 attaches to the dock using magnets for correct electrical contacts, and includes a locking nut for a more secure connection. Additionally, there are three mounting points on the bottom of the dock to optionally secure to a countertop or other surface.

In addition to mounting the Stripe Reader S700/S710 to a countertop surface, the S700/S710 Dock also provides power and USB connectivity pass-through to Stripe Reader S700/S710. This provides continuous power to Stripe Reader S700/S710, keeping it powered throughout the day. You can also use the S700/S710 Dock with the Hub to create a complete countertop I/O solution.
