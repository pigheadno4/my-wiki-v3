# Submit a warranty claim

Submit claims for defective readers in the Dashboard.

Unless stated otherwise by Stripe, Stripe provides a one-year limited warranty against defective materials and faulty workmanship in a Stripe Terminal product (excluding cables and any other accessories) as set out in the purchase terms for the Stripe Terminal products. While you must submit warranty claims for readers through the Stripe Dashboard, you must submit warranty claims for docks sold by Stripe to [Stripe support](https://support.stripe.com/).

## Submitting a claim

To submit a warranty claim:

1. In the Stripe Dashboard, go to the [Readers](https://dashboard.stripe.com/terminal/readers) page and click the overflow menu (⋯).
1. Click **Start warranty claims** to begin the process.
1. Enter the serial number (or multiple serial numbers separated by a comma) of the readers to be replaced.
1. Stripe checks the warranty status of the reader. If the reader’s under warranty, you’re prompted to edit the new warranty claim. If the reader’s not under warranty, the reader can’t be replaced.
1. Select the pencil icon (✏) to enter required information about each defective reader.
   - Select a reason and sub-reason for the warranty claim.
   - Upload images. Supported file formats are: PNG, JPG, GIF, TIFF, PDF, and ZIP. To upload videos, compress the video into a ZIP file.
   - Provide a written description or troubleshooting steps (required) that support this warranty claim.
1. When all claim items are ready, click **Submit**.
1. Enter other required information in the form, including the shipping address for the replacement. Stripe sends email notifications to the email address in the form. For each warranty claim, replacements are only sent to a single location. If you need replacement readers sent to multiple locations, submit multiple warranty claims.
1. Review your claim details and click **Submit claim**.

### After submitting

After you submit the claim, Stripe’s Support team reviews it within 48 business hours. If the Support team needs additional information, they’ll reach out to the contact who submitted the claim.

After the claim is approved, you receive an email notification with details about the replacement and a return label, if Stripe requires the defective reader to be sent back.

The replacement reader carries over the remaining warranty period from the original reader, rather than starting a new warranty period. If the original warranty has less than 3 months remaining, the replacement reader receives a minimum 3-month warranty.

### Claims status

To check the status of warranty claims:

1. In the Stripe Dashboard, go to the [Readers](https://dashboard.stripe.com/terminal/readers) page.
1. Click the overflow menu (⋯).
1. Click [**Manage warranty claims**](https://dashboard.stripe.com/terminal/warranty_claims).
