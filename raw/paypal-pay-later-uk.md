# Pay Later (UK)

Get paid in full at checkout while giving your customers the flexibility to pay in installments over time with no late fees.

![Pay Later UK hero](assets/paypal-pay-later-uk-hero.png)

# Pay Later products in the UK

Pay Later in the UK includes Pay in 3 and PayPal Credit.

**PayPal Credit**: Eligible UK buyers receive a revolving line of credit. Offers either:
- 0% interest for 4 months on purchases over £99, OR
- Merchant-specific installment offers

Any remaining balance after the promotional period, or transactions under £99, is charged at the standard variable rate. Terms apply.

| Product | Number of payments | Due | Purchase amount |
| ------- | ------------------ | --- | --------------- |
| Pay in 3 | 3 | Monthly | £20 – £3,000 |

![Pay Later UK how it works](assets/paypal-pay-later-uk-how-it-works.png)

# Eligibility

**Note:** Pay Later offers are available to UK merchants on a **limited basis**.

Eligible if you:
- Are a UK-based PayPal merchant
- Have a UK-facing website
- Transact in British pounds sterling (GBP)
- Have a one-time payment integration
- Abide by the PayPal Acceptable Use Policy
- Do not edit Pay Later messages with additional marketing content

**Not eligible:**
- Reference Transaction and Recurring Payment integrations
- Website Payments Standard (WPS) integrations
