# PayPal custom payment method

Add PayPal to your Checkout Sessions with a low-code solution.

Stripe supports adding PayPal as a custom payment method in your existing [Elements with Checkout Sessions](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/quickstart-checkout-sessions.md) and [hosted Checkout](https://docs.stripe.com/checkout/quickstart.md) integrations. Stripe shares an adapter hosted in your environment which integrates natively with Checkout for efficient orchestration.

If you exclusively process PayPal transactions in [supported European countries](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/paypal.md?pm-info=business-locations#business-locations), consider using [our standard PayPal payment method](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/paypal.md).
