# In-app purchases flow for lower processing fee on iOS

Let customers buy digital goods or subscriptions by redirecting from your app to the browser.

In certain countries, Apple allows apps to link to an external website for processing payments. Use these guides to learn on how to enable this type of flow using PayPal.

## Recommended options

### Payment Link

![Payment Link option](assets/paypal-ios-iap-payment-link-icon.png)

[Payment Link](https://developer.paypal.com/studio/checkout/no-code)

Create a Payment Link for your in-app digital goods or subscription.

### Payment Buttons

![Payment Buttons option](assets/paypal-ios-iap-payment-buttons-icon.png)

[Payment Buttons](https://developer.paypal.com/studio/checkout/no-code/shopping-cart)

Use PayPal buttons to create a custom payment form on your website to accept in-app purchases.

### Custom Checkout

![Custom Checkout option](assets/paypal-ios-iap-custom-checkout-icon.png)

[Custom Checkout](https://developer.paypal.com/docs/api/orders/v2/#orders_create)

Use PayPal API to redirect customers to a PayPal-hosted payment page.
