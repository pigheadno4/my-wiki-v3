# Let customers pay in their local currency

Learn how to display multiple currencies and allow customers to make payments in more than 100 currencies.

[Adaptive Pricing](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/custom/localize-prices/adaptive-pricing.md): Allow connected accounts to accept payments in a customer’s local currency with Adaptive Pricing.

[FX Quotes API](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/custom/localize-prices/fx-quotes-api.md): Determine which currencies to localize and whether to pass along fees to your customers. You can also establish exchange rates.

[Manual currency prices](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/custom/localize-prices/manual-currency-prices.md): Manually set prices in different currencies to offer your customers the same static pricing.
