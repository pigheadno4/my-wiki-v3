# Set up for Adyen payments

Follow the steps to set up your Adyen account, build your integration, and go live.

## Step 1. Create a test account

##### Adyen webinars

Sign up for one of our [upcoming webinars](https://help.adyen.com/academy) to learn more about managing your Adyen account, risk, reporting, and more.

Get started with Adyen by [creating a free test account](https://www.adyen.com/signup). Your test account gives you access to the [test Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/), which is a unified dashboard where you can manage your Adyen integration across regions and currencies.

Your [test Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/) account allows you to:

* Make test payments.
* Invite team members.
* Apply for a live account.
* Start building your integration.

A test account lets you try out Adyen's integrations, but does not guarantee that you will be able to process live payments.

If you want to process live payments, check the [list of prohibited or restricted products and services](https://www.adyen.com/legal/list-restricted-prohibited). If your business is eligible, contact our [Sales team](https://www.adyen.com/contact/sales) before you apply for a live account to find out whether Adyen is right for you.

## Step 3. Apply for your live account

A live account allows you to accept payments from your shoppers using Adyen. Because Adyen is a payment provider and acquirer, we are legally obliged to do detailed checks when you apply for a live account.

Before applying for a live account, make sure you have done the following:

1. Check the [list of prohibited or restricted products and services](https://www.adyen.com/legal/list-restricted-prohibited).
2. If your business is eligible, contact our [Sales team](https://www.adyen.com/contact/sales) to find out whether Adyen is right for you.
3. [Review the requirements for a live account](/get-started-with-adyen/application-requirements).

Reach out to your Adyen contact to take your account live.

## Step 4. Sign the contract and get your live account

If your application for a live account is approved, we send you the contract. If your integration uses card payments, we also send you a data security attestation form. Complete this form based on your technical integration to ensure [compliance with Payment Card Industry (PCI) standards](/development-resources/pci-dss-compliance-guide).

When we have received your signed contract and the completed data security attestation form, you get access to your [live Customer Area](https://ca-live.adyen.com/). The user who created the test account receives the login instructions by email.
