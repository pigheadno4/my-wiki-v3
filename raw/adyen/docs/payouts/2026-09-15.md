# Adyen Payouts

Learn how to send global payouts in real time.

Adyen payouts is a collection of services that enable you to transfer funds to accounts outside of Adyen. Depending on your business model and payment strategy, Adyen's payout services are available in the following combinations:

* **Payouts combined with Payments**: Adyen offers its merchant of record a payout service in combination with payments processing. The beneficiary of the payout is not onboarded with Adyen. For example, you can pay out to your supplier.
* **Payouts as part of Adyen for Platforms**: Adyen pays out processed payments to a merchant or a sub-merchant of a platform.
