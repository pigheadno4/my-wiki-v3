# Payouts and Payments

Combine payment processing with Adyen’s payouts service.

Adyen offers a payouts service in combination with processing [online](/online-payments) or [in-person](/point-of-sale) payments. However, the payout may or may not be directly related to an acquired payment. The payout may also be completely standalone to a third-party beneficiary. For example, to your supplier or partner.

By combining payment processing and payouts, you can:

* **Fund your payouts**: Use funds acquired through payments with Adyen or externally transferred funds.
* **Pay out funds to third-party bank accounts**: Pay out funds to any third-party beneficiary within the [supported locations](/payouts/payout-service/pay-out-to-bank-accounts#supported-currencies).
* **Pay out funds to third-party cards**: Pay out funds to Mastercard or Visa cards within the [supported locations](/payouts/payout-service/pay-out-to-cards#supported-locations-and-currencies).
