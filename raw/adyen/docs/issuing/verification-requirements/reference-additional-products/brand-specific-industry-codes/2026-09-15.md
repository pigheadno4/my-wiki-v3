# Brand-specific industry codes list

Select the industry code that most accurately represents the brand and the business activity of your user.

Certain brands have unique Merchant Category Codes (MCCs). We have assigned brand-specific Industry Codes (ICs) to capture unique MCCs for certain brands. Select the IC that best represents your user's brand and business activity.
