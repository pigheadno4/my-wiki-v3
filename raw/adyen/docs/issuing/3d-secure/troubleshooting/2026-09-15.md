# Troubleshooting failed payments

Learn about the reasons why 3D Secure authentication can fail for online payments made with Adyen-issued cards.

The following are common reasons why 3D Secure authentication can fail. This applies to online payments that a cardholder makes with your Adyen-issued card.

## Requirements

Before you begin, take into account the following requirements:

| Requirement                                                       | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Integration type**                                              | You must have an Adyen [Issuing](/issuing) integration.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **[Customer Area roles](/account/user-roles#financial-products)** | To view the **Payments** and **Transfers** lists, make sure that you have the **Balance platform base role**.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **[Webhooks](/development-resources/webhooks)**                   | Subscribe to the [balancePlatform.transfer.created](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/transfer-webhooks/latest/post/balancePlatform.transfer.created) and [balancePlatform.transfer.updated](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/transfer-webhooks/latest/post/balancePlatform.transfer.updated) webhooks to receive updates on soft-declined payments. |

## Authentication failed

When 3D Secure authentication fails, you likely receive feedback from your cardholder that they are unable to use their card. You do not receive a relayed authorization or a webhook. You also do not see the payment in the **Payments** list in your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/). This happens because the payment is already blocked after the authentication, and the authorization is not sent to Adyen.

To resolve this issue, make sure that the card is [enrolled in 3D Secure](/issuing/3d-secure) before retrying the payment. If the issue persists, contact our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other).

## Card blocked

When 3D Secure authentication fails multiple times, you likely receive feedback from your cardholder that they are unable to use their card. As required by [PSD2](/get-started-with-adyen/adyen-glossary/#payment-services-directive-two-psd2) regulations, Adyen temporarily blocks a card from performing 3D Secure authentication after *five consecutive failed authentication attempts*.

To resolve this issue:

* Wait an hour until the block is automatically lifted, then ask the cardholder to try again.
* If the card needs to be unblocked more quickly, contact our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other). We may require additional information about the cardholder to verify the ownership of the card.

## SCA skipped by the processing merchant

Following [PSD2](/get-started-with-adyen/adyen-glossary/#payment-services-directive-two-psd2) requirements, by default, Adyen declines a payment if the processing merchant skips 3D Secure authentication for a payment that requires [SCA](/get-started-with-adyen/adyen-glossary/#strong-customer-authentication-sca).

Alternatively, you can choose to enable [soft declines](/development-resources/raw-acquirer-responses#visa-and-mastercard-raw-responses) for the cards that you issue. When enabled, Adyen sends a soft decline and informs the processing merchant to retry the payment with a 3D Secure flow. It is up to the processing merchant to retry the payment. To enable soft declines, reach out to your Adyen contact.

When a payment is soft-declined, Adyen sends a [balancePlatform.transfer.created](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/transfer-webhooks/latest/post/balancePlatform.transfer.created) webhook for the failed authorization. This webhook contains a [validationFacts](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/transfer-webhooks/latest/post/balancePlatform.transfer.created#request-data-categoryData-IssuedCard-validationFacts) array. Look for the entry with `type` **psd2SoftDeclineCheck** and `result` **invalid**.

Adyen also sends a [balancePlatform.transfer.updated](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/transfer-webhooks/latest/post/balancePlatform.transfer.updated) webhook when the transfer status changes.

You can also see the payment attempt in the **Transfers** list in your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/).
