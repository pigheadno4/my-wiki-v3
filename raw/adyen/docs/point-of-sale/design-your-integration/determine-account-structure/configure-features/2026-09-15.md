# Configure features

Configure your account and terminals for the features you want to use.

The Adyen payment terminals support many features beyond the basic transactions of making, refunding, and canceling payments. Some advanced features require additional configuration steps. You can do this in your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/) or [using API calls](/point-of-sale/automating-terminal-management/configure-terminals-api). In some cases you need to contact our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other) to complete the configuration.

If you are looking for settings that moved or were renamed in the Customer Area, see [Customer Area UI change mapping](#customer-area-ui-change-mapping).

Here we explain the effect of configuring features at different levels, and where you can find feature settings in your Customer Area.

## Requirements

Before you begin, take into account the following requirements.

| Requirement                                    | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Integration type**                           | A [Terminal API integration](/point-of-sale/get-started) or [Standalone solution](/point-of-sale/standalone) using Adyen payment terminals.                                                                                                 |
| **[Customer Area roles](/account/user-roles)** | Make sure that you have the following roles:- To view terminal features: **Merchant POS standard role** or **Merchant POS Terminal View role**.
- To view and configure terminal features: **Merchant POS Terminal Management Admin role**. |

## Inheritance of settings

You can define terminal settings at different levels. From high to low:

* Company account level
* Merchant account level
* Store level
* Terminal level

The top level, above the company level, is the Adyen level. This is known as the *PSP level*. You cannot define settings at PSP level.

Settings at the company level apply to all terminals under the company. Settings at the merchant account level override settings inherited from the company level and apply to all terminals under the merchant account, and so on.

In other words: settings defined at a certain level propagate to all terminals at and under that level. But settings defined at a lower level replace values inherited from a higher level.

To manage your terminals effectively, we recommend setting configurations at the merchant account level.

In your Customer Area:

* Settings marked

  Adyen default

  are configured by Adyen. If you hover over the tag, you will see **Inherited from AdyenPspService**.

* If a setting is inherited from a higher level, that level is mentioned. For example:

  Company default

* The navigation path at the top of the page shows the inheritance hierarchy. You can select a level in the navigation path to go to its settings page.

* If a setting is enabled or disabled at a higher level, the toggle is grey. For example:

## When new settings take effect

After you changed the settings for a feature, the changes take effect when a payment terminal updates its configuration during the next reboot hour. To use the new settings immediately, you can [update the configuration manually](/point-of-sale/managing-terminals/maintain-performance#update-the-configuration-manually).

## Payment methods

The Adyen payment terminals accept global credit cards and Maestro out-of-the-box. If you also want to accept local credit or debit cards, wallets, or gift cards, you need to add these payment methods to your account.

In your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/), go to **Settings**, and under **Product settings** for **Payments**, select **Payment methods**.

For an overview of supported payment methods and instructions, see:

* [Payment methods](/point-of-sale/what-we-support/payment-methods)
* [Add payment methods](/point-of-sale/what-we-support/payment-methods/add-payment-methods)
* [Manage payment methods](/point-of-sale/what-we-support/payment-methods/manage-payment-methods)

## Find and edit settings

To access Device settings:

1. Log in to your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/).
2. Switch to your merchant account.
3. Go to **Devices** > **Device settings**. Or to define settings at the level of an individual terminal, go to **Devices** > **Device list**, select the **Terminals** tab, and then select your terminal.

The settings are organized into categories. You can find them under [**Device** ](#device), [**Device connectivity** ](#device-connectivity), [**Payment features** ](#payment-features), and [**Integrations** ](#integrations). Settings that are only available at the terminal level are noted in the description.

## Device category

You can use these settings to configure the terminal's regional, display, sound, and hardware preferences.

| Feature                                                              | Settings and descriptions                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Location & language**                                              | * **Location**: The terminal's country or region.
* **Timezone**: The terminal's timezone.
* **Language**: The default language on the terminal display and receipts. When the shopper presents their card, the terminal switches to the language of the country/region where the card was issued.
* **Secondary language**: A secondary language that the shopper can select during payment.
* **Amount format**: The decimal format for amounts shown on receipts and the Totals report. |
| **Device name**                                                      | The logical name used by the POS library to address the terminal and displayed in the Customer Area.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Sound**                                                            | - **Normal sound volume**: The volume for regular terminal sounds.
- **Error sound volume**: The volume for error alert sounds.
- **Contactless sound volume**: The volume for contactless payment sounds. On Android terminals, the normal sound volume also applies to the other sound types.
- **Enable keyboard beep**: Whether the terminal beeps when the keypad is used.                                                                                                            |
| **Theme**                                                            | The theme for your terminals. To customize or create a new theme, see [Theme customization](/point-of-sale/android-terminals/themes).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Home screen**                                                      | * **Settings menu**: Whether the Settings menu shortcut is shown on the home screen.
* **Transactions menu**: Whether the Transactions menu shortcut is shown on the home screen.
* **On-screen notifications**: Whether on-screen notifications are enabled on the terminal.
* **Display name**: The name that identifies the terminal on the home screen.                                                                                                                                |
| **[Kiosk mode](/point-of-sale/android-terminals/kiosk-mode)**        | - **Hide navigation bar**: Whether the navigation bar at the bottom of Android terminal screens is hidden from shoppers.
- **Apps allowed in kiosk mode**: The apps that can be accessed while the terminal is in kiosk mode.
- **Default start app**: The app that opens automatically when the terminal starts.                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Maintenance**                                                      | * **Restart hour**: The hour at which the terminal receives important updates, such as software updates.
* **Reset totals hour**: The hour at which the terminal resets its totals and generates the daily Totals report.
* **Maintenance interval**: How often the terminal checks for configuration changes.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **[Passcodes](/point-of-sale/menu-access#manage-passcodes)**         | Passcodes to protect access to the Transactions menu, Settings menu, refunds, and screen lock on the terminal.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Logos**                                                            | Upload your company logo to use as the terminal standby screen. Each setting represents a terminal model for which you can upload a logo file.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **[Barcode scanner](/point-of-sale/android-terminals/scan-barcode)** | Barcode scanner settings for supported terminal models.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
