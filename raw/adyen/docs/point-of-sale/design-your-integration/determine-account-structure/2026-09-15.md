# Set up your account for in-person payments

Determine your Adyen account structure and create stores.

Your account structure with Adyen can have a company account and one or more merchant accounts and stores. This page briefly discusses these account levels, discusses trade-offs to consider when determining the account structure that best suits your business, and how to add merchant accounts and stores.

## Requirements

Before you begin, take into account the following requirements.

| Requirement                                    | Description                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Integration type**                           | Any Adyen in-person payments integration or solution.                                                                                                     |
| **[Customer Area roles](/account/user-roles)** | To create stores using your Customer Area, you must have the following [user role](/account/user-roles):- **Merchant POS Terminal Management Admin role** |

## Account levels

For point of sale, your Adyen account has three levels:

* [Company level](/account/account-structure#company-account). A **single account** represents the core business entity. In most cases, this is where invoices for terminal orders are sent.

* [Merchant level](/account/account-structure#merchant-accounts). Under the company level, **one or more** merchant accounts will receive the payout of funds as well as reports for reconciliation. You can group merchant accounts together in [account groups](/account/account-structure#account-groups), but you will still receive payouts separately for each merchant account under the same company.

* [Store level](/point-of-sale/design-your-integration/determine-account-structure#create-stores). Under a merchant level, **one or more** stores represent the physical store locations, and are used to process point-of-sale transactions. Each store location is identified using a store ID.

To process payments, each payment terminal has to be assigned to either a store or a merchant account in the same location where the terminal is used. This means that you need at least one merchant account per country/region.

## 1. Determine account structure

Make sure to involve your Finance Department, because the account structure influences the bank settlement (payout of funds) and cost accounting (reconciliation).

There is no ideal merchant-store account structure. This comes down to your business needs. Considerations to take into account are:

* Customer Area access: if there are multiple stores under a merchant account, users with access to the merchant account also have access to all those stores.

* Various aspects are handled on the merchant account level:

  * Payout of funds: bank transfers per merchant account.
  * Invoice: created per merchant account.
  * Transaction fees: booked and visible as a total per merchant account.
  * Scheme fees, interchange, markup: this is transaction-level information, but in summary reports it is shown on the merchant account level only.
  * Graphical reports in the Customer Area: show information for the merchant account and the stores under it.

A common setup is to use one merchant account for each business unit or physical store location. Each store location is then represented by a merchant account and has their own separate reporting and payout of funds.

![](/user/pages/docs/03.point-of-sale/04.design-your-integration/04.determine-account-structure/account_simple.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)

If you operate a large number of physical store locations, we recommend using a merchant account per legal entity, country/region, or brand, with store IDs underneath to represent the physical stores. This reduces the number of reports and bank transfers to reconcile and reduces the effort to manage terminals, terminal configurations, and Customer Area users.

![](/user/pages/docs/03.point-of-sale/04.design-your-integration/04.determine-account-structure/account_complex.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)

For more considerations, see the next section, [Account structure and settlement](#account-structure-and-settlement), and our general [account documentation](/account).

## 2. Get merchant accounts

When you have determined an account structure, get one or more merchant accounts.

1. Log in to your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/).
2. Go to **Account** > **Merchant accounts**.
3. Select **Request new merchant account**.\
   In the test environment, select **Yes** to create a test merchant account, or **No** to switch to your live account.
4. Complete the requested details. For **Channel**, select **Point of sale** if that is your only sales channel, otherwise select **Ecommerce**.\
   We send you a confirmation by email when your merchant account is ready.

## 3. Create stores

If you decided to create an account structure of a merchant account with stores underneath, wait until you have received confirmation of your merchant account(s) and then create one or more stores.

To create stores by making API requests, see [Automate store management](/point-of-sale/design-your-integration/determine-account-structure/automate-store-management).

Select a tab to learn how to create stores in your Customer Area, either one by one or in bulk using a CSV file.

### Tab: One by one

1. Log in to your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/).

2. Go to **Devices** > **Stores**.

3. Select **Add stores**.

4. Select the merchant account that you want to add a store to.\
   The form now shows the default payment methods for the country/region.

5. To add more payment methods:

   1. Select **Edit payment methods**.
   2. In the side panel, select the checkboxes of the payment methods to add.
   3. Select **Confirm**.

   For payment methods that are not listed in the panel, see [Add payment methods to your account](/point-of-sale/what-we-support/payment-methods#add-payment-methods-to-your-account).

6. Select **Add store** and in the side panel complete the fields with the details of your store.

7. To add more stores under the same merchant account with the same payment methods, repeat selecting **Add store** and completing the store details.

8. Select **Submit request**.

The store is added immediately.

### Tab: In a CSV file

1. Log in to your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/).

2. Go to **Devices** > **Stores**.

3. Select **Add stores**.

4. Select the merchant account that you want to add a store to.\
   The form now shows the default payment methods for the country/region.

5. To add more payment methods:

   1. Select **Edit payment methods**.
   2. In the side panel, select the checkboxes of the payment methods to add.
   3. Select **Confirm**.

   For payment methods that are not listed in the panel, see [Add payment methods to your account](/point-of-sale/what-we-support/payment-methods#add-payment-methods-to-your-account).

6. To add up to 100 stores at once, select **Upload a CSV**:

   1. In the dialog box, select **Download CSV template**.
   2. Follow the instructions in the dialog box and add stores in the CSV template.
   3. Upload the CSV template and select **Continue**.

7. Select **Submit request**.

The stores are added immediately.
