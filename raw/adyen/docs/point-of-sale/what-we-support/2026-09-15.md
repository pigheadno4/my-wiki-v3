# What we support

Get an overview of what our in-person payments solution supports.

With Adyen, you have various in-person payments solutions to choose from or to use alongside each other. The solutions are supported around the world in many languages, and enable you to accept a wide range of global and domestic payment methods. For solutions that involve payment terminals, there is a variety of models to suit your business needs.
