# Shopper engagement

*Shopper engagement* refers to using the payment terminal to interact with your customers. For example:

* Show Terms and Conditions
* Ask for an opinion or get contact details
* Print a ticket

Shopper engagement operations are often used in advanced flows like loyalty flows.
