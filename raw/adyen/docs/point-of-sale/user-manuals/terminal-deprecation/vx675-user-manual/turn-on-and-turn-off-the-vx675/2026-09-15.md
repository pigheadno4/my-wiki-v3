# Turn on and turn off the VX675

Turn your terminal on to begin setting it up for payments.

## Turn on the VX675

* Hold **![](/user/pages/reuse/image-library/buttons/03.confirmvx675/6_vx675.png)** until the terminal displays the startup screen.

## Turn off the VX675

* Hold **![](/user/pages/docs/03.point-of-sale/48.user-manuals/26.terminal-deprecation/18.vx675-user-manual/02.turn-on-and-turn-off-the-vx675/7_vx675.png)**. The terminal will beep repeatedly and then turns off.
