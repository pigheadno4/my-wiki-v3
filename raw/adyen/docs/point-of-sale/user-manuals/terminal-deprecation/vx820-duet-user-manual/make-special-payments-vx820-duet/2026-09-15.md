# Make special payments - VX820 Duet

The special payment functions menu allows access to custom or ad-hoc payment options. The menu may vary depending on your merchant configuration.

## Steps

To enter the special payment functions menu, do the following:

1. Press  ****![](/user/pages/reuse/image-library/buttons/09.confirmvx820/15_vx820.png)****  + **8**.

2. Enter the PIN code to unlock the device.

3. Review one or more of the following payment options, which are available depending on your configuration:

   1. **Normal payment** - Choose this option to access the payment screen, where you can enter an amount.

   2. **Manual entry payment** - Choose this option to access the payment screen.

      1. After entering an amount, press  ****![](/user/pages/reuse/image-library/buttons/09.confirmvx820/15_vx820.png)**** .
      2. You are prompted to input the [PAN](/get-started-with-adyen/adyen-glossary/#card-number-pan), and then the card expiry date.

The transaction then follows the normal flow.

The special payment functions menu also includes the option to refund a payment. For more information, see [Perform a refund - VX820 Duet](/point-of-sale/user-manuals/terminal-deprecation/vx820-duet-user-manual/perform-a-refund-vx820-duet).
