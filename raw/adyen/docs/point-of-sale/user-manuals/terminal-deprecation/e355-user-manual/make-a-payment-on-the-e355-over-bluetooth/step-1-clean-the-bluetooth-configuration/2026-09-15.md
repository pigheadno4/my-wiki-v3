# Step 1- Clean the Bluetooth configuration

Start with a clean Bluetooth configuration. Make sure that you remove other paired devices unless they are necessary. 

## Steps

1. Press **![](/user/pages/reuse/image-library/buttons/01.confirme355/2_e355.png)** + **9** to open the Admin menu.\
   **Note:** Use the **\*** and **#** key to scroll up and down through the menu.

2. Press **1. Network**.

3. Press **3. Bluetooth**.

4. Press **1. Paired devices**.

5. Pres&#x73;**&#xA0;**![](/user/pages/reuse/image-library/buttons/01.confirme355/2_e355.png)**.**

6. Choose a device and remove it by pressing **1.** **Unpair**.

   Unpairing is only possible when the device is disconnected

7. Repeat these steps until all devices are removed.
