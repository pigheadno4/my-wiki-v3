# Turn on and turn off the e315

Turn your terminal on to begin setting it up for payments.

## Turn on the terminal

1. Connect the iPod or iPhone and the e315 and ensure both are fully charged.
2. Turn on the iPhone or iPod to automatically turn on the e315.

## Turn off the terminal

1. Turn off the iOS device.

   Disconnect the device from the power source before turning off.

2. Hold **![](/user/pages/reuse/image-library/buttons/12.cancele315/1_e315.png)** on the e315 for more than five seconds.

3. It will beep repeatedly until it begins to power down.
