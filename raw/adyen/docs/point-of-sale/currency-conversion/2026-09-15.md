# Dynamic Currency Conversion

Use Dynamic Currency Conversion to let shoppers pay in their own currency.

Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) gives shoppers from abroad a choice to pay in the local currency or their own currency. Adyen's DCC feature is easy to configure, does not require an extra contract, and includes integrated reporting. However, you must comply with [regulations](#regulations), including what is shown on receipts.

Currency conversion is supported for contact chip and contactless Visa, Mastercard, and Maestro payments and [authorization adjustments](/point-of-sale/pre-authorisation#capture-authorization).

## How it works

To use DCC:

1. You enable DCC [in the Customer Area](?tab=customer-area_1#enable-dcc) or [using API calls](?tab=api-calls_2#enable-dcc).

2. You initiate a payment like you usually do, and the shopper presents their card to the payment terminal.

3. The terminal recognizes the currency that is used for billing the shopper when they use their card.

4. If the shopper's billing currency differs from the local currency, the terminal prompts the shopper to choose between paying in the local currency and their own billing currency.

   ![Payment terminal showing a DCC currency selection screen with USD option](/images/3/a/d/4/d/3ad4d2288e74db8061cbee7c83aec96c387e0f7a-screen-endcc-portrait-usd.png)

5. If the shopper accepts DCC by choosing their own billing currency, foreign-exchange conversion is applied to the transaction. The Adyen-generated receipt includes information about the conversion. If the shopper rejects DCC by choosing the local currency, the transaction continues in the local currency.

## Enable DCC

You can enable DCC in the Customer Area or by using API calls. Select the tab below for your preferred method.

### Tab: Customer Area

1. Log in to your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/).

2. Select the merchant account, store, or terminal for which you want to enable DCC.

3. Go to **Devices** > **Device settings** > **Payment features**.

4. Select **Dynamic currency conversion (DCC)** and turn on the **Enable DCC** toggle.

5. Select **Save**.

6. Go to **Settings**, and under **Product settings** for **Payments**, select **Payment methods**. Check if the **Configured currencies** for Visa, MasterCard, and Maestro are set to **ANY**. The currency of payment methods is set by default and depends on the specific payment method type. If your currency configuration is different and you need to change it, contact our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other).

7. Contact our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other) to:

   * Enable DCC reporting for your merchant account(s). This ensures [financial reports](#reports) and [dashboards](#reports) include DCC details.
   * (Optional) Enable DCC for pre-authorization and authorization adjustment.

### Tab: API calls

1. Make a PATCH request to one of the following `/terminalSettings` endpoints for the company account, merchant account, store, or terminal:

   * PATCH [/companies/{companyId}/terminalSettings](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/Management/latest/patch/companies/\(companyId\)/terminalSettings)
   * PATCH [/merchants/{merchantId}/terminalSettings](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/Management/latest/patch/merchants/\(merchantId\)/terminalSettings)
   * PATCH [/stores/{storeId}/terminalSettings](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/Management/latest/patch/stores/\(storeId\)/terminalSettings)
   * PATCH [/terminals/{terminalId}/terminalSettings](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/Management/latest/patch/terminals/\(terminalId\)/terminalSettings)

   In the request body, include a [dcc](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/Management/latest/patch/companies/\(companyId\)/terminalSettings#request-dcc) object with [enableDcc](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/Management/latest/patch/companies/\(companyId\)/terminalSettings#request-dcc-enableDcc) set to **true**.

   **PATCH /terminalSettings request**

   ```bash
   curl https://management-test.adyen.com/v3/terminals/{terminalId}/terminalSettings \
   -H 'x-API-key: ADYEN_API_KEY' \
   -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
   -X PATCH \
   -d '{
        "dcc": {
           "enableDcc": true
        }
      }'
   ```

2. In your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/), go to **Settings**, and under **Product settings** for **Payments**, select **Payment methods**. Check if the **Configured currencies** for Visa, MasterCard, and Maestro are set to **ANY**. The currency of payment methods is set by default and depends on the specific payment method type. If your currency configuration is different and you need to change it, contact our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other).

3. Contact our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other) to:

   * Enable DCC reporting for your merchant account(s). This ensures [financial reports](#reports) and [dashboards](#reports) include DCC details.
   * (Optional) Enable DCC for pre-authorization and authorization adjustment.

## Complying with regulations

To avoid fines and the loss of DCC privileges, it is important to comply with the disclosure and receipt requirements of the card schemes when choosing to offer DCC to your shoppers.

According to card scheme regulations, the shopper must be given the choice to accept DCC, or decline it and pay in the local currency. The shopper must be made aware of the cost of the currency conversion, and it is not permitted to force a choice on the cardholder.

Refer to [Dynamic Currency Conversion - Rules & Regulations](https://help.adyen.com/knowledge/in-person-payments/terminal-features/dynamic-currency-conversion-dcc-rules-regulations) for detailed guidance on card scheme requirements.

### Disclosure and receipt requirements

The Adyen DCC feature allows you to offer shoppers the choice to accept or decline DCC in a way that is fully compliant with card scheme regulations, with full disclosure of the exchange rate and markups. The shopper can review DCC charges both on the payment terminal and on the Adyen-generated receipt.

The following illustrations show the information on a portrait, and landscape terminal display.

### If you do not use Adyen-generated receipts

To remain compliant with card scheme regulations if your integration defines the content of the receipt, make sure that the receipt contains the following information:

* Exchange rate
* Source of the exchange rate
* Markup
* Amount in shopper's billing currency
* Amount in local currency
* DCC acceptance statement

To find out more, contact your Account Manager or our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other).

### If you use authorization adjustment

To remain compliant with card scheme regulations if you use DCC with pre-authorization and authorization adjustment:

1. Make sure that you have [set up webhooks](/development-resources/webhooks) to receive updates and learn whether the final amount has been captured.
2. Add the details from the [CAPTURE webhook](/point-of-sale/pre-authorisation#capture-authorization) to the receipt or invoice.

## Reconciliation

DCC can affect your (automated) reconciliation. For more information, contact your Account Manager or our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other).

Funds are settled in your settlement currency. The amount that you start the transaction with (before DCC) will be paid to you. For example, you start a transaction for USD 100, and the shopper accepts DCC for EUR 90. Adyen will settle USD 100 (plus your share of the markup).

## Financial reports and overviews

When you have enabled DCC and DCC reporting, you can find information about DCC transactions in the following financial reports and overviews in your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/):

#### Reports

* The [Received payment details report](/reporting/received-payment-details-report) shows whether DCC is 'Accepted' or 'Declined'.
* The [Settlement details report](/reporting/settlement-reconciliation/transaction-level/settlement-details-report) includes the markup on the DCC transaction as a credit. To see the markup, you must first manually [configure the **DCC Markup** column](/reporting/settlement-reconciliation/transaction-level/settlement-details-report/#configure-report-columns).
* The [Aggregate Settlement details report](/reporting/settlement-reconciliation/batch-level/aggregate-settlement-details-report) includes all the markups on the DCC transaction as a credit on the store or the merchant account level. To see the markup, you must first manually [configure the **DCC Markup** column](/reporting/settlement-reconciliation/batch-level/aggregate-settlement-details-report/#configure-report-columns).

#### Overviews

* The Payments list, available under **Transactions** > **Payments**.\
  To see whether DCC was applied to a transaction, select the columns **DCC offered** and **DCC accepted**.
* The **DCC** pane on the Payment Details page, available when you open a payment in the Payments list: the pane shows whether DCC was offered/accepted, the base and transaction currency, and the applied exchange rate.
* The [DCC Overview dashboard](/point-of-sale/currency-conversion/download-and-use-the-dcc-overview-file/), available under **Reports** > **Point of sale** > **Dashboards** > **Dynamic currency conversion**. This shows DCC usage and acceptance across different geographies and retail locations, earnings from DCC, and the [reasons why DCC was not offered](/point-of-sale/currency-conversion/download-and-use-the-dcc-overview-file#dcc-not-offered). The DCC dashboard is intended for management or operations teams.
