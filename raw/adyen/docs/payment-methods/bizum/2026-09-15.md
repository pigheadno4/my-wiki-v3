# Bizum

Accept Bizum payments with your online payments integration.

![](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/24.bizum/bizum.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about Bizum on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/bizum).

Bizum is an Online Banking payment method in Spain that allows customers to make instant payments directly from their bank account, without the need for a credit or debit card.

## Activate Bizum

Before you add Bizum to your integration:

1. Make sure that you have a contract with a local supported bank (this is the acquirer).\
   Visit Bizum's official site to see a list of [available banks](https://bizum.com/es/en/banks).

2. Receive your *MID* and Terminal ID from the acquirer.\
   MID is also referred to as *FUC*.

3. Inform the acquirer that Adyen will process payments for you so they can connect your MID with Adyen as a gateway.\
   Adyen's *IPSP* number for e-commerce and Bizum operations is **31710**.

After you have received your MID and Terminal ID from the acquirer, you can start adding Bizum to your integration.
