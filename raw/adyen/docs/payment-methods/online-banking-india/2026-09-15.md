# Online Banking India

Accept Online Banking India payments with your online payments integration.

![logo](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/58.online-banking-india/billdesk_online.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about Online Banking India on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/online-banking-india).

Learn about pricing and settlement services for [Online Banking India on adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods#pmx=online-banking-india).

To process payments with Online Banking India, you must comply with [regulations in India](/online-payments/payments-india-compliance-guide/), and have a local entity and a local integration in India. Reach out to your account manager or our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other) for more information.

When a shopper pays with Online Banking India, they are presented with a list of issuing banks. After the shopper selects an issuing bank, they are redirected to the bank's website or mobile app to complete the payment.
