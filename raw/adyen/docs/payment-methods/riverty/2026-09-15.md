# Riverty

Accept Riverty payments with your online payments integration.

![Riverty logo](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/74.riverty/riverty_default.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about Riverty on [riverty.com](https://www.riverty.com).

Riverty is a Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) provider that enables merchants to accept invoice payments that are immediately settled in full. Riverty takes over the responsibility for the shopper's liability and credit risk. Riverty also handles payment reminders and other debt collection procedures. Riverty handles disputes directly with merchants. Funds are settled T+2. Riverty is not available for sales day payout.

With Riverty, the shopper can choose to pay:

* in 14 days
* a monthly consolidated invoice
* using a secure direct debit
* in split installment payments (only available in Germany, Austria and Netherlands)
