# Revolut Pay

Learn how to accept payments with Revolut Pay.

![Revolut Pay](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/74.revolutpay/revolutpay.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto) **Read more**

Learn more about Revolut Pay on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/).

Revolut Pay is in a pilot phase.

Revolut Pay is a digital wallet that enables customers to pay directly from their Revolut account using a fast and secure authentication flow.

When shopping online:

* On a mobile device, the shopper is redirected to the Revolut app, where they are directed to the appropriate screen for completing the payment. Most Revolut users have the app installed, but in case it isn't, shoppers are able to sign-in and checkout via the web browser.
* On a desktop or a laptop, a new tab will open, presenting shoppers with a login screen offering four different sign-in methods (QR code, email, SMS, and passkey) and two distinct payment channels (via Revolut app or the web browser).
