# Samsung Pay

Learn how to accept Samsung Pay payments.

![](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/75.samsung-pay/samsungpay.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)   **Read more**

Learn about pricing and settlement services for [Samsung Pay on adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods#pmx=samsung-pay).

Samsung Pay is a mobile wallet provided by Samsung for Android OS. The app comes pre-installed in new Samsung devices. Shoppers can add cards on their Samsung Pay app, and then use the app to make payments in your app or at your physical point of sale.

For point-of-sale payments, the payment terminal communicates with the Samsung Pay wallet through the [Near-Field communication (NFC)](/get-started-with-adyen/adyen-glossary/#contactless-or-near-field-communication-nfc) chip in the shopper's phone or smartwatch.

For ecommerce payments, when shoppers select Samsung Pay and the app is installed on their mobile device, they are presented with the Samsung Pay payment sheet. Shoppers can then select the card that they want to use and then proceed to complete the payment.

#### Compatibility

Samsung Pay is available on specific devices, in specific countries/regions, and with specific issuers.

* List of [supported Samsung devices](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/owners/app/samsung-pay)
* For a list of supported issuers in each country/region, refer to the Samsung Pay website for the specific country/region. For example, see the list for Samsung Pay issuers in the [US](https://www.samsung.com/us/samsung-pay/) or in the [UK](https://www.samsung.com/uk/samsung-pay/)
