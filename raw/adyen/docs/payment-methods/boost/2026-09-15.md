# Boost Wallet

Accept Boost payments with your online payments integration.

![](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/27.boost/boost.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about Boost Wallet on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/boost-wallet).

Boost Wallet is a digital wallet available in Malaysia.

We offer Boost Wallet via Razer Merchant Services (RMS - formerly known as MOLPay). Boost Wallet is backed by Malaysia's leading telco group, Axiata. It is seen as one of Malaysia's favourite home-grown lifestyle e-wallets.
