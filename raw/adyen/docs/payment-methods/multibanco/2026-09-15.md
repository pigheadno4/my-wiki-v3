# Multibanco

Accept Multibanco payments with your online payments integration.

![logo](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/50.multibanco/multibanco.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about Multibanco on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/multibanco).

Multibanco is the most popular payment method in Portugal. The shopper gets a voucher for the payment at the checkout. They use the voucher to make the payment at an ATM with a debit card, or using online banking.

A payment voucher for Multibanco includes:

* A payment reference.
* An entity number.
* The payable amount.
* The expiry date of the voucher.

You can set the expiry date of the voucher according to your business needs. To do so, contact the [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other).
