# Touch'n Go

Learn how to accept Touch'n Go payments.

Touch'n Go eWallet is a digital wallet that holds electronic money in Malaysia. Initially a public transportation card, it is now one of the most popular wallets in Malaysia.

When paying with Touch'n Go on desktop, the shopper is redirected to a web browser where a QR code is displayed. The shopper must use their Touch'n Go app to scan the QR code, and confirm the payment on their app.

When paying with Touch'n Go on mobile, the shopper is redirected to the Touch'n Go app on their phone. After confirming the payment, the shopper is redirected back to your webshop.

If a shopper is paying with Touch'n Go on mobile but doesn't have the Touch'n Go app installed on their phone, they are redirected to a Touch'n Go mobile browser to enter their phone number and a PIN. After confirming the payment, the shopper is redirected back to your webshop.
