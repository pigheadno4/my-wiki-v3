# Ratepay

Accept Ratepay payments in Austria, Germany, the Netherlands, and Switzerland

![](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/74.ratepay/ratepay.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about Ratepay on [ratepay.com](https://www.ratepay.com/en/).

Ratepay provides buy now, pay later (Open invoice) payments in Austria, Germany, the Netherlands, and Switzerland. Ratepay also provides secured direct debit payments in Austria, Germany, and the Netherlands.

Before approving the payment, Ratepay performs risk checks on the shopper. If successful, Ratepay takes on the full fraud risk for the payment, and pays you up front.

If the shopper chooses to pay with Buy Now Pay Later, Ratepay pays you as soon as you capture the payment.
