# Enhanced scheme data for American Express

Send enhanced scheme data for US domestic transactions with American Express.

As part of American Express's enhanced scheme data (ESD) program you can submit data for US domestic transactions as additional fraud protection or for reporting purposes. There is no financial incentive for sending ESD to American Express.
