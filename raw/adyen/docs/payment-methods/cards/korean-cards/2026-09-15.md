# Korean Card Payments via redirect

Accept Korean Card Payments via redirect with your online payments integration.

![](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/04.cards/korean-cards/kcp.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about Korean Card Payments on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/korean-cards).

Korea Cyber Payment (KCP) is a payment services provider that accepts all cards issued in South Korea, including single-branded and co-branded cards. When a shopper proceeds to pay on your website or app, they are redirected to a KCP-hosted payment page.
