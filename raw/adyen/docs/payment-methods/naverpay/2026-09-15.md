# NaverPay

Accept NaverPay payments with your online payments integration.

![](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/53.naverpay/naverpay.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about [NaverPay](https://pay.naver.com) on their website.

NaverPay is a digital wallet used in South Korea that links directly to a shopper's credit card. NaverPay is now the most popular mobile payment option in South Korea.
