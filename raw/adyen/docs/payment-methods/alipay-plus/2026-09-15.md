# Alipay+

Accept Alipay+ payments with your online payments integration.

![Alipay+ logo](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/11.alipay-plus/alipay-plus.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about Alipay+ on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/alipay-plus).

Alipay+ is a cross-border digital payment method network that accepts various mobile wallets from different countries through a single integration.

It acts as a "wallet of wallets", similar to a "scheme" with the underlying wallets as issuers. This allows consumers to use their preferred local payment methods, such as Alipay, GCash, or Touch 'n Go, at global merchants.

Alipay+ connects merchants to 11 wallets, and supports online browser and mobile app flows.

## Wallets included with Alipay+

The following wallets are included in Alipay+:

* Alipay
* AlipayHK
* DANA
* Kakao Pay
* K Plus
* NAVER Pay
* Touch 'n Go eWallet
* Toss
* TrueMoney
* GCash
* Rabbit LINE Pay

## Alipay+ onboarding

An onboarding request is sent to all wallets for review. Each wallet follows its own risk requirements to review the onboarding request. The expected SLA for all wallets to be onboarded is within 1 month.

For TrueMoney wallet onboarding, a separate TrueMoney onboarding form must be completed by Adyen Support and submitted to Alipay+ on your behalf.
