# Clearpay

Accept Clearpay payments with your online payments integration.

![](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/30.clearpay/clearpay.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about Clearpay on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/clearpay-afterpay).

Clearpay is a popular buy now, pay later service in the United Kingdom.

With Clearpay, the shopper can pay for the goods in four installments. Before approving the payment, Clearpay performs risk checks on the shopper. If successful, Clearpay takes on the full fraud risk for the payment, and pays you up front.

In the US, this service is known as [Cash App Afterpay](/payment-methods/cash-app-afterpay) or **$Afterpay**. In Australia, Canada, and New Zealand, it is called [Afterpay](/payment-methods/afterpaytouch).

## Chargebacks

Shoppers can initiate chargebacks directly with Clearpay up to 120 days after the payment, cancellation, or expected receipt of goods or services. When Clearpay opens a dispute, you have 13 days to defend or accept the dispute. If you do not respond within 13 days, or if you accept the dispute, the dispute is lost and your account is debited for the chargeback amount. If you defend the dispute, Clearpay has 30 days to make their decision.

For guidelines when a dispute is raised, see [Afterpay and Clearpay chargebacks](/risk-management/chargeback-guidelines/afterpay-chargebacks).
