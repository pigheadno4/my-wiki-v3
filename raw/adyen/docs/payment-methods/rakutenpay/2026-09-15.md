# Rakuten Pay

Accept Rakuten Pay payments with your online payments integration.

Rakuten Pay is a digital wallet in Japan. For both the desktop and mobile flows, the shopper is redirected to the Rakuten Pay website and then back to merchant page. Rakuten Pay is available for both Ecommerce and IPP.
