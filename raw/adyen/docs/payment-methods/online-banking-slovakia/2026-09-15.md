# Online banking Slovakia

Accept Online banking Slovakia payments with your online payments integration.

**Read more**

Learn about pricing and settlement services for [online banking Slovakia on adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/online-banking-slovakia).

Online banking is the second most popular method in Slovakia after paying by card.

When a shopper pays with Online banking Slovakia, they are presented with a list of issuing banks. After the shopper selects an issuing bank, they are redirected to the bank's website or mobile app to complete the payment.

If you integrate Online banking Slovakia through Adyen, you do not need to enter into contracts with acquiring banks.
