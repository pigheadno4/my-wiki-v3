# Online banking Poland

Accept Online banking Poland payments with your online payments integration.

**Read more**

Learn more about Online banking Poland on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/online-banking-poland).

Online banking is one of the most popular payment methods in Poland. We offer online banking in Poland through our partner Przelewy24 (P24).

When a shopper pays with Online banking Poland, they are presented with a list of issuing banks. After the shopper selects an issuing bank, they are redirected to the bank's website or mobile app to complete the payment.
