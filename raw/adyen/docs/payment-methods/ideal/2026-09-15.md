# iDEAL | Wero

Accept iDEAL | Wero payments with your online payments integration.

![](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/38.ideal/ideal.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about iDEAL | Wero on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/ideal).

iDEAL | Wero is the most used online banking method in the Netherlands, supported by all the major Dutch consumer banks. Nearly all online shoppers in the Netherlands know iDEAL | Wero, and have used it at least once.

When a shopper pays with iDEAL | Wero, they are presented with a list of issuing banks. After the shopper selects an issuing bank, they are redirected to the bank's website or mobile app to complete the payment.

If you integrate iDEAL | Wero through Adyen, you do not need to enter into contracts with iDEAL | Wero acquiring banks.

iDEAL is evolving into "Wero", a new pan-European payment solution by the European Payments Initiative (EPI). The first step is the introduction of the mandatory iDEAL-Wero co-branding on all existing iDEAL checkout pages.

If you use iDEAL on API-Only, you have between **January 29, 2026** and **March 31, 2026** to complete the following:

* Change the payment method name from **iDEAL** to **iDEAL | Wero**
* Update the iDEAL logo to the iDEAL Wero co-branded logo. Follow the instructions for [downloading logos](https://docs.adyen.com/online-payments/build-your-integration/advanced-flow/?platform=Web\&integration=API%2Bonly\&version=71#downloading-logos), and set `pm-type` to **ideal**.
