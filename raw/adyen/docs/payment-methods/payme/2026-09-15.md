# PayMe

Learn how to accept PayMe payments.

PayMe is a Hong Kong-based wallet payment method that allows shoppers to top up their balance from their bank account.

Shoppers using a desktop or laptop scan a QR code to pay with their PayMe app. Shoppers using a mobile device are redirected to the PayMe app on their device.

To accept payments with PayMe, you must have a local entity in Hong Kong.
