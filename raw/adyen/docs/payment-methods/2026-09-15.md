# Payment methods

An overview of the payment methods that you can add to your online payments integration.

This page shows the supported payment methods for online payments integrations using Drop-in, Components, or API-only. For supported payment methods for other integrations, see [Pay by link](/unified-commerce/pay-by-link/supported-payment-methods), [In-person payments](/point-of-sale/what-we-support/payment-methods), or [Plugins](/plugins).

## Available filters

Country/Region, Processing currency, Settlement currency, Feature, Supported integration
