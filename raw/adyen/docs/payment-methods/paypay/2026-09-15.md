# PayPay

Accept PayPay payments with your online payments integration.

![PayPay logo](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/71.paypay/paypay.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about PayPay on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/paypay).

PayPay is a digital wallet in Japan, where approximately half of the population are registered users.
