# Cash App Pay

Learn how to accept Cash App Pay payments.

**Learn more**

Learn about pricing and settlement services for [Cash App Pay on adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/cash-app-pay).

Cash App Pay is a US digital wallet that lets shoppers pay with the balance in their Cash App or with their linked credit card, debit card, or bank account.

## Authorization adjustment

When the final transaction amount changes after the initial authorization, you can incrementally increase the authorized amount on the existing transaction. This allows you to perform one consolidated capture of the total amount.

[Authorization adjustment](/online-payments/adjust-authorisation) is supported under these conditions:

* Cash App Pay only supports an increase in incremental authorization.
* Disable auto-capture, because authorization adjustment requires manual capture.
* We support synchronous authorization adjustment, not asynchronous authorization adjustment.
* To access the authorization adjustment feature, request manual approval from Cash App via [Adyen](mailto:aemen.hussain@adyen.com?subject=Authorization%20adjustment%20-%20Cash%20App%20Pay).

## Chargebacks

Shoppers can initiate chargebacks directly with Cash App Pay up to 120 days after the payment, cancellation, or expected receipt of goods or services. When Cash App Pay opens a dispute, the funds are charged back, and you have 13 days to defend the dispute. If you do not respond within 13 days, the dispute is lost in favor of the shopper. If you do respond within 13 days and win the dispute, the funds are returned to you.

For guidelines when a dispute is raised, see [Cash App Pay chargebacks](/risk-management/chargeback-guidelines/cash-app-pay-chargebacks).
