# PayPo

Accept PayPo payments with your online payments integration.

**Read more**

Learn more about PayPo on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/paypo).

PayPo is the most popular buy now pay later payment method in Poland, with over a million active users. PayPo allows shoppers to pay within 30 days. It also allows shoppers to split a payment up to 6 installments.
