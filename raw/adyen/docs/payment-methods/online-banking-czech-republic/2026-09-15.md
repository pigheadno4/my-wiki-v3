# Online banking Czech Republic

Accept Online banking Czech Republic payments with your online payments integration.

**Read more**

Learn more about Online banking Czech Republic on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/online-banking-czech-republic).

Online banking is the second most popular payment method in Czech Republic after paying by card.

When a shopper pays with Online banking Czech Republic, they are presented with a list of issuing banks. After the shopper selects an issuing bank, they are redirected to the bank's website or mobile app to complete the payment.

If you integrate Online banking Czech Republic through Adyen, you do not need to enter into contracts with acquiring banks.
