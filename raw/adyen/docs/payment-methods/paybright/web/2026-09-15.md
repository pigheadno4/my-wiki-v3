# PayBright for Web

Accept PayBright payments on your website.

PayBright has been acquired by [Affirm](/payment-methods/affirm). Adyen will not accept new PayBright integrations.

## How do you want to integrate?

###### [Drop-in](/payment-methods/paybright/web-drop-in)

[Our all-in-one UI solution to add PayBright to your website.](/payment-methods/paybright/web-drop-in)

###### [Components](/payment-methods/paybright/web-component)

[Our pre-built Component to render PayBright on your website.](/payment-methods/paybright/web-component)

For more information, refer to [Web Drop-in](/online-payments/drop-in-web) or [Components](/online-payments/components-web).
