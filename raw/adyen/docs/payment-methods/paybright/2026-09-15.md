# PayBright

Learn how to accept PayBright payments in Canada.

PayBright has been acquired by [Affirm](/payment-methods/affirm). Adyen will not accept new PayBright integrations.

[PayBright](https://www.paybright.com/) is the leading buy now, pay later (BNPL) service provider in Canada.

With PayBright, the shopper can pay for the goods in four equal installments. Before approving the payment, PayBright performs risk checks on the shopper. If successful, PayBright takes on the full fraud risk for the payment, and pays you up front.

With PayBright, the payment flow is the following:

1. The shopper chooses PayBright as their payment method during checkout.
2. Once they are redirected to PayBright webpage, the shopper needs to fill in or confirm their details, and validate their information.
3. The shopper will see a message confirming that the payment installment request has been accepted. They can then return to your website or app.
