# PaySafeCard

Accept PaySafeCard payments with your online payments integration.

![](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/70.paysafecard/paysafecard.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about PaySafeCard on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/paysafecard).

PaySafeCard is one of the leading online prepaid payment methods, allowing shoppers to pay as safely online as they would if they were paying with cash. It is the perfect solution for anyone who does not own a bank account or credit card, or for shoppers who are concerned about privacy and security. This makes it an appealing payment method for gamers and gamblers.

## Customer Data Exchange

In European Union countries where it is required to perform KYC on gambling customers, the [Customer Data Exchange](/payment-methods/paysafecard/customer-data-exchange) feature can be used to prevent acquiring funds from an anonymous shopper.
