# WeChat Pay QR for desktop browser and H5 for mobile browser.

Accept WeChat Pay payments on your website accessed using a desktop or mobile browser.

Accept WeChat Pay payments on your website, for shoppers using desktop or mobile browser. Our Drop-in or Component shows a WeChat Pay button on your checkout page.

If the shopper is using a desktop browser, show a QR code that they can scan with their WeChat Pay app. This flow works out of the box when you add WeChat Pay in your Customer Area.

![](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/89.wechat-pay/01.wechat-pay-desktop-qr-payments/wechat-desktop.png) 

If the shopper is using a mobile browser, redirect them to their WeChat Pay app to complete the payment, then redirect them back to your payment page. To use this flow, contact our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other) with your web shop URL for enrolment with WeChat Pay.

Note that if the shopper is using your mobile app, you need to implement the [in-app flow](/payment-methods/wechat-pay/wechat-pay-in-app-payments/).
