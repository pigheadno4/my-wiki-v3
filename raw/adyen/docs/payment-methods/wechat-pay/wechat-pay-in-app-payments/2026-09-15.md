# WeChat Pay in-app payments

Accept WeChat Pay payments from your mobile app.

To accept WeChat Pay payments on your mobile app, you can use your existing Android Drop-in or Component integration, or build the integration yourself using WeChat mobile SDKs.

When the shopper selects to pay with WeChat Pay, the shopper is switched to the WeChat app to verify the payment. When the payment is completed, they are switched back to your app. You can then present the payment result to your shopper.
