# Wallets India

Accept Wallets India payments with your online payments integration.

To process payments with Wallets India, you must comply with [regulations in India](/online-payments/payments-india-compliance-guide/), and have a local entity and a local integration in India. Reach out to your account manager or our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other) for more information.

When a shopper pays with Wallets India, they are presented with a list of available wallet options. After the shopper selects a wallet, they are redirected to the wallet's page to complete the payment.
