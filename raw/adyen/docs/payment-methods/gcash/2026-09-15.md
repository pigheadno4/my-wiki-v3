# GCash

Accept GCash payments with your online payments integration.

![](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/33.gcash/gcash.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about GCash on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/gcash).

GCash is one of the most popular e-wallets in the Philippines. Users sign up for GCash by registering with their mobile number and setting up a 4-digit Mobile Personal Identification Number (MPIN). After they register, they can start funding their GCash account by transferring funds from their bank accounts or by depositing cash at GCash stores.

#### How to pay with GCash

Shoppers can make a one-time purchase or sign up for a recurring transaction with GCash:

### Tab: One-time purchase

After shoppers select GCash on your website or mobile app:

1. Shoppers are redirected to the GCash's website where they are prompted to log in with their mobile number.
2. Shoppers authenticate the payment by providing a one-time password that they receive through SMS, and their 4-digit MPIN.
3. Shoppers are prompted to confirm the payment.
4. Shoppers are redirected back to your website or mobile app.
5. Shoppers get an SMS notification informing them of their purchase.

### Tab: Recurring

After shoppers select GCash on your website:

1. Shoppers are redirected to a page where they are presented with a QR code.
2. Shoppers scan the QR code with their GCash app.
3. Shoppers are redirected back to your website or mobile app.
4. Shoppers get an SMS notification informing them of the auto-debit transaction.

If shoppers are using your mobile app and they select GCash:

1. Shoppers are redirected to the GCash's website where they are prompted to log in with their mobile number.
2. Shoppers authenticate the payment by providing a one-time password that they receive through SMS, and their 4-digit MPIN.
3. Shoppers are prompted to confirm the transaction.
4. Shoppers are redirected back to your mobile app.
5. Shoppers get an SMS notification informing them of the auto-debit transaction.

When a shopper is redirected to GCash, the redirect page is valid for 30 minutes by default. After 30 minutes, the page will timeout. If you want to change the link expiry, contact our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other).
