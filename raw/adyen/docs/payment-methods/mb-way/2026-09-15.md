# MB Way

Accept MB Way payments with your online payments integration.

![MB WAY logo](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/46.mb-way/mbway.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about MB Way on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/mb-way).

MB WAY is a popular payment method in Portugal.

The shopper experience involves:

* Web: The shopper enters their mobile phone number and selects to pay. They receive a notification in their MB WAY app to confirm the transaction.
* App (App2App): The shopper selects MB WAY in your mobile app and is automatically redirected to the MB WAY app to authorize the payment. No phone number entry is required.

MB WAY is a Multibanco solution, but you do not need a contract with Multibanco if you integrate through Adyen.

MB WAY users have default daily cumulative limit of EUR 1000, which they can adjust up to EUR 5000 in their MB WAY app.
