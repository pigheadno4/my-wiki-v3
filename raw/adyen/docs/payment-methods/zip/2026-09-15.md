# Zip

Accept Zip payments in Australia or the United States.

![ZIP logo](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/91.zip/zip.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto) **Read more**\
Learn how Zip works on [zip.co](https://zip.co/how-zip-works).

Zip offers one of the leading Buy Now Pay Later services in Australia through their Zip Pay and Zip Money products. With Zip, shoppers can select their installment schedule—from weekly, fortnightly, or monthly payments.

#### How to pay with Zip

After shoppers select Zip on your website or mobile app:

1. Shoppers are redirected to Zip's website where they are prompted to sign in to their Zip account.
2. Shoppers authenticate the payment by providing a one-time password that they receive through SMS.
3. Shoppers are redirected back to your website or app.
