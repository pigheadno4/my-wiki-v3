# Alipay HK

Accept Alipay HK payments with your online payments integration.

![](/images/d/6/d/3/3/d6d33d6801caa9c84a77e2d644c89341c858161c-alipayhk-logo.png)  **Read more**

Learn more about Alipay HK on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/alipayhk).

AlipayHK is a popular wallet payment method available in Hong Kong, with over 2,000 online and physical stores accepting it as a payment method.

Shoppers use the app to scan a QR code, and complete the payment through password or biometrics authentication.
