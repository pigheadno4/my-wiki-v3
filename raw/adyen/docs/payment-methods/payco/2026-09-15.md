# PayCo

Accept PayCo payments with your online payments integration.

![](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/66.payco/payco.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about PayCo on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/payco).

PayCo is provided via Korea Cyber Payment (KCP).

PayCo is linked directly to the shopper's credit card.
