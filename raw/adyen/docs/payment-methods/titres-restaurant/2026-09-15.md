# Titres-restaurant

Learn how to accept Titres-restaurant payments.

Titres-restaurant meal vouchers are prepaid cards delivered by employers to their employees to buy food and beverages. These cards have a daily spending limit of EUR 25. When a shopper wants to buy something that costs more, they can pay the remaining amount with a different payment method. These cards cannot be used on a Sunday or on a public holiday.

With Adyen, you can process online payments for meal vouchers provided by [Bimpli](https://www.bimpli.com), [Sodexo](https://www.sodexo.com), and [Up Déjeuner](https://up.coop), and in-person payments for [Edenred](https://www.edenred.fr/ticket-restaurant) meal vouchers. All meal voucher cards are co-badged with [Conecs](https://www.conecs.fr).

## Activate Titres-restaurant

To accept meal voucher payments, you need an official authorization from the [Mealvoucher National Comission](http://www.cntr.fr). You also need a separate contract with each meal voucher issuer that you want to accept.

Before you add Titres-restaurant to your integration:

1. Get an official authorization from the [Mealvoucher National Comission](http://www.cntr.fr). From them, you receive a *Carte de domiciliation CONECS* with your CONECS MID.
2. For each meal voucher that you want to accept, contact the meal voucher issuer (Bimpli, Sodexo, or Up Déjeuner) for a contract.
