# Bancontact card

Accept Bancontact cards payments in Belgium.

![](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/19.bancontact/01.bancontact-card/bancontact.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn about pricing and settlement services for Bancontact on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/bancontact).

Bancontact is the most popular payment method in Belgium, and the market leader for both online and in-person transactions.

A Bancontact card payment is like a regular [card payment](/payment-methods/cards), except for the following:

* There is no CVC. For co-badged cards, the CVC field can still appear.
* [3D Secure authentication](/online-payments/3d-secure) is mandatory.
* Separate captures are not supported.

Bancontact cards are often co-badged, so your integration must be [compliant with European co-badged card regulations](/online-payments/co-badged-cards-compliance).

You can also accept payments through the [Bancontact app](/payment-methods/bancontact/bancontact-mobile).
