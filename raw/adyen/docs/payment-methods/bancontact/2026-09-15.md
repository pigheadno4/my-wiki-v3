# Bancontact

Learn how to accept Bancontact payments.

![Bancontact logo](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/19.bancontact/bancontact.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn about pricing and settlement services for Bancontact on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/bancontact).

Bancontact is the most popular payment method in Belgium, and the market leader for both online and in-person transactions.

When paying with Bancontact, the shopper can pay with either:

* [Bancontact card](/payment-methods/bancontact/bancontact-card/): this is like a regular [card payment](/payment-methods/cards), except that there is no security code, 3D Secure is mandatory, and payments are captured automatically.
* [Bancontact mobile](/payment-methods/bancontact/bancontact-mobile/): the shopper is redirected to the [Payconiq by Bancontact app](https://www.bancontact.com/en/private/online#smartphone), or to their banking app that is integrated with Bancontact, either directly on their mobile device, or by scanning a QR code on Desktop.
