# Bancontact mobile

Accept Bancontact payments in Belgium.

![Bancontact logo](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/19.bancontact/02.bancontact-mobile/bancontact.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn about pricing and settlement services for Bancontact on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/bancontact).

Bancontact is the most popular payment method in Belgium, and the market leader for both online and in-person transactions.

With Bancontact mobile, the shopper completes the payment using the [Payconiq by Bancontact mobile app](https://www.bancontact.com/en/private/online#smartphone) or another bank app that supports Bancontact mobile, installed on their mobile device. The shopper is redirected to the app either directly on their mobile device, or by scanning a QR code on Desktop.

You can also accept payments using the [Bancontact card](/payment-methods/bancontact/bancontact-card).
