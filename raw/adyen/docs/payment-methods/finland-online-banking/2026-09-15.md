# Online banking Finland

Accept Online banking Finland payments with your online payments integration.

![](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/56.finland-online-banking/finland-online-banking.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about Online banking Finland on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/online-banking-finland/).

We offer online banking in Finland through our partner Paytrail. This payment method is supported by all major banks in Finland. The shopper selects their bank from a drop-down in your checkout, and is redirected to their bank's website or app to complete the payment.

#### How to pay with Online banking Finland

After your shopper chooses to pay with Online banking Finland:

1. They are redirected to the Adyen-hosted payment page where they see a list of available banks.
2. They select their bank and are prompted to provide their online banking log-in credentials and authenticate as they would when logging into their online bank account.
3. They select the account from where they want to make the payment and are prompted to provide a one-time code to confirm the payment.
4. After they complete the payment, they are redirected back to your website or app.
