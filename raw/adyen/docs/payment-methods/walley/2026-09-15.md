# Walley

Learn how to accept Walley payments.

![](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/88.walley/walley.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn about pricing and settlement services for [Walley on adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/walley).

Walley is a Buy Now Pay Later payment method in the Nordic region, supporting both *B2C* and *B2B* payments.

Authorised payments must be captured within 90 days.

Payments in Denmark (**DK**) or with the **DKK** currency are available only for *B2C* payments.
