# Cash App Afterpay

Learn how to accept Cash App Afterpay payments in the United States of America.

![Cash App Afterpay logo](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/28.cash-app-afterpay/afterpaytouch_US.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

For more information, see [Introducing Cash App Afterpay](https://developers.cash.app/docs/merchant/welcome/getting-started) on the Cash App website.

Cash App Afterpay is a popular buy now, pay later service available to US merchants.

With Cash App Afterpay, the shopper can pay for the goods in four installments. Before approving the payment, Cash App Afterpay performs risk checks on the shopper. If successful, Cash App Afterpay takes on the full fraud risk for the payment, and pays you up front.

Cash App Afterpay is also known as **$Afterpay**. In Australia and New Zealand, this service is known as [Afterpay](/payment-methods/afterpaytouch). In the United Kingdom, it is called [Clearpay](/payment-methods/clearpay/).

## Chargebacks

Shoppers can initiate chargebacks directly with Cash App Afterpay up to 120 days after the payment, cancellation, or expected receipt of goods or services. When Cash App Afterpay opens a dispute, you have 13 days to defend or accept the dispute. If you do not respond within 13 days, or if you accept the dispute, the dispute is lost and your account is debited for the chargeback amount. If you defend the dispute, Cash App Afterpay has 30 days to make their decision.

For guidelines when a dispute is raised, see [Afterpay and Clearpay chargebacks](/risk-management/chargeback-guidelines/afterpay-chargebacks).
