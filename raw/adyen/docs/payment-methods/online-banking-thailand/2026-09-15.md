# Online banking Thailand

Accept Online banking Thailand payments with your online payments integration.

![This is the icon for Razer Merchant Services](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/62.online-banking-thailand/RazerMerchantServices.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about Online banking Thailand on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/online-banking-thailand).

Online banking is a payment method for ecommerce in many Asia-Pacific locations. In Thailand, we offer online banking through our partner Razer Merchant Services(RMS), formerly known as MOLPay.

When making an Online banking Thailand payment, the shopper is redirected to their bank's website to complete the payment. Getting a final result of such a payment can be asynchronous, so you will need to rely on webhooks to know whether the payment was successful.
