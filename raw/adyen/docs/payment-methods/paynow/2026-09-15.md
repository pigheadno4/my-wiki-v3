# PayNow

Accept PayNow payments with your online payments integration.

![](/user/pages/docs/08.payment-methods/68.paynow/paynow.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**

Learn more about PayNow on [adyen.com](https://www.adyen.com/payment-methods/paynow).

PayNow is Singapore's real-time payment network, managed by the Association Bank of Singapore (ABS) and overseen by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS). Consumers and businesses can sign up to the service using their mobile number, business registration number, or Virtual Payment Address. All users can then transfer Singapore Dollars (SGD) from one bank account or e-wallet to another.

Adyen only offers PayNow integration for QR code payments.
