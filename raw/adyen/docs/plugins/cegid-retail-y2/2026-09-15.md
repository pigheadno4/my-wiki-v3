# Cegid Retail Y2

Use our Cegid Retail Y2 CPOS plugin to accept in-person payments.

![Cegid logo](/user/pages/docs/05.plugins/14.cegid-retail-y2/cegid.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto)  **Read more**\
Learn more about [Cegid](https://www.adyen.com/partners/cegid) in our Partner Directory.

Cegid Retail Y2 is a [point-of-sale](/get-started-with-adyen/adyen-glossary/#point-of-sale-payments-or-in-person-payments) (POS) system. With our Cegid Retail Y2 CPOS plugin, you can process payments on the Adyen payments platform with Adyen payment terminals. The plugin and the terminals are integrated with our [Terminal API](/point-of-sale/design-your-integration/terminal-api). After you set up the CPOS plugin, you can also enable [Tap to Pay](/plugins/cegid-retail-y2/tap-to-pay) and [Pay by Link](/plugins/cegid-retail-y2/pay-by-link) with Cegid Retail Mobile POS on iOS and Cegid Retail Live Store on Android.

## Features

* **[Gift card payments](/point-of-sale/gift-cards-terminal-api)**: allow shoppers to pay with gift cards.
* **[Dynamic Currency Conversion](/point-of-sale/currency-conversion)**: allow shoppers from abroad to pay in the local currency or their own currency.
* **[QR code wallet payments](/point-of-sale/qr-code-wallets)**: allow shoppers to pay with their QR code wallet app on their phone.
* **[Partial authorizations](/point-of-sale/partial-authorizations)**: allow shoppers to pay the remainder with a different payment method.
* **[Manual Key Entry](/point-of-sale/enter-payment-manually)**: allow shopper to pay by manually entering their card details.
* **[Tax-free shopping](/point-of-sale/shopper-recognition/tax-free-shopping)**: allow shoppers from abroad to claim back taxes on their purchases with secure card sharing.
* **[Delayed capture](/point-of-sale/capturing-payments#delayed-automatic-capture)**: set up a delay between the authorisation and capture of the payment.
* **[Donations](/point-of-sale/donate)**: allow shoppers to donate to a chosen charity at checkout. This feature is available for credit and debit card payments.
* **[MOTO payments](/point-of-sale/mail-and-telephone-order-moto)**: allow shoppers to provide you with their order and payment details by regular mail (not email), fax, or telephone.
* **[Installments](/point-of-sale/installments)**: allow shoppers in Brazil, Japan, and Mexico to pay in installments.
* **[Pay by Link](/plugins/cegid-retail-y2/pay-by-link)**: create payment links for your shopper and allow them to pay in your physical store, with Cegid Retail Mobile POS or Cegid Retail Live Store.
* **[Tap to Pay on iPhone](/plugins/cegid-retail-y2/tap-to-pay#tap-to-pay-on-iphone)**: initiate and accept payments on an iPhone anywhere in your store, with Cegid Retail Mobile POS.
* **[Tap to Pay using the Adyen Payments app](/plugins/cegid-retail-y2/tap-to-pay#tap-to-pay-on-android)**: accept contactless payments on an Android device in your store, with Cegid Retail Live Store.

## Supported versions

You can use our plugin to integrate with Cegid Retail Y2 v11.05 and later.

We recommend to use local communications, but it is also possible to use cloud communications:

* [Local communications](/point-of-sale/design-your-integration/choose-your-architecture#local-communications): the POS system communicates directly with the payment terminal over your local network.
* [Cloud communications](/point-of-sale/design-your-integration/choose-your-architecture#cloud-communications): the POS system communicates with the terminal over the internet. Contact our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other) before you start building an integration using cloud communications.

You can use our [decision tree](/point-of-sale/design-your-integration/choose-your-architecture#choosing-between-cloud-and-local) to see which communication type is recommended for your situation.

## Get started

Before you begin to integrate, make sure you have followed the [Get started with Adyen guide](/get-started-with-adyen) to:

* Get an overview of the steps needed to accept live payments.
* Create your test account.

While you wait for your live account credentials, you can start integrating by using your test account:

1. [Set up the plugin](/plugins/cegid-retail-y2/set-up-the-plugin) in the Adyen Customer Area and the Cegid Retail Y2 back office.

2. [Set up gift card payments](/plugins/cegid-retail-y2/giftcards).

3. Optionally, set up:

   * [Tap to Pay](/plugins/cegid-retail-y2/tap-to-pay) on iPhone with Cegid Retail Mobile POS, or on Android with Cegid Retail Live Store.
   * [Pay by Link](/plugins/cegid-retail-y2/pay-by-link) with Cegid Retail Mobile POS or Cegid Retail Live Store.

4. [Test and go live with your integration](/plugins/cegid-retail-y2/set-up-the-plugin#test-and-go-live).

If you experience any issues with the plugin, check our [Troubleshooting guide](/plugins/cegid-retail-y2/troubleshooting).
