# Pay by Link

Learn how to use Pay by Link with Cegid Retail Mobile POS and Cegid Retail Live Store.

With Cegid Retail Mobile POS and Cegid Retail Live Store, you can use Adyen's [Pay by Link](/unified-commerce/pay-by-link) solution to create payment links for your shopper and allow them to pay in your physical store.

Payment links enable your shoppers to use payment methods that are not available on your payment terminal, such as online banking or buy now pay later payment methods. This is useful, for example, in scenarios where the shopper forgot their credit card or does not have enough balance or limit on their card.

See the supported payment methods in the [Pay by Link documentation](/unified-commerce/pay-by-link/supported-payment-methods).

You can set up Pay by Link with either Cegid Retail Mobile POS or Cegid Retail Live Store. The flow is the same for both apps.

Contact your Cegid representative if you need assistance with the setup.

## Requirements

The following software and hardware requirements apply to use Pay by Link:

* Cegid Retail Y2 InStoreHub installed on a Windows device, with the CPOS plugin installed.
* Cegid Retail Y2 version 18 or later.
* An Adyen payment terminal.
* For Cegid Retail Mobile POS: Cegid Retail Y2 Mobile POS 5.1.607 or later, and an iOS mobile device.
* For Cegid Retail Live Store: an Android or iOS mobile device, or any device with a web browser.

## How it works

1. In the Cegid Retail Mobile POS or Cegid Retail Live Store app, your store employee selects the option to pay with Pay by Link.

2. Your shopper navigates to the payment link by either:

   * Scanning the QR code on the store's device with their personal device.
   * Opening the payment link they received as a text message.

3. The shopper completes the payment on a secure Adyen payment page with their preferred payment method.

4. Your store employee receives the payment result in the app.

## Set up Pay by Link

The setup steps differ depending on your Cegid app. Follow the instructions that apply to your integration:

### With Cegid Retail Mobile POS

1. Contact your Cegid representative to learn more about activating the extension for your Cegid Retail account.
2. Use the [Cegid documentation](https://retail-docs.cegid.cloud/en/Y2/Mobile%20POS/Reference%20Product%20Documentation/Mobile%20POS%20V5/Accueil_MPOS_V5.htm) to set up the extension.
3. Inform your shoppers of the terms and conditions that apply to their purchase with Pay by Link by adding your terms and conditions to the Adyen-hosted payment page.\
   Refer to the [requirements for terms and conditions for Pay by Link](/unified-commerce/pay-by-link#terms-and-conditions-requirements) to learn about the information you have to include on the payment page.

### With Cegid Retail Live Store

If you want to enable Pay by Link with Cegid Retail Live Store, contact your Cegid representative.
