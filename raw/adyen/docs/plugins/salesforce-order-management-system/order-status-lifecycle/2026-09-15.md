# Order status lifecycle

Learn about order statuses in Salesforce Commerce Cloud and Order Management System.

Use the diagram to learn about order statuses, troubleshoot, and test the synchronization between [Salesforce Commerce Cloud](/plugins/salesforce-commerce-cloud) (SFCC) and the [Order Management System](/plugins/salesforce-order-management-system) (OMS).

There are two payment flows available for different payment method types in SFCC and OMS:

* **SALE flow**: The payment is captured directly after authorisation.
* **AUTH flow**: Authorisation and capture are two different steps.

For alternative payment methods, such as local payment methods, both flows are available and you can configure them in your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/). Card payment methods follow the AUTH flow by default.
