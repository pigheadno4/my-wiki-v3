# Payments

Accept online and in-person payments through a single platform with Adyen.

Adyen provides a single platform for accepting online and in-person payments. You can process payments across channels and countries/regions through one integration.

With Adyen, you can:

* **[Accept more than 200 payment methods](/payment-methods)**: Accept payments from over 200 payment methods, including cards, wallets, bank transfers, and buy now pay later options.
* **[Manage risk](/risk-management)**: Detect and mitigate fraud with risk rules, dynamic strong customer authentication (SCA), and machine-learning-powered fraud detection.
* **[Store payment details](/online-payments/tokenization)**: Tokenize payment details to let returning shoppers pay faster, support recurring payments, and accept scheduled subscription payments.
* **[Track and reconcile](/reporting)**: Use reports and dashboards to track transactions, reconcile settlements, and manage disputes.
* **[Optimize payments](/uplift)**: Increase conversion and reduce costs with data-driven recommendations from Adyen Uplift.

## Payment channels

Adyen supports online and in-person payment channels. You can integrate one or both, depending on your business needs. When you use both, omnichannel features let you connect them into a single view of your customers.
