# Partners

Become an Adyen partner.

##### Partner directory

![](/user/pages/docs/14.partners/Union.svg?decoding=auto\&fetchpriority=auto) To learn more about the plugin partners we work with, see our [Partner directory](https://www.adyen.com/partners/network).

Adyen offers a range of [plugins](/plugins) that allow customers to connect their existing systems to the Adyen payments platform with one integration. See our [plugins](/plugins) to learn about the features they offer, and how to integrate with them.

This section of our documentation is for partners that build integrations, such as plugins, that enable the partner's customers to accept payments with Adyen. As an Adyen partner, there are some functionalities and features that you must offer in your integration, and additional features that you can decide to offer your customer depending on your customer's needs. In this section, you can find guidance on the required and optional components of a partner integration.

## Become an Adyen partner

To become an [Adyen partner](https://www.adyen.com/partners/), fill our [partner application form](https://www.adyen.com/contact/partners).

Our Partnerships team will review your application, and get in touch with you about what Adyen has to offer.

The Partnerships team will also give you access to the Partner Portal. This is the dashboard where you manage all daily operations, such as managing customer accounts, or tracking commissions.

## Adyen integration toolkit

We offer various [development resources](/development-resources) that can help you integrate and reduce development time. Below are some of these resources:

* [Adyen on Github](https://github.com/orgs/Adyen/repositories).
* [API Libraries](/development-resources/libraries): use our API libraries to reduce development time.
* [Adyen plugins](/plugins): review our plugins when creating out of the box retail solutions.
* [Example integrations](https://github.com/adyen-examples).
* [PHP Webhook module](https://github.com/Adyen/php-webhook-module): handle webhooks in PHP.
* [Checkout API](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/Checkout/latest/overview): initiate and authorize online payments.
* [Management API](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/Management/latest/overview): automate the configuration and management of your Adyen account.
* [Terminal API](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/terminal-api/latest/overview): perform shopper-terminal interactions using Adyen payment terminals.

## Stay up to date

As an Adyen partner, you can benefit constantly from new features and bug fixes:

* Subscribe to our [developer mailing list](https://www.adyen.com/newsletter/developers) to ensure you stay up to date with our developer newsletter.
* Check our [new releases](/online-payments/release-notes) to learn about the latest features, fixes, and version dependencies.
* Make sure to keep your integration up to date and use the latest versions of our APIs.
* Read our [tech blog](https://www.adyen.com/knowledge-hub/payment-fraud) to learn about different types of digital payments fraud and our fraud prevention solutions.
