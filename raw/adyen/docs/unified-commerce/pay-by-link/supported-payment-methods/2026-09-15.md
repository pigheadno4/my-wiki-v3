# Supported payment methods

Learn about the supported payment methods for Pay by Link.

This page shows what payment methods are available for payment links. To offer a payment method, you must first add it to your merchant account.

## Add payment methods to your account

If you haven't done so already, add [available payment methods](#available-payment-methods) as needed to your merchant account.

1. Log in to your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/).
2. Switch to your merchant account.
3. Go to **Payments** > **Payment methods**.
4. Select **Add payment methods**.
5. Start entering the name of the payment method, then select it from the drop-down list.
6. Select **Submit**.

## Compliance of ACH Direct Debit payment links

For ACH Direct Debit payments, [Nacha](https://www.nacha.org) requires validating the customer's account details and collecting their authorization of the debit payments. Pay by Link doesn't automatically validate the bank account used, and doesn't provide the mandated text regarding the authorization. That means that for business-to-consumer (B2C) transactions, ACH Direct Debit payment links are non-compliant and put you at risk of returns.

You should use ACH Direct Debit payment links only for B2B use cases where you are confident that you have the accurate account details and that you separately collected and stored the payer's authorization (for example, in a written contract).
