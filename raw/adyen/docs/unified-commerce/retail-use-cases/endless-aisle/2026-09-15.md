# Endless aisle

Endless aisle use cases.

Endless aisle refers to a setup where you let customers place ecommerce orders in your store or other physical location. This enables you to:

* Extend your product range by letting shoppers order products that happen to be out of stock, or products that are not sold in-store because they are online exclusives or because you are operating a showroom store.

* Improve customer experience by offering the option to have the products delivered to any address. Traveling customers especially will appreciate this option.

We will discuss several variations of endless aisle:

* Order items in a store, and ship the order to a home address.
* Order items in a store, and pick up the order in a store.
