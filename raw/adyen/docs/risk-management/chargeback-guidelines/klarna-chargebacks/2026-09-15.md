# Klarna chargebacks

Learn about the Klarna dispute process and guidelines.

Klarna is a guaranteed payment method for business models with physical goods. Klarna pays you upfront and assumes the risk of non-payment by the shopper, but only if you meet Klarna's order fulfillment rules. For example, a shopper can dispute a charge if the goods were not delivered or if the delivered goods were faulty. If you do not follow the Klarna scheme rules, Klarna has the right to charge back the payment.

Klarna is updating their disputes infrastructure. Changes will take effect in phases, with the first changes taking effect on 1 November 2026, and further changes coming in March 2027.

Key changes from November 2026 include:

* **Dispute Notification removed**: You no longer receive the Dispute Notification from Klarna. Disputes move directly into the Request for Information (RFI) stage.
* **Longer defense timeframe for most reason codes**: The standard RFI response window increases from 14 to 21 days for most reason codes. Exceptions are `unauthorized_purchase` (7 days) and `high_risk_order` (96 hours).
* **Stricter evidence file requirements**: You can only upload files in PDF file format with a maximum size of 7 MB per file.

For more details about the changes and how to prepare for them, refer to the message in your [notification center](/account/notification-center). We will update the Klarna chargeback guidelines in the near future to reflect the upcoming changes.

The Klarna chargeback flow involves the stages [Dispute Notification](#dispute-notification) (until November 2026), [Request for Information (RFI)](#request-for-information), [Information Supplied](#information-supplied), and [1st Chargeback](#chargeback).

To manage this Klarna chargeback flow, you can:

* Use the [Disputes API](#klarna-dispute-api).
* Use the [Customer Area](#klarna-dispute-ca).

## Dispute Notification

When a shopper raises a complaint, Klarna creates a dispute notification. You have **21 days** to contact the shopper and resolve the complaint. For returns, the time period is **7 days**. If you cannot resolve the complaint with the shopper within that time period, Klarna will escalate the complaint to a Request for Information.

Disputes for unauthorized purchases and high risk orders must be addressed promptly. Therefore, they are escalated to a Request for Information much faster, within days or even hours after receiving a Dispute Notification.

To inform you of a Dispute Notification:

* We send you a [Request for Information dispute webhook](/risk-management/disputes-api/dispute-notifications). The reason code includes `notification` to indicate this is a Dispute Notification and not a Request for Information. For example, `notification_goods_not_received` for a Dispute Notification, compared to `goods_not_received` for a Request for Information.

* In your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/) we show the complaint under **Risk & disputes** > **Disputes** > **Requests for Information**. To indicate this is a Dispute Notification and not a Request for Information, the description of the dispute reason starts with **NOTIFICATION**. For example, *\[NOTIFICATION] The user informed that the invoice is incorrect*.

The following table shows the reason codes you can expect for Dispute Notifications.

| Dispute reason code                  | Description                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `notification_return`                | The shopper reported that they made a full or partial return.Adyen only creates return dispute notifications if the dispute is still open **14 days** after the dispute event is created. |
| `notification_goods_not_received`    | The shopper reported that they did not receive the full or partial order.                                                                                                                 |
| `notification_faulty_goods`          | The shopper reported that they received faulty goods.                                                                                                                                     |
| `notification_already_paid`          | The shopper reported that they already paid directly to the merchant, instead of paying via Klarna.                                                                                       |
| `notification_incorrect_invoice`     | The shopper reported that the invoice is incorrect.                                                                                                                                       |
| `notification_pandemic_impact`       | The shopper reported that the order was canceled due to COVID-19 circumstances.                                                                                                           |
| `notification_unauthorized_purchase` | The shopper reported that the order was placed by someone with unauthorized access to their account.                                                                                      |
| `notification_high_risk_order`       | Klarna suspects this to be fraudulent order, where a shopper's personal details, for example, their payment information, were wrongfully used.                                            |

## Request for Information

If the dispute is not resolved in time, Klarna escalates the dispute to a Request for Information (RFI). Unlike other payment methods, uploading the requested information is mandatory with Klarna.

You need to respond to an Request for Information within:

* **96 hours** for the dispute reason code `high_risk_order`.
* **7 days** for the dispute reason code `unauthorized_purchase`.
* **14 days** for other dispute reason codes.

If you do not respond to the Request for Information in time, a chargeback can take place where money will be deducted from the next payable batch.

To inform you of a Request for Information:

* We send you a [Request for Information dispute webhook](/risk-management/disputes-api/dispute-notifications).

* In your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/) we show the complaint under **Risk & disputes** > **Disputes** > **Requests for Information**.

### Respond to an RFI

If you do not agree with the RFI dispute, you can try to defend it. Note that you have only **one attempt** to submit all the necessary information. After that, it is no longer possible to make any changes to the documents. To defend the dispute, you need to:

1. Provide your defense documents. For each case, there is a template for you to fill in:

   * [Return](/risk-management/chargeback-guidelines/klarna-chargebacks/Template_Return.docx)
   * [Goods not received](/risk-management/chargeback-guidelines/klarna-chargebacks/Template_Goods_not_received.docx)
   * [Faulty goods](/risk-management/chargeback-guidelines/klarna-chargebacks/Template_Faulty_goods.docx)
   * [Already paid](/risk-management/chargeback-guidelines/klarna-chargebacks/Template_Already_paid.docx)
   * [Incorrect invoice](/risk-management/chargeback-guidelines/klarna-chargebacks/Template_Incorrect_invoice.docx)
   * [Impacted by COVID-19](/risk-management/chargeback-guidelines/klarna-chargebacks/Template_Impacted_by_covid19.docx)
   * [Unauthorized purchase](/risk-management/chargeback-guidelines/klarna-chargebacks/Template_Unauthorized_purchase.docx)
   * [High-risk order](/risk-management/chargeback-guidelines/klarna-chargebacks/Template_High-risk_orders.docx)

2. After filling in the template, save the document as a PDF file.

3. Submit the PDF file using our [Disputes API](/risk-management/disputes-api#supply-dispute-defense-documents) or the [Customer Area](/risk-management/manage-disputes#defending-disputes).

## Information Supplied

Adyen received your defense documents and forwarded them to the scheme. It is no longer possible to change these documents. The **InformationSupplied** journal is booked and we send you an [**INFORMATION\_SUPPLIED** ](/risk-management/disputes-api/dispute-notifications/#information_supplied)webhook.

Based on the information you provided, Klarna assesses the dispute and makes a decision:

* If Klarna accepts your defense, no further journals are booked, and this is the final status.
* If Klarna rejects your defense, you will receive a [chargeback](#chargeback).

## 1st Chargeback

Klarna resolves the dispute in the shopper's favor, and initiates a chargeback. The **Chargeback** journal is booked, your account is debited, and you receive a [CHARGEBACK](/risk-management/disputes-api/dispute-notifications#chargeback) webhook.\
You cannot defend this chargeback. This is the final status, and the dispute is lost.

## Managing chargebacks

You can manage the Klarna chargeback flow using the [Disputes API](#klarna-dispute-api) or your [Customer Area](#klarna-dispute-ca).

The [Klarna documentation](https://docs.klarna.com/payments/after-payments/disputes/disputes-management-v3/dispute-management-overview/) has more information about Klarna disputes.

**Discontinued email notification**

From May 1, 2023, you no longer receive an email about stop requests by Klarna. Instead, these are reported as a [Request for Information (RFI)](#dispute-codes) with the reason code `high_risk_order`. Resolve these RFIs through the [Disputes API](/risk-management/disputes-api) or your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/) using the correct [defense template](#respond-to-dispute).

### Manage chargebacks using the Disputes API

If you have integrated our [Disputes API](/risk-management/disputes-api), you receive a [dispute webhook](/risk-management/disputes-api/dispute-notifications) when a shopper starts a dispute process with their issuer. Using the API, you can manage the Klarna chargeback flow for the disputed payment.

### Manage chargebacks in the Customer Area

In your Customer Area, you can [view disputes](/risk-management/manage-disputes#view-disputes), and manage the four stages of a Klarna chargeback flow for a disputed payment.
