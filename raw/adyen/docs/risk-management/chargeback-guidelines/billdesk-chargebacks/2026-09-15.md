# India domestic chargebacks with BillDesk

When you [process payments in India](/online-payments/payments-india-compliance-guide/), Adyen partners with BillDesk to offer credit and debit card payments, and payments through wallets, unified payments interface (UPI), and online banking.

If a payment is disputed, you can manage disputes using the [Customer Area](#billdesk-dispute-ca) or the [Disputes API](#billdesk-dispute-api) for the following transaction types:

* [Cards](/payment-methods/cards/) - Visa, Mastercard, Diners, American Express, and RuPay.
* [Unified payments interface (UPI)](/payment-methods/upi/) - an instant real-time payment system that offers interbank transactions to shoppers.

You cannot manage disputes for [online banking India](/payment-methods/online-banking-india/) and [wallets India](/payment-methods/wallets-india/) (such as Amazon Pay) using the Customer Area or the Disputes API. For those disputes, the Adyen [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other) will help you through email.

The chargeback flow consists of the following steps:

1. [Notification of Chargeback](#notification-of-chargeback)
2. [Information Supplied](#information-supplied)
3. [1st Chargeback](#chargeback)
4. [Pre-arbitration](#pre-arbitration)

## BillDesk flow

## Notification of Chargeback

BillDesk initiates a Notification of Chargeback (NoC). The **NotificationOfChargeback** journal is booked. At this point, funds are not yet deducted from your account.

We assign the dispute to you for review in your [live India Customer Area](https://ca-live-in.adyen.com/) > **Risk & disputes** > **Disputes** or send you a [dispute webhook](/risk-management/disputes-api/dispute-notifications).

You have 7 days to respond to a chargeback notification.

The [reason code](/risk-management/understanding-disputes/dispute-reason-codes?tab=chargeback_1#billdesk) specifies why the chargeback was initiated.

After your review, you can decide to either:

* Accept the chargeback in your [live India Customer Area](https://ca-live-in.adyen.com/). The dispute is lost, and you can no longer defend it.
* Defend the dispute. You need to upload the relevant defense documents in your [live India Customer Area](https://ca-live-in.adyen.com/) or using the [Disputes API](/risk-management/disputes-api).

## Information Supplied

Adyen received your defense documents and forwarded them to the scheme. It is no longer possible to change these documents. The **InformationSupplied** journal is booked and we send you an [**INFORMATION\_SUPPLIED** ](/risk-management/disputes-api/dispute-notifications/#information_supplied)webhook.

Based on the information you provided, BillDesk assesses the dispute and makes a decision:

* If BillDesk accepts your defense, no further journals are booked, and this is the final status.
* If BillDesk rejects your defense, you will receive a [chargeback](#chargeback).\
  BillDesk imposes a 60-day period to determine the final status of a dispute. If the **Chargeback** journal has not been booked after 60 days, you can consider the dispute as won.

## 1st Chargeback

If the shopper or the issuing bank does not accept your supporting document, or you failed to provide the defense documents in time, you will receive a chargeback. The **Chargeback** journal is booked, you receive a [CHARGEBACK](/risk-management/disputes-api/dispute-notifications#chargeback) webhook, and your account is debited for the chargeback amount.

You cannot defend this chargeback. This is the final status and the dispute is lost.

## Pre-arbitration

If the issuer does not find the defense compelling enough, they can initiate pre-arbitration even after you win the chargeback and the chargeback did not appear on Adyen's settlement report for more than 60 days.

The disputes that go to pre-arbitration cannot be managed through the Disputes API or the Customer Area. The issuer will send an email to the Adyen [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other) highlighting the dispute that went to pre-arbitration. The Adyen [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other) will contact you for the next steps.

## Managing chargebacks

You can manage the BillDesk chargeback flow using your [Customer Area](#billdesk-dispute-ca) or the [Disputes API](#billdesk-dispute-api).

### Manage chargebacks in the Customer Area

In your [live India Customer Area](https://ca-live-in.adyen.com/), you can [view disputes](/risk-management/manage-disputes#view-disputes), and manage the four stages of a BillDesk chargeback flow for a disputed payment.

### Manage chargebacks using the Disputes API

If you have integrated our [Disputes API](/risk-management/disputes-api), you will receive a [dispute webhook](/risk-management/disputes-api/dispute-notifications) when a shopper starts a dispute process with their issuer. Using the API, you can manage the BillDesk chargeback flow for the disputed payment.

If you want to know more about BillDesk, you can read it [here](https://docs.billdesk.io/).
