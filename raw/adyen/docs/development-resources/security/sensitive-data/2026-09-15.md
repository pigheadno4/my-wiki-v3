# Protecting sensitive data

Protect sensitive information by implementing encryption good practices.

Adyen implements many security practices to protect the confidentiality and integrity of merchant data and systems.

However, in your Adyen integration, you too need to protect sensitive information, by implementing encryption good practices.

* **Encryption in transit**: implement TLS/SSL to secure data during transmission.

* **PGP encryption**: learn how to generate and upload a PGP key for the exchange of sensitive card data with Adyen
