# 3D Secure 2 redirect authentication

Learn how to support 3D Secure with a redirect page.

##### For a better shopper experience

Instead of redirecting shoppers, perform [native 3D Secure 2 authentication](/online-payments/3d-secure/native-3ds2) in your website or mobile app.

To complete a card payment with 3D Secure 2 authentication, the shopper needs to verify their identity using a passive, biometric, or two-factor authentication method.

In this integration, we provide a redirect URL where the shopper can complete authentication.

## Which platform are you integrating?

If you have an API only integration, use our Web, iOS, or Android Component to support 3D Secure.
