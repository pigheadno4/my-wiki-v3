# Payment methods

Learn how which payment methods we support for your integration.

Adyen's platform supports a wide range of global and local card payment methods with a single integration. Before you start processing payments for your users, you must [configure the payment methods](/marketplaces/payment-methods/add-payment-methods) they can accept in your Customer Area.
