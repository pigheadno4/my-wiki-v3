# Before payment

Overview of components used before payment.

Before you can accept payments on behalf of your users, you need to onboard them onto your marketplace and verify their identity and business information. To facilitate this process, you can use the Onboarding components. These components enable users to enter and submit their information to Adyen within your platform's UI.

[![individual legal entity](/user/pages/docs/04.marketplaces/04.components-overview/00.before-payment/individual-legal-entity-image.png)](/marketplaces/components-overview/before-payment/individual-legal-entity-component/)

#### [Individual Legal Entity](/marketplaces/components-overview/before-payment/individual-legal-entity-component/)

[Submit personal details and identity documents for verification. View status and update details as needed.](/marketplaces/components-overview/before-payment/individual-legal-entity-component/)

[![terms of service](/user/pages/docs/04.marketplaces/04.components-overview/00.before-payment/tos-image.png)](/marketplaces/components-overview/before-payment/terms-of-service-component/)

#### [Terms of Service](/marketplaces/components-overview/before-payment/terms-of-service-component/)

[View and accept required Terms of Service documents. Monitor status and download signed copies.](/marketplaces/components-overview/before-payment/terms-of-service-component/)

[![transfer instrument](/user/pages/docs/04.marketplaces/04.components-overview/00.before-payment/transfer-instrument-image.png)](/marketplaces/components-overview/before-payment/transfer-instrument-component/)

#### [Transfer Instrument](/marketplaces/components-overview/before-payment/transfer-instrument-component/)

[Add, update, or delete bank accounts. Verify via instant verification or document upload, and view verification status.](/marketplaces/components-overview/before-payment/transfer-instrument-component/)
