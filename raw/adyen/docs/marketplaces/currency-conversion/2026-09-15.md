# Currency conversion

Learn how to convert the currency of a transaction.

**Limited availability**\
Currency conversion in currently in pilot phase. Some of the processes and documentation may change as the feature evolves. If you are interested in piloting currency conversion or have any feedback, reach out to your Adyen contact.

***

Because you use Adyen's payment processing platform, you can process transactions on behalf of your users in many different currencies. When you accept payments in many different currencies, your users may not always use the same currency as their customers.

For each transaction you process, you can modify the API requests so that the transaction is accepted in one currency and settled in another. This way, you can convert the currency of the transaction and ensure that both your user and their customers are debited/credited in their preferred currencies.
