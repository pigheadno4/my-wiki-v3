# Industry codes list

Select the industry code that most accurately represents the business activity of your user.

Select the Industry Code (IC) that best represents your user's business activity. We map industry codes to Merchant Category Codes (MCCs) and adapt them to our internal regulations. You must select accurately: some MCCs correspond to multiple ICs.

Reach out to your Adyen contact or our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other) if you:

* Need approval for an industry code before onboarding users.
* Want to offer business accounts to users in *any new industries* that were not part of the previous approval process.

We only accept the following values for industry codes.
