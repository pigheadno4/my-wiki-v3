# Verify balances

Spot-check account balance positions across your balance platform.

There can be various reasons to keep track of the account balances in your balance platform. For example, to make an [on-demand payout](/marketplaces/custom-payouts/on-demand-payouts) to a user, you need to know how much cash is available on the balance account of that user.

To check the balance positions on the balance accounts of your users, you can:

* [Use the Balance Platform Balance report](#use-balance-report) to view daily balances.\
  The Balance report shows the opening and closing balances in your balance platform **on a given day** for all account holders, balance accounts (including your liable account), and balance account currencies.
* [Use the Balance Platform Accounting Report](#use-accounting-report) to calculate balances.\
  When using the Accounting report, an important concept is [value-date accounting](/marketplaces/reports-and-fees/balance-platform-accounting-report#value-date-accounting).

## View balances

To view the daily balances on the accounts in your balance platform:

1. [Get the Balance Platform Balance Report](/marketplaces/prepare-reports/generate-download-reports) for a specific date.
2. Filter the report to get the information you are looking for.

## Calculate balances

To calculate the balances on the accounts in your balance platform:

1. [Get the Balance Platform Accounting Report](/marketplaces/prepare-reports/generate-download-reports).
2. Filter the report on the balance account you are interested in.
3. Add up the amounts in the **Balance (PC)** column.

## Negative balances

Accounts in your balance platform can have a negative balance as a result of refunds or chargebacks. Adyen allows negative balances for up to 30 days. At the end of the month, if an account has had a negative balance for more than 30 days, Adyen compensates by debiting the amount from your balance platform's liable account. If you'd rather have account holders cover their negative balances instead, you can provide them the option to [top up their balance accounts](/marketplaces/top-up-balance-account).

When Adyen compensates a negative balance, this will be reflected in the Balance Platform Accounting Report in an entry with a description or reference of *Negative Balance compensation for {AccountHolderId}*. You will also receive the following [transfer webhooks](/marketplaces/internal-fund-transfers/internal-transfer-webhooks/):

* [balancePlatform.transfer.created](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/transfer-webhooks/latest/post/balancePlatform.transfer.created)
* [balancePlatform.transfer.updated](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/transfer-webhooks/latest/post/balancePlatform.transfer.updated)

Because this is an internal transfer between balance accounts in your balance platform, the entry in the Balance Platform Accounting Report and the transfer webhooks you receive will have category **internal** and type **internalTransfer**.

Note that negative balances only occur as a result of refunds or chargebacks. An account cannot have a negative balance due to paying out or transferring a balance. You can only pay out or transfer the available balance.

### Receive advance notice

If you want Adyen to inform your system when an account on your balance platform has a negative balance for over 20 days, you can configure a [Negative balance compensation warning webhook](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/negative-balance-compensation-warning-webhooks/latest/overview). This webhook provides advance notice of a scheduled internal transfer from your liable account to the balance account whose balance is negative.

This example of a [balancePlatform.negativeBalanceCompensationWarning.scheduled](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/negative-balance-compensation-warning-webhooks/latest/post/balancePlatform.negativeBalanceCompensationWarning.scheduled) webhook shows the following:

* There is a negative balance for the balance account with an `id` of **BA00000000000000000001**.
* That balance account has held a negative balance of EUR 14050.50 since 2024-10-19T00:33:13+02:00.
* That balance account belongs to the account holder with an `id` of **AH00000000000000000001**.
* The negative balance will be compensated through an internal transfer from the liable balance account with an `id` of **BA11111111111111111111** to the balance account with an `id` of **BA00000000000000000001**.
  * This compensation will happen if the negative balance amount has not been compensated by 2024-12-01T01:00:00+01:00 (the `scheduledCompensationAt` timestamp).

**Negative balance compensation scheduled warning webhook**

```json
{
  "data": {
    "balancePlatform": "YOUR_BALANCE_PLATFORM",
    "creationDate": "2024-10-22T00:00:00+02:00",
    "id": "BA00000000000000000001",
    "accountHolder": {
      "description": "Description for the account holder.",
      "reference": "YOUR_REFERENCE",
      "id": "AH00000000000000000001"
    },
    "amount": {
      "currency": "EUR",
      "value": -1405050
    },
    "liableBalanceAccountId": "BA11111111111111111111",
    "negativeBalanceSince": "2024-10-19T00:33:13+02:00",
    "scheduledCompensationAt": "2024-12-01T01:00:00+01:00"
  },
  "environment": "test",
  "type": "balancePlatform.negativeBalanceCompensationWarning.scheduled"
}
```

The default configuration for the number of days when a balance account can have a negative balance before you receive this webhook is 20 days. If you want to change that number to a different value, contact the Adyen [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other).

### Avoid internal transfers from your liable account

You can avoid an automatic internal transfer from your liable account to the negative balance account by doing one of the following:

* When you receive this webhook, you can proactively reach out to the account holder to [request an on-demand transfer of funds](/marketplaces/top-up-balance-account/on-demand-top-ups) to their balance account from their registered transfer bank account before the scheduled compensation date.
* Create automatic, [scheduled sweeps](/marketplaces/top-up-balance-account/scheduled-top-ups) for your account holders to top-up their balance account from their registered transfer bank account when their balance reaches a specific amount.
