# Industries and markets

See what features and recommendations Adyen has to offer for your industry or market.

![](/user/themes/adyen/images/illustrations/warning.svg) **Work in progress**

This is a new section in our documentation. We will add more topics over time.

***

In this section of our documentation we provide important overviews and recommendations on specific industries and markets. In this context, an "industry" is a group of companies within a sector of commerce that produce similar goods or services. And a "market" is a system that enables buyers and sellers to exchange goods and services. A market can be physical or online, direct or indirect, and can be defined by geographic boundaries.

Our recommendations will guide you in deciding what minimally required and what additional features you need to build into your Adyen integration. We also point you to the relevant documentation pages that explain how to build your integration. This integrated approach to the content is especially useful when you want to support multiple sales channels.
