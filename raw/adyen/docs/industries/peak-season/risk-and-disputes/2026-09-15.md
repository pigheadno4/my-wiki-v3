# Risk and disputes

Find out how to optimize your risk and dispute management setup for peak season.

Transaction volume, frequency, and value usually increase during peak season. Because there are generally more transactions, the number of chargebacks and fraudulent transactions increases as well. At the same time, legitimate shoppers might purchase more products, more often, and the average transaction value rises. Patterns that seem suspicious during other periods of the year can be completely legitimate during peak season.

To manage these and other risks, we recommend that you use the latest risk engine, Protect, in combination with Adyen Uplift. By using this combination, you benefit from advanced machine learning capabilities that help legitimate shoppers complete their purchase. This lets you increase conversion while keeping fraud under control.

## General recommendations

#### Send in high-quality data in payment requests

* Make sure that you send in [high-quality data](/risk-management/configure-your-risk-profile/risk-field-reference) to ensure the best performance, and that [data collection to detect fraud](/risk-management/fraud-data-collection) is enabled.

#### Use Protect and enable premium risk features

* We recommend that you use Protect, our latest risk engine, instead of RevenueProtect. [Find out which risk engine you use](/risk-management#get-started).
* [Enable premium risk features](/risk-management/configure-risk-settings) for more advanced AI-powered functionality such as [risk-based authentication](/risk-management/dynamic-3d-secure#risk-based-authentication), the [machine learning fraud risk rule](/risk-management/configure-your-risk-profile/machine-learning-rules), [custom rules](/risk-management/configure-your-risk-profile/custom-rules), and [risk profile analytics](/risk-management/configure-your-risk-profile#view-risk-profile-analytics). Premium features have to be configured on your account and involve additional risk fees.

#### Review Customer Area roles for your team

* Make sure that your team is ready to manage and monitor risk and disputes by checking that they have the required [Customer Area user roles](/account/user-roles#risk).

## Review risk profiles and rules

#### Protect

* Check that all merchant accounts [have been assigned](/risk-management/create-a-risk-profile/) to a Protect risk profile.
* If you have enabled [post-authorization rules](/risk-management/configure-your-risk-profile/post-auth-rules) for AVS or CVC, consider disabling these because there is a risk of shopper input errors for both the address details and the CVC field. Enabling these rules may result in a higher number of declined transactions because of mismatches. Instead of using post-authorization rules, we recommend that you use the Adyen Uplift [Optimize settings](/uplift#uplift-optimize). In the smart payment messaging section, we recommend to select **Allow security field optimizations on low risk transactions only**.
* Protect premium: review Adyen Uplift recommendations to see if there are underperforming [custom risk rules](/risk-management/configure-your-risk-profile/custom-rules) that you should address.

#### RevenueProtect

* To avoid overblocking, consider temporarily lowering the risk score for or creating a peak season risk profile with more lenient velocity, consistency and ShopperDNA [risk rules](/risk-management/configure-manual-risk/standard-risk-rules).

## Review 3D Secure settings

* Check your [default](/risk-management/dynamic-3d-secure/#default-rules) 3D Secure rule setting, and make sure that any [dynamic](/risk-management/dynamic-3d-secure/#configuring-dynamic-3d-secure-rules) 3D Secure rules are up-to-date.
* As an extra safeguard to block transactions that do not have a liability shift, set up and enable the [post-authorization rule](/risk-management/configure-your-risk-profile/post-auth-rules#configure-post-auth) **Liability shift status blocked**.
* Protect premium: if you have custom **Check for 3DS** rules that trigger dynamic 3D Secure rules, make sure that they are up-to-date.

## Monitor performance

#### Protect and Adyen Uplift

* Monitor for any anomalies during peak season. Use the [Risk and dispute management](/risk-management/dispute-and-fraud-monitoring) dashboards to analyze overall dispute and fraud rates performance, or dive deeper into risk performance by filtering views.
* In the [Adyen Uplift overview](/uplift/#uplift-dashboards), look at fraud rates to evaluate how your risk setup is doing.
* Protect premium: View [risk profile analytics](/risk-management/configure-your-risk-profile#view-risk-profile-analytics) to see how specific risk profiles perform.

#### RevenueProtect

* Use the [Risk and dispute management](/risk-management/dispute-and-fraud-monitoring) dashboards to monitor for any anomalies during peak season.
* If you are experiencing excessive fraud refusals, investigate which risk rules are causing these refusals and adjust the rules if needed.

## Avoid scripted attacks

Several features help you block fraudulent transactions and mitigate the effect of scripted attacks.

For example, [data collection](/risk-management/fraud-data-collection) helps identify fraudulent activity on your website. This data is one of the sources for the [machine learning bot attack risk rule](/risk-management/configure-your-risk-profile/machine-learning-rules/) in Protect.

We recommend that you [monitor risk performance](#peak-risk-monitor) to detect signs of a fraud attack. For example, look for activities such as an increase in issuer refusal rates, or payment attempts with zero-value or very small amounts. It is important to confirm that these activities are indeed fraudulent before you take further action. When you confirm that the activity is suspicious, you can:

* Block BINs that are used in the attack by adding them to a [risk list](/risk-management/configure-your-risk-profile/risk-lists).
* Temporarily add a [Dynamic 3D Secure rule](/risk-management/dynamic-3d-secure/#configuring-dynamic-3d-secure-rules) to always request a challenge.
* Protect premium: add [custom rules](/risk-management/configure-your-risk-profile/custom-rules/) to address patterns that you have identified.

## Handle disputes

Make sure that your team is ready to handle and monitor disputes.

* Review the [dispute process and flow](/risk-management/understanding-disputes/dispute-process-and-flow).
* Monitor incoming disputes. In your Customer area, in the [Notification center](/account/notification-center#configure-notification-settings) enable the **Chargeback notices** for incoming disputes, and regularly check the [Risk and dispute management](#peak-risk-monitor) dashboards.
* If a transaction seems fraudulent, block shipping and refund the transaction to avoid a chargeback and any fees related to high chargeback volumes.
