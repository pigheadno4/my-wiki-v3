# Compliant payments in Japan

Information you need to be aware of when operating in Japan.

To process domestic payments in Japan, you must comply with regulations in Japan. There are multiple regulations that you must take into account:

* Specified Commercial Transactions Act (SCTA)
* Payment Services Act (PSA)
* Credit Card Security Guidelines
