# Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC)

Use Dynamic Currency Conversion to let your users' customers pay in their own currency.

**Limited availability**\
Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) for platforms is currently in pilot phase. Some of the processes and documentation may change as the feature evolves. If you are interested in piloting DCC or have any feedback, reach out to your Adyen contact.

***

Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) allows you to convert the currency of your users' transactions at the point of sale. DCC gives your users' international customers the option to either pay in the local currency or their home currency. Before payment, the terminal shows the customer the exchange rate and markup, so they can make an informed decision.

When you offer DCC to your users' customers, you must comply with [regulations](#complying-with-regulations), such as what is shown on receipts.

## How it works

To use DCC on your platform:

1. You enable DCC [in the Customer Area](?tab=customer-area_1#enable-dcc-for-your-platform) or [using API calls](?tab=api-calls_2#enable-dcc-for-your-platform).

2. You set up how to split the DCC markup amount between your platform and your user. You can either:

   * [Configure a split profile](/platforms/automatic-split-configuration/create-split-configuration?tab=api_2#book-dcc-markups) with a rule to handle the DCC markup amount.
   * [Provide split instructions in each payment request](#make-a-payment-with-dcc). Instructions in your payment request override any instructions configured in the split configuration profile.

3. Your user initiates a payment like they usually do. Optionally, you can include [split instructions](#make-a-payment-with-dcc) for the DCC markup in your payment request. The customer presents their card to the payment terminal.

4. The terminal recognizes the currency that is used for billing the customer when they use their card.

5. If the customer's billing currency differs from the local currency, the terminal prompts the customer to choose between paying in the local currency and their own billing currency.

   ![Payment terminal showing a DCC currency selection screen with USD option](/images/3/a/d/4/d/3ad4d2288e74db8061cbee7c83aec96c387e0f7a-screen-endcc-portrait-usd.png)

6. If the customer accepts DCC by choosing their own billing currency, foreign-exchange conversion is applied to the transaction. The Adyen-generated receipt includes information about the conversion. If the customer rejects DCC by choosing the local currency, the transaction continues in the local currency.

## Complying with regulations

To avoid fines and the loss of DCC privileges, it is important to comply with the disclosure and receipt requirements of the card schemes when choosing to offer DCC to your users’ customers.

According to card scheme regulations, the customer must be given the choice to accept DCC, or decline it and pay in the local currency. The customer must be made aware of the cost of the currency conversion, and it is not permitted to force a choice on the cardholder.

Refer to [Dynamic Currency Conversion - Rules & Regulations](https://help.adyen.com/knowledge/in-person-payments/terminal-features/dynamic-currency-conversion-dcc-rules-regulations) for detailed guidance on card scheme requirements.

### Disclosure and receipt requirements

The Adyen DCC feature allows you to offer customers the choice to accept or decline DCC in a way that is fully compliant with card scheme regulations, with full disclosure of the exchange rate and markups. The customer can review DCC charges both on the payment terminal and on the Adyen-generated receipt.

To remain compliant with card scheme regulations if you use DCC with pre-authorization and authorization adjustment:

2. Add the details from the [CAPTURE webhook](/point-of-sale/pre-authorisation#capture-authorization) to the receipt or invoice.

## DCC markup

When a customer selects DCC at the payment terminal, they pay the current exchange rate plus an additional markup on the transaction. Adyen uses a revenue share model, where the revenue from the markup can be split between your platform, your user, and Adyen.

After you deduct the portion of the markup that Adyen keeps, you can choose if and how to split the remaining markup amount between your platform and your user. For every payment that uses DCC, you must [specify the percentage of the remaining markup that must be booked to your user's balance account](#make-a-payment-with-dcc). Adyen then books the rest of the markup amount to your liable balance account.

When Adyen books a DCC markup amount to a balance account, we always use the split type **DCCMarkup**. All funds booked under this split type have the default settlement delay of the balance account.

## Enable DCC for your platform

You can enable DCC in the Customer Area or by using API calls. Select the tab below for your preferred method.

1. Log in to your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/).

2. Select the merchant account, store, or terminal for which you want to enable DCC.

3. Go to **Devices** > **Device settings** > **Payment features**.

4. Select **Dynamic currency conversion (DCC)** and turn on the **Enable DCC** toggle.

5. Select **Save**.

6. Go to **Settings**, and under **Product settings** for **Payments**, select **Payment methods**. Check if the **Configured currencies** for Visa, MasterCard, and Maestro are set to **ANY**. The currency of payment methods is set by default and depends on the specific payment method type. If your currency configuration is different and you need to change it, contact our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other).

7. Contact our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other) to:

   * Enable DCC reporting for your merchant account(s). This ensures [financial reports](#reports) and [dashboards](#reports) include DCC details.
   * (Optional) Enable DCC for pre-authorization and authorization adjustment.

## Make a payment with DCC

To make a payment request with DCC and split the DCC markup amount between your platform and your user:

1. [Gather the split instructions for the payment](/platforms/in-person-payments/split-transactions/split-payments-at-authorization#gather-data). Always provide the split instructions in the **settlement currency** (the currency your user receives).

2. Gather the split instructions for the DCC markup:

   These instructions specify how to split the DCC markup amount between your platform and your user. You must gather the following data:

   | Field                                      | Required | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
   | ------------------------------------------ | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | `platformDccLogic.targetAccount`           |          | The account to which your user's share of the DCC markup must be booked. Your platform's share of the DCC markup is always booked to your liable balance account. By default, we book the full markup amount, after deducting Adyen's share, to your liable balance account. |
   | `platformDccLogic.accountHolderPercentage` |          | The percentage of the DCC markup that must be booked to your user, in basis points. By default, we book the full markup amount, after deducting Adyen's share, to your platform.                                                                                             |

3. Format the data you gathered in step 1 and 2, so that you can pass it in the `SaleToAcquirerData` field of your request. There are two ways to do this:

   * **Option 1**: as a string of form-encoded key-value pairs (using & as a separator).
   * **Option 2**: as a JSON object converted to a Base64-encoded string.

   The format you use in your request is the format that will be returned in the `AdditionalResponse`. To always receive the `AdditionalResponse` in a specific format, contact our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other).

   Select the tab for the option you want to use.

   ### Tab: Key-value pairs

   1. Turn the split instructions you gathered into key-value pairs.

      **Split instructions as key-value pairs**

      ```raw

      split.nrOfItems=3
      split.totalAmount=62000
      split.currencyCode=USD
      split.item1.amount=60000
      split.item1.type=BalanceAccount
      split.item1.account=BA00000000000000000000001
      split.item1.reference=Your reference for the sale amount
      split.item1.description=Your description for the sale amount
      split.item2.amount=2000
      split.item2.type=Commission
      split.item2.reference=Your reference for your commission
      split.item2.description=Your description for your commission
      split.item3.type=PaymentFee
      split.item3.account=BA00000000000000000000001
      split.item3.reference=Your reference for the payment fee
      split.item3.description=Your description for the payment fee
      platformDccLogic.targetAccount=BA00000000000000000000001
      platformDccLogic.accountHolderPercentage=3000
      ```

   2. Concatenate the key-value pairs into a string by separating them with ampersands (**&**). You will pass the resulting string in `SaleData.SaleToAcquirerData`.

      **Concatenated key-value pairs**

## Reconciling payments

Enabling DCC can have consequences for your (automated) reconciliation. For more information, contact your Account Manager or our [Support Team](https://ca-test.adyen.com/ca/ca/contactUs/support.shtml?form=other).

Funds are settled in your settlement currency. The amount that you start the transaction with (before DCC) will be paid to your user. For example, you start a transaction for USD 100, and the customer accepts DCC for EUR 90. Adyen settles USD 100 to your user's balance account. The DCC markup, after deducting Adyen's share, is then split between your user's balance account and your liable account based on the instructions you provided in your payment request.

The markup is booked to your liable account and your user's balance account under the split type **DCCMarkup**. Currently, this split type is only visible on the reports and in the webhooks. It is currently unavailable in the **Transfers** page of your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/).

### Financial reports and overviews

When you have enabled DCC and DCC reporting, you can find information about DCC transactions in the following financial reports and overviews in your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/):

#### Reports

* The [Received payment details report](/reporting/received-payment-details-report) shows whether DCC is 'Accepted' or 'Declined'.
* The [Settlement details report](/reporting/settlement-reconciliation/transaction-level/settlement-details-report) includes the markup on the DCC transaction as a credit. To see the markup, you must first manually [configure the **DCC Markup** column](/reporting/settlement-reconciliation/transaction-level/settlement-details-report/#configure-report-columns).
* The [Aggregate Settlement details report](/reporting/settlement-reconciliation/batch-level/aggregate-settlement-details-report) includes all the markups on the DCC transaction as a credit on the store or the merchant account level. To see the markup, you must first manually [configure the **DCC Markup** column](/reporting/settlement-reconciliation/batch-level/aggregate-settlement-details-report/#configure-report-columns).
* The [Balance Platform Account Report](/platforms/reports-and-fees/balance-platform-accounting-report) shows your platform's share of the DCC markup, booked to your liable account and your user's balance account under the split type **DCCMarkup**.

#### Overviews

* The Payments list, available under **Transactions** > **Payments**.\
  To see whether DCC was applied to a transaction, select the columns **DCC offered** and **DCC accepted**.
* The **DCC** pane on the Payment Details page, available when you open a payment in the Payments list: the pane shows whether DCC was offered/accepted, the base and transaction currency, and the applied exchange rate.
* The [DCC Overview dashboard](/point-of-sale/currency-conversion/download-and-use-the-dcc-overview-file/), available under **Reports** > **Point of sale** > **Dashboards** > **Dynamic currency conversion**. This shows DCC usage and acceptance across different geographies and retail locations, earnings from DCC, and the [reasons why DCC was not offered](/point-of-sale/currency-conversion/download-and-use-the-dcc-overview-file#dcc-not-offered). The DCC dashboard is intended for management or operations teams.
