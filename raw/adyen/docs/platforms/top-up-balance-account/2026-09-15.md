# Top up balance accounts

Set up a pull sweep to trigger a direct debit from a verified transfer instrument.

The funds in your users' balance accounts usually come from payments from their customers. However, you can also increase the balance in these accounts by allowing your users to top up their balance accounts. A top-up refers to an incoming transfer of funds from an external source that increases the balance in their balance account.

If needed, you can also top up your liable balance account.

## Use cases

It is useful to top up funds in balance accounts for the following reasons:

* To cover negative balances resulting from refunds or chargebacks.
* To ensure that your user's balance account has sufficient funds, for example to make a transfer.

## How it works

You can top up balance accounts in two ways:

* [On a schedule](/platforms/top-up-balance-account/scheduled-top-ups): you set up a schedule that automatically triggers the top-up based on timing and amount.
* [On demand](/platforms/top-up-balance-account/on-demand-top-ups): you manually top up your users' balance accounts when needed, in addition to (or instead of) the scheduled top-ups.

After you initiate a top-up, either on a schedule or on demand, Adyen triggers an incoming external transfer that pulls funds into your balance account from the source specified in your top-up request.
