# Onboarding guidelines

Learn about best practices for onboarding and see guidance for selected countries/regions

This page is in development and additional countries/regions will be added in the future.

This page provides an overview of general best practices for onboarding your users. We also list some more detailed guidance for selected countries/regions. For example, information about types of local documents and how to troubleshoot common verification issues.

For more detailed information about document formats, see [requirements for document uploads](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements).

## Suggested best practices for onboarding

As required by payment industry regulations, Adyen must verify the users in your platform before you can process payments, pay out their funds, and offer financial products to them. These verification checks are also called *Know Your Customer* (KYC) checks. During this process, Adyen verifies that your users provide accurate information about their businesses. If the submitted information is incomplete or incorrect, this can affect the onboarding process and result in delays due to requests for supporting documentation. Ensure that the information submitted during onboarding is correct.

### Identity verification

Adyen tries to verify the identity of the user using their personal details. In case automatic verification fails, Adyen may request a [photo ID](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#photo-id-requirements) to complete the verification process.

If a user’s ID document includes a married name, they may enter either one or both of their last names during onboarding. However, note that the word that references to the married name, for example **Epouse** or **Geb**, should not be included in the input data.

If a user onboards with a married name that is not yet reflected on their [photo ID](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#photo-id-requirements), they may provide a marriage certificate as a supporting document through the Legal Entity Management API. However, note that the marriage certificate can only be accepted if it was issued after their ID document.

Users may only submit shortened names or initials if they appear on their Identity document.

### Residency verification

Adyen tries to use the submitted information to automatically verify your user's address. In case automatic verification fails, Adyen may request a [proof of residential address](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#address-proof). For example, if the user provides an identity document from France during onboarding, but currently lives in Portugal.

### Organization and sole proprietorship verification

Adyen tries to use the legal name and registration number of the organization to automatically verify your user. In case automatic verification fails, Adyen may request a [registration document](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#registration-document).

The legal name of the organization must be entered exactly as it appears on the registration document or online registry. For example, the trading name cannot be submitted as the legal name.

Adyen tries to use both the submitted legal name and registration number of the sole proprietorship to automatically verify the sole proprietor's details. In case automatic verification fails, Adyen may request a [constitutional document](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#constitutional-doc).

For further information regarding company types, refer to [this page](/get-started-with-adyen/application-requirements#company-types).

### Bank verification

If the automatic verification of a bank account fails, Adyen may ask for a [bank document](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#bank-statement-requirements) to verify your user’s payout details.

Organizations can sign up with a bank account under their trading name, but both the correct trading name and legal name must be submitted by the user.

Sole proprietorships can sign up with either the individual bank account of the sole proprietorship owner or a bank account under their trading name. If the bank account is under their trading name, it cannot include a legal form indicating company ownership. The correct trading name must be submitted by the user when onboarding.

## Australia

### Tab: Individuals

Adyen tries to verify the identity of the user using their personal details. In case automatic verification fails, Adyen may request a [photo ID](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#photo-id-requirements) to complete the verification process.

Users that onboard using Hosted onboarding can choose to verify their identity using instant ID verification. Documents can also be uploaded manually in the hosted onboarding page or using the Legal Entity Management API.

#### Australian identity documents

Proof of age cards issued by Australian states that meet the [identity document requirements](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#photo-id-requirements) can be used for identity verification. All Australian driver’s license cards contain both a license number and a card number. The license number does not change, while the card number changes every time it is renewed.

#### Bank verification

Australian users are required to upload a bank document for review. Refer [here](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#bank-statement-requirements) for a full list of requirements for bank documents.

### Tab: Organizations

The legal name of the company must be entered exactly as it appears on the official registration document or online registry. Users frequently make the mistake of entering the trading name as the registered legal name. Ensure that all information provided by the user is accurate. For further information on company documentation requirements, refer to this [page](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#registration-document).

#### Examples of company documents in Australia

| Company type             | Document                                                 |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Incorporated Partnership | ABN Certificate of Registration (Incorporated)           |
| Association              | Constitution                                             |
| Company                  | ASIC Certificate of Registration or ASIC Company Extract |

### Tab: Sole proprietorships

The legal name of the sole proprietor should be entered exactly as it appears on the registration document or online registry. Commercial/trading names are not accepted for registration number verification.

Refer to this [page](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#constitutional-doc) for more information about constitutional documents for sole proprietorships. In Australia, this includes the ABN Certificate of Registration.

Sole proprietors may onboard with an individual bank account or a bank account under a trading name provided it does not include a legal form indicating company ownership. The correct trading name must be inputted at onboarding.

### Tab: Trusts

A trust is a relationship which exists where the trustee(s) holds property or assets for the benefit of one or more beneficiaries. Trusts can be onboarded via API or Adyen’s Hosted onboarding solution. Trusts are currently only supported in Australia and New Zealand.

For detailed information on onboarding requirements for trusts, Refer to this [page](/platforms/verification-requirements/?tab=trust_3_4).

In general, to onboard a trust, you must collect the following:

* Full name of the trust.
* The type of trust.
* Tax information.
* Registered address.
* A constitutional document that proves the existence of the trust, such as a trust deed.
* Full names of the settlors, trustees, protectors, beneficiaries, and ultimate beneficial owners for the trust. If the settlor has an exemption reason, you must provide it when you associate them to the trust.

When onboarding a trust, the correct Australian company number (ACN) and tax ID (ABN) need to be inputted in the correct fields at onboarding.

For AU trusts, the correct company legal name or individual name needs to be provided at onboarding. If the trustee is an organization, trading names can also be provided at onboarding.

For verifying the trust legal arrangement, the correct trust name as displayed on the registry and/or constitutional document needs to be provided in the input data for onboarding.

As part of the verification checks for a trust, Adyen also verifies the identity of individuals that are associated with the trust. It is mandatory to onboard at a minimum with a settlor, trustee and beneficiary trust members. For definitions of the trust members and further information on how to onboard a trust, refer to this [page](/platforms/verification-requirements/?tab=trust_3_4).

#### Trust documents

It is mandatory to upload the trust deed at onboarding for trusts in both Australia and New Zealand.

## Canada

### Tab: Individuals

Adyen tries to verify the identity of the user using their personal details. In case automatic verification fails, Adyen may request a [photo ID](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#photo-id-requirements) to complete the verification process.

Users that onboard using Hosted onboarding can choose to verify their identity using instant ID verification. Documents can also be uploaded manually in the hosted onboarding page or using the Legal Entity Management API.

#### Bank verification

[Instant bank account verification](/platforms/onboard-users/instant-bank-verification) is available in Canada for both individuals and organizations through Hosted onboarding. Refer [here](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#bank-statement-requirements) for a full list of requirements of bank documents.

### Tab: Organizations

Users in Canada are required to provide a registration or constitutional document to validate their [company type](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#registration-document). The legal name of the company must be entered exactly as it appears on the official registration document or online registry. Users frequently make the mistake of entering the trading name as the registered legal name. Ensure that all information provided by the user is accurate.

#### Examples of company documents in Canada

| Company type             | Document                                                                  |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Incorporated Partnership | Provincial Certificate of Registration or CRA Certificate of Registration |
| Association              | Articles of Association                                                   |
| Company                  | Articles of Incorporation or Certificate of Continuance                   |

### Tab: Sole proprietorships

If a sole proprietorship is registered provincially in Canada, they need to provide the correct registration number and legal name of the sole proprietor. Registration documents for sole proprietorships include Provincial Certificates of Registration and CRA Certificates of Registration.

## France

### Tab: Individuals

Users onboarding with our Hosted onboarding solution are required to provide an identity document at onboarding. For further information on our identity document requirements, refer to this [page](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#photo-id-requirements).

Note that [French identity cards](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#additional-information-for-specific-countriesregions) issued between January 2, 2004, and December 31, 2013, are automatically extended by five years beyond the original expiration and remain valid for onboarding. However, this extension is not applicable if the cardholder was under 18 at the time the card was issued.

Users may only input a shortened names or their initials if these also appear on the ID document. Suffixes, such as **Jr.**, **Sr.**, or **III**, may be provided in the first or last name input fields. However, titles such as **Ms.**, **Mrs.**, **Mr.**, and **Dr.** are not accepted.

#### Bank verification

[Instant bank account verification](/platforms/onboard-users/instant-bank-verification) is available in France for both individuals and companies through our Hosted onboarding solution. Alternatively users may upload a bank document for review.

For individuals, bank statements that display **M OU MME** along with the spouse’s last name are acceptable, as long as the last name provided is displayed on the user’s ID document. If the user is using a married name or has recently changed their name and this is not reflected on the bank document, a marriage certificate or other relevant documentation should be uploaded via API as a supporting document.

For French bank documentation, a *Relevé d'Identité Bancaire (RIB)* does not require an issuance date. However, if a date is provided, it must be within 12 months of the current date.

### Tab: Organizations

Users must enter the full legal name of their company exactly as it appears on the official registration document or national registry. Abbreviations of the company name are not permitted; only the legal form (e.g., SARL or SASU) may be abbreviated. It is important to note that commercial or trading names (e.g., Nom commercial) are not acceptable substitutes for the legal name.

Adyen uses both the legal name and the registration number provided to automatically verify the company details. If this automatic verification does not succeed, users may be required to submit a [registration document](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#registration-document).

For non-profit or charitable organizations, an alternative registration number, such as an RNA number, may be provided instead of a SIRET number. Refer to this page for further information on company [types](/get-started-with-adyen/application-requirements#company-types).

#### Examples of company documents in France

| Company type             | Document                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Association              | Récépissé de déclaration (Receipt of the declaration of creation)                                                    |
| Company                  | Extrait K-bis (Extract from commercial register)                                                                     |
|                          | Certificat d'Inscription au Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS) (Certificate of Trade and Companies Register) |
| Incorporated Partnership | Extrait K-bis (Extract from the commercial register)                                                                 |
|                          | Avis de Situation au Répertoire Sirene (Situation report)                                                            |
|                          | URSSAF Extract                                                                                                       |
|                          | INSEE Extract                                                                                                        |
|                          | Statuts (Articles of Association)                                                                                    |

### Tab: Sole proprietorships

When onboarding as a sole proprietor, the user must enter their legal name exactly as it appears on the registration document or online registry. Sole proprietors may fall under legal forms such as *Entreprise individuelle (EI)*, *Profession libérale*, or *Entrepreneur individuel*. Note that a SELARL, a limited liability company with a single owner, is not classified as a sole proprietorship. Entities of this type must be onboarded as an organization.

Examples of documents for sole proprietorships:

* Extrait K-bis (Extract from the commercial register)
* Avis de Situation au Répertoire Sirene (Situation report)
* URSSAF Extract
* INSEE Extract

## United Kingdom

### Tab: Individuals

Adyen tries to verify the identity of the user using their personal details. In case automatic verification fails, Adyen may request a [photo ID](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#photo-id-requirements) to complete the verification process.

Users that onboard using Hosted onboarding can choose to verify their identity using instant ID verification. Documents can also be uploaded manually in the hosted onboarding page or using the Legal Entity Management API. A [CitizenCard](https://www.citizencard.com/) is a supported form of ID and can be used for verification during onboarding.

Suffixes (**Jr.**, **Sr.**, **III**, etc.) can be provided in the first or last name input fields, but titles like **Ms.**, **Mrs.**, **Mr.**, and **Dr.** are not accepted.

### Tab: Organizations

Users must enter the legal name of their company exactly as it appears on the official registration document or online registry. The full legal name must be used, without any abbreviations, except for the legal form (e.g., LTD or CIC).

#### Examples of company documents in the United Kingdom

| Company type             | Document                     |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Association              | Articles of association      |
| Company                  | Certificate of Incorporation |
| Incorporated Partnership | VAT Registration Certificate |

### Tab: Sole proprietorships

For sole proprietorships, users are required to provide a constitutional document to verify their company details. This can be a tax return or Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR) confirmation letter.

## United States

### Tab: Individuals

Adyen tries to verify the identity of the user using their personal details. In case automatic verification fails, Adyen may request a [photo ID](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#photo-id-requirements) to complete the verification process. Users that onboard using Hosted onboarding can choose to verify their identity using instant ID verification. Documents can also be uploaded manually in the hosted onboarding page or using the Legal Entity Management API.

US users are required to input the last four digits of their SSN (Social Security Number). If Adyen is unable to verify their personal details using the last 4 digits of their SSN , the user can input the full 9 digit SSN, update personal details or upload a proof of their SSN.

#### Bank verification

[Instant bank account verification](/platforms/onboard-users/instant-bank-verification) is available in the US for both individuals and companies through our Hosted onboarding solution. Alternatively users may upload a bank document for review.

If the user is using a married name or has recently changed their name, and this is not reflected on the bank document, a marriage certificate or other supporting documentation must be provided through the Legal Entity Management API.

Direct deposit forms are not accepted if they do not have a stamp or signature issued by a bank.

For cheques, all details must be printed, not handwritten, and the account number cannot be masked with X’s.

### Tab: Organizations

Users must enter the legal name of their company exactly as it appears on the official registration document or national registry. The legal name of the company must not be abbreviated since only the legal form may be abbreviated. Users often incorrectly input the trading name of the company at onboarding that is displayed on IRS documentation. Ensure the user submits the correct trading name shown in the company document uploaded.

If the legal name of the company has been changed but is not yet reflected in the IRS database, upload a certificate of amendment issued by the state to confirm the name change via API to continue the onboarding process.

The Employer Identification Number (EIN) is the only accepted [registration/tax number](/platforms/verification-requirements/document-requirements#organization-tax-document).

#### Examples of company documents in the United States

| Company type             | Document                              |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Association              | Tax exemption certificate             |
|                          | Amendment of Articles of Associations |
| Company                  | EIN Confirmation Letter (CP 575)      |
| Incorporated Partnership | Letter 147C                           |
|                          | EIN Confirmation Letter (CP 575)      |

### Tab: Sole proprietorships

Sole proprietors may be required to provide a constitutional document to verify their company details.

#### Examples of sole proprietorship documents in the United States

* SS-4 confirmation letter
* Schedule C
* EIN Confirmation Letter (CP 575)
