# Payment methods

Learn how you can configure additional payment methods in your balance platform integration.

To start processing payments for your users, you must configure the payment methods they can accept during the [onboarding process](/platforms/onboard-users/onboarding-steps). Adyen's platform supports a wide range of global and local card payment methods with a single integration.

If you onboarded your users using the [onboarding on invite](/platforms/onboard-users?tab=1#onboarding-on-invite), they already have their [default payment methods](/platforms/onboard-users#default-pm) configured. You can request additional payment methods in your [Customer Area](https://ca-test.adyen.com/) or using the [Management API](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/Management/latest/overview).

Payment methods are configured separately for each store.
