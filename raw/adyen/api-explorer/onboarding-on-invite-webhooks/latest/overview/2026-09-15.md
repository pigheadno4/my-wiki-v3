# Onboarding on invite webhooks v1

Adyen sends webhooks to notify your system when a new account holder is onboarded in your balance platform using the onboarding on invite flow.

## Endpoints

### General
| Method | Path | Summary |
|--------|------|---------|
| POST | balancePlatform.accountHolder.onboarded | Account holder onboarded |
