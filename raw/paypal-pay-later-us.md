# Pay Later (US)

Get paid in full at checkout while giving your customers the flexibility to pay in installments over time with no late fees.

![Pay Later hero](assets/paypal-pay-later-hero.png)

# Features

- **Increased conversion:** "Buy now, pay later" messaging can improve conversion, attract new customers, and increase order values.
- **Dynamic messages:** Show customers a Pay Later offer based on the contents of their shopping cart.
- **Multiple touch points:** Add messaging on product pages, shopping cart, and checkout pages.

![How Pay Later works](assets/paypal-pay-later-how-it-works.png)

# Pay Later products in the US

| Product | Number of payments | Due | Purchase amount |
| ------- | ------------------ | --- | --------------- |
| Pay in 4 | 4 | Every 2 weeks (biweekly) | $30 to $1,500 |
| Pay Monthly | 3, 6, 12, or 24 | Monthly | $49 to $10,000 |

# Eligibility

Eligible if you:
- Are a US-based PayPal merchant with a Business Account
- Have a US-facing website
- Transact in US dollars (USD)
- Have a one-time payment integration (Pay Later available via PayPal checkout)
- Abide by the PayPal Acceptable Use Policy
- Do not edit Pay Later messages with additional content, wording, or marketing

**Not eligible:**
- Reference Transaction and Recurring Payment integrations
- Website Payments Standard (WPS) integrations
