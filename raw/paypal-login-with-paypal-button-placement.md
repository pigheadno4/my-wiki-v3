# Button placement

You can place the **Log in with PayPal** button anywhere on your website or app. To maintain a simple user experience, PayPal recommends that you display the **Log in with PayPal** button in a highly visible location on your dedicated log in or registration page.

![Generic,Login](assets/paypal-login-generic-login.png)

When configuring payout preferences, display the **Log in with PayPal** button on the page where users go to configure their payment preferences. This might be in the account settings page, or in part of the initial flow for the account configuration.

![Set,Preferences](assets/paypal-login-set-preferences.png)
