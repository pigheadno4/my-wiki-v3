# Store Sync overview

Store Sync is a service that connects your product catalog and commerce API with PayPal's agentic commerce services. This connection enables AI agents to discover your products, create and manage shopping carts, and complete purchases on behalf of customers through conversational interfaces.

## Eligibility and prerequisites

To be eligible to integrate PayPal's agentic commerce services, you must meet all of these requirements:

- Be an existing PayPal merchant. If you are not yet a PayPal merchant, complete onboarding first.
- Do business in a supported region and currency. Currently, only merchants who sell physical goods to US-based customers in USD are eligible.
- Use [Orders v2](/reference/api/rest/orders/create-order) or support an Orders v2 integration.
- Have a product catalog that you can share with PayPal. For information about creating your product catalog, see [Create a product catalog](/growth/agentic-commerce/store-sync/product-catalog/create-a-product-catalog/).

> **Important:** Store Sync has limited availability at this time. To access and use Store Sync, complete [this form](https://www.paypal.com/us/business/ai#form) to contact the AI team at PayPal and request access.
