# Request Dev Kit

Test our hardware and API using our Dev Kit and Braintree Sandbox Account.


### Requesting a Dev Kit

Once approved to move forward by your PayPal/Braintree Account Executive, you may request a dev kit, which includes a card reader that can communicate with the Braintree sandbox environment, as well as test cards specially designed for continued testing of card present transactions.

**NOTE**
To request a dev kit, reach out to your PayPal/Braintree Account Executive or Solutions Engineer. If you are **NOT** yet working with an Account Executive, fill out [this form](https://www.paypal.com/us/webapps/mpp/contact-sales) to reach our sales team to get more info.


### While you wait for your test kit to arrive:


- [Configure a Braintree Sandbox Account](/braintree/in-person/get-started-1/configure-sandbox/)


- [Review our Technical Overview](/braintree/in-person/about/technical-overview/)


- [View the Braintree Developer Docs](/braintree/graphql/)



**NOTE**
It is important to note that the sandbox readers that come in the dev kit are not compatible with production environments and also cannot read production cards.


### Requirements to receive a Dev Kit

In order to receive a dev kit, you must have a signed NDA in place with PayPal/Braintree, or you must be an existing Braintree merchant. Please discuss this with your PayPal sales rep to ensure you have a signed NDA in place before requesting your dev kit.
