### [](#keyboard-shortcuts)Keyboard Shortcuts

While using the Braintree Verifone Reader app you can access certain utility screens via keyboard combinations. Press **** the following combinations.

| BUTTON COMBINATION | FUNCTION |
| 2 + 8 | Displays the reader settings from the screensaver |
| Hold X

(8 Seconds) | Enters the Power Panel

**Reboot** or **Shutdown** the P400

& enter **Deep Sleep** on e285 device. |

The reader has a few screens to indicate its connectivity state. The default PayPal screensaver indicates that the reader is connected and ready for test transactions.
