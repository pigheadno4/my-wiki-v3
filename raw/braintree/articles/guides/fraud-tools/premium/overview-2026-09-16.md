# Overview

While configuring [Basic Fraud Tools](/braintree/articles/guides/fraud-tools/basic/overview) is an important first step in protecting your account from common types of payment fraud, more sophisticated schemes demand advanced solutions. To address these challenges, Braintree offers several Premium Fraud Management Tools:


- [Fraud protection Lite](https://developer.paypal.com/braintree/articles/guides/fraud-tools/premium/fraud-protection-lite/)- Designed for small to mid-size businesses
- [Fraud Protection Advanced](/braintree/articles/guides/fraud-tools/premium/fraud-protection-advanced)- For enterprises
- [Chargeback Protection tool*](/braintree/articles/guides/fraud-tools/premium/chargeback-protection)- For businesses who want to outsource fraud management and reduce chargeback risks
- [Effortless Chargeback Protection tool*](/braintree/articles/guides/fraud-tools/premium/effortless-chargeback-protection)- A premium version of the Chargeback Protection tool

Each tool provides a suite of fully integrated fraud prevention and detection checks before a transaction or verification request is processed, which helps catch fraudulent activity before authorization requests ever reach your customers' banks.


## How it works

When you enable and use either of our Premium Fraud Management Tools, the Braintree gateway will pass required information about transactions and verifications created via the API to your chosen fraud protection provider for evaluation.

Premium Fraud Management Tools are not set up on your account by default. If you would like to use this feature, you must Fraud Protection Advanced in the Control Panel and [update your client-side and server-side implementations](/braintree/docs/guides/premium-fraud-management-tools/overview). Additional fees may apply.


### Payment methods supported

Fraud Protection Advancedare compatible with all credit and debit cards and the following:


- [Apple Pay](/braintree/articles/guides/payment-methods/apple-pay#fraud-tools)
- [Google Pay](/braintree/articles/guides/payment-methods/google-pay#fraud-tools)
- [Secure Remote Commerce](/braintree/articles/guides/payment-methods/secure-remote-commerce#fraud-tools)

The Chargeback Protection tools are only available for debit and credit card transactions in certain markets. The Chargeback Protection tools are not available for other payment methods at this time.

## Fraud Protection Lite

Fraud Protection Lite provides additional features and capabilities on top of the basic fraud tools to help businesses to identify, investigate, and mitigate fraud more effectively. These include machine learning–powered transaction risk scores, an analytics dashboard for monitoring fraud trends, a detailed transaction insights view, customizable blocklists, historical transaction simulation to test rules against past data, and an integration health dashboard to track system performance.


## Fraud Protection Advanced

Fraud Protection Advanced provides additional features and capabilities on top of those offered by Fraud Protection Lite to help fraud teams more effectively identify, investigate, and mitigate fraud. It includes all Fraud Protection Lite features, along with support for block, review, and allow lists, manual review investigation workflows, enhanced reporting, the ability to review transactions in detail, and tools to create and manage new custom filters.

[Contact us](/braintree/help?issue=AdvancedFraudProtectionQuestion) if you are interested in learning more about [Fraud Protection Advanced](/braintree/articles/guides/fraud-tools/premium/fraud-protection-advanced).

[Read more about differences between our fraud protection options.](/braintree/articles/guides/fraud-tools/overview#fraud-tool-comparison)


## Chargeback Protection Tool

The Chargeback Protection tool* helps reduce risk associated with fraudulent transactions in real time by evaluating whether to approve or decline eligible credit or debit card transactions. If an approved, eligible transaction is later charged back with fraud or product not received reason codes, PayPal will request that you provide valid evidence that you have delivered the goods and services. Once the evidence is approved, PayPal will waive PayPal’s chargeback fees** and the disputed amounts for the chargebacks. Availability for the Chargeback Protection tool varies by country.


## Effortless Chargeback Protection Tool

The Effortless Chargeback Protection tool* expands upon the value provided by the Chargeback Protection tool by allowing merchants to forgo submitting evidence of delivery for eligible fraud chargebacks (evidence of delivery is required for product not received chargebacks). When the Effortless Chargeback Protection tool is enabled, PayPal automatically waives any PayPal chargeback fees** and does not debit the disputed amount for eligible fraud chargebacks. Availability for the Effortless Chargeback Protection tool varies by country.


## Enabling Premium Fraud Management Tools

Device data collection for Fraud Protection Advanced is available **only** for the following client SDK versions and higher:


- iOS 4.32.0: released on Feb 18, 2020
- Android 3.9.0: released on Feb 20, 2020
- Web 3.58.0: released on Feb 13, 2020

By updating to the **latest client SDK versions or at least the minimum versions above**, device data will be passed to our Fraud Protection Advanced risk models, which in turn will improve fraud decision performance on your transactions.

Whether you're using [Fraud Protection Lite](https://developer.paypal.com/braintree/articles/guides/fraud-tools/premium/fraud-protection-lite/) or [Fraud Protection Advanced](/braintree/articles/guides/fraud-tools/premium/fraud-protection-advanced), you will first need to [update your client-side](/braintree/docs/guides/premium-fraud-management-tools/client-side) and [server-side](/braintree/docs/guides/premium-fraud-management-tools/server-side) code to collect and pass device data. You may then enable this feature in the Control Panel. If there is any delay between enabling in the Control Panel and making the code changes, the integration will not work properly.

Immediately after you update your code:


- Log into the[Control Panel](https://www.braintreegateway.com/login)
- Click on the gear icon in the top right corner
- Click**Fraud Management**from the drop-down menu
- Next to**Premium Fraud Management Tools**, make a selection and click the toggle to turn it on


## Overriding rejections

When you use Premium Fraud Management Tools, transactions will be subject to rejection due to suspected fraud. While you can't allowlist certain payment methods, you can avoid rejections on specific transactions in a couple of different ways:


- Bypass Premium Fraud Checking completely by passing the[skip_advanced_fraud_checking](/braintree/docs/reference/request/transaction/sale/ruby#options-skip_advanced_fraud_checking)parameter when creating transactions via the API
- If using a Premium Fraud Management Tool, bypass Premium Fraud Checking by checking the[skip_advanced_fraud_checking](/braintree/docs/reference/request/transaction/sale/ruby#options-skip_advanced_fraud_checking)box when creating transactions from the Control Panel
- Note: The Chargeback Protection and Effortless Chargeback Protection tools do not support the ability to bypass or skip fraud checks

* Terms apply to the Chargeback Protection tool. Certain transactions and chargebacks are not eligible for the Chargeback Protection tool. See [Terms](https://www.braintreepayments.com/legal/payment-services-agreement#eligibility-criteria) for details.   ** You may be assessed additional chargeback fees by acquiring banks and card networks, which will not be waived but will be passed through to you at cost.
