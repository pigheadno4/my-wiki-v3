# Plans

Before creating subscriptions, you must create a plan. A plan is a template for your subscriptions: when you create a plan, it will be used to populate information such as amount, currency, and billing cycle when a new subscription is created.


## Creating a plan

To create a plan in the Control Panel:


- Log into the[Control Panel](https://www.braintreegateway.com/login)
- Click on**Subscriptions**in the navigation bar
- Click the**Plans**tab
- Click the**Create A Plan**button
- If you have already created one or more plans, this button will read**New Plan**


- Fill out any relative information – including:
- The[billing date](/braintree/articles/guides/recurring-billing/billing-cycles#billing-date)
- The[number of billing cycles](/braintree/articles/guides/recurring-billing/billing-cycles#number-of-billing-cycles)
- [Add-ons or discounts](/braintree/articles/guides/recurring-billing/add-ons-discounts)
- [Trial periods](/braintree/articles/guides/recurring-billing/trial-periods)


- Click the**Create**button


**IMPORTANT**
 If you are adding a trial period to a plan, read the trial period [risks and requirements](/braintree/articles/guides/recurring-billing/trial-periods#risks-and-requirements) first.

 


## Updating a plan

You can update a plan in the Control Panel. The billing cycle can't be modified on an existing plan, but you can change all other elements.


- Log into the[Control Panel](https://www.braintreegateway.com/login)
- Click on**Subscriptions**in the navigation bar
- Click the**Plans**tab
- Click the**Edit**link located to the right of the plan you'd like to update
- Make any desired changes
- Click the**Save**button

If you update the details of a plan, those changes will only apply to new subscriptions – [existing subscriptions will**not**be updated automatically](/braintree/articles/guides/recurring-billing/subscriptions#subscription-price).


**IMPORTANT**
 Merchants operating in the European Union must give customers 4 weeks’ notice before changing the price of their recurring billing plan; 4 weeks’ notice is also required before billing customers if it has been 6+ months since their last payment. If you don’t operate in the EU, these notices aren't required (but they're still good practice).

 


## Deleting a plan

You can only delete a plan in the Control Panel. A plan can't be deleted if it has any former or current subscriptions associated with it.


- Log into the[Control Panel](https://www.braintreegateway.com/login)
- Click on**Subscriptions**in the navigation bar
- Click the**Plans**tab
- Click the**Delete**link located to the right of the plan you'd like to remove
- Click the**Yes**button to confirm
