# Braintree Data Shared with PayPal

Braintree shares the following information with PayPal in order to create and/or link to a merchant’s PayPal account. All data is encrypted and sent to PayPal using TLS/SSL.


- Primary Business Contact Information
- First Name, Last Name, Title
- Date of Birth, SSN
- Residential Address


- Business Entity Information
- Address
- Business Description
- Phone number
- Business Type
- Tax ID number (TIN)
- Website URL
- Average Monthly PayPal Payment Volume


- PayPal Account Credentials
- Username
- Password


- Legal Agreement Consent on Merchant’s Behalf
- Annual Approved Volume
