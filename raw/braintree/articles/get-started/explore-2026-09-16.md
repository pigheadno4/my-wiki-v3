# Explore Braintree

Now that we’ve covered the basics, you might be wondering: what’s next? We have a wide variety of features that can make Braintree work for you and your business. If you’re not sure where to start, take a look at some of our favorite support articles.


### [Fraud Tools Overview](/braintree/articles/guides/fraud-tools/overview)

Fight fraud and protect yourself from chargebacks with our full suite of tools. From basic AVS and CVV checks to analyzing device data – we’ve got you covered.


### [PCI Compliance](/braintree/articles/risk-and-security/compliance/pci-compliance)

While Braintree alleviates your PCI compliance burden by securely storing your customer’s payment data for you, all merchants must be PCI compliant; learn how in this article.


### [Control Panel Overview](/braintree/articles/control-panel/overview)

Configure payment options, generate reports, access your API keys, and more.


### [Recurring Billing Overview](/braintree/articles/guides/recurring-billing/overview)

Learn how to charge your customers automatically in monthly increments.


### [Chargebacks and Retrievals](/braintree/articles/risk-and-security/chargebacks-retrievals/overview)

Unfortunately, chargebacks and retrievals are a standard part of accepting payments online. In this article, learn what they are, how to prevent them, and what to do if a customer disputes a transaction.


### [PayPal Overview](/braintree/articles/guides/payment-methods/paypal/overview)

Make shopping even easier for your customers by using one of the world’s most trusted payment providers.


### [Reporting Overview](/braintree/articles/control-panel/reporting/overview)

Learn about the different types of reporting we offer for your business, and where to find them.


### [Risk and Security](/braintree/articles/risk-and-security/overview)

Learn about why risk mitigation is vital when processing online payments, what we do to minimize risk, and what you can do to help.
