# Australia Overview


**NOTE**
 If you’ve received a link to this page, it means you are using Braintree Direct, and at least one of your merchant accounts is located in Australia and funded by Braintree.

 

Our [public support articles](/braintree/articles) provide a good foundation for understanding both payment processing and the basics of a Braintree integration. The more specific nuances of your account – such as the payment methods you can accept, the processes you need to follow for making account changes, and the resources available to you for reconciliation – vary based on your location and setup. This page is your portal to articles that reflect these nuances.

It’s important to note that these articles are only accessible via direct links. You won’t be able to find them by searching our public support articles. **We strongly recommend that you bookmark this page so you can easily reference this important information in the future**.
