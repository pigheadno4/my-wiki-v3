# Statements

Moneris will mail your statements to the business address on file 3 to 5 business days after the beginning of the month. If you have not processed any transactions, you will not be issued a statement.

Your statement provides a summary of all of your processing activity for each month, and can be used to help [reconcile the deposits in your account](/braintree/articles/moneris/reconciliation).


## Sales Summary by Card Type

The Sales Summary by Card Type section provides a high-level overview of your total sales and refunds for the month. You can find net and gross totals broken out for each card type. It also shows the total number of transactions for each card type, as well as the average sale amount.


## Monthly Summary

The Monthly Summary provides the total amount of discount and transaction fees and the total amount of chargebacks that have been debited from your account for the month.


## Daily Activity Summary

The Daily Activity Summary details the gross sales, return (or refund) amounts, net sales, discount and transaction fees, and chargeback amounts for each day. Totals are grouped by card type. This section also provides a total for each day, minus all fees and chargebacks. These totals should match the deposits in your account, and can be used for [reconciliation](/braintree/articles/moneris/reconciliation). You can also find grand totals for each column at the end of this section.


## Financial Details

The Financial Details section details the debits and credits that were applied to your account by card type for each day. You’ll also find any GST or HST amounts that may have been charged, and the account number that the credits/debits were applied to. The end of this section provides totals for GST/HST, and the total amount credited to your account for the month.


## Interchange and Wholesale Discount Fees

The Interchange and Wholesale Discount Fees section only applies to those on the [interchange plus pricing model](/braintree/articles/moneris/pricing-fees). Here you will find the monthly summary of interchange fees that have been assessed for your transactions. This includes the type of interchange fee, amount, and rate – organized by card brand.


## Transaction Fees

The Transaction Fees section details the total fees assessed by card brand, including chargeback fees. It provides the total transaction amount that each fee was applied to, the volume, the rate at which the fee was applied, and the total amount debited for each fee.


## Chargeback Summary

The Chargeback Summary details your chargebacks for the month, separated by card brand. It includes the chargeback amount, the number of chargebacks, and the reason for the chargeback.
