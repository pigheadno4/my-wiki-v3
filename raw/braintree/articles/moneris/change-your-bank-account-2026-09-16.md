# Change Your Bank Account

As a part of your original application, you provided bank information for a checking account associated with your business. All settled transactions are paid out to this bank account according to the [settlement and funding timeline](/braintree/articles/moneris/transactions/settlement-funding-timeline).


## Documentation requirements

If you'd like to update your business bank account information, upload either an embossed voided cheque or a signed bank letter for your new account on official bank letterhead using our [Business Uploads Tool](/braintree/articles/guides/account-information#securely-upload-business-documents) in the Control Panel.


**NOTE**
 Screenshots of an online banking profile will not be accepted.

 

Make sure that either document you provide meets all of the following requirements:


### Embossed voided cheque


- Includes the account holder's name (either the DBA or legal name of your business)
- Screenshots of sample checks are not valid


### Bank letter


- Includes the account holder's name (either the DBA or legal name of your business)
- Includes the bank account number
- Includes the transit number
- Printed on official bank letterhead
- Typed and signed by a branch officer with contact details – handwritten letters will not be accepted
- Was issued within the last 6 months, with the issue date clearly stated

Our Support team will review the uploaded document and follow up with the authorized signer of your account.


**NOTE**
 The bank account submitted must be a Canadian-based business checking account. Savings, deposit-only, and prepaid debit accounts will not be accepted.

 


### Authorized signer

The authorized signer for your Braintree account is the only person who can request access or make changes to sensitive account information, such as transaction details, bank account information, or statement descriptors. This individual and their associated authorized email address were determined during the application process.

Authorized signers are not the same as the Account Admin [user role](/braintree/articles/control-panel/users-roles/managing-users-roles) in the Control Panel, and can't be managed via the Control Panel; to change your authorized signer or add additional signers, [Contact us](/braintree/help/updateBusinessDetails).


**NOTE**
 If an authorized signer calls in to request information about their account, they will need to answer specific security questions to confirm their identity.

 


## Multiple bank accounts

You have the option of associating two bank accounts with your merchant account – one for fees and one for funding settlements. If you want to take advantage of this, please provide appropriate documentation for each bank account and specify which account will be for fees and which will be for funding.


### Bank accounts for fees

Fees must be debited from a CAD bank account. This means that if you have a merchant account for presenting in USD, you must have two bank accounts associated with it – a USD checking account for deposits, and a CAD checking account for fees to be debited from.
