## Reporting

We do not generate statements for merchants with our Chase banking partnership; however, Chase offers 125+ customizable reporting tools in their Paymentech Online portal. You can find more information about the different types of reports and what they detail on the [Chase Paymentech website](https://www.chasepaymentech.com/paymentechonline/).

Chase reports expire after 6 to 16 months – depending on report type – so we suggest downloading them on a regular basis to maintain your own records.


## Reconciliation

For daily, weekly, and monthly reconciliation, we recommend using the financial reports generated in Chase Paymentech Online. The **Deposit Activity Summary** (FIN-0010) and **Financial Activity Summary** (FIN-0025) include daily deposit amounts for settled transactions, chargebacks, fees, and any administrative adjustment activity.
