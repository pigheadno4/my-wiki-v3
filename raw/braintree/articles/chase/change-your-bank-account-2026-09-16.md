# Change Your Bank Account

As a part of your original application, you provided bank information for a checking account associated with your business. All settled transactions are paid out to this bank account according to the [settlement and funding timeline](/braintree/articles/chase/transactions/settlement-funding-timeline).

If you'd like to update your business bank account information, upload a completed [bank change request form](https://www.paypalobjects.com/btdevdoc/braintree/files/Chase_Bank_Change_Form.pdf) in the Control Panel, along with either a voided check or a bank letter for your new account. [Learn more about how to use our Business Uploads Tool in the Control Panel.](/braintree/articles/guides/account-information#securely-upload-business-documents)


**NOTE**
 Screenshots of an online banking profile will not be accepted.

 

Make sure that either document you provide meets all of the following requirements:


## Voided check


- Includes the account holder's name (either the DBA or legal name of your business)
- If an address is listed, it matches the address we have on file for your business
- Starter checks, temporary checks, and screenshots of sample checks are not valid


## Bank Letter


- Printed on bank letterhead
- Includes the account holder's name (must match the DBA or legal name of business)
- Includes the bank account number or IBAN
- Includes the routing number, EFT number, or SWIFT Code/sort code
- Signed by a bank representative, along with their printed name and title
- Was issued within the last 6 months, with the issue date clearly stated


**NOTE**
 The account submitted must be a business checking account. Savings, deposit-only, and prepaid debit accounts will not be accepted.
