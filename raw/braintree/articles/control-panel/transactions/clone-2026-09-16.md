# Clone Transactions

Cloning is an easy way to copy information from an existing transaction, including the payment method, so you don't have to enter it again.

As long as the original transaction meets [the compatibility requirements](#compatibility), the only information you'll need to provide is the transaction amount – the rest will be automatically filled in.


## Compatibility

Most transactions can be cloned, but there are some restrictions. A transaction cannot be cloned if the original transaction:


- Was processed through PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Venmo
- Was created from a Vault record – if the customer's information is stored in the vault, you'll need to[create a new transaction from their Vault record](/braintree/articles/control-panel/transactions/create#on-an-existing-vault-record)
- Has the statusProcessor Declined,Failed,Gateway Rejected* orSettling

*Gateway rejections with the [Fraud or Risk Thresholds reasons](/braintree/articles/control-panel/transactions/gateway-rejections) can be cloned in the case that a legitimate transaction was mistakenly rejected as fraudulent.


## Cloning a transaction

To clone a transaction:


- Log into the[Control Panel](https://www.braintreegateway.com/login)
- Click on**Transactions**in the navigation bar
- Scroll to the**Transaction Search**section
- Define your desired parameters and click the**Search**button
- Click on the desired transaction**ID**link
- Click the**Clone**button
- Enter the amount you want to charge for the new transaction and the CVV,[if applicable](#checking-cvv-on-cloned-transactions)
- For authorizations, uncheck the box next toSubmit for Settlement


- Click the**Create Transaction**button


**NOTE**
 If you don't see the option to clone the transaction, and you've confirmed it isn't an [ineligible transaction](#compatibility), check to make sure your user's role has the Create Sales and the Submit Sales for Settlement [role permission](/braintree/articles/control-panel/users-roles/role-permissions).

 


## Checking CVV on cloned transactions

If you have [CVV rules](/braintree/articles/guides/fraud-tools/basic/avs-cvv-rules) enabled and would like to check the CVV when cloning a transaction, fill in the Card Verification Value (CVV) field under the **Payment Information** section. If you do not include a CVV with the cloned transaction – regardless of whether you have CVV rules enabled – we will not check the CVV.
