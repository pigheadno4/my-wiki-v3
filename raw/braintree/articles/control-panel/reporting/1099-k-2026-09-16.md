# 1099-K

Depending on your account setup and your business’s location, we may be able to provide a 1099-K for [tax purposes](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/form-1099-k-merchant-card-and-third-party-network-payments). When applicable, we’ll make it available online in the Control Panel. If you have any questions or you want to request a paper copy of your 1099-K, [contact us](/braintree/help?issue=1099k).


## Viewing your 1099-K in the Control Panel

To access your 1099-K in the Control Panel, your user's role must have the View Statements [role permission](/braintree/articles/control-panel/users-roles/role-permissions) enabled; the Account Admin role includes this permission by default. To pull your 1099-K:


- Log into the[Control Panel](https://www.braintreegateway.com/login)
- Click on the gear icon in the top right corner
- Click**Business**from the drop-down menu
- Click on the**Documents**tab
- Click the**View Tax Statements**button
- Click on the desired**IRS 1099-K - YYYY**link to download the PDF

The previous year's statement is generated at the end of January and will be available until at least October 15.


**NOTE**
 If your 1099-K isn’t available in the Control Panel and you are a US Braintree Direct merchant who processed transactions during the previous tax year, we will send your 1099-K to you via email or snail mail.

 


## Reconciling 1099-K totals

Monthly and yearly totals on the 1099-K are calculated using the gross sales volume. This does not include credits, refunds, or chargebacks.

If any of the information is incorrect, [contact us](/braintree/help?issue=updateBusinessName) so we can provide a corrected 1099-K form.


### PayPal transactions

PayPal transactions are not included in the 1099-K that Braintree provides. If you process PayPal transactions with your Braintree account, you will receive a separate 1099-K from PayPal for those transactions. For information on where to find your PayPal 1099-K, [visit the PayPal Help Center](https://www.paypal.com/us/selfhelp/article/How-do-I-find-my-1099-tax-statement-in-my-PayPal-account-FAQ919).


### American Express transactions

If you process American Express using Braintree's aggregated Amex, the monthly totals on your 1099-K will include all Amex transactions. If you have your own account directly with Amex, you will receive a separate 1099-K from Amex for these transactions.
