# Change Your Bank Account

As a part of your original Braintree application, you provided bank information for a checking account associated with your business. All settled transactions are paid out to this bank account according to the [settlement and funding timeline](/braintree/articles/nab/transactions/settlement-funding-timeline).

If you'd like to update your business bank account information, upload either a bank statement or a signed bank letter for your new account using our [Business Uploads Tool](/braintree/articles/guides/account-information#securely-upload-business-documents) in the Control Panel.


**NOTE**
 Screenshots of an online banking profile will not be accepted.

 

Make sure that either document you provide meets all of the following requirements:


### Bank Statement


- Includes the account holder's name (either the DBA or legal name of your business)
- Includes the BSB and account number
- Includes your business address
- Was issued within the last 3 months, with the issue date clearly stated


### Bank letter


- Printed on bank letterhead
- Includes the account holder's name (either the DBA or legal name of your business)
- Includes the BSB and account number
- Includes your business address
- Signed by a bank representative
- Was issued within the last 6 months, with the issue date clearly stated


**NOTE**
 The account submitted must be an Australia-based business checking account. Savings, deposit-only, and prepaid debit accounts will not be accepted.
