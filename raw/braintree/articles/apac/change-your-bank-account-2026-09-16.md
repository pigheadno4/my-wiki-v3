# Change Your Bank Account

As a part of your original application, you provided bank information for a checking account associated with your business. All settled transactions are paid out to this bank account according to the [settlement and funding timeline](/braintree/articles/apac/transactions/settlement-funding-timeline).

If you'd like to update your business bank account information, upload a voided check for your new account using our [Business Uploads Tool](/braintree/articles/guides/account-information#securely-upload-business-documents) in the Control Panel. While a voided check is preferred, you can alternatively provide a direct deposit form or a bank statement that was issued within the last 3 months.


**NOTE**
 Screenshots of an online banking profile will not be accepted.

 

Make sure that the document you provide clearly states the following details:


- Bank name and address
- Date the document was issued
- Account holder's name (the legal name of your business)
- Bank account number
- BIC/SWIFT code
- Bank sort code*

*Requirements for the bank sort code vary depending on where your bank is domiciled. For a bank account in Hong Kong, you must provide both the Interbank Clearing Code and Branch Code; in Malaysia, the IBG Routing Number; in Singapore, both the ACH Bank/Institution Code and Branch Code.


**NOTE**
 The account submitted must be a business checking account domiciled in the same country as your business. Savings, deposit-only, and prepaid debit accounts will not be accepted.
