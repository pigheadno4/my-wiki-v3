# Change Your Bank Account

As a part of your original application, you provided bank information for a checking account associated with your business. All settled transactions are paid out to this bank account according to the [settlement and funding timeline](/braintree/articles/aib-af/transactions/settlement-funding-timeline).

If you'd like to update your business bank account information, upload either a bank statement or a signed bank letter for your new account using our [Business Uploads Tool](/braintree/articles/guides/account-information#securely-upload-business-documents) in the Control Panel.


**NOTE**
 Screenshots of an online banking profile will not be accepted.

 

Make sure that either document you provide meets all of the following requirements:


## Bank Statement


- Includes the account holder's name (the legal name of your business)
- Includes the IBAN and BIC/SWIFT Code
- States the account currency
- Has only letters and characters that are included in the English alphabet
- Was issued within the last 3 months, with the issue date clearly stated


## Bank Letter


- Printed on official bank letterhead
- Includes the account holder's name (the legal name of your business)
- Includes the IBAN and BIC/SWIFT Code
- States the account currency
- Signed by a bank representative
- Has only letters and characters that are included in the English alphabet
- Was issued within the last 6 months, with the issue date clearly stated


**NOTE**
 The account submitted must be a business checking account. Savings, deposit-only, and prepaid debit accounts will not be accepted.
