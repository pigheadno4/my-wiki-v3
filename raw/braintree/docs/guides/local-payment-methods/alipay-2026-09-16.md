# Alipay


### Overview


**AVAILABILITY**
Alipay is currently in limited release and is only available to buyers in China. If you want to integrate Alipay as a Local Payment method, please contact your Customer Success Manager.

| Payment type | Buyer countries | Seller countries | Currency codes | Customer transaction limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `alipay` | China | Global except Russia, Brazil and Mainland China | `AUD`, `CAD`, `EUR`, `GBP`, `HKD`, `NZD`, `SGD`, `USD` | Min: N/A Max: 300,000 CNY |
