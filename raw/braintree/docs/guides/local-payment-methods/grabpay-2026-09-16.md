# GrabPay


### Overview


**AVAILABILITY**
GrabPay is currently in limited release and is only available to buyers and sellers in Singapore. If you want to integrate Grabpay as a Local Payment method, please contact your Customer Success Manager.

| Payment type | Buyer countries | Seller countries | Currency codes | Customer transaction limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `grabpay` | Singapore | Global | `SGD` | Min: 0.01 SGD Max: 5,000 SGD |
