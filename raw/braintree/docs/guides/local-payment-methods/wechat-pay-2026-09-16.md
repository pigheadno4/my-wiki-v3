# WeChat Pay


### Overview


**AVAILABILITY**
WeChat Pay is currently in limited release and is only available to buyers in China. If you want to integrate WeChat Pay as a Local Payment method, please contact your Customer Success Manager.

| Payment type | Buyer countries | Seller countries | Currency codes | Customer transaction limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `wechatpay` | China | Austria, Belgium, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, Malta, Holland, Norway, Portugal, Spain, Sweden, United Kingdom | EUR,USD,GBP | Min: N/A Max: 10,000 USD |
