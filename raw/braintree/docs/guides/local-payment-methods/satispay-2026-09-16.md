# Satispay


### Overview


**AVAILABILITY**
Satispay is currently in limited release and is only available to buyers in Italy. If you want to integrate Satispay as a Local Payment method, please follow the [integration guides](/braintree/docs/guides/local-payment-methods/configuration) .

| Payment type | Buyer countries | Seller countries | Currency codes | Customer transaction limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `satispay` | Italy | EEA+ CH+ UK | `EUR` | Min:0.01 EUR Max:99,999 EUR |
