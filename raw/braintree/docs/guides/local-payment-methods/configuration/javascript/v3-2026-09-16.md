# Configuration

Before you can accept Local Payment Methods, you will need to ensure you have completed the following steps:


- [Create, verify, and link](/braintree/articles/guides/payment-methods/paypal/setup-guide)your PayPal business account in the Braintree Control Panel. In order to process Local Payment Methods, you need to have a valid PayPal business account.
- [Generate a client token](/braintree/docs/start/hello-server#generate-a-client-token)on your server
- You will use the client token when you initialize your components


- [Integrate Local Payment Methods](/braintree/docs/guides/local-payment-methods/client-side-custom)on your client
- The Local Payment Methods integration was introduced in v3 of the JavaScript SDK, v4 of the iOS SDK, and v2 of the Android SDK.


- [Create a local payment transaction](/braintree/docs/guides/local-payment-methods/server-side/)on your server
- [Configure Braintree webhooks](/braintree/docs/guides/webhooks/overview)
- Process a local payment transaction in[sandbox](/braintree/docs/guides/local-payment-methods/testing-go-live#local-payment-method-testing-with-linked-paypal-account)or[production](/braintree/docs/guides/local-payment-methods/testing-go-live#go-live)successfully
