# Overview

**AVAILABILITY**
Braintree's recurring billing is not compatible with Braintree Marketplace.

 With Braintree's recurring billing, you can charge your customers automatically in monthly increments.     To set this up, you'll need to create a plan [via the API](/braintree/docs/guides/recurring-billing/plans) or [in the Control Panel](/braintree/articles/guides/recurring-billing/plans) and [store your customers in the Vault](/braintree/docs/guides/customers#create). Then use [Subscription: Create](/braintree/docs/reference/request/subscription/create) to associate each customer's preferred payment method with the plan.


## Integration steps

In addition to the steps listed below, you must [create customers with payment methods](/braintree/docs/guides/customers#create) in the Vault before you can use recurring billing.

## Subscription statuses

![Subscription,statuses](https://www.paypalobjects.com/btdevdoc/braintree/img/developers/flow_subscription-workflow.png)


### Pending

Pending subscriptions are subscriptions that have not started yet. For example, if you create a subscription with a specific billing date in the future, the subscription will start out as Pending.


### Active

Active subscriptions will be charged on the next billing date. You can get the dates from the [subscription object](/braintree/docs/reference/response/subscription). Subscriptions in a trial period are Active.


### Past Due

**NOTE**
We can send email notifications to your customers when their subscription becomes past due. See our [support article](/braintree/articles/guides/recurring-billing/email-notifications) for more information.

 A subscription status can change to Past Due for multiple reasons. The most common reason is a failed subscription payment. Other less common reasons for a Past Due status include that the balance is too high to be processed **or** issues in the merchant account setup, that would prevent a transaction from being created.     If the reason for your Past Due status is a failed subscription payment, you can manually retry the charge, or you can [set up logic in the Control Panel](/braintree/articles/guides/recurring-billing/recurring-advanced-settings#automatic-retries) to automatically retry a declined or failed charge at specific intervals. Additionally, a Past Due subscription will be automatically retried if [the payment method associated with the subscription is updated](/braintree/docs/guides/recurring-billing/manage#updating-past-due-subscriptions) and you have [proration](/braintree/articles/guides/recurring-billing/recurring-advanced-settings#proration) enabled. If the retried transaction is successful and the subscription has not passed its final billing date, the status will change to Active.     If a retried transaction is unsuccessful, the balance on the past due subscription will continue to increase every billing cycle, and we'll continue to retry the transaction each billing cycle—either indefinitely or until the number of cycles in the subscription is reached. If all retries are unsuccessful, the subscription’s status will remain Past Due until the subscription a) reaches the specified [number of billing cycles](/braintree/articles/guides/recurring-billing/billing-cycles#number-of-billing-cycles) and the status changes to Expired, or b) is canceled.


### Expired

Subscriptions are Expired when they have reached the specified [number of billing cycles](/braintree/articles/guides/recurring-billing/billing-cycles#number-of-billing-cycles).


### Canceled

If you [cancel](/braintree/docs/reference/request/subscription/cancel) the subscription, the status will be Canceled and no further billing will occur.
