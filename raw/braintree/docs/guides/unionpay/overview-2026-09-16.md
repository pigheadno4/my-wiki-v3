# Overview


**AVAILABILITY**
This integration for UnionPay has been deprecated, as UnionPay can now be processed as a credit card through their partnership with Discover. 

 See our [credit card guide](/braintree/docs/guides/credit-cards/overview) for more information.

UnionPay is a commonly accepted payment method outside the United States. The Braintree SDKs enable
you to accept UnionPay cards as if they were any other credit or debit card.
