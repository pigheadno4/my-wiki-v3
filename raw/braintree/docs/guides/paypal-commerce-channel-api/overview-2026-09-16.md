# Overview

Users are likely already using your social network, marketplace, or mobile app to post links and
talk about products from third-party retailers. As a PayPal Commerce channel, you can give your
users a way to buy directly from those retailers without leaving your site or app.
## Why use the PayPal Commerce Channel API

While there are other solutions out there for retailers who want to accept payments on social
platforms, these solutions often exist outside their normal flows for managing, tracking, and
fulfilling orders. The Channel API doesn't require the retailer to change anything about how they
currently do business. You get an easy way to enable seamless commerce for your users, and retailers
gain the benefit of an expanded reach using their existing workflows.
## How to get started

To become a PayPal Commerce channel, you'll need:
- A[Braintree sandbox account](https://www.braintreepayments.com/sandbox)(production needed to go live)
- A name for your channel
- A destination URL for webhooks – the Channel API will use this URL to deliver webhook notifications about the transactions you initiate (a service like[requestb.in](http://requestb.in/)is fine in sandbox)

Once you are ready,[contact us](/braintree/help)with your information. We will get you
set up and provide you with a channel-specific client ID and client secret for your integration.
From there, you can get started on your Channel API integration using this guide. Because you'll
need to use payment method tokens from the Braintree Vault to initiate orders, you'll also use[Braintree SDKs](/braintree/docs/start/overview). When you're ready for retailers to
connect with your channel, you'll need an authorization grant to charge payment methods on their
behalf. For now, the Commerce team will assist with manual one-time authorizations. In the future,
we will allow retailers to manage their own authorizations with your channel automatically.
