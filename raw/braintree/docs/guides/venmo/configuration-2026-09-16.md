# Configuration


## Enable Venmo

If you [qualify for accepting Venmo as a payment method](/braintree/articles/guides/payment-methods/venmo#availability), you can proceed to [enable Venmo in your sandbox Control Panel](/braintree/articles/guides/payment-methods/venmo#testing).


## Get a Venmo account for testing

Your business does not need a Venmo account to accept payments, but you will need a Venmo account you can log into on a mobile device to test your integration. If you do not already have a personal Venmo account you can use, be sure to create one. You can sign up on the [Venmo website](https://venmo.com/) or by downloading the app and signing up on your mobile device.



If you already have a Venmo account, make sure that it’s a[supported version](/braintree/articles/guides/payment-methods/venmo#customer-availability).
