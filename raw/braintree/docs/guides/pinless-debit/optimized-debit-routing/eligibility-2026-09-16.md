## Eligibility

**Merchants supported:**
- US-domiciled merchants
- Transaction currency must be in US dollars

**Payment methods supported:**
- Vaulted and keyed debit/prepaid card
- Eligible card types from Apple Pay and Google Pay
- Only eligible Visa and Mastercard BINs are routed to PINless debit networks

**Eligible PINless card networks:**
- STAR
- NYCE
- PULSE
- ACCEL
- MAESTRO


**NOTE**
Apple Pay and Google Pay payment methods are now eligible on Pinless Debit Network.
