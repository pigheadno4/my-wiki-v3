# Configuration

There is no additional configuration needed to begin accepting most credit and non-PIN debit card payments. Some merchants may need to contact us before accepting Amex transactions, but this is typically enabled by default. If you have questions about what payment methods you can accept, [contact us](/braintree/help/acceptPaymentTypes).


## AVS and CVV

You can use [card verification](/braintree/docs/guides/credit-cards/server-side#card-verification) as well as AVS and CVV rules to protect yourself against fraudulent transactions. Read more about how to [enable AVS and CVV rules](/braintree/articles/guides/fraud-tools/basic/avs-cvv-rules) in our Support articles.
