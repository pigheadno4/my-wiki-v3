# Guides Overview

The guides are designed to help get you familiarized with the bare necessities, giving you a quick and clean approach to getting up and running. If you’re looking for ways to customize your workflow, or just enjoy breaking things down to build them back up, head to the [reference](/braintree/docs/reference/overview) section to dig into the mechanics of our API.

Our guides are split into these sections:




## Basics

These guides are designed to help you familiarize yourself with the fundamental components the API offers and how to use them.


## Payment Method Types

This section covers all of the forms of payment our gateway can process, and what to do to accept them.


## Tools

This section covers the tools that we created to make payment processing both easier to manage and more secure.


## Additional Features

Ready for more? The Additional Features guides will introduce you to the other products we offer with our gateway, and how they can help you take your payment processing to the next level.
