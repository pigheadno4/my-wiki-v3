# Grow and run your business with reports and analytics

PayPal provides a comprehensive suite of tools to help you grow and manage your business effectively. The Reports & Analytics features give you actionable insights into your business performance, transaction data, and customer behavior. With these tools, you can track business performance, analyze transaction patterns, and generate reports for accounting and business intelligence.

## Overview

PayPal offers multiple ways to access your business data, from simple dashboard downloads to sophisticated API integrations. Choose the method that best fits your technical capabilities and business needs.

## Getting started

To access and use PayPal Reports & Analytics, you need to have:

- An active PayPal Business account with reporting permissions.
- Developer or API credentials if you want to access reports programmatically.
- Appropriate permissions for report access.
- A clear understanding of your business reporting requirements.

Consider these factors when choosing your data access method:

- Technical expertise level
- Data volume requirements
- Automation needs
- Integration complexity

## Available options

### No-code solutions

- **Dashboard Downloads**: Manual report generation through PayPal's web interface
- **Scheduled Reports**: Automated delivery via email or SFTP
- **Basic Analytics**: Built-in dashboard charts and summaries

### Pro-code solutions

- **Transaction Search API**: Real-time programmatic access to transaction data
- **Reporting APIs**: Automated report generation and retrieval
- **Webhook Integration**: Event-driven data processing

## Key features

- **Reports and analytics**: Access detailed business data and insights.
- **Transaction monitoring**: Track all payment activities in real time.
- **Business intelligence**: Make data-driven decisions with historical and current data.
- **Automated reporting**: Schedule and automate report generation and delivery.
- **Flexible formats**: Download reports in CSV, PDF, or JSON formats.
- **API integration**: Integrate reporting features directly into your business systems.

## Best practices

- Start with simple dashboard downloads to understand your data.
- Gradually move to API solutions as your needs grow.
- Plan for data security and compliance requirements.
