# Send, track, and manage payouts to multiple recipients

Payouts helps businesses pay multiple recipients—vendors, contractors, employees, or customers—across regions in various currencies. PayPal offers two payout solutions to meet different business needs, from basic to enterprise-scale.

  <Card title="PayPal Payouts" href="/growth/payouts/overview">
