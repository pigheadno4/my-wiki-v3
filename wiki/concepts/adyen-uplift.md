## Definition

Adyen Uplift is a five-module set of recommendations, experiments, and automation spanning Protect, Tokenize, Authenticate, Optimize, and Personalize. The partner enablement guide distinguishes partner-built integration support, merchant configuration choices, and Adyen-side evaluation or routing. The modules have separate prerequisites and optional or premium features. [[source-adyen-docs-partners-uplift-enablement]]

## Evidence boundary

The captured enablement guide describes intended capabilities and vendor performance aims. It does not establish any merchant's entitlement or configuration, measured conversion or cost change, individual authentication or payment outcome, or bank settlement. Apply each module's exact source and account/flow conditions before making operational claims.
