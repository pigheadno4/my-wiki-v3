## Definition

Adyen's English Japan-market guide, captured 2026-09-15, presents provider-level considerations for domestic payments under the SCTA and PSA and separate in-person PayPay/JCB scheme setup. It says the SCTA section addresses consumer ecommerce in Japan, including sales by a business without a Japanese entity; the PSA section concerns specified digital currencies, in-app coins and payment instruments. The page expressly disclaims legal advice and recommends legal counsel. [[source-adyen-docs-industries-markets-japan-scta-and-psa]]

## Evidence boundary

This is a dated provider summary, not a current law text, an interpretation for a particular seller, proof of merchant-of-record or legal-entity allocation, a complete website-disclosure audit, FSA registration determination, PayPay/JCB store approval or successful payment. The English source refers to Japanese-language disclosures; the linked Japanese page was outside this reviewed raw.
