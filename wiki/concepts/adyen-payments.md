## Definition

Adyen's top-level Payments documentation page is a channel and product directory across online, in-person and omnichannel payments. It links to payment methods, risk, tokenization, reporting, Uplift and an account/integration/go-live checklist. The 2026-09-15 snapshot describes shared-platform and “one integration” positioning; it does not specify a single merchant architecture or guarantee feature availability. [[source-adyen-docs-payments]]

## Evidence boundary

This landing page is navigation. Online website/mobile integrations, POS terminals and Unified Commerce shopper-data views have separate sources and prerequisites. The page does not establish a merchant's method, region or account eligibility, deployed checkout or terminal configuration, customer-identity matching, authorization, capture, settlement or payout.
