## Definition

Adyen's Oracle Simphony guide describes a hospitality POS route where Simphony sends transactions through Oracle Payment Interface (OPI) to an Adyen payment terminal. The 2021-modified page, captured 2026-09-15, says the terminals implement OPI 6.2 and the plugin integrates with Simphony devices compatible with OPI 6.2 or later. A test account and Support-enabled OPI are setup prerequisites. [[source-adyen-docs-plugins-oracle-simphony]]

## Payment routes and boundary

Pay at counter is the default; Pay at table is an optional bill-on-terminal flow with print, split and tip examples. This is Simphony hospitality documentation, distinct from the Oracle Xstore retail POS route. The page does not establish particular terminal-model support, a live merchant setup, or a payment/refund/settlement result.
