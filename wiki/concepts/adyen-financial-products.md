## Definition

Adyen's Financial Products documentation groups four platform-facing offerings for users alongside payment processing: Business accounts for business-related incoming and outgoing third-party transfers, Capital financing offers with volume-linked daily repayment, virtual/physical business debit-card Issuing, and Payouts to accounts outside Adyen. This is an umbrella product family, not a selected architecture or a claim that one platform uses all four.

## Evidence boundary

The 2023-modified landing page captured 2026-09-15 supplies orientation and links, not product mechanics, country eligibility, licensing or legal role allocation, account/funds ownership, merchant-of-record identity, approval, transfer execution or bank receipt. Each child product guide owns its detailed prerequisites and lifecycle. The umbrella Payouts label should not be equated automatically with the separately documented legacy Online Payout API.
