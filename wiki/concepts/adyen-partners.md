## Definition

Adyen's partners documentation is for organizations building integrations, such as plugins, through which their customers can accept Adyen payments. The 2026-09-15 landing-page snapshot routes applicants to a Partnerships-team-reviewed application and describes later Partner Portal access for customer-account and commission operations. It does not grant partnership approval or specify the commercial contract, seller/merchant-of-record identity, account permissions, or money flow. [[source-adyen-docs-partners]]

## Integration route

The landing page points to required versus optional partner-integration components, a separate integration checklist, and development/API resources. The specific technical and permission requirements belong to those linked guides; this overview does not define OAuth scopes, credentials, merchant onboarding or payment outcomes.
