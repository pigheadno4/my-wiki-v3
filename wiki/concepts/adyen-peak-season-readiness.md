## Overview

Adyen’s peak-season readiness documentation is a cross-topic preparation hub for payment-volume spikes. Its dated overview combines Adyen operational-readiness statements with merchant planning routes for online payments, in-person payments, risk and disputes, refunds and payouts, platforms, Giving, and status/help. Seasonal schedules, resources and capacity descriptions must be read as snapshot claims rather than current service commitments.
