## Summary

Klarna network compliance requirements: prohibited business categories, UK FCA branding rules, termination rights, merchant obligations, and Australian DDO requirements.

## Key Details

**Prohibited** (beyond Stripe standard): charities, political organizations, B2B.

**UK FCA branding**: must only use Klarna-approved messaging — failure can result in **criminal charges**. PMME handles compliance automatically.

**Termination**: Klarna can suspend/terminate for prohibited categories OR high dispute rates not promptly remedied.

**Merchant obligations**:
- VAT/payment docs must name Klarna as payee (not merchant bank details)
- No surcharges or unfair treatment
- No confusingly similar designs to Klarna trademarks
- No purchases by financially-interested parties (owners, employees, directors)
- Must follow Stripe instructions to stop shipments for fraud risk

**Australia DDO**: active promoters of Pay in 4 are "distributors" — must follow TMD, report under-18 purchases and harm complaints to Klarna.
