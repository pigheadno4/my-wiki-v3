## Summary

Overview of PayPal's agentic commerce services — AI-powered shopping infrastructure enabling customers to find products, manage carts, and complete purchases through conversational AI assistants. Two main products: Store Sync and Agent Ready.

## Key Takeaways

- **Access is gated**: merchants must complete a form to contact PayPal's AI team and request access
- **Two products**: Store Sync (inbound — make your store discoverable by AI) and Agent Ready (outbound — accept payments via AI assistants like ChatGPT bots)
- **Platform partners**: Wix, Cymbio, BigCommerce, Feedonomics, Shopware
- **Connect once**: both products enable reaching multiple AI platforms through a single PayPal integration
- **VS Code extension**: available on Visual Studio Marketplace for API/code help

## Products

### Store Sync

Opens an existing online store to AI shopping assistants and automated shopping tools:

- **Product catalog integration**: makes products discoverable by AI assistants
- **Cart operations**: AI can place orders directly into existing order management systems
- Merchant retains control of brand appearance and customer communications

### Agent Ready

Minimal-change path to accepting payments through AI assistants:

- Works with existing PayPal tools — no separate connections per AI platform
- PayPal handles security and compatibility across platforms
- Supports both supervised and automatic shopping scenarios
- Example: a store's shopping bot within ChatGPT
