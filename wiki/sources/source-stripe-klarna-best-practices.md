## Summary

Best practices for maximizing Klarna conversion: Express Checkout Element placement, Payment Method Messaging on product pages, sending line items, and optional shipping/billing data for fraud scoring.

## Key Details

- **Express Checkout Element**: Klarna available for one-click checkout — place early in checkout flow.
- **Payment Method Messaging Element**: promote BNPL offers on product detail pages (not just at checkout).
- **Payment line items**: pass cart data to improve conversion and reduce disputes. Checkout passes these automatically.
- **Shipping + billing addresses**: optional but improves Klarna's fraud scoring.
