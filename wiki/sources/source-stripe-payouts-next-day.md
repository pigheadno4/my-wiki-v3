## Summary

Next-day settlement accelerates access to funds to the next business day. US-only, 0.6% monthly fee, excludes ACH direct debits.

## Key Details

- **Availability**: US only (Stripe Dashboard users); Connect platforms contact account team
- **Eligible funds**: Domestic transactions except ACH direct debits
- **Speed**: Next business day
- **Fee**: 0.6% monthly (prior month's accelerated charges, auto-debited at month start)
- **Payout limit**: >$1M automatic payout adds 1 extra day (US banking limitation)
- **Eligibility**: Not available to new users immediately — check payout settings
- **Toggle**: On/off in Dashboard → Payout settings; switch to standard at any time

## vs Instant Payouts

| | Next-day settlement | Instant Payouts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Automatic/continuous liquidity | On-demand/manual |
| Eligible funds | Settled USD (no ACH) | All pending after card charge |
| Speed | Next business day | Within 30 min |
| Activation | Toggle (applies to all eligible) | Manual per payout |
| Fee | 0.6% monthly | Varies |
