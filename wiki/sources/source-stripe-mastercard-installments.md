## Summary

Mastercard Installments is a BNPL product where customers with an Installments virtual card pay in 4 interest-free installments. Auto-enrolled when accepting Mastercard. 7 countries, 7 currencies.

## Key Details

**Auto-enrolled**: no setup needed; automatic when accepting Mastercard.

**Customer experience**: virtual card; 4 interest-free installments; same fees as regular Mastercard; surcharging prohibited.

**Countries**: AE, AU, CA, DE, GB, MY, US. **Currencies**: USD, CAD, GBP, AUD, AED, MYR, EUR.

**Recurring note**: properties show "Yes" but recurring/subscription purchases are actually declined — the virtual card doesn't support them.

**Prohibited MCCs**: money transfer, funding transactions, quasi cash, financial institution merchandise/services, payment transactions, MoneySend.

**Opt-out**: email Stripe Support with "MC Installments Opt Out"; takes 1-3 days.
