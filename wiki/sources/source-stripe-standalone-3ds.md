## Summary

Thin overview of Stripe's Standalone 3DS product — runs EMV 3DS authentication on Stripe while allowing authorization with any PSP.

## Key Facts

- **Decoupling**: authentication on Stripe; authorization with any PSP
- **Output**: 3DS cryptogram → import into Stripe OR submit to any other PSP
- **Availability**: IC+ pricing only; global except Malaysia and Thailand
- **Supported cards**: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, Cartes Bancaires
- **Enterprise use cases**: API-level control, issuer observability, per-transaction customized flows
