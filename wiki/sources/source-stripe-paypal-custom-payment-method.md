## Summary

Thin landing page introducing PayPal as a Stripe Custom Payment Method (CPM). Stripe provides a low-code adapter hosted in the merchant's environment that integrates PayPal natively with Checkout Sessions and hosted Checkout.

## Key Fact

Two PayPal integration paths via Stripe:

- **Standard PayPal PM** — for merchants exclusively processing PayPal in supported European countries
- **PayPal CPM adapter** (this page) — low-code solution for global/non-EU PayPal in Checkout Sessions and hosted Checkout

The adapter is hosted in the merchant's environment and integrates natively with Checkout for efficient orchestration.
