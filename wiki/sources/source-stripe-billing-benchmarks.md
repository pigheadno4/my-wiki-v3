## Summary

Stripe Billing benchmarking lets businesses compare their metrics against similar Stripe businesses. Uses k-NN algorithm for peer matching. Access requires ≥5 active subs; peer group membership requires ≥100 subs.

## Access requirements

- ≥5 active subscriptions
- ≥1 paid subscription in past year

## Peer group algorithm

k-nearest neighbors (k-NN) using: MCC, AI-generated industry, ARR, ARPU, website info.

**Peer group eligibility** (to appear in others' groups): ≥100 active subs, >5 active for ≥1 year, positive ARR.

## Benchmarked metrics (past 12 months)

- Gross and net MRR churn rate
- Subscriber churn rate
- Subscriber and revenue retention
- MRR growth rate
- LTV
- ARPU
- Trial conversion rate

Display: top/median/bottom trends + percentile position.
