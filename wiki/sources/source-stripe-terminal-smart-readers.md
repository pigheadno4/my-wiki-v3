## Summary

Overview of Stripe Terminal's smart readers. Compatible with JS/iOS/Android/React Native SDKs. WisePOS E, S700/S710, and Verifone also support server-driven integration. Communicate with Stripe over the internet.

## Software Updates

- Auto-updates at midnight (location timezone) when connected to power
- Unplug overnight = update may start on next power-on
- Manual update: reboot
- **PCI compliance**: restart every day at midnight; disconnect from POS app every morning
