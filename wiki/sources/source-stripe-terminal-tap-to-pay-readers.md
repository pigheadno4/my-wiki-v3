## Summary

Overview of Stripe Terminal's Tap to Pay readers (commercial off-the-shelf). iOS/Android/React Native SDKs only.

**Country availability** (both ~38 countries with one difference):
- **iPhone**: same as Android but **includes JP, excludes GI**
- **Android**: same as iPhone but **includes GI, excludes JP**

**Updates**: auto-start on POS connection; failing critical update blocks payments.

**Compliance**:
- iPhone: Apple completed MPoC (Mobile Payments on COTS) certification as Monolithic Solution
- Android: MPoC evaluation in progress; prior to MPoC, major payment network security assessments completed
- Stripe: PCI Principal Participating Organization + PCI Board of Advisors member
