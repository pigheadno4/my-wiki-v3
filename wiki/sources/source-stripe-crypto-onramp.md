## Summary

Stripe fiat-to-crypto onramp lets customers purchase and exchange cryptocurrencies directly within a platform or Dapp. Stripe acts as merchant of record, handling fraud liability, KYC, regulatory requirements, and sanctions screening. Customer data (payment methods, KYC, wallet info) saved for faster repeat onramps.

## Three Integration Modes

| Mode | Status | Use Case | Hosting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stripe-hosted | Public preview | No code; redirect to crypto.link.com | Stripe |
| Embedded | Public preview | Embed in website/mobile webview via Onramp API | Your website |
| Embedded components (mobile SDK) | Private preview | Native iOS/Android with full UI customization | Your app |

## Access Requirements

Must submit an onramp application at dashboard.stripe.com/crypto-onramp/get-started. Applications reviewed within 48 hours. Required even for sandbox/testing access.
