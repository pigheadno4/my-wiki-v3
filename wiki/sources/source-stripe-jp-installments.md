## Summary

Japan-specific card installment payment method. Merchant receives full amount; JPY only; JP-issued credit cards only; Japan Stripe accounts only.

## Key Details

**3 plan types**: installments (fixed split), revolving credit, bonus (paid at company bonus).

**Brand support**: Visa/MC (60 installments max, revolving, bonus); JCB (24 max, revolving, bonus); Diners (revolving + bonus only); Amex/Discover (none). Amex stopped Dec 2022.

**Bonus windows**: Summer (Dec 16–Jun 15), Winter (Jul 16–Nov 15). Unavailable Jun 16–Jul 15 and Nov 16–Dec 15. Checkout-to-payment cutoff crossing causes payment failure.

**No merchant fees**; customer may pay interest (not on 2-installment or bonus plans).
