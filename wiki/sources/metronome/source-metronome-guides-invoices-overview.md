## Overview

This overview presents three Metronome invoicing paths: native Stripe invoicing, marketplace invoicing, and ERP invoicing. The options cover Stripe product-suite capabilities, metering and invoice creation for AWS, Azure, and GCP marketplaces, and ERP billing and revenue workflows. Metronome frames them as capabilities an organization can select according to its billing motions, distribution channels, contracting, and revenue processes.

## Key takeaways

- Stripe Invoicing natively integrates Metronome with Stripe and can use Stripe Tax, dunning, and other Stripe product-suite features.
- Marketplace Invoicing automates metering and invoice creation for AWS, Azure, and GCP marketplaces, supports all Metronome charge types, and does not require a third-party integrator.
- ERP Invoicing includes out-of-the-box and custom ERP integrations for collection, book-closing, and revenue workflows.
- The page highlights native entry points for Stripe, marketplaces, and NetSuite.
- The options are not mutually exclusive; Metronome says each capability can be used where it fits the business.

## Details

### Stripe invoicing

Metronome describes its Stripe connection as a native invoicing integration optimized for most billing use cases. The overview explicitly names Stripe Tax and dunning as Stripe product-suite capabilities available through this path.

### Marketplace invoicing

The marketplace integration automates metering and invoice creation for AWS, Azure, and GCP. Metronome states that it supports all Metronome charge types, allowing new pricing plans to be made available through marketplace distribution channels without a third-party integrator.

### ERP invoicing

Metronome offers out-of-the-box and custom ERP integrations for organizations that execute billing or revenue workflows in ERP systems. The page describes transaction synchronization for collection and book closing and mentions ASC 606 guidelines. Its navigation card identifies NetSuite as a native ERP billing and revenue-workflow integration.

### Scope boundary

This is an option overview. It does not document invoice objects, lifecycle states, synchronization mechanics, or setup steps for Stripe, marketplaces, or NetSuite; those details remain in the dedicated integration guides linked by the page.
