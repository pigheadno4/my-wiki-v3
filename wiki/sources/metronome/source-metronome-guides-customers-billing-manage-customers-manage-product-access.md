## Overview

This short Metronome navigation guide frames product access as contract terms and entitlement status, then routes readers to customer provisioning, contract lifecycle, trial, and notification guides. It does not document an API, configuration schema, or application-side enforcement flow.

## Key takeaways

- Metronome says it encodes contract terms defining customer access across packaging models and tracks entitlement status from usage and payment.
- Customer provisioning and contract assignment are the stated basis for encoding entitlements.
- Contract lifecycle changes can reflect changed customer entitlements for renewals, upsells, supersedes, and upgrades.
- The guide identifies trials as a temporary-access model and notifications as a way to communicate entitlement-state changes.

## Documentation boundaries

This page is a navigation overview. It does not define entitlement fields, the timing or mechanics of usage- or payment-based status evaluation, notification delivery, contract-transition semantics, temporary-access configuration, or whether Metronome itself enforces access in the merchant product. Use the linked dedicated guides for those subjects.
