## Overview

This documentation landing page maps Metronome's pricing-and-packaging guides across billing models, pricing changes, credits and commitments, and example implementations. It is a navigation catalog: it identifies the documented areas and routes readers to follow-on guides, but does not specify their APIs, schemas, calculations, or operational behavior.

## Key takeaways

- The billing-model route groups pay-as-you-go, subscriptions, enterprise commits, and hybrid approaches as the pricing-model options covered by the documentation.
- The pricing-change route points to rate updates, scheduled changes, contract modifications, customer-specific overrides, and currency or unit configuration.
- The credits-and-commits route points to targeted usage, prepaid commitments, payment-status-based activation, and application-priority guidance.
- The market-example route names OpenAI, Anthropic, and Databricks as examples to explore; it does not establish their actual commercial terms or Metronome implementation details.

## Navigation versus implementation boundary

The page provides category descriptions and two starting links rather than procedures or technical references. It does not define pricing or contract request fields, rate calculations, scheduling semantics, discount eligibility, commitment behavior, currencies or custom-unit rules, payment-state handling, or the implementation of the named market examples. Use the linked billing-model and market-leader guides, plus their dedicated API references, for those details.
