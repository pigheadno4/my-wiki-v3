## Overview

This Metronome documentation landing page routes readers to introductory material and organizes pricing and packaging guidance around four models: pay as you go, enterprise commitments, subscriptions with usage, and prepaid credits. It also points to contracts, invoicing, and revenue recognition, but leaves implementation mechanics to dedicated guides.

## Key takeaways

- Pay-as-you-go billing is presented as charging customers in arrears only for what they use.
- Enterprise commitments can include prepaid and postpaid commitments, negotiated discounts, one-time charges, and contract renewals.
- Hybrid subscriptions combine recurring revenue with usage-based components.
- Prepaid credits support upfront credit purchases, with auto-recharge or gated access when credits run out.
- The getting-started routes cover how Metronome works, reaching a first invoice, and planning billing architecture.
