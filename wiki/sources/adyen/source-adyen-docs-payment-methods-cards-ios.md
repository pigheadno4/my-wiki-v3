## Overview

Adyen iOS card-payment integration chooser. The 2026-09-15 snapshot offers three navigation routes: Drop-in, prebuilt Components, and a custom UI that collects and encrypts shopper card details with Adyen classes. The page itself gives no integration prerequisites, API/SDK minimum, PCI determination, card-data-handling procedure or payment result; consult the selected implementation guide for those facts.

## Key takeaways

- Drop-in is described as an all-in-one iOS card-payment UI; Components as a prebuilt iOS card Component; custom integration as a merchant-built UI collecting card details and using Adyen classes to encrypt them (raw lines 19–31). These are route labels and brief descriptions, not proof of a completed or safe integration.
- The final line points to general iOS Drop-in and Components build-your-integration guides (raw line 33). The chooser neither establishes current method availability nor authorizes live card collection, tokenization, authentication, authorization, capture or settlement.
