## Overview

This [[adyen]] **e315** power-control page was last modified **2019-04-18** and captured 2026-09-15 under the **terminal-deprecation** manual tree. It gives a device-specific iPod/iPhone-coupled power-on sequence and a separate power-off sequence. Powering a unit on or off is not evidence of boarding, network reachability, current e315 transaction support or a processed payment. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]] and [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation]].

## Key takeaways

- To turn the **e315 on**, the page says to connect an **iPod or iPhone** to the e315, ensure **both are fully charged**, then turn on the iOS device, which automatically turns on the e315. This is conditional on the described accessory pairing; it is not a general statement about all e315 configurations. See raw lines 18–21.
- To turn it **off**, the page says to turn off the iOS device, **disconnect the device from the power source before turning off**, then hold the **pictured e315 key** for **more than five seconds**. It says repeated beeps continue until power-down begins. The text does not identify the pictured key by a name; consult its linked image/raw page rather than assigning a button label. See raw lines 23–31.
- The related charging guide says **not** to insert an iPod/iPhone during the initial eight-hour e315 charge and to fully charge the e315 before using its USB power to charge the accessory. That initial-charging condition precedes this page's fully-charged, connected power-on sequence; do not collapse them into one step. See [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation-e315-user-manual-charge-the-e315]].
- The parent eVo deprecation page lists a historical **29 March 2024 “No more transactions”** date for e315. This 2019 power procedure does not establish that an e315 can now process payments. See [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation]].
