## Overview

This Issuing-path reference (last modified and captured 2026-09-15) says Adyen assigned brand-specific Industry Codes (ICs) to represent unique Merchant Category Codes (MCCs) for certain brands. It directs selection of the IC that best fits the user's brand and business activity. This is classification guidance, not an onboarding approval, verified capability, issued-card state or actual brand-to-code mapping. [[adyen]] [[adyen-issuing]]
