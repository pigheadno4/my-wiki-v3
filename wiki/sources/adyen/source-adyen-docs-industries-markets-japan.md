## Overview

This Adyen **Japan-market orientation page** says domestic payment processing in Japan requires attention to Japanese regulations. It names the **Specified Commercial Transactions Act (SCTA)**, **Payment Services Act (PSA)**, and **Credit Card Security Guidelines**, then routes readers to separate compliance guides. It also links pages on Japanese transaction descriptions and in-person or online installments. The snapshot is a navigation entry, not a legal interpretation, compliance checklist, account approval, method eligibility determination, or payment result.

## Key takeaways

- The SCTA/PSA child route is described as consumer-protection regulation information for payments in Japan; the Credit Card Security Guidelines child route is described as guidance for Japanese credit-card payments. This page does not itself give their requirements, thresholds, merchant-role allocations, implementation steps, or proof of compliance.
- The additional transaction-description and installment links are adjacent Japan-market resources, not statements that every merchant or shopper can use those features. Their specific terms, versions and market conditions require reading those guides.
- Nothing on this landing page establishes that a named business may process domestic payments, that a particular legal entity is merchant of record, or that an authorization, capture, payout or settlement happened.
