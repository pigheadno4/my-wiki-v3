## Overview

This 2026-09-15 [[adyen]] Marketplace-path compliance-guideline page describes an **ORIAS intermediary-registration route** for a platform incorporated and registered in France that offers Adyen financial products to French users. The page expressly says it is **not legal advice**. Its parent Marketplace compliance matrix assigns the France topic to Business accounts, Capital and Issuing; this page is historical provider guidance, not a current legal determination, registration proof or product authorization. [[adyen-financial-products]]

## Key takeaways

- The page states a **conjunctive** condition: offering Adyen to French users **and** platform incorporation/registration in France. Its requirements table specifies an Adyen for Platforms integration offering financial products and a French-user limit. Do not generalize this route to every platform with a French customer or to every payout/payment-processing use case. See raw lines 19–36.
- Its ORIAS path calls for proof of professional capacity for **at least one director** through specified recent relevant experience, diploma or approved training. It also lists disqualifying criminal and disciplinary conditions. The exact experience, certification categories and legal references are dated source details to verify before any real filing. See raw lines 38–57.
- The guide maps NAF codes to Level I/II IOBSP registrations, with Level III when those codes do not apply. It describes validation as **usually** under two months and annual renewal; neither timing nor acceptance is guaranteed for a particular applicant. See raw lines 59–67.
- Its procedure calls for a kbis extract less than three months old, director-capacity proof, an ORIAS legal-person account and IOB declarations. The applicant provides SIREN/legal name so Adyen can issue an **Attestation de Mandat**; after receipt, the applicant uploads it and pays fees. The specified “no funds entrusted” declaration is a registration step in this guide, not evidence of a platform's actual fund custody or money flow. See raw lines 69–96.
- **After ORIAS email approval**, the guide instructs adding its French intermediary disclaimer to the website and renewing registration yearly. The placeholder text in the raw must not be treated as a completed registration or publishable legal statement. See raw lines 98–102.
