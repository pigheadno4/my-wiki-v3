## Overview

This [[adyen]] VX675 manual page, last modified in 2019 and captured 2026-09-15, specifies receipt-paper dimensions and the physical steps to install or replace a roll in that terminal's printer. The page says that without receipt paper the VX675 could not accept payments. That is a historical operating instruction, not a claim that adding paper makes this retired model transaction-capable today. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]].

The separate [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation]] schedule lists VX675 with **29 March 2024** under “No more transactions” (that page's raw lines 32–34). This later cutoff limits the old manual's payment wording; a loaded printer does not establish current support, certification, successful payment or receipt production.

## Key takeaways

- **VX675 paper specification:** The page gives 57 mm width × 25 mm diameter, 12 mm core and 50-foot roll length. Those are device-specific manual dimensions; see pinned raw lines 20–24.
- **Old paper-stock condition:** The manual says to keep enough receipt paper because it describes payments as unavailable without it. It gives no independent evidence of present device eligibility or a completed transaction. See pinned raw line 26 and the separate retirement schedule above.
- **Loading sequence:** Release the top latch, lift the printer cover, remove any partial roll, loosen or remove the new roll's glued edge/protective strip and unwind past glue residue. Position the roll so paper feeds from the bottom when the terminal is inverted, place it in the tray, pull paper past the residue, close the cover until it clicks with some paper extending outside, then tear against the serrated strip. The pinned raw contains images for the latch, placement and cover. See pinned raw lines 28–52.
