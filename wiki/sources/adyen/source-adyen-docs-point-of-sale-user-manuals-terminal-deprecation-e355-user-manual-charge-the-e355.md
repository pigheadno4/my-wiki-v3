## Overview

This [[adyen]] e355 charging page was last modified 2019-04-23 and captured 2026-09-15 under the terminal-deprecation manual tree. It gives a short physical charging procedure for the e355 before use. It does not establish a particular device's battery condition, charging success or present-day transaction support. The separate [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation]] records a historical 29 March 2024 "No more transactions" date for e355. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]].

## Key takeaways

- The page instructs charging the e355 before use with the provided Micro-USB cable. Its two steps are to connect the charger to a wall outlet or powered surge protector, then insert the Micro-USB cable into the port on the side of the e355. See raw lines 15–22.
- This is a dated device instruction, not a battery-health test, charger certification statement, or evidence that boarding, security inspection, connectivity or a payment succeeded. The e355 manual hub describes those other setup steps separately: [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation-e355-user-manual]].
