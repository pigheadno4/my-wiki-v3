## Overview

This 2026-09-15 Adyen Capital compliance-guideline page describes ORIAS intermediary-registration steps for a platform incorporated and registered in France that offers Adyen Capital to French users. It expressly says it is not legal advice. It is a dated provider instruction, not a determination of current French law, a particular platform's registration status, approval to market Capital or proof of a financed grant. See [[adyen]], [[adyen-capital]], and [[source-adyen-docs-capital-compliance-guidelines]].

## Key takeaways

- The stated applicability is conjunctive: the platform offers Adyen Capital to French users **and** is incorporated and registered in France; the Requirements table also identifies an Adyen for Platforms integration offering financial products and limits this page to that France/French-user case (raw lines 19–36). Do not apply it solely because a user is French, or globally across Capital.
- As dated Adyen guidance, the page describes ORIAS registration and professional-capacity proof for at least one director through specified recent experience, a relevant diploma or approved training; it also lists conviction and disciplinary exclusions (raw lines 27, 38–57). These conditions require present legal/Adyen confirmation before use in an actual registration.
- The page maps IOBSP Levels I/II/III to listed NAF codes and says ORIAS validation usually takes under two months, then annual renewal is required. The timing is an estimate, not a service commitment or evidence of acceptance (raw lines 59–67).
- The procedural sequence calls for a recent kbis extract, director capacity evidence, an ORIAS legal-person account and IOB subcategory, declarations including no funds entrusted and Capital as ancillary activity, an Adyen Attestation de Mandat obtained after giving SIREN/legal name, ORIAS upload and fees. After email confirmation of ORIAS approval, the page instructs a specific website disclaimer and annual renewal (raw lines 69–102). The declarations are registration steps in the source, not independently verified money-flow or merchant-of-record facts.
