## Overview

This Adyen reporting page is a navigation hub for platform and marketplace reports. It says Adyen-generated platform reports help check balances, reconcile payments and fees, and track transfers, and that platforms use them in addition to payment-processing reports. The page separates new Adyen for Platforms documentation from classic integration documentation; it does not define report columns, eligibility, reconciliation procedures, or a merchant’s financial outcome. See [[adyen-reporting]].

## Key takeaways

- The new Adyen for Platforms route links to reports and fee types, report preparation, common reconciliation processes, the **Balance Platform Accounting Report**, and the **Balance Platform Balance Report**.
- The classic integration route instead links to a reporting overview, **Marketplace payments accounting report**, **Marketpay balance report**, and **Marketpay payout report**. The hub’s distinction is integration-specific; names alone do not establish field definitions or interchangeable report grain.
- The raw page is a 2026-09-15 capture of a page marked last modified that day. Its links are reference paths only. A balance, transfer, payment or payout report route is not evidence of account access, completed reconciliation, available funds, final settlement, or bank receipt.
