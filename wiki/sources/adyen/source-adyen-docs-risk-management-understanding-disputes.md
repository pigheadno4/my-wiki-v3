## Overview

This Adyen Docs page is a short entry point to issuer-raised payment disputes and the documents, reason codes, scheme guidelines, and timeframes used to manage them. It describes a cardholder contacting the issuer to initiate a chargeback, after which the issuing bank raises a dispute and Adyen communicates it to the merchant. See [[adyen]] and [[adyen-risk-management]].

The page groups disputes as fraud, consumer disputes, processing errors, or authorization issues. It says disputability depends on payment method, giving credit cards as a disputable example and bank transfers as a non-disputable example. These are this page's examples, not a complete rule for every bank-based payment or return process.

## Key takeaways

- A merchant can enable **Chargeback notices** in Customer Area to receive dispute notifications. This is an optional notification setting in this snapshot; it does not decide the dispute or replace the defense process.
- Before managing a dispute, the page routes readers to the dispute flow, defense-document requirements, issuer chargeback guidelines, reason codes, and submission timeframes, then to the manage-disputes guide. It gives no specific defense deadline, evidence set, eligibility decision, or outcome; follow the relevant linked guide for those details.
- This is a navigation and definition page, not evidence that a particular chargeback was received, defended, reversed, or finally resolved.
