## Overview

This 2026-09-15 Adyen guide offers **in-person payment planning recommendations** for merchants anticipating international visitors during major soccer tournaments; it says much of the advice applies to other international sporting events. It does not name or verify a particular event date, location, merchant, venue, device fleet, account configuration or payment outcome. Plan and test ahead; avoid major changes immediately before the tournament. See [[adyen]] and [[adyen-payment-terminals]].

## Key takeaways

- Consider adding QR wallets such as Alipay, WeChat Pay and Alipay+, offering Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC), and reviewing international card-scheme acceptance. The guide directs merchants to add methods to their account, learn the flows and **test QR payments before going live**. Its currency count and method descriptions are dated provider claims, not proof of current market, account or terminal availability.
- The guide describes card-based terminal display-language selection, conditional tax-free shopping with a provider for eligible visitors, and tip prompts where relevant. Eligibility, tax treatment, language coverage and merchant configuration require their own checks; these examples do not establish a requirement for every venue.
- For peak traffic, Adyen recommends Ethernet **if possible**. Cellular failover applies to terminals **with built-in cellular connectivity** when Wi-Fi or Ethernet fails. Offline payments are a suggested backup with explicit risks and limits; the linked offline-payment guide carries details. None of these suggestions proves a particular payment stayed connected, was authorized or settled.
- Hardware preparation includes ordering terminals early, testing backups, checking supported software and charging portable batteries. The regional-endpoint advice applies **only to Terminal API integrations with cloud communications**.
- Contactless, autonomous stores, pay-at-table/seat, Giving and hotel pre-authorizations are venue-specific options to consider. The guide also recommends reviewing risk profiles, arranging sufficient refund reserves and keeping status/support contacts handy. These are planning prompts, not evidence of enabled products, guaranteed queue performance or a successful live event.
