## Overview

This 2024-modified page, captured 2026-09-15, sets out Adyen's Business Accounts service-record guidance for a platform offering accounts to users. It says the platform must keep detailed records while offering Business Accounts and for at least five years after termination of the Business Accounts offering, unless the platform's internal data-retention requirements call for longer. The page expressly disclaims legal advice. Its retention trigger is the offering's termination, not a particular user's account closure or an individual transaction date.

## Key takeaways

- The example record categories are user experience (including deployed-version screenshots and application, dashboard and support flows), all versions of described marketing materials, user communications and complaints, and historical statements generated and made available to users. The communications row also mentions loan/charge-card notices; do not infer from that example that every Business Accounts program includes those products.
- The communications/complaints row adds UK-and-Ireland-only vulnerable-customer wording. It says recording systems **must** be able to categorize users showing signs of vulnerability during an interaction and **should** include at least health, life events, resilience and capability options. It also says communications from a vulnerable user must be accessible, in line with legal requirements, to staff responsible for assisting users. The captured table formatting runs this regional subsection into other content, so inspect raw lines 29–32 for exact scope and wording.
- This dated compliance guideline is neither a current legal retention determination nor evidence that a platform retained a record, made a statement available, notified a user, or provided accessible support. Linked compliance, UI, statement, marketing, support and complaint guides are navigation only here.
