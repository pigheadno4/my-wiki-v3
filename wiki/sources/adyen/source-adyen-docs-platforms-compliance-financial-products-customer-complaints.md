## Overview

This 2024-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] for Platforms financial-products compliance-guideline page describes how a platform offering an Adyen financial service identifies, handles, records and escalates user complaints, including added UK and Ireland provisions. It expressly says it is not legal advice. Use [[adyen-financial-products]] for the product-family route; this historical snapshot is not a current jurisdiction-specific legal determination or proof that a platform's process complies.

## Key takeaways

- The page defines a complaint by substance: dissatisfaction with Adyen products, services, terms, communications or actions when a response or resolution is expected, whether oral or written and regardless of the word “complaint.” Routine information, technical assistance or fee-refund/chargeback requests are listed as noncomplaints only until they allege unfair treatment or express dissatisfaction. [Raw: lines 19–33]
- Platforms offering an Adyen financial service must operate accessible support and identify complaints across available channels. A complaint form may be required, with a link supplied in the channel where the user complained. The process includes acknowledgement, a stated resolution deadline, investigation and updates, with Adyen involved in regulatory/legal complaints. Adyen's stated general expectations are acknowledgement within five business days, resolution within ten business days from submission, and notice if resolution takes longer. [Raw: lines 35–59]
- The platform must retain support-inquiry and complaint response records for five years from both Adyen Service termination and user-account closure, unless its own retention rule requires longer, in a durable accessible form. [Raw: lines 61–63]
- Security and regulatory/legal complaints are to be reported to Adyen within one to two working days. The separate escalation procedure says to supply information as soon as possible and no later than two business days after receipt, answer Adyen's further-information request within two business days, and deliver Adyen's prepared final response to the user within guideline timelines. Preserve these as the page's stated timelines, including its differing “working” and “business” wording. [Raw: lines 65–84, 102–107]
- The escalation table allocates direct legal-action response to Adyen while the platform normally handles the complaint aspect; Adyen responds directly on data privacy and to regulators/ombudsmen on regulatory matters, while the platform handles the remaining resolution unless Adyen input is required. Financial-crime and technical-issue handling/resolution are assigned to the platform in that table. Category and role details must be checked in the raw table, not inferred from a topic name alone. [Raw: lines 86–100]
- For customers in the UK and Ireland, the captured page adds acknowledgement contents (ombudsman rights/contact and a complaint contact), notice of decision and reasons within five working days after the investigation concludes, investigation updates at no more than 15-business-day intervals, and a notice with anticipated timeframe and ombudsman referral after 35 working days unresolved. It also says the platform must answer Adyen within two business days for a BACS complaint received directly by Adyen. These region-specific historical instructions, including a 15-working-day unresolved reference and a UK six-month ombudsman note, require current legal/product review before operational use. [Raw: lines 109–149]
