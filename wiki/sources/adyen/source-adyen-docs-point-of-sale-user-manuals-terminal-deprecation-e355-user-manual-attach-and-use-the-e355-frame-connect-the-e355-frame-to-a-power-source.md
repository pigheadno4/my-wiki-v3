## Overview

This [[adyen]] e355 frame page was last modified in **2019** and captured 2026-09-15 under terminal-deprecation manuals. It describes connecting external power to the frame so a mounted tablet and e355 device can charge. It is physical power guidance within [[adyen-payment-terminals]], not proof of charging status, battery health, terminal boarding or payment readiness. The separate [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation|eVo/Verix deprecation schedule]] lists **29 March 2024** as the e355 “No more transactions” date.

## Key takeaways

- The page specifies a **Verifone-certified wall-mount charger** connected to a wall outlet or powered surge protector (raw line 23). It gives no substitute-adapter rating, certification process or electrical troubleshooting procedure.
- Insert the charger's barrel connector into the port at the bottom of the e355 frame's front panel (raw line 24). The page states this external-power arrangement is to charge the tablet and e355 device (raw lines 17–21).
- These two steps do not report an LED result, successful charge, Bluetooth connection or processing outcome. A powered frame cannot be treated as evidence of current e355 transaction support.
