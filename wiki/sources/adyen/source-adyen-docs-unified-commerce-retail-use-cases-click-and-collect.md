## Overview

This 2026-09-15 Adyen retail landing page defines **click and collect** as a shopper ordering or buying products online and collecting them at a physical store. It routes readers to three variants: reserve online and try in store before buying; buy online and pick up, with possible item removal/refund or additional in-store purchase; and buy online for an assistant to pick up. The page is a scenario index, not a payment-integration procedure.

## Evidence boundary

The landing page's labels describe merchant order and fulfillment possibilities. It does not establish when any payment is authorized or captured, how a refund or added purchase is executed, which merchant account or legal entity processes it, who is merchant of record, or where funds settle. Use each linked scenario's reviewed source for its own conditions and financial flow; do not import those details into this landing entry.

## Scenario routes

- [[source-adyen-docs-unified-commerce-retail-use-cases-click-and-collect-c-and-c-try-then-buy]] — reserve online, try in store before buying.
- [[source-adyen-docs-unified-commerce-retail-use-cases-click-and-collect-c-and-c-add-return]] — buy online, pick up in store, with possible order changes and additional store purchase.
- [[source-adyen-docs-unified-commerce-retail-use-cases-click-and-collect-c-and-c-assistant-pickup]] — buy online and have an assistant collect the order.
