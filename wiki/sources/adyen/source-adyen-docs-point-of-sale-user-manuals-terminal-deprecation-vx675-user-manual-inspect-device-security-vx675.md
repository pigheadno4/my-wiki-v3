## Overview

This [[adyen]] VX675 manual page gives staff a visual device-integrity and tamper-inspection checklist for a physical terminal handling sensitive card data. It was last modified 2019-04-18 and captured 2026-09-15 under terminal deprecation. The page's general statement about secure, certified terminals is a dated claim, not proof of current VX675 PCI approval or a particular device's integrity. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]].

## Key takeaways

- Staff should compare the device with manual pictures and check that the Adyen or company logo appears at power-on; they should not use a damaged or sticker-covered device. See raw lines 20–26.
- Before third parties modify or troubleshoot a device, staff should verify their identity. The checklist also calls for regular checks for cameras/skimming equipment, verification before device installation, replacement or return, awareness of suspicious activity, and reporting suspected tampering or substitution to appropriate personnel. See raw lines 28–36.
- Staff should avoid seeing customers' PINs on a handheld device without a privacy shield. Perform visual integrity inspections weekly. **When in doubt, stop using the device and contact Adyen's Support Team**; the raw links a historical Support URL. See raw lines 38–40.
- The related [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation]] overview lists VX675 under PTS PCI 3 and gives **29 March 2024** as “No more transactions” (that overview's raw lines 32–35). This inspection checklist does not override that later cutoff or establish present certification, safe operation, authorization or settlement.
