## Overview

This [[adyen]] **e355** physical-terminal manual hub was last modified **2021-03-25** and captured 2026-09-15 under the terminal-deprecation tree. It states that new e355 terminals could no longer be ordered and routes readers to charging, device security inspection, Wi-Fi connection and boarding. It is a historical directory, not an operational endorsement of current e355 use. Its parent [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation]] lists e355 among terminals with a **29 March 2024 “No more transactions”** date. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]].

## Key takeaways

- The quickstart sequence names **charging**, a linked **device security inspection**, linked **wireless-network connection**, then linked **boarding**. This hub does not give the child procedures or show that any step was completed on a particular device; inspect the linked guides for their conditions and failure handling. See raw lines 23–30.
- The page's “ready to process payments” sentence follows the quickstart list, but predates its parent deprecation cutoff. Do not treat completion of the list, a network connection, or a boarded status as proof of present-day transaction acceptance, authorization or settlement. See raw lines 32 and 19 plus the parent deprecation source.
- The listed provided components are an **e355 payment terminal** and **USB-to-Micro-USB cable**. The hub says to integrate with the Adyen POS App or one's own POS app, without defining API, mobile-OS, credential or account prerequisites here. See raw lines 34–39.
- The same snapshot says ordering new e355 terminals was not possible, yet its generic quickstart introduction refers to “your new terminal.” Read that phrase as template wording for an existing device, not an assertion that new ordering remained open. See raw lines 19 and 23–25.
