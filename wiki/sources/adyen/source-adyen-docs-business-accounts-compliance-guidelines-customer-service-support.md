## Overview

This [[adyen]] Business Accounts-route compliance-guideline page, last modified 2024-01-10 and captured 2026-09-15, assigns the platform first- and second-line customer support and the customer-communication contact role with Adyen. Its body speaks about financial products and Adyen for Platforms generally, with an additional UK/Ireland vulnerable-customer section. It expressly says the information is not legal advice; it does not prove present compliance or actual support performance. See [[adyen-business-accounts]].

## Key takeaways

- The platform must give customers a way to submit support requests and obtain resolution support, and act as Adyen's contact for customer communications. First line handles intake and simple solutions or escalation; second line covers more complex issues. The guide calls for adequate surge capacity, severity prioritization, relevant case numbers, clear case status, expected-delay notices and centrally available information for subsequent advisors. [Raw: lines 19–39]
- Support guidance and channels should be easy to find and navigate. The page calls for multiple communicated channels and live support suited to the user base, human touchpoints when a digital/chatbot interaction reveals complex needs or a merchant requests them, reasonable support during outages and other incidents, no barriers to inquiries or complaints, and channels available free of charge. [Raw: lines 40–46]
- For possible unauthorized use, fraud, or loss/theft of security credentials or a card, the platform must enable a **24/7 ability to raise** a query or notification. The passage does not promise a continuously staffed response or resolution deadline. Front-line training is phrased as `should`, quality monitoring as `required`, and support-query/response records as `requested`; do not flatten these modal differences. [Raw: lines 47–50]
- The recordkeeping row requests records of all support queries and responses for five years **from the date of termination of the Adyen Service and the user's account closure**, unless the platform's own retention rules require longer. The source uses that combined trigger without defining how to resolve differing dates; do not substitute an account-opening or individual-query date. [Raw: line 50]
- The section explicitly headed **UK and Ireland** asks for reasonable assistance to customers in vulnerable circumstances, staff training, a prominent UI notice with standard support link/phone/email that lets users share needs at their pace, human touchpoints with digital channels, and assistive-technology-compatible information. It is region-scoped historical guidance, not a global or implemented-accessibility claim. [Raw: lines 52–62]
