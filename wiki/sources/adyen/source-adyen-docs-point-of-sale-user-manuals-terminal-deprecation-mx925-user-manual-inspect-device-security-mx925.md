## Overview

This 2019-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] MX925 manual page instructs staff to inspect a physical payment terminal for substitution, damage, overlays and suspicious activity. It sits under the terminal-deprecation manual path, so its procedure is historical MX925 guidance; it does not establish present device support, certification status, payment safety or current cutoff dates. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]].

## Key takeaways

- **Routine inspection:** Staff should compare the device with manual pictures and check for the Adyen or company logo after power-on. The page calls for regular checks for cameras and skimming equipment and visual integrity inspections weekly. These are physical checks, not proof that a device is uncompromised. See pinned raw lines 20–30 and 40.
- **Stop use and escalation:** Do not use a damaged terminal or one covered with a sticker. When in doubt, stop using the device and contact Adyen Support. Staff should report suspicious behavior or signs of tampering/substitution to appropriate personnel such as a manager or security officer. See pinned raw lines 26, 34–40.
- **People and custody:** Verify the identity of purported third-party repair/maintenance personnel before access to modify or troubleshoot. Do not install, replace or return devices without verification; watch for unknown people unplugging/opening devices, and avoid seeing customers' PINs on a handheld device without a privacy shield. See pinned raw lines 28–38.
