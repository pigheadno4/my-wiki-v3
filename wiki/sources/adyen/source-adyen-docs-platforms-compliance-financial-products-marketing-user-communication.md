## Overview

This Adyen for Platforms financial-products page (last modified 2023-12-14; captured 2026-09-15) describes Adyen's review process and communication principles for platform collateral about its financial-product offerings. The page expressly says it is not legal advice. It covers user-facing material in the platform interface and wider marketing, with separate dated matrices for Issuing, Business accounts and Capital by country/region. It does not establish current legal compliance, permission to publish, product availability or a platform's approval. See [[adyen]], [[adyen-financial-products]] and [[adyen-platforms]].

## Key takeaways

- **Collateral and review:** The page's collateral scope includes platform webpages and application flows, dashboards, support pages, user statements, ads, mail, press, card packaging/proofs and app-store descriptions/screenshots. It says material must follow product principles and applicable bank disclosures. For financial products it requires Adyen review and pre-approval before publishing or distributing; substantive changed material and additional post-go-live material are to be submitted for approval. The page excludes typographical/grammatical changes and changes that do not affect product/service description or marketing from resubmission. A previously approved asset does not automatically approve a changed offer or new placement.
- **General presentation:** Guidance calls for plain, audience-appropriate language; clear benefits, limits, availability and risks; substantiated, dated claims and fair comparisons; real attributed testimonials with written permission and paid-testimonial disclosure; legible, accessible disclosures that are not buried. It cautions against absolute, misleading or unqualified terms such as free, pre-approved, guaranteed, protected and secure, and against implying a credit check is required. Use the raw's General Principles section for exact wording and layout details.
- **Jurisdiction and product boundaries:** The captured page has UK and Ireland service-provider disclosure examples and separate Issuing (EU/UK/US), Business accounts (EU/UK/US), and Capital (EU/US/AU/UK/CA) review tables. Issuing rows address card/network naming, required issuing statements, scheme-image rules and prepaid/business-purpose distinctions. Business-account rows address avoiding platform-as-bank claims and region-specific account/deposit/e-money disclosures. Capital rows identify different Adyen provider entities and disclosures by country, including business-purpose and credit-approval qualifications. The row checkmarks are dated Adyen review guidance, not a universal current rule or product eligibility finding.
- **Approval boundary:** The guide points to Adyen contact for non-legal clarification and a stated submission route for collateral review. Its examples and disclosure text should be checked against current Adyen instructions and qualified counsel before a real campaign; this wiki entry does not certify collateral or authorize sending it.
