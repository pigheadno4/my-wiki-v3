## Overview

This 2026-09-15 Adyen overview describes **NaverPay**, an external digital wallet used in South Korea that links to a shopper's credit card, as an online **redirect** payment method. It gives a dated **KR/KRW** capability row and links to separate Web and API-only integration guides. The page's popularity statement is a snapshot claim, not current market evidence; the overview does not establish merchant eligibility or a payment outcome. [[adyen]] [[adyen-online-payments]]

- The matrix scopes this method as **Wallet / redirect / KR / KRW**. It marks **refunds, partial refunds and multiple partial refunds** with checkmarks, and **recurring, separate captures, partial captures, multiple partial captures and chargebacks** with X. These are overview marks, not proof that a particular refund was executed or that all merchant/issuer conditions are met. (raw lines 23–27)
