## Overview

This Adyen for Platforms financial-products compliance-guideline page, last modified 2024-01-10 and captured 2026-09-15, describes platform disclosures of user pricing, fee changes, product-specific fee limits, and eligibility criteria. It expressly is not legal advice or a current tariff, approved pricing model, country eligibility decision, or offer. See [[adyen]], [[adyen-platforms]], and [[adyen-financial-products]].

## Key takeaways

- Under this dated Adyen guideline, the platform's own terms of service and fee schedule must clearly outline the fees and terms it implements alongside Adyen-supplied agreements; this is the source's instruction, not a current legal conclusion. The dated guide says fee increases for an Adyen product require affected-user written notice at least 30 days ahead in the EU and US, versus 60 days in the UK (raw lines 19–32). These are country-qualified guideline periods, not proof of current law or actual notice.
- For Adyen for Platforms, extra platform fees are described as allowed when disclosed before product enrollment; FX cost presentation should make rate and associated fees clear at conversion confirmation and in a periodic statement or UI transaction overview, and costs remain available on user request. UK-only fees must be reasonable/appropriate (raw lines 34–39). This is a disclosure rule, not a live conversion quote or rate.
- For Business accounts and Issuing, the page adds Adyen advance notification/agreement of platform-charged fees and a reasonableness/actual-cost condition; EU/UK Business accounts instant-payment fees may not exceed regular-payment fees. For Capital, the platform may not directly or indirectly charge extra user fees, and discounts need prior written Adyen approval (raw lines 41–54). Product restrictions should not be merged into one generic platform tariff.
- In general, platforms should not add eligibility criteria for Business accounts, Capital or Issuing beyond Adyen's requirements; if they have extra request criteria, the page says submit them to Adyen for review of disclosure. Platform-specific criteria must be disclosed before offering and not discriminatory, unfair or deceptive (raw lines 56–63). This does not mean an additional criterion is automatically approved or any user eligible.
- A UK-only onboarding questionnaire asks for pricing-model and fee detail across Platforms, Business accounts and Issuing, including FX fees, discounts, review and fairness/communication processes (raw lines 65–93). It is an assessment intake, not approved pricing.
