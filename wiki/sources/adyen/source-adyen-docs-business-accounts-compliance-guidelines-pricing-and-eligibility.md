## Overview

This Adyen compliance-guidelines page was last modified 2024-01-10 and captured 2026-09-15. Its body describes pricing and eligibility disclosures for Business Accounts while sharing some rules with Adyen for Platforms, Capital and Issuing. Its metadata and introductory description say Capital, a scope mismatch with the Business Accounts body; use the section labels below for product-specific claims. The page expressly disclaims legal advice and does not establish current regional availability, eligibility approval or compliant pricing for a platform. [[adyen-business-accounts]]

## Key takeaways

- The page says users must receive clear pricing and eligibility explanations before Business Accounts are offered. Platform terms and fee schedules must describe the platform's fees and terms alongside Adyen-supplied agreements. Its dated regional fee-increase notice periods are at least 30 days in the EU/US and 60 days in the UK, in writing to affected users before the increase. See raw lines 19-32.
- The shared Business Accounts and Issuing subsection permits additional platform fees if disclosed before enrollment, requires platform-charged fees/costs to be notified to and agreed with Adyen before enrollment, keeps costs available on user request and says charges must be reasonable, appropriate and tied to actual platform-service costs. Separate Adyen for Platforms FX and UK-only pricing statements have their own scope. See raw lines 34-46.
- For Business Accounts in the EU and UK, the guide says instant payments cannot carry higher fees than regular payments; both must have the same pricing. This is the page's dated product/region rule, not a finding about a particular fee schedule. See raw lines 48-50.
- On eligibility, the page says that, in general, platforms must not apply additional criteria beyond Adyen's for Business Accounts, Capital or Issuing. Separately, if a platform has additional criteria, they should be submitted to Adyen for review of proper user disclosure; platform-specific requirements must be disclosed before offer and must not be discriminatory, unfair or deceptive. See raw lines 52-59.
- The UK-only onboarding pricing questionnaire requests detailed pricing-model, Business Accounts, FX, incentives, review, fairness and customer-communication information. Its rows label which questions apply to Adyen for Platforms, Business Accounts or both; they are assessment questions, not an approved price list. See raw lines 61-82.
