## Overview

This 2026-09-15-captured Issuing page is a short before-payment component index. It says account holders need verification checks before cards can be issued, and Adyen performs those checks against the account holder's legal entity. Onboarding components let users enter and submit information to Adyen within the platform UI. The page lists the **Transfer Instrument** component for bank-account management and verification interaction.

## Key takeaways

- The listed Transfer Instrument component supports adding, updating and deleting bank accounts, instant-verification or document-upload paths, and viewing verification status. This describes available UI functions; it does not establish that any legal entity or bank account has passed verification.
- The page links onward to the Onboarding components and broader component-libraries overview. For the narrower integration steps and conditions, follow the Transfer Instrument guide.
