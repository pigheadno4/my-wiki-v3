## Overview

This 2019-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] VX820 Duet manual page describes how a refund was initiated on the terminal in standalone mode. Its device-specific historical steps belong with [[adyen-payment-terminals]], with broader mode context at [[adyen-standalone-terminals]].

## Key takeaways

- The guide says Adyen enabled refund functionality **per device** for security and required a password for a standalone refund. Its numbered procedure then says to open the Special Payments menu with a pictured key plus **8**, enter a PIN to unlock the device, and enter the refund amount before proceeding like a normal transaction. The page does not explain whether its “password” and “PIN” are the same credential; the pictured key should be checked in the raw image.
- An exclamation icon at the top bar is described as held data that failed to synchronize with Adyen because outgoing internet access was lacking. This is a warning about synchronization, not documented evidence that any refund was accepted, blocked or later completed.
- The steps describe initiating a refund, with no response, final refund status or shopper card-credit evidence. The parent [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation]] table lists VX820 Duet under **“No more transactions” on March 29, 2024**. Thus the old instructions do not establish current VX820 Duet refund availability; per-device enablement and a PIN alone do not override the later cutoff.
