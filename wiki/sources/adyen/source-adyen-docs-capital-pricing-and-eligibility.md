## Overview

This Capital-path pricing and eligibility page (last modified 2024-01-10; captured 2026-09-15) describes dated Adyen for Platforms disclosure guidelines and a separate Capital fee restriction. It says the platform must clearly explain pricing and eligibility requirements, and expressly says the page is not legal advice. These are Adyen guideline statements, not a current tariff, legal certification, approved platform pricing or a financing offer. See [[adyen]], [[adyen-capital]] and [[adyen-financial-products]].

## Key takeaways

- **General Platforms fee guidance versus Capital:** For Adyen for Platforms services, the guide says the platform's terms and fee schedule must outline its service-program fees and terms, user fees must be disclosed in advance of product enrollment, and costs remain available on request. It gives written fee-increase notice periods for affected users: at least 30 days in the EU and US, and at least 60 days in the UK. It separately calls for clear FX cost display at conversion confirmation and in statements/transaction overview, and UK-only reasonable fees. These general provisions do not override the Capital-specific sentence: the platform may not charge users additional fees directly or indirectly for Adyen Capital, and may not discount fees without Adyen's prior written approval. The generic permission to disclose additional platform fees is not permission to add Capital fees.
- **Eligibility and review:** The page says platforms generally must not apply extra eligibility criteria beyond Adyen's requirements for Business accounts, Capital or Issuing. If a platform does have additional criteria for requesting those products, it says they should be sent to Adyen for review of proper user disclosure; it also says platform-specific criteria must be clearly disclosed before offering and must not be discriminatory, unfair or deceptive. The related Capital compliance-guidelines overview states that Capital-request eligibility criteria go to Adyen for approval; neither statement approves extra Capital pricing. This is a process description, not proof a user's offer is available or a platform's criteria were accepted.
- **UK onboarding questionnaire:** The UK-only questionnaire asks about platform/service pricing model, payment-processing and FX fees, discounts, rate-setting, reviews, fairness and communication. Its product column labels the rows Adyen for Platforms; it is not a Capital fee authorization or a current UK tariff.
