## Overview

This 2024-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] for Platforms financial-products compliance-guideline page assigns the platform first- and second-line customer support and an Adyen customer-communications contact role. It describes support-team, channel, critical-query, monitoring and recordkeeping expectations, plus additional UK/Ireland support for customers in vulnerable circumstances. It expressly disclaims legal advice. See [[adyen-financial-products]] for product context; this page is historical guidance, not proof of current legal compliance or actual support performance.

## Key takeaways

- The platform is responsible for first- and second-line support, a way for customers to submit requests and receive resolution support, and serving as Adyen's point of contact for customer communications. First line handles intake/simple issues and determines escalation; second line brings more technical expertise. The captured guidance calls for sufficiently resourced helplines, severity-based prioritization, case numbers where relevant, clear case status, delay notices and centrally accessible customer information. [Raw: lines 19–39]
- Customer support guidance should make channels easy to find and navigate. The platform must offer and communicate multiple support channels, including live support suited to its user base. Digital/chatbot support needs human touchpoints for complex needs or when a merchant asks; reasonable support should remain available during incidents such as outages or cyberattacks. The page rejects barriers to inquiries/complaints and says channels must be available and free of charge. [Raw: lines 40–46]
- The page requires a **24/7 ability to raise** queries or notifications about possible unauthorized use, fraud, or loss/theft of credentials or cards. It does not state a 24/7 staffed-response or resolution guarantee. It also calls for front-line training, support-quality monitoring and records of all support queries/responses for five years from Adyen Service termination and user-account closure unless longer platform retention applies. [Raw: lines 47–50]
- In its UK/Ireland vulnerable-customer section, Adyen calls for reasonable assistance, staff training to recognize/respond to needs, a prominent UI notice with standard support link/phone/email so customers can disclose needs at their pace, human touchpoints alongside digital channels and assistive-technology compatibility. This section has explicit regional scope and should not be treated as a universal legal specification. [Raw: lines 52–62]
- Support inquiries can become complaints based on their substance. The linked customer-complaints guide owns complaint identification, response deadlines and Adyen escalation; this source alone does not establish those timelines. [Raw: lines 40–46; linked complaint guide]
