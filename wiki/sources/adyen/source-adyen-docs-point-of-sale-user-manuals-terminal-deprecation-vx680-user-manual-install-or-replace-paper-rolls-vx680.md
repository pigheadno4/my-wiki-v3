## Overview

This 2019-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] VX680 guide describes installing or replacing a thermal-paper roll for receipt printing. It specifies **57 mm × 35 mm × 12 mm thermal receipt paper** and says the VX680 cannot accept payments without receipt paper. That is the historical guide's operating statement, not proof that a loaded terminal is currently eligible or able to process a payment: the separately captured [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation|eVo deprecation table]] lists VX680 under **29 March 2024 "No more transactions"**. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]].

## Key takeaways

- **Paper and handling:** Use the stated 57 × 35 × 12 mm thermal roll. The guide directs removal of a partial roll, removal of the new roll's glued edge or strip, and unwinding past glue residue before feeding paper. See pinned raw lines 20–24 and 28–34.
- **Orientation and closure:** With the terminal inverted, the paper should feed from the bottom of the roll. Place it in the tray, leave a small amount of paper beyond the glue residue outside the cover, and press the cover until it clicks shut. See pinned raw lines 35–39. The images and full procedure remain in the pinned raw.
- **Outcome boundary:** Installing paper is a physical receipt-printer step. The page states no paper means no payment acceptance in its historical context (raw line 24), but it does not report a print test, terminal repair, boarding, authorization or settlement. The separate deprecation table limits any present-day payment inference.
