## Overview

This Business accounts landing page was last modified 2023-07-06 and captured 2026-09-15. It describes an Adyen for Platforms financial product through which eligible users can make business-related domestic and international transfers and receive payouts directly to an Adyen business account. It labels the feature **in development**, warns that APIs, documentation and processes may change, and routes to onboarding and verification guides. It is not evidence of current availability, an opened account, completed transfer or bank receipt.

## Key takeaways

- The page frames Business accounts as a separate Adyen for Platforms offering implemented through API integration or Hosted Onboarding. Once a user has an account, described uses include paying business suppliers/utilities, receiving business-related third-party transfers, and receiving platform payouts to the Adyen account rather than a third-party bank account. This product is distinct from Capital financing and from a payout to an external bank.
- Eligibility in this snapshot requires the user to be an **organization or sole proprietor** operating in a supported country/region, associated UBOs and individuals to be **tax residents** in supported countries, and additional Business accounts verification information. The country section separately says associated UBOs must **reside** in approved regions. Those words are not identical; retain the page's wording and use the dedicated verification and current account-specific authority before deciding eligibility.
- The dated supported-region description names the United States, mainland United Kingdom, and Eurozone legal entities, with a table of Eurozone countries. It expressly excludes the Channel Islands and says US account onboarding needs state-specific reporting details. The page also maps these to Europe/EEA, UK and US acquiring regions; an acquiring-region label is not itself an eligibility grant or proof of local account features.
- The page routes to getting started, an integration checklist and verification requirements. It does not define account/legal-entity ownership, consent records, exact API roles, capability states, transfer status lifecycle, settlement timings, or current legal protections. Child sources and live account evidence own those questions.
- Its 2023 development warning is material to any use of this snapshot: do not infer that the same API, geography, onboarding flow or user eligibility still applies on 2026-10-18.
