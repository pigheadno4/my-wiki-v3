## Overview

Adyen's brief terminal-customization guide, captured 2026-09-15 and marked last modified 2020-09-25, routes in-person operators to Customer Area settings for device display, receipts, branding, and related configuration. It identifies where to find settings and points to more detailed feature and inheritance guidance; it does not show that a saved setting has reached a physical terminal. ## Key takeaways

- Customer Area exposes many point-of-sale settings that an operator can manage. General customization examples are languages, signature handling, branding on the terminal display or receipt, and terminal receipt printing. The stated path is **Devices > Device settings**, under **Device**, **Device connectivity**, **Payment features**, and **Integrations**.
- Some settings depend on the payment feature, network, or integration type. The page names tipping options, offline-transaction limits, and standalone options as examples and routes to their topic pages for details. Do not treat this short overview as a complete settings or eligibility list.
- The **Next steps** links point to account/terminal settings at different levels and to general feature customization. Their detailed inheritance behavior, credential or Customer Area roles, save procedure, effective timing, and currently available options are not stated on this page; use the linked detailed guides to answer those questions. The linked Customer Area URL is a test-domain example, not evidence of an operator's access or device state.
