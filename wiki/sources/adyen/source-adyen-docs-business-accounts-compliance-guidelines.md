## Overview

This 2022-10-26-modified Adyen page, captured 2026-09-15, is a **Business Accounts compliance-guidelines overview** for platforms or marketplaces offering accounts to users. It maps Adyen's UI, customer communications, support, complaints, pricing, reporting, recordkeeping and conditional French-intermediary topics to detailed child guides. It expressly says it is **not legal advice**; its dated requirements are Adyen guideline descriptions, not a current legal opinion, approval of a platform's offering or proof that any user has an opened account.

## Key takeaways

- The page says a platform offering Business Accounts **must follow** Adyen guidelines for UI, communications and reporting. Its topic table points to specific child guides, while the separate best-practices/templates PDF is described as further guidance on evidence formats, policy drafting and complaint-handling templates. This overview should be used to find those topics; operational detail remains in the linked authority.
- After Adyen approves the financial-product offering, **where pre-approval of the relevant material is required by the Compliance Guidelines**, the platform must submit new financial-product materials for Adyen review before publication/distribution, make requested changes if applicable and resubmit if applicable. A new platform uses its Adyen contact; an existing platform emails the stated merchant-KYC address. The page also says materials must be provided annually, for periodic review and on Adyen's request. The pre-approval condition must not be broadened to every material.
- The table says the UI must not present the platform as a bank and must support Business Account requests, KYC transmission, access to terms, user consent and account information. It adds a UK/EU payee-confirmation notice after payment details and an EU Depositor Information Sheet. These are jurisdiction-qualified rows of a 2022-modified overview, not a present coverage or legal determination.
- Other topic rows cover Adyen-approved account-statement templates and data, Adyen-approved marketing disclosure of Adyen as provider, first/second-line and critical support, complaint acknowledgement/escalation and quarterly reporting, Adyen approval of user fees/credits and eligibility criteria, daily fraud reporting, and records. The table says to retain specified consent, UI, statements and marketing/communication copies for **at least five years after termination of the financial-product offering**. Its France row applies **when** a platform is registered in France and offers Business Accounts to French users; it lists intermediary registration, a website disclaimer and annual renewal.
