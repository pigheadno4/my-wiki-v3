## Overview

This captured Adyen overview describes Online Banking India for online payments. To process it, the page says merchants **must comply with regulations in India and have both a local entity and a local integration in India**; it directs merchants to an account manager or Support for more information. A shopper chooses an issuing bank from a list, then is redirected to that bank's website or mobile app to complete payment. The captured capability table scopes the method to **India (IN) and INR**. These are documentation claims at the captured date, not proof of current merchant eligibility or a completed payment. [[adyen]] [[adyen-online-payments]]

## Key takeaways

- The table describes an **Online Banking redirect** method. It marks refunds, partial refunds, and multiple partial refunds available. It marks recurring, separate captures, partial captures, multiple partial captures, and chargebacks unavailable. See the raw table before applying these feature labels to an integration.
- The page routes website implementers to its **Web** guide for prebuilt UI and to **API only** for a custom UI on website, iOS, or Android. It also links Pay by Link. These are route choices, not an implementation recipe or evidence of transaction success.
- The “See also” links include Online payments, 3D Secure, Capture, and Payment methods; their presence does not make those features applicable to Online Banking India.
