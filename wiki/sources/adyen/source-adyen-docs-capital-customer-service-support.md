## Overview

This 2024-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] Capital-route compliance-guideline page assigns platform first- and second-line customer support and the customer-communications contact role with Adyen. Its body discusses financial products and Adyen for Platforms generally, with additional vulnerable-customer measures explicitly headed UK/Ireland. The page expressly disclaims legal advice; it is guidance, not proof of actual support performance or current compliance.

## Key takeaways

- The platform is responsible for first- and second-line support, giving customers a way to submit requests and obtain resolution support, and acting as Adyen's contact for customer communications. First line intakes/simple issues and routes complex matters; second line adds technical expertise. The page calls for adequate surge capacity, severity prioritization, relevant case numbers, clear case status, delay notices and centrally accessible customer information. [Raw: lines 19–39]
- The captured guidance calls for clear, navigable support information and multiple communicated channels with live support suited to users. Human touchpoints are required where digital/chatbot interactions reveal complex needs or a merchant requests them. Support should remain reasonable during outages/other incidents; channels must avoid barriers to inquiries or complaints and be available free of charge. [Raw: lines 40–46]
- **Critical support:** the platform must enable a 24/7 **ability to raise** queries or notifications about possible unauthorized use, fraud, or loss/theft of credentials or cards. The text does not state a 24/7 staffed response or resolution deadline. It says front-line staff **should** be trained, including vulnerability recognition; support quality monitoring is **required**; and support-query/response records are **requested** for five years from Adyen Service termination and user-account closure unless longer platform retention applies. Preserve these distinct modal verbs rather than turning every item into the same duty. [Raw: lines 47–50]
- The UK/Ireland vulnerable-customer section says Adyen requires reasonable assistance for customers in vulnerable circumstances and training that staff understand. It calls for a prominent UI notice allowing disclosure of support needs at the user's pace, containing standard-channel link, phone and email; human touchpoints alongside digital support; and compatibility with assistive technology such as text-to-speech. These are region-scoped captured instructions, not a claim of global applicability or implemented accessibility. [Raw: lines 52–62]

Use the canonical Capital guide in Related for navigation and verify current links before implementation. [Raw: line 56]
