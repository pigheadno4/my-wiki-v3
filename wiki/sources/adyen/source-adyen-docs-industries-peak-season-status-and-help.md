## Overview

This Adyen peak-season operations page tells merchants where to check platform service status and how to prepare support contacts and issue details. It describes a follow-the-sun support model and 24/7 urgent support, status-page incident information, Customer Area service-status notifications, tickets, and phone support. These are statements in a 2026-09-15 documentation snapshot, not a live status check, incident record, service-level commitment, or proof of a specific payment outcome. ## Key takeaways

- When payments encounter a problem, the page directs merchants to check [Adyen's status page](https://status.adyen.com) first. It says the page shows overall service performance and active/past incidents, often including elevated error rates for a particular payment method or issuer. This captured guide does not itself show whether an incident is active now or identify the cause of a merchant's error.
- Adyen's **system messages** can be received by email or in the Customer Area Notification center after selecting the desired **Service status** alerts in its settings. Merchants should keep their organization's emergency and support contacts current at the applicable Company or Merchant scope.
- The page warns that contact details can be shared with payment partners or appear on shopper billing statements, so it calls for business-specific email and phone details rather than private personally identifiable information.
- A Customer Area support ticket is the primary documented contact route: choose an issue category, provide issue details, and attach relevant screenshots, recordings, or logs. The phone route uses the local support number shown in Customer Area; the page says Support connects 24/7. A ticket or call does not establish incident confirmation, payment repair, or a guaranteed response time.
- Useful issue identifiers depend on the case: merchant account; API/webhook error code; report period, invoice or batch; payout currency; integration type and API/SDK or plugin/partner version; PSP and merchant references and payment method; affected terminal count/software, store ID, or balance-platform/account-holder/transfer IDs. Gather the concrete identifier for the affected event, not an unrelated code or a guessed transaction state.
