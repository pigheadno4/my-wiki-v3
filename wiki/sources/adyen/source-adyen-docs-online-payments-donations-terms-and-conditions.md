## Overview

This Adyen Giving page tells a merchant using Giving to give shoppers the **partner nonprofit's** Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions. It permits presentation that fits the merchant platform, including a separate page or linked document, and points to Giving Component configuration for a message and terms link. The included sample is a fill-in template for a partner nonprofit, not a universal Adyen policy or a completed agreement for a particular campaign. The 2026-09-15 snapshot reports `last_modified` 2023-10-31 and does not establish current legal requirements or a nonprofit's actual approved terms.

## Key takeaways

- The actor addressed is the merchant using Giving: it must make the nonprofit's own Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions available to shoppers. These are the nonprofit's documents, not a statement that Adyen's general terms substitute for them.
- The page allows the merchant to choose presentation style; a separate page or document link is an example. The Giving Component has a configuration to show a message and terms link. The page does not make that Component the only permitted presentation route.
- The optional template calls for partner-specific merchant and nonprofit details and contains illustrative donation, refund, jurisdiction, third-party and privacy-policy language. Its placeholders and sample clauses must be filled or assessed for the actual nonprofit and campaign; do not treat the template's one-off wording, refund limitation, money-flow phrasing or governing-law blanks as verified terms for every Giving transaction.
