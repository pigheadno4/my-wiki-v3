## Overview

This 2026-09-15 [[adyen]] Marketplace page is a **navigation hub** for building reconciliation processes from balance-platform reports. It identifies prerequisite knowledge and routes to first-year tasks, payments, fund transfers, fees and balance verification. Its short descriptions do not themselves define report fields, matching keys or evidence that any reconciliation ran. [[adyen-reporting]]

## Key takeaways

- Before building a process, the page directs readers to understand the payment lifecycle, balance-platform account structure and fund flow; select the relevant reports; configure, download and combine them; and understand fee types. The linked guides own those operational details. See raw lines 21–28.
- The five child routes are **first-year operational tasks**, **reconcile payments** from payment processing to balance-platform accounts, **reconcile fund transfers** using the Balance Platform Accounting Report for cash management, **reconcile fees**, and **verify balances** across the platform. These are navigation descriptions, not completed-workflow claims. See raw lines 30–48.
