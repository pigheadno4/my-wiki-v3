## Overview

This Adyen guide routes a merchant to account-balance reports when closing a fiscal period. It says to use the **Monthly finance report** when the period ends on the last day of a month and the **Daily finance report** otherwise; a balances dashboard is also mentioned for manual bookkeeping or users who do not want a spreadsheet. The page was last modified in 2022 and captured in 2026. It is report-navigation guidance, not accounting advice or proof that a bank received funds. See [[adyen]] and [[adyen-reporting]].

## Key takeaways

- Adyen says its account has several balances recorded in different registers. The merchant must determine which amounts are relevant to its fiscal closing process; this page does not make that accounting determination.
- In the chosen finance report, the **Payment balances** sheet presents tables for account registers beneath a heading with account, included date range and currency. Each register has its own opening and end balance. Journal types, record counts and monetary amounts show movement through the report's date range.
- The separate **Balances** sheet is the route for opening and closing balances. The page says the monthly report shows the month's opening and end balance; for the daily report it specifically describes the opening balance of the report date and the opening balance of the first day of the month. Do not substitute a daily row, report date or opening balance for a verified month-end or bank-deposit outcome.
- This page supplies no generation role, independent bank evidence, financial sign-off procedure or universal fiscal-period treatment. The linked [[source-adyen-docs-reporting-closing-your-fiscal-period-monthly-and-daily-finance-report|Monthly and Daily finance report]] owns report-level field, date, currency and scope detail.
