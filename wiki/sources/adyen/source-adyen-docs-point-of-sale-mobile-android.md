## Overview

Adyen's Android Mobile solutions hub introduces in-person payments on an Android mobile device such as a phone, either on the mobile device or with a card reader. The dated page names Tap to Pay on Android, Card reader on Android, and Android Payments app as its three solution routes. It is an overview and navigation entry, not a device, regional, merchant-eligibility, SDK/API, or payment-result specification. [[adyen]] [[adyen-mobile-pos]]
