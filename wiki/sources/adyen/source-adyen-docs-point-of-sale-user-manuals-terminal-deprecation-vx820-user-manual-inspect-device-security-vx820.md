## Overview

This 2019-modified, 2026-09-15-captured instruction is a **historical VX820 non-Duet** staff visual-inspection checklist for a payment terminal that processes sensitive card data. It does not record an actual inspection or certify a particular device as safe. The separate [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation-vx820-user-manual|VX820 non-Duet manual hub]] distinguishes this device from VX820 Duet, and the separate [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation|terminal deprecation schedule]] lists **29 March 2024** as “No more transactions” for both VX820 variants. The inspection text is therefore not evidence of current transaction support. [[adyen]] [[adyen-payment-terminals]]

## Key takeaways

- Staff are instructed to compare the VX820 with the manual pictures and check for the Adyen or company logo at power-on. These are visual cues, not proof of firmware integrity, certification status, boarding, connectivity, or payment readiness. (raw lines 20–24)
- The checklist says **do not use** a damaged or sticker-covered device; verify the identity of claimed third-party repair/maintenance personnel before allowing modification or troubleshooting; check regularly for cameras and skimming equipment; and do not install, replace, or return devices without verification. (raw lines 26–32)
- Staff should notice suspicious behavior around devices, report suspected tampering or substitution to appropriate personnel, and avoid seeing customers' PIN entry on a handheld device without a privacy shield. (raw lines 34–38)
- Perform visual device-integrity inspections **weekly**. When in doubt, **stop using the device and contact Adyen Support**. The page names no test result, inspection record, replacement authorization, remediation outcome, or renewed payment support. (raw line 40)
