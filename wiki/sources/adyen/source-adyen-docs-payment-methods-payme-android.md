## Overview

This 2019-modified, 2026-09-15-captured PayMe Android landing page lets a reader choose **Drop-in** or **Components** for an Android app. It also links broader Android integration guides. It is navigation: versions, prerequisites, redirect behavior, merchant eligibility and payment outcomes require the respective detailed guides. [[adyen]] [[adyen-online-payments]]

## Key takeaways

- The **Drop-in** link leads to the PayMe Android Drop-in child guide and is described here as an all-in-one UI option. The **Components** link leads to the PayMe Android Component child guide and is described here as a prebuilt Component option. The short descriptions do not supply setup or runtime evidence. (raw lines 19–27)
- The page's final line points to broader Android Drop-in and Components integration guides, both via Sessions-flow URLs. Those links are route labels, not a statement that every PayMe child path supports Sessions or that a payment was completed. (raw line 29)
