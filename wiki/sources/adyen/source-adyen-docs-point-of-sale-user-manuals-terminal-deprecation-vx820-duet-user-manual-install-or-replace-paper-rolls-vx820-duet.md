## Overview

This [[adyen]] **VX820 Duet** manual page, last modified **2019-04-19** and captured 2026-09-15 under terminal deprecation, gives a physical procedure to install or replace thermal paper in the Duet printer for receipts. Its paper size and loading sequence are model-specific historical guidance within [[adyen-payment-terminals]]. The separate [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation|eVo deprecation schedule]] lists **29 March 2024** as VX820 Duet “No more transactions” (that source's raw line 34). Loading paper does not establish current terminal support, boarding, payment acceptance or successful receipt printing.

## Key takeaways

- The specified VX820 Duet stock is **57 mm × 47 mm × 12 mm thermal receipt paper** (raw lines 20–22). The page says that without receipt paper it is **not possible to accept payments** on this device and advises sufficient stock (raw line 26). That is a dated model-guide statement, not a universal rule for other terminals or current VX820 Duet processing.
- For installation, lift the printer cover and paper tray, remove any partial roll, and hold the replacement roll so paper **feeds from the bottom** before pressing it into the tray (raw lines 28–33).
- Pull paper past the roll's **glue residue**, close the cover gently until it clicks while leaving a small amount of paper outside, then tear it against the printer's serrated strip (raw lines 34–36). The page includes image-only illustrations at raw lines 29, 33 and 38; their detail is not converted into extra textual steps.
- The document describes a mechanical receipt-paper procedure only. It reports no printed receipt, terminal connectivity or payment outcome.
