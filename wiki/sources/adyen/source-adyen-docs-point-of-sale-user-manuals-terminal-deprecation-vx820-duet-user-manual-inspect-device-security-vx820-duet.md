## Overview

This [[adyen]] **VX820 Duet** manual page, last modified **2019-04-19** and captured 2026-09-15 under terminal deprecation, gives staff a physical-device integrity inspection checklist. It treats integrity as material because the terminal processes sensitive card data (raw line 16). The page's generic description of “secure, certified” terminals is a historical statement, not evidence that any particular VX820 Duet is currently secure or PCI approved. The separate [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation|eVo deprecation schedule]] lists **29 March 2024** as VX820 Duet “No more transactions” (its raw line 34). Route model-specific inspection through [[adyen-payment-terminals]].

## Key takeaways

- Staff should compare the device with the manual pictures and verify that the Adyen or company logo appears after power-on; the page says **not to use** a device that is damaged or covered with a sticker (raw lines 20–26). A visual match or logo alone does not certify a particular device.
- Verify the identity of third-party repair or maintenance personnel **before** giving them access to modify or troubleshoot a device. Also do not install, replace or return devices without verification (raw lines 28–32).
- Inspect regularly for cameras and skimming equipment, watch for attempts by unknown people to unplug or open devices, and report suspicious behavior or indications of tampering or substitution to appropriate personnel such as a manager or security officer (raw lines 30–36). Staff should not view customers' PINs on a handheld device without a privacy shield (raw line 38).
- Perform **weekly visual integrity inspections**. **When in doubt, stop using the device and contact Adyen Support** (raw line 40). This is an operational escalation instruction, not a statement that inspection alone establishes current device support, certification or payment readiness.
