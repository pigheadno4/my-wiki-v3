## Overview

This [[adyen]] N.V. EU depositor information sheet, modified and captured 2026-09-15 under the Business Accounts compliance-guidelines route, summarizes the Dutch statutory Deposit Guarantee Scheme's stated limit, aggregation, exceptions and reimbursement information. It is disclosure content, not an account-opening procedure, a current legal opinion, or confirmation that a specific deposit, person or product is covered. See [[adyen-business-accounts]] and [[adyen-financial-products]].

## Key takeaways

- The sheet says deposits in Adyen N.V. are protected by the Dutch statutory scheme up to EUR 100,000 **per depositor per credit institution**. Deposits at the same institution, including across its trademarks, are aggregated toward that cap. It says exceptions exist for certain deposits and that the institution can confirm product coverage on request and on an account statement if covered; do not infer eligibility for every Business Account balance. [Raw: lines 23–25, 32, 34–35]
- For a joint account, the sheet states a per-depositor limit. It specifically qualifies accounts held by members of a partnership, association or similar group **without legal personality**: their deposits are aggregated and treated as made by one depositor for this calculation. [Raw: lines 26, 36]
- The table lists reimbursement in euros within 10 working days after the credit institution's failure. Its note says the period will gradually move to seven working days and mentions transitional basic-needs amounts on request and possible time limits to claim. These are dated sheet statements, not a current reimbursement timetable or proof of payment to any named depositor. [Raw: lines 27–28, 37]
- The sheet itself does not describe a platform delivery or consent workflow. For a separate captured EU Business Accounts user-interface instruction, follow [[source-adyen-docs-business-accounts-compliance-guidelines-user-interface]]; keep that guide's duties distinct from the terms of this sheet. [Raw: lines 15–37]
