## Overview

This 2026-09-15 Marketplace-path [[adyen]] financial-products compliance-guideline page is titled **Vulnerable users (UK and Ireland only)**. It instructs platform staff who interact with marketplace users to identify possible vulnerable circumstances, tailor support, document disclosed facts and follow up. The page expressly says it is not legal advice; it is historical Adyen partner guidance, not evidence that any platform currently satisfies its obligations. See [[adyen-financial-products]] and [[adyen-platforms]].

## Key takeaways

- **Scope and staff responsibility:** The page asks platforms to give the instructions to all directly user-facing support, sales/marketing and supervising managers and to ensure they read and understand them; unclear points go to Adyen. Its UK/Ireland title limits this page's scope even though its background notes obligations in some countries. Staff should have time to support distressed customers, and the organization should flag support available to the staff themselves. See pinned raw lines 15–39.
- **Recognizing possible need:** Adyen groups vulnerability drivers as health, life events, resilience and capability. In B2B cases, it says vulnerability is less common than in retail services but may affect sole proprietors, and it can be temporary. Listed circumstances, communication cues and BRUCE prompts are indicators for support, not a diagnosis or proof that an individual is vulnerable. See pinned raw lines 41–122.
- **Response, consent and referral:** The TEXAS prompts include thanking the user, explaining information use, seeking explicit consent before recording shared information, asking about impact and referring/signposting where suitable. IDEA covers impact, duration, experience and assistance. Staff are told to use clear language, check channel preference and understanding, and offer internal escalation or locally suitable external resources when needed. See pinned raw lines 124–169.
- **Records and follow-up boundary:** The guidance says the user should normally need to share their vulnerability information only once; attach an indicator/support/follow-up record to their file. Record facts the user shared, not inferred diagnoses or condition names absent explicit disclosure, and consult internal privacy/legal teams on domestic recording duties. The example of asking finance whether more time to pay is possible is conditional, not a promise. Follow up where needed and review interactions; neither a note nor a scheduled contact proves case resolution. See pinned raw lines 171–204.
