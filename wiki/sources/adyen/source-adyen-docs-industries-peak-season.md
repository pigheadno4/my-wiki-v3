## Overview

Adyen’s peak-season readiness hub introduces its operational-readiness claims and routes merchants to topic-specific preparation for online and in-person payments, risk and disputes, refunds and payouts, Adyen for Platforms, Giving, and status/help. This is a 2026-09-15 capture of a page marked last modified 2025-10-22. Its seasonal examples, resource links, utilization statement, deployment-freeze description, and support-hours statement are dated documentation claims, not a current capacity guarantee, live service status, or confirmation that any merchant completed the recommendations. See [[adyen-peak-season-readiness]] for the cross-topic route.

## Key takeaways

- The page defines retail/ecommerce peak season as typically late October through early January while noting that other industries can have other or additional peaks. It says most preparation guidance applies to any peak period.
- In this snapshot, Adyen describes active/active stateless architecture, a generally below-30% resource-utilization rate, continuous payment-function releases without scheduled downtime, periodic load tests, and added resources. It also describes a modified release schedule and extra support hours during the typical retail/ecommerce peak. These are provider statements in a dated hub, not independently measured uptime or a current transaction-per-second commitment.
- The stated code freeze surrounds named days including Singles Day, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Giving Tuesday, Christmas Eve/Day and Boxing Day, and New Year’s Eve/Day. The snapshot says critical fixes may still deploy after senior-engineer review and operational approval; it does not establish a fixed annual freeze calendar.
- Merchant-facing preparation spans conversion, authorization and cost tuning, monitoring and support readiness, refund reserves and dispute training. Adyen advises planning changes early enough to test and fix errors and advises against new features or major upgrades immediately before peak season. The page is a planning hub rather than a procedure to change a live merchant account.
- The next-step cards route to status/help, online payments, in-person payments, risk/disputes, refunds/payouts, Adyen for Platforms, and Giving. Webinars, blog and checklist links are additional resources; their content and present availability were not verified by this snapshot.
