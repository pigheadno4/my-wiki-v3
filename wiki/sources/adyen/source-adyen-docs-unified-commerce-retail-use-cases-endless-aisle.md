## Overview

This Adyen retail landing page defines **endless aisle** as letting customers place ecommerce orders while in a store or other physical location. It gives out-of-stock, online-exclusive and showroom items as examples, and navigates to two variants: **order in store, ship to home** and **order in store, pick up later in store**. See [[adyen]] and [[adyen-unified-commerce]].

## Key takeaways

- The page presents wider product range and delivery to an address as potential uses, not guaranteed inventory, delivery or commercial outcomes.
- Its two child links are scenario navigation. This landing page does not specify a payment API, terminal setup, seller/payee, merchant of record, authorization/capture timing, inventory synchronization, return or fulfillment procedure. Those mechanics belong to the separately reviewed child guides; do not infer that the two variants share one payment flow.
