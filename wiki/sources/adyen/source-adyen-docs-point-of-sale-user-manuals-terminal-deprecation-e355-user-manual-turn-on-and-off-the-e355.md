## Overview

This **2019-modified, 2026-09-15-captured** [[adyen]] e355 manual page gives the physical power-on and power-off procedure for this terminal. It is under the **terminal-deprecation/e355** path, so use [[adyen-payment-terminals]] for historical device retrieval; this page does not establish current model support, live configuration or successful payment setup. [Raw: lines 5–17]

## Key takeaways

- Before turning on the e355, the guide says to ensure the device is **fully charged** and points to a separate charging guide. [Raw: lines 19–21]
- To turn it on, hold the **button depicted in the guide** until the device starts. A successful startup shows a home-screen logo: a supplied merchant logo if one was provided, otherwise the Adyen logo. The source renders the button as an image rather than a text key name; use the raw image reference for identification. A logo is a power-on cue, not boarding, connectivity or payment-readiness proof. [Raw: lines 23–26]
- To turn it off, hold the **other button depicted in the guide** for **more than five seconds**. It beeps repeatedly during that hold until shutdown begins. The guide supplies no remote power action or success signal for completed shutdown beyond that description. [Raw: lines 28–30]
