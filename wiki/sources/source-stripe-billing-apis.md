## Overview

Stripe's Billing API overview relates the 11 subscription, billing, payment, customer, and entitlement objects. A subscription connects a Product, a Price, and either a customer-configured Account (Accounts v2) or a Customer (Customers v1). See [[stripe]] and [[stripe-subscriptions]].

## Billing object chain

The 2026-11-07 narrative says a subscription generates an Invoice each billing cycle. It describes PaymentIntent creation when the invoice has an amount due, with the payment tracked in the invoice `payments` list. For automatically charged subscriptions, it says Stripe charges the default payment method on the subscription or customer.

> [!warning] Contradiction
> The same 2026-11-07 snapshot's PaymentIntent object definition says, without the amount-due condition, that invoices automatically create PaymentIntents. The 2026-05-12 narrative and object table also used the broad formulation. These are conflicting statements in the captured documentation; neither establishes a universal runtime rule. Consult the dated raw passages before relying on the Invoice → PaymentIntent relationship.

The 2026-11-07 Accounts v2 diagram uses `customer_account`; its Customers v1 diagram uses `customer`. Their sample `active`, `open`, and `processing` statuses illustrate object relationships, not a completed payment or guaranteed transition. See the diagrams in the November raw, lines 29–177.

## 11 API objects

| Object | Role in this overview |
| --- | --- |
| Account (customer-configured) | Customer model in Accounts v2 |
| Customer | Customer model in Customers v1 |
| Product | Good or service; service products can include Features |
| Price | Unit price, currency, and billing cycle |
| Subscription | Customer's scheduled recurring purchase of a Product |
| Invoice | Amounts owed and payment status; generated by subscriptions |
| PaymentIntent | Payment flow and state; creation wording conflicts across the November passages above |
| PaymentMethod | Customer payment instrument |
| Feature | Function or ability included in a service product |
| ProductFeature | Association between a Feature and a Product |
| Entitlement | Access to a Feature associated with a subscribed service Product |

The object definitions and their links are in the November raw, lines 179–195.

## Entitlements model

The captured example creates Features and Products, associates each included Feature with its Product through ProductFeature, then creates a subscription for a recurring service Product. The documentation says an active Entitlement is created for each Feature associated with that Product. For provisioning, it says to retrieve the customer's active entitlements or listen to the Active Entitlement Summary event; it does not document a `customer.activeEntitlements` accessor here. See the November raw, lines 187 and 197–208, and the May raw, lines 40 and 50–61.

## Customer model and Checkout scope

The 2026-11-07 page says Accounts v2 is generally available for Connect users and in public preview for other Stripe users. Preview participants need a preview version in code; the page describes enabling **Reusable payment methods for Global Payouts** under Dashboard account previews and features. It recommends customer-configured Accounts for most use cases. These statements do not verify availability or enrollment for a particular account.

The page recommends Checkout Sessions for subscription creation. Its November wording says that when the customer completes the session, Stripe creates the subscription, creates a customer if none was supplied, and collects any payment due. The May capture instead said Checkout creates the customer and activates the subscription; retain that as May wording, not a November guarantee of activation or payment finality.
