## Summary

Stripe's overview of the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), an open standard for agentic commerce interoperability. Stripe is a member of the UCP Tech Council.

## UCP Key Capabilities

- **Checkout**: cart management, tax, payment, with or without human intervention
- **Identity linking**: OAuth 2.0 — platforms act on buyer's behalf
- **Order**: webhook-based shipping/delivery/returns tracking
- **Payment token exchange**: secure exchange between platforms, businesses, credential providers

## Stripe's Role

Stripe is a member of the UCP Tech Council. UCP payment handlers define how payment instruments are acquired, exchanged, and processed.
