## Summary

Stripe's overview of the Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP), an open standard co-built by Stripe, OpenAI, and Meta. Defines how AI agents interact with businesses to complete purchases on behalf of buyers.

## ACP Five Capabilities

1. **Agentic checkout**: cart management, fulfillment options, payment processing
2. **Cart and feed**: browse product catalogs, manage shopping carts
3. **Delegate payment**: payment token exchange between buyers, agents, businesses
4. **Delegate authentication**: OAuth 2.0 — agents act on buyer's behalf with a business
5. **Orders and webhooks**: lifecycle updates (confirmation, shipping, delivery, refunds)
