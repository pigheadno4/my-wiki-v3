## Summary

US businesses can accept USDC/USDP/USDG stablecoins from customers globally via crypto.stripe.com, settling in USD. 10K per transaction. No chargebacks. Refunds in stablecoins.

## Key Details

**Stablecoins**: USDC (Ethereum/Solana/Polygon/Base), USDP (Ethereum/Solana), USDG (Ethereum).

**Limits**: US businesses only; $10,000 USD per transaction; settle in USD only.

**No disputes**: customer authentication required → no chargebacks.

**Refunds**: returned as stablecoins to original wallet (not USD).

**Connect**: `crypto_payments` capability per connected account. Standard accounts via Dashboard; others via API/platform Dashboard.

**Products**: Checkout, Elements, Invoicing, Payment Links, Billing (invoices + subscriptions).
