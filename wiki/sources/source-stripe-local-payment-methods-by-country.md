## Summary

Hub page linking to country-specific guides for South Korea and Nigeria. Stripe supports local payment methods in both markets without requiring a local entity.

- **Nigeria**: Accept local payment methods (e.g. Naira cards) without a local entity.
- **South Korea**: Accept wallets (e.g. KakaoPay) and all local cards without a local entity.
