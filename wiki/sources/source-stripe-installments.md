## Summary

Index of Stripe's installment payment products (credit card-based payment splitting).

**Three products**:
- **Mastercard Installments**: virtual card; 4 interest-free installments at checkout
- **Mexico (meses sin intereses)**: consumer credit card feature; splits across multiple billing statements
- **Japan (分割払い)**: fixed payments, revolving credit, or bonus-time payment
