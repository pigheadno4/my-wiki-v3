## Overview

Download URLs for payment method icons used in Orders API integrations. Icons available for 6 APMs in color and white variants.

Source URL: <https://developer.paypal.com/docs/checkout/apm/reference/method-icons/>

Last updated: 2025-05-14

## Available Icons

| APM | Color icon | White icon |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bancontact | `paypal_bancontact_color.svg` | `paypal_bancontact_white.svg` |
| BLIK | `paypal_blik_color.svg` | `paypal_blik_white.svg` |
| eps | `paypal_eps_color.svg` | `paypal_eps_white.svg` |
| iDEAL | `paypal_ideal_color.svg` | **No white variant** |
| MyBank | `paypal_mybank_color.svg` | `paypal_mybank_white.svg` |
| Przelewy24 | `paypal_przelewy24_color.svg` | `paypal_przelewy24_white.svg` |

All hosted at: `https://www.paypalobjects.com/images/checkout/alternative_payments/`

**APMs without icons listed**: Multibanco, Trustly, Pay upon Invoice, Google Pay, Apple Pay (handled separately).

> [!info] Doc typo
> Source spells "Przelewy24" as "Prezelewy24" in the table — corrected in raw file.
