## Summary

Short guide on Apple Pay post-payment activities. Disputes and refunds follow the same process as card payments. Liability shift nuance: Visa requires iOS 16.2+ globally; below 16.2 only gets liability shift if card issued in Europe.

## Key Details

**Disputes**: same process as card disputes. Users can dispute even after authentication. Submit evidence via Dashboard.

**Liability shift**: supported globally for all major networks. **Visa exception**: iOS 16.2+ required for liability shift globally; devices below iOS 16.2 only get Visa liability shift if card was issued in Europe.

**Refunds**: same as card — full or partial.
