## Overview

This collected Braintree webpage is a historical navigation overview for card-network releases. It explains that networks change operating rules and mandates throughout the year, often around April and October, and routes readers to dated 2024 release items. It is not current card-network or legal authority, a merchant-specific fee schedule, an eligibility determination, or evidence that a merchant completed any required action.

## Key takeaways

- The page says network updates can introduce or change fees, add mandates or features, and revise existing features. Its examples include regulatory-compliance mandates, new fees, and use of schemes such as 3DS 2.0 to help reduce fraud; the examples do not establish a universal merchant requirement.
- Many updates require no merchant action, while some require integration changes or adoption of services; some also carry new or changed fees. Whether an item applies depends on the specific release, network, market, transaction or account conditions described by the dated detail page.
- The captured tables identify ten October 2024 updates and a separate Summer 2024 set by scheme, update name, type, and geographic impact. Those rows are historical navigation and classification, not proof of current policy, present pricing, merchant eligibility, compliance, or transaction-level fee application.

> [!warning] Historical and merchant-specific scope
> Use the dated release detail linked from each table row to evaluate its stated effective date, market, network, transaction and merchant-action conditions. This overview alone does not establish a current obligation or that a fee, rule, feature, or service applies to a particular merchant.
