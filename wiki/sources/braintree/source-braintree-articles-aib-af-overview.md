## Overview

This collected Braintree-hosted page, filed under the AIB AF path, is a direct-link portal to articles tailored to nuances associated with the recipient's account. It says those articles cannot be found by searching the support website and recommends bookmarking the portal.

The page says receiving its link means at least one of the recipient's merchant accounts is provisioned and funded by AIB. It does not define the AF label, identify a region or pricing model, or document any linked article's behavior. Treat this as a 2026-09-16 navigation snapshot, not independent current bank policy or proof of current account status or payment, settlement, or funding execution.

## Key takeaways

- The page says some payment-processing details, giving accepted payment types as an example, can vary by banking partner; generic public support articles can therefore omit important details. It does not state which payment types this account accepts.
- According to this page, the partner bank was determined during the application process and depends mostly on signup timing and where the business is domiciled. A merchant with multiple merchant accounts can have multiple partner banks.
- The AIB condition applies to at least one merchant account. Do not generalize it to every account or transfer guidance reached through this portal to another account, partner, processor, region, or pricing arrangement without reading that target and verifying its scope.

> This portal does not enumerate its linked articles or establish their instructions, values, eligibility, availability, pricing, accepted payment methods, or transaction behavior. The route label includes AF, but the page does not explain that label.
