## Overview

This collected Braintree article is a direct-link portal for merchants told that at least one of their merchant accounts is provisioned by Wells Fargo. It routes those merchants to articles tailored to account-specific nuances and recommends bookmarking the portal because the articles cannot be found by searching Braintree's support website.

The 2026-09-16 snapshot is Braintree-owned merchant-account navigation, not independent current Wells Fargo authority. The portal itself does not give prices, interchange-cost terms, a list of accepted payment types or transaction outcomes.

## Key takeaways

- Payment-processing details, including accepted payment types, can vary by banking partner, so Braintree says its generic public support articles can omit important account-specific details.
- According to this page, the partner bank was determined during the application process and depends mostly on when the merchant signed up with Braintree and where the business is domiciled.
- A merchant with multiple merchant accounts can have multiple partner banks. Keep the account and banking-partner scope attached rather than transferring linked guidance between accounts.

> [!warning] Keep the captured scope attached
> Receiving this portal link means at least one merchant account is provisioned by Wells Fargo; it does not establish that every account is. Read any linked article before relying on its details, and do not infer a current Wells Fargo policy, pricing model or interchange-cost treatment from this navigation page.
