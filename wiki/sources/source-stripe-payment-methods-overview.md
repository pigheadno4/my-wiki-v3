## Summary

Comprehensive overview of all 8 payment method categories Stripe supports, with regional availability tables.

## Categories (with key methods)

- **Cards**: Visa/MC/Amex/Diners (global); Discover/Interac (US/CA); Cartes Bancaires (EU); eftpos/JCB/CUP/Korean (APAC)
- **Bank debits**: ACH/Instant Bank/Canadian PADs (US/CA); Bacs/SEPA (EU); AU/NZ BECS (APAC)
- **Bank redirects**: Bancontact/BLIK/EPS/iDEAL|Wero/P24/TWINT (EU); FPX/PayNow/UPI (APAC)
- **Bank transfers**: USD/SEPA/UK/Japan Furikomi/Mexico
- **BNPL**: Affirm, Afterpay/Clearpay, Klarna, Meses sin intereses (MX), Zip
- **Real-time**: Swish (EU invite only), PayTo/PayNow/PromptPay (APAC), Pix (BR)
- **Vouchers**: Multibanco (PT), Konbini (JP), OXXO/Boleto (LATAM)
- **Wallets**: Apple Pay/Google Pay/Link/SRC/Stablecoins (global); Cash App Pay (US); PayPal/MobilePay/Revolut Pay/Satispay (EU); Alipay/WeChat Pay/GrabPay/Korean wallets (APAC)
