## Summary

Mexico-only consumer credit card installment feature. MX Stripe accounts only. MXN only. Merchant receives full amount minus an additional fee; bank handles collection.

## Key Details

**Plans and fees** (6 options):

| Months | Min amount | Additional fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 300 MXN | 5% |
| 6 | 600 MXN | 7.5% |
| 9 | 900 MXN | 10% |
| 12 | 1,200 MXN | 12.5% |
| 18 | 1,800 MXN | 17.5% |
| 24 | 2,400 MXN | 22.5% |

Custom min/max amounts configurable via Dashboard.

**Requirements**: MX Stripe account; Mexico-issued consumer credit card (no corporate); MXN; above minimum for selected plan; supported issuer.

**33 supported issuers**: Afirme, Amex, Banamex (3/6/9/12 only), BanBajío, Banca Mifel, Banco Azteca/Famsa/Invex/Multiva, Bancoppel, Banjercito, Banorte, Banregio, BBVA, Caja Morelia, CrediClub, Didi, Efevoo Pay, Falabella, Hey Banco, Inbursa, Klar, Konfio, Liverpool, MercadoPago, NanoPay, Nubank, RappiCard, Santander, Scotiabank, Stori, Suburbia.

**Connect**: platform sets default config; Standard accounts can override via Dashboard.
