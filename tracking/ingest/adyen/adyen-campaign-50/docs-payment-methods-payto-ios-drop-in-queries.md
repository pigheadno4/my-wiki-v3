# Fixed queries — PayTo iOS Drop-in

## Q1 — purpose, scope and limits

**Query:** What does the captured PayTo iOS Drop-in guide cover, and what does a Pending await action establish?

**Expected answer:** The page at `https://docs.adyen.com/payment-methods/payto/ios-drop-in` is a 2026-09-15-captured iOS Drop-in add-on for one-time PayTo mandates, subscription setup/first payment and Advanced later charges. It covers bank approval/decline, agreement-bound stored methods and webhook outcomes. A `Pending` response with `action.type: await` means waiting for bank action, not authorized payment or live success (full pinned `raw/adyen/docs/payment-methods/payto/ios-drop-in/2026-09-15.md`, `# PayTo iOS Drop-in` lines 15–23; Advanced response lines 358–372; bank notification line 475). ## Q2 — flows, mandate conditions and finality

**Query:** How do Sessions and Advanced PayTo iOS Drop-in differ for one-time payments, subscription setup and later charges, and which webhook/status cautions apply?

**Expected answer:** Both require existing iOS Drop-in, Checkout API v70+ and Customer Area addition; Advanced handles **await**, even though its action link points to `#handle-the-redirect` (pinned `raw/adyen/docs/payment-methods/payto/ios-drop-in/2026-09-15.md`, `## Sessions flow Drop-in` lines 25–34; `## Advanced flow Drop-in` lines 279–289). Sessions one-time requires first/last name and returns an adhoc exact one-payment mandate with default one-day end; a unique reference is recommended, not required, but has a 35-character PayTo maximum (lines 52–100). Sessions subscription setup requires storage, Subscription/Ecommerce and mandate terms; bank acceptance precedes `RECURRING_CONTRACT`, and **later charges require Advanced or API-only** (lines 121–159, 216–223). Advanced one-time requires PayTo, shopperAccountIdentifier and name, returning Pending/await in the example (lines 309–372). Advanced subscription setup and later `/payments` require an accepted agreement; a later charge uses `storedPaymentMethodId` from `recurring.token.created` and `ContAuth`, only within mandate conditions (lines 374–403, 475–488). The guide also names `recurringDetailReference` in `RECURRING_CONTRACT`; do not conflate the two reference names (lines 218, 477, 486). `AUTHORISATION` may be authorized or rejected, `OFFER_CLOSED` signals failed/canceled agreement and `REFUND_FAILED` can follow a refund (lines 553–583). Test email scenarios are simulations (lines 585–599). Several sample requests have missing commas/curl continuations, so use the tables rather than copy snippets (lines 70–89, 161–193, 327–354, 417–455, 490–513).
