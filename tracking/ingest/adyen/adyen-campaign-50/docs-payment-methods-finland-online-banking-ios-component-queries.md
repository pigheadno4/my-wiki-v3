# Fixed queries — Online banking Finland iOS Component

## Q1 — purpose, scope and limits

**Query:** What does the captured Finland iOS Component guide cover, and what does its redirect result not prove?

**Expected answer:** The page at `https://docs.adyen.com/payment-methods/finland-online-banking/ios-component` is a 2026-09-15-captured iOS Components guide for Sessions and Advanced integrations, including shopper email, InstantPaymentComponent, redirect and Paytrail testing. A `RedirectShopper` response and Component initiation do not establish completed payment or live merchant eligibility. Method-level Paytrail/FI/EUR context and separate bank-selection/refund-note qualifications are in `wiki/sources/adyen/source-adyen-docs-payment-methods-finland-online-banking.md` (full pinned `raw/adyen/docs/payment-methods/finland-online-banking/ios-component/2026-09-15.md`, `# Online banking Finland iOS Component` lines 15–23; Advanced response lines 713–727). ## Q2 — flow fields, conditional issuer and warnings

**Query:** Which Finland iOS Component request fields and redirect prerequisites are stated for Sessions and Advanced, and what conflicts or limits affect the examples?

**Expected answer:** Sessions requires existing iOS Components Sessions, Checkout API v68+, redirect handling, Customer Area addition and `shopperEmail` in `/sessions` (pinned `raw/adyen/docs/payment-methods/finland-online-banking/ios-component/2026-09-15.md`, `## Sessions flow Component` lines 25–46). No method-specific configuration is needed; `InstantPaymentComponent` uses `.other("ebanking_FI")` and initiates payment (lines 342–368). Advanced requires iOS Components, redirect handling and Customer Area setup, but its Components prerequisite URL points to Drop-in (lines 376–386). Its `/payments` table requires `paymentMethod.type: ebanking_FI` and `shopperEmail`; `issuer` is required **only for Issuer redirect flow**, and returnUrl is listed with a 1024-character maximum (lines 415–426). Advanced curl/SDK examples omit `paymentMethod` entirely (lines 430–710), so they are not full requests; many examples also show an email with two `@` signs (lines 67, 446). The response shows `RedirectShopper`/redirect, not finality (lines 713–727). Paytrail test data is advised and multiple partial refunds cannot currently be tested (lines 370–374, 729–733); that test limitation is distinct from the overview matrix's multiple-partial-refund mark.
