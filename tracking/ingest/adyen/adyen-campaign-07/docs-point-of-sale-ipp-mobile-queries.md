# Fixed retrieval checks: Mobile POS support comparison

1. **Q:** Does Adyen's Android Payments app in this comparison support card readers or iOS, and how do the SDK routes differ? **A:** In the 2026-09-15 snapshot the separate Payments app is Android-only and Tap-to-Pay-only. The embedded Android and iOS Mobile SDK paths each have Tap-to-Pay and card-reader entries; if the merchant POS app supports selecting the interface, it can use both with one SDK. In every described route, the POS app must integrate Terminal API. Tap to Pay uses the merchant device for card/wallet presentation and cannot insert or swipe; a paired reader handles tap, insert and swipe, while required PIN entry is on the mobile device. This comparison names no SDK or OS version and does not prove current eligibility. [Raw lines 23–47](/Users/tengtao/Development/wiki-v2/raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/ipp-mobile/2026-09-15.md).

2. **Q:** Can a checked Mobile POS feature or payment method be assumed to work in every country and interface? **A:** No. The source separates country/region, method and functionality tables for Android Tap to Pay, Android reader, Tap to Pay on iPhone, and iOS reader. For example, Canada and Japan show Tap to Pay on iPhone but not the other columns; Interac Debit and JCB are marked on Tap to Pay on iPhone only; Diners/Discover reader entries are US-only. Store and Forward is marked for iOS routes but not Android, and chip/magstripe require reader routes. PIN is marked in all four columns, with the solution text placing entry on the mobile device. These are dated comparison entries, not account activation or transaction proof; use the exact table cell and current detailed requirement guide.
