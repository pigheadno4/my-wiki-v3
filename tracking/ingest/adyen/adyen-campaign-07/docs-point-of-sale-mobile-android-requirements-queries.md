1. **What must an Android device meet for Adyen Mobile POS, and does this page prove current availability?** The captured guide allows Android phones, handheld computers and tablets, but requires no integrated card reader, integrated NFC and touchscreen, hardware key attestation and Google certification. It states Android 12 as a changeable OS minimum, latest security patch with a March 5, 2022 floor, and a POS app integrated with Terminal API. It recommends a screen-lock passcode and current Google-supported OS. Its separate linked availability page covers country/region, method and functionality; these requirements alone do not approve a device, account or transaction. Locators: raw lines 19–42, 68–84, 91–94.

2. **Which NYC1, PIN and dock conditions apply to the card-reader path?** NYC1 is described as PIN-capable SCRP with Mobile SDK for Android 2.4.0 or later; NYC1-SCR is no-PIN only. Disabling PIN on an NYC1 requires SDK 2.9.0 or later plus Support configuration at company, merchant or store level. If a countertop setup uses a dock, the guide permits only Adyen's NYC1 dock and power adapter, with SDK 2.4.0 and card-reader firmware 3.X.00.76 minimum; other readers and USB docks are unsupported. A dock is optional in this guide. Its US NYC1 and NYC1-SCR order dates are historical snapshot notices. The page does not make these reader/dock conditions universal for Tap to Pay or the separate Payments app. Locators: raw lines 44–66.
