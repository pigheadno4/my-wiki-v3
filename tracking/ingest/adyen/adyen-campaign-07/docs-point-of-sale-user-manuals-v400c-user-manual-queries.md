# C07 V400c Plus manual — fixed retrieval checks

1. **Q: What does the captured V400c Plus manual say about ordering/repairs, and what safety and setup steps precede use?**
   **A:** Its 2026-09-15 snapshot marks V400c Plus discontinued and unavailable for new orders, states Adyen repairs existing devices until April 2029, and suggests P630 for new orders. The manual requires checking intact box/tamper tape, matching box/device serial numbers (and Customer Area order/carrier record if accessible), inspecting for physical tampering, and contacting Support immediately if a check fails. Its setup inserts a 57 × 40 mm paper roll, connects the rear dongle and power, configures Ethernet or Wi-Fi, then selects **Board the terminal**, a store, and **Confirm**; boarding can take up to 30 minutes. The Ethernet cable belongs in the dongle's **ETH** port: a wrong cable or the RS232 port may severely damage the terminal. The dated notice does not prove current repair entitlement, availability, device certification or successful boarding.

2. **Q: Do Successful diagnostics or the V400c Plus menus prove payment readiness, and what does resetting Totals do?**
   **A:** No. **PSP connection** checks connectivity to Adyen's platform at the test time. POS-integrated use separately checks **Terminal API** and four **Endpoints** URLs; standalone use skips the POS test. A Successful display does not prove continuing connectivity, authorization or settlement. Admin & Settings protects network/software/configuration/power operations; Transactions protects history/totals and standalone special-payment access, with Refunds passcode possible for Refund or MOTO refund. Menu entries alone do not prove feature enablement. The Totals report covers processed sales, refunds, gratuities and cancellations since the last reset; **Reset** begins a new reporting period, so the prior totals no longer appear as current, though the previous report is accessible. Automatic daily reset requires Support.
