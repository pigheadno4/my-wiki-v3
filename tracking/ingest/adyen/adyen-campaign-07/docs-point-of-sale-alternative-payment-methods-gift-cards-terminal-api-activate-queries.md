1. **Can an Adyen POS gift card be activated without loading value, and is activation a payment?** The guide says a newly issued card needs activation before redemption, and `StoredValueData.ItemAmount` may be `0` for an activation without initial balance; the request also carries `Currency`. This is a Terminal API `MessageCategory: StoredValue` request with `StoredValueTransactionType: Activate`. A `StoredValueResponse.Response.Result` of `Success` indicates activation succeeded in the guide, not that a purchase was authorized or that the example occurred live. 2. **How are card details and security codes supplied, and can a request safely be retried by reusing an identifier?** For scanning, the POS obtains and sends card details first; for swipe or manual key entry, the terminal prompts after the request. The request includes provider/account identity and entry-specific fields; optional `ssc` up to eight digits is stated specifically for SVS gift cards, not every gift-card PIN. The page requires `ServiceID` uniqueness for 48 hours per terminal and recommends a unique `SaleTransactionID.TransactionID`; it gives no idempotent-retry guarantee. An uncertain activation outcome must be resolved from response/status evidence before another operation. Locators: raw lines 37–40, 52–84, 176–186.
