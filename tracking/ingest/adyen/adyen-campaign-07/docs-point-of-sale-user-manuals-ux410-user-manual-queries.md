# Fixed retrieval checks: UX410 manual

1. **Q:** What must happen before a UX410 unattended terminal can board, and how does the manual distinguish a blocked board from ready? **A:** Assign the unattended UX410 to a store before boarding, using the controller-unit serial number for Customer Area/API assignment. After installation and network/platform connection, blinking LED 3 with LED 4 on indicates it has not boarded; the guide says an unassigned unit stays there. Slowly blinking LED 4 with LEDs 1–3 off indicates the manual's ready state. The terminal retries after two minutes but stops after ten attempts even though the LEDs do not reveal that stop; disconnecting and reconnecting controller power restarts boarding. These cues do not prove a payment succeeded. [Raw lines 66–72 and 134–167](/Users/tengtao/Development/wiki-v2/raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/user-manuals/ux410-user-manual/2026-09-15.md).

2. **Q:** What safety and lifecycle limits matter when installing or continuing to use a UX410? **A:** The manual says to verify shipment integrity, matching controller/reader identifiers and tamper signs; failed checks require immediate Adyen Support contact. A red LED 1 warning may mean tampering, so stop use and contact Support; a failed key load calls for replacement. Connect regular Ethernet only to the controller LAN port because using another cable/port such as COM1 RS232 may severely damage the unit. Its 2026-09-15 snapshot says new orders are unavailable except EU/UK/UAE, repairs continue until April 2029, and S1U2 is recommended; the same-date catalog states a broader no-new-orders position. Confirm current regional availability and repair terms before acting.
