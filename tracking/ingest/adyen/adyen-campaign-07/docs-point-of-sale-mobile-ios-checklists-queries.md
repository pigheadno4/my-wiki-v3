# C07 iOS Mobile checklists — fixed retrieval checks

1. **Q: What does the pinned Adyen checklist require before building and testing an iOS Mobile solution?**
   **A:** Choose Tap to Pay on iPhone, Card reader, or both against the linked availability and iOS requirements; prepare an Adyen test account and ask Support to configure it for the iOS Mobile SDK; obtain a test card and, for the reader path, a test NYC1; use a supported iOS mobile device and a POS app integrated with Terminal API; build and test the solution. SDK warm-up is recommended for SDK integrations, not described as a universal gate. Evidence: `raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/mobile-ios/checklists/2026-09-15.md:23-51`; canonical URL: https://docs.adyen.com/point-of-sale/mobile-ios/checklists. The checklist gives no passing test result or current region/device eligibility.

2. **Q: Which extra go-live gates apply to Tap to Pay on iPhone, and what live credentials and validation does the checklist call for?**
   **A:** Tap to Pay on iPhone needs an Apple LIVE entitlement application, which the page says may take weeks for approval, and acceptance of Apple Terms of Use. The broader iOS Mobile go-live list also calls for the Adyen contract addendum/pricing and Support configuration of the live Mobile SDK account. For live Tap to Pay or reader SDK integration, it calls for a live basic-auth credential with **Allow SDK download for POS developers**, a live SDK, a live API-key credential with **Checkout webservice role**, and code using the live key and communication-session endpoint. It then calls for live penny tests. Evidence: `raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/mobile-ios/checklists/2026-09-15.md:53-63,77-93`; canonical URL: https://docs.adyen.com/point-of-sale/mobile-ios/checklists. These steps do not prove entitlement approval, account enablement, a successful live payment, settlement or reconciliation.
