# C07 P400 Plus user manual — fixed query answers

1. **Can a merchant order a new P400 Plus, and what repair window does the manual state?** The page captured 2026-09-15 says P400 Plus terminals are no longer available for new orders **except in Brazil**, that Adyen repairs existing units **until April 2029**, and recommends P630 for new orders. Those are statements in a dated manual, not a live inventory or repair-eligibility check. Evidence: `raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/user-manuals/p400-plus-user-manual/2026-09-15.md`, `# P400 Plus user manual` lines 15–20; proposed `[[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-p400-plus-user-manual]]`.

2. **Which P400 Plus checks come before payments, and does a successful connection diagnostic prove a payment succeeded?** Inspect the shipment and tamper tape. If Customer Area access is available, compare the box serial number with the order and use its track-and-trace link to verify that the carrier confirms delivery. Compare the terminal serial number with the box, then inspect the terminal for tampering or physical alterations; contact Adyen Support immediately if any check fails. Attach the rear dongle safely, configure Ethernet or Wi-Fi, board the terminal to the selected store, then test the Adyen platform connection and POS-side Terminal API/Endpoints connections. The manual warns that a wrong cable or Ethernet/RS232 port can severely damage the terminal. A **Successful** PSP or POS diagnostic confirms that connection test at that moment; it does not show an authorized or settled payment. After boarding, changing Wi-Fi uses **Settings > Network** and a passcode. Evidence: same raw, `## Get started` lines 43–64, `## Inspect the terminal` lines 66–81, `## Connect to your network` lines 83–119, and the two connection-test sections lines 121–139; proposed `[[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-p400-plus-user-manual]]`.
