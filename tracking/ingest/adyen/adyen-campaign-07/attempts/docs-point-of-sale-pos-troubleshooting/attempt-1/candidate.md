## Overview

This dated Adyen page is a **terminal troubleshooting directory**. It first branches on whether the physical payment terminal turns on, then points to lost/stolen reporting, per-model manuals, connection and hardware tests, and an Android-terminal remote-session guide. It provides navigation, not the diagnostic procedures or outcomes in those linked guides. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]] for the physical-device route.

## Key takeaways

- The opening choice is **terminal turns on** versus **terminal does not turn on**, each leading to a separate troubleshooting guide. See raw lines 19–33.
- For a **lost or stolen** terminal, the page directs readers to instructions to inform Adyen immediately. The reporting procedure itself is in that linked guide. See raw lines 35–37.
- New users are sent to the terminal-manual directory. Operators seeking tests are routed to separate **connection tests** and **hardware tests** guides. See raw lines 39–45.
- The remote-session link concerns an **Android terminal** and says a staff member can help start the session. It is a route to a separate guide, not evidence that any session was established or that a given model/account is eligible. See raw lines 47–49.
