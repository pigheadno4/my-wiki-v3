1. **How can a POS handle a gift card when the balance may be below the purchase amount?** If the balance is unknown, request the full amount with `AllowPartialAuthorisation`, then inspect `Response.Result` and `PaymentResult.AmountsResp.AuthorizedAmount`. A `Partial` result means only the authorized amount was covered; subtract it from the requested amount and make a follow-up request for the remainder or collect cash. If the balance is checked first, request no more than the balance on the gift card, then collect the remainder with a regular payment request or cash. The linked setup and balance guides would need their own review for deeper behavior. Locators: raw lines 33–47, 84–105, 229–234, 292–299 and 367–429.

2. **Do the provider list, optional security code, and request references prove which cards work or make retries safe?** No. `StoredValueProvider` includes examples such as SVS, Givex and Valuelink; the optional `ssc` is specifically an SVS secondary security code, not a universal PIN requirement. `ServiceID` must be unique for a terminal within 48 hours, `SaleTransactionID.TransactionID` is recommended unique, and `SaleReferenceID` associates partial payments for one order. The guide gives no idempotent-retry guarantee and no current brand, provider, account, currency, or terminal eligibility matrix. Its sample identifiers and amounts are illustrative; response state must be checked before another tender. Locators: raw lines 73, 81–86, 107–126, 229–234 and 311–320.
