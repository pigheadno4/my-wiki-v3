# C07 iOS Mobile management — fixed query answers

1. **How does accepting Apple Tap to Pay terms differ in test and live?** The 2026-09-15 guide applies this requirement to Tap to Pay on iPhone, not Card reader on iOS. Test acceptance occurs on the iPhone. In live Adyen for Platforms integrations, users accept on their iPhone when starting its first live Tap to Pay transaction. A live merchant that is neither a platform nor marketplace can instead accept for its merchant account through live Customer Area, covering iPhones under that account. Adyen says it does not know every acceptance outcome; check Apple Business Connect if uncertain. Evidence: `raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/mobile-ios/manage/2026-09-15.md`, `## Accept Apple Terms of Use for Tap to Pay on iPhone`, lines 21–49; proposed source `[[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-mobile-ios-manage]]`.

2. **What stops iOS Mobile transactions as SDK and OS versions age?** For both iOS Mobile paths, the guide says an SDK version expires six months after release, then reader PIN transactions are blocked while Tap to Pay on iPhone transactions and reader transactions without PIN remain possible. Twelve months after expiry, the version is removed, blocking all transactions using it. An iOS version below the required minimum separately blocks all Mobile SDK transactions; this page links to software requirements for the minimum. Reader and dock firmware must also be checked and updated for the reader solution. These are dated policy statements, not proof that a particular installation can currently transact. Evidence: `raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/mobile-ios/manage/2026-09-15.md`, `## Keep the Mobile SDK up to date` lines 79–103, `## Keep the device OS up to date` lines 123–131, and firmware sections lines 160–190; proposed source `[[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-mobile-ios-manage]]`.
