1. **What should an operator do before boarding an M400, and what do the diagnostics establish?** Inspect the package and device, matching order/box/device serials and checking for tampering; contact Adyen Support immediately if any check fails. Connect the rear dongle and power, configure Ethernet or Wi-Fi, select **Board the terminal**, choose a store and confirm. The guide says boarding can take up to 30 minutes and a missing boarding option may require waiting for the next synchronization, averaging three hours. Then test **PSP connection** separately from **Terminal API** and **Endpoints**. A successful test shows connectivity at that time, not a completed payment or lasting network health. Locators: raw lines 45–82, 84–86, 122–140.

2. **Is the M400 still orderable, and what physical or protected-control risks does its manual flag?** The captured manual says new M400 orders are discontinued, existing units are repaired until April 2029, and it recommends M450 or SFO1; this is a dated statement, not a current stock or repair-policy check. It warns that wrong Ethernet cabling or plugging Ethernet into the RS232 port may severely damage the device. Configuration, Network, Software, and Power require an Admin & Settings passcode; Transactions and some standalone actions have separate passcodes. The menu lists totals reset, configuration removal, and **Bypass PIN**, but listing a control does not establish account or transaction eligibility or permission to use it. Its shared-menu statement applies from terminal software 1.90, with possible older menus below that version. Locators: raw lines 19–20, 90–95, 163–197.
