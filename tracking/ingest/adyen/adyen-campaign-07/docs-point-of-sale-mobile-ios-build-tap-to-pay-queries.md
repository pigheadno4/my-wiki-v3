# C07 iOS Tap to Pay build guide — fixed query answers

1. **What must the merchant prepare before accepting Tap to Pay on iPhone through its own POS app?** In this 2026-09-15 snapshot, the merchant's iOS POS app needs Terminal API integration, the iOS Mobile SDK, an eligible iPhone, Adyen account configuration, and Apple Tap to Pay entitlement. TEST and LIVE entitlements and SDK app targets are separate. The SDK download needs a basic-auth credential with the `Allow SDK download for POS developers` role; the backend session call needs an API-key credential with `Checkout webservice` role and a client key. The guide also requires accepting Apple's Tap to Pay terms for the merchant account or store before the first payment. Check the linked requirements/support pages and Apple/Adyen accounts for current device, market, entitlement and review eligibility. 2. **Does seeing Done on the iPhone prove payment success, and how does the POS app obtain the result?** No. The guide says **Done** follows card reading and does not mean authorization; **Approved** appears only once authorized. The POS app creates or receives a Terminal API payment request, passes it through `Payment.Request`, selects `.tapToPay` as the `PaymentInterface`, invokes `PaymentService.performTransaction`, and checks the returned Terminal API payment response for result or error and receipt data. This describes the documented flow, not an observed transaction. Evidence: same raw, `## 7. Handle a payment` lines 432–534 and `### Approval screen` line 710; proposed `[[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-mobile-ios-build-tap-to-pay]]`.
