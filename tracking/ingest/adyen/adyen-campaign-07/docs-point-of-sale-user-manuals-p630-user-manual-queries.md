# C07 P630 manual — fixed retrieval checks

1. **Q: What must be checked before first use of a received P630, and what placement hazard does the manual identify?**
   **A:** Verify the unopened shipping box/tamper tape and match the box and terminal serial numbers. If Customer Area access is available, compare the box serial number with the order there and use the order's track-and-trace link to verify that the carrier confirms delivery. Inspect the device for alterations. If any check fails, contact Adyen Support immediately. Keep the P630 at least 30.5 cm (12 inches) from electronic security tag deactivators because they can interfere with or freeze it. 2. **Q: How does the P630 manual say to access its menus, and does it prove that a specific terminal is boarded and ready to transact?**
   **A:** P630 menu icons are disabled: press 9 then Confirm for Settings or 8 then Confirm for Transactions; protected options require their listed passcodes. Initial setup requires network connection and confirmation or correction of the displayed store. If the store screen does not appear, the manual says to wait for the next synchronization (on average three hours) and retry. These manual steps do not show that a particular unit completed boarding or a payment.
