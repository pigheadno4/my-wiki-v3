# Retrieval probes — docs-marketplaces-compliance-financial-products-keeping-records

Q1. What Marketplace financial-products records does this Adyen page tell a marketplace to keep, and for how long?
A1. The dated, non-legal-advice guide says a marketplace offering financial products keeps detailed service records for at least five years after termination of that offering unless internal requirements are longer (raw lines 19–21). Examples cover deployed UI/application/dashboard/support screenshots, marketing versions, communications and complaints, and historical statements (raw lines 23–33). Q2. Does this establish current legal compliance or actual record retention, and what UK/Ireland condition applies?
A2. No: the page expressly disclaims legal advice (raw line 19), and its guidelines do not evidence that a particular marketplace kept records. For UK/IE-only vulnerable-customer records, the communications/complaints table says recording systems must be able to categorize signs of vulnerability, while they should include at least health, life events, resilience and capability options; communications from vulnerable users must be accessible to responsible marketplace staff in accordance with legal requirements (raw lines 29–32). The five-year clock is stated relative to financial-products offering termination, with longer internal requirements prevailing (raw line 21).
