# C27 reporting-to-Adyen retrieval checks

## Q1 — Trigger, product and cadence

**Query:** For a Marketplace offering financial products, what notification and reporting does this dated Adyen page describe?

**Answer:** The 2024-modified, 2026-09-15-captured page says to notify Adyen within 1–2 business days for listed Security/Regulatory/Legal Complaints and support circumstances, with an API-request notification exception (raw lines 19–37). It lists daily Accounts/Issuing fraud-related data and an Issuing-only card-status report when relevant instances occur (raw lines 39–52). Quarterly Adyen-product complaint reporting is qualified by applicable law and starts after the first user is onboarded live; the snapshot gives four 10th-of-month deadlines (raw lines 54–96). ## Q2 — UK/Ireland and legal boundary

**Query:** Must every Marketplace send user-level vulnerability details, and does this page establish current legal compliance requirements?

**Answer:** No. The vulnerable-user reporting addition is headed UK & Ireland; it lists totals, four-driver category counts and outcomes of support requests, then says only total figures for vulnerable users are required, so individual-record submission cannot be inferred and support-outcome granularity is not fully resolved here (raw lines 98–112). The page says it is not legal advice and is a dated Adyen compliance-guideline snapshot (raw lines 11, 19).
