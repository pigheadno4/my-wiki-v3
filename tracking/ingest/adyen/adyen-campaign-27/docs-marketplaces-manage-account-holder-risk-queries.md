# Query proposal — docs-marketplaces-manage-account-holder-risk

Q1. What does Marketplace Score let authorized staff do, and what access does the guide require?
Answer: Score supports rules/automated actions after risk signals, case review, direct account-holder actions, muting, high-risk lists and conditional webhooks (raw lines 19, 31-166). **Merchant Marketplace Risk** is listed for cases/direct actions and **Merchant Marketplace Risk Admin** for rules/automated actions (line 27). Its availability text is a dated, partly future rollout statement, not proof of a given marketplace's enablement (line 29). Q2. What are the consequential action and identity limits?
Answer: Direct actions include blocking/re-enabling payouts, suspending an account and adding KYC values to high-risk lists only for confirmed fraudulent users (raw lines 91-98). Excluding a KYC value from linked-account matching is permanent and cannot be undone (lines 114-126). The optional webhook needs an accepting server, Configuration-webhook subscription and Adyen enablement (lines 159-166); sample `data.id` changes per score calculation and differs from `data.accountHolder.id` (lines 170-204). A risk flag or example does not prove fraud or payout completion.
