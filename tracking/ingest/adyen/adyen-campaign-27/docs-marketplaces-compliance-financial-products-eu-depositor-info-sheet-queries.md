Q1. What is this EU depositor sheet for, and what scope must be established before applying its stated limit?

A1. It is an Adyen N.V. deposit-protection disclosure, not a Marketplace setup procedure (raw lines 15–23). The sheet states the Dutch scheme and EUR 100,000 per depositor per credit institution, with all same-institution/trademark deposits aggregated; it also notes exceptions and institution confirmation for particular products (raw lines 23–25, 32–35). A particular depositor/product's eligibility is not established by the sheet. Q2. May the joint-account limit and reimbursement period be used as unconditional current coverage or payment promises?

A2. No. The general joint-account per-depositor statement has an exception for a partnership, association or similar grouping without legal personality, treated as one depositor (raw lines 26, 36). The captured table says euro reimbursement within 10 working days, and its note forecasts a move to seven days; both are dated sheet text, not proof of current terms or an actual reimbursement (raw lines 27–28, 37). The sheet does not itself state a platform delivery/consent workflow (raw lines 15–37).
