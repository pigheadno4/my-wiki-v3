# Retrieval checks: docs-marketplaces-compliance-financial-products-customer-complaints

Q1. How does the dated Marketplace guideline identify a complaint and what process does it expect?
Answer: Dissatisfaction with Adyen products or services that expects a response counts regardless of whether the user says “complaint” or uses a particular channel; ordinary help or refund requests become complaints if they allege unfair treatment or dissatisfaction. The marketplace is to offer accessible support, identify complaints across channels, investigate and update users, and keep durable records. Adyen's general guideline expectation is acknowledgement within 5 business days, resolution within 10 business days from submission, and notice for delays (raw lines 21–63). Q2. When is Adyen involved, and how do UK/Ireland rules qualify the general complaint timings?
Answer: Security and regulatory/legal complaints are reported to Adyen within one to two working days; the escalation table splits direct legal/privacy/regulator responses from marketplace resolution, with information due no later than two business days after receipt. UK/Ireland guidance adds conclusion notice within five working days, ombudsman language after an unsatisfactory final response or 15 working days unresolved, updates at intervals of at most 15 business days, and a further notice after 35 working days unresolved. These additional regional steps coexist with, rather than replace in the source, the general 5/10-day expectation. The page is dated and disclaims legal advice (raw lines 19, 65–149).
