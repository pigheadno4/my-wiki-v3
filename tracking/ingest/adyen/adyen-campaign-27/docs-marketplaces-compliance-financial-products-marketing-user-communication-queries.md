Q1. What collateral requires Adyen review before publication, and when must Marketplace changes be resubmitted?
Answer: The page covers user-visible financial-product collateral, including marketplace web/app, marketing, statements, card and app-store material (raw lines 21-38). It requires relevant bank disclosures and Adyen pre-approval before publishing or distributing; changed material is resubmitted except typographical/grammatical changes or changes that do not affect product/service description or marketing, and additional post-go-live material is submitted too (raw lines 40-49).
