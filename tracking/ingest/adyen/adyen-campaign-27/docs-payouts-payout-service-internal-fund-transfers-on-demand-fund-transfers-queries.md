Q1. How is an on-demand internal balance-account transfer initiated, and whose capabilities matter?
A1. Use Customer Area or Transfers API POST `/transfers` to debit the source `balanceAccountId` and credit the counterparty `balanceAccountId` with `category: internal` (raw lines 40–70, 90–104). Adyen for Platforms, role/subscription/Support setup applies, and `sendToBalanceAccount` on source holder plus `receiveFromBalanceAccount` on recipient holder is specified when the accounts belong to different holders (raw lines 21–38). Q2. Does initiation prove booked movement, and is a pull operation shown?
A2. No. The response example is `received` / `pending` (raw lines 428–460); await outgoing-source and incoming-recipient Transfer webhook series to `booked` or failure (raw lines 463–471). The introduction mentions internal direct debit (raw lines 15–19), but the detailed fields/examples show a push from source to target (raw lines 54–58, 92–104); this page provides no separate pull recipe. It is not an external bank payout.
