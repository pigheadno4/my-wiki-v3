## Overview

This 2024-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] Marketplace financial-products compliance-guideline page describes service records a marketplace should retain while offering financial products and after the offering ends. It expressly says the page is not legal advice. Use [[adyen-financial-products]] for the product-family route; the dated guidance does not establish present legal duties, a current retention policy or that any marketplace actually retained records. [Raw: lines 11, 15–21]

## Key takeaways

- The guide says a marketplace offering financial products to users must keep detailed service records for at least **five years after termination of the financial-products offering**, unless its internal data-retention requirements call for longer. The trigger is the offering's termination; do not silently replace it with a user-account closure or a different service termination. [Raw: lines 19–21]
- Its example record categories are screenshots of deployed financial-product, application-flow, dashboard and support-page versions; versions of marketing materials; user communications and complaints, including support interactions and complaint documentation; and historical statements generated and made available to users. These are record examples, not proof that a statement or communication was delivered. [Raw: lines 23–33]
- In the communications/complaints row, the captured table adds **UK and Ireland only** guidance on vulnerable-customer records: systems should categorize signs of vulnerability using at least health, life events, resilience and capability, and communications from a vulnerable user must be accessible, in accordance with legal requirements, to marketplace staff responsible for assisting users. The raw table is collapsed across lines; retain the regional qualifier. [Raw: lines 29–32]
