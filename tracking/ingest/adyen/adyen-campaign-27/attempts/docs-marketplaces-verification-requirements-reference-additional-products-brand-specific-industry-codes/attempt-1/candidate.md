## Overview

This 2026-09-15 Marketplace-path [[adyen]] reference says brand-specific Industry Codes (ICs) correspond to unique Merchant Category Codes (MCCs) for certain brands and directs selection based on a user's brand and business activity. It sits within the [[adyen-platforms]] additional-products verification route (raw lines 15–19). The pinned export contains classification and contact guidance, but no actual brand-specific code values.

## Key takeaways

- Adyen says certain brands have unique MCCs and it assigned brand-specific ICs to capture them. Select the IC that best represents **both** the user's brand and business activity; the page does not say all brands need such an IC (raw lines 17–19).
- Contact an Adyen contact or Support if an industry code needs approval **before onboarding users**, or if offering business accounts to users in new industries outside the previous approval process (raw lines 21–24). This is a conditional contact direction, not confirmation of approval, account eligibility, enabled capabilities or completed onboarding.
- Although the page says it accepts only following values, the pinned raw has no list or table between that statement and “See also” (raw lines 26–32). Do not derive a specific accepted IC, brand-to-code mapping, or approval status from this export. The linked general industry-codes list is a separate reference.

## Evidence boundary

This is a dated Marketplace-path reference, not a code catalogue in the captured form. The absent accepted-value list prevents validating a specific code from this source. Selecting or contacting about an IC does not itself prove that Adyen approved an industry, onboarded a user, opened a business account or assigned an MCC.
