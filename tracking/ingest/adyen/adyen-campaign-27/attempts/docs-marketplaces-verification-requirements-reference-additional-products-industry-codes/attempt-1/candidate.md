## Overview

This 2026-09-15 [[adyen]] Marketplace-path page directs a platform to select the Industry Code (IC) best representing a user's business activity. Adyen says it maps ICs to Merchant Category Codes (MCCs) and adapts them to internal regulations. The page belongs under [[adyen-platforms]] as a classification and approval-contact reference. **The pinned Markdown export contains no list of accepted IC values**, despite saying “We only accept the following values”; do not derive code choices or eligibility from this capture.

## Key takeaways

- Select an IC accurately for the user's business activity; some MCCs correspond to multiple ICs, so an MCC alone does not determine a unique IC. See raw lines 17–19.
- Contact an Adyen contact or Support if approval for an IC is needed before onboarding users, or if offering business accounts to users in new industries outside the prior approval process. The page does not identify which codes require approval or evidence that approval was granted. See raw lines 21–24.
- The sentence introducing accepted values is followed by a divider, three orphaned footnotes and a link to a separate brand-specific list, **not an accepted-value table** in this raw snapshot. The footnotes refer to an ATV ceiling, included product categories and American Express support, but without their missing rows they cannot be assigned to any IC. See raw lines 26–36.

## Evidence boundary

This source is a navigation and caution entry. It does not enumerate acceptable industry codes, brand-specific codes, country/product coverage, current eligibility, account verification or a business-account grant. Consult a complete authoritative list and the relevant approval path before using a code; the unlinked footnotes here cannot be treated as code-specific rules.
