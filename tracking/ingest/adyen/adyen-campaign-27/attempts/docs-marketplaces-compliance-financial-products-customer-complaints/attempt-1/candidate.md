## Overview

This Marketplace financial-products complaints guideline, last modified 2024-01-10 and captured 2026-09-15, describes identifying, handling, recording and escalating user complaints. It expressly disclaims legal advice. Its time limits and regional procedures are dated Adyen guideline statements, not current legal advice, proof of actual compliance or a completed resolution. See [[adyen]] and [[adyen-financial-products]] (raw lines 5–21).

## Key takeaways

- A complaint is an expression of dissatisfaction about Adyen products, services, terms, communications or actions where a response or resolution is expected. The user's exact word, tone, format or channel does not control classification. Routine information, assistance, technical or refund/chargeback requests are not complaints by themselves, but become complaints when they allege unfair treatment or express dissatisfaction (raw lines 21–37).
- The marketplace must make support accessible, identify complaints across channels, provide resolution guidance and investigation updates, and may direct complaints raised in support channels to a complaint form. The page says Adyen expects acknowledgement within **5 business days**, resolution within **10 business days** from submission, and notice if resolution takes longer. It calls for durable accessible records of support inquiries, complaints and responses for five years from Adyen Service termination **and** user-account closure, unless the marketplace requires longer retention (raw lines 39–63).
- Security and regulatory/legal complaints are reported to Adyen within **one to two working days**. The marketplace forwards complaints it cannot substantively resolve without Adyen input and cases Adyen is responsible for handling. The table assigns different responders: Adyen directly handles legal actions and privacy matters, and responds to regulators/ombudsmen; marketplace handles complaint resolution unless Adyen's substantive input is needed, and handles financial-crime and technical complaints. Escalation information is due as soon as possible and no later than two business days after receipt; further requested marketplace information is due within two business days; the marketplace delivers Adyen's prepared final response to the user within complaint timelines (raw lines 65–109).
- For **UK and Ireland customers**, additional acknowledgement and resolution elements apply: decision/reasons and any offer/settlement and ombudsman referral notice within five working days after the investigation concludes; acknowledgement of possible ombudsman referral after dissatisfaction with a final response or non-resolution within 15 working days; complaint contact point and ombudsman details; investigation updates at intervals no longer than 15 business days; and, after 35 working days unresolved, notice of anticipated timeframe and possible ombudsman referral. These are additional regional statements alongside the general 5/10-business-day expectation, not a single interchangeable deadline (raw lines 111–143).
- For a BACS complaint received directly by Adyen, the marketplace supplies requested information within two business days. The page separately says Adyen does not consider user payment-services complaints to be forwarded under DISP 1.7; this does not erase its earlier category-specific escalation instructions (raw lines 145–149).
