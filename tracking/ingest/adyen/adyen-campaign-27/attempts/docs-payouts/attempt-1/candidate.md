## Overview

This 2023-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] Payouts landing page describes outbound transfers to accounts outside Adyen and separates two product routes. **Payouts combined with Payments** is offered to an Adyen merchant of record alongside payment processing for a beneficiary not onboarded with Adyen, such as a supplier. **Payouts as part of Adyen for Platforms** pays processed-payment funds to a platform merchant or sub-merchant. These routes have different actors and should not be conflated. [[adyen-payouts]]

## Key takeaways

- The landing page links the merchant-of-record/third-party route to **Payout Service** and the platform-user route to **managed payouts**, which it describes as on-demand or scheduled. Its page title says global payouts in real time, but this hub gives no route-specific eligibility, funding, API, timing or completion criteria; follow the child guide for those conditions. See raw lines 15–30.
- This is navigation and product positioning, not evidence that a particular payout request was accepted, funds were debited, a beneficiary was paid, or a bank received funds. See raw lines 19–30.
