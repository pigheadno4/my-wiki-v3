## Overview

This 2024-modified, 2026-09-15-captured Marketplace-path [[adyen]] compliance-guideline page concerns support for users of [[adyen-financial-products]] and Adyen for Platforms. It assigns the marketplace first- and second-line customer support and the role of Adyen's contact for customer communications (raw lines 15–22). The page explicitly says it is **not legal advice**; these are dated Adyen guidelines, not proof of current legal duties or actual support performance.

## Key takeaways

- **Support ownership and operation:** The marketplace must let customers submit support requests and receive resolution support. First line handles intake/simple issues and escalation; second line handles more technical issues. The guide calls for resourced helplines, severity-prioritized tickets, case numbers where relevant, visible case status, expected-delay notices and centrally accessible customer information for subsequent advisers (raw lines 21–39).
- **Channels and human access:** Customers should easily find and navigate support channels. The marketplace must communicate multiple channels, include live support appropriate to its users, provide human touchpoints where digital/chatbot support encounters complex needs or a merchant asks, and maintain reasonable access during incidents such as outages or cyberattacks. It prohibits barriers to queries or complaints and says support channels should be available free of charge (raw lines 40–46).
- **Critical intake, monitoring and records:** The guide requires 24/7 **ability to raise** queries or notifications about possible unauthorized use, fraud, or lost/stolen credentials or cards; this is not a stated 24/7 staffed resolution guarantee. It calls for front-line training, quality monitoring and records of all support queries/responses for five years from the date of Adyen Service termination and the user's account closure, unless marketplace rules require longer retention (raw lines 47–50). The wording names both events without specifying a tie-break rule here.
- **UK and Ireland vulnerable-customer section:** Adyen calls for reasonable assistance, staff recognition/training, a prominent UI notice with standard support link/phone/email so customers can disclose needs at their own pace, human touchpoints alongside digital channels and assistive-technology compatibility (raw lines 52–62). This section is explicitly scoped to UK and Ireland customers; it should not be generalized into a universal legal specification.
