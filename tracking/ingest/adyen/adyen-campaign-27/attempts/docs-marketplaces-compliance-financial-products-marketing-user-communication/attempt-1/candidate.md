## Overview

This Adyen Marketplace financial-products page (last modified 2023-12-14; captured 2026-09-15) describes Adyen's collateral review process and communication principles for marketplace material about financial-product offerings. It expressly says the overview is not legal advice. Its product and country matrices are a dated source snapshot, not evidence of current rules, eligibility, Adyen approval or permission to publish. See [[adyen]] and [[adyen-financial-products]].

## Key takeaways

- **Collateral scope and approval:** Collateral includes webpages, app flows and dashboards, support pages, user statements, advertisements, press material, card distribution material and app-store descriptions/screenshots. The guide says marketplace collateral must follow product-specific principles and include relevant bank disclosures. For financial products, it requires Adyen review and pre-approval before publication or distribution; changed material must be resubmitted, except typographical/grammatical edits or changes that do not affect the Adyen product or service description or marketing. Additional material after go-live is also submitted for approval. See raw lines 21-49 for the exact scope and stated submission route.
- **Presentation:** The general principles call for plain, audience-appropriate language; accurate, clear terms, benefits, limits, availability and risks; substantiated and dated claims; attributed genuine testimonials with written permission and payment disclosure; fair comparisons; prominent, legible and accessible disclosures. The page warns against misleading absolutes and unqualified terms such as “free,” “pre-approved,” “guaranteed,” “protected” and “secure”; it also says material must not imply a consumer credit check is required for the product. See raw lines 51-88 for exact conditions and layout recommendations.
- **Regional and product matrices:** UK and Ireland provider-role disclosure examples appear at raw lines 90-99. The Issuing EU/UK/US matrix (lines 101-122) distinguishes prepaid from debit and business-purpose cards, issuing statements and scheme marks; some rows are US-only. The Business accounts EU/UK/US matrix (lines 124-136) distinguishes marketplace versus provider identity, UK account terminology, and EU deposit-guarantee, UK e-money/FSCS and US FDIC/sweep wording. The Capital EU/US/AU/UK/CA matrix (lines 138-148) identifies region-specific provider entities, business-purpose and credit-approval disclosures. These rows cannot be generalized across products or regions.
- **Use boundary:** This is Adyen's historical, non-legal compliance-guideline snapshot. The page points to an Adyen contact for non-legal clarification and describes collateral review; neither this page nor this wiki entry establishes current law, a live financial-product offer, an approved asset or completed review.
