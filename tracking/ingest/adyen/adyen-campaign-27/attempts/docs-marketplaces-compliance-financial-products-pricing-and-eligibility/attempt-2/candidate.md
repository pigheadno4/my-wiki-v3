## Overview

This 2024-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] Marketplace-path page summarizes Adyen's **compliance guidelines** for disclosing financial-product prices, fees and eligibility to platform users. It expressly says the content is **not legal advice**. The product- and region-qualified statements below are historical provider guidance, not current law, an approved tariff, an actual disclosure, or a determination that a marketplace or user is eligible. [[adyen-financial-products]]

## Key takeaways

- Alongside Adyen-supplied agreements, the marketplace's terms and fee schedule should describe its program fees and terms. In this dated guide, increased user fees for an Adyen product require **written notice at least 30 days before** effect in the **EU and US**, or **60 days before** in the **UK**. These are Adyen's stated regional guideline periods, not proof of live notice or current law. See raw lines 19–32.
- For **Adyen for Platforms**, additional marketplace fees are described as permissible if disclosed before enrollment; FX rate and fee costs should be clear when the user confirms conversion and in a periodic statement or UI transaction overview. Costs must remain available on user request; the reasonableness condition in this subsection is **UK only**. See raw lines 34–39.
- For **Business accounts and Issuing**, marketplace-charged user fees must be disclosed before enrollment and notified to and agreed with Adyen in advance; costs must be available on request and reasonable, appropriate and tied to actual marketplace service costs. For **EU/UK Business accounts**, instant-payment fees must not exceed regular-payment fees, and instant and regular payments must have the same pricing. For **Capital**, the marketplace may not charge extra user fees directly or indirectly, and discounts need prior written Adyen approval. Do not transfer one product's fee rule to another. See raw lines 41–54.
- In general the guide says marketplaces must not add eligibility criteria for Business accounts, Capital or Issuing beyond Adyen's requirements. If a platform has additional criteria for requests, it should submit them to Adyen for review of their disclosure; criteria must be disclosed before offering and must not be discriminatory, unfair or deceptive. Review submission is not approval or user eligibility. See raw lines 56–63.
- A **UK-only** onboarding questionnaire asks how the marketplace prices Platforms, Business accounts and Issuing services, including payment processing, FX, discounts, fairness and user communications. It is an assessment intake, not evidence of approved pricing. See raw lines 65–93.
