## Overview

This pinned Adyen **Payout Service** landing page describes outbound disbursements offered alongside Adyen online or in-person payment processing. A payout may be related to an acquired payment **or standalone**, such as paying a third-party supplier or partner. This page is product orientation and navigation; it does not define a payout request API, beneficiary eligibility, current route availability or execution outcome. See [[adyen]] and [[adyen-financial-products]] (raw lines 15–19, 27–43).

## Key takeaways

- Funding can come from funds acquired through payments with Adyen **or externally transferred funds**. The page does not make an acquired payment a prerequisite for each payout (raw lines 19–23).
- The destination paths are **third-party bank accounts** and **third-party Mastercard or Visa cards**, each limited to the supported locations in its linked child guide. This overview itself does not enumerate countries, currencies, bank rails or card eligibility (raw lines 24–25, 37–43).
- The page routes readers to getting started, an integration checklist, bank-account payouts and card payouts. Those child guides own onboarding, API, compliance, lifecycle and supported-location detail; this page alone does not prove enabled service, approved beneficiary, sent funds or bank/card receipt (raw lines 27–43).
- Keep this Payout Service route distinct from the separately documented **legacy Online Payout API** described under [[adyen-payouts]]; this page does not authorize substituting legacy Payout API behavior for its child guides.
