## Overview

This 2026-09-15 [[adyen]] Marketplace-path EU depositor information sheet describes the Dutch statutory Deposit Guarantee Scheme as it relates to deposits in Adyen N.V., including limits, aggregation, joint-account treatment and reimbursement information. It is disclosure content, not a platform setup guide, current legal conclusion, confirmation that a particular account/deposit qualifies or evidence of actual reimbursement. [[adyen-financial-products]]

## Key takeaways

- The captured sheet states a **EUR 100,000 per depositor per credit institution** limit and aggregates deposits at the same institution, including deposits under the Adyen trademark, toward that limit. It also notes that certain deposits are excepted and says the credit institution can confirm whether a specific product is covered. Do not infer coverage for every Business Account balance or depositor. See raw lines 23–25 and 32–35.
- For a joint account, the general per-depositor limit is qualified: deposits held by a partnership, association or similar grouping **without legal personality** are aggregated and treated as one depositor for calculating the limit. See raw lines 26 and 36.
- The sheet's table lists reimbursement in euro within **10 working days** if the institution fails; its note says the period will gradually move to **seven working days** and describes transitional basic-needs access. Those are dated statements, not a current payout timetable or guarantee for a named depositor. See raw lines 27–28 and 37.
- This sheet does not state when a marketplace must deliver it or obtain consent. The separate financial-product UI guideline owns any delivery/acceptance workflow; do not infer one from this disclosure alone. See raw lines 15–37; [[source-adyen-docs-platforms-compliance-financial-products-user-interface]].
