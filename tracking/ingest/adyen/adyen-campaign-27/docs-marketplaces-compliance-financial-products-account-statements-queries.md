Q1. What does this Marketplace statement guideline cover, and what does it require of the platform before relying on a statement template?

A1. It covers Business accounts and Issuing account statements, with requirements varying by region and product, and expressly says it is not legal advice (raw lines 15–23). The platform supplies items absent from Adyen APIs, keeps API data unchanged and submits a statement template for Adyen review/approval; Issuing asks for a sample before cards are issued (raw lines 21, 27, 98, 150–152, 355–357). Q2. Does the opening statement sentence mean every prepaid Issuing program must send periodic PDFs, and are country disclosures interchangeable?

A2. No. The later prepaid-program section exempts periodic sending but requires on-demand access to all card transactions; UI presentation requires screenshots submitted for review and retained as replacement statement records (raw lines 307–313). The EU Issuing passage permits UI display instead of downloadable statements, whereas UK Issuing requires PDF or another durable download (raw line 211). Business-account EU/US/UK fields and disclosures differ, including dated EU DGS, US non-FDIC and UK FSCS statements, and the regional institution table marks the Amsterdam row for EU, US and UK while also marking separate San Francisco and London branch rows; earlier prose gives region-specific institutions and UK prose/table naming also differs (raw lines 25–146, 225–245, 301–303). These are snapshot guidelines, not current legal or account-coverage proof (raw line 19).
