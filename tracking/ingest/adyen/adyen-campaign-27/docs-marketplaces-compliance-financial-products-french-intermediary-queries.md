Q1. When does this Marketplace guide route a platform to French ORIAS intermediary registration, and what sequence does it describe?

A1. The dated page combines offering Adyen to French users with the platform being incorporated and registered in France; its requirements table narrows scope to an Adyen for Platforms integration offering financial products to French users (raw lines 19–36). It describes at least one director's professional-capacity evidence, NAF-based IOBSP levels, a recent kbis, ORIAS legal-person/IOB steps, Adyen's mandate after SIREN/legal-name submission, upload and fees, then a website disclaimer after ORIAS email approval and yearly renewal (raw lines 38–102). Q2. Is this a current legal ruling, proof of ORIAS acceptance, a guaranteed two-month timeline, or evidence that no funds are actually entrusted to a platform?

A2. No. The page expressly disclaims legal advice (raw line 19). Its ORIAS timing says “usually” under two months, and its flow requires an eventual ORIAS approval email before the website disclaimer (raw lines 67, 98–100). The “no funds entrusted” phrase is a specified registration selection, not observation of actual custody (raw lines 79–85). The exact statutory references, NAF categories and current applicability need separate current verification before action; this captured page alone proves none of them as live law (raw lines 54–65).
