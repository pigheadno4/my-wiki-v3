# C27 Q1–Q2: Payout Service payout amount

**Q1. What balance inputs does this guide say Adyen considers before a payout?**
Follow [[index]] → [[adyen-index]] → [[adyen-payouts]] → [[source-adyen-docs-payouts-payout-service-getting-paid-payout-amount]] → [[raw/adyen/docs/payouts/payout-service/getting-paid/payout-amount/2026-09-15]]. The Payout Service page says Adyen verifies the available balance before a payout to account for potential negative-balance obligations (raw line 19). It distinguishes the existing balance, including value dates through the calculation day, from future debits/credits with future value dates that were already booked by that day (raw lines 21-24). It considers the future-change total only when negative and ignores zero or positive totals (raw lines 26-28). This page provides a calculation explanation, not a payout request or an additional integration prerequisite.

**Q2. What arithmetic conflict and outcome limits matter?**
At the same route, Example 1 uses USD 100 existing balance and future USD −50 plus USD +30, net USD −20, then states USD 80 available (raw lines 34-41). Yet the prose says to take the existing balance “minus future balance changes”; literal USD 100 − (USD −20) equals USD 120, so the wording conflicts with the worked result and should not be encoded as a verified formula (raw lines 26, 38-41). Zero and positive future totals leave the stated available amount at USD 100 (raw lines 45-63). The page links separate scheduled and on-demand guides but shows no accepted payout, account debit, transfer completion or beneficiary-bank receipt (raw lines 65-68).
