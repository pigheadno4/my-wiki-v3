Q1. What is an enterprise account here, and what setup gates apply?
A1. It is connected to a balance account and receives account-identification details for incoming third-party bank transfers and outgoing bank/card transfers (raw lines 36–41). Setup goes through an Adyen contact; Payouts or Enterprise Issuing integration, Transfers API roles and Transfer/Transaction webhook subscriptions are listed, with an EMI/PI license needed for **some** types (raw lines 19–34). Q2. Whose funds does the example track, and what outcomes are unproven?
A2. The intro mentions merchant and customers' funds (raw line 19), but the detailed sequence is customer payment → merchant account → merchant's connected balance account settlement → later third-party transfers (raw lines 43–52); it does not assign legal ownership of a particular customer's funds. US/NL/UK account details are sample formats and the EU/UK/US region list is a dated snapshot (raw lines 58–99), not live account eligibility or proof of settled funds, booked transfer or bank receipt.
