Q1. What is the Getting paid flow and what must be set up?
A1. In the Payout Service, Adyen-processed merchant proceeds pass from merchant account to balance platform/balance account, then scheduled or on-demand payout moves them to the company's payout-account transfer instrument (raw lines 23–30, 41–43). A Payouts integration, Merchant manage bank accounts Customer Area role, and a verified **valid** payout account are required to manage/use the account (raw lines 32–56). Q2. What lifecycle and timing limits prevent treating a payout request as bank receipt?
A2. Transfer-instrument deletion is irreversible and leaves schedules/on-demand requests using its ID failing (raw lines 58–68). Priority, cut-off, region, currency and amount bounds vary; cross-border arrival cannot be guaranteed and fees can lower beneficiary receipt (raw lines 70–155, 215–287). USD payouts to US bank accounts are unsupported for a Canada- or Hong Kong-located legal entity (raw line 236). The line-219 instruction for a separate `numberAndBic` cross-border instrument conflicts in scope with the destination table's IBAN-required/optional formats (raw lines 281–287); choose the applicable destination format rather than assuming `numberAndBic` universally. SEPA wire example 16:30 PM ET conflicts with table 16:55 CET (98–101, 139); TEST bank accounts are TEST only (289–297). The page does not prove a specific bank receipt.
