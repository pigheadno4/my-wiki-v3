Q1. What does this Marketplace guideline say about fee disclosure, fee changes and additional eligibility criteria?

A1. It asks the marketplace to explain pricing and eligibility, with program terms/fees in its terms and schedule (raw lines 19–28). Its dated EU/US user-fee-increase notice is written at least 30 days ahead, versus 60 days in the UK (raw lines 30–32). Product-specific fees differ for Platforms, Business accounts/Issuing and Capital (raw lines 34–54). In general, added criteria for Business accounts, Capital or Issuing must not exceed Adyen's defined requirements; any platform-specific request criteria should be disclosed before offering and submitted to Adyen for disclosure review (raw lines 56–63). Q2. Can these regional/product rules or the UK questionnaire be treated as current legal advice, a universal fee schedule, approved criteria, or proof of compliance?

A2. No. The page expressly disclaims legal advice (raw line 19), differentiates EU/US and UK notice periods (raw lines 30–32), applies distinct rules to Platforms, Business accounts/Issuing, EU/UK Business accounts instant payments and Capital (raw lines 34–54), and calls for review rather than automatic approval of added eligibility criteria (raw lines 56–63). The UK-only questionnaire gathers proposed pricing/fairness/communication details for onboarding; it does not show Adyen approval or a current tariff (raw lines 65–93).
