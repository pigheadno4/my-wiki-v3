# C27 vulnerable-user guidance retrieval checks

## Q1 — Scope and staff procedure

**Query:** Who does the Marketplace vulnerable-user page address, and how does it ask staff to respond?

**Answer:** The page is headed UK and Ireland only and states that it is Adyen compliance guidance, not legal advice (raw lines 15–21). Platforms are told to instruct directly user-facing support, sales/marketing staff and their managers (raw lines 29–37). It describes health, life events, resilience and capability drivers, BRUCE prompts for possible decision-making difficulty, TEXAS including explicit consent before recording shared information, IDEA follow-up questions, tailored communication and referral/signposting (raw lines 41–169). ## Q2 — Documentation and legal boundary

**Query:** May an agent diagnose a user from observed cues or promise extra time to pay, and does this page prove current compliance?

**Answer:** No. The page says vulnerability can be temporary, and observed examples are indicators for care rather than diagnoses (raw lines 58–69, 77–114). Records should reflect facts shared by the user, without proposed diagnoses or a named condition unless expressly disclosed, and internal privacy/legal teams should be consulted for domestic recording obligations (raw lines 171–200). Its extra-time-to-pay examples say to ask the finance team whether an extension can be given (raw lines 186–192). The page expressly disclaims legal advice and is a dated UK/Ireland guidance snapshot, not a current compliance determination (raw lines 15–19).
