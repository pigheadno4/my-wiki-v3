# docs-payouts-payout-service-settle-funds — worker retrieval checks

Q1. What is the guide's purpose and what must happen before funds reach a platform balance account?
A. It describes payment proceeds moving from Adyen stores through merchant accounts to the balance platform (raw lines 17–19). Payments arrive pending; after the configured settlement delay (T+2 by default), they become available in the merchant account. Adyen deducts fees, aggregates net proceeds into payable batches at the configured frequency, can generate a Settlement Details Report per batch, then settles each batch as one internal transfer to the balance account configured for that merchant account (lines 23–28). Q2. What are the money-flow and example boundaries?
A. The page stops at the internal transfer into the configured balance-platform account; it does not establish an onward external payout or beneficiary-bank receipt (raw lines 19, 27–28). Its hotel illustration gives EUR 285 and SEK 380 net settlements, but line 48 says “EUR 400.00” enters the SEK merchant account even though the guest payment and later fee/net values use SEK (lines 43, 48, 57–68). Keep this unresolved source inconsistency visible; do not infer a conversion. The example and T+2 default do not prove any actual merchant's configured timing or completed movement.
