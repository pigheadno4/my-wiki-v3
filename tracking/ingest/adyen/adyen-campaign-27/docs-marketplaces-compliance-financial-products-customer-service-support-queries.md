# docs-marketplaces-compliance-financial-products-customer-service-support — worker retrieval checks

Q1. What is the page's purpose and who provides support?
A. This is a 2024-modified Adyen compliance-guideline overview for financial products and Adyen for Platforms, expressly not legal advice (raw lines 15–21). The marketplace provides first- and second-line customer support, request/resolution channels and Adyen's customer-communications contact; it must resource, prioritize, track and hand off cases, and communicate multiple free, navigable channels with human access where needed (lines 21–46). Q2. Which 24/7, retention and regional limits qualify the guideline?
A. The source requires 24/7 ability to **raise** notifications about possible unauthorized use, fraud or lost/stolen credentials/cards; it does not promise a 24/7 staffed response or resolution (raw line 47). It requests support-query/response records for five years from Adyen Service termination and user-account closure unless longer retention applies, without resolving the two-event trigger (line 50). Extra vulnerable-customer training, disclosure notice, human contact and assistive-technology measures sit under the UK/Ireland heading (lines 52–62). This dated, non-legal-advice page is not current legal or implementation proof.
