Q1. What does the dated Marketplace Capital UI guideline require for offers, KYC and agreement acceptance?
Answer: It supplements the general financial-products UI guide. For eligible users, it requires contract type, Adyen-supplied dynamic/static offer fields, a business loan maximum/fixed repayment period with full repayment before it ends, and a product request before any financing offer. The interface must collect KYC for Adyen review, show offer-specific amount/repayment/fee terms, make Capital terms and Terms of Service available for acceptance, and capture acceptance information. Canada requires the fee as an annual interest rate (raw lines 19–59). Q2. What are the material information and Ireland precontract boundaries?
Answer: The guide calls for fund and repayment insights plus security controls, with GET grant details and webhooks as possible data routes; a rendered screen does not establish disbursement or repayment. In Ireland only, before the user becomes bound, it specifies credit-reporting statements, a formatted Central Credit Register notice for credit applications/agreements of €500 or more, amount lent and offer-validity length, and tick-box consent to the specified statement. The page disclaims legal advice and cannot establish current eligibility or valid agreement (raw lines 19, 81–123).
