Q1. What does the page ask a Marketplace to select, and when does it direct contact with Adyen?
A. The page says certain brands have unique MCCs captured by brand-specific ICs and asks selection of an IC matching the user's brand and business activity (raw lines 15–19). It directs an Adyen contact or Support when industry-code approval is needed before onboarding users or when the platform wants to offer business accounts in industries outside its previous approval process (lines 21–24).
