# Capital-path customer complaints — retrieval probes

## Q1 — Purpose and scope

**Query:** Is every fee-refund or chargeback request a complaint, and is this Capital-path page limited to Capital-only cases?

**Expected answer:** The 2024-modified page addresses platforms offering Adyen financial services even though it sits under Capital. An expression of dissatisfaction expecting response/resolution counts across channels and need not use the word complaint; a pure support or refund/chargeback request is not a complaint unless it expresses dissatisfaction or alleges unfair treatment. The page is dated Adyen guidance and disclaims legal advice; it does not prove a specific platform's compliance or a case outcome.

## Q2 — Timelines, escalation and country conditions

**Query:** What does the captured guideline ask a platform to do with a complaint, when must Adyen be involved, and do UK/Ireland timings replace every general timeline?

**Expected answer:** The platform provides accessible channels, identifies, acknowledges, investigates and updates complaints; Adyen expects acknowledgement within five business days, resolution within ten from submission and notice if longer, with durable records retained for the stated five-year-from-termination/account-closure period unless longer internal retention applies. Security and regulatory/legal complaints go to Adyen within one to two working days; other specified complaints are forwarded when Adyen's input/response role applies, with no-later-than-two-business-day information procedure. The responder table distinguishes Adyen's direct legal/privacy/regulator roles from platform handling of complaint aspects and technical/financial-crime issues. UK/Ireland adds an initial acknowledgement that states possible ombudsman referral if dissatisfied with a final response or unresolved within 15 working days, plus contact/decision wording, updates at most 15 business days apart and a separate 35-working-day unresolved notice; do not turn these additions into global rules or evidence of completed handling.
