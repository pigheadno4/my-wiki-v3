## Overview

This 2024-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] Capital-route compliance-guideline page describes events a **platform offering Capital** must notify Adyen about, quarterly complaints reporting relating to Adyen products, and additional UK/Ireland vulnerable-user reporting. See [[adyen-capital]] for the product route. It expressly disclaims legal advice; its dated deadlines and address are not a current-law determination or proof that a notification/report was submitted or accepted.

## Key takeaways

- The page says platforms offering Capital must notify Adyen **immediately (within 1–2 business days)** for a Security, Regulatory or Legal Complaint, using a dedicated complaints template. It also lists support circumstances to raise through normal Adyen support: unresolved user support request, unauthorized use/lost credentials/potential fraud or suspicious activity, disputed unauthorized payment or statement error, material incident/security or personal-data breach, an explicit user statement of trouble repaying a loan, and material user non-compliance. The trigger is occurrence/awareness of the listed circumstance, not a routine periodic report. [Raw: lines 19–37]
- For notifications handled through API requests, such as account blocking or termination, the page says **no additional reporting** is required. Do not apply this exception to unrelated complaint or support events, and do not infer that a sample API request was made. [Raw: line 38]
- **To the extent not prohibited by applicable law**, the platform must provide quarterly reporting to Adyen using its template for all user complaints relating to Adyen products. The reporting period begins when the first user is onboarded in the **live** environment. The captured page lists complaint-received/acknowledged/resolved/communicated dates, status, upheld/not-upheld outcome, user and legal-entity reference, country, product where applicable, category, description and resolution steps; a complaint must have an Upheld or Not upheld determination before closure. These fields are report requirements in this captured guideline, not evidence of an actual complaint or a live account. [Raw: lines 40–75]
- The captured due dates are **10 January, 10 April, 10 July and 10 October** for sending the template to the listed Adyen complaint-reporting address. Verify current applicable instructions and secure submission method before operational use; no submission is performed by this source entry. [Raw: lines 77–82]
- For **UK and Ireland**, complaint reporting must include vulnerable-user total and category breakdown (Health, Life Events, Resilience, Capability), and outcomes of requests for additional support. The page says **only total figures for vulnerable users** are required, with the same quarterly deadlines, and additionally requires annual sharing of specific vulnerability patterns that may need Adyen attention/action. Preserve the aggregated-figure limit rather than assuming individually identifiable vulnerable-user data must be sent. [Raw: lines 84–98]
