## Overview

This 2023-12-14-modified, 2026-09-15-captured Adyen financial-products compliance-guideline page defines platform collateral broadly and gives review, communication and country-specific provider-disclosure principles for Capital. It expressly disclaims legal advice. It is a dated provider guideline, not evidence that specific collateral was submitted, approved, published lawfully, or that any financing was offered/funded. See [[adyen]], [[adyen-capital]] and [[adyen-financial-products]].

## Key takeaways

- “Collateral material” covers user-visible communications about Adyen offerings through the platform UI, including web/app pages, marketing, statements, print/media, press and app-store listings. The page says all platform collateral must follow product principles and include relevant bank disclosures. For all financial products, it says materials must be Adyen-reviewed and pre-approved before publication/distribution; substantive changes and post-go-live additions require resubmission, with a narrow exception for typo/grammar or changes not affecting the Adyen product/service description or marketing (raw lines 21–47). This is the source's approval instruction, not proof an individual asset is approved.
- Materials must use plain language suited to target users, accurately describe terms, benefits, limits, availability and risks, substantiate dated research/claims, distinguish multiple services, and obtain written permission for real, dated testimonials with payment disclosure where applicable. The page cautions against unsupported absolutes, “free” when fees exist, and “pre-approved”/“guaranteed” or “protected”/“secure” unless conditions are clear; it permits “pre-qualified.” It also prohibits implying a consumer credit check is required or performed (raw lines 49–76).
- The dated layout instructions prevent hiding key information, call for readable contrasting disclosures, recommend rather than mandate specific 8-point paper/10-point online sizes, and include accessibility and direct prominent link guidance (raw lines 78–86). Preserve recommendation versus requirement.
- UK-only page/application wording distinguishes Adyen UK as regulated provider from the platform; Ireland-only web pages about Adyen-partner financial services accessed from Ireland have a specified Adyen N.V./regulator statement. The Capital matrix differentiates provider branches/entities across EU, US, Australia, UK and Canada, with a US credit-approval sentence, an Australian business-purpose qualification, UK Consumer Credit Act sentence and EU fixed-rate statement (raw lines 88–109). These are region/entity-specific snapshot directions, not one global legal characterization or current authorized wording.
