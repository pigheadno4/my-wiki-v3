## Overview

This complaints and escalation page is under Capital but addresses platforms offering one of Adyen's financial services. Last modified 2024-01-10 and captured 2026-09-15, it describes Adyen's required complaint-handling process, escalation to Adyen, responder allocation and added UK/Ireland steps. It expressly says it is not legal advice. Its “must” statements are dated Adyen guidelines, not current legal advice, proof that a platform followed them, or evidence of a resolved individual complaint. See [[adyen]], [[adyen-capital]] and [[adyen-financial-products]].

## Key takeaways

- **Recognize substance, not label:** A complaint is dissatisfaction with an Adyen product, service, term, communication or action where a response or resolution is expected, whether oral or written and whether or not the customer says “complaint.” A pure information/support request, including a fee refund or chargeback request, is listed as non-complaint unless it alleges unfair treatment or expresses dissatisfaction. The platform must identify complaints across its support channels and may direct a raised complaint to a form.
- **Handling and record:** The page says the platform must operate an accessible process, acknowledge and investigate, give the ultimate deadline and updates, and notify the user when resolution will take longer. Adyen expects acknowledgement within five business days, resolution within ten business days from submission, and notice if longer. It says to keep support-inquiry/complaint response records in durable accessible form for five years from termination of Adyen Service and user-account closure, unless the platform's own retention period is longer. These are page-level guidance timelines, not evidence that any case met them.
- **Escalation and roles:** Security and regulatory/legal complaints are to be reported to Adyen within one to two working days. The page also describes forwarding complaints that cannot be substantively resolved without Adyen or that fall within Adyen's response responsibility, with information no later than two business days after receipt. Its table assigns legal actions directly to Adyen but typically the complaint aspect to the platform; Adyen responds directly to privacy court/regulator/user matters and regulator/ombudsman referrals, while the platform handles the cited financial-crime and technical-issue complaints. Adyen's prepared final response is sent to the platform for delivery to the user under the guideline's timelines. The category determines who responds; “escalate” does not mean every complaint is answered directly by Adyen.
- **UK and Ireland additions:** For customers in the UK or Ireland, the page adds decision/settlement and ombudsman information within five working days after concluding an investigation, a point of contact and ombudsman details in acknowledgements, including a statement that the user may be entitled to ombudsman referral if dissatisfied with the platform's or Adyen's final response or if the complaint remains unresolved within 15 working days. It calls for investigation updates no more than 15 business days apart and a notice with anticipated timing/ombudsman details if unresolved after 35 working days. The BACS information request clause has a two-business-day response window. These are country-specific additions in the dated page, not globally applicable deadlines.
