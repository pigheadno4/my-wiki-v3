## Overview

This 2024-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] Capital compliance-guideline page says a platform offering Capital must keep detailed service records and retain them after the offering ends. It lists user-experience, marketing, communication/complaint and UK/Ireland vulnerable-customer record examples. See [[adyen-capital]] for the product route. The page expressly disclaims legal advice; the listed duties do not prove that a platform retained, delivered or retrieved any records or meets current law.

## Key takeaways

- While offering **Capital**, the platform **must** keep detailed service records and retain them for at least **five years after termination of the Capital offering**, unless its internal retention requirements specify longer. This Capital-offering trigger is distinct from the support/complaints guides' wording about Adyen Service termination and user-account closure; do not combine them into one assumed retention clock. [Raw: lines 19–21]
- User-experience examples are screenshots of deployed product versions, application flow, dashboard and support pages, but this screenshot requirement **applies only to API-only integrations**; the page says the records are **not needed when UI components are used**. This exception is specific to that category, not a waiver of the general service-record duty or other listed categories. [Raw: lines 23–27]
- The page also lists **all versions** of marketing material described in the linked Capital marketing guide, and user email/website/chat/support communications, complaint documents and notices of loan/charge-card action. These are categories to retain; the list is not evidence that such communications happened, were delivered to users, or were correctly logged. [Raw: lines 28–29]
- In the collapsed communications table entry, **UK and Ireland only** vulnerable-customer guidance says recording systems **must** be able to categorize signs of vulnerability during an interaction and **should** include at least Health, Life Events, Resilience and Capability. Communications from a vulnerable user **must** be accessible, in line with legal requirements, to platform staff responsible for assisting the user. Preserve the regional and modal distinctions; do not infer a diagnosis or individual consent from a category tag. [Raw: lines 29–32]
- The linked Capital compliance, interface, marketing, support and complaints guides own their underlying processes. This page provides record categories and a retention trigger, not storage architecture, an audit result, legal advice or proof of service performance. [Raw: lines 34–40]
