# Fixed retrieval checks — Capital keeping records

Q1. What is this source's product scope and retention trigger, and what must not be inferred?
- Expected: A 2024-modified, 2026-09-15-captured Capital compliance-guideline page, not the generic financial-products record page. It says the platform must keep detailed service records while offering Capital and for at least five years after termination of the **Capital offering**, unless longer internal retention applies. This differs from support/complaints pages' Adyen Service/user-account-closure wording. It expressly disclaims legal advice and does not prove actual retention, delivery, retrieval, compliant storage or current law.

Q2. Which record categories are named, and how do API-only and UK/Ireland qualifications change them?
- Expected: User-experience screenshots of deployed product/application/dashboard/support versions are required only for API-only integrations; UI component integrations do not need that screenshot category, but general service recordkeeping still applies. Retain all versions of described marketing material plus communications/complaint documentation and loan/charge-card action notices. For UK/Ireland vulnerable customers, systems **must** categorize signs during interaction and **should** include at least Health, Life Events, Resilience and Capability; communications received from such users **must** be accessible to assisting platform staff in line with legal requirements. These categories are not proof a communication happened or a record was kept.
