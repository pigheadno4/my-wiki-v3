# Capital pricing and eligibility — retrieval probes

## Q1 — Purpose and product scope

**Query:** Does this page authorize a platform to add disclosed fees to Adyen Capital, or establish a current tariff or approved offer?

**Expected answer:** No. It is a 2024-modified, 2026-09-15-captured Adyen guideline page, expressly not legal advice. Its general Platforms section discusses fee disclosure, but its Capital subsection prohibits direct or indirect additional user fees for Adyen Capital and requires Adyen's prior written approval for fee discounts. It does not prove current pricing, platform approval or a user's financing offer.

## Q2 — Country notices versus eligibility review

**Query:** What fee notices and eligibility-criteria process does the captured page state, and does submitting eligibility criteria approve Capital pricing?

**Expected answer:** It states at-least-30-day written increase notices for affected users in the EU/US and at-least-60-day notice in the UK, plus general advance fee and FX disclosure and UK-only reasonable-fee language. These are dated Platforms/product guidelines and do not permit extra Capital fees. It generally discourages criteria beyond Adyen's for Business accounts, Capital and Issuing; extra platform criteria should be submitted for Adyen review of proper disclosure, clearly shown to users beforehand and not discriminatory, unfair or deceptive. The related Capital compliance overview says Capital-request criteria require Adyen approval, which is distinct from pricing or fee approval. Its UK-only onboarding questionnaire concerns Adyen for Platforms pricing, not a Capital tariff.
