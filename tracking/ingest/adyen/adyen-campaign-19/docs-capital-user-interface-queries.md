## Q2 — Capital, country and acceptance conditions

**Query:** Which offer, term, consent and country-specific items must a Capital interface present, and can one-time user terms replace per-grant Terms of Service acceptance?

**Expected answer:** The guide calls for contract type, Adyen-provided offer fields, maximum repayment period, request-before-offer framing, KYC, fee/total repayment/percentage, terms and captured acceptance, plus ongoing Capital insights. It states Capital user terms are accepted one time with updates communicated, while the linked How Capital works guide requires Terms of Service acceptance for each new grant; do not collapse them. Canada requires annual-interest-rate presentation of the Capital fee. Ireland adds precontract credit-reporting notices, balance/validity detail and a tick-box consent; only an Authorized Signatory natural person can give Adyen consent. These are dated guidance conditions, not proof of acceptance or disbursement.
