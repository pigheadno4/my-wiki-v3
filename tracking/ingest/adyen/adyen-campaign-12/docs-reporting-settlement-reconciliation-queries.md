Q1: What is this page for, what scope does it cover, and what must it not establish?
A: A dated Adyen settlement-reconciliation navigation hub: configure payout frequency, then choose transaction-level or batch-level guidance; Adyen for Platforms merchants are sent to Platform reports. It does not define report columns, roles, time zone, exact payout timing, or prove a particular bank receipt. Retrieve [[source-adyen-docs-reporting-settlement-reconciliation]] through [[adyen-reporting]] → Sources → [[raw/adyen/docs/reporting/settlement-reconciliation/2026-09-15]] lines 14–33.

Q2: What are the two routes and the main prerequisite/warning?
A: Payout frequency should be configured before reconciliation. Transaction-level navigation promises payments/refunds/chargebacks and their costs within one payout batch; batch-level navigation promises payout-batch matching to a bank statement and batch commission costs. Those are route descriptions only; use their canonical source pages for fields/matching rules and actual bank evidence for a receipt.
