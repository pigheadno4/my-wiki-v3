## Q1. What is this page for, what is in scope, and what should not be inferred?

It documents Adyen's monthly **Payment processing invoice**, an itemized payment-service cost statement based on CET, for a company account's same-legal-entity merchant accounts, with a separate merchant invoice if the legal entity differs (raw `raw/adyen/docs/reporting/invoice-reconciliation/payment-processing-invoice/2026-09-15.md:15-23`). It covers exceptions/tax (lines 25–36), Customer Area access roles (lines 38–47), summary and detail cost/deduction fields (lines 49–70), and positive/negative Amount due with reasons for differences (lines 72–80). Do not infer merchant-of-record identity, actual payment of an invoice, final bank payout, universal invoice currency, a universal tax outcome, or completed accounting reconciliation. ## Q2. What is the central capability, prerequisites, and material warnings?

A user with **Merchant financial** or **Manage key financial tasks** role can view/download the invoice in Customer Area at **Reconciliation > Invoices** (raw lines 38–47). The invoice's summary shows fees/commissions and distinguishes **Already deducted from settlement** from **Amount due**; the details page explains calculated fees including discounts and deducted amounts, with optional merchant-account pages (lines 49–70). Amount due can be positive or negative; tiered pricing, month-end transaction commissions and price changes can explain differences, and Payment instructions describe next-batch treatment (lines 72–80). Adyen says terminal orders are separately invoiced and sales tax/VAT is not deducted from settlement batches, with jurisdiction-specific historical examples (lines 25–36). The invoice uses CET and groups by legal entity (lines 19–23); align those scopes and the actual invoice/report currencies when reconciling Interactive payment accounting and Settlement details. A stated future credit/debit is not evidence that it happened or reached a bank.
