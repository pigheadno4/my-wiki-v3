Q1: What is this page for, what scope does it cover, and what must it not establish?
A: A dated POS DCC reporting field guide for finding transaction DCC choice and DCC markup when DCC and its reporting are enabled. It does not provide the FX rate/formula, all processing fees, roles or report timing, proof of final settlement, or bank receipt. Retrieve [[source-adyen-docs-reporting-dcc-transactions-and-fees]] through [[adyen-reporting]] → Sources → [[raw/adyen/docs/reporting/dcc-transactions-and-fees/2026-09-15]] lines 15–28, 30–48.

Q2: Which report fields and warnings are central?
A: Settlement details report position 24/column W `DCC Markup (NC)` is markup in settlement currency, with negative meaning a merchant credit. Received payment details position 44/column AR `DCC` distinguishes accepted (shopper currency), declined (merchant currency), and not offered (shared currency). Enabling DCC may affect automated reconciliation; this source has no role/date/time-zone or conversion calculation, so use each report's full source before implementing joins or fee accounting.
