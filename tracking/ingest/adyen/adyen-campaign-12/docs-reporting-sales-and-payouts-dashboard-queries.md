## Q2 — What central values and drilldowns does it provide, with what qualifications?

The Sales tab totals amounts for `SentForSettle` and `Settled` transactions under Sales, and for `SentForRefund` and `Refunded` under Refunds; Sales minus Refunds is their difference, while Pending totals the two pending statuses (raw lines 33–42). Its day/week/month history bars distinguish pending, ready-for-payout and paid-out categories; a selected bar opens batch and payment-method details (lines 44–73). The Payouts tab instead shows the total monetary Sales amount for `Settled` transactions and total monetary Refunds amount for `Refunded` transactions. It also shows transaction costs, adjustments, payout amount, payout-history bars and batch details, without specifying an arithmetic formula among those summary values (lines 75–111). Batch CSV and bank instruction letter support tracing; the letter does not establish receiving-bank credit (lines 113–114).
