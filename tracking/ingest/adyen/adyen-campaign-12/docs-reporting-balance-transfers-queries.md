## Q2. What is the central capability, prerequisites, and material warnings?

The workflow finds the transfer **reason** in the Company Payout report and the **to/from batch entry** in Settlement details; optional balance notices are another route (raw lines 21–31, 33–46, 135–149). The page gives no universal Customer Area/report-access role; it names **Merchant financial** specifically for viewing liability-risk/deposit details under `MPL_EXCEEDED` (lines 75–89). The listed codes have different effects: several move a payout balance to the next batch, `MPL_EXCEEDED` moves an amount toward required deposit, and negative balances or payout-account/currency problems have separate explanations and actions (lines 48–133). `Modification Merchant Reference` identifies to/from batch; `Net Credit` is for positive and `Net Debit` for negative transfers, in `Net Currency` (lines 135–149). The paired EUR 4.19 debit rows are examples, not a universal sign pairing (lines 151–163). A next-batch entry does not confirm a bank transfer or final payout.
