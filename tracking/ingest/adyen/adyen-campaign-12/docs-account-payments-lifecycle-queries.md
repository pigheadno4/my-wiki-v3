Q1: What is this page for, what scope does it cover, and what must it not establish?
A: A dated Adyen Customer Area status guide spanning offer, authorization, capture/settlement, refund, dispute, online payout, and stored-value stages. It shows how to track an entry by PSP/merchant reference and journal, with View Payments or View key payments data role. It is not a live transaction record, universal proof of merchant payout, shopper receipt, or final financial effect for every operation. Q2: What stage transitions and financial-finality warnings are central?
A: Offer Received/Open precede authorization; Authorised reserves funds but capture can still be pending or expire. SentForSettle awaits funds; Settled means Adyen received them, not merchant payout. Refunded says merchant debit and institution's intention to credit, which may take up to 40 days for shopper receipt. Online payout ReceivedPayout/PayoutAuthorised/SentForPayout precede PaidOut; that flow is not ordinary merchant settlement. Sales day payout-specific SettleScheduled/RefundScheduled/PaidOutScheduled appear under other names in reports; failures/reversals can require further investigation.
