# Fixed retrieval checks — Adyen donation campaign reporting

## Q1 — What is this page for, what is its scope, and what should not be inferred?

The 2026-09-15 Adyen Donation campaigns page explains a Giving campaign insights dashboard, a per-donation Processed Donations Report and a nonprofit-only grouped Received Donations Report. A campaign must already be set up; the dashboard and reports have distinct audiences and grains (raw lines 23–45, 60–85, 125–152). The report wording about settlement or payout is not, by itself, proof that a specific donation reached a bank or that a reconciliation/accounting conclusion is final. Canonical route: `[[index]]` → `[[adyen-index]]` → `[[adyen-reporting]]` → `[[source-adyen-docs-reporting-donation-campaigns]]` → `[[raw/adyen/docs/reporting/donation-campaigns/2026-09-15]]`; canonical URL: https://docs.adyen.com/reporting/donation-campaigns.

## Q2 — What are the central reporting capabilities, prerequisites and warnings?

Donation Campaigns Viewer can inspect campaigns and generate Processed reports, Manager can also manage them, and Merchant financial can generate Received reports (donation raw lines 27–39). The donation guide's blanket claim that these roles do not give access to other payment information conflicts with the separate [[source-adyen-docs-account-user-roles|User roles reference]]: Merchant financial also has access to payouts, invoices, balances, payment analytics and reports (raw/adyen/docs/account/user-roles/2026-09-15.md:76–92). That reference separately says Donation campaigns viewer and manager do not give access to payment information (lines 67–74). Verify actual grants before assigning Merchant financial; do not treat it as donation-only. The per-campaign dashboard can lag up to four hours, so the page recommends `DONATION` webhooks for real-time information (donation raw lines 41–58). Both downloadable reports are company-account-only and apply the account time zone and selected date range (lines 68–77, 136–144). Processed rows represent single donations, with donation PSP reference and processing currency/amount; its configurable commercial-transaction PSP reference points to the separate triggering payment (lines 81–123). Received rows group donations for nonprofits, with original-donation gross currency, settlement net currency, exchange rate and count (lines 125–172). Column changes can break automated consumers and require listed report-configuration roles (lines 174–193). Use the same concept→source→raw route, and `[[adyen-giving]]` for the distinct donation transaction and webhook outcome context.
