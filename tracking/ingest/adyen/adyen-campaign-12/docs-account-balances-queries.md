Q2: What are the five balance identities, formula, conditions, and warning?
A: Available funds are the sum of pending (unsettled gross sales/refunds), next payout (changing amount slated for next bank payout), reserve (merchant-configured operational cover), and Adyen-eligible negative refund allowance where applicable. Deposit is separate withheld funds against processing exposure, with Adyen-determined daily requirement; increases book sales into deposit and decreases release excess to next payout. Reserve availability and a projected next payout are not bank receipt. This raw does not specify viewing roles or currency-conversion/aggregation mechanics; route those to the separate viewing guide.
