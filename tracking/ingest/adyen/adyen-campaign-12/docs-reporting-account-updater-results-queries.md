Q1: What is this page for, what scope does it cover, and what must it not establish?
A: A dated Adyen reference for the Account Updater results report: date-bounded aggregate outcomes/statuses and fees for batch and realtime updater requests at company or merchant scope, with daily/weekly/monthly CET reporting. It is not a per-card payment result or guarantee that later authorization or settlement will succeed. Q2: What are the report's central counts, prerequisites, dates, and fee warnings?
A: Viewing needs Merchant view Account Updater Reports (Adyen) role. First three lines contain summary metadata; entries after the header hold grouped values. Service is batch/realtime; creation date is request submission period, booking date is response receipt, responses received count through report end, and pending counts no response by that end. Result counts cover changed number/expiry, closed, Visa contact-cardholder, no change and technical error. Fee currency/per-response fees and monthly-invoice total are distinct from payment or bank receipt.
