# Fixed retrieval checks — docs-account-balances-next-payout

## Q1. What is this page for, what is in scope, and what should not be inferred?

It defines the merchant-account **Next payout** balance as the **current** amount to be settled to a bank account at the next payout moment, changing with sales, refunds and adjustments (raw `raw/adyen/docs/account/balances/next-payout/2026-09-15.md:15-19`). It covers negative-balance causes and possible effects (lines 21–33), conditional Reserve auto-funding and linked alternatives (lines 35–40). It does not specify a universal payout frequency, cutoff, currency, positive amount or bank arrival; a balance display does not prove completed settlement, a successful bank debit, legal ownership or merchant-of-record identity. ## Q2. What is the central capability, prerequisites, and material warnings?

The guide explains why a Next payout position can be negative when merchant-account expenses exceed processed payments. It lists chargebacks/refunds/payouts, invoice deductions and transaction fees including refund fees (raw lines 27–31). A negative balance **may** restrict refunds, payouts or receipt of a deposit (line 33). If Reserve auto-funding **has been configured**, the guide says the needed amount will be debited from the payout bank account; otherwise it links to another positive merchant-account balance transfer or account top-up procedures (lines 35–40). These are conditional mechanisms, not evidence of actual setup, successful transfer/debit, a final payable amount, or a bank receipt. Payout cadence and currency conditions require the separate account/payout guides.
