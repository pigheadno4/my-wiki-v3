## Overview

This 2026-09-15 [[adyen]] Docs reporting landing page routes merchants to transaction-information reports and reporting guidance. It groups getting started, financial, and operational topics; it does not define report row grain, fields, permissions, balances, settlement status, or accounting treatment. [[adyen-reporting]]

## Key takeaways

- Getting-started links point to finding reports in Customer Area, mapping reports to payment-lifecycle stages, and setting a report time zone. These are routes to detailed pages rather than rules specified here.
- Financial links point to a sales-to-payouts dashboard, settlement reconciliation, invoice reconciliation, and fiscal-period closing. Operational links point to 3D Secure, disputed-transaction, and balance-transfer reports. The page's short descriptions identify use cases but do not establish that a given dashboard row proves payout arrival, that report data is complete, or that a specific accounting entry is correct.
