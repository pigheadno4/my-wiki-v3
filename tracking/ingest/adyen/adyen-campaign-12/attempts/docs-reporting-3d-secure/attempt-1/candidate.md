## Key takeaways

- The page says report data can help users analyze authentication and conversion rates when they use 3D Secure authentication. It does not define a rate formula, denominator, sampling rule, or proof that a specific transaction authenticated or paid.
- The **3DS Authentication report** route is labeled **Latest version** in this captured snapshot and described as explaining why transactions were authenticated and analyzing authentication performance. That label is snapshot navigation, not an independent current-version guarantee or permission to combine it with the older report's counts.
- The separate **3D Secure Authentication report** link is described as explaining why 3DS transactions were authenticated, and the **Conversion report** link promises detailed conversion information. Follow each linked report for its own scope; these brief descriptions do not provide field definitions or establish payment authorization, capture or settlement outcomes.
