Use Settlement details to reconcile **each installment** (raw line 56), finance reports in installments to read account credits/debits and status categories (lines 58–64), Forecast for estimated **net** amounts/dates subject to refunds and chargebacks (lines 66–78), and Payment accounting with the specified enabled columns to track open installments (lines 80–99). The guide's enablement list says singular `Installment` (line 82), but its sequence description says `Installments` (line 90); the separate Payment accounting schema names the sequence column `Installments` (its raw line 141), so verify configured columns rather than assuming an alias. `Installments (PC)` negatives indicate refund debits; `ACCELL` is refund-request acceleration and `RETRY` an installment capture retry classification, not success proof (lines 86–97). Advancements are optional upfront funding and require account-manager/Support contact for enablement and fees; the guide states receipt within one day but does not verify any merchant's receipt (lines 101–105). They span batches sharing an advancement code.
