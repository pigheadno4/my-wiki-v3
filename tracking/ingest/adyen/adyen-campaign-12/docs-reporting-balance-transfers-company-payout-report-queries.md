Q1 — What is this report for, at what account and row grain, and what must not be inferred? The Company Payout report is available at company-account level to summarize successful, returned and non-executed merchant payouts. Overview counts journal types and payout statuses; MerchantPayout, BankInstructionReturned and BalanceTransfer sheets have payout, returned-payout and non-executed-payout rows respectively. Its one-to-one relationship to merchant-account Settlement detail entries supports reconciliation, not bank-receipt proof; a company-account report is not a company-level payout. Q2 — What capabilities, fields, conditions and warnings matter? The merchant-payout status is a report-generation snapshot: Credited is Adyen's final successful status; Failed needs a later status check; Rejected is final, while Sent and Debit Confirmed are earlier stages. MerchantPayout rows include merchant Account Code, payout Currency and Amount, batch, bank instruction reference, Status and Status Date. BankInstructionReturned's Returned Date means booking back to Payable, usually arrival at Adyen's bank; its Reason Code is conditional on bank provision. BalanceTransfer means non-executed payout with a reason code, not a confirmed inter-account transfer. Amount sign/subunit rules are not supplied.
