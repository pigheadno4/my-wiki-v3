## Q1 — Purpose, scope, and what not to infer

**Question:** What is the Aggregate settlement details report's grain, and what does it establish?

**Answer:** This 2026-09-15 capture describes a **batch-level** reconciliation report for payout batches in a selected date range. For each settlement batch, it separates credits, debits and transaction counts by merchant account, store, terminal, payment method, journal type and creation date (raw lines 15–28). It is available for company and merchant accounts, manually or automatically, with the account time-zone setting and Customer Area date selection (lines 30–36). Each post-header line is an aggregate entry, not a transaction-level payment record (lines 44–64). A `MerchantPayout` entry or payout-reference field can support matching to bank-statement text but does not by itself prove a bank deposit arrived (lines 75–79, 101–109). ## Q2 — Central capability, prerequisites, and warnings

**Question:** Which date, currency, amount, fee and access distinctions matter when consuming or configuring the report?

**Answer:** The report range corresponds to `Batch Closed Date`; `Creation Date` means sale day for payment-level bookings but Adyen booking timestamp for balance-level bookings, and `TimeZone` describes Creation Date using the account setting (raw lines 75–79). Gross Currency is transaction currency and Net Currency is settlement currency; gross and net debit/credit are separate columns. The raw describes Net Debit/Credit as request amount less Bank/Card Commission; commission includes markup, scheme fee and interchange. A negative DCC Markup means account credit, not a general sign rule (lines 90–99). Store/terminal are empty for balance-level entries such as `MerchantPayout` or fees, or when those dimensions are disabled (lines 73–74). Optional Payable booking date, MerchantPayout-only payout reference and Settlement Flow can be added; configuring columns/order can break automated consumers and needs Merchant admin, Merchant report, Merchant financial or Merchant POS Report role (lines 101–130). These are configuration roles; this page does not supply a distinct report-generation permission. The sample may omit account-specific extra data or columns (lines 38–42).
