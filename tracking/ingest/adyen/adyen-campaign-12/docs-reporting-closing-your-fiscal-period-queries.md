# Fixed retrieval checks — Closing your fiscal period

Q1. Which report does Adyen's page direct a merchant to for fiscal-period closing, and what should not be inferred?
- Expected: A period ending on the month's last day routes to the Monthly finance report; other period-end dates route to the Daily finance report. A balances dashboard is mentioned for manual or spreadsheet-free review. Several account registers exist, and the merchant must identify relevant balances. This navigation does not establish accounting advice, financial sign-off, bank arrival or current access.

Q2. How do the report sheets, date ranges and balance rows support review without proving financial finality?
- Expected: Payment balances shows account-register tables under account, included date range and currency; registers have opening/end balances, with journal types, counts and monetary amounts for movement. The separate Balances sheet shows monthly opening/end balance; the daily wording specifically says opening balance of report date and first day of month. A report date or row is not a bank transfer or independent accounting sign-off.
