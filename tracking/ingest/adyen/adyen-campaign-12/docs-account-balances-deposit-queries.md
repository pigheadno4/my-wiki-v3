Q1 — What does this source cover and what must it not establish? It describes Adyen's merchant-account deposit, which Adyen withholds to cover potential processing losses and recalculates rather than letting the merchant choose it as with a reserve. The guide describes conditional accounting and payout effects, not a specific merchant's current requirement, a completed transfer or a guaranteed six-month return. Raw lines 17–23, 39–45, 47–54. Q2 — What is the central calculation and what are the prerequisites or warnings? Adyen compares estimated exposure with coverage daily before payout. A gap over EUR10,000 triggers next-payout withholding for the full uncovered difference in the example (EUR30,000 minus EUR18,000 = EUR12,000), while a lower requirement releases excess into the next payout amount. Exposure includes fulfillment and forecast reversals; coverage includes pending/payable balances, actual deposit, case-reviewed bank guarantees and accepted risk. Dynamic delivery-date deposit requires `deliveryDate`; viewing deposit details needs the Merchant financial role in the live Customer Area. Amounts and payout/release timing remain conditional.
