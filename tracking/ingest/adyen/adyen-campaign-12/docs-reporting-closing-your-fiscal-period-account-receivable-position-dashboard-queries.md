## Q2. What is the central capability, prerequisites, and material warnings?

The dashboard generates account/date-specific open-position overviews and a downloadable CSV of underlying transactions. First access requires configuring a monthly schedule; later additional past-date views can be manually generated with a selected time zone (raw lines 34–78). The guide requires an online payments or Terminal API integration and **Merchant Financial** role; historical views are stated as generatable anytime after July 1, 2025 (without specifying the earliest position date), Advancements availability at the end of March 2026, and India processing excluded (lines 21–32). **Total receivable** is captured but unpaid transactions, converted to account base currency; **total balance** adds reserve and deposit, which have different purposes and must not be collapsed into receivable (lines 84–103). The CSV is retrieved from the dashboard, with neither report-ready webhook nor SFTP delivery; optional columns and balance-type/amount definitions need the exact raw table (lines 105–145). A `Payable` type refers to next-batch availability, not completed bank payout (lines 133–136). These are snapshot claims and not current eligibility or financial-finality guarantees.
