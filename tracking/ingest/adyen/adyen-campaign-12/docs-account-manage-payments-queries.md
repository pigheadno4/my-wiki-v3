Q1: What is this page for, what scope does it cover, and what must it not establish?
A: A dated Adyen Customer Area guide to lookup, payment detail/refusal inspection, and submission of refund, cancel, capture, and re-authorization actions. It names API alternatives for refund, cancel, and capture, while documenting re-authorization through Customer Area. It lists role and payment-state eligibility, but a UI action or submit button does not prove final modification, merchant funds transfer, shopper credit, or safe retry. Q2: Which roles, state gates, amount limits, and warnings control these actions?
A: Refund needs Merchant manage payments or Refund payments role and captured state SentForSettle/Settled; amount cannot exceed captured amount. Cancel requires Merchant manage payments or Cancel payments, separate-capture support, and uncaptured Authorised status. Capture requires Merchant manage payments or Capture payments and Authorised status, with amount/reference input. Re-authorization needs Merchant re-authorise payments, an eligible latest journal state and listed card/SEPA method; issuer refusal and merchant-initiated chargeback liability remain possible. Export Payments and Merchant view PII are distinct viewing privileges.
