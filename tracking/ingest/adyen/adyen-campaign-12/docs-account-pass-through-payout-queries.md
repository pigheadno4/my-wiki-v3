## Q1. What is this page for, what is in scope, and what should not be inferred?

It describes Adyen **Pass-through payout** for merchant-account sales proceeds, where scheme/payment-method settlement timing controls when sales enter payable batches (raw `raw/adyen/docs/account/pass-through-payout/2026-09-15.md:15-30`). It states that one sales day can span several batches, so day-total reconciliation is unavailable and automated reconciliation is needed (lines 19–24). Adjustments and holiday delays are covered at lines 34–46. This is a merchant-settlement model, not a recipient payout API or a legal-ownership/MOR definition; the example dates do not establish an exact schedule, and assignment or a payout instruction does not prove bank receipt. ## Q2. What is the central capability, prerequisites, and material warnings?

The model assigns a sale to a payable batch when its scheme or payment method sends funds to Adyen; the batch closes according to the merchant's configured payout frequency and Adyen describes payout to that merchant (raw line 28). No separate API request, recipient onboarding step, fixed cutoff, universal currency, or eligibility condition is supplied on this page. One day's sales may enter multiple batches and one batch may include several sales days; the January 1–7 daily-frequency example is illustrative (lines 19–30). Refunds, chargebacks and other adjustments enter the payable batch as they occur, with Payable balance linked for current-batch information (lines 34–38). A currency-specific bank holiday from capture day through payout day delays timing, potentially by one or more scheme/method settlement days (lines 40–46). Use the actual account payout frequency, currency and calendar before planning timing, and verify independent bank evidence for receipt.
