## Q2

What do the response/errors establish, and which lifecycle, deletion, verification/approval, propagation, asynchronous financial outcome, version/environment or missing-evidence boundaries prevent broader inference?

Answer: 200 says request succeeded; 202 says accepted for processing but not completed (raw lines 36–75). `originalReference` denotes the original transfer; optional returned `pspReference` uniquely identifies the refund request, and other listed response fields are optional (lines 41–75). Errors listed are 400, 401, 403, 422 and 500 (lines 78–140). The endpoint snapshot does not itself specify the eventual-result route. The related dated Classic guide says the initial refund response only acknowledges receipt; `REFUND_FUNDS_TRANSFER` supplies the outcome, and successful reversal has a `FundTransferReversed` journal entry (guide raw lines 139–151). This does not prove any particular reversal, recipient balance, external payout or bank outcome.
