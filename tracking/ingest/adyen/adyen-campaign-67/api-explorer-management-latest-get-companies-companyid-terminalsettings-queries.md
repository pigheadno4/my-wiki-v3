## Q1

For `GET /companies/{companyId}/terminalSettings`, the 2026-11-05-captured [[source-adyen-api-explorer-management-latest-get-companies-companyid-terminalsettings]] is a Management API company-settings read on the listed TEST `https://management-test.adyen.com/v3` server. The required string `companyId` identifies the company account; there is no query or body. The caller needs **Management API—Terminal settings read** or **Terminal settings read and write**, plus **Terminal settings Advanced read and write** for sensitive settings. Configured company values apply to terminals unless overridden at merchant, store or device level. Live requests have a rate-limit notice without a number. A `200` response has optional top-level settings categories for receipts, connectivity, hardware, localization, `nexo`, offline processing, refunds, standalone, surcharge and Wi-Fi, among others. Optional `surcharge.configurations` entries have required `brand` and `currencies`; optional Wi-Fi `profiles` entries have required `authType`, `bssType`, `ssid` and `wsec`. ## Q2

A `200` reports configured company-level settings; it does not change them or prove lower-level effective settings, terminal application, device readiness, authorization, capture or settlement. Authentication and permission failures are `401` and `403`; the reference also lists `400`, `422` and `500`. No idempotency, deletion, reversal, asynchronous approval/activation or propagation timing is established by this GET. The response schema itself confines `connectivity.simcardStatus` to terminal-level reads on cellular-capable models. DCC carries receipt/terms responsibility, first-time contactless currency change requires Adyen compliance checking, and unreferenced refunds carry merchant liability; readback is not compliance proof. The `storeAndForward` request/inheritance condition must not be converted into a required GET response field. The example's passcodes, Wi-Fi credentials and URL strings are illustrative. This TEST snapshot and Live rate-limit notice do not establish a Live URL, current eligibility or financial result.
