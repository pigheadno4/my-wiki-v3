# Klarna chargebacks fixed-query answers

1. **Purpose, scope, and limits.** This 2026-09-15 Adyen snapshot describes Klarna disputes for physical-goods business models. Klarna's upfront payment and non-payment-risk protection depends on meeting order-fulfillment rules; rule violations can lead to chargeback (`raw/adyen/docs/risk-management/chargeback-guidelines/klarna-chargebacks/2026-09-15.md:15-19`). It documents a pre-November 2026 notification → RFI → defense → Klarna decision → possible first chargeback route, alongside changes announced for 1 November 2026 and further changes announced for March 2027 (`:21-36`). Do not combine the earlier 14-day standard RFI deadline with the announced 21-day standard window, infer that the announced process is already active for a case, or treat the snapshot as proof of current Klarna eligibility or policy (`:23-29`, `:67-77`).

2. **Retrieval route, conditions, and detail.** `wiki/index.md:5-10` → `wiki/adyen-index.md:150-154` → `wiki/concepts/adyen-risk-management.md:18-53` → proposed `wiki/sources/adyen/source-adyen-docs-risk-management-chargeback-guidelines-klarna-chargebacks.md` → pinned raw above. The proposed concept `## Sources` entry completes the reciprocal route after promotion. In the earlier flow, Dispute Notification gives 21 days for shopper resolution or 7 days for returns; unauthorized and high-risk complaints escalate faster, and `notification_return` is created only if the dispute stays open 14 days after its event (`:42-65`). At RFI, the guide requires information and gives 96 hours for `high_risk_order`, 7 days for `unauthorized_purchase`, and 14 days for other reasons; an unanswered RFI can produce a chargeback deducted from the next payable batch (`:67-77`). The announced November flow removes notification, changes the standard RFI window to 21 days for most reasons while retaining those two exceptions, and limits evidence to PDF files of at most 7 MB each (`:21-29`). For defense, use the reason templates, submit PDF via Disputes API or Customer Area, and account for the single irreversible submission (`:100-117`). Adyen forwards evidence, Klarna decides, and a rejected defense leads to an account debit and non-defensible final chargeback (`:119-131`). The snapshot leaves the applicable current case phase, actual deadline, and outcome to case-specific evidence.
