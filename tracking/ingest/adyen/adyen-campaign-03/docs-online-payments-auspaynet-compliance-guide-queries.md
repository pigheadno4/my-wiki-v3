1. **Purpose, scope, and limits.** This 2026-09-15 Adyen snapshot supplements the linked AusPayNet CNP code and says it is not legal advice (`raw/adyen/docs/online-payments/auspaynet-compliance-guide/2026-09-15.md:14-20`). Its stated scope requires a CNP transaction both issued and acquired in Australia; card-present, MOTO, externally issued or acquired, corporate, gift, and prepaid-card transactions are excluded (`:22-33`). The guide does not verify current legal obligations, a merchant's present fraud-threshold status, an available exemption, or a particular authentication result.
