# C03 retrieval checks: Afterpay and Clearpay chargebacks

1. **What is the purpose and scope, and what cannot be inferred?** This Adyen Docs snapshot covers Afterpay disputes in Australia, Canada, and New Zealand, Cash App Afterpay in the United States, and Clearpay in the United Kingdom (raw lines 15–29). For disputes opened on or after August 25, 2025, Notification of Chargeback replaces Request for Information; disputes still in the 13-day defense period on that date remain on the old flow until finalized (lines 19–24). Shoppers can initiate within the stated 120-day window; merchants have 13 days to accept or defend; Afterpay/Clearpay has 30 days after defense to decide (line 29). This does not establish a particular dispute's outcome, a numeric fee for every case, or rules for another payment method or region. Fraud liability is conditional on successful pre-approval risk checks, and the no-fee exception is stated for unauthorized-payment disputes (lines 39–43, 86–90).
