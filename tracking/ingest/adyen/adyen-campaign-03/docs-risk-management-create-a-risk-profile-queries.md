1. **Purpose, scope, and limits.** This 2026-09-15 Adyen Protect snapshot explains risk profiles as sets of risk rules and settings, created/configured at company-account level and assigned to one or more merchant accounts (`raw/adyen/docs/risk-management/create-a-risk-profile/2026-09-15.md:15-23`). The guide assumes an online-payments integration, enabled risk management, and a listed Customer Area risk role; some controls require Protect premium, while in-person profile creation follows separate considerations (`:25-50`). It does not prove a merchant's premium enablement, a payment's risk decision, or authorization success. The JSON request values are examples (`:99-146`).
