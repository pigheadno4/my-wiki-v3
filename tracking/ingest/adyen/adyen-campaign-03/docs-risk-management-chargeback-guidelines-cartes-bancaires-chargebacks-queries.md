It does not establish a merchant-submitted defense flow, a response deadline, or rules for a CB card transaction processed on Visa or Mastercard. The existing generic CB concept's “Cannot contest” claim comes from a Stripe source and should be read as Stripe-scoped, not as a rebuttal of Adyen's separate automatic-defense handling.
