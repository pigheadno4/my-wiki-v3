## Overview

This [[adyen]] Chargeback guidelines landing page defines a chargeback as an action initiated by the issuing bank or payment method on behalf of a cardholder to collect funds back. It routes readers to scheme- and payment-method-specific guidelines. The 2026-09-15 snapshot is a directory; the linked guides carry their own procedures, and this page gives no shared defense deadline or outcome rule. See [[adyen-risk-management]].

## Key takeaways

- Adyen says schemes have their own chargeback guidelines. The directory includes card-network guides such as Mastercard and Visa and payment-method guides such as ACH Direct Debit and PayPal. Follow the applicable linked guide for its scope and requirements.
