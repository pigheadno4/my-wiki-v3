The actor selects a merchant account and a dispute in **Risk & disputes > Disputes** (`:38-49`), then can accept or upload evidence for a defensible chargeback (`:49-64,66-88`). The page does not specify a regional restriction; applicable timeframes and evidence requirements are delegated to linked scheme/reason-code guidance (`:77,120-124`). It was fetched 2026-09-15 with last_modified 2023-04-14 (`:1-12`) and does not prove current Customer Area access, scheme rules, case eligibility, an upload result, or a won dispute. An upload books *InformationSupplied* and makes an RFI *Responded* or chargeback *Pending*, which are intermediate states (`:82-84`).

Pre-arbitration is conditional on eligibility, flow support and Adyen approval; accepting loses with a second chargeback, while declining can reach arbitration, where new evidence may be ignored and a captured fee up to USD 600 may apply if the issuer wins (`:101-118`).
