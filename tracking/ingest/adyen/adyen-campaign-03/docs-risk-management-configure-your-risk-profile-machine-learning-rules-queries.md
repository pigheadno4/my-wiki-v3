# Machine-learning risk rules fixed-query answers

1. **Purpose, scope, and limits.** This 2026-09-15 Adyen Protect snapshot describes two online-payment machine-learning rules: bot attack risk for basic/premium Protect, with no configuration options, and fraud risk for premium Protect (`raw/adyen/docs/risk-management/configure-your-risk-profile/machine-learning-rules/2026-09-15.md:21-46`). It assumes an online-payments integration and enabled risk management; changing fraud-rule settings requires one of the listed Customer Area risk roles (`:21-30`). Fraud risk currently runs on disputable transactions such as card payments, assigns a level before authorization, and blocks when risk exceeds the selected threshold unless an Allow rule triggered before authorization (`:43-48`, `:54-60`). It cannot detect every fraud case and does not prove premium enablement, a specific classification, or a payment's authorization outcome (`:60`).
