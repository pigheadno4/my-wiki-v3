# PULSE chargebacks fixed-query answers

1. **Purpose, scope, and limits.** This 2026-09-15 Adyen snapshot describes PULSE's default dispute flow and separate fraud-reason-code 4868 flow (`raw/adyen/docs/risk-management/chargeback-guidelines/pulse-chargebacks/2026-09-15.md:15-42`). Both begin with Notification of Chargeback (NoC), then first chargeback and defense; the merchant has 30 days after NoC to defend or accept (`:38`, `:55-74`, `:110-131`). The guide is scheme-specific snapshot evidence, not proof of current scheme rules, a case's eligibility, fee schedule, or outcome. NoC warning of a forthcoming debit and first-chargeback debit/journals describe the same chargeback sequence, not two independent debits (`:55-65`, `:110-122`).
