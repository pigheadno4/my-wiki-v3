# C03 retrieval checks: TWINT chargebacks

1. **What is the purpose and scope, and what cannot be inferred?** This 2026-09-15 Adyen Docs snapshot explains the TWINT chargeback path. A shopper may initiate with TWINT up to 100 days after payment, refund, or expected receipt; when TWINT opens the dispute, the merchant has 10 calendar days to defend and funds have not yet been debited (raw lines 15–21). If the merchant does not respond, the dispute is lost and funds are charged back; a timely successful defense has no debit (line 21). The page says there are *currently* no TWINT chargeback fees, which is a dated provider statement rather than later fee-policy proof (line 21). It cannot determine a particular case's deadline or outcome, and the TWINT clocks should not be applied to other schemes. The 45-day path is conditional on no TWINT loss notice after `AcquirerPreArbitrationUploaded`, not an unconditional win timer (line 23).
