1. **Purpose and scope.** This Adyen guide describes how a merchant handles a UnionPay/CUP chargeback, from optional RFI through NoC, defense, automatic pre-arbitration and possible reversal. Its flow applies to UnionPay chargebacks **received on or after 13 April 2020**; earlier ones are not defendable through Adyen. It gives a scheme-specific workflow, not a rule for other schemes, a guarantee of current account eligibility, or a prediction of a particular dispute result. Central action: answer an optional RFI with recognition/validity evidence; failing to answer makes a later chargeback for that transaction undefendable (`:35-49`).
