1. **Purpose and scope.** This Adyen NYCE guide explains the merchant's chargeback sequence from Notification of Chargeback (NoC) through first chargeback, evidence submission, provisional reversal and possible second chargeback. The 30-day defense period starts at NoC, and a second chargeback may follow within 30 days of reversal if the first was defended. These are NYCE-specific rules; do not infer other schemes' deadlines or that a reversal is already final.
