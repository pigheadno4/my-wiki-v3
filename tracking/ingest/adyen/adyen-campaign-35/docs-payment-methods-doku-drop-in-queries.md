Q1. What is the DOKU Drop-in page's purpose and scope, and what cannot be inferred?
A1. It adds Indonesian DOKU bank-transfer and convenience-store methods to an existing Web Drop-in, with Sessions or Advanced base integration and Customer Area method setup (raw lines 17–39). It does not prove current variant eligibility, an executed transfer or cash payment, an authorization webhook, settlement or fulfillment; status and webhook links are navigation, not observed outcomes (raw lines 66–78).
