## Q2. What is the central capability, prerequisites, and material warnings?

OOBO lets a service employee place a Salesforce order with card entry or generate an Adyen-hosted Pay by Link for the shopper (raw lines 19–21,44–76). The guide requires an Adyen SFCC SFRA/SiteGenesis cartridge, installed OMS package, connected OMS Org, Standard webhooks and API PCI Payments role; Pay by Link also needs a Customer Area Merchant admin or Pay by Link Settings role and employee credential/permission setup (lines 23–35,78–98,142–146). Pay by Link requires OMS **v3.2.0+** and the listed snapshot methods; upgrading from v3.1.0 or earlier to v3.2.0 for this feature requires webhook updates (lines 33,37–42,98). The shopper account needs email and matching store/account currency; the merchant must share the generated link through its own logic (lines 63–74). WebStore and Payment Gateway IDs are joined through `StoreIntegratedService` (lines 107–140). The default link expiry is 24 hours; Expire or Regenerate invalidates all prior order links (lines 148–202). Conditional 403/permission diagnostics are at lines 204–234. Configuration, link creation and the page's card-verification wording do not prove authentication exemption, paid order, capture or settlement.
