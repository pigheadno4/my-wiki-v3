## Q1. What is this page for, what is in scope, and what should not be inferred?

This historical Adyen guide interprets Japan Credit Card Security Guidelines 5.0 for online **credit cards processed over Japan acquiring connections**, citing April 1, 2025, and also describes in-person verification changes (raw `raw/adyen/docs/industries/markets/japan/credit-card-security-guidelines-compliance-guide/2026-09-15.md:15-44`). It is expressly not legal advice (line 21). It does not impose a universal current 3DS2 rule across card types, acquiring regions or channels, certify a merchant's configuration, prove a specific payment's authentication/authorization, or establish PCI DSS status. ## Q2. What is the central capability, prerequisites, and material warnings?

The page says in-scope online credit-card payments on Japan acquiring connections require 3DS2, including cards issued outside Japan, while qualifying out-of-scope and recurring transactions separately (raw lines 19,31–35,46–108). Its recurring tables distinguish initial Ecommerce and later ContAuth CardOnFile/Subscription/UnscheduledCardOnFile cases, with risk, prior authentication, token age and changed-agreement conditions (lines 52–95). In-person guidance removes PIN bypass/signature, but its no-action claim for Adyen integrations is a dated provider assertion (lines 37–44). Adyen 3DS2 offers default Authentication Engine routing if implemented; the guide specifies **Prefer Not** and avoiding `attemptAuthentication: never` for Checkout v69+ or `executeThreeD: false` for v68−. When Adyen 3DS2 is not implemented, the guide makes the merchant responsible for ensuring relevant in-scope online credit-card payments over Japan acquiring connections are authenticated with 3DS2 and for sending third-party authentication data in payment requests, subject to its out-of-scope qualifications (lines 110–141). Per-request preferences can override default handling and cause non-compliance. This is not current legal advice, merchant-compliance proof, PCI certification, or evidence of a successful payment.
