# Fixed retrieval checks — Adyen partners overview

## Q1 — Purpose, scope, and non-inference

**Question:** Who is this documentation landing page for, and what does an application or resource link not prove?

**Expected answer:** It addresses partners who build integrations, such as plugins, for their customers to accept Adyen payments. It routes applicants to a partner application and points builders to an integration checklist and resources. Submitting an application does not mean the Partnerships team approved it or granted Partner Portal/customer-account access. A toolkit link does not establish an OAuth architecture, merchant permission, live payment processing, commercial contract, merchant of record, commission entitlement, or payout flow.

## Q2 — Central capability, prerequisites, and warnings

**Question:** What sequence and resources does the overview actually describe, and where are implementation requirements found?

**Expected answer:** A prospective partner fills the partner application; Adyen's Partnerships team reviews it, contacts the applicant, and describes later Partner Portal access for operations such as managing customer accounts and tracking commissions. The page says partner integrations have required and optional components but sends readers to a separate integration checklist for the steps. It links API libraries, plugins/examples, a PHP webhook module and Checkout, Management, and Terminal APIs. These are navigation/intent statements, not proof of accepted partner status, permissions, technical implementation, merchant account eligibility, actual customer onboarding, or financial settlement. Release/update guidance is advisory and contains no exact package baseline.
