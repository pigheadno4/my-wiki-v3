## Q1. What is this page for, what scope does it cover, and what should a reader not infer?

**Expected answer:** The page explains reconciliation of Mirakl connector seller invoices with Adyen platform balance changes, Mirakl invoice payout/commission fields, and Adyen transfer references. Mirakl invoices are issued per seller at billing-cycle end (documented default ten days) and show a net amount remaining for the seller and commission for the platform liable balance. ## Q2. What central reconciliation capability, prerequisites, and warnings does it give?

**Expected answer:** The connector retrieves Mirakl invoice details via IV01. If Payment Confirmation is enabled, invoice status starts Pending, commission moves from seller to platform, and after payout completion IV07 sets Paid. IV07 distinguishes `invoice.summary.amount_transferred_to_operator` (commission to platform liable balance) from `invoice.summary.amount_transferred` (seller payout). The daily Balance Platform Accounting Report covers all platform balance accounts and can be scheduled or generated for a day; pair it with invoices. Adyen `/transfers` response `referenceForBeneficiary` carries connector invoice ID, attempt count, and P/C/S type into transfer webhooks; `description` provides type/invoice ID. These references and invoice/report statuses help reconcile account movements, but request acceptance, an invoice marked Paid, or a report entry is not independent evidence of seller bank receipt.
