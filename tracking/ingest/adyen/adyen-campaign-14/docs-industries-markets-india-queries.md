## Q1. What is this page for, what is in scope, and what should not be inferred?

This Adyen overview is for **domestic payments in India**, summarizing local entity/integration and Adyen India onboarding, stored-card and recurring conditions, payment-method navigation and BillDesk chargebacks (raw `raw/adyen/docs/industries/markets/india/2026-09-15.md:15-116`). It is not a cross-border guide or a grant of live acquiring, credentials, merchant-account or method eligibility. It does not determine merchant-of-record or settlement ownership for a specific arrangement, prove legal compliance, or show payment/dispute finality. ## Q2. What is the central capability, prerequisites, and material warnings?

For domestic India processing the page calls for a local entity and integration plus account-manager onboarding/India Customer Area setup (raw lines 19–21). It says only last-four card digits and issuer name may be stored, maps one-off/subscription/top-up to `CardOnFile`/`Subscription`/`UnscheduledCardOnFile`, and requires consent, pre-debit notice and a 48-hour wait for subscription or top-up charges (lines 23–39). The recurring summary adds initial `mandate` parameters, POST `/notifyShopper` before later charges and a notification reference (lines 91–104). It says gross receipts over 50 crore INR trigger a RuPay/UPI offering requirement; this is a dated conditional claim, not universal method enablement or an INR-only payment rule (lines 46–53). BillDesk card/UPI disputes have a seven-day response period; the 60-day defense wording remains qualified by possible later pre-arbitration (lines 106–116). Consult the linked detailed India compliance/go-live guides and current authorities for actual rules.
