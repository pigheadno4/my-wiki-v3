## Overview

This 2026-09-15 [[adyen]] Docs page introduces its industries-and-markets documentation section and routes readers to more specific recommendations. It is marked **Work in progress** and says more topics will be added. This landing page is not a feature matrix, merchant eligibility statement, integration procedure, legal classification, or merchant-of-record determination.

## Key takeaways

- Adyen describes an industry as companies in a commerce sector producing similar goods or services, and a market as a system for exchange that may be physical or online, direct or indirect, or geographically defined. These are the page's framing definitions, not eligibility or legal rules for a specific merchant.
- The section says its recommendations help readers decide minimally required and additional integration features, especially when supporting multiple sales channels. The actual requirements, conditions and product behavior belong to the linked topic pages; this hub does not supply them.
- Its next-step links are **Industry recommendations** (`/industries/feature-packs`), **Markets** (`/industries/markets`), **Peak season readiness** (`/industries/peak-season`), and **World Cup readiness** (`/industries/world-cup`). The last label describes preparing in-person payments for international visitors. Treat all four as navigation labels, without importing unread child-page behavior or claims about travel, retail or hospitality segments.
