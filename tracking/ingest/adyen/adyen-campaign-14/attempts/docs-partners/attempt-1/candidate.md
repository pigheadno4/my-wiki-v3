## Overview

This Adyen documentation landing page addresses **partners who build integrations**, such as plugins, that let their customers accept payments with Adyen. It routes prospective partners to an application and existing partners to integration resources and a checklist. It is not approval of a particular partnership, Partner Portal access grant, merchant onboarding, payment processing, or a statement of seller, merchant-of-record, or payout ownership. See [[adyen]] and [[adyen-partners]].

## Key takeaways

- The page distinguishes Adyen's plugin directory from partner-built integrations. It says a partner integration has **required and optional components**, with detailed steps routed to the separate integration checklist; this landing page does not enumerate those requirements (raw lines 19–25, 57–61).
- To apply, fill the **partner application form**. Adyen says its **Partnerships team** reviews the application and contacts the applicant; the team also provides access to a **Partner Portal** for daily operations such as managing customer accounts and tracking commissions (raw lines 27–33). Submission is not acceptance, portal access, customer-account permission, or earned commission.
- The toolkit section links development resources, API libraries, plugin/example repositories, a PHP webhook module, and Checkout, Management, and Terminal APIs (raw lines 35–46). These are navigation routes, not an integration architecture, OAuth scope/consent specification, or proof a partner may use any API against a given merchant account.
- The page advises subscribing to developer updates, checking releases and version dependencies, and keeping integrations current (raw lines 48–55). It gives no package/version baseline, merchant eligibility rules, supported-region matrix, or financial settlement terms.
