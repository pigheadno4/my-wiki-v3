## Overview

Adyen's Financial Products landing page (metadata last modified 2023-12-12; captured 2026-09-15) orients platforms offering financial products to their users in addition to processing payments. It introduces Business accounts, Capital, Issuing and Payouts, then links to each product area. It is an umbrella catalog, not an implementation guide, jurisdiction/eligibility matrix, merchant-of-record or licensing determination, account-ownership statement, or evidence that any funds moved. See [[adyen-financial-products]] for the family route.

## Key takeaways

- **Business accounts** are described as allowing users to send business-related transfers to third-party bank accounts and receive business-related transfers from third-party accounts into the business account. Invoice/utilities and business/customer receipts are examples, not a country, account-type or eligibility guarantee (raw lines 19–23).
- **Capital** is described as Adyen business-financing offers presented by a platform to its users without an additional application process, with daily repayment at a fixed percentage of volume processed through Adyen. The page does not state financing approval, pricing, jurisdiction or repayment execution for a particular user (raw lines 21–24).
- **Issuing** is described as virtual and physical Mastercard/Visa debit cards for business purposes, with Adyen operating in the background while the platform manages delivery, customization and funds. The page does not allocate legal ownership, compliance duties or a specific cardholder/issuer role (raw lines 21–25).
- **Payouts** are described as services for sending funds to accounts outside Adyen, including third parties such as suppliers or partners. This family-level description does not specify API route, speed, recipient eligibility, bank acceptance or how it relates to the separately documented legacy Online Payout API (raw lines 21–26).
- The four product-card links are navigation to separate Business accounts, Capital, Issuing and Payouts documentation; this page supplies no child product setup mechanics, country matrix or live account entitlement (raw lines 28–46).
