## Overview

This **English-language Adyen webpage captured 2026-09-15** summarizes Japan compliance considerations for domestic payments under the Specified Commercial Transactions Act (SCTA) and Payment Services Act (PSA), plus separate in-person PayPay/JCB scheme steps. Adyen explicitly says the page is **not legal advice** and recommends review with legal counsel. It is a provider snapshot, not current statutory authority, a merchant-specific legal conclusion, account eligibility or proof of compliance. See [[adyen]] and proposed [[adyen-japan-scta-and-psa]].

## Key takeaways

- Adyen describes the SCTA as consumer-protection rules for ecommerce transactions with consumers in Japan, **including a seller without a Japanese entity**; the page says B2B transactions and listed transaction categories are outside its described SCTA scope. The merchant/seller's actual contracting entity and applicability need legal review rather than inference from the Adyen account structure.
- For transactions Adyen describes as in SCTA scope, the page says the business must show required information **in Japanese** on its website. Its table includes the business's official name and address (country if outside Japan), contact and representative, total price including tax/fees, payment timing/method, delivery period and return/refund policy. These are captured provider descriptions, not a complete or current disclosure checklist.
- Adyen describes PSA scope as providing digital currencies or in-app coins, or issuing instruments such as digital wallets or prepaid cards. For an in-scope business, it says FSA notification or registration **may** be required before processing; a foreign entity that issues digital payment instruments to consumers in Japan must take measures to comply with the PSA, while a Japan operational base is only **recommended**. The page lists Japanese-language website disclosures and a pre-order confirmation screen with payment/order details. Do not treat ordinary Adyen payment acceptance alone as proof that the merchant issues such instruments.
- The separate in-person section states that **before accepting PayPay or JCB**, each physical POS should have an Adyen store with complete address/phone and those methods added **at store level**. These are scheme/setup instructions in the captured guide, not evidence that a particular store or merchant account is approved or transacted.
