# Fixed retrieval checks — Adyen peak-season refunds and payouts

## Q1 — What is this page for, what is its scope, and what should not be inferred?

The 2026-09-15 guide gives seasonal preparation advice for refund reserves, channel-specific refund handling and payout-schedule changes (raw/adyen/docs/industries/peak-season/refunds/2026-09-15.md:19–26). Its reserve and scheduling suggestions are recommendations, not universal requirements. A refund request/validation webhook does not prove shopper-bank credit, and changing a payout schedule does not prove merchant-bank receipt. ## Q2 — How do refund routes, funding and payout timing differ?

The reserve section suggests a threshold and optional auto-funding, bank-transfer top-up or inter-merchant transfer; transfer may take a day or two (raw lines 28–38). Ecommerce Checkout refunds reference the original PSP reference and yield asynchronous `REFUND` validation outcome, with rare later `REFUND_FAILED` (lines 48–54). POS referenced refunds use both tender and PSP references; unreferenced refunds have fraud/error risk, `REFUND_WITH_DATA`, optional delay/card check, and offline-only-unreferenced/max-amount limits (lines 56–72). Omnichannel refunds are always referenced against the original processing merchant account; ecommerce-at-POS needs original PSP reference, date and currency (lines 74–80). Adyen advises making payout schedule/account changes before peak season: direct-merchant frequency changes start next original-schedule period, while platform push-sweep changes are described as immediate (lines 82–94). None of these statements establishes a particular refund's final bank credit or payout arrival. Use the same concept→source→raw route.
