# Fixed retrieval checks — docs-reporting-your-payment-terminals

## Q1. What is this page for, what is in scope, and what should not be inferred?

This dated page defines the **POS Terminal fleet Scheduled report**: one row per unique terminal, added upon shipment, with inventory/assignment, firmware, network and last-seen fields (raw `raw/adyen/docs/reporting/your-payment-terminals/2026-09-15.md:15-27,39-81`). It describes manual/automatic retrieval, account time zone and company/merchant-account availability (lines 29–33), not a user-role grant or a live terminal-monitoring feed. Do not infer an installed/boarded/online device, full fleet visibility, a payment log or successful authorization/capture/settlement from a row or timestamp. ## Q2. What is the central capability, prerequisites, and material warnings?

The report lets company or merchant accounts retrieve terminal-fleet inventory and technical details in their account time zone (raw lines 19–33), with one unique terminal per row (lines 39–43). Identifiers include `POIID`, permanent/serial ID, company and merchant account, inventory/in-store and store assignment (lines 49–68). Its `Status` is last-online age: Online today, Last seen 1–7 days, or “Switched off” after more than a week; that is not a live power diagnosis (line 55). `Last Transaction`/`Last Activity` timestamps are omitted after 14 days, so missing values do not prove no past activity; the page gives no transaction amount/result fields (lines 69–70). Firmware/network/SIM columns describe inventory (lines 56,72–81). The page does not state report user roles or prove device/payment state.
