Q1 — What is this source for, what scope does it cover, and what must it not establish? It is a planning/build/document/go-live checklist for Adyen partner ecommerce and in-person integrations for customer merchants. Multi-store support across merchant accounts under one company account is required. It does not identify the legal seller or merchant of record for a use case, grant partnership/live approval, or prove a payment, capture, refund or bank receipt. Raw lines 17–33, 49–57, 158–174. Q2 — Which capabilities, prerequisites and warnings matter? Management API and OAuth are optional customer-configuration paths, with additional roles for the former and delegated API access for the latter. Online partner requirements include modifications, 3DS2 where required, application information, webhooks, error/response and result-code handling; POS requirements include application information, terminal security, refunds, receipts, in-progress cancellations, status checks and logs, with P2PE optional and terminal-limited. Region-specific rules can add requirements. Headless customer UI requires API-only implementation. Before processing for customers, they must apply for live accounts, provide AOC/SAQ and sign an Adyen contract; pilot is recommended. Raw lines 35–43, 55–86, 102–134, 158–174.
