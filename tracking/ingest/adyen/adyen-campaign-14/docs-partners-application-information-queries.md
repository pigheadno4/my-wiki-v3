## Q1. What is this page for, what is in scope, and what should not be inferred?

This page explains partner **`applicationInfo` metadata in Adyen API requests**—for example Checkout `/payments`, `/sessions` and in-person IPP—not the partner-program application or its approval review (raw `raw/adyen/docs/partners/application-information/2026-09-15.md:15-39`). It distinguishes technology partners from service partners/integrators and gives request examples (lines 26–149). Sending metadata does not prove Adyen approved a partner, issued credentials, granted merchant permissions, assigned merchant-of-record responsibility or completed a payment/payout. ## Q2. What is the central capability, prerequisites, and material warnings?

Adyen says partners should attach `applicationInfo` so it can understand partner/product identity and improve operations; omission limits troubleshooting support (raw lines 19–24,33–39). A technology partner's table marks `applicationInfo.externalPlatform.name` required and `merchantApplication.name` optional, while a service partner/SI's table marks `applicationInfo.externalPlatform.integrator` required (lines 53–58,112–116). The guide lists values up to 40 characters, beginning with a letter/number, using letters/digits/dashes/underscores/spaces and consistent formatting across requests (lines 151–160), plus optional version/name suggestions (lines 162–170). The example `/payments` payloads are illustrative test requests, not successful payment or schema-validation proof. The page does not assign legal seller, MOR, payee or merchant-account access.
