# Fixed retrieval checks — docs-financial-products

## Q1 — Purpose, scope, and what not to infer

**Query:** What is Adyen's Financial Products landing page for, and what does it not determine?

**Answer:** It is an umbrella orientation page for a platform to offer financial products to its users alongside payments, listing Business accounts, Capital, Issuing and Payouts and linking to each product area. It does not choose an architecture, prove that a merchant or country is eligible, define licensing or merchant-of-record status, identify legal account/funds owner, or evidence a transfer, payout or bank receipt. ## Q2 — Central capability, prerequisites, and warnings

**Query:** Which four product families are named, and what conditions or boundaries accompany those descriptions?

**Answer:** Business accounts are described for users' business-related transfers to and from third-party accounts; Capital for presenting Adyen financing offers with daily repayment at a fixed share of Adyen-processed volume; Issuing for virtual/physical Mastercard and Visa business debit cards; and Payouts for transfers to accounts outside Adyen, including suppliers/partners. The page is a catalog and does not give country eligibility, platform/user roles beyond broad labels, approval/underwriting, card-issuer or funds-ownership allocation, API setup or payout-finality conditions. Follow the separate linked product guides for those mechanics; the Payouts umbrella description must not be assumed identical to legacy Online Payout API behavior. Evidence: raw lines 19–26, 28–46.
