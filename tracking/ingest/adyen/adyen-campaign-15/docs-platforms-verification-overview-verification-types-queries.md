## Q1 — What is the purpose and scope, and what must not be inferred?

The 2026-09-15 snapshot compares upfront and staggered verification-data collection for Adyen Platforms users (raw lines 15–24). Upfront is the general platform recommendation; only payment facilitators may request staggered collection via an Adyen contact (24, 30–32). It describes an account-holder verification and payout-permission process, not an individual account's approved capability, completed payment, completed payout or bank receipt. Automatic requesting of receive-payments capability (24) is not a granted capability. ## Q2 — What capabilities, prerequisites, conditions, and warnings are central?

Upfront users complete full verification before processing payments and receiving payouts, with data verified after account-holder creation (raw lines 21, 26–28). For eligible payment facilitators using staggered verification, additional data is collected by tier; crossing the current tier's processed-amount limit moves a user up, but verification checks and high-risk capability requests can also do so (32–44). Payouts pause at higher-tier transition and resume only after additional data and Adyen verification; webhooks convey the change and needed data (38). Four tiers depend on country, legal-entity type and processed amount; processed-amount currencies are AUD, USD, CAD, NZD, SGD and HKD for the listed countries/regions, EUR otherwise (40–56). The page supplies no numeric tier thresholds, fixed time to verification, API role, or proof of payment/payout execution. For current conditions and capability status, follow the linked requirements and capabilities guides (58–61), then the canonical `[[adyen-platforms]]` route to this source and pinned raw.
