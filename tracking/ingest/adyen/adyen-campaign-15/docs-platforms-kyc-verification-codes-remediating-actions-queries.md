Q1 — What is this source for and what must it not establish? It is a 2021-modified catalog of Adyen for Platforms/Issuing remediation messages that may accompany verification errors, at `verificationErrors` level only when an action is common across `subErrors`, or on a specific `subErrors` item. A listed action is not a universal field requirement, completed remediation, successful verification, activated capability or payment eligibility. Raw lines 15–38, 298–357. Q2 — How should a code be read and where are the key warnings? Match the code/message to the actual error object: general, individual, organization, legal arrangement, bank account, signatory, business line, missing-data, contract or data-review families. `1_100` means no remediation, `1_101` contact Support; `2_1xx`/`2_2xx` rows may appear for `dataMissing` and name different target objects, while `2_9xx` rows address contracts. `3_100` points to confirming reviewed data, not guaranteed approval. The printed `2_198` has two different organization-address actions, so the current payload/message and current authority must resolve it. In the example, verification/suberror codes differ from nested remediation codes. Raw lines 25–46, 115–153, 248–249, 283–357.
