Q2. What central capabilities, prerequisites and warnings does it introduce?
A. The page maps company and merchant accounts/stores to the Management API; user account holders and balance accounts plus platform liable counterparts to the Configuration API; and legal entities, verified transfer instruments, supporting documents and business lines to Legal Entity Management API. An account holder must link to its user's balance account and main legal entity; negative user balances from refunds or chargebacks can cause deductions from the liable balance account. It points separately to payments, onboarding/KYC, payouts, terminals, reporting, Customer Area and support; contact-role descriptions are not Customer Area roles and child links are navigation, not imported instructions. No verification approval, capability enablement, production activation, financial outcome or bank receipt follows from these descriptions.
