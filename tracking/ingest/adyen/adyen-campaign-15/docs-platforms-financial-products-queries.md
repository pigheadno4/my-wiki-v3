**Question:** What does this financial-products page cover, and what should it not be used to prove?

**Answer:** It is a dated product-orientation hub for financial services a platform may offer users alongside payment processing: business accounts, Capital, Issuing and Payouts. It links separate product guides but provides no region, legal-entity, KYC, capability or API-version eligibility matrix. Its descriptions do not prove a user’s product activation, borrowing approval, issued card, payout execution or bank receipt. ## Q2 — Central capability, prerequisites, and warnings

**Question:** How does the hub distinguish business accounts, Capital, Issuing and Payouts, and where do detailed conditions belong?

**Answer:** Business accounts cover business-related third-party incoming/outgoing transfers; Capital describes Adyen financing offers and daily repayment based on a fixed percentage of processed volume; Issuing describes virtual/physical Mastercard and Visa business debit cards; Payouts describes transfers to accounts outside Adyen. These are separate product descriptions, with links to `/business-accounts`, `/capital`, `/issuing` and `/payouts`. The Capital phrase “without an additional application process” does not mean every user receives an offer. Current country, eligible-entity, verification, product/capability and transfer conditions must come from the respective guides and account state. Raw lines 19–26 and 28–46.
