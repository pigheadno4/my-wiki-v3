## Q2. What central capabilities, prerequisites, and warnings does it give?

**Expected answer:** Adyen says user verification precedes processing payments, paying out funds and offering financial products; incorrect/incomplete information can delay onboarding and require documents. Identity, residence, organization registration and bank details are distinct checks. Legal names must match official records, while trading-name bank accounts have specific conditions. Selected examples vary by country and legal form: Australia's Individuals tab requires a bank document, and this snapshot limits trust support to Australia/New Zealand with trust deed and core member information; Canada requires organization documents; France has hosted-ID and narrow older-ID extension conditions; Germany and UK have different organization/sole-proprietor rules; the US requires last-four SSN digits for individuals with fallback options and EIN as its only accepted organization registration/tax number. Consult the exact raw country and entity sections before applying a condition. Neither document upload nor available instant verification means successful verification, granted capability or bank receipt. **Evidence:** pinned raw `raw/adyen/docs/platforms/verification-requirements/onboarding-guidelines/2026-09-15.md:27-59,61-122,124-150,152-200,202-260,262-306`; canonical retrieval route `[[source-adyen-docs-platforms-verification-requirements-onboarding-guidelines]]` via `[[adyen-platforms]]` and `[[adyen-index]]`.
