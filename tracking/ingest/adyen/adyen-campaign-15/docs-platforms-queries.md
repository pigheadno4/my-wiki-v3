## Q1. What is this page for, what is in scope, and what should not be inferred?

This is the 2026-09-15 **Adyen for Platforms** landing guide for businesses processing online and/or POS payments on behalf of platform users, with onboarding, verification, split-payment, payout, risk and reporting routes (raw `raw/adyen/docs/platforms/2026-09-15.md:15-40`). It explicitly points to a different marketplace integration guide when this model does not fit (line 23). It is not the classic Marketplace/Marketpay object model or the separate Issuing/business-accounts/Capital product contract. It does not assign legal merchant of record, seller, payee or fund ownership, define API roles and account objects, or prove user verification, payment or bank payout. ## Q2. What is the central capability, prerequisites, and material warnings?

The overview's central capability is letting a platform onboard and have Adyen verify sellers/service providers/contractors before payout, process and split payments, and select managed or custom payout timing while routing to risk and reporting (raw lines 25–40). Example legal-entity, split `/payments` and `/transfers` API links are navigation, not schemas or execution proof (line 34). The user-onboarding country list and acquiring-region list are separate, dated scope; the Channel Islands and Isle of Man exclude in-person payments/store creation (lines 42–113). Optional card issuing, business accounts and Capital have a separate availability/additional-verification route (lines 115–142). Get-started, integration and verification guides are next steps (lines 144–162). No exact merchant role/credential grant, account-holder or balance ownership, MOR determination, current eligibility, approved verification or completed transfer is established.
