## Q1 — Purpose, scope, and what not to infer

**Question:** What does this guide determine before platform-user verification, and what does it not establish?

**Answer:** This 2026-09-15 capture of a 2023-modified Adyen guide says a platform collects and submits verification information based on three axes: the user's operating country/region, legal-entity type, and financial products offered (raw lines 15–25). The main legal entity is the user in the contractual relationship with the platform; associated owners or other entities may need their own linked legal-entity records (lines 65–69). The Requirements line saying no additional preparation for Adyen for Platforms or Issuing does not erase the page's verification requirements (lines 27–35). The tables are historical provider guidance, not current legal/compliance advice, actual verification approval, enabled capability, payout receipt, seller or merchant-of-record allocation. ## Q2 — Central conditions, exceptions, and warnings

**Question:** Which country, entity, age and financial-product conditions change what data must be collected?

**Answer:** Country rules differ; minors can be onboarded only on Legal Entity Management API v3/v4, never under 13, and only under the age/residency and legal-representative/signatory conditions shown in the raw table, with Hong Kong/Singapore under-18 exclusions (raw lines 31–63). Legal-entity support varies by country. Organizations need entity data plus qualifying UBOs/controllers/signatories and, in specified countries, directors; the individual type is payment-facilitator-only; trusts are listed for Australia/New Zealand; unincorporated partnerships have a bounded country list and partner-association rules, while incorporated partnerships route to organizations (lines 65–310). Business accounts require extra information and the stated BE/DE/ES/FR/GB/NL/US entity-and-associated-person scope; US business accounts add onboarding disclosure/terms/certification timing and exclude governmental organizations (lines 311–350). Card issuing has its own EEA/UK/US and organization/sole-proprietor scope plus VAT/tax-ID and source-of-funds data, with low/medium/high per-card-per-day capability thresholds that do not prove grant (lines 352–375). Capital is invite-only and says no extra checks beyond the baseline, not no verification (lines 377–386). Check current official requirements and the specific user's verification/capability state before acting.
