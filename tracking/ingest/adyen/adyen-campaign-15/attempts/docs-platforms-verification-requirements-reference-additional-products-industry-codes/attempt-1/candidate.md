## Overview

This 2026-09-15 Adyen for Platforms reference directs a platform to select the Industry Code (IC) that most accurately describes its user's business activity. It says Adyen maps ICs to Merchant Category Codes (MCCs) and adapts them to its internal regulations. The captured raw states that only listed values are accepted, but the actual value list/table is absent from this snapshot; it cannot be used to look up or validate a specific IC.

## Key takeaways

- IC selection must describe the user's actual business activity accurately because some MCCs map to multiple ICs. An IC and an MCC are related classifications, not interchangeable proof of merchant acceptance, permitted products or enabled capabilities.
- Contact Adyen or Support when approval for an IC is needed before onboarding users, or when offering business accounts to users in industries outside a prior approval. These are explicit escalation cases, not proof that a requested industry or business account is approved.
- Three footnote texts survive in the raw: an average transaction value limit of EUR 2,000, inclusion of CBD/kava kava/baby formula/baby food, and lack of American Express support. Because the referenced code rows are missing, this snapshot does not identify which IC each footnote qualifies. Do not attach these conditions to an invented code or apply them across all industries.
- The page links a separate brand-specific industry-codes list under See also. That link is navigation only. This source does not establish any user's legal eligibility, KYC status, merchant acceptance, business-account availability, capability grant or production approval.
