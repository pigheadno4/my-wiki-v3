## Overview

This Adyen for Platforms Customer Area quickstart (last modified and captured 2026-09-15) introduces the interface for business-process visibility and user support. It lists views for payment/onboarding summaries, user payments and splits, account-holder onboarding errors, and user payouts. These are UI navigation and access descriptions, not proof of a particular payment, settlement, payout or verification outcome. See [[adyen]] and [[adyen-platforms]].

## Key takeaways

- **Access scope:** Team members receive unique Customer Area logins. An account can access the company account or only specified merchant accounts; assigned user roles determine available tasks. Company/merchant account access is distinct from platform users' account-holder and balance-account records. The guide recommends having an administrator able to manage access, rather than making that an explicit universal prerequisite.
- **Operational views:** The page identifies high-level payment/onboarding insights, payments processed by users with splits and destination balance accounts, account-holder error detail, and payout views. It does not supply transaction-level evidence, bank receipt, a live/test account-state check or authority to initiate a payout.
- **Role hygiene:** The page recommends current team-user lists, deactivation when a member leaves, granting PII roles such as View account holders PII only where needed, and separating bank-account update rights from payout-initiation rights. These are documented recommendations; the page does not enumerate every required role for a specific action.
