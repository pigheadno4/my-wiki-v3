## Overview

This 2023-modified, 2026-09-15-captured Adyen for Platforms page is a product-orientation hub for financial services that a platform may offer users in addition to payment processing. It names business accounts, Capital, Issuing and Payouts and links to their separate guides. The descriptions do not establish availability, account-holder verification, a granted capability, a financing offer, card issuance, transfer execution or bank receipt. See [[adyen-platforms]].

## Key takeaways

- **Business accounts** are described as accounts through which users can send business-related funds to third-party bank accounts and receive business-related funds from third parties. Invoice, utility and customer/business-payment examples illustrate use, not individual account access.
- **Capital** is described as Adyen business-financing offers presented by the platform to users without an additional application process, with repayment daily as a fixed percentage of processed Adyen volume. This page does not state offer eligibility, approval, terms, region or a user’s actual borrowing status.
- **Issuing** is described as virtual and physical Mastercard and Visa debit cards for business purposes, with platform-managed delivery, customization and funds while Adyen works in the background. It does not establish current card geography, a user’s issuing capability or that a card was created.
- **Payouts** are described as services for transfers to accounts outside Adyen, including third-party suppliers or partners. The directory does not define payout rails, recipient controls, transfer status or final receipt.
- Each product has its own linked guide. This page contains no country, legal-entity, KYC, capability, API-version or activation matrix, so those conditions belong to the product-specific source and account evidence, not this hub.
