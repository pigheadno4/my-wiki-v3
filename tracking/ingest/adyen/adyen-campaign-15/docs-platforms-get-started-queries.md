## Q2 — Central capability, prerequisites, and warnings

**Query:** What are the platform setup stages and resource identities, and what test/live warning matters?

**Answer:** Contact Adyen for fit assessment, then design user data/document collection, payment channels/splits/fees, risk, user payouts and reporting/reconciliation. Adyen supplies a test account and Customer Area admin credentials, where the overview names company/merchant accounts, account holders and balance accounts plus payment/transfer/credential/webhook/reports management. Build and test in test, contact Adyen for live-account application, replicate configuration in live because test setup is not copied, and test live again. The page does not assign legal ownership or liability to the listed resources, define reserve/transfer-instrument entities, or prove a user passed KYC, capabilities were granted, payments split or payouts arrived.
