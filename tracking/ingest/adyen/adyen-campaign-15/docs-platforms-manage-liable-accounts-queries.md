Q2. What do the three accounts do, and what conditions and warnings matter?
A. Commissions are booked to the main liable account, which may also fund user fees or balance corrections. A split directed to a nonexistent or nonreceiving balance account is booked to reserved incorrect splits; nonreceiving reasons include closed/suspended balance account or holder, or missing receive-funds capability. A reserved compensation account is debited if a balance remains negative more than 30 days from refunds or chargebacks, on the first day of the following month; it may then need top-up or internal transfer to offset its own negative balance. Linked transfer and top-up pages are navigation. These conditional rules do not prove an individual transaction or final payout.
