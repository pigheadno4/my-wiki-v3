# Fixed retrieval checks — Manage transfer instruments

## Q1. What is this page for, what is in scope, and what should not be inferred?

The 2023-modified, 2026-09-15-captured Adyen for Platforms guide explains external-bank-account transfer instruments. The API covers creating, viewing, updating and deleting users' and platform bank-account instruments; Customer Area management covers only company payout accounts linked to the platform liable account. User account-holder, legal-entity, balance-account and transfer-instrument identities are distinct. The page does not prove a given platform's eligibility, current API version, legal seller or fund owner, completed verification, payout execution or bank receipt. ## Q2. What is the central capability, its prerequisites, and material warnings?

The Legal Entity Management API creates a bankAccount instrument with the owner's legalEntityId and location-dependent bank details, returning a separate id that sweep or manual-transfer requests may target. The stated API roles are Manage LegalEntities via API and Balance Platform BCL Legal Entity role; Customer Area roles include Manage account holders & legal entities and Merchant manage bank accounts for payout-account management. The example's capability is pending and not allowed, verification problems can be returned, and Customer Area says the company payout account is usable at valid status. PATCH of bank details retains the same id in the example; DELETE is irreversible and future payouts using that id fail, so the guide sequences new-account creation, payout-reference changes, verification of receipt, then old-account deletion. The dated test examples mix lem/v3 create with lem/v4 later operations; no current availability, country/currency eligibility or real payout follows from them.
