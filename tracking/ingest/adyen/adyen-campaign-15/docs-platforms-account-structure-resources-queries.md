# Fixed retrieval queries — Platforms account structure

## Q1. What is this page for, what scope does it cover, and what should a reader not infer?

**Expected answer:** It maps the current Adyen for Platforms processing account hierarchy—company, merchant accounts and stores—to balance-platform resources for the platform business and its third-party users. The 2021-modified snapshot is a design reference, not evidence of a specific merchant's current setup, regional eligibility, legal seller/merchant-of-record identity, issued product, verified capability, payment, settlement, payout or bank receipt. It does not supply Classic Platforms or Issuing account rules. **Evidence:** pinned raw `raw/adyen/docs/platforms/account-structure-resources/2026-09-15.md:11,15-19,21-78`; canonical retrieval route `[[source-adyen-docs-platforms-account-structure-resources]]` via `[[adyen-platforms]]` and `[[adyen-index]]`.

## Q2. What central capabilities, prerequisites, and warnings does it give?

**Expected answer:** The company account holds one or more merchant accounts; merchant accounts configure methods, currencies, risk, stores and reports. Stores route user payments and aid payer statements/reconciliation. Merchant accounts connect to a balance platform, with a separate balance platform required per acquiring region in this guide. A user account holder must link to the user's balance account and main legal entity; the balance account holds funds and payouts go to a verified bank transfer instrument. Issued payment instruments (cards/business accounts) and Capital grant accounts are different, use-case-dependent resources. The platform has its own Adyen-created liable account holder and liable balance account, from which funds can be deducted if user balance accounts turn negative from refunds/chargebacks. These relationships support design but do not prove verification, actual splits, fund movement, reserve treatment or bank receipt. **Evidence:** pinned raw `raw/adyen/docs/platforms/account-structure-resources/2026-09-15.md:21-78`; canonical retrieval route `[[source-adyen-docs-platforms-account-structure-resources]]` via `[[adyen-platforms]]` and `[[adyen-index]]`.
