Q2. What central onboarding and verification capabilities, prerequisites and warnings does it describe?
A. Hosted onboarding is recommended and offers invite or API-initiated links; API-only uses a platform-owned UI and API data flow with higher integration effort. Country/region and legal-entity type determine available types, data and formats. Full upfront verification is default; staggered verification is conditional for some ecommerce platforms. Automated review may fall back to manual review, generally within 48 hours, with Support after longer delays. The page specifies conditional PCI documentation and Terms acceptance branches, Customer Area role requirements, and test/live context in support requests. These process and support steps do not guarantee verification approval, capability activation or payout.
