**Q2 — Central capability, prerequisites, and warnings.** How does a review move from notice through confirmation to verification, and when are capabilities affected?

**Answer:** Adyen's account-holder webhook signals review required with error `3_10`, action `3_100`, `verificationStatus=invalid`, `allowed=true`, and a `verificationDeadlines` date for capability disallowance if unresolved (raw lines 33–45). The user must confirm data and clear any errors; allowed capabilities can continue while the deadline is active, but a review is finalized only after both conditions are met (raw lines 23–31). A platform may proactively POST `/legalEntities/{id}/requestPeriodicReview` only under onboarding v3/v4; 204 confirms the request, not the completed review, and eligibility/timing cases may prevent activation with 422 errors (raw lines 104–195). Hosted and API-only modes have distinct review/confirmation steps; in API-only mode `confirmDataReview` returns `dataReviewedAt`, a confirmation time (raw lines 197–237). Adyen then reports valid or invalid checks by webhook; invalid can coexist with `allowed=true` until a deadline, while `allowed=false` is also possible (raw lines 239–289). The test header `x-requested-verification-code: 3_10` is a test-flow instruction requiring already allowed capabilities (raw lines 341–363), not live outcome evidence.
