# Giving Sessions retrieval checks

1. **Purpose, scope, and inference boundary.** This 2026-09-15 Adyen Giving guide covers Web Drop-in and Web Components with the Sessions flow. After a successful original payment, an eligible shopper can see a donation form for an active campaign; a donation uses the original payment method and may be fixed amount or round-up (`raw/adyen/docs/online-payments/donations/integration-options/sessions-flow/2026-09-15.md:15-31,205-217`). The guide does not prove a merchant's current configuration, shopper eligibility, donation success, or the outcome of an individual webhook. Its separate Advanced-flow guide labels a Web Components route Advanced while linking its sequence to Sessions setup (`raw/adyen/docs/online-payments/donations/integration-options/advanced-flow/2026-09-15.md:15-17,37-50`); do not combine those explicit Advanced request steps with this Sessions route.
