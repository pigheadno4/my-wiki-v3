# Fixed queries — Auto Rescue for SEPA payments

1. **Purpose, scope, and non-inferences.** This 2026-09-15 Adyen guide covers automatic retry of shopper-not-present SEPA direct debit after a chargeback coded **AM04** (insufficient funds) or **MS03** (no reason specified, used in some regions instead of AM04). Its title and description use “refused,” but the operative text says SEPA first returns `AUTHORISED` and the rescue trigger is a chargeback (`raw/adyen/docs/online-payments/auto-rescue/sepa/2026-09-15.md:6-26,445-508`). The parent Auto Rescue overview also contrasts refused cards with charged-back SEPA (`raw/adyen/docs/online-payments/auto-rescue/2026-09-15.md:19-23,44-48`). Neither an initial `Authorised` response nor an example `AUTORESCUE` payload proves collection, live enablement, or a successful recovery for a merchant (`sepa/2026-09-15.md:445-508,510-644`).

2. **Route, behavior, warnings, and detail retrieval.** `wiki/index.md:10` leads to `[[adyen-index]]`; `wiki/adyen-index.md:105` leads to `[[adyen-online-payments]]`; `wiki/concepts/adyen-online-payments.md:16,21` has the Sources anchor and parent Auto Rescue source. The proposed reciprocal link will reach this candidate source, whose `## Raw Sources` links the pinned SEPA raw; source/index additions are pending coordinator promotion. Adyen Support or an Account Manager configures account-level rescue (42 days) or transaction-level rescue with `autoRescue`, unique `merchantOrderReference`, and optional 1–42-day `maxDaysToRescue` (30 days recommended) (`raw/adyen/docs/online-payments/auto-rescue/sepa/2026-09-15.md:47-106`). SEPA permits at most two attempts (`:26-29`). A qualifying `CHARGEBACK` starts rescue after the initial `Authorised` response (`:445-508`). `AUTORESCUE` arrives 16 days after an attempt; `retryWindowHasElapsed` with `success: true` describes first-attempt success, while `maxRetryAttemptsReached` occurs with either second-attempt success or two-chargeback failure, distinguished by `success` (`:510-644`). The optional five-day Pay by Link requires Support activation (`:646-694`). Cancellation uses classic `/cancel`, the payment response's `rescueReference`, and `cancellationType: autoRescue`; `[cancel-received]` precedes `CANCEL_AUTORESCUE` with the actual cancellation outcome (`:696-764`). Test scenarios and versioned examples are at `:82-444,766-817`; unique retry `pspReference` and report linkage/fallback are at `:819-851`. These are snapshot details, not live account or provider-run proof.
