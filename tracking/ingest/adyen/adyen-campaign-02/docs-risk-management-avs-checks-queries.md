# AVS checks fixed-query answers

1. **Purpose, scope, and limits.** This 2026-09-15 Adyen snapshot explains AVS as a card-not-present billing-address check, including online payments and a named POS MOTO use case; the latter has a separate response-mapping route (`raw/adyen/docs/risk-management/avs-checks/2026-09-15.md:15-20`, `:88-92`). For enabled AVS, the authorization request carries `billingAddress`, and Adyen maps the issuer/acquirer response to an AVS code; a matching address makes shopper/cardholder identity more likely, not certain (`:19-20`, `:35-43`). Do not infer merchant enablement, a fraud-free transaction, universal issuer support, guaranteed interchange benefit, or that test results came from a live acquirer (`:22-33`, `:45`, `:71`, `:101`).
