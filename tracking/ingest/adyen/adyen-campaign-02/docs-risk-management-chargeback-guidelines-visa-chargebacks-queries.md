# Visa chargebacks fixed-query answers

1. **Purpose, scope, and limits.** This 2026-09-15 Adyen snapshot explains Visa's default Allocation workflow for fraud/authorization cases and Collaboration workflow for consumer disputes/processing errors, plus separate, rule-conditioned RDR refunds before a claim becomes a chargeback (`raw/adyen/docs/risk-management/chargeback-guidelines/visa-chargebacks/2026-09-15.md:19-46`, `:167-178`). It is a process guide, not a determination of a live case's deadline, eligibility, fees, evidence sufficiency, or outcome. Its defense window is 9 days only for disputes opened from 21 July 2025 on payments processed locally in the US or Canada; the guide gives 18 days for earlier US/Canada disputes and all other regions (`:68-75`, `:124-129`). Current case details and scheme rules are needed before acting on those dated windows or the guide's USD 600 arbitration fee (`:161-165`).
