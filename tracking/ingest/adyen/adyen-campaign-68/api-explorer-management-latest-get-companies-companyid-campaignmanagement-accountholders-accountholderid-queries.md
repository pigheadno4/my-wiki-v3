# C68 worker query answers: api-explorer-management-latest-get-companies-companyid-campaignmanagement-accountholders-accountholderid

## Q1

The 2026-11-07 Management API `latest` reference documents `GET /companies/{companyId}/campaignManagement/accountHolders/{accountHolderId}` at the listed `https://management-test.adyen.com/v3` TEST server. It reads donation campaigns associated with an account holder. Required path IDs are a unique company-account `companyId` and a unique `accountHolderId`; neither is the campaign `id` or nonprofit `causeId`. The credential needs Campaign Management read or read and write. Optional `pageSize` defaults to 10 with a maximum of 100; `pageNumber` selects a page; `status` permits `inactive`, `active`, or `ended`. On `200`, `itemsTotal` and `pagesTotal` are required, while `_links` and `campaigns` are optional. Returned campaign entries require `id`, `name`, `nonprofitCause` and `status`; optional channel settings distinguish in-person and online merchant accounts, store IDs, amounts and donation types. ## Q2

A `200` establishes that the campaign-list read succeeded, with pagination counts and any returned campaign metadata; it does not establish a donation request, a terminal prompt, payment authorization, capture, nonprofit settlement or bank receipt. The reference lists 400, 401, 403, 422 and 500 failures and a specific `503`: managing donation campaigns is temporarily unavailable for this account, but the donations themselves are not affected. The GET does not specify an idempotency key, mutation, deletion, asynchronous approval/activation, reversal, or version/conflict transition. Campaign status and optional `activatedAt`/`endedAt` are readback fields, not proof of an individual donation outcome. This TEST snapshot does not establish current Live access or eligibility.
