# Retrieval checks: Adyen Apple Pay overview

Q1. What does this Apple Pay page cover, and what are the certificate and shopper prerequisites? It is Adyen's channel chooser for web, iOS, React Native, Flutter, API-only and in-person Apple Pay guides (raw lines 84–120). The shopper uses the payment sheet and Face ID/Touch ID (lines 23–25); availability is qualified by issuer, device/browser, country/region and a wallet-added card (lines 50–56). Processing requires an Apple Pay certificate: Adyen's may be used for web, whereas mobile requires the merchant's Apple Developer account and certificate (lines 80–82).
