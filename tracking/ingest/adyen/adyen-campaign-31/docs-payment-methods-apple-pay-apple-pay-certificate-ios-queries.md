# Apple Pay own certificate for iOS retrieval checks

Q1. Who sets up what for an iOS Apple Pay certificate? For an existing iOS Drop-in/Components Advanced or Sessions integration, a Customer Area Merchant admin or Manage API Credentials user and Apple Developer Program/Enterprise account are prerequisites (pinned raw lines 21–31). The merchant creates distinct test/live Apple merchant identifiers, enables the app capability, generates a CSR for the transacting Adyen web service user, creates one Apple payment processing certificate per identifier and uploads each CER to that user's matching Customer Area record; Apple encrypts and Adyen decrypts/processes the token (lines 33–87). Q2. Does activating the replacement certificate instantly retire the old one or prove live payment completion? No. Renewal requires new CSR/CER, matching `KeyID`, Customer Area upload and Apple Developer activation; old certificate can remain in use for about four hours after activation (pinned raw lines 89–119). The guide compares decoded `paymentMethod.applePayToken` header `publicKeyHash`/`KeyID` with the web service user's certificate record; an old `KeyID` during rollout is not by itself failure, while a new `KeyID` on an authorized payment confirms the guide's setup steps (lines 121–157), not capture or settlement.
