Q1. What does the historical VX820 inspection guide ask staff to check and how often?
A1. For the VX820 non-Duet terminal, staff compare its appearance to the manual, check the power-on logo, look for cameras/skimmers and suspicious behavior, verify claimed maintenance personnel before access, avoid observing customer PIN entry, and carry out visual integrity inspections weekly. They must not use a damaged or sticker-covered device. Raw lines 14–40. Q2. What is the stop-use and historical-support boundary?
A2. Staff should report suspected tampering or substitution to appropriate personnel and, when in doubt, stop using the VX820 and contact Adyen Support (raw lines 34–40). The checklist is historical guidance, not an actual security inspection or certification. The separate VX820 non-Duet manual hub and deprecation schedule place this model under “No more transactions” on 29 March 2024; this inspection page does not restore processing support.
