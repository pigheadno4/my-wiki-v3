# Retrieval checks — card payment facilitators

**Q1 — What is this guide for, and which facilitator route does it cover?** It records Visa, Mastercard and American Express sub-merchant identity/location data for a payment facilitator's Checkout `/payments` authorization; the sub-merchant is Merchant of Record in that source's facilitated-transaction framing (raw lines 23–27). Already registered Adyen for Platforms facilitators are redirected to separate Platforms documentation; the one-sub-seller value is specifically stated for facilitators not using AfP (raw lines 19–21, 40–46). This is not legal-seller or funds-flow proof. **Q2 — What must be supplied, and what limits the examples and automatic fields?** Initial `/payments` authorization carries `AdditionalDataSubMerchant` in `additionalData.subMerchant.subSeller1.[parameter]`; later cancellations, captures and refunds inherit details from the initial payment per the guide (raw lines 34–45). Brazil tax ID/MCC and selected-country postal code conditions remain distinct (raw lines 47–54). The automatic-field paragraph says “two” but lists three named fields and says not to send them manually (raw lines 58–66). Its raw-card curl example is a test v72 illustration, not a PCI, live or payment-outcome claim (raw lines 68–104).
