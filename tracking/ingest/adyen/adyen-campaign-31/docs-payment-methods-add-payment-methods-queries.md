# Add payment methods retrieval checks

Q1. What must be in place, and how is a payment method requested for test or live?
A. The page requires an online payments integration/checkout UI, Customer Area Change payment methods role and Payment method webhook subscription in its requirements table (raw lines 21–29); the later section recommends Management webhooks for asynchronous store addition (lines 49–53). In test or live Customer Area, switch to the merchant account, select methods, apply to This merchant account, provide prompted information, review and submit (lines 34–47). Adyen may contact the merchant about additional requirements, and availability is not guaranteed within the typical one-day estimate (lines 19, 30–31). Q2. What distinguishes request/configuration from enablement and payment proof, and what failure/deletion limits apply?
A. A submitted merchant-account request can have additional requirements; the later `paymentMethod.created` event is described after store addition, with a test Visa example containing merchantId/storeId/result (raw lines 19, 49–71). An immediate submit error requires correction; for the described rare post-submission failure, the page says wait 24 hours before retrying, and an unsubscribed merchant has no automated failure alert (lines 74–78). Activate/Deactivate and ANY region are configuration settings (lines 89–109), while Inactive plus 90 days unused triggers automatic deletion (line 96). None alone proves live eligibility, checkout presentation, authorization or settlement.
