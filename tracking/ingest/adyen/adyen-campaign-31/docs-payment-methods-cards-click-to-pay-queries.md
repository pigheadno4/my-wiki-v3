# C31 retrieval checks: Click to Pay

Q1. What is Click to Pay here and what setup does Adyen require?

It is a Web card checkout route for recognized Click to Pay shoppers to select scheme-stored cards without manual card-detail entry (raw lines 17–25). Sessions lists Web Drop-in/Components v5.39.0+ and Checkout API v69+; Advanced also lists API-only with a Custom Card Component. Both tabs require adding cards and Click to Pay in Customer Area, and the separate Custom Card/ClickToPay Component path calls for Support or Account Manager enablement (raw lines 27–51). Yet the standalone Click to Pay Component instructions require creating a Session with `shopperEmail` and passing that Session to `AdyenCheckout` (raw lines 161–174). The guide does not reconcile this with its Advanced API-only table, so the routes remain scoped rather than interchangeable. Q2. What material limits or conflicts prevent assuming general recurring support or a working payment?

The page says recurring Click to Pay support is Mastercard-only (raw line 25). A standalone Click to Pay Component must use its supplied pay button (raw line 192). Its Mastercard no-cookie console 400 can accompany a continuing flow if email is registered, but is not a blanket success signal (raw lines 194–205). The Advanced request sample duplicates `paymentMethod` and is malformed, and the Drop-in configuration example omits a comma (raw lines 98–122, 292–329). Adyen test cards cannot test this method; scheme sandbox cards/accounts are specified (raw lines 355–383). A sample `Authorised` response does not prove a live authorization, capture or settlement.
