## Q1. What is this Issuing page, and how should its status legend be read?

Answer: It is a dated Adyen Issuing matrix of account-holder information for KYC verification, selected by legal entity type and operating country/region. The four entity tabs are individuals, organization, sole proprietorship, and trust; the captured trust table is Australia-only. Required means minimum for KYC checks, conditionally required means particular setups or financial products, optional is recommended, and not applicable is separately marked. It was last modified 2023-03-13 and captured 2026-09-15; it is not a current legal checklist. ## Q2. Which requirements and relationship roles can this matrix establish?

Answer: Consult the exact country/entity cell in the full raw: individuals are in lines 45–94, organizations 96–152, sole proprietorships 154–175, and Australian trusts 177–207. The rows cover entity, address, contact, document, bank and association fields, with requiredness varying by cell. The captured “More info” cells do not explain their triggering conditions; its `nationalIdExempt` field is not a blanket KYC exemption, and the association rows do not enumerate UBO, controller, or signatory eligibility. Follow the separately linked verification and document guides for those decisions.
