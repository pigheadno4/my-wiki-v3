1. **How are company, merchant, store, and terminal related, and does this apply only to Terminal API?** The guide applies to any Adyen in-person solution. The company is the core business; merchant accounts under it receive payouts and reconciliation reports; stores under merchants represent physical locations and have store IDs. A terminal must be assigned to a store or merchant account in its actual location, yielding at least one merchant account per country/region. This is account structure, separate from Terminal API message semantics. Locators: raw lines 21–40. 2. **How should the merchant/store structure be chosen and stores created?** Adyen says there is no single ideal structure. Per-store merchant accounts separate payouts/reporting but increase files and transfers; merchant accounts per legal entity, country/region, or brand with store IDs can reduce that workload while moving some fees and summary reporting to the merchant level. The comparison table qualifies bank transfers by settlement currency. Involve Finance. Customer Area store creation needs the Merchant POS Terminal Management Admin role and, when stores sit under a new merchant, merchant-account confirmation first; it supports individual creation and CSV batches up to 100 stores.
