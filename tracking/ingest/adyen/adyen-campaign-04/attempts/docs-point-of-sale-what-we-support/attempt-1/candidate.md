## Overview

This Adyen Docs page is an **in-person payments support directory**. It says Adyen offers multiple in-person solutions that can be chosen or used alongside one another, and routes readers to separate pages for countries/regions and languages, implementation solutions, payment methods, terminal models, and industry feature recommendations. See [[adyen]]; [[adyen-terminal-api]] is a related in-person API route, but the directory is broader than Terminal API.

## Key takeaways

- The page's broad claims about worldwide support, multiple languages, global and domestic methods, and terminal models are an introduction to other support pages. It contains no country list, language table, method-by-market matrix, terminal compatibility list, or merchant-specific entitlement decision.
- The linked **Countries/regions** page is the route for geographic coverage and terminal-language information; **Solutions** compares ways to implement in-person payments; **Payment methods** is the method-support route; **Terminals** is the model-selection route; **Features per industry** leads to industry recommendations. These labels are navigation, not independently reviewed behavior from the linked pages in this raw.
- The scope statement says the pages listed under Next steps are relevant for Adyen point-of-sale payments. The directory does not establish present-day availability for a particular merchant, device, country, payment method, integration type, or industry feature. Check the relevant destination and current account/market conditions before making an eligibility claim.
