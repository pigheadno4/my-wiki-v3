## Overview

This is Adyen's POS **Network and connectivity** topic directory, captured 2026-09-15 with a 2022-03-17 `last_modified` value. It routes readers to network configuration, Wi-Fi profiles, cellular fallback, Bluetooth beacons, Bluetooth base-station troubleshooting, Bluetooth PAN pairing/tethering, and networking issues. The page gives only short labels and one-line descriptions; it does not specify local-versus-cloud Terminal API architecture, terminal/model support, security requirements, configuration steps, or a current network availability guarantee. Use the linked topic's own evidence for those claims.

## Key takeaways

- For general terminal network recommendations, follow **Network configuration**; for Customer Area terminal Wi-Fi setup, follow **Wi-Fi profiles**; for switching to cellular when Wi-Fi or Ethernet internet access is unavailable, follow **Cellular connection**. Those are the directory's stated purposes, not full requirements or failover behavior.
- The Bluetooth routes have distinct labels: **Bluetooth beacons** for connecting a mobile POS app to a nearby terminal, **Bluetooth base station** for connection troubleshooting, and **Bluetooth pairing** for PAN/tethering between a terminal and mobile POS app. The page also links **Networking issues** as a troubleshooting topic. Do not equate beacons, base-station pairing, and PAN internet sharing.
