1. **Purpose, scope, and what not to infer?** This 2026-09-15 POS Terminal API guide describes issuer-dependent partial authorization when a card cannot cover the purchase, followed by card or cash collection of the remainder. The terminal can show approval of only a partial amount; that is not payment in full. It is separate from Adyen's online Checkout API partial-authorization guide, whose scheme/API/account rules must not be transferred to this POS source. This page itself does not establish current issuer/account/card-network/region eligibility, refund behavior, or production authorization success.
