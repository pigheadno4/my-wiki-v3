## Query 1: What does this Adyen POS manage-payment-methods page cover, and what should not be inferred?

Answer: It documents Customer Area actions for payment methods in a **merchant account** with an Adyen POS integration: view configured methods/details, activate or deactivate, and change country/region settings (raw lines 15-26, 41-70). It does not document a Management API mutation or company/store/terminal inheritance for these method changes; those are separate topics. A listed or active method is not proof of merchant allowance, currency acceptance, current availability or a successful transaction. ## Query 2: What role, actions, timing and destructive consequences does the page specify?

Answer: It requires a POS integration and Customer Area **Change payment methods** role and calls for the **Payment method webhook** (raw lines 28-36). In Customer Area, switch to the merchant account and use **Payments > Payment methods** to view/filter details, select a method, choose **More actions > Activate/Deactivate**, or **Edit > Country/Region**; **ANY** removes restrictions in that setting (lines 41-70). Inactive methods unused for 90 days are automatically deleted (line 57). After a method request, external factors can delay availability; the page says methods typically appear within a day and recommends requesting at least a day before opening, without guaranteeing that timing. Method currency defaults vary; changing currency requires contacting Adyen Support (lines 37-38). Follow the same index → concept → source → raw route above; catalog promotion remains pending.
