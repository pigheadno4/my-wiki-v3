# POS CVM: fixed retrieval checks

1. **What does this guide cover, and what should it not establish?** It describes cardholder verification for credit/debit cards at Adyen terminals in a Terminal API integration: PIN, PIN bypass, signatures, card CVM lists, response result and NFC limits. Its Mastercard live list and Adyen test-card list are examples, and the snapshot does not define CDCVM, wallet biometric approval or a PSD2 SCA result. Read `## Requirements` (raw lines 18-25), `## Personal Identification Number (PIN)` (27-39), `## CVM lists` (88-179), and `## CVM results` (181-193). Snapshot/last-modified dates are raw lines 2 and 10.
2. **Which actor chooses and checks CVM, and what are the warnings?** The card encodes the CVM list and, in the detailed NFC section, indicates signature or PIN when the terminal requests CVM above the limit; PIN in that case is online and prevents offline processing. Get the changing limit in Customer Area, not a static number here. PIN bypass requires Support enablement, works only on eligible cards, and may shift chargeback liability. Signature prompting depends on card/configuration; in Terminal API the prompt follows authorization, and the merchant POS/staff must inspect `CapturedSignature` for a manual check, then refund a declined signature unless a prior capture-delay arrangement permits authorization cancellation. The early line 31 says above-limit NFC always uses online PIN, while detailed line 211 makes it conditional on card selection; do not erase that conflict. Locate raw lines 29-47, 54-86, and 195-211.
