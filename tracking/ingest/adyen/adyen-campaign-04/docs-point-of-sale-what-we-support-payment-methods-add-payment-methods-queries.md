# Add POS payment methods: fixed retrieval checks

1. **What is this guide for, and what should not be inferred?** It documents requesting additional in-person payment methods through a merchant account's test or live Customer Area using the **Change payment methods** role. It gives location/type filters, any prompted information, a review/submit step, asynchronous store addition and terminal configuration timing. It does not prove a selected scheme is contractually or regionally available, that a request succeeded, or that a terminal is boarded and ready. Locate raw lines 17-31 (`## Requirements`), `## Request payment methods` (34-46) and `## When changes take effect` (79-81).
2. **What happens after submission, and when may a terminal use the method?** Adyen says external factors affect timing; methods typically appear within a day, so request ahead of opening. The requirements table asks for the Payment method webhook and the later section recommends Management webhooks; `paymentMethod.created` reports addition to a store, while the shown Visa event is a TEST example. Correct an immediate submission error before resubmitting. In the rare post-submission failure case with no webhook failure alert, the guide says wait 24 hours before another attempt; do not generalize that to pending requests. Once added, the terminal picks up the method at its next maintenance call or manual configuration update. Locate raw lines 28-31, `## Webhooks` (48-70), `## Troubleshooting` (73-77), and `## When changes take effect` (79-81).
