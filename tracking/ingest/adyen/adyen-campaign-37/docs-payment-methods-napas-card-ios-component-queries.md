## Q1 — What is this page's purpose and scope, and what cannot be inferred?

It adds Napas card to an **existing iOS Components** integration, with test Customer Area addition as a prerequisite (raw lines 15–26; [[source-adyen-docs-payment-methods-napas-card-ios-component]]). It is titled Redirect Component but does not detail a complete redirect-return, `/payments/details`, webhook or final-result flow; Component initiation and shopper test entry are not payment authorization/settlement proof. The `momo_atm` v5+ example identifier (lines 48, 64) is not explained on this page, though the pinned overview identifies partner MoMo; do not silently substitute a different method type. ## Q2 — What are the request/version, test and live conditions?

No extra Napas-specific fields are stated; `/sessions` is described as default for iOS Components **v5.0.0+**, and `/payments` for an additional use case (raw lines 28–33). The v5+ examples use `InstantPaymentComponent` after `momo_atm` lookup, with `session` delegate in Sessions and `self` in Advanced; the v4.x.x example uses generic `PaymentMethod.self`/`Component` instead (lines 35–90). The test table distinguishes successful, blocked, insufficient-funds and amount-limit cards with OTP, then points to test Customer Area for status (lines 92–103). Before live acceptance, the page requires a Napas card request in live Customer Area (line 105). These are test/setup claims, not verified live operation ([[source-adyen-docs-payment-methods-napas-card-ios-component]]).
