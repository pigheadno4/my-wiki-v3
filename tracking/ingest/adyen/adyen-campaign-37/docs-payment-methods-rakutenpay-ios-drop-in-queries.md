Q1. What does the Rakuten Pay iOS Drop-in guide cover, and what cannot be inferred?
A1. It describes method-specific Sessions and Advanced additions for an existing iOS Drop-in integration, restricted recurring use and a TEST DGFT simulator route (raw lines 17–66, 68–110). It does not establish current live merchant enablement, a token, final redirect/webhook result or authorized/captured/settled payment (raw lines 25–110). Q2. What are the flow prerequisites and material recurring/test restrictions?
A2. Sessions requires iOS Drop-in Sessions, Checkout API v68+ and Rakuten Pay in Customer Area, with no extra method request/configuration fields stated (raw lines 29–46). Advanced requires iOS Drop-in Advanced, redirect handling and Customer Area setup, also with no extra method fields (raw lines 72–90). One-off card-on-file use is review-gated; without periodic cadence a card-on-file Recurring Processing Model is prohibited, and every model requires application. Shopper permission is strongly recommended, not stated as a mandatory step (raw lines 50–56, 94–100). The TEST account redirects to a DGFT simulator; no Rakuten Pay app is needed there (raw lines 60–66, 104–110).
