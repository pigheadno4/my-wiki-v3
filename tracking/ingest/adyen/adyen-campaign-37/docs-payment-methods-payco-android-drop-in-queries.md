Q1. What does the PayCo Android Drop-in guide cover, and what cannot be inferred?
A1. It gives method-specific additions for existing Android Drop-in Sessions and Advanced integrations (raw lines 17–46, 56–76). It does not establish country/currency or merchant eligibility, a live enablement path, shopper return/webhook procedure or an authorized/captured/settled payment (raw lines 15–82). Q2. What are the flow prerequisites and testing limits?
A2. Sessions requires Android Drop-in Sessions, Checkout API v68+ and PayCo in Customer Area, with no extra PayCo `/sessions` fields or Drop-in configuration stated (raw lines 29–46). Advanced requires Android Drop-in Advanced, redirect handling and Customer Area setup, with no extra PayCo `/payments` fields/configuration; no Advanced version minimum is stated (raw lines 58–76). Both sections say there are no PayCo-specific test credentials and refer merchants to Support for testing help, without saying TEST is unavailable (raw lines 48–54, 78–82).
