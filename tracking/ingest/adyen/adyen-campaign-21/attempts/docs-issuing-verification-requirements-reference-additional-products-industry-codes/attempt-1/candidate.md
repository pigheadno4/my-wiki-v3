## Overview

This Issuing-path Adyen industry-code reference (last modified and captured 2026-09-15) instructs a platform to choose an Industry Code (IC) that best represents a user's business activity. It says Adyen maps ICs to Merchant Category Codes (MCCs), adapts them to internal regulations, and may require approval before onboarding. [[adyen]] [[adyen-issuing]]

## Key takeaways

- The page says accurate IC selection matters because some MCCs map to multiple ICs. It directs readers to their Adyen contact or Support Team if approval for an IC is needed before onboarding users. See raw lines 17–21.
- **Snapshot gap:** Although the page says it accepts only the following IC values, the captured raw contains no code/value list after that sentence. It has three numbered footnotes without their associated entries: an average-transaction-value condition of EUR 2000 or less, a product-inclusion note, and an American Express exclusion. The missing entries prevent identifying which IC, if any, each footnote qualifies. Do not infer an accepted code, an industry approval requirement for a particular business, or card-brand support from this snapshot. See raw lines 23–29.
- The captured page points to a brand-specific industry-codes list under See also; that target is navigation only here. See raw lines 31–33.
