Q1. What is this Issuing page for, and what must be known before creating a legal entity?

Answer: It is the captured Issuing guide for creating an individual or organization legal entity on the Balance Platform by API `POST /legalEntities`, then viewing and updating the resource through the documented API, Customer Area, or Hosted onboarding paths. Q2. What is the material boundary when updating or changing a legal entity type?

Answer: A PATCH updates only supplied parameters (raw line 643). The Hosted onboarding detail-update section says verification checks rerun when details are updated through that flow (lines 964–973). A type change reruns all checks, applies only to the main legal entity, and an incorrectly typed associated entity requires creating a new entity (lines 975–984). API-only onboarding directly changes only individual to organization or the reverse and then needs additional required information; the guide calls for another PATCH to complete information such as address, and describes a two-step route to sole proprietorship (lines 1021–1028, 1219–1239). For viewing, raw GET prose links at lines 428 and 608 point to PATCH reference URLs; use the GET method in the prose, curl, and SDK examples.
