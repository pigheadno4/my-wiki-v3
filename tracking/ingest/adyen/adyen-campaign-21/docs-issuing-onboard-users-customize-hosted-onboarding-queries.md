Q2 — What are the settings and troubleshooting boundaries? Locale defaults to browser language, with `en-US` fallback; an unsupported supplied locale falls to the next available value for its language (raw lines 124-128). The onboarding-link settings table gives dated defaults, including `allowDebugUi=true`, `enforceLegalAge=false` and `instantBankVerification=true` (158-176). Bank credentials in the table are test examples (178-204). Debug reports can contain sensitive legal-entity and task data; restrict sharing to the team or Adyen Support to the minimum needed (206-227). These steps do not prove completed verification.
