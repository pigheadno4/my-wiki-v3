# Q1 — purpose and prerequisite

**Query:** What is the purpose of this Issuing brand-specific industry-code page, and when does it tell a platform to contact Adyen?

**Answer:** Adyen says it assigned brand-specific Industry Codes to capture unique MCCs for certain brands and directs selection of the code best matching the user's brand and business activity (raw lines 17–19). It says to contact an Adyen contact or Support **if approval for an industry code is needed before onboarding users** (raw line 21); it does not say every selection is automatically approved.
