Q1 — What is an Issuing authorisation hold, and who can release it? An Adyen-issued-card authorisation reserves funds in the cardholder's balance account until capture or hold release (raw line 19). Only the processing merchant can actively cancel/reverse an existing authorisation; Adyen as issuer and the platform cannot cancel it or change its expiry timeline themselves (21-27). Expiry automatically releases the reserve and emits a transfer.updated webhook (29-31). Q2 — How long can holds last, and what happens after expiry? The dated Visa table uses MCC/location: 31 days for cruise/lodging/vehicle rental, 3 days for US commuter/bus/rail and 7 days otherwise; it separately notes an acquirer-requested card-not-present extension up to 30 days (raw lines 40-50). Mastercard pre-authorisations last 30 days with adjustment/extension possible; final/default last 7 days (52-62). A merchant may still submit a capture after expiry, and returned available funds can be captured later (64-68); expiry is not a final no-capture outcome.
