Q1 — What does Create cards do, and what comes first? After an account holder and balance account exist, the Issuing guide creates a linked card `paymentInstrument`; the card needs a pre-approved Visa or Mastercard scheme-program use case (raw lines 19-36). Virtual creation returns card details, while physical creation starts manufacturing and omits full card details; physical production needs profile/delivery information (33-42). Q2 — Which card and authorized-user limits or failure paths matter? Cards default active, while Adyen recommends inactive physical cards until delivered (raw line 53). The companion [[source-adyen-docs-issuing-account-holder-status]] calls inactive the initial payment-instrument status (its raw line 36); the captured default descriptions conflict and do not establish a universal default. Test physical cards are not delivered (591-596); live physical card and mailer designs require scheme approval (25-29), and stopping personalization must happen before manufacture (972-976). Company-card authorized users need legal entities and allowed `authorisedPaymentInstrumentUser` capability; invalid needs additional information, rejected is final, and later invalid/rejected status can suspend the card (927-958). PATCH replaces the entire user list (968). The dated guide does not prove live eligibility or successful card/payment operation.
