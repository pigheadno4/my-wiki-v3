Q1. What are the Issuing account-structure models, and what resources must be in place for a user to use Issuing?
Answer: Enterprise issuing is an organization issuing cards to its own employees/departments and primarily funding them; embedded issuing is a platform offering cards to independent business users, who issue and fund the cards (raw lines 26–34). The guide says to create a user account holder linked to the user's balance account and main legal entity, a funds-holding balance account, and a payment instrument linked to that balance account (raw lines 67–71). Q2. Who can bear a negative user balance, and what regional condition does this page state?
Answer: The business's liable balance account can be debited if user balance accounts incur negative balances (raw lines 73–77). For an Adyen for Platforms integration that splits incoming sales funds, the guide says to have a separate balance platform for each region in which payments are acquired (raw lines 62–66).
