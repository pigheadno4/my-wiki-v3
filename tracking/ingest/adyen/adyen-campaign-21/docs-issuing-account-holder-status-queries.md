Its table lists active → suspended/closed, suspended → active/closed, and permanent closed (25-36). Q2 — What inheritance, settlement and conflict boundaries matter? Only Adyen can reverse its own suspensions (raw line 49). For **Adyen-initiated** suspension, linked balance accounts also suspend; Adyen-initiated closure closes related accounts and payment instruments (51-56). Customer Area status reason and account-holder update webhook are observation paths (44-47). In the distinct platform API `PATCH` suspension, payout capability is disabled but related balance accounts and payment instruments remain active ([[source-adyen-docs-issuing-manage-account-holders]], its raw line 752). Some capability effects can inherit through a shared legal entity, with case-by-case variation (58-85). The payment section concerns post-settlement split routing: blocked pay-in or closed-holder splits go to the liable holder's Reserved Incorrect Splits Account (87-93), not a guarantee of a specific settlement. This page's line 36 says inactive is a payment instrument's initial non-editable state, conflicting with the Create cards guide's default-active wording and Manage cards activation procedure.
