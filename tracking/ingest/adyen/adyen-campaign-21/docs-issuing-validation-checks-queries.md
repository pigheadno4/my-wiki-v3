Q1. Where can an Issuing operator diagnose a refused transaction, and what do validation result values mean?
Answer: For a refused balancePlatform.transfer.created webhook, inspect validationFacts for types with invalid results; Customer Area Balances > Transfers also shows validation results/refusal reasons (raw lines 21–38). valid means passed, invalid failed, notValidated means no check due to unavailable service or information, and notApplicable means Adyen deemed it inapplicable to the transaction state (raw lines 40–45). Q2. What timing do the captured guides state for relayed-authorisation stand-in and reply/fallback, and does a valid partialApproval mean the full amount was authorised?
Answer: This validation-checks page says no response after 1500 ms triggers relayedAuthorisationStip, with valid/invalid determined by the configured fallback (its raw lines 158–168). The dedicated [[source-adyen-docs-issuing-authorisation-relayed-authorisation]] guide states a 2000 ms reply/fallback window (its raw lines 23, 82, 260). Their timing relationship is unresolved; neither source alone establishes which threshold governs a live integration. The partialApproval validation is conditional on acquirer support and certain MCCs and is always valid when performed; the separate Partially approved refusal-reason entry describes approval only up to the available balance (validation-checks raw lines 130–132, 295–297). That valid result does not establish full-amount authorisation.
