Q1. When does this captured Issuing guide say a card must be enrolled in 3D Secure, and how can cardholders authenticate?
Answer: Both conditions must hold: the card is issued in the EEA or UK and usable for online payments there (pinned raw lines 29–32). Issuing credentials need the Bank SCA Webservice Role to enroll cards (lines 34–39). The page offers password plus phone OTP or a registered eligible device for OOB authentication in the platform's secure application using passcode/biometrics (lines 41–46). These are issuer-side enrollment and cardholder-authentication steps, not successful payment authorisation. Q2. Why can an EEA/UK online payment skip 3D Secure, and what happens next?
Answer: The guide says some transactions are out of PSD2 SCA scope or exempt; when no challenge is required the payment proceeds to separate authorisation (pinned raw lines 48–50). Listed out-of-scope cases include a card **issued** outside EEA/UK or an **acquirer** based outside EEA/UK, MIT recurring/subscription payments except the initial setup transaction, and MOTO (lines 54–61). Possible exemptions are conditional and may be applied by Adyen or requested by the acquirer; the guide says to check applicability with Adyen (lines 52, 63–69). A skipped or passed authentication does not itself prove an authorised/captured payment.
