Q1. Who configures Issuing OTP authentication, what is required, and what happens next?
Answer: The Issuing platform's API credential needs the Bank SCA Webservice Role (raw line 38). The platform adds password plus cardholder phone/email authentication data when creating a card (lines 40–48), or changes it later through `PATCH /paymentInstruments/{id}` (line 638). The cardholder provides password and SMS/email OTP (lines 19–26); successful authentication then sends the payment to Adyen for a separate authorisation decision (lines 27–31). Q2. What failure and scope boundaries prevent an incorrect payment or OTP claim?
Answer: SMS normally has priority, but some countries always use email; no contact method means decline because an OTP cannot be sent (raw lines 50–54). The OTP guide says `balancePlatform.authentication.created` is sent regardless of outcome (line 945), with frictionless, challenge and rejected example payloads (lines 951–1081). The separately captured [[source-adyen-docs-issuing-3d-secure-troubleshooting]] says failed authentication yields no relayed authorization or webhook without naming the event family (troubleshooting raw line 33); the scope difference is unresolved; sample retries or OOB fields are examples, not guaranteed OTP behavior. Authentication success precedes authorisation (line 28), and purchases still require funding, payment processing and lifecycle choices (line 1085). The examples are test/inactive-card snippets (lines 62–108), not evidence of a live card, capture or settlement.
