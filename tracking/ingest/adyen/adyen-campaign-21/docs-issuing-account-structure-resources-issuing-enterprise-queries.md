Q1. Who funds cards in Enterprise Issuing, and how do the two models differ?
Answer: The issuing organization provides cards to its own employees or departments and is the primary funder (pinned raw lines 19–24). With acquiring it uses payments processed through Adyen; a merchant account's settled funds go to one balance account, while multiple merchant accounts can share one balance account (lines 26, 44–46, 70–72). Standalone does not process payments through Adyen for funding; it tops up via a verified transfer instrument/bank account (lines 27, 82–86). Q2. Which resource and location rules are conditional, and why does the standalone example still mention a merchant account?
Answer: For acquiring, the page says a balance platform and merchant account are required in each region where the organization is registered **and wishes to acquire** (line 37); a store is required only for in-person processing (line 53), and the business line supplies regional industry data for KYC checks before cards (line 59). Counts of hotel branches, merchant accounts, platforms and cards are illustrative (lines 31–79). The standalone consultancy example includes a merchant account **to link resources** but expressly funds cards by verified-bank top-ups rather than Adyen-acquired payments (lines 84–105). The guide does not prove eligibility, verification or successful issuance/payment.
