Q1. Where do Issuing KYC remediating action codes appear, and what does the table tell an integration to do?
Answer: Adyen sends action codes/messages in `verificationErrors` only when the action is common to its suberrors, and in individual `subErrors` (pinned raw lines 16–19). One error may have multiple actions. The example distinguishes outer error `1_30`, suberror `1_3001`, and actions `1_300`/`1_301` (lines 278–337). Match a received action to its context; the catalog does not prove remediation success. The page says existing Platforms or Issuing integrations need no additional preparation for this reference (line 23). Q2. What should a reader do with a `dataMissing` action or ambiguous `2_198`, and which actions have hard boundaries?
Answer: Missing-data codes may be returned for error type `dataMissing` and identify fields/documents on legal entities, bank accounts, business lines or documents (pinned raw lines 127–262); they are conditional, not global field requirements. The same `2_198` is printed for registered-address state/province and principal-place-of-business street (lines 228–229), so use the returned message and current provider guidance before choosing a field. General `1_100` says no remediation possible and `1_101` says contact Support (lines 37–43). `3_100` directs use of `/legalEntities/{id}/confirmDataReview` to indicate data confirmation, not proof of accepted review (lines 272–276).
