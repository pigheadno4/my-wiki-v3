# Retrieval checks — embedded Issuing account structure

## Q1: What are the two embedded Issuing funding models and the central account prerequisites?

The 2026-09-15 snapshot says independent business users fund cards. With acquiring, the platform acquires payments for users through Adyen; it must have a balance platform and merchant account in each region where it is registered and wants to acquire. In standalone, the platform does not acquire users' payments through Adyen; users top up from verified transfer instruments. Both example structures separate the user-funds balance account from card-linked balance accounts, although multiple cards may share a card-linked account. Raw lines 19–24, 34, 68–74, 86–90, 112–118. ## Q2: Who owns the resources, and what limits or source conflicts matter before treating the example as an operational flow?

In the acquiring salon example, the platform owns company/regional merchant and balance-platform resources plus liable and business-legal-entity resources; cardholder/user resources include stores, account holders, separate funds/card-linked balance accounts, verified transfer instruments, legal entities for KYC, business lines, and cards (raw lines 38–80). In the standalone construction example, the platform still has a merchant account and linked balance platform although it does not acquire user payments through Adyen; its users top up by verified transfer instrument (raw lines 86–122). The acquiring salon example unexpectedly calls the business line food/delivery and card use food orders (raw lines 30, 78–80); do not generalize that wording.
