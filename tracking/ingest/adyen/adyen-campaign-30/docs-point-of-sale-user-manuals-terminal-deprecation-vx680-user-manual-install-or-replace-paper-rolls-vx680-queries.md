# C30 VX680 paper-roll retrieval checks

Q1. Which paper does the VX680 guide name, and how is the roll installed?
Answer: It specifies 57 mm × 35 mm × 12 mm thermal receipt paper (pinned raw lines 20–22). The guide opens the cover, removes a partial roll, removes/unwinds past glue residue, places the roll so paper feeds from its bottom when the terminal is inverted, and closes the cover with a little clean paper outside (lines 28–39). These are physical steps, not an observed print test. Q2. Does a loaded roll establish payment readiness or current VX680 support?
Answer: No. The historical guide says payments cannot be accepted without receipt paper (pinned raw line 24), but loading paper does not establish the other conditions or a successful payment. The separately captured eVo table places VX680 under “No more transactions” on 29 March 2024 (raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/user-manuals/terminal-deprecation/2026-09-15.md lines 32–35).
