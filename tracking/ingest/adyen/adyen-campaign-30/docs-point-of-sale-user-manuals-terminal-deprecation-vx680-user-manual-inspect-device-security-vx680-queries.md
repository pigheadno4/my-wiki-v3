# VX680 security-inspection retrieval checks

Q1. What is the purpose and what should staff inspect or verify?
A. This historical VX680 guide is a visual integrity checklist (raw lines 14–16). Staff compare the device to manual pictures, check the logo, avoid a damaged or sticker-covered device, verify third-party personnel before access, check for cameras/skimmers, verify devices before install/replace/return, notice suspicious behavior, report tamper/substitution indications to appropriate personnel and protect customer PIN privacy (raw lines 20–38). Q2. What cadence and escalation apply, and does inspection prove current certification or transaction support?
A. The guide says visual integrity inspections weekly; when in doubt, stop using the device and contact Support (raw line 40). Suspicious behavior/tampering should be reported to appropriate personnel such as a manager or security officer (raw line 36). The page's generic secure/certified wording is historical (raw line 16). The parent deprecation table identifies VX680 as PTS PCI 3, lists approval expiry April 30, 2021 and “No more transactions” March 29, 2024 (parent raw lines 23–35); a visual inspection does not establish current certification or payment capability.
