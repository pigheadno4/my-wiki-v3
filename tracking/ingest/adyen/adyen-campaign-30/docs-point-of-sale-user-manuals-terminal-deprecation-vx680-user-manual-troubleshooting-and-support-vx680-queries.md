# C30 VX680 troubleshooting and support retrieval checks

## Q1 — Main purpose and prerequisites

**Question:** Where does the historical VX680 page send someone with a terminal issue, and what details does it ask for if they need assistance?

**Answer:** It says to try the linked POS troubleshooting documentation first (pinned raw lines 16–18). If that does not resolve the issue, it says to email the linked Support Team (lines 20–22). Supply the merchant account and terminal serial number, and the transaction PSP reference **if applicable**; the serial can be found in Device info or on the back of the device (lines 24–28). ## Q2 — Material boundary or failure

**Question:** Does the guide promise a fix or establish current VX680 transaction support?

**Answer:** No. It says self-service is the quickest solution **in many cases**, then offers a conditional escalation if unresolved (pinned raw lines 18–22). It gives no diagnosis, reply, repair or payment result. This 2019-modified page was captured in the deprecation tree (raw lines 1–10); the separate eVo deprecation table lists VX680 with a historical **29 March 2024** “No more transactions” date (deprecation raw lines 32–35).
