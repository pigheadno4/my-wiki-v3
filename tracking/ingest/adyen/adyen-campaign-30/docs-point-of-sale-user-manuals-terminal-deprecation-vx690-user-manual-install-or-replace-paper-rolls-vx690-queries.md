# C30 VX690 paper-roll retrieval checks

Q1. Which paper does the VX690 guide specify, and what are the loading cautions?
Answer: It specifies 57 mm × 40 mm × 12 mm thermal receipt paper (pinned raw lines 20–22). The VX690 latch is at the top (line 28); the guide removes the old roll, clears the new roll's strip/glue residue, feeds paper from the bottom when the terminal is inverted, places it in the tray and closes the cover until it clicks (lines 29–36). It documents a procedure, not a print test. Q2. Does installing paper show the VX690 can currently accept payments?
Answer: No. The historical guide says payments cannot be accepted without receipt paper (pinned raw line 24), but a roll does not establish other conditions or a successful transaction. The separately captured eVo table places VX690 under “No more transactions” on 29 March 2024 (raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/user-manuals/terminal-deprecation/2026-09-15.md lines 32–35).
