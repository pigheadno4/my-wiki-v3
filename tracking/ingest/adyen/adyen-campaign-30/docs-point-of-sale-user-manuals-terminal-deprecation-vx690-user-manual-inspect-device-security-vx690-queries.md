# C30 VX690 security-inspection retrieval checks

## Q1 — Main purpose and prerequisites

**Question:** What does the historical VX690 inspection page tell staff to check, and how often?

**Answer:** Staff are told to compare the device with manual photos, check the Adyen or merchant logo after power-on, avoid using a damaged or sticker-covered device, verify third-party repair or maintenance identity before access, check for cameras and skimmers, verify device installation/replacement/return, report suspected tampering, and protect PIN privacy on handheld devices without a shield (pinned raw lines 20–38). It calls for visual integrity inspections **weekly** (line 40). ## Q2 — Material boundary or failure

**Question:** What action does the VX690 page require when security is in doubt, and does this prove current certification or transaction support?

**Answer:** When in doubt, stop using the device and contact the linked Adyen Support Team (pinned raw line 40); suspicious behavior or indications of tampering/substitution should be reported to appropriate personnel such as a manager or security officer (line 36). This 2019-modified page was captured in 2026 under terminal deprecation (lines 1–10). It documents an inspection procedure, not a passed inspection or current certification. The separate deprecation table lists VX690 with a historical **29 March 2024** “No more transactions” date (deprecation raw lines 32–35).
