# C30 VX690 Bluetooth base-station retrieval checks

Q1. What is needed to pair a VX690 with its Bluetooth base?
Answer: This historical guide says the network uses DHCP (pinned raw line 22); it instructs removal of terminal Wi-Fi profiles through Admin-menu Wi-Fi reset, which restarts the device (lines 24–31). The base is powered by mini USB and networked via its ETH-marked Ethernet port (lines 33–35). The pairing path scans, makes the base discoverable, chooses its serial number, sets the connection and checks status (lines 39–49). A PIN may be requested at Admin-menu entry (lines 26, 41). Q2. Does “Ethernet/BT Connected” prove current VX690 boarding or payment readiness, and what destructive step precedes it?
Answer: No. `Ethernet/BT` = `Connected` is the guide's expected local connection display (pinned raw line 49), not an observed boarding or payment result. The preliminary Wi-Fi reset removes profiles and restarts the terminal (lines 24–31). The separate eVo table lists VX690 under “No more transactions” on 29 March 2024 (raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/user-manuals/terminal-deprecation/2026-09-15.md lines 32–35).
