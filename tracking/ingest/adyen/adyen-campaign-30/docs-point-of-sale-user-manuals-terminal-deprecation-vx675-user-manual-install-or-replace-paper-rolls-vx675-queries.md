# VX675 receipt-paper retrieval checks

Q1. What does this guide cover, and what paper does it specify? It describes installing or replacing VX675 receipt rolls (pinned raw lines 14–16) and lists 57 mm width × 25 mm diameter, 12 mm core and 50-foot length (pinned raw lines 20–24). The loading sequence, including bottom feed when inverted and paper extending beyond the closed cover, is at pinned raw lines 28–52. Q2. Does loading paper establish current VX675 payment support? No. The 2019 manual says no receipt paper means payments could not be accepted (pinned raw line 26), but the separate eVo deprecation table lists VX675 with 29 March 2024 under “No more transactions” (its raw lines 32–34). This physical maintenance step establishes neither a current device capability nor an actual payment or printed receipt.
