# C30 VX690 standalone-refund retrieval checks

Q1. What are the VX690 guide's scope and conditions for starting a refund?
Answer: It applies when the VX690 is in standalone mode (pinned raw lines 15–17). The guide says Adyen enables refund functionality per device and requires a password (line 22); its steps show an illustrated key + `8` for Special Payments, a PIN to unlock the device, and amount entry (lines 28–30). The page does not equate the password and PIN or give a final refund response. Q2. What does the exclamation mark mean, and does this procedure prove a refund or current device support?
Answer: The guide says a top-bar exclamation means the terminal holds data that failed to synchronize with Adyen because it lacked outgoing internet (pinned raw line 19). It does not identify a particular refund's result; the listed steps stop at amount entry and “proceed like in a normal transaction” (line 30). The separate eVo table lists VX690 “No more transactions” on 29 March 2024 (raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/user-manuals/terminal-deprecation/2026-09-15.md lines 32–35).
