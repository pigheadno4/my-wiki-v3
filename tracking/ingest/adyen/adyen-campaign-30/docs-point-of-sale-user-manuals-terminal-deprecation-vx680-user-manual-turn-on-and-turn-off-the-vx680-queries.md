# VX680 power retrieval checks

Q1. How does this historical guide say to turn the VX680 on and off?
A. Hold the pictured key for a couple of seconds to turn on (raw lines 18–20). After full boot, the Adyen logo appears, or a custom logo if supplied and the terminal was already registered to the account (raw line 21). To turn off, hold the separately pictured key; repeated beeps precede power-down (raw lines 23–25). The raw uses images for both controls, so their text labels are not inferred. Q2. Does the displayed logo or power-on show payment readiness or current VX680 transaction support?
A. No. The page says power-on begins setup (raw line 16), and the custom logo has a conditional registration explanation (raw line 21). It does not show network connection, completed boarding or payment authorization. The parent eVo table lists VX680 “No more transactions” on March 29, 2024 (parent raw lines 32–35).
