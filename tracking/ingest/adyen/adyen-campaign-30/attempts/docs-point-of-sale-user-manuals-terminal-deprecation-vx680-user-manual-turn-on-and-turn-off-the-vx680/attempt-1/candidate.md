## Overview

This 2019-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] VX680 manual page gives historical power-on and power-off instructions for the physical terminal. It belongs with [[adyen-payment-terminals]].

## Key takeaways

- To turn on, the page says to hold the **pictured key** for a couple of seconds. Once fully booted, the terminal displays an Adyen logo, or a custom logo if the merchant supplied one and Adyen had already registered the terminal to the account. The key symbol is image-only in the raw; this entry does not assign it a text label.
- To turn off, hold the separately **pictured key**; the page says repeated beeps precede power-down. Consult the raw image locator for that control. The page states no battery-charge condition or charging procedure.
- The opening line frames power-on as a step toward setting up payments, not evidence of boarding, network access, current model support or an actual payment. The parent [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation]] table lists VX680 under **“No more transactions” on March 29, 2024**. Power or logo display cannot override that historical transaction cutoff.
