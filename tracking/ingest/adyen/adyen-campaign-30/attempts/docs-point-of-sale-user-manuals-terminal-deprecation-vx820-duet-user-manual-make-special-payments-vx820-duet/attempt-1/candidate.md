## Overview

This [[adyen]] **VX820 Duet** manual page, last modified **2019-05-20** and captured 2026-09-15 under terminal deprecation, explains access to a **PIN-unlocked special payment functions menu** for custom or ad-hoc options. The menu's contents depend on the merchant configuration (raw lines 16, 24–26). It is historical physical-device guidance in [[adyen-payment-terminals]], not evidence that a given merchant has an option enabled or that any payment or refund completed. The separate [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation|eVo deprecation schedule]] lists VX820 Duet **“No more transactions” on 29 March 2024** (that source's raw line 34).

## Key takeaways

- Access uses an **image-only confirm-key symbol plus 8**, then a **PIN to unlock the device** (raw lines 20–24). The raw illustration supplies the symbol; its unlabeled image is not converted into a guessed button name.
- Depending on configuration, **Normal payment** opens a payment screen to enter an amount. **Manual entry payment** opens a payment screen, then after amount entry prompts for the card **PAN and expiry date** (raw lines 26–35). The page says the transaction then follows the normal flow; that is a procedural continuation, not an authorization, capture or settlement outcome.
- The menu also includes a **refund option**, but this page routes its procedure to the separate VX820 Duet refund guide (raw line 37). It provides no refund result or eligibility test.
- Because availability varies with merchant configuration, neither this menu list nor the 2019 instructions establish current device, account, card-entry or refund capabilities.
