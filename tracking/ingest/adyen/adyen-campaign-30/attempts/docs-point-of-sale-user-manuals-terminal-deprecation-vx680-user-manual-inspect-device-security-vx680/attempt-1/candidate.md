## Overview

This 2019-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] VX680 manual page gives staff a visual device-integrity checklist. It belongs with [[adyen-payment-terminals]] and describes historical inspection practice, not a current certification finding.

## Key takeaways

- Staff should compare the device with manual pictures and check for the Adyen or company logo at power-on. The guide says not to use a device if it is damaged or sticker-covered.
- It tells staff to verify the identity of third-party repair or maintenance personnel before allowing access, check regularly for cameras and skimmers, verify devices before installing, replacing or returning them, and report suspicious behavior or possible tampering/substitution to appropriate personnel. It also calls out protecting the customer's PIN on a handheld device without a privacy shield.
- Perform visual integrity inspections weekly. **When in doubt, stop using the device and contact Adyen Support.** Reporting indications to a manager or security officer and stopping use/contacting Support are separate instructions.
- The page's generic “secure, certified” description was written for the old VX680 guide. The related [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation]] table identifies VX680 as PTS PCI 3, gives **April 30, 2021** as its approval expiry and lists **March 29, 2024** under “No more transactions.” A visual check cannot prove certification, current support or suitability for transactions.
