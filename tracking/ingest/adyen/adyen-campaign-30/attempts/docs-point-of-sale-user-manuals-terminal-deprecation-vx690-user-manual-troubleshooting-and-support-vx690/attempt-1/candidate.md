## Overview

This 2019-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] VX690 manual page routes a terminal issue first to separate POS Troubleshooting documentation, then to the linked Support Team if the issue remains unresolved. It is a historical escalation pointer within the terminal-deprecation subtree. The separately captured [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation|eVo deprecation table]] lists VX690 under **29 March 2024 "No more transactions"**; this support route does not prove present support eligibility, repair or restored payment operation. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]].

## Key takeaways

- **Escalation condition:** The page recommends self-diagnosis through linked POS Troubleshooting documentation first and says to email the linked Support Team if that does not resolve the issue. This page itself does not contain a diagnostic or repair procedure. The captured Support URL points to a `ca-test.adyen.com` address; the snapshot does not establish a current LIVE support channel. See pinned raw lines 16–22.
- **Information to provide:** The list asks for merchant account and terminal serial number. A transaction PSP reference is requested **if applicable**, not for every device issue. It says the serial is in Device info and on the terminal back; the sample serial format is an example, not a real device identifier. See pinned raw lines 24–28.
- **Outcome boundary:** Following the route or supplying identifiers does not itself show that Support was contacted, the problem was fixed, the terminal became eligible to transact or a payment succeeded.
