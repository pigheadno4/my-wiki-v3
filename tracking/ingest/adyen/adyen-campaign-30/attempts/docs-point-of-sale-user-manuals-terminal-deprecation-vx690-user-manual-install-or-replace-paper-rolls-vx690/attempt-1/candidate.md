## Overview

This 2019-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] VX690 guide describes installing or replacing a receipt-paper roll. It specifies **57 mm × 40 mm × 12 mm thermal paper** for this model and says payment acceptance is not possible without paper. That is the historical guide's operating statement, not proof that loading paper makes a terminal currently eligible to transact: the separately captured [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation|eVo deprecation table]] lists VX690 under **29 March 2024 "No more transactions"**. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]].

## Key takeaways

- **Model-specific paper and access:** Use the stated 57 × 40 × 12 mm thermal roll; the guide locates the paper-cover latch at the **top** of the VX690. These details are specific to this model. See pinned raw lines 20–22 and 28–29.
- **Loading cautions:** Remove any partial roll, loosen or remove the new roll's glued edge or protective strip, and unwind past glue residue. With the terminal inverted, paper should feed from the bottom of the roll. Place it in the tray and gently close the cover until it clicks. See pinned raw lines 30–36.
- **Outcome boundary:** The guide says lack of receipt paper prevents payment acceptance (raw line 24), but it reports no print test, device repair, boarding or successful transaction. The later dated deprecation table limits any current-payment inference.
