## Overview

This [[adyen]] VX680 manual page, last modified in 2019 and captured 2026-09-15, describes charging that model on a cradle before use. It specifies the cradle, power-supply cable, electrical-grid connection and an at-least-six-hour charge with a steady LED as the page's full-charge instruction. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]]. The page does not identify a charger certification, establish a particular battery's health, or verify that a device actually charged or powered on.

The separate [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation]] table lists VX680 with **29 March 2024** under “No more transactions” (that page's raw lines 32–34). These older charging steps are historical device maintenance, not present device support, certification, boarding or payment-readiness proof.

## Key takeaways

- **Power path:** Connect the charging cradle to its power-supply cable and the supply to the electrical grid, then place the VX680 on the cradle. The page says cradle placement charges the device and powers it on. It does not authorize substituting an unspecified power accessory. See pinned raw lines 18–22.
- **Charge condition:** The page instructs leaving the VX680 on the cradle for **at least six hours**, until the LED indicator remains lit, to fully charge it. That is the guide's stated condition; it is not a measured battery-capacity or current-health result for a specific terminal. See pinned raw line 24.
