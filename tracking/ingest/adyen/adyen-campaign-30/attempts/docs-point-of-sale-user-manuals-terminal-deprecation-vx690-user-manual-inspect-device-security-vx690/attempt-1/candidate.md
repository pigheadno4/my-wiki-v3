## Overview

This [[adyen]] **VX690** physical-terminal inspection page was last modified 2019-04-19 and captured 2026-09-15 under the terminal-deprecation manual tree. It instructs staff to look for tampering or substitution, control third-party access, protect shopper PIN privacy and perform weekly visual integrity checks. **When in doubt, stop using the device and contact Adyen Support.** These historical instructions do not establish that a particular VX690 passed inspection, remains certified or can process current payments. The separate [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation]] lists VX690 with a historical **29 March 2024** "No more transactions" date. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]].

## Key takeaways

- Staff should compare the device with manual photos and check that the Adyen or merchant logo appears after power-on. **Do not use a damaged device or one covered with a sticker.** The page directs regular checks for cameras and skimming equipment. See raw lines 20–30.
- Verify the identity of anyone claiming to be third-party repair or maintenance personnel **before granting access** to modify or troubleshoot a device. Do not install, replace or return devices without verification; report suspicious behavior or indications of tampering or substitution to appropriate personnel such as a manager or security officer. See raw lines 28–36.
- Do not view customers' PIN entry on a handheld device without a privacy shield. Perform **visual device integrity inspections weekly**; if there is doubt, **stop using the device and contact Adyen Support**. See raw lines 38–40. The linked Support URL is the historical page's escalation route, not proof of current contact details or an incident outcome.
