## Overview

This [[adyen]] VX675 manual page gives historical power-on and power-off button actions for beginning device setup. It was last modified 2019-04-18 and captured 2026-09-15 under terminal deprecation. The buttons are shown only as images in the raw; the page does not name either key in text. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]].

## Key takeaways

- For power-on, hold the pictured key until the startup screen appears (raw lines 18–20). The startup screen is a power-on cue, not evidence of boarding, connection or payment readiness.
- For power-off, hold the separately pictured key; the guide says the terminal beeps repeatedly and then turns off (raw lines 22–24). The text gives no hold duration or textual key name, so consult the source image instead of guessing one.
- The related [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation]] overview lists VX675 “No more transactions” on **29 March 2024** (that overview's raw lines 32–35). This older startup instruction does not establish current device support, PCI approval or transaction processing.
