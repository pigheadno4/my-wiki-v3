## Overview

This [[adyen]] VX690 user-manual hub is a historical setup and component directory for a physical payment terminal, last modified 2021-03-25 and captured 2026-09-15. It says new VX690 terminals cannot be ordered because the model is being deprecated. Its older quickstart routes do not establish current model support, successful boarding or payment processing. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]].

## Key takeaways

- The manual says VX690 does not support 4G or later cellular technologies. For reliance on cellular connectivity, it advises choosing a 4G-capable terminal, citing the end of 2G/3G coverage by mobile operators. This is historical model and network guidance, not a statement about a particular operator's present coverage. See raw lines 19–20.
- Its quickstart points to charging, paper-roll installation, device security inspection, wireless-network setup or the Bluetooth base-station route, and boarding. These are links to separate procedures, not evidence that any step succeeded. See raw lines 24–38.
- The supplied-component list names a VX690 terminal, EU power cord/plug, receipt roll and privacy shield. See raw lines 40–48.
- The related [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation]] overview lists VX690 under PTS PCI 4 and gives **29 March 2024** as its “No more transactions” date (that source's raw lines 32–35). Treat the older manual's get-started wording as historical guidance; it does not override that later cutoff or prove current PCI approval, device eligibility or a payment outcome.
