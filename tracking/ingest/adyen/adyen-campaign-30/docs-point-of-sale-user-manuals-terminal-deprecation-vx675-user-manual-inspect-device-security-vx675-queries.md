# Retrieval checks: VX675 device-security inspection

Q1. What should staff inspect, and how often? This historical VX675 guide asks staff to compare the device with manual images and power-on logo, reject damage or stickers, verify third-party repair identity, check for cameras/skimmers and suspicious access, report tampering, and protect customer PIN entry (raw lines 20–38). It calls for weekly visual integrity inspections (line 40). Q2. What happens when integrity is doubtful, and does the page prove current certification? The page says to stop using the device and contact its linked Support Team when in doubt (raw line 40), after reporting suspected tampering to appropriate personnel (line 36). Its broad certified-terminal statement (line 16) is historical; the related deprecation overview lists VX675 “No more transactions” on 29 March 2024 (raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/user-manuals/terminal-deprecation/2026-09-15.md lines 32–35). Neither source proves current certification or a specific device's integrity.
