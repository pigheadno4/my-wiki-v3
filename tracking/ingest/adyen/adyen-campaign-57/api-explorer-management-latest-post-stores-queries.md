The request property table requires `address`, `description`, `merchantId`, `phoneNumber` and `shopperStatement`, with `country` required inside address (lines 20–41). `stateOrProvince` is required for AU, BR, CA, IN, MX, NZ and US; `businessLineIds` is required for a merchant with multiple business lines, otherwise the merchant business line is used (lines 30–31).
