## Q1. How do upfront and staggered Marketplace verification differ, and what controls payout?

Upfront collects all required data at onboarding and requires full verification before payments/payouts; the LEM `verificationPlan: upfront` setting is made on POST/PATCH legal-entity requests (raw lines 19–30). This Marketplace page says staggered is the default: it initially collects minimum data, then adds checks when a user reaches a higher tier (raw lines 19–24, 32–36). On a higher tier, payouts are temporarily disallowed until data is supplied and Adyen verifies it, with change/data requests sent by webhook (raw line 38). Four tiers depend on country/entity/processed amount and can also change due to checks or high-risk capabilities (raw lines 40–56). ## Q2. Is staggered verification universally available by default to every Platforms business?

The two same-date sources do not support that conclusion. The Marketplace page says staggered is applied by default and upfront can be enabled (raw line 24), but the separate [[raw/adyen/docs/platforms/verification-overview/verification-types/2026-09-15|general Platforms raw lines 24, 30–32]] recommends upfront and limits staggered requests to payment facilitators via an Adyen contact. The captures do not explain whether this is model-specific or inconsistent guidance. The Marketplace table gives tier **currencies**, not numeric thresholds (raw lines 40–56), and no page proves a user’s verification, payout entitlement or bank receipt.
