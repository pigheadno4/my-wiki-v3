# C24 worker retrieval checks: docs-classic-platforms-reconciliation-use-cases-understand-fees

Q1. Whose fees are reconciled, and which reports support each grain?
Answer: Fees are deducted from settlement and debited to the Classic platform's liable account (raw lines 19–23). Settlement details breaks down settled-transaction fees, Payment accounting includes transaction-related fees even when unsettled, and the payment-processing invoice totals monthly fees (lines 25–27). The guide cross-checks three totals using Settlement details and Marketplace Payments accounting (lines 35–56). Q2. What prevents double counting or a false mismatch?
Answer: The invoice accounts for fees already deducted, while outstanding debits/rebates appear as `InvoiceDeduction` rows (raw lines 29–33). Align report period and time zone first (lines 39–44). The example's Marketplace Payments accounting `Fee` row is −EUR 9.72 (line 76), versus a positive EUR 9.72 fee total (lines 93–96), so compare consistent signs. EUR 0.32 processing fees include a refused transaction (lines 104–114), showing why settled-only rows do not cover every transaction-related fee. Selected rows are illustrative, not a completed platform reconciliation.
