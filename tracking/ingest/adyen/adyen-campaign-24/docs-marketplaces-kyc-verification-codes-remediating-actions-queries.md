# C24 Marketplace-path remediating actions — retrieval checks

**Q1. What is this page for, and what preparation does it state?** This is a code-to-message lookup for actions attached to Adyen verification errors; multiple actions may resolve an error, with parent `verificationErrors` placement only when actions are common to `subErrors` (raw lines 14–19). For Adyen for Platforms or Adyen Issuing integrations, its Requirements section says there are no additional preparations (line 23). The route is [[index]] → [[adyen-index]] → [[adyen-platforms]] → [[source-adyen-docs-marketplaces-kyc-verification-codes-remediating-actions]] → [[raw/adyen/docs/marketplaces/kyc-verification-codes/remediating-actions/2026-09-15]].

**Q2. What boundary or ambiguity matters when choosing an action?** Read the action in its error level and object context: `2_1xx`–`2_2xx` may occur for `dataMissing` and name legal-entity, bank-account or business-line fields (raw lines 147–281), while `2_901`/`2_902` are Sign PCI/Accept TOS messages and `3_100` points to data-review confirmation (lines 283–296). The raw prints `2_198` for two different organization-address fields (lines 248–249), so this snapshot cannot choose between them. Example payload codes have distinct parent-error, suberror and remediation roles (lines 298–357); no row or example proves verification or capability approval.
