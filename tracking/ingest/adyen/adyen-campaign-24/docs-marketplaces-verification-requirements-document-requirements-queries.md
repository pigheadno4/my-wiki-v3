Q1. When may Adyen ask a Marketplace user for extra documents, and which section-level conditions matter?
Answer: After automatic verification fails or data cannot be verified, Adyen may request category-specific documents; submit original unedited material (raw lines 19–29). Bank proof uses bankStatement after bank verification fails (raw lines 64–66). Live selfie is scoped to business-account onboarding v4 signatories/sole-proprietor owners with consent and 24-hour upload fallback conditions (raw lines 208–260); source-of-funds/wealth sections apply only to business accounts and card issuing (raw lines 620–646). Q2. Does a document upload prove verification, and what unresolved photo-ID and bank-date wording should a reader retain?
Answer: No; the guide states document conditions and possible requests, not a completed verification result (raw lines 19–21). Photo IDs require front and back as separate files in the same POST, yet the section's size rule says maximum 1 file (raw lines 322, 337–340, 360–364); packaging is unresolved. Bank proofs have date-visibility exceptions, with dated RIB/online views still under 12 months, alongside a general >12-month rejection list (raw lines 92–109).
