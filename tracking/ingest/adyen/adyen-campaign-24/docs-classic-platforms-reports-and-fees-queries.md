# C24 query checks — docs-classic-platforms-reports-and-fees

Q1. Which Classic reports does this page route to for accounting and reconciliation?

It is a **Classic Adyen for Platforms** report/fee catalog, not a modern Balance Platform report definition (pinned raw 15–27). It separates Adyen-account reports for payment processing/invoices from platform reports tying events and balances to account holders (31–50). The page labels Payment accounting, Settlement details, Monthly invoice and Marketplace Payments accounting as required for full financial reconciliation (38–40, 48). It also routes to Received payment details, Marketpay Balance (the hub says balances are available on a daily basis; its child [[source-adyen-docs-classic-platforms-reports-and-fees-marketpay-balance-report]] lists Daily, Weekly, or Monthly report frequency), and a Marketpay Payout Report restricted to accounts with scheduled payouts (37,49–50). Report preparation and worked reconciliation are separate next steps (79–91). Q2. What fee, scope and payout boundaries matter?

Payment accounting includes payment-event fees, including unsettled items, but excludes platform transaction charges such as payout/fund-transfer/KYC charges (raw 38, 60–62). Settlement details concerns settled payable-batch entries and assessable payment costs; monthly invoice summarizes prior-month fees/adjustments; Marketplace Payments accounting includes pending and credited/debited account changes, including liable and account-holder accounts (39–40,48). Adyen says fees are deducted from settlement and debited to the platform liable account (54–56), with example fee locations in 66–77; FanF and Merchant Location Fee are US-only in this snapshot (69,75). Marketpay Payout Report is scheduled-payout-only (50). A catalog or report label proves neither a specific bank receipt nor complete reconciliation.
