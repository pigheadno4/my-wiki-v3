## Q1. Which bank accounts do transfer instruments represent, and how does this guide create and manage them?

A transfer instrument represents an **external bank account** for moving funds into or out of the marketplace (raw line 19). User-owned bank instruments belong to account holders; marketplace company payout accounts connect to its liable account for settlement to the company (raw lines 21–26). The Legal Entity Management API creates a `bankAccount` instrument with the owning/contracting legal entity ID and location-specific bank details (raw lines 48–58); API operations are POST/GET/PATCH/DELETE (raw lines 38–46), while Customer Area management here is only for company payout accounts (raw lines 989–1006). ## Q2. What limits payout use, and what happens on update or deletion?

The TEST create response illustrates a requested-but-**not allowed** `sendToTransferInstrument` capability with verification pending (raw lines 344–372), and create/update instructions say to inspect `problems` for bank-verification issues (raw lines 373, 835). PATCH retains the instrument ID for existing schedule/transfer references (raw lines 816–837), but the page does not show a successful payout. DELETE is irreversible; future scheduled or on-demand payouts still targeting the deleted ID fail (raw lines 839–987). For company payout-account rotation, add and verify a replacement, update payout references, then remove the old instrument (raw lines 1006–1018). Neither `valid` status nor an API response proves bank receipt.
