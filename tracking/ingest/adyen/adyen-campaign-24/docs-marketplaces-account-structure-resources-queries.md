# C24 worker retrieval checks: docs-marketplaces-account-structure-resources

Q1. How are Marketplace payment-processing accounts and balance-platform resources connected?
Answer: The marketplace has a company account and merchant accounts, with merchant accounts processing users' payments (raw lines 21–41). They connect to a balance platform that accounts for fund flows and can split sale proceeds between marketplace and user (lines 45–56). Depending on business model and use case, user resources to create include an account holder carrying capabilities and linked to the user's main legal entity and balance account; that balance account holds user funds (lines 64–69). The marketplace has separate liable account-holder/balance-account resources (lines 71–77). Q2. Which regional and liability boundaries prevent a wrong structure or money-flow claim?
Answer: The default is a separate balance platform per acquiring region (raw line 47), but a global marketplace with multiregional sellers transferring to each other must use one across regions and cannot offer additional financial products (lines 59–62). User balance accounts hold user funds; the marketplace liable balance account holds its own funds and may be debited for user negative balances caused by refunds or chargebacks (lines 68–75). These are possible flows, not proof of a completed split, payout or bank receipt.
