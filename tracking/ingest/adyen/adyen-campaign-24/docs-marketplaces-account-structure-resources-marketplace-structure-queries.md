Q1. Which marketplace and user resources does the structure example distinguish, and what path does its NL payment follow?

The example has an Online Marketplace company account, three merchant accounts, EU/US balance platforms, a liable account holder/balance account, and legal entities by acquiring region (raw lines 30–51). Each user has account holder(s) by operating country, balance account(s) with base currency, a verified-bank-account transfer instrument per balance account for payout, and legal entities by operating country (lines 53–61). Its NL payment is processed through `OnlineMarketplace_NL`, settled in the `OnlineMarketplace_EU` balance platform's accounts, kept in a user balance account, and paid to the account holder's transfer instrument (lines 69–76). This is an illustrative sequence, not proof of a real payout or bank receipt. Q2. When does the page require a single balance platform and prohibit additional financial products, and does its two-platform example negate that condition?

The special rule applies to a **global marketplace with multiregional sellers who can transfer funds to each other**: one balance platform for all acquiring regions, and no additional financial products to users (raw lines 25–28). The later EU/US example has two balance platforms but does not say its sellers make those cross-region transfers (lines 30, 44–47), so it does not negate the conditional rule. The page also states that a registered acquiring region needs a balance platform and merchant account (line 23). This dated guide does not establish a particular marketplace's regulatory status or live product eligibility.
