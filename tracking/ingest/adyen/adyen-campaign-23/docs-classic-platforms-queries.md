# C23 worker retrieval checks — docs-classic-platforms

## Q1: What is Classic Platforms for, and which integration route does the page recommend?

The page is specifically for **Classic Adyen for Platforms**; it sends newly starting implementations to a separate new guide (raw line 19). The Classic platform onboards sellers/service providers/contractors as sub-merchants, accepts buyer payments on their behalf, can split funds and costs, move balances between platform/sub-merchant accounts, and pay out to sub-merchants (lines 21–35, 112–140). The page describes Adyen-run or combined verification and staggered/upfront approaches (lines 96–110). ## Q2: What limits this page as current availability or financial-outcome evidence?

Its country list is a 2023-modified, 2026-09-15-captured snapshot, and POS processing has an additional supported-country condition (raw lines 41–94). Its immediate-acceptance, splitting, holding, transfer and payout descriptions are overview capabilities, not proof of a particular sub-merchant's verification, live eligibility, successful payment, executed split or bank receipt (lines 96–140). The Classic route must not be treated as interchangeable with the separately linked new integration (line 19).
