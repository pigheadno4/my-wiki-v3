Q1. What Classic Platforms identity and organization data does this reference tell a platform to collect for verification?
Answer: It rejects PO boxes and asks individuals' names to match photo ID when one is needed (raw lines 23–28). For organizations, collect all owners at or above the 25% threshold, a controller when no such owner exists (at least one controller), and at least one/all signatories as applicable (raw lines 30–38). Country-specific national ID and organization registration examples are at raw lines 40–111; higher-tier business legal entities must supply a company TIN (raw lines 113–132). Q2. What does the page require for a payout bank account, and do submitted formats or examples prove verification or payout?
Answer: Adyen says it checks existence and ownership for every bank account added as a payout method; owner name and bank country should match the account holder and operating country (raw lines 140–147). Where IBAN is available, submit IBAN or bank/branch/account codes; branch code may be required and US/UK rows are examples (raw lines 149–163). These format instructions do not show a verified account or completed payout. The page is explicitly for Classic integrations and redirects new implementations elsewhere (raw line 19).
