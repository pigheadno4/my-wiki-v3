# C23 worker retrieval checks: docs-classic-platforms-identify-account-holder-risk-review-and-manage

Q1. What is this Classic Score guide for, and who can view or act on identities?
Answer: It is expressly Classic Platforms, with Score enabled through an Adyen Account Manager and Customer Area roles assigned (raw lines 19–23). Merchant MarketPlace views case management/risk signals; Merchant Marketplace Risk takes actions; Risk Admin configures settings and can trust identities, exclude linking KYC identifiers and bulk-edit high-risk lists (lines 25–29). The dashboard groups identities/account holders and orders by score (lines 31–43). Q2. Which actions have prerequisites, irreversible effects or narrower automation scope?
Answer: Manual refund is possible only after suspension, and high-risk-list KYC values should be confirmed fraudulent (raw lines 63–69). Dismissing all identity signals resets its score (line 83). Excluding a linked KYC value is permanent, cannot be undone and needs confirmation (lines 85–93). The automated options are only No action, Bump KYC tier or Disable payouts (lines 146–162); manual refund/suspension is not listed there. These controls and example risk signals do not prove any actual action or financial outcome.
