Q1. From whose account can a Classic manual payout be initiated, and through which channels?
Answer: It uses the positive balance on a Classic account, less negative pending balance, for off-schedule or partial payouts (raw lines 19–23). Customer Area can initiate bank payouts with the all-account-holder payout role and a positive selected account balance (lines 29–50). Classic Fund API `POST /payoutAccountHolder` can target the account holder's bank account or payout-eligible card (lines 54–73).
