# C23 Classic Platforms verification-process retrieval checks

## Q1 — What must happen before a Classic account holder can receive payouts?

The Classic page says the account holder must pass verification; additional information is generally requested after a first payment or tier advance (raw lines 19–34). Adyen's required checks depend on legal-entity type, operating country, tier and applicable legal arrangements, with organization owner/controller/signatory criteria in the captured table (raw lines 36–55). Verification success may enable payouts; failure does not (raw line 34). These are dated Classic conditions, not current account-specific eligibility proof. ## Q2 — What are the deadline, refund and status limits?

Missing information changes status to `Inactive` after 30 days, disabling payouts, then to `Suspended` after another 42 days; the page says payments not yet paid out are refunded on suspension (raw lines 57–68). If the holder cannot meet an inactivation or suspension deadline for valid reasons, the guide allows a one-week extension once in each period (raw lines 87–93). The worked March 5 → April 5 → May 18 dates are one day later at each transition than ordinary addition of the stated 30 and 42 days; use the upcoming-deadline webhook's `executionDate` for the actual notified date (raw lines 61–64, 70–81). Unsuspension is allowed only when no verification check is `FAILED`; for deadline suspension it returns to `Inactive` with a reset 42-day clock (raw lines 104–108). The `Active` status row has both payout check and X and says payouts may still be disabled for missing information; `Closed` is final (raw lines 140–145). Neither an API `pspReference` nor a status label alone proves verification approval or bank receipt (raw lines 121–145).
