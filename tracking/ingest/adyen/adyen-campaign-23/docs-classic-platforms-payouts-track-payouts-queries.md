Q1. How does a Classic Platforms operator track payout initiation and confirmation, and what does Confirmed establish?
Answer: ACCOUNT_HOLDER_PAYOUT Initiated marks initiation; notification version 5 can include content.estimatedArrivalDate, and Customer Area payout History is an alternate view (raw lines 25–40). Confirmed is emitted after debit from the platform account (raw lines 42–59), not proof of receiving-bank credit. The guide gives general 1–2-business-day confirmation and possible additional 3-business-day bank arrival timing (raw line 21). Q2. Can a Confirmed payout later fail, and what distinguishes scheduled from manual return handling?
Answer: Yes. A later bank return triggers Failed after Confirmed; Customer Area can show Payout reversed, and payable-balance rebooking after Adyen receives funds may take around 3–6 business days (raw lines 77–124). A returned scheduled payout blocks further payouts to that bank account, while retrying a manual payout with unchanged details likely returns again; verify/correct bank details before manual reissue or scheduled payout-state unblocking (raw lines 126–152, 199–215). A new instruction does not prove bank receipt.
