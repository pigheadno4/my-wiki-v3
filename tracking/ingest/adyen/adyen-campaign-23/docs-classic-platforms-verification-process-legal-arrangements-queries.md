Q1. When must a Classic Platforms account holder provide legal-arrangement information, and how does a custom onboarding flow submit it?
Answer: The guide covers account holders acting for another entity under a contractual agreement (raw lines 19–21). Hosted onboarding collects requirements by default; a custom UI must collect the arrangement data and use Classic Account API version 6, updating an existing account holder via /updateAccountHolder and accountHolderDetails.legalArrangements (raw lines 28–33, 130–147). The captured support list limits arrangements by country and type (raw lines 51–60). Q2. Does an update/upload response prove verification, and which material document or country conflicts need care?
Answer: No. /updateAccountHolder and /uploadDocument are asynchronous; notification webhooks carry final update/check status (raw lines 529–534, 599–601). Documents are required for an Australian trust or if Adyen requests one after automatic verification failure; text requires the arrangement code, but its upload sample omits it (raw lines 566–597). Association US-only support conflicts with a cross-country collection sentence (raw lines 55, 60, 72); Singapore-only sole-proprietorship support conflicts with the AU sample address (raw lines 57, 343–383). Failure to declare can block payouts or suspend accounts (raw line 33).
