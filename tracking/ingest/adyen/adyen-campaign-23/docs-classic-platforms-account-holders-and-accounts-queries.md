## Q1: How are Classic sub-merchant account holders and accounts created, and what information is needed?

The Classic page first chooses a sub-merchant legal-entity type and collects the **2021-modified creation guide's** type-specific minimum information, then calls `/createAccountHolder` with a unique `accountHolderCode` (raw lines 19–29, 33–93). Creating the holder also creates an account with `accountCode` for transactions; optional extra accounts use `/createAccount` and the existing holder code (lines 396–411). The samples use Classic Account API v6 **test** URLs (lines 95–98, 415–421). A separate [[raw/adyen/docs/classic-platforms/verification-process/requirements-update/2026-09-15|Classic requirements update]] applies additional rules to all new sub-merchants from 1 August 2022, including EEA owner/controller versus non-EEA controller information and at least one identified signatory (related raw lines 21–27, 43–45, 144–146). The older creation minimum alone does not establish that later checks pass. ## Q2: What do responses and Active status establish, and what still gates payouts?

Both holder and extra-account creation are asynchronous; HTTP 200/202 responses are not final creation proof. Wait for `ACCOUNT_HOLDER_CREATED` and `ACCOUNT_CREATED` (holder flow) or `ACCOUNT_CREATED` (extra account flow) before business logic (raw lines 396–401, 424–429). The page says a created, Active holder may process payments, but payouts require additional information including bank details and passed verification (lines 21–23, 403–405). The optional-account HTTP 202 sentence calls the result holder creation even though that step creates an account (lines 407–411, 429), so do not infer a second holder flow.
