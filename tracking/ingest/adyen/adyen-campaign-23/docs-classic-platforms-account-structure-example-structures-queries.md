Q1. How does the Classic example place company, merchant and platform accounts, and where do the illustrated trip-payment funds go?
Answer: Fictional Example Travel has one Adyen company account, NL/US merchant accounts, a platform with business/individual account holders and a platform liable pair (raw lines 21–37). The Dutch trip payment is processed through the NL merchant account; the example splits settlement between the liable account and the individual holder's Account 2, holds that holder's funds there, then describes payout to the holder's verified bank account (raw lines 41–47). These are illustrative money-flow steps, not evidence of a completed transaction. Q2. When an account holder has multiple platform accounts and bank accounts, what must a payout instruction identify?
Answer: The platform selects the source platform account and verified destination bank account for a many-to-many arrangement; the illustration uses Account 2 to Bank account 1 (raw lines 60–71). In one-to-many or many-to-one relationships it specifies the source account or destination bank account as needed (raw line 79). The page does not prove a particular bank account is verified or a payout arrived.
