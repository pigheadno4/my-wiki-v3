# Q1 — Purpose, scope, and inference limit

[[source-adyen-docs-payment-methods-payme-android-component]] is Adyen's 2026-09-15 PayMe Android Redirect Component add-on for an existing Components integration, supported from version 4.0.0 (pinned raw raw/adyen/docs/payment-methods/payme/android-component/2026-09-15.md:15–31). Its sample `RedirectShopper` and `Authorised` responses plus sandbox result fixtures do not establish a real successful payment, capture, settlement, or live availability (raw/adyen/docs/payment-methods/payme/android-component/2026-09-15.md:95–150, 154–164).
