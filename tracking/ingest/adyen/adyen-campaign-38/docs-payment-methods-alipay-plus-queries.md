# Q1 — Purpose, scope, and inference limit

[[source-adyen-docs-payment-methods-alipay-plus]] is Adyen's 2026-09-15 Alipay+ network overview: a cross-border aggregation of 11 local wallets with online browser and mobile-app flows (pinned raw raw/adyen/docs/payment-methods/alipay-plus/2026-09-15.md:23–27, 33–47). Its redirect/country/currency/capability row is overview-level; it does not prove each wallet has identical merchant eligibility, current support, accepted transactions, authorization, capture or settlement (raw/adyen/docs/payment-methods/alipay-plus/2026-09-15.md:29–31, 49–53). # Q2 — Central capability, prerequisites, warnings

The page describes Alipay+ as a “wallet of wallets” and its table marks Wallets/redirect/International/Multiple, checkmarks for refunds including partial and multiple partial refunds, and x marks for recurring, separate/partial/multiple partial captures, and chargebacks (raw/adyen/docs/payment-methods/alipay-plus/2026-09-15.md:23–31). Those marks are historical overview labels; the table does not say ordinary captures are unavailable. Each wallet reviews onboarding against its own risk requirements, with an *expected* one-month SLA for all wallets; TrueMoney additionally needs a form completed by Adyen Support and sent to Alipay+ on the merchant's behalf (raw/adyen/docs/payment-methods/alipay-plus/2026-09-15.md:49–53). Web, iOS, Android and Pay by Link are navigation routes only (raw/adyen/docs/payment-methods/alipay-plus/2026-09-15.md:55–79).
