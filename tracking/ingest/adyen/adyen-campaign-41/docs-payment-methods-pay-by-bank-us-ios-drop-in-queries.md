# Pay by Bank (US) iOS Drop-in — Q1/Q2

## Q1 — Purpose and scope; what cannot be inferred?

This is the 2026-09-15 capture of an **add-on to an existing iOS Drop-in** for Pay by Bank (US), with separate Sessions and Advanced prerequisites, each requiring v5.70.0 or later and Support setup (raw `raw/adyen/docs/payment-methods/pay-by-bank-us/ios-drop-in/2026-09-15.md:15-37`). It says Drop-in already handles redirect and payment outcome, with no method-specific configuration (same raw `:59-69`). It does **not** prove current merchant eligibility, Plaid registration, live availability, successful capture or settlement: live acceptance is gated on completed registration and signalled by `paymentMethod.created` (same raw `:142-146`), and ACH capture can fail despite immediate capture (same raw `:71-77`). ## Q2 — Central capability, prerequisites and warnings

The guide adds Pay by Bank (US) to iOS Drop-in Sessions or Advanced v5.70.0+, after Support adds the method (same raw `:21-37`). Its `/sessions` table marks `countryCode` required while the description defaults omitted values to US; `/payments` marks it optional but recommends sending it for possible future non-US bank support, while current support is US only (same raw `:39-57`). Immediate capture is strongly recommended because Plaid checks at authorization do not hold funds, and asynchronous ACH capture may still find insufficient balance (same raw `:71-77`). Recurring tokenization is supported, with strong advice for explicit permission and transparent schedule/amount; operational token details are delegated to the general guide (same raw `:79-89`). TEST chargebacks first report `Authorised`, then begin after capture and settlement; live acceptance waits for completed Plaid registration and the readiness webhook (same raw `:121-146`).
