## Overview

This 2026-09-15 Adyen page is a Pay by Bank (US) Android integration chooser. It links method-specific Drop-in and Redirect Component child guides and general Android Drop-in/Components Sessions-flow guides. It has no method setup, request, Plaid redirect, ACH capture, tokenization or result procedure of its own. See [[adyen]] and [[adyen-online-payments]].

## Key takeaways

- The Drop-in option is described as an all-in-one Android UI and links [[source-adyen-docs-payment-methods-pay-by-bank-us-android-drop-in]]. The Components option describes a Redirect Component and links [[source-adyen-docs-payment-methods-pay-by-bank-us-android-component]]. Those labels are routes; child guides own version, action and server-side details.
- The page separately links general Android Drop-in and Components guides, both with Sessions-flow URLs. It does not establish that Sessions is the only supported flow, nor does it state SDK/API minimums, merchant eligibility, country/bank support, funding outcome or a completed payment. Consult the selected child guide and [[source-adyen-docs-payment-methods-pay-by-bank-us]] for method-level ACH qualifications.
