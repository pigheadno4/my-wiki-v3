# Fixed locator answers — docs-point-of-sale-managing-terminals-battery-management

**Q1: What does this battery guide cover, and what should a reader not infer?** It is Adyen's 2026-09-15 snapshot for battery-powered terminals used with Terminal API or standalone, addressing version-qualified sleep, monitoring, charging, network/app effects and storage. It explicitly declines to give precise runtime because battery age, configuration, traffic, connectivity and environment vary. It does not certify a device or battery, prove current model availability/charge, or prove payment success. See opening lines 17–36, “Requirements” (38–49), and “Sleep mode” (51–72). **Q2: Which charge, model, safety, and storage conditions matter?** The snapshot says Engage terminals stop transactions below 5%, while Android has no Adyen-enforced equivalent; it recommends a 20% warning, charging near 15%, and using the supplied charger. e285/e280 without a supplied charger are listed at 5V DC, 1A. Spare batteries are listed for V400m, e285 and Android terminals; failure routes to return or replacement. Store at −20°C to 60°C, 5%–95% non-condensing humidity, and 40%–70% charge, checked at least every six months. The page does not give faulty-battery disposal, packing, or carrier instructions, so its return link must not be taken as shipping approval. See “Monitor the battery charge level” (74–151), “Battery replacement” (153–159), “Battery charging” (161–170), and “Storage conditions” (196–202).
