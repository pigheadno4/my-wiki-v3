## 1. What should staff do to bring a new S1E4Pro into service safely?

**Purpose and scope:** The 2026-09-15 S1E4Pro manual covers physical first-use setup, not a live inventory or completed payment record.

**Capability:** Inspect the shipment and terminal, power it with at least four hours of charging during setup, choose a language, connect to a network, and confirm the correct store on the device; edit a wrong store. The page describes Wi-Fi and cellular failover. If the store screen is missing, it says to wait for the next Adyen synchronization (on average three hours) and retry.

**Prerequisites and warnings:** Compare serial numbers and tamper tape, then contact Adyen Support immediately if checks fail. Later Wi-Fi changes need a passcode. The manual warns that terminals boarded before 2022 may lack an active SIM subscription, and a first/long-idle maintenance call may take 10–20 minutes. These steps do not prove that any specific device is boarded or that a payment succeeded.

## 2. What access and operational risks do the S1E4Pro Apps, kiosk, airplane and support menus carry?

**Purpose and scope:** These are device-menu behaviors in a dated S1E4Pro manual. They do not by themselves establish current feature enablement, Android OS version or standalone eligibility.

**Capability:** The Apps menu shows installed apps/certificates, and opening it carries out a scheduled app install or removal. It requires Admin & Settings passcode, as do Network and several other settings. Kiosk mode hides navigation and Settings/Transactions buttons for unattended use. Airplane mode is a Support-enabled airline feature that turns off Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and cellular connections; the page says turning it off restores the previous connections. A remote troubleshooting session begins only after staff select Start session and enter a passcode, after which support can see and interact with the screen.

**Prerequisites and warnings:** The manual routes kiosk enablement elsewhere; it does not claim kiosk mode is a comprehensive tamper or security control. Airplane mode interrupts all listed network paths. Use the Adyen-supplied USB-C cable, avoid socket damage, and never put a faulty battery in a terminal return package. A Totals reset begins a new reporting period, with the previous report still accessible.
