# Fixed retrieval answers: docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-s1e-user-manual

1. **What should staff check before using an S1E or S1E Barcode terminal?** The 2026-09-15 manual says to inspect the security tape and shipment, compare the serial numbers on the box and device (and the Customer Area order when accessible), and check for tampering such as missing screws, added wires or attachments. If a check fails, contact Adyen Support immediately. This is a physical security check for the payment terminal, not a certification of a particular device. Route: [[adyen-android-terminals]]. Evidence: [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-s1e-user-manual]]; [[raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/user-manuals/s1e-user-manual/2026-09-15|raw § Inspect the terminal]].

2. **Does the S1E manual guarantee cellular failover and how are pending apps applied?** The dated manual describes S1E/S1E Barcode cellular 3G/4G failover, but says a SIM may be inactive on terminals boarded before 2022 without a SIM subscription at the time; it does not establish present carrier coverage or this device's SIM state. Its Settings > Apps menu lists installed apps/certificates and carries out a scheduled install/removal when opened; that menu needs the Admin & Settings passcode. Route: [[adyen-android-terminals]]. Evidence: [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-s1e-user-manual]]; [[raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/user-manuals/s1e-user-manual/2026-09-15|raw §§ Cellular 3G/4G failover, Payment terminal menus]].
