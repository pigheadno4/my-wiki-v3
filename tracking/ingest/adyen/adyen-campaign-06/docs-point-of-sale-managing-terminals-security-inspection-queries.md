1. **What should store staff check when a terminal arrives, and what if a check fails?** The 2026-09-15 Adyen inspection guide says to check unopened packaging and intact tamper tape; where Customer Area access exists, match the box serial to the order and confirm carrier delivery; then match the device serial to the box and inspect for missing screws, extra wires or labels, holes, or attachments. If any check fails, contact Adyen Support immediately. If integrity is in doubt, stop using the terminal and contact Support. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]] and the pinned raw's `Delivery inspection` heading. Completing a checklist does not certify a particular unit safe or PCI compliant.

2. **How often should a deployed terminal be inspected, and what happens for a tamper warning or missing device?** Adyen recommends regular inspection every week. Check for **TAMPER** or **TAMPERED** (including localized variants), unexpected attachments around the card reader, ports, display or keypad, physical alterations, suspicious camera footage, and PIN visibility; a tamper display requires immediate stop-use and Support contact. For loss or theft, report model/serial, where and how it went missing, how it was discovered, active-use state, preventive steps, and the requested police-report details if theft is suspected; copy terminals@adyen.com in the support form. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]] and raw headings `Regular inspection`, `Awareness of suspicious activity`, and `Lost or stolen terminal`. The snapshot does not establish current contact details or a resolved security incident.
