# Fixed retrieval answers: docs-point-of-sale-android-terminals-accessibility-mode

1. **What are the two activation routes for audio accessibility on Adyen Android payment terminals?** The overview says Adyen must first enable accessibility mode for the terminals. A merchant POS app can put a Base64-encoded `Operation` object in a payment request, or someone can activate it on the terminal using headphones, the on-screen accessibility button, or a physical keypad's 5 key where present. The overview routes to detailed guides for model and method conditions; it does not establish a given terminal's eligibility. Route: [[adyen-android-terminals]]. Evidence: [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-android-terminals-accessibility-mode]]; [[raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/android-terminals/accessibility-mode/2026-09-15|raw § Methods to turn on accessibility mode]].

2. **Does accessible mode stay on after an accessible payment?** The overview says the terminal switches back to regular mode automatically after a successful or canceled accessible payment. It describes mode lifecycle, not a guarantee that a transaction will succeed or that the merchant meets a location-specific legal requirement. The detailed terminal-side guide owns model/software gates and limited-feature conditions. Route: [[adyen-android-terminals]].
