# Fixed locator answers — docs-point-of-sale-managing-terminals-order-terminals

**Q1: How does the Customer Area ordering route work, and what does it not prove?** The 2026-09-15 Adyen guide separates test-only terminals from live Customer Area orders. A user with a qualifying sales-order role selects the merchant account and use country/region, edits a terminal/accessory cart, enters shipping details and an optional PO number, reviews, then places the order; the delivered device still needs boarding. The page's regional modem limit means a terminal ordered for one region will not work in another merely with a new cord. This snapshot does not prove current device availability, account eligibility, dispatch timing, boarding, or payment success. See raw “Requirements” (27–57), “Regional compatibility” (59–63), and “Submit a sales order” (65–101). **Q2: What are the return, replacement, fee, cancellation, and status conditions?** A faulty-device return differs from a TRS replacement that pairs return and new order. TRS needs signup and covered defect for the stated replacement benefit; the guide describes examination/repair charges, inventory billing, and a replacement-device charge if the old device is not returned within two weeks. Accessories cannot be returned. A placed order cannot be changed and can only be cancelled before processing while `Placed`; updates appear in Customer Area/email and optionally Terminal order update webhooks. Adyen recommends `Shipped` rather than delayed carrier `Delivered` for direct-to-store assignment, but a logistics event does not prove boarding or transaction readiness. See “Returning or replacing terminals” (103–114), “Terminal Replacement Service (TRS)” (116–150), “Cancel an order” (284–294), and “Terminal order updates” (308–327).
