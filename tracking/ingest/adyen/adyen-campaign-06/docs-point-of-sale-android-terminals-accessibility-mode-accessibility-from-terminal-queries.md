1. **Can a shopper turn on audio accessibility from an Adyen Android payment terminal at the PIN screen?** The pinned 2026-09-15 guide says headphone connection and holding keypad 5 cannot activate the mode while the PIN screen is displayed, and the on-screen accessibility button is absent there. Supported activation methods vary by model; P630 is keypad-only. Adyen Support must configure the mode, and the specified model/software thresholds apply. Route: [[adyen-android-terminals]]. 2. **What happens if the shopper unplugs headphones during an accessible terminal transaction?** The guide says audio guidance continues through the terminal speakers. The mode turns off automatically after the accessible payment completes or is canceled. It is incompatible with tipping, Giving, signature cardholder verification, and the four listed languages, so availability must be checked for the actual transaction. These audio and completion descriptions do not establish a successful payment result. Route: [[adyen-android-terminals]].
