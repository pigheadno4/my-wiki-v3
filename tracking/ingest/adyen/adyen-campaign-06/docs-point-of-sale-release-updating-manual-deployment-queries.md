1. **Can I schedule any terminal software version for a fleet, and what must be selected?** In the 2026-09-15 Customer Area guide, manual scheduling applies to terminals on the manual update strategy and only Long Term Support (LTS) versions. A user with the Merchant POS Terminal Management Admin role selects terminal ranges and models, an LTS version, an optional pilot of up to 20 devices, optimized or manual batches, and dates before submitting for Adyen review. All ranges are selected by default; manual list selection supports up to 1,000 comma-separated IDs per selection. Adyen recommends seven days between batches and says it needs two business days for approval; updates occur at the restart hour, 6:00 AM by default. See [[adyen-terminal-management]] and raw headings `Requirements`, `1. Select the software version` through `4. Select the date`. These are snapshot workflow statements, not a current release catalog or approval guarantee.

2. **Does changing the default software version update existing terminals, and how do I tell if a scheduled rollout succeeded?** No. The guide says a model's default version applies at boarding for new terminals, does not schedule updates to existing ones, and yields to merchant/store-level version settings. In Customer Area **Devices > Terminal software > Updates**, plan validation states such as To be reviewed or Approved are separate from each terminal's Successful, Pending, Failed, or Cancelled update status. Pending means scheduled but not yet started, most likely because the terminal is off or offline. The guide offers cancellation of pending updates and rescheduling of failed ones. See [[adyen-terminal-management]] and raw headings `Change the default software version`, `Monitor the status of your update plan`, and `Update your plan`. A submitted or approved plan does not prove a device installed the update.
