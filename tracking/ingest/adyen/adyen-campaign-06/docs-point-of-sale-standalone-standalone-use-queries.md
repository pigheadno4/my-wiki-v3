**Q1: How does standalone use differ from a POS integration, and what is the evidence limit?** After standalone setup, the operator enters the amount directly on the terminal and hands it to the shopper; variants include card/NFC, QR wallet, MKE and MOTO. This guide is for terminal-operated transactions, with older non-Android menu variants, not POS-app Terminal API requests. Its 2026-09-15 snapshot does not prove current account or method enablement, terminal eligibility, a particular authorization, or settlement. **Q2: What are the cancel, refund, passcode, and cashout boundaries?** A Cancel press is an attempt only while processing; if it fails and the payment was approved, use the refund path. Referenced refunds select the original transaction in History and use a Transactions passcode; unreferenced Refund/MOTO refund begins a new transaction and may also ask for the six-digit Refunds passcode. Support enablement is stated for tipping, referenced refunds, stored value and MOTO. The page excludes purchase-free card-account cashout on standalone terminals, while separately permitting full-only gift-card cashout on non-Android standalone terminals. Totals reset starts a new report period.
