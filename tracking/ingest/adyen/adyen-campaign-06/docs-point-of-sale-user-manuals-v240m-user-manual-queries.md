**Q1: What does the V240m Plus manual establish, and what should a reader not infer?** This 2026-09-15 model-specific snapshot describes checking the shipment for tampering, charging and powering the device, configuring Wi-Fi, boarding it to a selected store, and running PSP plus conditional POS connection diagnostics. A `Successful` diagnostic reflects a point-in-time connection check. It does not prove current model availability, continuing connectivity, account/device eligibility, successful payment, or a setup requirement for every Adyen terminal. See raw “Get started” (42–62), “Inspect the terminal” (64–79), and the two connection-test headings (122–140). **Q2: Which model-specific limits and operational warnings matter?** The V240m Plus does not support 4G or later, despite the manual's cellular-failover description; 2G/3G sunset and possible inactive SIMs on pre-2022-boarded devices make current cellular service uncertain. Failed tamper or identity checks call for immediate Support contact. Charge during setup, observe battery storage guidance, and never include a faulty battery in a terminal return because of leakage/thermal risk. Network, Software, Power and Configuration menus require the listed passcode; Totals reset starts a new period. Standalone mode initiates transactions on the terminal and skips the POS connection test.
