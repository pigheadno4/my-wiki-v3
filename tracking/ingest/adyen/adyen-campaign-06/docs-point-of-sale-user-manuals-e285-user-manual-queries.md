# C06 fixed-query answers: docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-e285-user-manual

## 1. Purpose, scope, and what not to infer

**Answer:** This pinned 2026-09-15 Adyen user manual covers the e285p and standard e285 physical payment terminals: inspection, first setup and store boarding, Wi-Fi/cellular connectivity, connection tests, power and battery care, menus, device identification, and troubleshooting. It labels both discontinued for new orders, states e285p repairs through April 2029 and no e285 repairs, and says the standard e285 supports software only through 1.86. Those are dated manual claims, not current inventory, support eligibility, carrier coverage, account enablement, device integrity, or payment-success evidence. Start at [[adyen-payment-terminals]] once the coordinator promotes that concept; then read [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-e285-user-manual]] and its [[raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/user-manuals/e285-user-manual/2026-09-15|pinned raw]]. The provider catalog entry is pending coordinator promotion.

## 2. Central setup, prerequisites, warnings, and retrieval route

**Answer:** The manual's setup path is inspect the delivered device and serials, charge at least four hours, configure network, select the assigned store while boarding, then test Adyen-platform and POS-system connectivity separately. It estimates boarding at up to 30 minutes and advises waiting for the next synchronization, on average three hours, if the boarding option is missing. A failed packaging, serial or tamper check calls for immediate Adyen Support contact. The models lack 4G; the manual warns about 2G/3G shutdowns, and pre-2022-boarded terminals may have unactivated SIM subscriptions. Storage guidance keeps batteries at 40–70% and checks leakage/swelling every six months; a faulty battery is excluded from the terminal return package and disposed of under local rules. Menu access is passcode-gated for configuration and some transactions. Its generic menu statement for software 1.90+ should not be applied to the standard e285, whose stated ceiling is 1.86. Use [[adyen-payment-terminals]] for hardware/model lookup, [[adyen-terminal-management]] for store assignment and boarding context, then the source and raw section locators for exact steps. The snapshot does not prove a safe device, successful boarding or processing, current regional connectivity, or permission to use a listed standalone action.
