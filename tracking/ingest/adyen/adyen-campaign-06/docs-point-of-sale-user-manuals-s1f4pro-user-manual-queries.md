1. **What must an operator check before using a newly delivered S1F4Pro, and what if the store confirmation screen is absent?** The 2026-09-15 manual says to inspect intact tamper tape, match serial numbers on the box, terminal, and Customer Area order when available, confirm carrier delivery, and inspect for physical alterations. If a check fails, contact Adyen Support immediately. After inserting paper, powering and charging the device, choosing a language, and connecting to a network, confirm or correct the shown store. If that screen does not appear, wait for the next platform synchronization (the manual says an average of three hours) and try again. See [[adyen-android-terminals]] and the pinned raw headings `Get started` and `Inspect the terminal`; this does not prove that a particular device is safely boarded or ready to accept payments.

2. **Can S1F4Pro use its scanner button or cellular network, and what applies to a faulty battery?** The manual says the barcode-scanner button is nonfunctional. Camera scanning depends on an organization's implementation in the on-terminal Android app. It describes automatic cellular 3G/4G failover and a preinstalled eSIM, with an exception for terminals boarded before 2022 that lacked a SIM subscription; contact Support if activation is uncertain. For a return with a faulty battery, dispose of the battery under local rules and do not include it in the return package. See [[adyen-android-terminals]] and pinned raw headings `Supplied components`, `Cellular 3G/4G failover`, and `Maintain battery performance`. The dated manual does not establish current regional connectivity, device eligibility, or a successful transaction.
