# Fixed locator answers — docs-point-of-sale-android-terminals-deploy-apps-schedule

**Q1: What does this Customer Area scheduling guide do, and what should not be inferred?** It schedules install or removal of uploaded Android apps and certificates on selected Adyen-provided Android payment terminals in Terminal API or standalone setups. For apps, choose a Ready app, Install/Uninstall, its version, schedule and terminal targets, then review the plan. Actions execute at a maintenance call if the terminal is on and network-connected. A scheduled plan is not proof of per-terminal installation, approval, current eligibility, or payment execution. See raw “Requirements” (21–30), “Install or uninstall apps” (32–62), and “Install or uninstall certificates” (64–81). **Q2: What are the timing, removal, and status cautions?** `Schedule now` targets the next maintenance call; a dated action targets the first maintenance call after the date/time. The terminal must be powered and connected. Opening its passcode-protected Apps menu carries out an already scheduled app install/removal immediately, so inspection can trigger the pending change. The page has a Ready gate for apps, selection filters for multiple terminals, and a separate certificate action flow. It gives no cancellation procedure or per-terminal completion receipt. See “Install or uninstall apps” (32–62), “Install or uninstall certificates” (64–81), and “Check apps on a terminal” (83–91).
