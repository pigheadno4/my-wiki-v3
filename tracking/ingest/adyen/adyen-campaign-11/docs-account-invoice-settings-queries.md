# Invoice settings retrieval checks

Q1. Which invoices and account scopes does this page cover, and what should it not be used to infer? Route through `[[adyen-account-structure]]` to `[[source-adyen-docs-account-invoice-settings]]`. It is a 2026-09-15 capture of Adyen's 2021-modified Customer Area guide for Adyen invoices on payment processing, terminal orders, and other services, configured for one account or several. Its charged-account, invoice currency, and invoice-payment settings concern Adyen invoice charges; they do not establish seller or merchant-of-record identity, shopper-payment settlement, pooled funds, payout routing, or a completed financial result. Raw locator: `raw/adyen/docs/account/invoice-settings/2026-09-15.md:5-19`, `:21-60`, `:62-90`.

Q2. How are settings changed, and what legal-entity, contact, and permission qualifications matter? Route to the same source and pinned raw `## Configure settings for one account` and `## Configure settings for multiple accounts`. A merchant account uses Reconciliation > Invoices > Settings; merchant-invoicing toggles cannot disable invoices when its legal entity differs from the company account's. General settings include invoice currency/display and month-end email; invoice email address applies when email delivery is enabled. The alternative/charged merchant account and invoice address require Support, while invoice-payment method changes require an Adyen contact. From the company account, Batch edit selects accounts/settings and then Apply changes. The source states no Customer Area user role and the email is an invoice-delivery contact, not a shopper identity field. Raw locators: `:21-60`, `:62-90`.
