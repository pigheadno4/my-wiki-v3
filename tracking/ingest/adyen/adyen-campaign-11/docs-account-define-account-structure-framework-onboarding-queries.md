Q1: What is this page for, what scope does it cover, and what must it not establish?
A: A dated Adyen guide to framework onboarding of multiple legal entities under a common holding entity. It maps the main contracting entity to a company account and processing affiliates to merchant accounts, then gives test-Customer-Area application steps. It does not identify a transaction's merchant of record, signed contract terms, regional eligibility, active account, or actual funds flow. Q2: What must applicants submit and what is the go-live boundary?
A: Obtain a test account, provide legal-structure details to Adyen sales, and complete main-contracting-entity company/ownership/contact fields plus processing-entity payment-channel/company/payout-account/ownership/contact fields. Send request submits both for Adyen review. The account only goes live after main contracting and first processing entity applications have been submitted and approved by Adyen; submission or the review notice is not approval. The guide gives no region-specific topology, and “generally” qualifies holding-entity nonprocessing.
