# Retrieval checks — manage account structure

## Q1. What is this page for, what is its scope, and what must a reader not infer?

This Adyen Customer Area guide describes requesting a new merchant account, requesting closure, creating/editing account groups, and switching between company and merchant views (`raw/adyen/docs/account/manage-account-structure/2026-09-15.md:15-24`). It was last modified 2024-02-27 and captured 2026-09-15. The **Submit** and **Request Closure** controls initiate requests; their selection does not prove Adyen approved, provisioned, or completed a particular real-account change. Nor does this interface page establish merchant of record, legal ownership, complete funds flow, or current user eligibility.

## Q2. What central capability or process does it describe, with prerequisites and warnings?

Requesting a merchant account requires both **Merchant admin** and **Merchant additional merchant accounts** user roles at company-account scope; the user sets desired account code, channel, and initial payment methods, then submits the request. The merchant name can only be changed later if the account has processed no transactions (`raw/adyen/docs/account/manage-account-structure/2026-09-15.md:26-48`). Closure requires **Merchant admin** on the target merchant account and a requested inactivation month. The page says it becomes inactive on the first day of that month, stopping new transactions and merchant-account charges; the page states that remaining report, chargeback-defense, and refund access lasts until six months **after the last transaction date**, then permanent closure ends those actions and account access. Its own examples conflict with that timing: January 1→August 1 spans seven months, while June 30→December 1 spans about five months. Do not calculate a guaranteed cutoff from the examples; confirm the specific account's access end date with Adyen (`raw/adyen/docs/account/manage-account-structure/2026-09-15.md:50-77`). Group creation or membership edits require **Manage Merchant Account Groups** plus company-account access; user access to a newly created group is a separate step (`raw/adyen/docs/account/manage-account-structure/2026-09-15.md:79-127`). Switching account views changes the scope of visible payment lists, subject to existing access (`raw/adyen/docs/account/manage-account-structure/2026-09-15.md:129-144`). These instructions describe roles, requests and documented lifecycle, not completed approvals or a universal live account state.
