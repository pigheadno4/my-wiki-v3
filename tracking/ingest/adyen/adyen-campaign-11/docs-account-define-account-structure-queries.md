Q1: What is this page for, what scope does it have, and what must it not establish?
A: It is a dated Adyen guide to choosing merchant-account structure under a company account based on legal entities, acquiring, payouts, reporting, risk, permissions, and POS operations. It recommends few merchant accounts consistent with business needs, while explaining why more may be required. It does not decide merchant of record, contractual liability, complete funds flow, live eligibility, or a payment result. Q2: What are the central setup constraints and important trade-offs?
A: At least one merchant account per payment-processing legal entity; one merchant account cannot span two legal entities. Acquiring connection is merchant-account-level and limited to one acquiring region. Merchant account must align with the legal entity owning its ultimate settlement bank account; only one bank account per settlement currency, so same-currency country/region separation needs multiple merchant accounts. Payouts and reconciliation reports are merchant-account-level; company-user access spans underlying accounts, while separate merchant accounts enable narrower access. The dated POS section recommends separate online/POS accounts and specifies local-entity/region requirements; current applicability should be checked with Adyen.
