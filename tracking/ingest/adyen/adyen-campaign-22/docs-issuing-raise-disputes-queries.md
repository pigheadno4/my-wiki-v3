# C22 docs-issuing-raise-disputes retrieval checks

Q1. Who raises Issuing disputes, for what card transactions, and what limits and prerequisites apply?

Answer: A card-issuing platform raises disputes on cardholders' behalf for **captured** Adyen-issued-card payments, through a cardholder UI/Raise disputes API or a Customer Area bulk-upload **pilot** (raw lines 19–33). The stated roles are Bank disputes webservice role and Manage issuing disputes for Customer Area viewing, with dispute and transfer webhook subscriptions (35–45). Reasons are `duplicate`, `fraud`, and `notDelivered`; the cardholder limit is 35 disputes over possession of the card (52–56, 69–71). Duplicate/fraud deadlines are 120 days after the authorised transaction; non-delivery runs 120 days after expected delivery (75–85). For suspected loss or theft, the guide directs closing the card (25–26, 49–50). Q2. What do closed, submitted, chargeback, won, and secondPresentment mean; how are status and money movement tracked?

Answer: An API-raised dispute starts `draft`; it may be edited until submitted, while a `closed` dispute cannot be edited, reopened or submitted (raw lines 96–115). Submission sends it for card-scheme review (102). `chargeback` means funds returned to the Disputes balance account; `won` means the acquirer can no longer challenge, with source-stated Mastercard 45-day and Visa 30-day periods after chargeback; `secondPresentment` means funds returned to the acquirer (116–119, 129). Follow `dispute.created`/`dispute.updated`, correlate capture `transactionId`, dispute `id` and ARN, and watch `transfer.created` for chargeback/second-chargeback mutations; its `reference` contains the ARN (98–104, 121–138). A chargeback status alone does not prove a final win. The related [[source-adyen-docs-issuing-webhook-types]] catalog differs: its raw line 79 says second-chargeback transfer `type: chargeback`, `status: secondChargeback`, and line 88 lists `secondChargeback` as a dispute-updated status; this Raise disputes raw uses `secondPresentment` and transfer `type/status: secondChargeback` (raw lines 119, 136). Keep these as conflicting snapshot mappings and verify actual webhook payloads.
