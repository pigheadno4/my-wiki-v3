# C22 query check: Issuing funds transfers

Q1. How do funds reach a card-linked balance account, and what is required for an internal transfer? This guide describes a verified external-bank transfer to Adyen's bank account followed by settlement to the linked balance account after Adyen receives the funds (raw lines 28–35). Internal push/pull transfers are among balance accounts on one balance platform; a push credits and a pull debits the target (raw lines 39–42). For an internal transfer, Adyen must add a Transfers API role to the credential and enable the source balance account; POST `/transfers` specifies the initiating and counterparty balance-account IDs and `category: internal` (raw lines 50–64). Q2. What proves completion, and what example conflict matters? The liable-account example pushes EUR 100 to a user's balance account (raw lines 69–93), but its response is `status: received` and `reason: pending` (raw lines 419–424). This guide says to track webhooks or query a balance account for completion, and notes that refusal can carry `notEnoughBalance` (raw lines 386–389, 428). Its linked [[source-adyen-docs-issuing-webhook-types-fund-transfers-webhooks|fund-transfer webhook guide]] covers bank transfers (`category: bank`, `type: bankTransfer`; linked raw lines 28–38), while this example is `category: internal`, `type: internalTransfer` (raw lines 411–424); do not infer its internal-transfer event/status sequence from the bank samples. The `referenceForBeneficiary` table allows alphanumerics, yet the sample contains hyphens (raw lines 67, 90); confirm field formatting before relying on that sample.
