## Q1 — How does the guide use the Accounting Report for balances and transfer reconciliation?

## Q2 — Which sample-column differences limit a mechanical join or money-flow conclusion?

The internal-transfer prose prescribes matching the same `Reference For Beneficiary`, but its two-sided table puts the shared user-supplied reference in outgoing `Reference` and incoming `Description`, while the incoming `Reference For Beneficiary` cell is not a shared value (raw lines 38, 40–47). The text introduces a bank-transfer flow at line 34, but the detailed rows all say `internal`/`internalTransfer` (raw lines 40–47). The rows illustrate an EUR 15 debit and credit between two balance accounts; they do not show an external-bank transfer or bank receipt.
