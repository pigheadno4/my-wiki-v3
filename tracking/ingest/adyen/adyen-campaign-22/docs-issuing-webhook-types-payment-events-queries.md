# C22 query checks — docs-issuing-webhook-types-payment-events

Q1. What does this page document, and what context is needed to interpret the events?

It is a 2020-modified Issuing guide captured 2026-09-15 for webhooks Adyen sends to a platform server about a merchant-initiated Adyen-issued-card payment. Identify its payment transfers with `category: issuedCard` and `type: payment`; `direction` is outgoing from the balance account for payment events and incoming for refunds. The page assumes an issued-card payment context; it does not itself document webhook setup or card-program eligibility. Raw lines 15–29, 31–33. Q2. What are the consequential boundary, failure and conflict cases?

`received` is a payment request, `authorised` reserves funds, `refused` can result from a transaction-rule violation, and adjustment has authorised/refused/error statuses (raw lines 31–33, 110–112, 300–302, 404–406). Scheme advice may supersede the initial authorisation outcome: `data.reason: schemeAdvice`, new `adviceAuthorised`/`adviceRefused` event, and `data.status` is the final outcome of that payment decision, distinct from capture (lines 495–505). Capture deducts funds and produces both transfer and transaction webhooks (844–849); partial-capture remainder expires (1004–1010, 1646–1648), and forced capture deducts without prior valid authorisation via synthetic authorisation (1496–1501). Refunds are incoming and not linked to original payments (23–29, 1772–1777). The multiple-partial prose says EUR while example event JSON says USD (1178–1183, 1241–1281); refund transfer IDs differ across its two examples (1788, 1895–1903). These test examples cannot establish a reconciliation ID rule or actual settlement.
