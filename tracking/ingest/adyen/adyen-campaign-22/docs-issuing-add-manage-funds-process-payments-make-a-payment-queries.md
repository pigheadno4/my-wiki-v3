# C22 retrieval probes — Issuing make a payment

Q1. Whose payments does the guide process, where does the revenue go, and what integration and capability prerequisites apply?

Answer: It covers online or in-person payments **from the platform's customers** through Adyen's payment processing platform, with revenue booked to the balance platform and usable to fund Adyen-issued cards (raw lines 17–19). Online requires Adyen online payments and a checkout UI; in-person requires Terminal API integration with terminals or a mobile solution (line 27). The guide lists Checkout, Terminal and Transfers API credentials, standard webhook acceptance, and account-holder capabilities `receivePayments`, `receiveFromPlatformPayments`, `sendToTransferInstrument` (lines 28–35). Its Requirements table says subscribe to **any of** Transfer or Transaction webhooks (lines 31–32), while its final Webhooks section says subscribe to **Transfer and Transaction** webhooks (line 179); it gives no unambiguous single minimum. This is a documented route, not proof that a particular account is eligible or funds have settled. Q2. How do online and in-person payment requests differ, and does the sample prove a successful split to a balance account?

Answer: Online uses Checkout POST `/payments` or `/sessions` with amount in minor units (raw lines 37–49), while in-person uses Terminal API `PaymentRequest` with `SaleToPOIRequest.MessageHeader`, and `PaymentTransaction.AmountsReq.RequestedAmount` as a decimal rather than minor units (lines 81–107). The in-person example headings say “split instructions” and “Payment split successful,” yet its shown request has no split instruction or allocation details. Its request is EUR 200.00 (lines 108, 131–135), while the purported response includes `posAuthAmountCurrency=USD&posAuthAmountValue=8000` (line 166); those examples are not a coherent amount/currency trace and cannot substantiate the requested payment or split. Actual payment outcome requires inspecting a real response and later events (lines 142–179). No specific receiving balance account or spendable-funds timing is established here.
