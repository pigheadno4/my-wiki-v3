## Q1: What is the before-payment component page for, and whose verification does it describe?

It is a short Issuing component index. The page says account holders need verification checks before card issuing, with Adyen checking the **account holder's legal entity**; Onboarding components let users enter and submit information to Adyen in the platform UI (raw line 19). It lists the Transfer Instrument component for bank-account add/update/delete, instant or document verification and status display (lines 21–25). ## Q2: What does the component listing establish about verification or issuance outcomes?

It establishes UI functions and navigation to more detailed Onboarding and Transfer Instrument guides (raw lines 19–30). It does not show that any legal entity or bank account passed verification, that a card was issued, or that a bank transfer succeeded. The status-display function itself is not a successful verification result.
