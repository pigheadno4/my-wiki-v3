# C22 worker retrieval checks — docs-issuing-add-manage-funds

## Q1: What is this page for, and what must be true before a cardholder can pay?

It is a 2026-09-15-captured Issuing overview of ways to add funds to a cardholder's balance account. The balance account associated with the card needs enough funds before the cardholder can use it to make a payment (raw lines 15–25, especially 19). ## Q2: Which funding routes and material conditions does the page state?

It names a bank transfer **from your verified external transfer instrument (bank account) to the cardholder's balance account** (raw line 23); an internal transfer **between balance accounts within your balance platform** (line 24); and booking acquired-payment revenue to cardholders' balance accounts **if you acquire payments with Adyen** (line 25). The page points to further guides at lines 27–39 and contains no transfer execution steps or outcome evidence.
