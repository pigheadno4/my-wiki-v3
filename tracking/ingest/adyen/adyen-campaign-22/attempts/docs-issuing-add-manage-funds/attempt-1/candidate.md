## Overview

This 2026-09-15-captured Adyen Issuing overview names ways to get funds into a cardholder's balance account. A cardholder's card-associated balance account needs enough funds before the card can be used for a payment. The page identifies funding routes; it does not provide transfer steps or confirm a particular receipt or payment outcome.

## Key takeaways

- An external bank transfer can be sent from your **verified external transfer instrument (bank account)** to the cardholder's balance account.
- An internal funds transfer moves funds between balance accounts **within your balance platform**.
- **If you acquire payments with Adyen**, revenue from those payments can be booked to cardholders' balance accounts. This is conditional on acquiring with Adyen.
