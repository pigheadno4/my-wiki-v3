## Overview

This 2022-modified, 2026-09-15-captured Issuing report index points to balance-platform reports for financial activity, balances, payment instruments and card-payment attempts. It says these reports can be generated in Customer Area. It is a navigation overview, not proof that a report has been generated or that a particular balance or payment reconciles.

## Listed reports and formats

- **Balance Platform Accounting Report** — overview of financial activities on the platform.
- **Balance Platform Balance Report** — overview of account balances across the platform.
- **BalancePlatform Payment Instrument Report** — payment instruments and their status for the current date.
- **BalancePlatform Received Payments Report** — payment attempts with platform payment instruments on a specified day.

The page says all four reports can be downloaded as **CSV**, and the Accounting Report also as **TSV**. See [[raw/adyen/docs/issuing/report-types/2026-09-15|raw lines 21–28]] for the exact report links and wording.

## Related-guide qualification

This overview still lists the Received Payments Report (raw line 26), but its [[source-adyen-docs-issuing-report-types-received-payments-report|dedicated guide]] says it **has been deprecated** and directs readers to the Accounting Report ([[raw/adyen/docs/issuing/report-types/received-payments-report/2026-09-15|related raw line 19]]). Do not treat the overview listing as evidence that the deprecated report is the preferred current reconciliation route.
