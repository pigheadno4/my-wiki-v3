## Q1

This is an **incoming Adyen** `balancePlatform.incomingTransfer.created` Payment webhook, sent when incoming funds are due to a refund or funds-transfer request. `data.paymentId` relates the event to the originating request, including its `balancePlatform.payment.created` event. Refund content includes the payment instrument to be refunded; incoming funds-transfer content includes balance-account information (raw lines 5–11, 45). Top-level `data`, `environment` (`test` or `live`) and `type` are required; `timestamp` is optional and records queue time. In `data`, `id` is the event resource ID, `reference` an Adyen-generated transfer reference, while account-holder, balance-account, counterparty and payment-instrument objects, `paymentId`, amounts and dates are optional. The `amount` and `originalAmount` parent objects are optional, but each has required nested `currency` and minor-unit `value` when supplied (raw lines 18–70). The status table gives `PendingIncomingTransfer` for this created event; `Refunded` or `IncomingTransfer` belong to the separate updated event. `valueDate` is an expected settlement date. No event-specific reversal, deletion or completion timing rule is stated here (raw lines 61–70). [[source-adyen-api-explorer-payment-webhooks-latest-post-balanceplatform-incomingtransfer-created]]

## Q2

Any HTTP `2xx` accepts **receipt of the webhook**, separate from the incoming transfer's financial state (raw lines 115–123). The test example carries `PendingIncomingTransfer`, a positive EUR amount, balance-account and counterparty IDs, and a `paymentId`; it illustrates a pending case, not completed credit or bank receipt (raw lines 72–114). The amount descriptions define positive and negative signs relative to the balance account, while `valueDate` is expected settlement only (raw lines 23–25, 42–44, 67). The companion [[source-adyen-api-explorer-payment-webhooks-latest-overview|Payment webhooks v1 overview]] explicitly marks this family deprecated and directs use of accounting webhooks; the separate Transfer/Transaction webhook families have their own contracts. This event page states no delivery order, retry, deduplication or signature guarantee, and neither a created status nor receiver acceptance proves final funds movement.
