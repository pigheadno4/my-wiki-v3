# Fixed query answers — api-explorer-managed-risk-webhooks-latest-post-balanceplatform-managedrisk-rollingreserve-updated

## Q1

The 2026-09-15 capture is an incoming Managed Risk `POST balancePlatform.managedRisk.rollingReserve.updated` webhook. Adyen sends it when it updates a rolling-reserve policy applied to an account holder's payouts because of risk level. Required `data.accountHolderId` identifies the affected account holder. Required `data.rollingReservePercentage` is the percentage of the user's daily sales volume withheld from the settlement batch; required `data.withHoldingPeriodInDays` is the number of days after which Adyen releases that withheld percentage. The `data` object, `environment` (`test` or `live`) and `type` are required; `data.balancePlatform`, `data.creationDate` and top-level `timestamp` are optional. The example's `data.id` is absent from the parameter table, so it is not a documented required identifier. `creationDate` is event-trigger time and `timestamp` queue time. No minimum/maximum percentage, policy effective-date, payout execution, deletion or reversal contract is given. Raw lines 5–22 and 24–40. ## Q2

The response section shows `200 Success`, and any `2xx` status accepts the webhook; `notificationResponse` is optional. This acknowledges receipt of a policy-update event. It does not prove a settlement-batch withholding, payout, release of withheld funds, or balance movement for a particular account holder. The page gives no receiver decision, delivery ordering, retry, deduplication, signature, policy rollback, financial failure/reversal or current enablement rule. Raw lines 5–7, 11–22 and 41–49.
