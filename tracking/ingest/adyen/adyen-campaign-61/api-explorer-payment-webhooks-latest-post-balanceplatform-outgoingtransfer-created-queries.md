# Fixed query answers — legacy outgoingTransfer.created

## Q1

The 2026-09-15 Payment webhooks `latest` page describes Adyen's incoming `balancePlatform.outgoingTransfer.created` event when funds were deducted from a balance account because of a capture or funds transfer. `data.status` is `Captured` for captures and `OutgoingTransfer` for outgoing fund transfers; `paymentId` links the original authorisation or transfer request. The endpoint page does not state a numeric family version. Outer `data`, `environment` (`test` or `live`) and `type`, plus `data.counterparty`, are required; status, payment ID, amount and balance-account identifiers are optional in its table. Where `amount` or `originalAmount` appears, currency and minor-unit value are required; negative means deduction. Optional `purposeCode` distinguishes manual payout (`payoutManual`) and sweep (`payoutSweep`), and `valueDate` is an expected settlement date. The example's outgoing transfer is a negative EUR amount and internal balance-account counterparty, not a universal route rule. See raw lines 5–13, 17–89 and 91–134 in [[source-adyen-api-explorer-payment-webhooks-latest-post-balanceplatform-outgoingtransfer-created]] ([canonical URL](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/payment-webhooks/latest/post/balancePlatform.outgoingTransfer.created)).

## Q2

This created event describes a balance-account deduction, while the receiver's any-`2xx` response accepts the webhook. Neither that acknowledgment nor the expected `valueDate` proves a later transfer result or beneficiary bank receipt. This endpoint does not define delivery ordering, retry, deduplication, signature, final failure/reversal, or a completed external payout. The separate [[source-adyen-docs-platforms-webhook-types]] catalog marks the Payment webhook family deprecated in favor of Transfer webhooks; use the Transfer webhook contract for its newer IDs and status meanings. See raw lines 7–13, 75–89 and 135–143 in [[source-adyen-api-explorer-payment-webhooks-latest-post-balanceplatform-outgoingtransfer-created]] ([canonical URL](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/payment-webhooks/latest/post/balancePlatform.outgoingTransfer.created)).
