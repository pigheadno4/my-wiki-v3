## Q1

Adyen produces `balancePlatform.managedRisk.reserveWaiver.applied` when it applies a reserve waiver to an account holder, so reserves are no longer withheld from that holder's payouts. The managed-risk webhook request requires `data`, nested `accountHolderId` and `eventDate`, and outer `environment` (`test` or `live`) and `type`. `balancePlatform`, `creationDate`, and queued `timestamp` are optional. `eventDate` records the waiver application date; `creationDate` and `timestamp` refer to trigger and queue timing respectively. The example's `data.id` is not defined by the request table. No deletion, reversal, limit, or expiry rule is stated. [[source-adyen-api-explorer-managed-risk-webhooks-latest-post-balanceplatform-managedrisk-reservewaiver-applied]] (https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/managed-risk-webhooks/latest/post/balancePlatform.managedRisk.reserveWaiver.applied), raw lines 5–21, 23–37.

## Q2

The endpoint describes a reserve-withholding change for the identified account holder; it does not report a payout execution, amount transferred, or beneficiary bank receipt. A receiving server accepts the webhook with any HTTP `2xx`; that response acknowledges the message rather than causing or proving a payout. The page supplies no delivery/order/retry/deduplication, signature, waiver-removal, or financial reversal rule. Its sample `data.id` cannot be treated as a required resource identifier because the table omits it. [[source-adyen-api-explorer-managed-risk-webhooks-latest-post-balanceplatform-managedrisk-reservewaiver-applied]] (https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/managed-risk-webhooks/latest/post/balancePlatform.managedRisk.reserveWaiver.applied), raw lines 7, 11–21, 23–46.
