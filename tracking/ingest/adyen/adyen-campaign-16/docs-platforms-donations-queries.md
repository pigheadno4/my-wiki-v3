# Fixed retrieval checks — Platforms Giving donation campaigns

## Q1. What is this page for, and who receives the donation?

The 2026-09-15 Platforms Giving overview explains how a platform can manage campaigns for its users' account holders so their shoppers can opt to donate during an online or POS purchase. The donation uses the purchase payment method but is a separate transaction. Adyen describes direct nonprofit-bank settlement of the full donation with Adyen covering fees; this page does not prove an individual donation, settlement or receipt. ## Q2. What gates and lifecycle constraints matter before a shopper sees a campaign?

Campaigns are for account holders and require a nonprofit, selected account holders, online/POS channel, at least one store per channel and donation options. A platform-created campaign can include at most 400 participating account holders; a store can be in only one active campaign. New campaigns start inactive and must be started separately; ended campaigns cannot restart. Online changes are immediate in the guide, whereas POS terminals pick them up at the next maintenance call, usually overnight. Prompts depend on shopper region, currency and payment method; available nonprofits depend on account-holder legal-entity country and industry, and unapproved nonprofits need onboarding. The dated POS matrix requires terminal software at least 1.109 generally, with some 1.110 gates, a 1.124 gate for combined round-up/fixed one-step donations, and SFO1 one-step at 1.125. Singapore fundraising is limited to local nonprofits in the captured region note. None of these guide conditions proves current eligibility, active setup or donation success.
