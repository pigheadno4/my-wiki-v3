Q2. What central capability, prerequisites and warnings does the guide describe?
A2. With Checkout API credentials, webhook handling, manual capture enabled and a method supporting separate capture (raw lines 27–45), send `splits` with POST `/payments/{paymentPspReference}/captures` using the authorization reference (lines 51–72). Capture-time instructions override payment-time instructions, though payment-time splits are recommended for methods without delayed capture (lines 25, 43, 1678). If the capture amount differs from authorization, omitting capture splits books all funds to the liable balance account; `Surcharge` and `Tip` are unsupported (lines 47–49). Split types and conditional currency/reference fields are at lines 61–72; fees, FX remainder and additional routes are at lines 1641–1672. A `received` capture response requires webhook outcome tracking; split-format validation does not validate destination accounts, and invalid/closed destinations send the full transaction and fees to the liable account (lines 593–666).
