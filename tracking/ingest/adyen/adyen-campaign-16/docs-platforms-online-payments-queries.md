Q1. What is this page for, what is its scope, and what should not be inferred?
A. This 2023-modified, 2026-09-15-captured Adyen for Platforms page introduces online payment processing on behalf of users, user-versus-liable balance allocations, split instructions and an example booking/settlement/payout flow. It does not identify merchant of record or legal fund owner for a transaction, document test/live readiness, or prove authorization, settlement, payout or bank receipt. Q2. What are its central capabilities, prerequisites, split rules and warnings?
A. The page requires an online-payments integration and checkout UI, Checkout/Transfers API credentials, standard-webhook receiving plus transfer and/or transaction webhook subscription, and specified account-holder capabilities. Without `receiveFromPlatformPayments` in valid state, the full amount books to the platform liable account rather than that user's balance account. Server-side Sessions is the recommended default; Advanced uses three Checkout endpoints for more use cases. API-request split instructions override store profile instructions; absent both, the whole amount and fees book to liable. The USD 400 example is illustrative and separates booking, settlement and payout; its currency, fees and outcome are not general guarantees.
