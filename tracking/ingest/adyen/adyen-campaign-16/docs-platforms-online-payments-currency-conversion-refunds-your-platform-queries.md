Q1. What is this document's purpose and scope, and what must not be inferred?
A1. It is a 2026-09-15 Adyen for Platforms guide for a limited-availability pilot that refunds a captured currency-converted online payment while assigning its FX remainder to the platform's liable balance account (raw lines 15–24, 46–62). The customer was charged in processing currency and the user credited in settlement currency; the refund reuses both original amounts (raw lines 48–51, 61–62). It does not prove current pilot access, a platform's entitlement, a realized FX gain or loss, refund completion, shopper receipt, payout, or merchant-of-record identity. The PLN/CZK and PLN 6.31 figures are illustrative, and the sample response has only `status: received` (raw lines 24–30, 502–536). Q2. What is the central capability, what prerequisites and consequential warnings apply?
A2. After capture, send Checkout `POST /payments/{paymentPspReference}/refunds` with original customer-paid processing amount in top-level `amount`, original user settlement amount in `splits.amount`, and a `Remainder` split naming the platform's liable balance account without specifying the remainder amount (raw lines 43–44, 53–62). The raw guide says the shopper is credited the original processing amount, the user is debited the original settlement amount, and any remainder books to the liable account (raw lines 26–30, 48–51). Its line 26 says “book the remainder amount to the customer,” which conflicts with the title, line 30, request procedure and example; follow the platform route while retaining this textual discrepancy (raw lines 15, 26–30, 55–62, 91–96). Prerequisites are an online checkout integration, Checkout API credential, standard plus transfer or transaction webhooks, account-holder capabilities and Support configuration of merchant-account currencies (raw lines 32–44). The documented original-amount path does not call the FX API; do not import the other refund option's rate-calculation requirement. A separate refund split guide warns that an invalid or closed-holder destination can fall back to the platform's liable balance; the `received` response is not final booking evidence (raw lines 502–536; [[source-adyen-docs-platforms-online-payments-split-transactions-split-refunds]]).
