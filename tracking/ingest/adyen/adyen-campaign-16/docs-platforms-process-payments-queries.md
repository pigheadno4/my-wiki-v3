# Fixed retrieval checks — Process payments

## Q1. What is this page for, what is in scope, and what should not be inferred?

The 2023-modified, 2026-09-15-captured Adyen for Platforms overview covers online and in-person payment channels, booking transaction amounts/fees across user and platform liable balance accounts through split instructions, and later settlement/payout orientation. Its USD diagram is illustrative, not evidence of a real payment, settlement, payout or bank receipt. It does not determine merchant of record, legal fund owner, current payment-method eligibility, or absent authorization/capture/refund/chargeback/partial/failure rules. ## Q2. What is the central capability, prerequisites, and material warnings?

Online use first requires an Adyen online-payments integration and checkout UI; in-person use first requires a POS app integrated with Adyen terminals and Terminal API. Splits can be instructed per online/in-person request or through store split profiles; request instructions override profiles, and with neither, the entire transaction amount and fees book to the platform liable balance account. The page describes settlement to balance accounts and scheduled payout to transfer instruments. Its two USD 100 examples show user credits of USD 95/96, commissions USD 5/4 to liable balance and fees USD 2/1 from liable balance, then illustrate user/platform transfer-instrument payouts of USD 191/USD 6; these figures are not universal fee, currency, timing or completed-funds rules.
