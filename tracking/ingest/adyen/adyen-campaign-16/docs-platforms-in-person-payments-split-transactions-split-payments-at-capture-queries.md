# Fixed retrieval checks — In-person split payments at capture

## Q1. What is this page for, what is in scope, and what should not be inferred?

The 2023-modified, 2026-09-15-captured guide covers capture-time split instructions for previously authorized Adyen for Platforms in-person Terminal API payments when split amounts are not known at authorization. It does not establish online capture behavior, legal seller/MOR, eligibility for a given payment method, a real successful capture, bank receipt or payout. The API's received response is separate from CAPTURE outcome and later balance-platform booking. ## Q2. What is the central capability, prerequisites, and material warnings?

Manual capture or capture delay is required, with general Platforms in-person/Terminal API prerequisites and CAPTURE/CAPTURE_FAILED monitoring. Capture-time splits require a payment method supporting separate capture; most in-person wallets do not, and Alipay/WeChat Pay capture immediately. Capture splits can override payment-request splits, though Adyen recommends initial splits for methods without delayed capture. If capture amount differs from authorization and no capture splits are supplied, all funds book to liable balance. BalanceAccount sale splits target user balanceAccountId; Commission books to liable balance without an account field; PaymentFee allocates unknown-at-payment-time fees, while amount.currency is conditional for conversion and reference is conditional for BalanceAccount. Surcharge and Tip are expressly unsupported at capture despite later general surcharge navigation. Capture format validation does not validate account existence/status: invalid or closed-holder targets fall back to the liable balance for full transaction and fees. Track distinct authorization/capture PSP references and CAPTURE plus per-split transfer/transaction states; received is not final. USD/test SDK samples conflict on currency and booked identities: fee transfer samples use the second user balance account while fee transaction.created uses the first; commission prose/transfer describe an incoming liable-account credit, but its captured transfer changes liable-account ID and transaction.created shows -400 on a user balance account.
