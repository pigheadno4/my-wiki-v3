Q2. What calculation, disclosure, request-format and warning boundaries govern the flow?
- Expected: Terminal API/payment terminals need software **1.118+**; issuer-country details must be enabled and single tap requested from Support. Card acquisition is recommended to obtain brand, funding source, issuer country and transaction details for the platform's or third party's surcharge calculation. Australia may consider skipping card acquisition and Mobile currently lacks it, while the worked payment path still requires a CardAcquisitionReference; do not assume the guide resolves that tension or establishes Mobile dynamic-surcharge support. The customer must receive surcharge disclosure; hiding the Adyen confirmation screen or using Mobile requires another form. `SaleToAcquirerData` carries both split instructions and `surchargeFee`; the Surcharge split names one user's balance account, cannot be divided among balance accounts, and `surchargeFee` is **not** a split field and **not** in minor units. RequestedAmount excludes surcharge; the response's TotalFeesAmount/AuthorizedAmount and AdditionalResponse minor-unit fields describe different amounts. The sample payment-request acquisition timestamp begins `026-` at line 410, so follow specified response fields rather than copying the example as a validated payload. Country, legal and scheme constraints require separate current assessment.
