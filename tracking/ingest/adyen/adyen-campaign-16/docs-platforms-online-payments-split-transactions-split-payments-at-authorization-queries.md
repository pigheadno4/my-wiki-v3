Q2. What are the prerequisite, allocation, precedence and failure conditions?
- Expected: Online checkout, Checkout/Transfers credentials, webhooks and account-holder receivePayments/receiveFromPlatformPayments/sendToTransferInstrument capabilities are listed. Without valid receiveFromPlatformPayments, the full amount goes to platform liable balance. Split fields distinguish user balance-account ID, liable commission, store reference, conditional amount currency/value/reference and fees/remainder defaults; TopUp is unsupported for sessions. Missing or closed destination also books full transaction and fees to liable despite format-valid request, with internal transfer as a correction route. Transfer/transaction webhooks track distinct received, authorised, captured and booked stages, but the fee `transaction.created` sample names a different balance account from the fee transfer sample, and the commission `transaction.created` sample shows a negative user-account amount despite prose/transfer credit to liable; do not infer exact booked sign/account from these illustrative payloads without real webhook/accounting evidence. Capture-time instructions override auth-time ones, but payment-time instructions are recommended because delayed capture is method-dependent; partial/overcapture amounts are cited reasons to update at capture.
