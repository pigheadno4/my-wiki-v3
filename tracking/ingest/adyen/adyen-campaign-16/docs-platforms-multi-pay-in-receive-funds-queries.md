# Fixed retrieval checks — Receive multi pay-in partner funds

## Q1. What is this page for, and whose account is credited?

The 2026-09-15 Platforms page explains receipt of a payout from a third-party acquirer or PSP pay-in partner into a multi pay-in account linked to the platform's Adyen balance platform. Adyen says it creates and legally holds this account for users' benefit. The partner must use it as its payout account; Adyen allowlists the partner bank account and rejects other sending bank accounts. A booked incoming transaction credits the multi pay-in account before any separate allocation to user balance accounts. The sample liable account-holder field does not change that distinction. ## Q2. Which webhooks and references evidence the receipt, and what should not be inferred?

The guide identifies partner funding transfer webhooks by category bank, direction incoming and type bankTransfer. It shows transfer.created received, transfer.updated authorised then booked, and transaction.created for credited funds in the multi pay-in account. The booked transfer and transaction event carry related IDs and partner-supplied reference fields; use exact observed event data rather than assuming a universal join. In the USD 100 test sample, the authorised accounting event sits in an update whose outer status is still received, and the booked example's nested and outer transactionId values differ. Region/currency account-format examples cover EUR and GBP IBANs and a USD US account/routing number; they do not establish current eligibility or operation timing. Receipt or authorisation is not booked credit, and even booked credit here is not allocation to a user's balance account, user payout or shopper-payment completion.
