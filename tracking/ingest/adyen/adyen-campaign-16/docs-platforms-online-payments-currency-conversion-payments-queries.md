A1. It is a 2026-09-15 Adyen for Platforms overview of a limited-availability online-payment currency-conversion pilot: the customer pays in the processing currency while the platform's user receives a different settlement currency (raw lines 15–26). It maps the two fixed-price choices to separate procedures (raw lines 35–51). Q2. What is the central capability, what prerequisites and consequential warnings apply?
A2. The platform fixes the goods or services price either in the user's settlement currency or in the customer's processing currency, calculates the other amount using the prevailing exchange rate of the day, and sends both amounts in `POST /payments`; the page describes customer debit and user credit in their respective currencies (raw lines 28–39). Fixing settlement currency keeps the user's amount fixed; fixing processing currency keeps the customer's amount fixed (raw lines 43–51). The only availability warning on this overview is that conversion is in pilot and processes/documentation may change; it lists no concrete API credentials, roles, account capabilities, webhook setup, rate formula, remainder allocation or SDK/API version, so those must be checked in the selected child guide rather than inferred here (raw lines 19–20, 41–51).
