# Fixed retrieval checks — Platforms terminals quickstart

## Q1. What is this page for, and what does it prove?

The 2026-09-15 Adyen for Platforms quickstart helps a platform plan an in-person offering for its users: software versus physical terminals, tabletop/mobile/unattended journeys, cloud/local integration, delivery, onboarding, fleet work and commercial reconciliation. Its choices and examples do not prove an account or store is enabled, a terminal is assigned or boarded, a payment processed, or a settlement deduction completed. ## Q2. What are the main decisions, prerequisites and material warnings?

The page recommends considering Android/iOS software solutions early, choosing hardware by user/shopper setting, and treating cloud as a common integration path while local communication needs security work and may serve offline requirements. Tap to Pay through Mobile SDK, Android Payments App and Pay-by-Link are distinct routes; the page does not establish that a Pay-by-Link transaction is card-present. Physical hardware calls for drop-shipping or warehouse/return capability, orders through Customer Area or Management API, regional lead-time checks and user setup support. Stock unused for over six months can have battery, firmware and reserve-battery problems. Custom packaging is described at 1,000 or more terminals yearly with a request at least three months before go-live. Customer Area supports store assignment and boarding for a user's store; Terminal Replacement Service is described for the whole fleet. Match Order Number to order CSV and Invoice Report for cost reconciliation; settlement deduction and platform-to-user sell-through/lease/gift are recommendations or business choices, not observed financial events.
