# Fixed retrieval checks — docs-platforms-multi-pay-in-refunds

Q1. What is this page for, what is its scope, and what must not be inferred?
A. This 2026-09-15 Adyen for Platforms guide covers refunding third-party-processed payments whose proceeds settled in user balance accounts. Adyen's Checkout API refund debits balance accounts and returns funds to the multi pay-in account. The pay-in partner must separately send the amount to the user's customer, so an Adyen request, REFUND event, platform debit or multi pay-in credit does not prove customer receipt. It does not identify a merchant of record or legal funds owner. Raw locators: lines 15–23, 779–792, 1412–1424; source `## Overview` and `## Key takeaways`. Q2. What are the central refund prerequisites, split rules and warnings?
A. Arrange the pay-in partner's customer-return process first. Refund against the original payment PSP reference with amount and merchant account; value equals or is below captured amount and top-level currency matches authorization currency. Explicit split instructions are strongly recommended for partial refunds. A full refund without splits reuses original *authorization* splits, not capture splits; absent original authorization splits it debits the liable account. The API checks split format but not the specified balance accounts; invalid/nonexistent accounts or closed holders can route full transaction and fees to liable, requiring separate correction. REFUND, transfer and transaction webhooks distinguish request outcome, outgoing user/liable deductions and incoming multi pay-in credit. The page's v72/test examples and USD payloads are illustrative, and its split account field matrix conflicts with the Commission example.
