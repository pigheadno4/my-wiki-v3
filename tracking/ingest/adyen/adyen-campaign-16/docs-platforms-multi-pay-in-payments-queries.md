**Q2 — Central capability, prerequisites, and warnings.** How are funds allocated and evidenced, and which conditions or examples limit interpretation?

**Answer:** The page calls for Checkout API credentials, Transfer/Transaction webhook subscriptions, and allocation of each payment within 24 hours of settlement in the multi pay-in account (raw lines 27–36). For each payment, POST `/payments` with `multi_payin`, `MultiPayIn`, the source multi pay-in BA, merchant account, reference, amount and splits; `BalanceAccount` targets user/platform BAs, `Commission` books to platform liable, and fee types can target a BA (raw lines 38–67). If no fee-booking instructions are supplied, fees default to liable; the USD 400/396/4 request is illustrative (raw lines 69–128). Match four separate event legs—source debit, user credit, commission credit, fee debit—using webhook category/direction/type/status and transaction IDs; `Authorised` response and `received`/ `authorised` stages do not establish final booking, while report excerpts have separate booking/value dates, time zones, signs, and account IDs (raw lines 407–425, 427–1667, 1668–1688). The examples conflict on user-credit captured BA suffix `00002` versus request/other rows `00001`, and on commission booked transaction `-400` versus positive credit narrative/events; do not copy them as authoritative reconciliation values (raw lines 108, 767–769, 918–920, 1026–1029, 1038–1051, 1320–1334). Payout is a later configured-schedule or on-demand step after user-balance settlement (raw lines 1689–1691). No partial-failure/atomicity behavior or current regional coverage is specified in this page.
