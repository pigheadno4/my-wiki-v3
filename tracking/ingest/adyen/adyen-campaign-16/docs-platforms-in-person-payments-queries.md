# Fixed retrieval queries — Platforms in-person payments

## Q1. What is this page for, what scope does it cover, and what should a reader not infer?

**Expected answer:** It is the Adyen for Platforms in-person payment guide for a POS/Terminal API or Mobile integration serving platform users and allocating funds/fees between user balance accounts and the platform liable balance account. It does not decide who is legal seller or merchant of record, prove country/account-holder capability enablement, or show that any particular POS payment, capture, settlement, payout or bank receipt occurred. The USD 620 diagram is illustrative. **Evidence:** pinned raw `raw/adyen/docs/platforms/in-person-payments/2026-09-15.md:11,15-19,21-37,58-79`; canonical retrieval route `[[source-adyen-docs-platforms-in-person-payments]]` via `[[adyen-platforms]]` and `[[adyen-index]]`, with `[[adyen-terminal-api]]` for separately sourced POS context.

## Q2. What central capabilities, prerequisites, and warnings does it give?

**Expected answer:** The page calls for Terminal API POS integration with terminals or a Mobile solution, Transfers API credentials, chosen-channel setup, Standard webhooks plus Transfer or Transaction event subscriptions, and account-holder `receivePayments` and `receiveFromPlatformPayments` capabilities. Automatic store split profiles and per-request instructions can book funds/fees; request instructions override profiles. If neither is supplied, the entire amount and fees book to the platform liable balance account. Standalone is a fallback/profile-only route and cannot provide per-request payment or refund splits. The sample USD 620 booking and payout illustrates the lifecycle, not guaranteed values, authorization, final settlement or bank credit. **Evidence:** pinned raw `raw/adyen/docs/platforms/in-person-payments/2026-09-15.md:21-56,58-79`; canonical retrieval route `[[source-adyen-docs-platforms-in-person-payments]]` via `[[adyen-platforms]]` and `[[adyen-index]]`.
