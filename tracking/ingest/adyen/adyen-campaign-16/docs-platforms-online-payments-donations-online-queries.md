# Fixed queries — docs-platforms-online-payments-donations-online

Q1. What is this page for, what is its scope, and what must a reader not infer?
A1. The 2026-09-15 guide is for Adyen for Platforms with a platform model and API-only online checkout, where a user offers a shopper a separate Giving donation to a nonprofit alongside a purchase (raw lines 15–36). Adyen says donation fees are covered and the full donation goes to the nonprofit; purchase splits do not apply to the donation (line 23). This snapshot does not prove current method/region/currency eligibility, a live campaign, actual donation, nonprofit receipt, merchant of record or legal ownership. It does not specify the Web Components, Sessions or terminal route. Q2. What central flow, prerequisites and outcome warnings does it give?
A2. Requirements include Checkout API v67 or later, an API-only integration, Giving merchant webhook, supported methods/regions/currencies and an active account-holder campaign (raw lines 27–50). The server requests campaigns with merchant account, currency and user-store reference, then displays fixed amounts or round-up plus nonprofit details/terms (lines 52–114, 326–341, 368–377, 589–667). A pre-checkout donation choice still waits for authorized original payment and `donationToken`; post-checkout likewise uses the token from `/payments` or iDEAL `/payments/details` before POST `/donations` (lines 669–689). Donation response status may be completed, pending or refused, while the Giving `DONATION` webhook supplies the outcome; iDEAL may take a day or more (lines 715–775). Sample v72 test calls and the malformed round-up curl JSON at lines 739–757 are not production proof or copy-ready.
