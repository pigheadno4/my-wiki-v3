# Fixed retrieval checks — Platforms multi pay-in overview

Q1. What is this source's purpose and scope, and what must not be inferred?
- Expected: A 2026-09-15 Adyen for Platforms **online-payment** service overview for consolidating Adyen-acquired payments and third-party pay-in-partner volume in one balance platform. A partner's payout enters a distinct multi pay-in account, described as legally held by Adyen solely for users' benefit before allocation to user and platform liable balance accounts. One account is described per partner. This does not establish current partner/account enablement, region or currency availability, Merchant-of-Record identity, ownership of a particular payment, successful booking, payout, or bank receipt. The diagram is illustrative.

Q2. Which prerequisites, timing, allocation fallback and refund-responsibility boundaries govern multi pay-in?
- Expected: The guide requires an Adyen for Platforms online payments integration, Checkout and Transfers API credentials, Transfer and/or Transaction webhooks, and account-holder `sendToBalanceAccount` and `receiveFromBalanceAccount` capabilities. It says **separate authorization and capture are unsupported** and received funds must be allocated from the multi pay-in account to platform balance accounts within **24 hours** for regulatory compliance. After partner payout, Checkout POST `/payments` with splits allocates to user and/or liable balance accounts; with no splits all funds and fees book to liable. The external pay-in partner generally returns a refund to the customer and withholds the amount from a later payout; the platform requests an Adyen refund that debits selected balance accounts and offsets the multi pay-in account. An Adyen balance movement is not proof of the partner-side customer return. The refund diagram's EUR 98/EUR 2 debits and USD 100 offset conflict on currency, so do not infer a conversion rate or verified refund outcome.
