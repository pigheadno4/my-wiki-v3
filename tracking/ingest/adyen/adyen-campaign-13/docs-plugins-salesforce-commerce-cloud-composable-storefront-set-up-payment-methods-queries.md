# Fixed queries — SFCC Composable Storefront payment methods

## Q1 — purpose, scope, and what must not be inferred

**Query:** Which Salesforce integration does this guide configure, and does method activation prove a usable live payment or order?

**Answer:** It covers Adyen's Salesforce Composable Storefront package/Retail React App, not SFRA. Add wanted methods in Adyen Customer Area before enabling `AdyenComponent` and `CREDIT_CARD` with `Adyen_Component` processor in Salesforce Business Manager. Most enabled methods need no extra page-specific setup, while named methods do. These configuration steps do not establish current merchant/method eligibility, a live transaction, an order, refund or settlement. The page does not specify user/API/webhook-role grants. ## Q2 — central capability, prerequisites, and warnings

**Query:** Which method-specific requirements and package-version boundaries control Apple Pay, Click to Pay, installments, PayPal express and Google Pay?

**Answer:** Apple Pay needs HTTPS/valid SSL and test Customer Area setup; localhost testing is excluded. Earlier express tabs confine display to basket/cart, while v4.2.0+ adds product-detail launch, without quantity/variant handling there. Click to Pay starts v4.1.0 and needs Visa/Mastercard CSP sources. Card installments start v4.3.0, require merchant/scheme/country enablement and the stated BRL/MXN/JPY country gates, and do not apply to express methods. PayPal express starts v4.2.0 with a PDP temporary basket and optional review-page confirmation handoff. Google Pay standard needs test enablement and live Merchant ID/origin registration; express starts v4.3.0 with shipping/locale checks, distinct cart/PDP inputs and silent non-mount on unsupported devices. Match implementation snippets to their version tabs. The page's **Next steps** marks the Composable Storefront go-live checklist as required before accepting live payments; follow that linked checklist for live readiness. This page does not establish the checklist's contents, and neither configuration nor the checklist route proves an actual payment or order outcome.
