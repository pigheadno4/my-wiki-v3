## Q2. What is the central capability, prerequisites, and material warnings?

If **manual capture** is configured, the merchant must request capture per order; Commerce uses **Invoice**, optional reduced quantity for partial capture, and **Submit Invoice**, with `CAPTURE` webhook and later invoice Paid status described (raw lines 25–37). Mixed-method orders with different capture modes get an invoice only after the **full order amount** is authorized or captured, regardless of individual webhook order (lines 39–41); that does not equate all payment states. Cancellation needs authorized/not-captured status and manual capture; after capture, refund is the route (lines 43–55). A refund uses a selected invoice and Credit Memo, with partial/full Commerce statuses and `REFUND` notification distinguished from actual shopper receipt (lines 57–72). The page says `CANCELLATION` at line 55 while the separate setup guide's raw line 202 says `CANCEL_OR_REFUND`; verify installed version/event behavior. Card/other expiry defaults are dated. For expired SVS gift-card payments, Adyen does not send `OFFER_CLOSED`; only abandonment via **Previous** auto-closes the offer, while other abandonment needs manual Commerce closure or a configured pending-payment lifetime and scheduled cron route (lines 74–91). Optional case management needs RevenueProtect premium, user roles, triggers, actions and webhooks before accept/capture or reject/cancel behavior (lines 93–124).
