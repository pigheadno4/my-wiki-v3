## Q2. What is the central capability, prerequisites, and material warnings?

After SFRA metadata import and merchant-account method setup, verify `AdyenComponent` and `CREDIT_CARD` are enabled with `Adyen_Component` processor in Business Manager (raw lines 25–33). Display depends on order amount, currency and shipping country (line 37). Apple Pay branches differ: Adyen-certificate setup covers checkout/express with test site URLs, no localhost and a live domain association route; own-certificate Salesforce integration is express-only, needs separate cartridge/merchant identifier/certificate/domain/Basic-auth setup and is outside Adyen troubleshooting support (lines 53–183). Amazon Pay Express, optional Klarna widget and region-limited credit-card installments each have separate prerequisites/rules (lines 185–235); gift cards need Support confirmation (lines 237–241). Google Pay express and live Merchant ID are distinct from standard test enablement (lines 243–260). PayPal live setup needs PayPal Merchant ID; Venmo is US merchant + US shopper conditional, tokenization needs additional Adyen configuration, and old Express users are sent to Support (lines 262–281). PayPal display prose and toggle list differ on product/shipping placement, so verify actual installed-cartridge behavior. All steps are configuration, not payment, capture or settlement proof.
