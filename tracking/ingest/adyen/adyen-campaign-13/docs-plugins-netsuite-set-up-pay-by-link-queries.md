Q1 — What is this source for, what record scopes does it cover, and what must not be inferred? It sets up Pay by Link in NetSuite for invoice, sales-order and Customer Deposit records. Each link takes a customer to an Adyen-hosted payment page. Q2 — What setup conditions and payment-state warnings matter? The SuitePayments Bundle and Customer Area setup precede a method instance, processing-profile fields, Custom Preferences and profile/subsidiary config record. Pay by Link invoices additionally need Pay by Reference and a Checkout API key; tokenization is optional and default-via-tokenization depends on it. Test mode is a separate Custom Preference; invoice URL timing may be before or after submit. An invoice payment record is described as appearing about 15 minutes after shopper completion; a sales order remains on Payment Hold until payment. Customer Deposit links support full/partial shopper-adjusted amounts only for cards, alternative methods and ACH, with application to invoice only after successful payment.
