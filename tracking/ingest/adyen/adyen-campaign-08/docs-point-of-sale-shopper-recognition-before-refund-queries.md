# C08 card identification before refund — fixed retrieval checks

1. **Q: Does a matching Adyen card alias authorize a refund or establish the shopper's identity?**
   **A:** No. In this captured Terminal API guide, the alias represents the presented card's PAN for recognition; Adyen says it cannot be used to make payments. The merchant compares the Refund-type card-acquisition response's `AdditionalResponse.alias` **and proposed refund amount** against purchase records in its own back end. That is an optional same-card check before an unreferenced refund, not proof of who holds the card, consent, refund eligibility, or original transaction status. The page does not specify an alias hashing method, cross-store/account matching guarantee or NFC-wallet fallback. It requires the separate unreferenced-refund setup and identifier return; Adyen Support enablement of single tap avoids a second contactless tap. 2. **Q: What request chain follows a positive same-card check, and does a successful immediate response mean the refund reached the shopper?**
   **A:** The POS first sends `CardAcquisitionRequest` with `CardAcquisitionTransaction.TotalAmount` and `PaymentType=Refund`, then saves the acquisition response's `POIData.POITransactionID.TimeStamp` and `TransactionID`. After the alias/amount match and merchant decision, it sends a separate `PaymentRequest` with `PaymentData.PaymentType=Refund`, refund currency/amount, and `PaymentData.CardAcquisitionReference` containing **the acquisition transaction** timestamp and ID; the request does not reference the original purchase transaction. If it declines that card, it cancels the card acquisition. The immediate refund `Result=Success`/terminal approval is request receipt; the guide says to wait for `REFUND_WITH_DATA` webhook for the asynchronous result. A missing final result is no reason to replay the refund blindly. The raw sample's two request `POIID` serials differ, so its literal IDs are not a tested same-device pair.
