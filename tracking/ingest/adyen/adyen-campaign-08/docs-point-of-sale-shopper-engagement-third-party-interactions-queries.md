# C08 third-party terminal interactions — fixed retrieval checks

1. **Q: Can a third party use this Adyen POS route to take payment or acquire card data, and how are roles divided?**
   **A:** No. The dated guide permits third-party non-payment Input/Display requests and delivery of terminal display notifications or Adyen platform standard webhooks; it says payment/card-acquisition requests are refused with the source's `010 Not Allowed` label. The merchant POS app starts payment, the merchant configures endpoints and a limited third-party API credential, and the third party targets a terminal by notified `POIID` using `x-API-key`. The example asks for input after payment finishes. Evidence: `raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/shopper-engagement/third-party-interactions/2026-09-15.md:19-44,45-76,255-306`; canonical URL: https://docs.adyen.com/point-of-sale/shopper-engagement/third-party-interactions. This historical snapshot does not prove current model/merchant eligibility or a payment outcome.

2. **Q: What privacy/security, timeout and cancellation limits does this third-party guide retain?**
   **A:** The guide calls for public endpoints and live HTTPS/TLS 1.2, secure sharing of a role/account-scoped third-party key, standard-webhook HMAC verification advice and successful acknowledgement, and minimizing webhook detail because restrictions on third-party standard-webhook detail were then unenforced. It says local terminal display notifications need Support to make them unencrypted for the third party. Input/Display requests default to a three-second timeout without `MinimumDisplayTime`. POS-app cancellation overrides with an Idle DisplayRequest; its prose reports `Cancel` while its JSON example reports `Busy`, an unresolved source conflict.
