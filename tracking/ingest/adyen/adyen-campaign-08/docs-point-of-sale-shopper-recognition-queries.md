# C08 POS card recognition — fixed query answers

1. **Does card acquisition for recognition charge the shopper?** The 2020-modified, 2026-09-15-captured overview says card acquisition obtains card and shopper identifiers before a next step, without making a payment yet. It lists parking entry/exit amount calculation and a same-card check before an unreferenced refund as examples. A later payment or refund needs its own operation and result; this overview does not authorize either. Evidence: `raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/shopper-recognition/2026-09-15.md`, `# Card recognition` lines 19–27 and `## General preparation` lines 29–34; proposed source `[[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-shopper-recognition]]`.

2. **Which recognition scenarios does the overview route to, and does it define the identifier fields?** It mentions card-based parking entry/exit, a same-card check before unreferenced refund, gift-card detection, and tax-free shopping using issuer country/region and card BIN. The page links to a separate card-and-shopper-identifiers guide and scenario guides; it does not define alias, PAN, token, fingerprint or shopper-reference semantics, privacy/consent conditions, or current scheme/region eligibility. Those examples do not prove personal identity or tax-refund qualification. Evidence: `raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/shopper-recognition/2026-09-15.md`, `# Card recognition` lines 19–27, `## General preparation` lines 29–34 and `## What do you want to do?` lines 36–48; proposed source `[[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-shopper-recognition]]`.
