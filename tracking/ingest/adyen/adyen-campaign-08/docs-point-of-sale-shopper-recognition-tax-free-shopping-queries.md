# C08 tax-free shopping — fixed query answers

1. **Who decides tax-free eligibility and issues the refund, and what does Adyen send?** In this 2026-09-15 guide, the merchant POS app checks eligibility locally or with the TFS provider for a Terminal API/card-acquisition flow; for Mobile it checks the provider's requirements using a passport or previously saved CRM details. The TFS provider, described here as **Global Blue** for the supported secure-card-capture integration, refunds the shopper and handles authority paperwork. After shopper consent and `taxfree.indicator=true`, Adyen's webhook sends the transaction PSP reference, encrypted card number and BIN to the TFS provider. Those data and Customer Area's provider identifier are notification/acknowledgement evidence, not proof of export validation or refund payout. The page's region/VAT constraints are dated and do not determine a shopper's current tax eligibility. Evidence: `raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/shopper-recognition/tax-free-shopping/2026-09-15.md`, opening lines 19–23, `## Requirements` lines 25–35, `## How it works` lines 37–82 and `## Set up notifications to your TFS provider` lines 113–133; proposed `[[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-shopper-recognition-tax-free-shopping]]`.

2. **What happens if the shopper declines secure card capture, and is a card-acquisition response itself a payment?** For the terminal flow, decline makes `taxfree.indicator` **false** in the automatic consent flow; with custom/POS consent, the merchant sets it false or omits it. The guide says that prevents secure card capture, while the shopper can still claim a tax refund from the TFS provider using the form and receipt. A terminal `CardAcquisitionResponse` supplies issuer country/BIN and related data for the POS/provider eligibility check, plus an ID referenced by a later payment request; it is an optional information step, not a completed payment. A Mobile flow may ask verbal or POS-screen consent and likewise sends a true flag only when eligible and consented. Evidence: same raw, terminal and Mobile tabs lines 43–82, `### 1.
