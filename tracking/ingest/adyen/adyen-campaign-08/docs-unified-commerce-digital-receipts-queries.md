# C08 digital receipts — fixed query answers

1. **Does Adyen automatically email a receipt after the shopper taps Yes on the terminal?** The 2026-09-15 guide says the merchant needs software to generate a receipt from the Terminal API payment response and send it. `GetConfirmation` returns `Input.ConfirmedFlag=true` when the shopper chooses email; this records the receipt preference before the POS app makes a separate payment request. The response's `PaymentReceipt` provides data for the receipt, while the merchant uses its **CRM or email tool** to send to `shopperEmail`. The prompt, response and sample `Result: Success` do not prove an email was generated, delivered or legally compliant. Evidence: `raw/adyen/docs/unified-commerce/digital-receipts/2026-09-15.md`, `## Requirements` lines 23–28, known-email input lines 119–165, and `## Step 3: Continue with payment` lines 265–340; proposed `[[source-adyen-docs-unified-commerce-digital-receipts]]`.

2. **If card acquisition has no `shopperReference`, is the shopper necessarily new, and how is an unknown email handled?** No. The card is unrecognized, but the shopper could be returning with a new card. The guide says the merchant can collect an email and compare it with its customer database. If the email is not already linked and the shopper wants an emailed receipt, ask first with `GetConfirmation`, then collect the address through the linked profile flow and create a customer profile; if the shopper declines, continue to payment with a paper-receipt preference. Choosing email is a receipt-channel choice, not proof of marketing consent or automatic card storage. Evidence: same raw, `## Step 1: Recognize customer` lines 30–108 and `### Email address is unknown` lines 190–263; proposed `[[source-adyen-docs-unified-commerce-digital-receipts]]`.
