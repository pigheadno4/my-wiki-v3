# C08 creating customer profiles — fixed retrieval checks

1. **Q: Who holds the cross-channel customer profile, what consent and enablement steps does the pinned guide state, and what must a shopper reference avoid?**
   **A:** The merchant creates the profile in its CRM or other database and makes that record readable and writable across sales channels. Adyen says to ask for customer consent before storing personal information or tokenizing a card. To receive loyalty identifiers, ask Adyen to enable card `alias` in ecommerce and POS payment responses and select **Recurring details** under Customer Area **Developers → Additional data**. `shopperReference` is the merchant's unique customer reference, at least three characters, and must not contain PII such as name or email. Evidence: `raw/adyen/docs/unified-commerce/loyalty-program/create-profiles/2026-09-15.md:15-35,48-52,282-300`; canonical URL: https://docs.adyen.com/unified-commerce/loyalty-program/create-profiles. These instructions do not prove a specific account is enabled or that a customer consented.

2. **Q: How do online and POS profile-creation requests differ, and is a card alias a saved-payment token?**
   **A:** Online Checkout `/payments` sends `shopperEmail` and PII-free `shopperReference`; `storePaymentMethod: true` is conditional on the shopper choosing to save details. At POS, a separate terminal GetText input can collect email, then Terminal API PaymentRequest carries `shopperEmail` and `shopperReference` in `SaleToAcquirerData`; `recurringContract=UnscheduledCardOnFile` is conditional on agreement to save payment details. The `alias` is for card recognition, whereas `tokenization.storedPaymentMethodId` or `recurring.recurringDetailReference` is the saved-method token for future tokenized payments. The guide says the online alias is unavailable for NFC wallet transactions. Evidence: `raw/adyen/docs/unified-commerce/loyalty-program/create-profiles/2026-09-15.md:36-109,113-125,203-213,250-300`; canonical URL: https://docs.adyen.com/unified-commerce/loyalty-program/create-profiles. The POS example's `tokenization.shopperReference=12345` conflicts with the request and `recurring.shopperReference=ShopperID-A16729`, so verify actual returned identifiers before linking records (`raw/adyen/docs/unified-commerce/loyalty-program/create-profiles/2026-09-15.md:237,269`).
