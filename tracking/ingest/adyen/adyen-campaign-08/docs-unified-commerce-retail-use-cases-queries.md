# Fixed retrieval checks: retail use-cases hub

1. **Q:** What do click and collect and endless aisle mean in this Adyen retail overview? **A:** Click and collect means the shopper orders or buys products online and picks them up at a physical store. Endless aisle means the shopper places an ecommerce order in a store or other physical location. The hub links reserve/try and buy/pickup click-and-collect variants, plus ship-home and pickup endless-aisle variants, but those labels do not specify stock reservation, checkout UI, fulfillment or payment completion.
