# Fixed retrieval checks: autonomous stores

1. **Question:** What does card acquisition establish for autonomous-store entry, and can its card alias be charged?
   **Expected answer:** Acquisition returns an alias and transaction reference; the alias recognizes a card but cannot make payments. The raw calls `authorisationType=PreAuth` in the acquisition request a pre-authorization of `TotalAmount`, yet separately directs a Terminal API payment request to pre-authorize the same amount. Do not resolve whether acquisition alone reserves funds from this conflict. The acquisition response alone does not establish entry approval or a final charge; after successful follow-up pre-authorization, merchant-side logic decides when to open the door. The guide does not specify a separate seller/MOR model. Source: pinned raw opening, `## 1. Get the card details`, `## 3. Pre-authorize an amount` (2026-09-15 snapshot).

2. **Question:** After exit, can an autonomous-store operator treat amount-update or capture `received` as success, or charge the card alias for a shortfall?
   **Expected answer:** No. The amount update goes to an Adyen backend API against the original payment PSP reference, not to the terminal. The asynchronous route's `received` response needs an `AUTHORISATION_ADJUSTMENT` webhook; the separately enabled synchronous `/adjustAuthorisation` route reports `Authorised` for the new amount and returns a blob used as-is for later adjustments. Manual capture returns `received` and needs `CAPTURE`/`CAPTURE_FAILED` outcome handling. If adjustment fails, the guide permits capture of the original pre-authorized amount and recovery of a remainder only through a previously created recurring token or a payment link if contact details were collected. The alias cannot be charged. Source: pinned raw `## 4. Adjust the pre-authorized amount`, `## 5. Capture the amount`, `## Recover failed payments` (2026-09-15 snapshot).
