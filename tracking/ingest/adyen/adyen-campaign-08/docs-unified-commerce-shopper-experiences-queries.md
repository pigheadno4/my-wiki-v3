# Fixed retrieval checks: Shopper Experiences overview

1. **Q:** How does this page distinguish endless aisle, click and collect, and reserve and collect? **A:** It frames a journey as the order, product/service and payment, which can occur at different touchpoints. Endless aisle is an in-store ecommerce order and in-store payment for an item unavailable there, followed by home delivery; a kiosk/tablet/terminal is only one possible setup. Click and collect is purchase online then store pickup. Reserve and collect is an online reservation followed by in-person payment and collection. These are use-case examples, not proof of stock synchronization, fulfillment, capture or a particular checkout UI. 2. **Q:** Does the overview specify how Pay by Link or cross-channel referenced refunds execute? **A:** It says a merchant can send a payment link for orders from channels such as email, call center or social media alongside an online-payments integration. It also says a unique payment reference supports tracking payments/refunds and in-store returns for POS or online purchases. It gives no API request schema, link expiry, refund eligibility or asynchronous result, capture/cancel mechanics, seller/payee/MOR allocation or proof of current payment success. The linked Pay by Link and referenced-refunds pages are navigation for those details.
