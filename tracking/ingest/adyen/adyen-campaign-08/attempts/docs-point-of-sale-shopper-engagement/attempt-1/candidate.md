## Overview

Adyen's shopper-engagement overview describes using a payment terminal to interact with customers. Its examples are showing Terms and Conditions, asking for an opinion or contact details, and printing a ticket; it says these operations are often used in advanced flows such as loyalty. The page is a navigation hub for terminal display, input, print and multi-request session guides. It does not describe a payment, refund or completed shopper interaction. - The subject is **shopper interaction on the payment terminal**.
