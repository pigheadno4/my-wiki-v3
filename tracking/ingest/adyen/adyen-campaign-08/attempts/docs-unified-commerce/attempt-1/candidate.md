## Overview

This Adyen Docs overview frames **Unified Commerce** as Adyen's merchant solution for joining payment-data views across online, in-app and in-person customer journeys and merchant back-end systems. It organizes the offering around Insights, Experiences and Engagement, then routes to separate guides and use cases. The page is a high-level product and regional-availability overview, not an implementation contract or proof that a merchant has linked shopper identities or achieved an outcome. See [[adyen]] and [[adyen-unified-commerce]].

## Key takeaways

- The customer journey is shopper-facing across online, in-app and in-person channels; the described integration problem is the merchant's disconnected customer-facing and back-end systems. Adyen says its single payments platform can consolidate payments data to support a unified customer view. This is a merchant data/experience proposition, not a statement that the shopper is the merchant, seller, payee or merchant of record. The page specifies no identity key, matching rule, consent/privacy configuration, API, payment object, seller/payee allocation or funds flow.
- Adyen labels three pillars: **Insights** into customers, trends and cross-channel shopping behavior; **Experiences** for flexible and consistent journeys; and **Engagement** for personalization and loyalty. These are overview categories. The linked shopper-insights, shopper-experiences, shopper-engagement and retail-use-case pages are navigation targets; their mechanisms and prerequisites have not been read as evidence here.
- The page states that Unified Commerce is available wherever Adyen's point-of-sale solution is available, then lists Americas, APAC, Europe and UAE countries/regions in its table. Its UK footnote includes Guernsey and the Isle of Man but excludes Jersey and other overseas territories/crown dependencies. Its UAE note says no 5 GHz network coverage for Verifone payment terminals. This 2026-09-15 capture carries a 2021 last-modified date; current country, terminal, merchant and feature eligibility require verification.
