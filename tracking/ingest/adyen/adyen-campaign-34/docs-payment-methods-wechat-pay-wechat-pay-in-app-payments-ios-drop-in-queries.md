Q1. What does this iOS in-app WeChat Pay Drop-in guide add to an existing integration?
A1. It requires an existing Sessions- or Advanced-flow iOS Drop-in integration and WeChat Pay added in Customer Area, plus WeChat developer/app registration and Xcode URL-scheme/dependency setup (raw lines 21–69). It says no additional method-specific fields and no required method-specific Drop-in configuration, with /sessions described as default for Drop-in v5.0.0+ and /payments for an additional use case (raw lines 71–86). Q2. Does this page provide a TEST payment path or prove live acceptance and a completed payment?
A2. No. In this 2026-09-15 snapshot it says WeChat Pay has no test platform, offers historical personal-account cancellation or low-value live-payment suggestions, and requires a separate live Customer Area request before accepting live WeChat Pay (raw lines 88–97). A payment-list lookup is a status route, not an observed authorization, webhook, capture or settlement result; this page gives no such result (raw lines 88–97).
