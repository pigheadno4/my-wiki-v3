It says no extra WeChat-specific endpoint fields, while leaving base requests and optional general fields to the integration (raw lines 47–52). Q2. What version, QR, webhook and live-result boundaries apply?
Answer: Web Components v6 imports `WeChat` and v6.0.0+ mounts `new WeChat(checkout)`; v5.x.x-or-earlier uses `checkout.create('wechatpayWeb')` (raw lines 39–45, 64–84). This page does not define a QR payload/expiry or webhook event/outcome; its webhook entry is only a link (raw lines 92–96). It points to live Customer Area status and says a separate live WeChat Pay request is needed (raw lines 86–90), without proving approval or payment settlement.
