Q1. What UPI scope and prerequisites does this overview describe?
A1. It says a local India entity/integration and linked India-regulations compliance are needed, then describes Intent app redirect, QR scan and Collect VPA/app completion; the matrix lists Direct debit, await, IN/INR (raw lines 19, 26–36). It routes to Web/native/API-only child guides rather than specifying their implementations (raw lines 38–80). Q2. Does the Collect notice ban all existing Collect flows, or does the recurring mark establish AutoPay mandates?
A2. No. The November 2025 NPCI plan is followed by Adyen's no-Collect-for-new-merchant-integrations statement and a strong migration recommendation for existing custom Checkout API/UI users; v6.30.0 is the cited UI update (raw lines 21–24). The recurring check has an unresolved “See note” on this page, with no AutoPay mandate or consent procedure (raw lines 34–36).
