# UPI iOS Component retrieval questions

## Q1 — What is this guide for, and what is required?

It adds UPI Intent to an existing iOS Components integration. Sessions requires Checkout API v68+ and UPI added in Customer Area, with no extra base `/sessions` parameters (raw lines 25–41). Smart Intent can detect only apps whose schemes the merchant declares in iOS `Info.plist`, within the 50-scheme limit; no installed app is handled gracefully (lines 43–92). Advanced requires additional `action.type: await` handling and, for `upi_intent`, `paymentMethod.appId` (lines 138–148, 222–252). The Advanced Integration type link misroutes to Drop-in despite Components prose (line 146). ## Q2 — What is the material scope and outcome boundary?

This raw page explains iOS **Intent** app discovery and an Advanced Intent request; its test checklist mentions QR **on web** only and gives no iOS QR or Collect implementation or Collect deprecation date (raw lines 43–114, 222–274). Sessions sample delegates to the session, Advanced to self; both leave `configuration:` blank (lines 98–114, 205–220). Advanced `await` prose and the linked “handle-the-redirect” anchor need separate base-guide verification (line 147). The `v72`/`INR`/`IN`/`gpay` request is a test example; the iOS TEST checklist spells the webhook eventCode `AUTHORISED` (raw lines 128–133, 266–271), whereas the UPI API-only outcome tables spell it `AUTHORISATION` (raw API-only lines 112–117, 216–221, 382–387), even though that page's TEST checklists repeat `AUTHORISED`. This conflict means neither spelling is verified as the implementation contract; consult the authoritative webhook schema or live evidence. The simulator outcome and Customer Area check do not prove a live authorization (iOS raw lines 232–274).
