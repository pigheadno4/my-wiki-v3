Q1. What steps does this snapshot place before WeChat Pay in-app acceptance, and which additional path applies to a self-built integration?
Answer: Create a WeChat Open Platform developer account and wait for WeChat verification, register the mobile app, obtain AppID/AppSecret and provide them to Adyen Support (raw lines 19–22, 28–52). The mobile SDK steps are additional only for a merchant building its own integration instead of Adyen Drop-in or Component (raw lines 24–26, 54–67). Q2. Does the app/credential handoff establish live enablement, SDK version, or actual settlement, and is the registration section Android-only?
Answer: No. The historical text says the AppID/AppSecret Support handoff is intended to ensure settlement to the Adyen account, but documents no completed handoff or observed transaction (raw lines 50–52). The registration section opens with Android wording yet expressly includes an iOS-only App Store link and Bundle ID (raw lines 39–50); the SDK download description gives an iOS “version x.x.x” placeholder, not a minimum (raw lines 54–67).
