Q1. What does the iOS in-app WeChat Pay Component require from Adyen integration and WeChat app setup?
Answer: Existing Sessions or Advanced iOS Components plus Customer Area addition are listed at raw lines 23–39. The page says no extra WeChat Pay-specific API fields, with /sessions default from Components v5+ and /payments for an additional use case (lines 73–78); v5+ and v4-specific component notes are at lines 80–99. It recommends cancel-at-verification or low-value live testing, suggests live Customer Area inspection, and requires a live Customer Area request before live acceptance (lines 103–110).
