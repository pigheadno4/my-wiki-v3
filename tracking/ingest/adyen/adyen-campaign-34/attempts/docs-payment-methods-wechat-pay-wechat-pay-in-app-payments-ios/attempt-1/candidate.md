## Overview

This Adyen **WeChat Pay for iOS** page, last modified 2020-03-06 and captured 2026-09-15, is a navigation chooser for accepting WeChat Pay in an iOS app. It links in-app **Drop-in** and **Components** child guides and two general iOS base integration guides. It does not itself establish method setup, provider registration, SDK version, app-switch behavior, or payment outcome. [[adyen]] [[adyen-online-payments]]

## Key takeaways

- The chooser calls Drop-in an **all-in-one UI** option to accept WeChat Pay in the iOS app and Components a **pre-built Component** for presenting it. Those descriptions identify child-guide destinations, not evidence that a merchant's app or WeChat account is configured. (raw lines 19–27)
- Its final 'For more information' line links iOS Drop-in and Components **Sessions-flow** base guides. It does not link an Advanced base guide or select a required server-side flow for a particular child integration. Consult the selected child and base guide before inferring flow, request fields, provider credentials, app switch, test/live state or final authorization. (raw line 29)
