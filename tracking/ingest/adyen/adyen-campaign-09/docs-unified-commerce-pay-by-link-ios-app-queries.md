# C09 retrieval probes — Pay by Link iOS app

Q1. What does the iOS app guide enable, and what account setup and payment-method caveat apply?
Expected: The 2026-09-15 Adyen guide says staff can create and manage payment links on an iOS device without development work. It says the iOS app accepts most Pay by Link methods except BNPL; that exclusion is app-specific and must not be generalized to Customer Area or API routes, whose method matrix has its own unresolved BNPL indications. Before use, the merchant adds terms, adds account payment methods, configures creation settings, and gives the Customer Area user the Pay by Link Interface role. A created or shared link is not evidence of shopper payment or successful authorization. Raw lines 15–41; canonical route https://docs.adyen.com/unified-commerce/pay-by-link/ios-app.

Q2. What does the app show and let staff change after login, and what are its visibility limits?
Expected: First login chooses test or live, uses Customer Area credentials, and selects merchant accounts. The guide says credentials are encrypted on device and later login can use Face ID or passcode. The New payment link form follows Customer Area creation settings. The Overview lists links created using the app, with status filters and share, QR, or expire actions; it does not claim to list all links from Customer Area or API. Settings can change the selected merchant account or store; changing credentials requires logout and login. The page mentions emailing payment webhook events without specifying delivery or result fields. Raw lines 43–81; same canonical route.
