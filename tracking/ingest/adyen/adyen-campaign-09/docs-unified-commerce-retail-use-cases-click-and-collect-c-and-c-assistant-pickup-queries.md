## Q1 — purpose, scope, and what not to infer

The shopper orders online and authorizes the payment; a separate assistant collects the goods in a store. The merchant first gets the assistant's non-financial card identifiers through a zero-amount link, matches them against terminal-acquired identifiers at pickup, and captures the **shopper's** online authorization at pickup (`raw/adyen/docs/unified-commerce/retail-use-cases/click-and-collect/c-and-c-assistant-pickup/2026-09-15.md:19–26, 81–97, 417–420`). This is card-record matching within the merchant's order system, not civil-identity proof. The zero-amount link is not a charge or authorization for the order, and card acquisition is not a payment. The page does not establish seller/payee/MoR allocation, consent implementation, live account eligibility, a successful capture, settlement, or completed handoff.

## Q2 — central capability, prerequisites, and warnings

The described route needs both ecommerce and POS integrations plus a consolidated order/inventory or ERP system, or an orchestration layer that connects systems and consumes webhooks (`:28–36`). The merchant decides sale attribution, enables manual capture for the online purchase, and retains its `AUTHORISATION` webhook PSP reference and amount (`:69–75, 143–218`). Pay by Link must be configured, including terms; the assistant receives a zero-amount `POST /paymentLinks` link restricted to `scheme` so its card or NFC wallet can later be recognized by terminal card acquisition (`:220–275`). The merchant enables identifying data in API responses/webhooks and matches the link's assistant alias/PAR against the pickup terminal response, then ends acquisition (`:81–101, 277–376`). The page suggests explaining the use of the assistant's information; it does not define permission handling (`:270`). The final Checkout capture uses the **online purchase** PSP reference and must not use the payment-link `AUTHORISATION` reference (`:417–420`). Inventory/order release and actual capture outcome remain merchant-system and payment-lifecycle responsibilities.
