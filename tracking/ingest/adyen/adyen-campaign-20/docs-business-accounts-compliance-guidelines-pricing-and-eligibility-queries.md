# Retrieval checks — pricing and eligibility compliance

## Q1 — purpose and prerequisites

**Question:** What does this guide say a Business Accounts platform must disclose about fees and eligibility?

**Located answer:** The body calls for clear pricing and eligibility explanations to users, plus platform terms and a fee schedule that explain platform fees alongside Adyen agreements (raw lines 21-28). For Business Accounts and Issuing, additional platform fees are conditional on disclosure before enrollment, notification/agreement with Adyen before enrollment, continued user access to costs and a reasonable, actual-cost basis (raw lines 41-46). In general, platforms must not add eligibility criteria beyond Adyen's; if additional platform criteria exist, they should be submitted for Adyen review of proper user disclosure and must be clearly presented before offer without being discriminatory, unfair or deceptive (raw lines 52-59). The page disclaims legal advice (raw line 19).

## Q2 — material boundary or conflict

**Question:** Can the page be read as a universal pricing rule or proof of present Business Accounts eligibility?

**Located answer:** No. The captured 2024-modified page mixes product and region scopes: its metadata and opening description say Capital although the body addresses Business Accounts (raw lines 6-24); EU/US written fee-increase notice is at least 30 days, UK at least 60 days (raw lines 30-32); the same-price instant-payment statement applies to Business Accounts in EU/UK (raw lines 48-50); and the onboarding questionnaire is UK-only with product labels per row (raw lines 61-82). The eligibility section says platforms generally must not add criteria beyond Adyen's, with a conditional submission path for additional platform criteria (raw lines 52-59). Current legal requirements, eligibility and Adyen fee approval need separate evidence.
