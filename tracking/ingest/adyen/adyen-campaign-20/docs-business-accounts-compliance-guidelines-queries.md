# Business Accounts compliance guidelines query probes

## Q1 — What is this page for, and when does Adyen's material pre-approval instruction apply?

It is a 2022-10-26-modified, 2026-09-15-captured **overview** of Adyen compliance-guideline topics for platforms offering Business Accounts; it expressly is not legal advice (raw lines 6–21). The page says platforms must follow Adyen guidelines on UI, communications and reporting, and its table routes to detailed topic guides (lines 21, 29–31, 50–110). **After** Adyen approves the financial-product offering, materials must be submitted before publication/distribution **where the Compliance Guidelines require pre-approval of that material**; requested changes and resubmission are conditional (lines 33–42). New platforms share with their Adyen contact; existing ones use the stated email, and material review is also required annually, for periodic review and on request (lines 35–48). This does not mean every artifact automatically needs pre-approval, nor that this captured page approves any specific offering. ## Q2 — Which table items have jurisdiction, timing or evidence boundaries, and does the overview provide current legal advice or live account proof?

The captured table says the UI must not present the platform as a bank, and lists Business Account request, KYC, terms, consent and account-information access; the payee-confirmation notice is marked **UK/EU**, and the Depositor Information Sheet **EU** (raw lines 54–70). It also lists complaint acknowledgement and escalation plus quarterly reports, approval of user fees/credits and eligibility criteria, and daily fraud reports (lines 88–98). The record row specifies keeping named consent/UI/statement/communications copies for **at least five years after termination of the financial-product offering**, not a universal five-year period for every record (lines 99–105). France intermediary registration, website disclaimer and annual renewal are conditional on being registered in France **and** offering Business Accounts to French users (lines 106–110). This 2022-modified guideline map is explicitly not legal advice and cannot establish current requirements, offering approval or live account opening (lines 11, 19).
