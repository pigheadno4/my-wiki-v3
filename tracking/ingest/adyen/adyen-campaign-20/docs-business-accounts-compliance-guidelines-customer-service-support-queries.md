# Retrieval checks — Business Accounts customer support

## Q1 — What support must the platform provide, and is the critical-support requirement a 24/7 response guarantee?

Answer: The 2024-modified guide assigns the platform first- and second-line support, a way to submit requests and obtain resolution support, and Adyen's contact role for customer communications (pinned raw lines 19–39). It calls for multiple clearly communicated, free channels, live support suited to users, and human touchpoints for complex needs or merchant request in digital/chatbot support (lines 40–46). The 24/7 requirement is specifically the ability to **raise** queries or notifications about possible unauthorized use, fraud, or loss/theft of credentials or cards (line 47); the page gives no continuously staffed response or resolution guarantee. ## Q2 — What are the retention trigger and jurisdiction limits, and does this prove legal compliance?

Answer: The row **requests** records of all support queries and responses for five years from termination of the Adyen Service **and** the user's account closure, unless longer platform retention requirements apply (pinned raw line 50). It does not say five years from each query or explain differing trigger dates. Front-line training is `should`; quality monitoring is `required` (lines 48–50). The additional vulnerability measures are explicitly headed UK and Ireland and cover reasonable assistance, staff understanding, a prominent notice with standard contact channels, human touchpoints and assistive-technology compatibility (lines 52–62). The page disclaims legal advice (line 19), and its captured text does not prove current compliance or actual support operation.
