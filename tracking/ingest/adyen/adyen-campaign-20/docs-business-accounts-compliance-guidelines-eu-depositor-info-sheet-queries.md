# Retrieval checks — EU depositor information sheet

## Q1 — What protection and limits does the captured EU sheet describe?

Answer: The Adyen N.V. EU sheet names the Dutch statutory Deposit Guarantee Scheme and EUR 100,000 per depositor per credit institution (pinned raw lines 23–24). Deposits at the same institution or trademarks count together (lines 25, 35). It says some deposits are excepted and the institution can confirm whether a product is covered, including on the statement if covered (line 32). Joint accounts generally have a per-depositor limit, but partnership/association/similar groups without legal personality are treated as one depositor for aggregation (lines 26, 36). This disclosure alone cannot confirm coverage for a specific Business Account or depositor. ## Q2 — What does the sheet say about reimbursement currency/timing, and what are the delivery or current-law boundaries?

Answer: The captured table says reimbursement is in euros within 10 working days after institution failure (pinned raw lines 27–28); its note says the period will gradually move to seven days, mentions an interim basic-needs amount on request, and warns claim rights may expire (line 37). That dated text is not proof of a current deadline or actual reimbursement. The sheet contains no platform delivery or consent procedure (lines 15–37); the separate Business Accounts user-interface source owns any such captured instruction.
