# C20 Business Accounts complaints retrieval checks

## Q1 — What counts as a complaint, and what does the process say about timing and records?

Route: `wiki/index.md` → `wiki/adyen-index.md` (`[[adyen-business-accounts]]` and Business Accounts source catalog) → `wiki/concepts/adyen-business-accounts.md` → candidate `wiki/sources/adyen/source-adyen-docs-business-accounts-compliance-guidelines-customer-complaints.md` → `raw/adyen/docs/business-accounts/compliance-guidelines/customer-complaints/2026-09-15.md`.

The page covers platforms offering Adyen financial services and disclaims legal advice (raw lines 19–21). A complaint is dissatisfaction about Adyen products/services/terms/communications/actions where response or resolution is expected; substance rather than the word used, tone or channel governs (21–23). An ordinary assistance, technical, refund or chargeback inquiry becomes a complaint if it alleges unfair treatment or dissatisfaction (25–33). The platform must identify complaints across channels and provide guidance, investigation and updates (35–53). Adyen **expects** acknowledgement within five business days, resolution within ten business days from submission and notice if longer (55–59). The page states durable, accessible retention of inquiry/complaint responses for five years from its combined Service-termination and user-account-closure trigger unless a longer platform period applies (61–63).

## Q2 — What must be escalated, who responds, and which UK/Ireland conditions are additional?

Security and regulatory/legal complaints are to be reported to Adyen within one to two **working** days (raw 65–79). The separate escalation procedure says to provide information as soon as possible and no later than two **business** days after receipt, respond to Adyen's further-information request within two business days, and relay Adyen's final response to the user within guideline timelines (102–107). The type table assigns direct legal-action response to Adyen while the platform handles the complaint aspect unless input is needed; Adyen directly handles listed privacy responses; for regulatory referrals Adyen responds to the regulator or ombudsman, while the platform resolves the user's complaint unless it needs Adyen input for a substantive response (86–100). The platform handles the listed financial-crime and technical complaints (98–100). For **UK and Ireland customers**, the page additionally requires a reasoned decision notice within five working days after investigation ends, an initial acknowledgement that already states the user may be entitled to refer the complaint to the relevant ombudsman if dissatisfied with the platform's or Adyen's final response or if the complaint remains unresolved after 15 working days, plus the relevant contact details (121–125); the separate general expectation is acknowledgement within five business days (55–59). The UK/Ireland section also requires updates at intervals of at most 15 business days from receipt and anticipated-timeframe/referral notice after 35 working days unresolved (139–143). A BACS-origin Adyen request has a two-business-day platform information response (145–149). The UK contact block carries a six-month statement (132–137), not a general rule for all jurisdictions. This Business Accounts page's substantive body matches the separate Platforms financial-products capture; the path and See also links differ. Both are historical compliance guidance, not current legal advice or proof of fulfilment.
