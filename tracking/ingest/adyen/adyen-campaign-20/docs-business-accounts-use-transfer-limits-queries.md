# C20 transfer-limit retrieval checks

## Q1 — Which limit is configured where, and when does it affect an outgoing transfer?

Route: `wiki/index.md` → `wiki/adyen-index.md` (`[[adyen-business-accounts]]`) → `wiki/concepts/adyen-business-accounts.md` → candidate `wiki/sources/adyen/source-adyen-docs-business-accounts-use-transfer-limits.md` → `raw/adyen/docs/business-accounts/use-transfer-limits/2026-09-15.md`.

The guide calls a balance-platform limit a default across its balance accounts and a balance-account limit specific to one account linked to an Adyen business account; user configuration at account level requires SCA (raw lines 28–33). The overview says the latter overrides any platform default, while the detailed account source `[[source-adyen-docs-business-accounts-use-transfer-limits-balance-accounts]]` narrows override to matching `scope` and `transferType` and provides an effective-limit readback route. The account-level limit may be pending SCA or scheduled before active (49–56), and an active limit checks an outgoing external-bank request's amount; exceeding the limit fails the transfer request (35–40). `scope` is per-transfer or per-day total, and `transferType` is instant-only or all (58–65). Missing start date defaults to request time; missing end date remains until override (42–47). The EU option language is an attributed 2026 snapshot, not current legal advice (26).

## Q2 — What status, overlap, calculation and decline boundaries matter?

The four documented limit statuses are `pendingSCA`, `scheduled`, `active` and `inactive` (raw lines 49–56). This overview says only one pendingSCA or scheduled limit may share a `scope`/`transferType` combination at a moment and allows different combinations; once a limit is active, another scheduled/pending limit with that same combination may be created for later override (65–69). The detailed balance-account guide's `startsAt` table instead says unqualified that no more than one limit can be scheduled in the future (its raw line 134; `[[source-adyen-docs-business-accounts-use-transfer-limits-balance-accounts]]`). These statements conflict for concurrent future limits with different combinations, so neither a definitive API-wide cap nor permission can be inferred. The example's TRLI_1/TRLI_1 comparison is a typo (71–89). The calculation section says authorized amounts count even before capture and are released after expiry/cancellation (91–95), but does not reconcile that authorization/capture model with the outgoing-transfer amount check (35–40); do not infer complete accounting or bank receipt. Declines can be inspected through Transfer webhooks with `transactionRulesResult` and `reason: declinedByTransactionRule`, or Customer Area Balances > Transfers (99–110). A configured `active` limit is a rule status, not proof of an executed or received transfer.
