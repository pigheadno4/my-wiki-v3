# Vulnerable users query probes

## Q1 — What is this guide for, who must receive it, and what jurisdiction and legal boundary apply?

It is a 2026-09-15-modified and captured Adyen compliance-guideline instruction page titled “Vulnerable users (UK and Ireland only)” for Adyen partners' platform staff to identify and assist users who may be vulnerable (raw lines 6–21). It expressly says the page is not legal advice (line 19). Platforms are required to give the instructions to all staff who directly interact with users, including customer support, direct-contact sales/marketing and their managers; platforms must ensure in-scope staff read and understand them and should seek Adyen clarification if unclear (lines 29–37). B2B examples include sole proprietors and small merchants, and vulnerability may be temporary (lines 56–69). The page's historical jurisdiction and staff instructions do not determine current law or an individual's status. ## Q2 — Do observed cues justify a diagnosis or unrestricted recording, and which actions are required versus guidance?

No. The four drivers and examples are possible vulnerability indicators; BRUCE **can help** identify possible decision-making difficulties (raw lines 41–54, 77–114). TEXAS instructs staff to explain the intended use of disclosed information and ask explicit consent before noting and saving it; IDEA is for seeking more context when needed (lines 124–143). The page says customers generally **should** only have to share vulnerability information once; their details **must** be recorded; identified or shared indicators **should** be recorded using the listed tags; and a record **must** be attached to the customer file; notes should contain only user-shared facts, with no speculative diagnosis or condition name unless the user explicitly supplied it (lines 171–200). It says to consult internal privacy/legal teams on compliant recording, gives conditional follow-up **if necessary**, and **recommends** periodic review (lines 200–204). Staff examples do not prove a user has a condition or create a guaranteed payment or account outcome.
