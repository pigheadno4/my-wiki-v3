# Retrieval checks — financial-products user interface compliance

## Q1 — purpose and prerequisites

**Question:** What UI obligations does this dated page describe for a platform offering Business Accounts?

**Located answer:** The general platform section calls for a product-request route, KYC data, required agreements/terms, affirmative consent, ongoing accepted-term access and free continuing account/product access to transactions and fees (raw lines 21-35). Hosted onboarding places KYC fields, agreements and terms on Adyen's hosted UI, while components may embed certain requirements (raw line 27). The Business Accounts terms and UI rows add a regional user agreement/privacy statement, balance/transaction details, references and region-specific bank identifiers (raw lines 45-67). The page says it is not legal advice (raw line 19).

## Q2 — material boundary or failure

**Question:** Which disclosures and consent controls are conditional, and can this page alone establish a legally complete flow?

**Located answer:** No. The page disclaims legal advice (raw line 19) and prohibits preselected read/understood confirmation or presenting the platform as a bank (raw lines 37-39). Its Ireland-only wording, EU Depositor Information Sheet timing, EU/UK payee notices, UK BACS-only mandate/refund functions, and US FDIC/Regulation GG controls are jurisdiction or product conditional (raw lines 41-43, 69-118). A US internet-gambling `Yes` response must not proceed under its dated row (raw lines 115-118). The embedded procedure restricts consent to an Authorized Signatory, requires evidence retention and expressly excludes other potentially required agreement disclosures (raw lines 129-154). Current obligations and actual user consent require separate evidence.
