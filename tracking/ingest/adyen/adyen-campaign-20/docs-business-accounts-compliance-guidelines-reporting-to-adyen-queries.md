# Query checks — Business Accounts reporting to Adyen

## Q1 — Purpose, scope and reporting routes

**Query:** What does this Business Accounts page say a platform reports to Adyen, through which routes and on what schedule?

**Answer:** The 2024-modified, 2026-09-15-captured, expressly non-legal guide describes event notification for security/regulatory/legal complaints within 1–2 business days, using its dedicated template, and separate support-line escalation for listed support, fraud, statement, breach and user-noncompliance circumstances. It says API-notifiable events such as account block/termination need no additional reporting for that notification. For relevant Accounts and Issuing services, it calls for daily fraud incident/dispute counts and reasons through the Disputes API or an Adyen-communicated email route. Complaints related to Adyen products are reported quarterly via Adyen's template to the stated complaint address by 10 January/April/July/October, subject to applicable law; the cycle starts on first live-user onboarding. This is dated guidance, not confirmation of a platform's present legal duties or reporting completion.

## Q2 — Jurisdiction, records and submitted data

**Query:** For UK and Ireland vulnerable users, does the platform send all personal CRM details in the quarterly report, and is the annual pattern report the same thing?

**Answer:** The UK/Ireland section first says quarterly complaint reporting must contain three data points: the total number of vulnerable users, totals broken down by vulnerability category, and outcomes of requests for additional support. The table separately tells the platform to record individual vulnerability categories and support-request outcomes in its CRM. A later sentence says only total figures for vulnerable users are required. These statements leave tension about the detail to submit, especially the support-outcomes row; they do not establish that individual CRM details must be sent or that a single overall count suffices. The same quarterly deadlines and recipient apply. Specific vulnerability patterns believed to need Adyen's attention are shared annually. The section is UK/Ireland-scoped and the page disclaims legal advice.
