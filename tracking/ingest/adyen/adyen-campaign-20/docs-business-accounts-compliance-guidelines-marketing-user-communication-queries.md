# Retrieval checks — Business Accounts marketing collateral

## Q1 — What review and presentation rules does this captured page give for Business Accounts collateral?

Answer: It treats platform web/UI pages, user communications, statements, ads, press and app-store material as collateral (pinned raw lines 21–36), calls for relevant bank disclosures, and says all financial-product collateral must be reviewed and pre-approved by Adyen before publication or distribution (lines 38–47). It calls for plain, audience-appropriate, accurate and substantiated claims, attributed testimonials with written permission, and legible accessible disclosures (lines 49–86). The 8-point paper and 10-point online sizes are explicitly recommended (line 82). A specific asset is not shown as submitted or approved.
