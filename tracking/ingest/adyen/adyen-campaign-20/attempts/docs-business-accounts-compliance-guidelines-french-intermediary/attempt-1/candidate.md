## Overview

This 2026-09-15 Adyen Business Accounts compliance-guideline page describes an ORIAS intermediary-registration path for a platform incorporated and registered in France that offers Adyen business accounts to French users. It expressly disclaims legal advice. Its opening description instead says the purpose is to offer Capital in France, while the operative trigger, registration section and post-approval website wording refer to Business Accounts. Treat that as an internal product-label conflict, not authority to apply the Business Accounts instructions to Capital.

## Key takeaways

- The page states a conjunctive France trigger: a platform offers Adyen business accounts to French users **and** is incorporated and registered in France. Its requirements table further frames this as an Adyen for Platforms integration offering financial products and says the limitation applies to France-registered platforms serving French users. This historical guideline does not determine a particular platform's current legal status or obligation.
- It describes professional-capacity proof for at least one director: recent relevant employment experience, a specified diploma, or approved training, with listed conviction and disciplinary exclusions. The exact thresholds, diploma codes and legal references are in the raw guide and require current authoritative legal confirmation before action.
- The page maps Level I, II and III IOBSP intermediary categories to specified NAF codes. It estimates ORIAS validation usually takes less than two months and calls for annual renewal; the estimate is not approval or permission to offer accounts.
- The described registration sequence includes a recent kbis extract, director capacity proof, ORIAS legal-person account and IOB declarations, Adyen's Attestation de Mandat after receiving the platform's SIREN and legal name, upload and fees. It instructs a website disclaimer only after ORIAS approval email. The page's choice declaring that no funds are entrusted to the intermediary is a proposed registration declaration, not independent evidence about a specific platform's funds flow.
- The page is not legal advice, evidence of an ORIAS filing or approval, a live Business Account, or proof of present eligibility. The `See also` Capital compliance link is navigation only.
