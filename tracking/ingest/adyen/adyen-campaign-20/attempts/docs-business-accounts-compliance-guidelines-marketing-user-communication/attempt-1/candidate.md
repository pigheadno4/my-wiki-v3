## Overview

This [[adyen]] Business Accounts compliance-guideline page, last modified 2023-12-14 and captured 2026-09-15, describes Adyen's review and presentation rules for platform collateral, then gives an EU/UK/US Business Accounts matrix. Its description and opening sentence say "Capital offering" despite that Business Accounts section; preserve this source-label mismatch. The page expressly disclaims legal advice. It does not establish approval of a particular asset, current legal wording or product availability. See [[adyen-business-accounts]] and [[adyen-financial-products]].

## Key takeaways

- Collateral includes user-facing platform web and UI pages, communications, statements, ads, press material and app-store descriptions/screenshots. The page says platform collateral must follow product principles and relevant bank disclosures. For financial products, Adyen review and pre-approval precede publication or distribution. Substantive revisions and additional post-go-live collateral go back for approval; the stated exception covers typo/grammar corrections and changes that do not affect product/service description or marketing. This is a review requirement, not proof a specific item was approved. [Raw: lines 21–47]
- General principles call for plain, audience-appropriate and nonmisleading language about terms, benefits, limits, availability and risks; substantiated dated claims; clear distinction among services; real attributed testimonials with written permission and paid-testimonial disclosure. The page cautions against unsupported absolutes and unqualified `free`, `pre-approved`, `guaranteed`, `protected` or `secure` phrasing. It also addresses legible, accessible disclosures and prominent direct links. Its 8-point paper/10-point online font sizes are recommendations, not requirements. [Raw: lines 49–86]
- The dated region matrix bars platform-as-bank claims across EU, UK and US. Its `bank account` terminology restriction is checked only for the UK; separate EU, UK and US rows give different institution and deposit/e-money/protection disclosures. The EU row refers to Dutch deposit guarantee coverage of eligible funds, the UK row says the e-money is not a deposit and lacks FSCS protection, and the US row says Adyen Business Bank Accounts are not FDIC insured while describing a conditional cash-sweep possibility. Apply only the relevant checked column; none certifies current account-level protection. [Raw: lines 99–111]
- Outside the Business Accounts matrix, the page also gives UK-only provider-role wording and an Ireland-only webpage statement for visitors based in Ireland. These are dated source examples, not permission to publish or legal advice. [Raw: lines 88–97]
