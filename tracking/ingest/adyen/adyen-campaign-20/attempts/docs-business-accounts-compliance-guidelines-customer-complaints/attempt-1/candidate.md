## Overview

This Business Accounts path captures Adyen's 2024-modified complaints and escalation guideline on 2026-09-15. The page expressly applies its guidance to platforms offering Adyen financial services, not only Business Accounts, and expressly says it is **not legal advice**. Its body matches the separately captured Platforms financial-products complaints guide; the canonical path and child-guide links differ. It describes the guideline, not proof of a platform's actual compliance or a current jurisdiction-specific legal determination.

## Key takeaways

- A complaint is dissatisfaction about Adyen products, services, terms, communications or actions where a response or resolution is expected. Substance governs, whether or not the user says “complaint,” and oral and written channels count. Routine information, technical-assistance and fee-refund/chargeback requests are examples outside that definition **unless** they allege unfair treatment or express dissatisfaction.
- The platform must offer accessible support, identify complaints across support channels, investigate and update the user. A complaint form may be required, with a link in the channel where the user raised the issue. Adyen **expects** acknowledgement within five business days, resolution within ten business days from submission, and notice if resolution takes longer. The page separately requires durable, accessible records of support inquiries, complaints and responses for five years from the stated Adyen Service termination and user-account closure trigger, unless the platform's retention rule is longer.
- Security and regulatory/legal complaints must be reported to Adyen within **one to two working days**. The escalation procedure separately says to supply information as soon as possible and no later than **two business days** after receipt, reply to further Adyen information requests within two business days, and deliver Adyen's prepared response to the user within guideline timelines. The role table gives Adyen direct response for legal actions, data-privacy matters and regulator/ombudsman referrals in the listed circumstances; it assigns platform handling/resolution for financial-crime and technical issues.
- The **UK and Ireland** section adds a reasoned decision notice within five working days after the platform concludes its investigation, qualified ombudsman-referral wording in acknowledgement when the user is dissatisfied or the complaint remains unresolved after 15 working days, updates at intervals no longer than 15 business days from receipt, and a timeframe/referral notice if unresolved after 35 working days. It also states a two-business-day platform information response when Adyen receives a BACS complaint. These are additional captured instructions, not general timelines for all countries.

- Raw lines 19–33: disclaimer, scope, complaint definition, inquiry examples and dissatisfaction exception. `## Complaint handling process` (35–63): access, identification, forms, investigation, Adyen involvement, general **expected** response times and recordkeeping trigger.
