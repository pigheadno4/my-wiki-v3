## Overview

This UK-and-Ireland-only instruction page, last modified and captured 2026-09-15, gives Adyen partners' platform staff a process for recognizing and supporting users who may be in vulnerable circumstances when interacting about financial services. It states that it is an overview of Adyen compliance guidelines, **not legal advice**. Its B2B examples include sole proprietors and small merchants; vulnerability may change over time. This is a staff-support and recordkeeping guide, not evidence of a particular user's condition, current regulatory coverage, account eligibility or completed financial operation.

## Key takeaways

- The page says platforms **are required** to provide these instructions to all staff who interact directly with users, including customer-support staff, sales/marketing staff communicating with users and their managers. Platforms **must** ensure in-scope staff read and understand them, and **should** seek Adyen clarification when unclear. It also encourages adequate agent time and support for staff handling distressing cases.
- Adyen's definition concerns users especially susceptible to harm due to personal circumstances. The four drivers are health, life events, resilience and capability. The page gives possible individual, behavioral, situational and organizational indicators, and says the BRUCE method **can help** identify possible decision-making difficulty. These are prompts for attentive support, not diagnoses or proof that an individual lacks capacity.
- The staff sequence is initial empathetic contact; observe possible indicators; support the user with **TEXAS** (thank, explain use of information, explicit consent to record, ask, signpost/refer) and, when more context is needed, **IDEA** (impact, duration, experience, assistance); tailor communication and consider escalation or country-appropriate external help. The page's examples and scripts guide conversations; they do not create automatic entitlements to a specific outcome.
- For documentation, the page says customers **should** generally need to explain vulnerability only once. Their details **must** be recorded so other staff can meet their needs; identified or shared vulnerability indicators **should** be recorded using the listed tags; and a record **must** be attached to the customer file. Notes should contain facts shared by the user and omit speculative diagnoses or condition names unless explicitly given by that user. It advises consulting internal privacy or legal teams on a recording method that complies with domestic privacy obligations, such as GDPR.
- Follow-up by call or email is conditional **if necessary**; periodic review of interactions and feedback is a recommendation. The page links separately to reporting vulnerable-customer information to Adyen but does not specify that reporting procedure here.
