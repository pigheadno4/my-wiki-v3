## Overview

This 2026-09-15-captured Business Accounts verification reference says Adyen assigned brand-specific Industry Codes (ICs) to represent unique Merchant Category Codes (MCCs) for certain brands. Selection should match the user's brand and business activity. This is a classification and contact route; it does not establish onboarding approval, a Business Account opening, capability enablement or an assigned MCC. The captured body matches the separate Adyen for Platforms brand-specific IC page apart from URL and related-link paths.

## Key takeaways

- Adyen says to contact an Adyen contact or Support if approval for an industry code is needed **before onboarding users**, or if the platform wants to offer Business Accounts to users in **new industries** outside a previous approval process. These are conditional contact triggers; the page gives no approval decision, turnaround, country scope or eligibility outcome.
- The raw says only certain brand-specific IC values are accepted, but the promised values/list are absent from this 32-line capture. No actual code, brand-to-code mapping or acceptance matrix can be recovered from this source. Its See also link points to a separate general industry-codes list; do not invent brand-specific values or assume that choosing a code itself grants approval.

## Detail locators

- Raw lines 15–19: brand, business activity, MCC and IC relationship. Lines 21–24: approval and new-industry contact conditions. Lines 26–32: accepted-values statement with no listed values, then the separate general industry-codes link.
