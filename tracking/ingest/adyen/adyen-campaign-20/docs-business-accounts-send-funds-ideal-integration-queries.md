# iDEAL A2A integration query probes

## Q1 — What is this Business Accounts iDEAL flow for, and what prerequisites does the captured guide state?

It describes a Netherlands iDEAL A2A checkout in which a user pays from an Adyen Business Account through their platform or marketplace, selecting Adyen and the platform, signing in, reviewing payee/amount/source balance account, and authorizing with SCA (raw lines 19, 32–45, 84–92). The 2025-08-22-modified, 2026-09-15-captured guide requires a Business Accounts-capable Adyen for Platforms integration, an Adyen Business Account, the A2A Issuer payments API role, SCA, and redirect or in-app QR integration; the scope is Netherlands platforms/marketplaces and iDEAL only (raw lines 28–41). It also calls for HTTPS redirect URLs and iDEAL screen/wireframe review (raw lines 137–153, 527–540). ## Q2 — Does a details token, SCA challenge, success link or cancel API response prove final payment or cancellation; what failures or conflicts matter?

No. `/payments/details` yields a pending-transaction token valid around 15 minutes for iDEAL (raw lines 182–186). The first confirm yields a 401 SCA challenge, and the second must follow authentication within 10 minutes (raw lines 318–322, 346–352, 434–475). The guide describes a success-path return link, but its prose asks for a `success` object while the example shows `links.success.href` (raw lines 460–475). For cancellation, the guide expressly says the payment is considered canceled when `links.cancel.href` is opened, not merely when `/payments/cancel` returns it (raw lines 484–486, 511–523). Expired/invalid token or payload and an already locked payment are listed errors, including `1069_005` for confirmed, canceled or simultaneous processing (raw lines 672–681). The page is a dated integration snapshot, and none of these intermediate responses alone proves live settlement, bank receipt or current eligibility.
