## Q1 — What retention period and record types does the captured Business Accounts guide describe?

The guide says the platform must keep detailed service records while offering Business Accounts and for at least five years **after termination of the Business Accounts offering**, unless its own retention requirements prescribe longer (raw line 21). Example categories include deployed UI/application/support screenshots, versions of marketing material, user communications and complaint documents, and historical statements generated and made available (lines 23–33). It expressly says this is not legal advice (line 19), so the captured page does not determine current legal requirements or prove records were actually kept.
