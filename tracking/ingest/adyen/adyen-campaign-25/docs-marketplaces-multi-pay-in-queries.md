# Retrieval checks — docs-marketplaces-multi-pay-in

**Q1. What does Marketplace multi pay-in consolidate, and what must be in place?**
It combines Adyen-acquired and third-party pay-in-partner volume in a balance-platform environment, with partner payouts entering a distinct multi pay-in account before user/platform allocation (raw lines 15–19, 36–50). The guide requires an Adyen for Platforms online-payments integration, Checkout and Transfers API credentials, Transfer or Transaction webhooks, and account-holder `sendToBalanceAccount`/`receiveFromBalanceAccount` capabilities; it does not support separate authorization and capture (raw lines 21–34). Adyen says the platform must allocate received source-account funds within 24 hours for a stated regulatory reason (raw lines 36–40). **Q2. Where do funds and refund liability move, and what evidence boundary applies?**
After receiving partner proceeds, the platform uses Checkout `POST /payments` splits to allocate each payment to user and liable accounts; omitting splits books all funds and fees to the liable account (raw lines 52–56). For third-party refunds, the partner generally returns funds to the user's customer and withholds the amount from a later batch, while the platform's Checkout refund debits specified balance accounts and offsets the multi pay-in account (raw lines 79–90). The refund illustration mixes EUR 98.00/EUR 2.00 deductions with a USD 100.00 transfer without explaining conversion (raw lines 95–104); it does not prove a valid rate or a customer refund. The guide is dated service guidance, not evidence of account enablement, booking, payout or customer receipt.
