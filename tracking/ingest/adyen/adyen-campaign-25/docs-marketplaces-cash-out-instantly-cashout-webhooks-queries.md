# Retrieval checks — docs-marketplaces-cash-out-instantly-cashout-webhooks

Q1. Which Marketplace cashout transfer legs and webhook setup track funds, fees, payout and repayment?

A1. Route: [[index]] → [[adyen-index]] → [[adyen-platforms]] → [[source-adyen-docs-marketplaces-cash-out-instantly-cashout-webhooks]] → [[raw/adyen/docs/marketplaces/cash-out-instantly/cashout-webhooks/2026-09-15]]. A cashout for a marketplace user triggers incoming internal `cashoutFunding` to the user's balance account, optional outgoing internal `cashoutFee`, optional outgoing bank `bankTransfer` to a specified transfer instrument, and outgoing internal `cashoutRepayment` (raw lines 19–24, 43–74). The server must accept webhooks and subscribe to Transfer and Transaction webhooks; the guide names transfer.created/updated and transaction.created events (raw lines 26–37). Its TEST v4 example advances EUR 1,000 of sales funds with a EUR 10 fee ahead of scheduled settlement (raw lines 78–115).

Q2. Does booked cashout or payout transfer status prove bank credit, and when is repayment triggered?

A2. Route: [[index]] → [[adyen-index]] → [[adyen-platforms]] → [[source-adyen-docs-marketplaces-cash-out-instantly-cashout-webhooks]] → [[raw/adyen/docs/marketplaces/cash-out-instantly/cashout-webhooks/2026-09-15]]. The optional bank payout's booked transfer and transaction show a debit from the user's balance account; a later transfer update carries `tracking.status: credited` for the transfer instrument. The TEST example is not independent beneficiary-bank receipt evidence (raw lines 881–1121). The opening says repayment transfer is triggered on scheduled settlement day, but its detailed section says the request is created on cashout day and the amount is deducted at settlement; the authorised/booked sample envelopes also retain transfer.created/top-level received (raw lines 24, 1124–1416). The fee destination and the Marketplace versus Platforms example request-body transfer-instrument shape differ (Marketplace raw lines 78–115, 553–664; related Platforms source), so those examples do not settle universal behavior.
