Q1. What is the purpose and what must be ready before capture-time splitting?
A. A Marketplace can send or override split instructions in a Checkout capture request when amounts are learned after authorization, using the authorization PSP reference and a `splits` array (raw lines 19–25, 51–59). The guide requires Checkout API credentials, standard webhook reception plus transfer or transaction webhook subscription, manual capture or capture delay, and a payment method supporting separate captures (lines 27–45). Q2. What material booking and outcome limits apply?
A. Capture-time splits override initial splits, but payment-time splits are recommended for methods without delayed capture; a changed capture amount requires explicit splits or all funds book to the marketplace liable account, and Surcharge/Tip are unsupported at capture (raw lines 25, 43–49, 1678). A `received` capture response is not an outcome: the CAPTURE webhook and per-split transfer/transaction webhooks distinguish request, movement and booked entries (lines 593–666). Format-only validation means an absent or closed-holder target can redirect the full transaction and fees to the liable account, requiring a separate corrective transfer (lines 646–650). The fee and commission example payloads contradict their narrative/account roles, so they are not account-specific booking or payout proof (lines 74–79, 1006–1034, 1594–1638).
