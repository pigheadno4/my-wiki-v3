# Q1 — Purpose, prerequisites, and forward route

The Marketplace page describes Transfer webhooks for an internal balance-account transfer, with `balancePlatform.transfer.created` for initiation and `balancePlatform.transfer.updated` for status changes (raw lines 23–26). The server must accept webhooks and subscribe to Transfer webhooks in test Customer Area (raw lines 33–36). `category=internal`, `type=internalTransfer`, and the outgoing source/incoming target direction identify the two account views (raw lines 28–46). The initiated, authorised/reserved and booked stages appear for each account (raw lines 48–54, 371–885). # Q2 — Boundary, ambiguity, and forward route

The guide labels Payment webhooks deprecated and uses the Transfer-webhook route here (raw lines 19–26). A return is a modification of the original transfer (raw line 905), with nested `modification` states received, authorised and booked (raw lines 909–1170, 1174–1459); the original transfer's top-level booked state is not the return outcome (for example raw lines 977–1002). Two account views at booked return are described at raw lines 1174–1185 and 1338–1352. The EUR 10000 TEST request and EUR 1000 webhook examples differ (raw lines 68–70, 398–400), so they are illustrative rather than a single exact trace. Internal booking and TEST examples do not prove bank payout or live execution.
