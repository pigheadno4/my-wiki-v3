# C25 Marketplace settlement — retrieval checks

**Q1. How and when do split funds reach balance accounts?** The page routes processed transactions through a merchant account, split among users and marketplace, then into corresponding balance accounts (raw lines 15–19). Captured funds enter sales-day batches in the balance account's local time zone, with configurable closing time and settlement after a contract-dependent delay (lines 33–43, 61–70). Managed delays are Adyen-optimized to contractual ETA; custom payouts may override the waiting period (lines 65–68). **Q2. Why might a captured amount differ from available funds or later payout?** Net settlement deducts costs, refunds, chargebacks, reversals and other adjustments, possibly from other sales days (raw lines 45–59). Business days and holidays affect timing (lines 72–93); pass-through is deprecated for new integrations except named methods that follow their own schedules (lines 23–31). If funds are not received within 30 days of capture, Adyen reverses them from the current batch (lines 95–97). Neither capture nor balance settlement proves a bank payout.
