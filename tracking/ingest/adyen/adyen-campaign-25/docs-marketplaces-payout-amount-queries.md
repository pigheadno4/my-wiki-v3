# Q1 — Purpose and prerequisite route

Before scheduled or on-demand payout to a user, Adyen calculates a maximum from the user's balance account (raw lines 17–21). The default cap is available balance; an Adyen-contact-enabled alternative uses the current, already-settled balance, one platform-wide choice for all balance accounts (raw lines 36–43). For that alternative, the platform needs sufficient available reserve-account funds (raw lines 31–34, 95–104). # Q2 — Failure, conflict and evidence boundary

In current-balance mode, if current exceeds available, Adyen blocks the difference in the platform reserve when sufficient; otherwise payout fails (raw lines 110–124). Collateral releases as the user's negative available balance is compensated, or remaining collateral transfers internally from reserve to user after 30 days of negative balance with a webhook (raw lines 157–198). The default method's phrase “subtracts the future balance changes” at raw line 57 conflicts as signed arithmetic with the USD 100 plus USD -20 example's USD 80 result at lines 90–93; read as a reduction by the magnitude, not a validated arithmetic API formula. These calculations do not establish bank payout execution or receipt; follow [[source-adyen-docs-marketplaces-payout-webhooks]] for those states.
