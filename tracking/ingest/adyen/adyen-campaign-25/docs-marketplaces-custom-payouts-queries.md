# C25 Marketplace custom payouts — retrieval checks

**Q1. What does this guide cover and what must be in place?** It routes custom scheduled or on-demand bank transfer requests from a user's balance account to that user's verified bank account, with configurable settlement delay (raw lines 15–33). It requires Adyen for Platforms, a Transfers API Initiate role, Customer Area payout role, user's `sendToTransferInstrument` capability, and account holder/transfer instrument linkage to the same legal entity (lines 35–48). Third-party beneficiary transfers require separate Business Accounts and verification (lines 19–23). **Q2. What route or timing boundary could make a payout interpretation wrong?** Local and cross-border priorities differ; the latter is `crossBorder` only, excludes two USD-to-US-bank user cases, and has uncertain arrival/lifting-fee risks (raw lines 50–80). Cross-border prose demands `numberAndBic`, but the matrix requires or permits IBAN for some destinations, and its CZK-to-Slovakia prose example is outside table currencies (lines 67–80, 117–123). The SEPA narrative cutoff uses 16:30 ET while the table says 16:55 CET (lines 133–138, 176). TEST bank fixtures include `cashout` outside the priority list and do not establish live availability or receipt (lines 194–211, 218–276).
