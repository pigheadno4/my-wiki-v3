Q1. How is a recurring internal balance-account transfer configured, and which prerequisites and amount controls apply?

A sweep pushes funds out or pulls them into a balance account on a schedule (raw lines 17–23). The page lists platform integration, Transfers API initiate role, Customer Area transfer/base roles, Transfer webhooks, Support enablement of the source, and cross-holder `sendToBalanceAccount`/`receiveFromBalanceAccount` capabilities when holders differ (lines 25–42). Create in Customer Area or POST `/balanceAccounts/{balanceAccountId}/sweeps` with destination balance-account counterparty, matching currency and account-time-zone cron schedule; `push` is the default type (lines 44–46, 112–130). The optional trigger threshold, retained target balance, or fixed sweep amount modify the full-available-balance default under their respective conditions (lines 452–460). Q2. Does an active sweep or Customer Area Success status prove funds moved, and which examples should not define the internal destination?

No. POST returns schedule ID/default `active` status and the sweep-created webhook reports creation (raw lines 415–451); resulting internal transfers have separate outgoing/incoming webhook series through `received`, `authorised`, `booked` or failure (lines 819–825). The Events tab calls `Success` merely transfer created (lines 869–877). The core destination is `counterparty.balanceAccountId` (lines 118–123, 140–145), but the optional dynamic-description example uses `transferInstrumentId` and mentions bank statements/payout reporting (lines 489–521, 812–817). The parameter table says `referenceForBeneficiary`, while later text says `remittanceForBeneficiary` (lines 125, 495). These conflicts do not turn the internal sweep into an external payout or prove bank receipt.
