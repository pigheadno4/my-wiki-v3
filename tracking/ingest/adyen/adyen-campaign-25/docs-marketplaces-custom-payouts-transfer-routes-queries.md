# Retrieval checks — docs-marketplaces-custom-payouts-transfer-routes

**Q1. What does the Marketplace transfer-routes guide calculate, and what access and request fields does it require?**
It recommends calculating bank-payout routes before sending funds to a new recipient because banks or regulators may constrain priorities, amounts or required recipient information (raw lines 15–21). An Adyen for Platforms integration and Configuration API credential with Balance Platform BCL role are required (raw lines 23–30). Both `POST /transferRoutes/calculate` variants include `balancePlatform`, three-character `currency`, `category: bank`, and optional `priorities`; one supplies a two-character counterparty `country`, while the other supplies a `counterparty` bank account or transfer instrument, with `balanceAccountId` required when that counterparty contains `transferInstrumentId` (raw lines 36–48, 359–372). **Q2. What do route results establish, and what do they leave open?**
Country results list supported currency, priority and additional requirements; counterparty results also state country (raw lines 301–305, 716–721). TEST examples show route-specific amount limits, address, bank-identification and payment-instrument requirements for illustrated Netherlands requests, not universal limits or live eligibility (raw lines 54–71, 307–357, 378–401, 723–780). The page links to separate scheduled and on-demand payout procedures (raw lines 782–785). A returned route is only a calculation and does not prove that the recipient qualifies, funds are available, a payout was sent/booked, or a bank received it (raw lines 19–21, 301–305, 716–721).
