# docs-marketplaces-payment-methods — worker retrieval checks

Q1. What is this Marketplace payment-method page for, and what must happen before processing?
A. It is a linked catalog of method families the snapshot says Adyen supports for marketplaces (raw lines 15–23, 25–59). Before processing on behalf of users, the marketplace must configure methods the users can accept in Customer Area (line 19). The page links to add and manage guides for the operational steps (lines 61–71). Route: [[index]] → [[adyen-index]] → [[adyen-platforms]] → [[source-adyen-docs-marketplaces-payment-methods]] → [[raw/adyen/docs/marketplaces/payment-methods/2026-09-15]].

Q2. What does a listed method not establish?
A. Names such as PayPal, Apple Pay, iDEAL, Mastercard and Visa are catalog links, not a per-merchant or per-store activation record (raw lines 25–59). This page supplies no variant codes, ecommerce/POS matrix, country/currency eligibility, approval status or successful transaction. The add/manage links point to separate configuration guidance (lines 61–71).
