Q1. How does a Marketplace online payment direct sale, commission and fee booking at authorization, and what prerequisites apply?

A1. Route: [[index]] → [[adyen-index]] → [[adyen-platforms]] → [[source-adyen-docs-marketplaces-split-transactions-split-payments-at-authorization]] → [[raw/adyen/docs/marketplaces/split-transactions/split-payments-at-authorization/2026-09-15]]. The marketplace processes an online customer payment on behalf of a user and places `splits` in Checkout POST `/payments` or `/sessions` (raw lines 19, 36–42). It requires online Checkout integration/UI, Checkout and Transfers credentials, webhook handling, and a valid `receiveFromPlatformPayments` capability for user allocation (raw lines 21–33). `BalanceAccount` directs the sale to a named user balance account, `Commission` goes to the liable account, and `PaymentFee` can charge a selected account; unspecified fees and default `Remainder` go to liable (raw lines 44–55). The TEST example splits USD 80.00 into USD 75.00 sale and USD 5.00 commission, with fee amount determined later (raw lines 57–113).

Q2. When can an authorized split fail to land in the intended account, and what evidence or precedence resolves it?

A2. Route: [[index]] → [[adyen-index]] → [[adyen-platforms]] → [[source-adyen-docs-marketplaces-split-transactions-split-payments-at-authorization]] → [[raw/adyen/docs/marketplaces/split-transactions/split-payments-at-authorization/2026-09-15]]. A non-valid `receiveFromPlatformPayments` capability sends the full amount to liable (raw lines 32–33). Checkout validates split format but not account existence; a missing account or one tied to a closed holder can send the whole transaction and fees to liable despite payment success (raw lines 681–685). The authorization example's `Authorised` response is not booked split proof (raw lines 665–679); each split needs Transfer and Transaction webhook tracking through received, authorised, captured and booked stages (raw lines 687–703, 721–979). The fee/commission sample legs contain destination/sign mismatches (raw lines 1051–1075, 1292–1338, 1515–1669), so the examples are not a reliable joined ledger. Capture-time splits override initial payment splits, but the page warns some methods lack delayed capture support (raw lines 1704–1708). No bank payout or receipt is shown.
