# docs-marketplaces-cash-out-instantly — worker retrieval checks

Q1. What does CashOut do, and which money-movement legs are conditional?
A. It makes a user's pending available balance accessible before ordinary settlement; cashout amount, plus any charged fee, must fit that balance (raw lines 19–35). Possible legs are funding credit to the user balance account, instant bank payout only when a transfer instrument is specified, optional fee debit to the marketplace liable balance account, and later repayment debit when the underlying captured payments settle (lines 41–46). Q2. What availability, timing and evidence limits prevent interpreting CashOut as a completed bank payout?
A. The snapshot lists AU/AUD, EEA/EUR, US/USD and UK/GBP, conditional on the receiving bank supporting instant payouts; an unsupported bank returns funds to pending available, and Australia may see ANZ-rail delays (raw lines 48–59). A rolling 24-hour approved platform maximum and same-account cooldown apply, with pass-through-settlement proceeds excluded (lines 61–69). Full cashout may mean no scheduled Payout report for that account; webhooks track transfers (lines 71–87). The seller example calls January 1 the repayment settlement date despite saying a two-day delay makes funds available January 3 (lines 29–31, 46), so no exact repayment date follows. Request/funding/TEST examples do not prove bank credit.
