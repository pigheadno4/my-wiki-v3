Q1. What is the custom-payouts route and how does it differ from managed payouts or third-party transfers?
A. This 2024-modified, 2026-09-15-captured Adyen for Platforms overview covers platform-controlled settlement delay, scheduled bank payouts, and on-demand bank payouts from that same user's balance account to that user's verified bank account. The payout source identified by this page is the user's balance account. Managed payouts are a separate linked approach; third-party bank transfers are routed to optional Business Accounts with additional verification. These are payout request paths, not proof of actual bank receipt or legal ownership. Raw locators: lines 15–35; source `## Overview` and `## Key takeaways`. Q2. What gates, routing limits, and timing cautions apply to custom payouts?
A. The page requires the Transfers API `TransferService Webservice Initiate role`, Customer Area `Initiate payouts to transfer instruments`, user `sendToTransferInstrument` capability, and account holder/transfer instrument linked to the same legal entity. Local and cross-border payouts to the same bank account need distinct local and `numberAndBic` instruments. Country/currency, bank format, priority, amount and cutoff constrain routing; cross-border uses `crossBorder` priority and has correspondent-bank timing/fee and geographic limits. Its CZK example conflicts with a EUR/GBP/USD cross-border table, and the SEPA cutoff example differs from the table; verify current route details. TEST bank details include return/failure scenarios and `cashout` rows absent from the priority list. This overview does not supply approval/cancellation or bank-receipt proof.
