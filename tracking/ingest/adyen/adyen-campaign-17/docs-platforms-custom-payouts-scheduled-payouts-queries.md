Q1. What is this source's purpose and scope, and what must not be inferred?

Q2. Which prerequisites, time/amount/route rules and event distinctions control scheduled payout interpretation?
- Expected: A Platforms integration, Transfers API `TransferService Webservice Initiate role`, Customer Area `Balance platform base role` and `Configure scheduled payouts`, user `sendToTransferInstrument` capability, and an account holder and transfer instrument linked to the same legal entity are specified. POST `/balanceAccounts/{balanceAccountId}/sweeps` names destination, currency, `schedule.type: cron`, expression evaluated in the balance account's time zone, and `type: push`; the response defaults to active and PATCH can update. By default the available amount is paid out; optional `triggerAmount`, `targetAmount` and `sweepAmount` constrain evaluation, with trigger higher than target and at least sweep amount. A priority list affects fees/speed, needs `category: bank`, and all supplied priorities require valid routes at configuration; execution selects the first route valid for the actual transfer amount, subject to route amount limits. Regional cutoffs affect expected arrival. The source does not specify a universal business-day SLA, manual approval/cancellation, insufficient-funds retry or returned-payout policy. Sweep created/updated events, sweep-execution results, transfer status updates and balance-account deduction transactions are different evidence grains; none alone proves bank receipt. Example requests are test-mode and their USD/EUR currency samples differ.
