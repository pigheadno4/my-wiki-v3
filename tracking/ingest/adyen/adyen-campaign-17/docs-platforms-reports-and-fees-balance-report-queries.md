# Fixed retrieval checks — Balance Platform Balance Report

## Q1. Which balance-account currencies appear, and what does absence mean?

The 2026-09-15 report includes each account-holder/balance-account currency with a nonzero opening balance or any movement during the selected day. A zero-opening account that receives USD 500 and sends USD 500 still appears despite zero closing balance. A nonzero opening balance appears without movement; a zero opening/closing balance with no movement does not appear. An absent row therefore does not show that the account is nonexistent, and the net opening/closing difference cannot reveal offsetting gross movements. ## Q2. What is the report's time and evidence grain?

The report samples 00:00 opening and next-day 00:00 closing balances for the selected day, in the balance platform's configured time zone or CET. Its standard fields identify platform, account holder, balance account, opening/closing currency and amount, dates and time zones, plus reserved columns. The sample labels its time-zone columns Opening Time and Closing Time, while the standard table calls them Opening TimeZone and Closing TimeZone; generated columns may also vary by platform settings. This is an account-currency balance snapshot, not an individual transfer ledger or a live balance. It does not prove payout settlement or beneficiary bank receipt.
