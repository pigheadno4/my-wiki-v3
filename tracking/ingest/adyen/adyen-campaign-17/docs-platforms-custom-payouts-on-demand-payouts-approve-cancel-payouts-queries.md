Q1. How is an additional review triggered and what happens on approval, cancellation, or inaction?
A. In this 2020-modified, 2026-09-15-captured Adyen Platforms custom-payout guide, the platform includes a `review` object in `POST /transfers`. The payout request waits for review. Approval authorizes it and Adyen continues processing; cancellation fails that request; no action within 30 days also fails it and Adyen no longer processes it. This does not show completed bank credit, and the page gives no same-request retry or replacement rule. Raw locators: lines 15–50; source `## Overview` and `## Key takeaways`. Q2. What API permissions, actions, and webhook evidence are documented?
A. The guide requires `TransferService Approval role` on the review API key and Transfer Webhooks. `POST /transfers/approve` and `POST /transfers/cancel` each accept `transferIds` for one to 500 initiated payouts; HTTP 200 is success of that action. A created webhook example marks review required as `received/pending` with approval count 1; updated examples mark approved as `authorised/approved` or canceled as `cancelled/unknown`. The page mentions expiry events but shows no expiry payload. TEST v4 examples and review status are not proof of booked funds or beneficiary bank receipt.
