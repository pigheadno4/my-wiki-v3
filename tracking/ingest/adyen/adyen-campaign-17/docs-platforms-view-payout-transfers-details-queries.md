Q1. What is this document's purpose and scope, and what must not be inferred?
A1. It is a 2026-09-15 Adyen for Platforms guide to viewing payout-related transfer records in Customer Area and through Transfers API `GET /transfers` or `GET /transfers/{id}`, while recommending webhooks for recurring monitoring (raw lines 19–34, 47–49, 475–496). It applies to balance-platform transfer records and its examples include both a bank transfer and an internal transfer (raw lines 270–473, 652–736). A viewed `received`, `authorised` or `booked` status, or a confirmation letter, does not by itself prove the beneficiary bank credited the recipient; the letter confirms Adyen sent a transfer instruction (raw lines 642–650, 738–740). Q2. What is the central capability, what prerequisites and consequential warnings apply?
A2. A user with the **Balance platform base role** can view Transfers in Customer Area, filter one balance platform at a time, search by transfer ID/description and open details; the API retrieval role is **TransferService Webservice Retrieve role** (raw lines 35–58, 475–496). `GET /transfers` exposes date/account filters and pagination, and `GET /transfers/{id}` returns amount, direction, events, reference and current status (raw lines 70–82, 263–268, 642–650). The guide states a less-than-six-month `createdSince`/`createdUntil` interval and at least one unique identifier, but its example prose spans roughly a year and its curl sample uses dates without an account ID; consult the current API schema rather than treating the examples as conclusive (raw lines 74–98). The detail sample is `internalTransfer`, not a beneficiary-bank payout (raw lines 652–735). A booked bank transfer's confirmation PDF requires **Download transfer confirmation letter**; unmasked PII additionally requires **View bank transfers PII**. It confirms instruction sent and may carry ACH/FPS tracing IDs, but does not establish bank acceptance or receipt (raw lines 738–779).
