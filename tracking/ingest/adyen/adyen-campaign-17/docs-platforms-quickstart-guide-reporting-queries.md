Q2. Which date, payout-report and accounting-report constraints matter for reconciliation?
- Expected: Booking date is the event date; value date is when a balance account is credited/debited and funds become available there. Settlement delay can separate sales day and value date, and the two-business-day illustration is not a general timing guarantee. BPPR is a daily report of scheduled payouts initiated that day and their constituent transactions; it supports managed and custom scheduled payouts, excludes on-demand payouts and cashouts, requires the sales-day settlement model, and gives incorrect data when more than one scheduled payout occurs on a day. BPAR tracks platform balance changes across payments, transfers, payouts, fees and invoice deductions; Adyen recommends ingesting BPARs to build a ledger while removing nonmovement events such as Received, Authorized, Canceled and Expired. The Balance Report can pair with BPAR for balance reconciliation. Distinguish payment-processing reports from balance-platform reports, and never infer beneficiary bank receipt from an initiated payout report row or balance-account value date.
