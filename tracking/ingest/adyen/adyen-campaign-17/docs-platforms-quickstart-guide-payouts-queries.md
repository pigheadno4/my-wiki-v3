# Fixed retrieval checks — Platforms payouts quickstart

Q1. What payout path and product choices does this quickstart describe, and what must not be inferred?
- Expected: The 2026-09-15 Adyen for Platforms quickstart describes payment funds settling to a platform user's balance account, then an external payout to that user's verified bank-account transfer instrument. Managed payouts provide Adyen-managed automatic scheduling; custom payouts include scheduled and on-demand timing controlled by the platform; cashout is a distinct path for pending funds before settlement. Payout webhooks can supply events and an estimated bank arrival time after trigger. A shopper payment, balance-account credit, transfer request, ETA or dashboard view does not prove beneficiary bank receipt, account-specific eligibility, legal fund ownership or merchant-of-record status. This guide is separate from the legacy Payout API.

Q2. What timing, available-funds, partner-method and missed-payout conditions qualify the route?
- Expected: Payout timing/amount depend on local balance-account sales day, settlement delay, value date, route and priority. The snapshot states a default midnight-to-midnight local sales day and default two-business-day settlement delay, with configurable closing time up to 07:00 and conditional premium reduction of delay. Managed/custom scheduled payout amounts follow available-funds calculation; on-demand payout requests specify amount. Custom scheduled initiation should follow account credit and precede route cutoffs that vary by location, currency and priority; the UK/US priority suggestions are dated guidance, not guarantees. For methods without sales-day settlement, credit follows Adyen's receipt from the partner; for partner-settled methods such as Titres-restaurant, no value date is shown and users arrange payout with the partner. A single-user miss may relate to missing/unverified KYC; multiple-user misses may relate to Adyen or recipient-bank incidents, requiring investigation and support rather than an assumed retry. A supplied statement description is passed to the bank but this source does not prove how it appears to a specific beneficiary.
