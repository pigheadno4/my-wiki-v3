Q1. What is this document's purpose and scope, and what must not be inferred?
A1. It is a 2026-09-15 Adyen for Platforms description of per-currency balance categories in a balance account: current Balance, Available, Pending, Pending available and Reserved (raw lines 15–30). It explains how settled transactions, future settlement and holds relate to transfer availability, and points to API, webhook and Customer Area views (raw lines 23–40). It does not prove a named user's actual account balance, legal fund ownership, payout eligibility, payment settlement or beneficiary-bank receipt; the dated tables are illustrations (raw lines 42–104). Q2. What is the central capability, what prerequisites and consequential warnings apply?
A2. For each currency, Balance/current sums settled transactions; Pending sums future-settling transactions and can have either sign; Reserved includes authorized outgoing transfers before booking and blocked funds (raw lines 19–30). Available is the transferable balance, and Pending available is the balance expected to become available after pending settlement, but both printed formulas conflict with the example tables. Example 1's EUR 100 current, EUR 20 pending and EUR 30 reserved yields EUR 50 under the literal Available formula, while the table says EUR 70. Example 2's EUR 50 current and EUR -70 pending yields EUR 50 under that formula, while the Available table says EUR -20; its literal Pending available formula `min(-70, -70 + -20)` yields EUR -90, while the Pending available table says EUR -70 (raw lines 24–28, 58–64, 78–86). Verify both current formulas before using them for payout or top-up decisions. The page suggests observing multi-currency balances through `GET /balanceAccounts/{id}`, `balancePlatform.balanceAccount.balance.updated` and Customer Area, and optionally configuring notifications to guide top-ups/internal transfers; those are separate actions, not automatic financial outcomes (raw lines 32–40, 106–116).
