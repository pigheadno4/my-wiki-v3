# Fixed retrieval checks — Balance Platform Fee Report

## Q1. What does this report show, when is it available, and how is it obtained?

The 2026-09-15 Platforms page describes a monthly Balance Platform Fee Report available on the first day of the month, covering payment and processing fees incurred by balance-platform users, including scheme, interchange, chargeback and refund fee categories. The page says it can be generated in Customer Area on a schedule or manually for a month and gives the balanceplatform_fee_report_YYYY_MM.csv pattern. Its sample/standard columns organize rows by Billing Month, BalancePlatform, AccountHolder, BalanceAccount, Fee Type, Fee Name, Currency Code and Fee Amount, with optional account descriptions/references. The sample has no transaction ID, and generated columns can vary by platform settings; the page does not prove that a specific file exists or was delivered. ## Q2. Can a fee row identify the debited account or replace individual fee and invoice evidence?

No. The guide explicitly says the report shows where a fee was incurred, not where billed; a fee that cannot be tied to a platform user appears on the liable account. It recommends API responses and webhooks for current individual assessed-fee status and monthly reconciliation of those fees against the report and invoice. The sample's positive and negative Fee Amount values do not define a universal debit/credit rule, and its Currency header differs from the standard-column name Currency Code. A report row cannot by itself identify the actual fee-debit account, prove a booked balance movement, or establish bank payout or accounting finality.
