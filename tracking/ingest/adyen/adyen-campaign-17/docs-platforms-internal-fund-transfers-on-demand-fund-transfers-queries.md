# Fixed retrieval checks — On-demand internal fund transfers

## Q1. Who can initiate an internal transfer, between which accounts, and with what request fields?

This 2020-modified, 2026-09-15-captured Platforms guide covers on-demand push or internal direct debit between balance accounts through Customer Area or Transfers API. It requires a Platforms integration, source-account enablement, the documented API or Customer Area roles, and Transfer-webhook setup. When source and target accounts have different account holders, the sender needs sendToBalanceAccount and receiver receiveFromBalanceAccount. Customer Area selects source balance account, counterparty balanceAccount and target ID, currency and amount. POST /transfers requires amount (currency/value), balanceAccountId as source, counterparty.balanceAccountId as target and category internal; references are optional. ## Q2. When is a request booked, and what can fail or be cancelled?

The sample API response has status received and reason pending, so HTTP 2XX does not prove a debit or credit. Adyen performs capability and internal checks; optional review.numberOfApprovalsRequired supports one additional team approval before processing. For a future date within 30 days, a request remains received/pending until the execution date, when Adyen checks source available funds and any team approval. The page says it makes one processing attempt; error yields failed with a webhook, and cancellation is available before that date. The guide describes outgoing and incoming webhook series with received, authorised, booked or failed states and two Customer Area entries, one for each balance account. Its booked-flow sentence says funds go to a transfer instrument, conflicting with the required balance-account counterparty; treat the route as an internal balance transfer and verify both observed sides. The prose and field table also differ on the timezone default for future execution. No bank payout or automatic retry follows from these examples.
