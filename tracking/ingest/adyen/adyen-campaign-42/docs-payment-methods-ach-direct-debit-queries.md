## Q2 — Central capability, prerequisites and warnings

`source-adyen-docs-payment-methods-ach-direct-debit` requires a US registered entity, Nacha-required consumer-account validation, a correct SEC code, and a signed customer mandate before one-off or recurring initiation. For B2B, it **advises** business customers to allowlist Adyen's ACH Company ID to avoid R29 (raw `raw/adyen/docs/payment-methods/ach-direct-debit/2026-09-15.md:23-25`, `:35-63`). It supports one-off and recurring but **advises against one-off** due to chargeback risk; ACH has no real-time authorization, and the page says unauthorized/revoked-authorization consumer disputes may occur up to two years after settlement (same raw `:27-29`). Reversals yield a `CHARGEBACK` webhook, cannot be defended, and retry depends on reason code (same raw `:65-74`). Refunds require settlement and a merchant bank-refund role, are delayed three days, and are canceled if a chargeback starts within that period (same raw `:76-80`). Terminal returns can block a bank account Adyen-wide; the raw lists specific R codes, immediate failure signals, and documented details-update or bank-documentation-plus-Support paths (same raw `:82-128`).
