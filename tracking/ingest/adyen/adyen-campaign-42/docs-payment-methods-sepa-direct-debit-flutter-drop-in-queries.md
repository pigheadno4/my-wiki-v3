## Q2 — Central capability, prerequisites and warnings

`source-adyen-docs-payment-methods-sepa-direct-debit-flutter-drop-in` requires an existing Flutter Drop-in Sessions flow at Checkout API v68+ with Support setup, or an existing Advanced flow with Customer Area setup. The Sessions prerequisite link mistakenly targets Advanced; Advanced says `action.type: direct` but links a redirect-handling anchor (raw `raw/adyen/docs/payment-methods/sepa-direct-debit/flutter-drop-in/2026-09-15.md:25-47`, `:892-908`). Sessions has no extra ordinary `/sessions` or configuration fields; Advanced requires account owner and unencrypted IBAN, plus billing address conditionally for listed non-EEA SEPA IBAN origins (same raw `:39-47`, `:910-922`). Without automatic capture, the guide specifies delayed or manual capture and says authorizations do not expire, while multiple partial captures need Support (same raw `:958-967`). Recurring uses standard token creation, a `recurring.token.created` webhook and shopper-absent `/payments` reuse for Subscription/UnscheduledCardOnFile; explicit shopper permission is strongly recommended, and several Advanced token-creation samples omit the ordinary-request table's bank fields (same raw `:51-59`, `:258-287`, `:914-922`, `:969-979`, `:1008-1138`, `:1328-1359`, `:1639-1666`). TEST IBAN fixtures and the live Customer Area request gate appear at `:878-890` and `:1946-1958`.
