## Q1 — What is this page for, and what cannot be inferred?

[[source-adyen-docs-payment-methods-sepa-direct-debit-android-drop-in]] is the SEPA Direct Debit Android Drop-in add-on for existing Sessions or Advanced integrations; it addresses method fields/configuration, Advanced capture and separate recurring token routes (raw lines 15–74, 273–305, 909–1010, 1359–1389). It is not a full base integration, a complete mandate procedure, or proof that the example `Received` payment was authorized, captured or settled; the related SEPA overview requires an actively accepted mandate before debit and describes later chargebacks (this raw lines 955–987; overview raw lines 29, 37–71, 100–109). The 2026-09-15 snapshot does not establish current merchant setup or live operation (raw lines 2, 11, 893–907, 1977–1989).

## Q2 — What central capability, prerequisites, and warnings are stated?

[[source-adyen-docs-payment-methods-sepa-direct-debit-android-drop-in]] requires existing Sessions Drop-in with Checkout API v68+ or an Advanced Android Drop-in with additional-action handling, plus Customer Area addition (raw lines 25–34, 909–917). Ordinary Sessions needs no extra SEPA-specific `/sessions` fields, while Advanced `/payments` marks owner name and unencrypted IBAN required and makes billing address conditional on listed non-EEA IBAN locations (raw lines 38–50, 941–953). The separate SEPA overview says shopper mandate acceptance is required before debit; this child guide's explicit recurring permission language is a strong recommendation and does not replace that mandate (this raw lines 68–74, 1004–1010; overview raw lines 29, 37–61). Advanced capture may be delayed automatic or manual; the page says authorizations do not expire and multiple partial captures need Support enablement (raw lines 989–998). Tokens are reported by `recurring.token.created` and reused through shopper-absent `/payments` with Subscription or UnscheduledCardOnFile (raw lines 273–305, 1359–1389, 1670–1697). The Advanced initial-token curl lacks a storage flag and several library examples omit fields marked required elsewhere; do not treat them as validated complete requests (raw lines 951–953, 1008–1074, 1170–1205). Live acceptance requires a request in live Customer Area (raw lines 893–907, 1977–1989).
