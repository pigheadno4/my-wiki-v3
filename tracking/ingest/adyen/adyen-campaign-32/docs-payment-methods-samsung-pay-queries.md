Q1. What Samsung Pay routes and prerequisites does this overview describe?
A1. It describes a Samsung-provided Android wallet: NFC communication from shopper phone/watch to a terminal for POS; for ecommerce, an installed Samsung Pay app opens a payment sheet for card selection. The chooser links an API-only merchant-built website/Android UI guide and an in-person guide. Availability depends on specific devices, countries/regions and issuers, which this overview does not enumerate. Raw lines 23–27, 33–38, 40–52.
