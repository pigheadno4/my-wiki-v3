Q1. What are the direct-merchant PayPal test and live setup steps, and whose permission is given?
Answer: Marketplaces are routed to another guide (raw line 19). Test needs developer, business-sandbox and personal-sandbox accounts (lines 21–26, 35–51), then PayPal in test Customer Area and an activation link granting Adyen permission on the business sandbox account. Live separately needs a live PayPal business account, live Customer Area setup and a link granting Adyen permission on that live business account (lines 28–33, 97–112). Q2. Does Customer Area 'Active' or default recurring configuration establish a billing agreement, shopper consent, token or completed payment?
Answer: No. The page says recurring is enabled by default in both Customer Area tabs (raw lines 63–64 and 72–73), then describes an inactive link-sent state, seven-day link, PayPal business login/permission acceptance, and expected same-business-day Active status (lines 75–95). The separately read PayPal Web Component raw says Vault permissions condition direct-merchant recurring and strongly recommends explicit shopper permission (its lines 1414–1426). This setup page shows no shopper agreement/token or authorization/capture/settlement result.
