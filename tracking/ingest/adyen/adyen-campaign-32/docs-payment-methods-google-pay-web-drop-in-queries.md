Q1. What does this guide require for Google Pay Web Drop-in, and how do Sessions and Payments differ?
Answer: Existing Web Drop-in, Google's setup steps and Customer Area addition are listed at raw lines 23–29. No extra Google Pay API fields are required (line 33); /sessions is default at Drop-in v5+ and needs no extra Google Pay configuration (lines 35–36, 60–64). /payments is the additional-use-case route, with amount/currency, merchant-account countryCode and environment in its configuration table and conditional native-3DS2 fields (lines 66–77). Q2. What keeps a visible/approved Google Pay sheet or test setup from proving final live payment availability?
Answer: The sheet lets a shopper choose a card and may redirect for 3DS verification (raw lines 19–21); optional onAuthorized is described after shopper approval, not as final Adyen authorization (lines 216–224). Test cards are either suite cards or real cards mapped to Adyen test cards, with non-suite Amex/Discover 3DS2 scope (lines 252–276). Live requires the listed API role, Google Merchant ID and production-access/deployment steps, plus logged-in shopper and valid funding method (lines 278–290). This page gives no capture/settlement result.
