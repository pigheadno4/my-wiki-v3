Q2. Does `Authorised` mean the order is final, or does a received partial refund prove funds were returned? No. The page warns a synchronous `Authorised` response can later fail and says to use AUTHORISATION webhooks for the final payment outcome (pinned raw lines 1172–1192); capture/settlement are separate. Before capture it directs cancellation; after capture, refund (lines 1270–1277). The immediate-capture partial-refund prose/table differ on `lineItems` requiredness, the example differs from its earlier order, and a `status: received` refund response is not completed shopper credit (lines 1281–1353). For later capture, `capturePspReference` is required only under the listed three conditions (lines 1355–1373).
