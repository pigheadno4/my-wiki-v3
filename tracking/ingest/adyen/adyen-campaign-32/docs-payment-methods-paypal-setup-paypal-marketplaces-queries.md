# Retrieval checks — PayPal marketplace setup

Q1. Which marketplace integration and account/permission steps does the guide require to configure PayPal?

A1. It requires a modern Adyen for Platforms marketplace, excluding Classic Platforms (raw line 33). For TEST, the operator creates a PayPal developer account with business/merchant and personal/shopper sandbox accounts (raw lines 55–68), obtains a PayPal marketplace account and Adyen referral URL, and grants Adyen access through the referral URL (raw lines 70–78). After Adyen confirms Customer Area configuration, the integration includes split data and account-manager-specified `paypalRisk` fields (raw lines 80–97). LIVE requires separate PayPal business and marketplace accounts and live Adyen configuration (raw lines 109–127). Q2. What are the regional, seller-dispute and refund boundaries, and does setup prove a live payment?

A2. The page lists specified **settlement** currencies for EU-registered marketplaces and calls support for Australia, New Zealand, UK and US marketplaces `Coming soon` in this 2026-09-15 snapshot (raw lines 35–53). The marketplace must implement its own seller-dispute mapping/management because Adyen lacks the seller association in PayPal's dispute notice; responses are time-limited (raw lines 129–140). Refund through Adyen live Customer Area or API, not directly in PayPal portal because of reconciliation discrepancies (raw lines 142–146). Sandbox account setup and Customer Area payment-list inspection (raw lines 55–68, 99–107) do not prove merchant enablement, live payment authorization, capture or settlement.
