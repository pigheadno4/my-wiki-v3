Q1. How are bank transfer routes calculated, and what inputs vary by query?
A1. With Payouts integration and Balance Platform BCL role, POST Configuration API /transferRoutes/calculate either by recipient country plus balancePlatform/currency/category bank, or by concrete counterparty details (raw lines 23–48, 359–372). Priorities are optional; a transferInstrumentId counterparty also requires source balanceAccountId (raw line 372). Q2. Does a returned route mean the payout was sent or received?
A2. No. transferRoutes lists supported currency/priority and extra requirements such as amount, address, identification and instrument type (raw lines 301–357, 716–780). The page then directs a separate payout request and webhook step (782–790). Its 2023-modified TEST examples do not establish current availability, booking or bank credit.
