# Q1 — What does this Payout Service card-payout webhook guide require and report?

Route: [[index]] → [[adyen-index]] → [[adyen-payouts]] → [[source-adyen-docs-payouts-payout-service-pay-out-to-cards-payout-webhooks]] → [[raw/adyen/docs/payouts/payout-service/pay-out-to-cards/payout-webhooks/2026-09-15]].

It covers outgoing balance-account payouts to Mastercard/Visa cards, identified by `category=card`, `direction=outgoing`, `type=cardTransfer` (raw lines 38-61). The stated prerequisites are a Payout Service integration, webhook-receiving server and subscriptions to Transfer and Transaction webhooks (raw lines 28-36). `balancePlatform.transfer.created` reports request creation; `balancePlatform.transfer.updated` reports authorisation/reservation, booking/deduction with transaction ID, and later tracking or failure (raw lines 59-72, 305-307, 584-592). This page's examples are Transfer events, not a Transaction webhook payload specification.

# Q2 — Does booked or accepted mean the cardholder has received funds, and what failure/conflict boundaries matter?

No. `booked` deducts the source balance account but is explicitly nonfinal and can fail after scheme/issuer rejection (raw lines 584-592). Internal review appears as `trackingData.status=pending` / `type=internalReview`, while its sample transfer remains `status=booked`; a review failure can carry `refusedForRegulatoryReasons` (raw lines 920-929, 1030-1048, 1296-1302). Scheme `trackingData.status=accepted` / `type=confirmation` means accepted for processing, while that example's transfer status remains booked; it does not prove cardholder receipt (raw lines 1304-1313, 1412-1432). Failure after unsuccessful retries is reported with `status=failed`; examples show a prior booked event and compensating balance mutation (raw lines 1436-1446, 1531-1572). The opening bank-account wording and later “external banking system” wording conflict with card-specific title/fields/examples (raw lines 17-25, 44-61, 1436-1438). The EUR 100.00 example narrative and JSON `amount.value=100` EUR are not reconciled by this page (raw lines 57, 85-88). Reason-code remediation is code-specific (raw lines 1829-1844).
