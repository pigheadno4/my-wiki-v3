# docs-payouts-payout-service-pay-out-to-cards-reconciliation — worker retrieval checks

Q1. Which reports and keys reconcile Balance Platform-initiated card payouts, and where are amount and fees?
A. Use the Settlement Details Report from merchant account(s) used for card payouts and the Balance Platform Accounting Report from the instructing balance account(s) (raw lines 19–23). A `BPPaidOut` merchant-report line carries the fees; `Net Debit (NC)` is total fees, not amount plus fees, while the balance-platform report has the capture/card-payout amount (lines 25–32). Join Settlement Details `Merchant Reference` to Accounting `Transfer Id` only where Accounting `Status` is `booked` (line 34). Q2. What status and timing claims are outside this reconciliation page?
A. `booked` is the Accounting Report join condition, not evidence that the recipient card was credited or external settlement finished (raw line 34). The short page gives no report-lag/timezone, card-network receipt or currency-conversion rule (lines 19–34). It calls the amount “capture or card payout” without defining a payment-capture lifecycle (lines 32–34).
