## Overview

This 2026-09-15 [[adyen]] Payout Service guide reconciles funds moving from a merchant account on the payment-processing side to a balance-platform balance account through internal transfers. It combines payment-side Standard webhooks and settlement reports with balance-platform Transfer webhooks and reports. Each settled payable batch is represented as one incoming internal transfer to the balance account; the guide is about cross-system matching, not third-party beneficiary-bank receipt. See [[adyen-reporting]] and [[adyen-financial-products]].

## Key takeaways

- **Event identity and stage:** Standard payment-side authorization/capture webhooks include `settlementReference` and `expectedValueDate` for matching; balance-platform `balancePlatform.transfer.created` announces an incoming transfer initiated for a settled batch and `balancePlatform.transfer.updated` reports status changes. The example created webhook is `status: received`, which is not a booked balance entry. Its sample CAPTURE payload also lacks a comma between two additional-data fields, so it is illustrative, not copy-ready JSON. See pinned raw lines 21–129.
- **Report grain and clocks:** SDR gives transaction detail for one payable batch; ASDR covers settlement batches in a period. Both are generated by payment-side booking date in the merchant-account time zone. BPSR is a daily balance-platform opening/closing and fund-movement report with one entry per balance adjustment, generated by transfer value date in CE(S)T. BPIAR is balance-platform fund-movement detail generated by transfer booking date in CE(S)T. The page also calls balance-platform reports 'always CET'; keep that wording's seasonal ambiguity visible rather than silently treating all dates as interchangeable. For a non-CET merchant account it directs adding `Booking Date (AMS)` to settlement reports. See pinned raw lines 131–143.
- **Daily match:** Generate BPSR for the sales day, identify its `InternalTransfer` entries and `settlementBatch`, then obtain an SDR for each batch or for the corresponding dates. Match each SDR `MerchantPayout` with the BPSR internal transfer by batch, amount/description and BPSR `Value Date` versus SDR `Creation Date` or `Booking Date (AMS)`. The guide says 1–24 payable batches/internal transfers can occur per day. A merchant sales day can cross two CET dates; the worked example says SDRs for both 16 and 17 July are needed but links only one 17 July SDR sample. See pinned raw lines 144–174.
- **Monthly match and dashboard limit:** Compare booked BPIAR `InternalTransfer` amounts whose `Value Date` falls in the target month with ASDR `MerchantPayout` totals across all relevant merchant accounts; use the Description to identify source merchant accounts when multiple feed one balance account. The Liable account summary dashboard alternative is restricted here to one account holder; its steps say generate ASDR, then call the MerchantPayout source 'SDR' in the count/amount checks. Treat that SDR/ASDR label mismatch as unresolved and check the actual report grain before using the dashboard route. See pinned raw lines 176–224.
- **Final ledger boundary:** The guide presents BPSR starting balance plus net incoming settlement and minus payouts/internal transfers/fees as an ending-balance check. A report row or matching illustrative totals do not prove a live reconciliation, final settlement to a recipient bank or accounting sign-off. See pinned raw lines 226–241.
