Q1. How does this Payout Service guide spot-check balance-account positions?
Answer: It directs a dated Balance Platform Balance Report and filtering for the needed account/currency; the report shows opening/closing balances for a day across holders, accounts and currencies (raw lines 21-31). It separately describes real-time `GET /balanceAccounts/{id}` for a payout account (lines 33-34). The June 8, 2022 example has three account/currency rows across two accounts, opening June 8 and closing June 9 at 00:00 CEST (lines 36-44). Q2. Does this guide give a pending/booked balance roll-forward formula or independent payout/bank-receipt proof?

It states no signed transaction-sum formula, pending/booked event selection, universal timezone or beneficiary receipt evidence. The sample's CEST timestamps are illustrative (lines 38-44); a daily balance cannot alone prove later available funds or bank/card payout completion.
