# Query proposal — docs-payouts-payout-service-top-up-balance-accounts

Q1. What does a Payout Service recurring top-up fund, from where, and what triggers it?
A1. It pulls funds by outgoing direct-debit request from a linked bank transfer instrument into a balance account, to fund later transfers rather than itself paying a beneficiary (raw lines 17–28, 50–52, 64–70). Choose a fixed or target amount; schedule and threshold jointly drive checks, with the account-timezone 07:00 schedule options or default continuous monitoring (raw lines 30–48). Q2. Does a configured or triggered top-up prove balance credit, and which setup/status boundaries qualify the overview?
A2. No. This page narrates direct-debit request then incoming transfer (raw lines 64–70); its linked creation guide adds role, capability, funding/currency and Terms gates, while its webhook guide separates configuration, transfer state and credited transaction and illustrates later rejection/return. The Terms guide covers direct-debit acceptance. The overview says below threshold (raw line 48), whereas the creation guide's Customer Area wording says less-than-or-equal, leaving exact-threshold behavior channel-dependent/unresolved.
