Q1. When and how can a payout be reviewed, approved or cancelled?
A1. The sender optionally includes `review` in POST /transfers, making the bank payout pending a team decision; TransferService Approval role and Transfer webhooks are listed (raw lines 19–40). A reviewer POSTs /transfers/approve or /transfers/cancel with one to 500 transferIds (raw lines 52–58, 261–267). No action for 30 days causes expiry/failure; cancellation or expiry prevents further processing (raw lines 44–50). Q2. Does approval or HTTP 200 prove booked debit or bank credit, and what statuses appear?
A2. No. Approval lets Adyen continue processing (raw line 46); HTTP 200 confirms the approve/cancel request (259, 468), not bank receipt. Review webhooks show created received/pending, approved updated authorised/approved, and canceled updated cancelled/unknown; expiry is described but has no separate sample payload (raw lines 470–495, 563–571, 653–660). Prose calls cancellation/expiry failure while the sample says status cancelled (44–48, 653–735).
