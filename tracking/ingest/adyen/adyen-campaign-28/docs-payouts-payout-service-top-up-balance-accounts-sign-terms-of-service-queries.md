Q2. How does the guide distinguish generated documents, recorded acceptance and actual top-up outcomes?
Answer: GET acceptance information for the main entity can find `type: directDebit` and skip repeated generation. Otherwise POST generates document(s), which must be presented; more than one may be required. PATCH records acceptance with the individual `acceptedBy` ID and main entity/document IDs, followed by GET confirmation of the directDebit acceptance entry. An acceptance record alone does not establish authorized live signatory status, a created schedule or a credited top-up.
