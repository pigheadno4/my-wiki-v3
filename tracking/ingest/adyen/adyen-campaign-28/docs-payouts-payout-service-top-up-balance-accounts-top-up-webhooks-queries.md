Q1. Which webhook families indicate recurring top-up schedule changes, direct-debit progress and credited funds?
Answer: The dated enterprise-Payout Service guide separates Configuration events for successful schedule created/updated/deleted, Transfer created/updated events for a triggered bankDirectDebit's stages, and Transaction created events when funds are credited to the balance account. A successful Top-up API response alone does not establish schedule success, and transfer initiation or authorization does not establish credit (raw lines 19–49, 164–178, 706–751). Q2. Why should a booked transfer or sample direction not be treated as final bank receipt?
Answer: The guide shows rejected and returned later transfer states after an earlier booked accounting event, so booked alone is not irrevocable outcome proof. Its same illustrated top-up transfer is outgoing at initiation/authorization but incoming in the booked sample, an unresolved direction conflict. The transaction webhook is the guide's balance-account-credit evidence, a separate grain that still does not prove a specific external bank's final receipt or immunity from later return (raw lines 168, 216, 291, 380, 435–710).
