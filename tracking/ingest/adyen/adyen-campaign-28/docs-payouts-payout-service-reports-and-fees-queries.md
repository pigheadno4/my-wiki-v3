# Retrieval checks: docs-payouts-payout-service-reports-and-fees

Q1. Which report families and specific reports does the dated Payout Service overview map to full monthly reconciliation?
Answer: It combines Adyen-account and balance-platform reports. It labels Settlement details, Monthly invoice and Balance Platform Statement required, while Payment accounting and Balance Platform Accounting are optional for full financial reconciliation. Payment accounting contains payment lifecycle processing costs but excludes balance-platform payout/transfer/KYC charges; the Statement gives daily opening/closing balances for accounts that changed (raw lines 28–47). Q2. How do the fee-report grains differ, and what outcome cannot be inferred?
Answer: The Balance Platform Accounting Report breaks down calculated transaction-level fees; the Monthly invoice totals period fees and adjustments; Settlement details aggregates processing-fee lines and overall invoice adjustments. The matrix puts processing fees in Payment accounting when Adyen is PSP and per-instruction payout/fund-transfer charges in the invoice, with US-only FanF and Merchant Location Fee qualifiers. These report locations and status rows do not prove a merchant's final bill, fee debit, complete reconciliation or beneficiary-bank receipt (raw lines 49–73).
