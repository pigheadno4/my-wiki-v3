# PCI merchant-level retrieval checks

Q1. What does this page cover and what cannot it prove? Route through `[[adyen-pci-dss-compliance]]` to `[[source-adyen-docs-development-resources-pci-dss-compliance-guide-merchant-levels]]`. It is a 2026-09-15 capture of Adyen's 2020-modified PCI merchant-level and validation-document guidance, with 12-month counts per acquiring region and scheme plus an ADC-triggered Level 1 route. It does not assign a real merchant's level, establish current scheme/acquirer rules, or prove completed PCI validation or compliance. Its v3.2.1 RoC-template link should be distinguished from the separate v4.0.1 guide snapshot. Raw locators: `raw/adyen/docs/development-resources/pci-dss-compliance-guide/merchant-levels/2026-09-15.md:5-21`, `:41-59`.

Q2. Which level and document distinctions should a reader find, and what qualifications matter? Route to the same source and raw `## Validation requirements` and `## Determine your PCI compliance level`. The dated table lists Level 1 over 6m annually per acquiring region/scheme or prior ADC event, Level 2 1–6m, Level 3 20k–1m, and Level 4 below 20k; adjacent 'between' wording does not resolve exact shared endpoints. The table gives Level 1 RoC, Levels 2–4 RoC or SAQ, with integration-dependent ASV quarterly scans. The page describes QSA/internal assessment and ISA training/accreditation conditions, and says to send Adyen the AoC summary, never the full RoC. These are validation routes, not compliance evidence.
