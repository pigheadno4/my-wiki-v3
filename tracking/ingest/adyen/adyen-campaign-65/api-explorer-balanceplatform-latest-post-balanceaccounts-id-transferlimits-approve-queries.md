A `401` requires resolving an SCA challenge; `404` says one submitted limit was not found, and `422` is validation error. The capture gives no credential role, idempotency, batch partial-success semantics, propagation, deletion, reversal, conflict/version or retry rule. The dated TEST capture and `latest` URL do not establish current LIVE availability or a specific account's eligibility.
