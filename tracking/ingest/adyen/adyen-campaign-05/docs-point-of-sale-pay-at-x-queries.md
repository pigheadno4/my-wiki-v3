# Fixed retrieval checks — docs-point-of-sale-pay-at-x

1. **Purpose, scope, and what not to infer?** This is a terminal-initiated, POS-integrated Pay-at-table/location guide: staff start at a portable terminal, `SaleWakeUp` reaches the POS, the POS finds the bill and sends each split `PaymentRequest`. It covers table, hotel-room and curbside scenarios, payment methods added to the account plus cash, and optional tipping; it is not a hosted/mobile checkout or the separate issuer partial-authorization flow. The dated snapshot does not prove current account/device eligibility, secure staff identity beyond its configured reference/card prompt, settlement, or payment success. 2. **Central capability, prerequisites, identities, outcomes and warnings?** Require Terminal API, portable printer/Wi-Fi/cellular terminal, event notifications, Customer Area Pay@Table settings, Support-configured staff reference/card method, and POS `SaleWakeUp` handling; employee-card swipe is unavailable on Android (raw lines 28–38, 79–118). POS resolves the bill, optionally requests more input or prints, and sends `SplitPaymentFlag: true` with `RequestedAmount` remaining and `PaidAmount` already paid; one unique sale `TransactionID` is reused across all splits for that sale (143–185). Optional input-request session is not payment completion (153–156). Read each response: `Success` covers the current requested amount, `Partial` means a remaining amount; `AuthorizedAmount` records that split (262–279). `Pay later` yields `Failure/PaymentRestriction` for the interrupted flow, not a reversal of an earlier successful split; staff restart later (71–77, 359–364). Errors outside processing reach POS and can be relayed with a display request (389–391). The page gives no concurrent-bill, duplicate/retry, or final-settlement guarantee.
