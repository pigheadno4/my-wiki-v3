# Adyen C05 — terminal tipping override fixed answers

1. **Purpose/scope; what not to infer.** The 2026-09-15 Adyen guide lets a merchant Terminal API POS override Customer Area default tipping suggestions in a payment request, with merchant logic deciding when to trigger the different terminal choices (`raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/tipping/tipping-from-terminal/override-tipping-configuration/2026-09-15.md:15-30`). It does not update stored defaults, let staff impose a tip automatically, prove merchant enablement, or establish capture/settlement. The EUR 10 request and EUR 12 response examples have different `ServiceID` values (`:147-149`, `:199-203`), so they are illustrative, not a matched transaction.

2. **Central capability/prerequisites/warnings and route.** Terminal software 1.97+ is stated; 1.97–1.100 use `Variant`/`Data` with `Percent1`–`Percent3`, while 1.101+ use `TipOptions.TipSuggestions` with `Percentage1`–`Percentage3` (`:27-30`, `:40-104`). A payment offers either up to three fixed amounts or three percentages of the original purchase, optional custom tip, and always no tip (`:32-34`, `:50-52`, `:80-83`). The POS sends `AskGratuity` and Base64 `Operation`; `RequestedAmount` remains the purchase base (`:36-40`, `:126-135`). The shopper's choice yields a tip amount and an authorized total of base plus tip; no-tip omits gratuity fields, while additional-response amounts are in minor units and depend on response-format settings (`:172-188`). The page states no separate Support enablement condition.
