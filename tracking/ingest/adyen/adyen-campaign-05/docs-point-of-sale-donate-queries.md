# POS Giving: fixed retrieval checks

1. **What is this guide for, and what should it not establish?** It is Adyen's in-person Giving route: the shopper may donate after a store purchase, using the original payment method for a separate donation transaction; the nonprofit receives the full donation directly, with Adyen covering fees. It covers Terminal API terminals or standalone at software 1.109+, subject to model/feature 1.110/1.124/1.125 gates, an onboarded nonprofit, active campaign and supported region/currency/method. It does not define online Giving's Web SDK/Sessions/Advanced implementation, establish current eligibility for a merchant, or say a purchase refund reverses the donation. Locate raw lines 15-41, `## Donation campaigns` (83-124), `## Supported terminals and donation options` (569-589), `## Supported regions and currencies` (591-606), and `## Supported payment methods and flows` (608-635).

2. **Who prompts and processes a donation, where does money go, and how is outcome known?** The merchant selects a nonprofit and merchant accounts/stores in Customer Area, creates then starts the campaign. The terminal prompts after an eligible payment automatically or, for Terminal API with the configured option and Support contact, when the merchant uses `AskGiving`. One-step needs one shopper card presentation but still produces purchase and donation transactions; two-step needs two presentations and can give a POS-retrievable donation response/receipt. The purchase `PaymentResponse` is not donation finality; the Giving merchant `DONATION` webhook reports the outcome and `originalReference` ties it to the original purchase. Offline donations decline even if the purchase can process offline. Locate raw lines 23-25, 43-69, 120-170, 213-227, 302-350 and 528-537.
