1. **Which POS surcharge method applies, and can the two amounts stack?** Configuration-based rules use payment method, funding source, issuer country, or currency and can be scoped at account, store, or terminal. Dynamic surcharge is merchant/third-party calculated and specified in an integrated payment request; standalone terminals use configuration-based surcharge only. If both methods are in use, the request amount overrides the configured amount, so the guide does not describe charging both. Supported solution/hardware details are in linked method pages, which remain navigation here. Locators: raw lines 21–34, 52–73. Route through the surcharging concept to this POS source/raw, keeping the online pilot separate.

2. **What disclosure, DCC, regional, firmware, and outcome boundaries matter?** The snapshot lists Australia, Canada, Europe, Great Britain, New Zealand, and the US, but local/scheme exclusions and merchant disclosure still apply; Adyen does not enforce global compliance automatically. A terminal confirmation screen can show surcharge details, but Mobile lacks that screen and needs another disclosure path. DCC and a surcharge cannot apply to the same transaction; before terminal software v1.112 Adyen recommends not configuring both on one terminal, while v1.112+ skips DCC when surcharge is positive. No surcharge may be added on adjustment/overcapture above purchase disclosure. Optional AUTHORISATION webhook/report fields expose surcharge amount, but this page does not establish refund, capture, or settlement outcomes. Locators: raw lines 27–50, 75–120, 160–167. For current compliance, check current regional law and card-scheme guidance before use.
