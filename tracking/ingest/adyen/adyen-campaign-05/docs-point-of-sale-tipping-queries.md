2. **Three integrated paths, actors, money flow, and standalone boundary?** In the POS-app path, the merchant POS sends a suggested tip and the shopper accepts, changes, or declines it on the terminal before completing payment (raw line 25). In the terminal path, a payment request triggers fixed/percentage/custom tip choices or a total-entry prompt; the shopper chooses or enters an amount above purchase for a tip (27–31). In the receipt path, the merchant pre-authorizes, the shopper writes a tip afterward, and the merchant may increase the authorization before manually capturing the final amount (33). A standalone account can be configured for an automatic tip prompt, outside the integrated POS request description (19–21).
