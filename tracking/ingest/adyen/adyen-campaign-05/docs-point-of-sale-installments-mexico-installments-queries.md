# Fixed retrieval checks — docs-point-of-sale-installments-mexico-installments

1. **Purpose, scope, and what not to infer?** This 2026-09-15 Adyen guide is for Mexican in-store Terminal API card payments: store staff enter the shopper's debit/credit choice and, for credit installments, the count into the POS, which sends a `PaymentRequest`. It is limited to named issuer programs or an appropriate direct bank agreement for other issuers; it does not prove current card/scheme/account/region eligibility, an interest-free offer, legal compliance, or payment approval. 2. **Central capability, prerequisites, response, money flow and warnings?** Require a Terminal API terminal integration in Mexico with supported issuing bank/program (raw `## Requirements`, lines 25–30). Staff ask debit/credit, one-off versus installments, and installment count; POS sets `DebitPreferredFlag` true for debit/false for credit and adds `PaymentType: Instalment` plus `TotalNbOfPayments` **3–18** for credit installments (32–46, 81–96). An approved response provides receipt `productType`; an installment response also returns `PaymentResult.PaymentType` and installment details (216–252). The full purchase amount uses shopper credit limit; issuing bank collects monthly installments, pays merchant full purchase amount, and charges installment fees that the merchant bears (254–258). The snapshot lists five programs and requires a direct bank agreement for other issuers (260–274). It gives no interest rate, merchant settings, preauth/capture process, software-version floor, or settlement time.
