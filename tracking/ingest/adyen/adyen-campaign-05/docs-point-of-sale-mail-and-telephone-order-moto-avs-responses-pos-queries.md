# Fixed retrieval checks — Adyen POS MOTO AVS responses

1. **What does this source establish, and what must AVS not be mistaken for?** It maps issuer AVS responses for POS Mail Order/Telephone Order payments into `AdditionalResponse.avsResult` after terminal entry of postal code and house number. It is an address-comparison signal for a merchant decision, not the payment authorization result, automatic fraud rejection, liability protection, or proof of cancellation/refund. The 2026-09-15 page is a snapshot. See `# AVS responses for point of sale` and `## AVS responses` in [[raw/adyen/docs/point-of-sale/mail-and-telephone-order-moto/avs-responses-pos/2026-09-15|pinned raw]].

2. **Which support and outcome qualifications matter, and where is the route?** The page covers Terminal API payment terminals and standalone MOTO, lists Amex/Discover/Mastercard/Visa and says other issuers support AVS in the US, UK and Canada; it excludes Android and legacy terminals and says nothing affirmative about Brazil. `Unknown` follows no issuer response; mismatch, unavailable, unsupported, no-data and unchecked codes differ. The related MOTO guide says an issuer can still authorize despite incomplete AVS match; a merchant may review and cancel an uncaptured authorization with capture delay, while captured state can require refund, and MOTO retains merchant fraud-chargeback liability. See raw `## Requirements` and `## AVS responses`, plus [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-mail-and-telephone-order-moto]] for those separate flow and liability claims.
