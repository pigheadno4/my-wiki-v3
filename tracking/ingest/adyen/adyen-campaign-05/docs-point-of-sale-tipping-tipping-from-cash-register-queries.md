2. **Request, shopper choice, authorized and receipt amounts?** The POS computes the proposed tip and sends original `RequestedAmount` plus `TipAmount` in `PaymentTransaction.AmountsReq` (raw 31–38, 54–64, 87–92). The shopper accepts, changes, or declines on the terminal, which sends original plus chosen tip for authorization (99–101). In a successful response, `AmountsResp.TipAmount` is the actual added tip and is absent if none; `AuthorizedAmount` is original plus tip, and `PaymentReceipt` includes original, tip if any, and total (103–117). `AdditionalResponse` amounts for original, gratuity and authorized total are in minor units (119–123).
