# C29 e355 charging retrieval checks

## Q1 — Main purpose and prerequisites

**Question:** What does the historical e355 charging page tell an operator to do before use, and what equipment or power source does it name?

**Answer:** It instructs charging the e355 before use with the provided Micro-USB cable (raw line 17). Connect the charger to a wall outlet or powered surge protector (line 21), then insert the Micro-USB cable into the port on the side of the e355 (line 22). This is a 2019-modified page captured in 2026 (raw lines 2, 11). ## Q2 — Material boundary or conflict

**Question:** Does following the charging step show that an e355 can process current transactions or that its battery is healthy?

**Answer:** No. The pinned charging raw provides a before-use instruction and two connection steps (raw lines 17, 21–22); it reports no observed charge, battery-health result or transaction. Its path places it in terminal-deprecation (raw lines 1, 8–10). The separately ingested deprecation overview lists e355 with a historical 29 March 2024 “No more transactions” cutoff; see [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation]] and its raw lines 32–35.
