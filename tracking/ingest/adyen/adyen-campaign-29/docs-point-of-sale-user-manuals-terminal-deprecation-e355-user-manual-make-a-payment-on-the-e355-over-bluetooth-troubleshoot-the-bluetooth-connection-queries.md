Q1. What does the historical e355 guide suggest when the app initializes indefinitely or the Bluetooth icon is missing?
A. It calls protocol-version incompatibility the most likely initializing cause and says to verify compatibility, without naming a version matrix (raw lines 16–18). For no icon, check proximity (within 10 m for troubleshooting), then if paired devices show no icon after 20 seconds restart the app, and only if that fails restart the terminal (lines 20–24). Q2. What limits qualify the range and clean-configuration advice?
A. The 60 m open-space and roughly 30 m office figures are manual estimates; the operational troubleshooting instruction is to stay within 10 m (raw line 22). For other issues it recommends removing **other paired devices unless necessary** via a separate step-1 procedure (lines 26–28), not indiscriminately unpairing or factory resetting. These historical instructions give no proof of restored connection, current e355 support, boarding or payment outcome.
