Q1. How does this historical e355 page pair and board an iOS or Android mobile device?
A. An unboarded or already-boarded e355 can pair with a mobile device used as the register (raw line 16). The Admin → Network → Bluetooth path selects the app OS; boarded devices skip that screen and an OS mismatch goes to Support (lines 22–32). iOS confirms a pairing code, then uses the Adyen POS App to board if needed; Android uses the app's Payment device/Add new device/Bluetooth route and can board an unboarded terminal after pairing (lines 34–93). With the e355 frame on iOS, connect to the frame and restart instead of using the Bluetooth menu (line 20). Q2. What firmware, configuration and payment-status limits qualify the instructions?
A. On firmware through v36, iOS may show the e355 as “not connected” after restart; the page directs opening the Adyen POS App rather than tapping in the Bluetooth menu (raw line 52). Android boarding includes app identification, platform synchronization and configuration verification before restart (lines 89–93). These historical terminal-deprecation instructions do not establish current device support, continued connectivity, live payment authorization or settlement; pairing and boarding are setup states, not payment outcomes (lines 14–16, 54–72, 74–93).
