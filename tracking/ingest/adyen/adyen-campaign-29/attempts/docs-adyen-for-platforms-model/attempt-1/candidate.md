## Overview

This 2026-09-15 [[adyen]] guide introduces Adyen for Platforms as a solution for onboarding platform or marketplace users, processing payments for them, and paying out their funds. It compares the Marketplace and Platform integration models and routes readers according to business model, shopper interaction, in-person payment needs, and earlier Classic integration status. See [[adyen-platforms]] for the broader platform route.

## Key takeaways

- The page describes onboarding sellers, service providers or contractors with Adyen verification before payout; accepting payments on users' behalf; managed or custom payout timing; risk monitoring; and reports for bookkeeping and reconciliation (raw lines 17–27). These are product capabilities, not proof that a particular user is verified, a payment is accepted, funds are owned by a given party, or a payout has arrived.
- Model selection depends on business model and payment processing requirements (line 31). In the comparison table, shoppers interact directly with the marketplace brand in the Marketplace column and directly with the user in the Platform column. Both columns list online payments and direct sub-merchant payouts; only the Platform column lists in-person payments and payment facilitators (lines 35–42). These are dated model comparisons, not case-specific legal seller, merchant-of-record or fund-owner determinations.
- The Marketplace route describes an online marketplace, ecommerce site or mobile app whose brand is known to users and customers; the Platform route describes commerce, ordering/POS and retail-management systems or payment facilitators (lines 44–56).
- The decision tree asks whether an integration predates 1 August 2022 and whether it has upgraded to the balance platform. Its Classic branch identifies a preexisting Classic integration and says to contact Adyen about a possible upgrade for additional financial products or enhanced payment processing (lines 66–104). The branch is not an automatic migration or live eligibility decision. Other branches ask about in-person payments and customer-facing brand visibility (lines 106–134).
