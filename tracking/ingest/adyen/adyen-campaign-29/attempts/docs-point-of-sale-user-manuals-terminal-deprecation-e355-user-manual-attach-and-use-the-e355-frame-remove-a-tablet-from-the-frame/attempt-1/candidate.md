## Overview

This **2019-modified, 2026-09-15-captured** [[adyen]] e355 user-manual page gives the physical sequence for removing a tablet from its e355 frame when no longer in use. It is a historical **terminal-deprecation/e355** hardware procedure under [[adyen-payment-terminals]], not current support, device inspection, account boarding or payment evidence. [Raw: lines 5–17]

## Key takeaways

- First detach the **Removable Module**: turn its locking screws **counter-clockwise**, then slide the **Eject button right**. Next, **carefully pull the tablet upward** to disengage it from the frame. These actions have a specified order; pulling before releasing the module is not the documented procedure. [Raw: lines 19–23]
- After removing the tablet, guide the Removable Module's **pins into the slots on the e355 frame** and turn the locking screws **clockwise**. The page does not say to leave the module detached. [Raw: lines 25–27]
- For attaching **or** removing the tablet, place the e355 on a **smooth, flat surface** to avoid scratching the tablet screen. This is the stated screen-protection precaution, not a claim that the method prevents all damage or verifies device integrity. [Raw: line 29]
