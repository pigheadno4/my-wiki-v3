## Overview

This [[adyen]] page, last modified 2025-01-02 and captured 2026-09-15, describes documents and visible website/app content for a **live account application**. It says Adyen checks the company, shareholders and business model before granting live accounts and during periodic reviews. It is an application checklist under [[adyen-account-structure]], not evidence of approval, current eligibility, completed verification, PCI compliance or payment acceptance.

## Key takeaways

- The initial upload list names a company registration document, proof of tax information **or** VAT information, proof of identity and residency, and a **merchant bank statement marked optional**. Adyen separately says it must verify the bank account and its link to the legal entity; do not turn “optional” into a blanket bank-verification waiver. The bank-document section also repeats both legal-entity-or-trading-name and legal-entity-only matching on the same line. Keep that ambiguity with the raw source (lines 21–28, 752–786).
- Registration documentation must identify the legal entity and registration number; the page excludes a DBA/trading name as the legal entity name. Its 12-month issuance/signature alternatives, public-company annual-report exception, and selected-country registration-number and entity-type tables are at lines 30–629. Tax/VAT alternatives, format limits, authorized-signatory exemption form and selected-country VAT table are at lines 630–750; these snapshot examples are not current jurisdiction advice.
- Identity evidence covers the contract signatory and shareholders at **25% or more**, with an **India 10% or more** parenthetical. Physical, unexpired photo-ID and image restrictions plus country-specific caveats are at lines 825–887; residence document types and 3-/12-month age limits are at lines 890–912. The page's later UBO table uses a generic 25% ownership test and additional control/signatory/director conditions, while its chart example again calls out India 10%; apply the relevant country and role, not one universal threshold (lines 994–1062).
- **Only for online payments**, the applicant must provide a live-site or test-deployment URL for review of visible website/app information: terms, conditional subscription/free-trial consent/reminders/receipts/cancellation, privacy, delivery, refunds, contact details, and checkout disclosures/acceptance checkbox (lines 914–992). A checkable test deployment is documentary evidence for review, not live activation or a successful payment.
- Ownership/control documentation is requested **in some cases**. The chart calls for intermediate entities, UBO relationships/percentages and a dated signature; supporting external official documents can be required depending on signer qualification (lines 994–1062). The page states no PCI certification procedure or security attestation.
