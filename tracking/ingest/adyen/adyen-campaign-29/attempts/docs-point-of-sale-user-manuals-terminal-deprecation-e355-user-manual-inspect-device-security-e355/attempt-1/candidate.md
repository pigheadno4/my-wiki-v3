## Overview

This **2019-modified, 2026-09-15-captured** [[adyen]] e355 user-manual page instructs staff to inspect the physical terminal for signs of damage, substitution, skimming or other tampering. It sits under the **terminal-deprecation/e355** manual path and is historical model-specific operational guidance; it does not establish current e355 availability, certification or security status. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]] for the physical-device route. [Raw: lines 5–17]

## Key takeaways

- Staff should compare the device with the manual pictures and verify an Adyen or company logo appears after power-on. They should **not use** a device that is damaged or covered with a sticker. These are visual checks, not proof that a terminal is uncompromised. [Raw: lines 20–26]
- Verify the identity of third parties claiming repair or maintenance authority **before** allowing them to modify or troubleshoot a device. Regularly check for cameras and skimming equipment; do not install, replace or return devices without verification. Watch for unknown people trying to unplug or open the terminal. [Raw: lines 28–34]
- Report suspicious behavior or signs of tampering/substitution to appropriate personnel such as a manager or security officer. Staff must avoid seeing customers' PINs on a handheld terminal lacking a privacy shield. The page calls for **weekly visual integrity inspections**; when in doubt, **stop using the device and contact Adyen Support**. It gives no remote attestation or remediation proof. [Raw: lines 36–40]
