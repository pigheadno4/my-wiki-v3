## Overview

This 2019-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] e355 manual page gives a short mechanical procedure for removing the terminal from its frame when no longer in use. It belongs to the terminal-deprecation subtree; this snapshot does not establish current model support or authorization to operate a device. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]].

## Key takeaways

- **Order of release:** Turn the frame locking screws counter-clockwise to unlock, push the Eject button downward to release the e355, then turn the screws clockwise. Keep that three-step order; the page does not instruct pulling the unit before release. See pinned raw lines 19–25.
- **Surface precaution:** When attaching or removing the tablet, place the e355 on a smooth, flat surface to avoid scratching the tablet screen. See pinned raw line 27.
- **Scope:** The page contains no power-off, cable-disconnect, transaction or security-inspection instruction. Do not infer such a prerequisite or a completed safe-removal result from this short mechanical guide. See the full pinned raw lines 15–27.
