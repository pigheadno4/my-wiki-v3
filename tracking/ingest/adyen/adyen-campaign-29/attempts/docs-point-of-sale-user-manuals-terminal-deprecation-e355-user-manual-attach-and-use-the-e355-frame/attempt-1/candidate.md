## Overview

This [[adyen]] e355 manual page, captured 2026-09-15 under terminal deprecation, is a short guide to using the physical frame to connect a tablet and the e355 terminal and to charge both through the frame. It is a navigation hub for attachment, power connection and removal procedures within [[adyen-payment-terminals]], not a complete procedure or evidence of a working connection, successful charging, boarding or payment. The separate [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation|historical eVo terminal deprecation schedule]] lists **29 March 2024** as the e355 "No more transactions" date (that source's raw line 35); this frame guide should not be read as current transaction-support guidance.

## Key takeaways

- The page says the frame connects a tablet to the e355 and can charge both (raw line 16). It states no model or tablet compatibility matrix, electrical specification, charging observation or current support claim.
- The five linked child topics cover attaching a tablet, attaching the e355, connecting the frame to power, removing a tablet and removing the e355 (raw lines 18–24). Their actual steps and prerequisites belong to those child guides; this hub gives no step sequence beyond the links.
