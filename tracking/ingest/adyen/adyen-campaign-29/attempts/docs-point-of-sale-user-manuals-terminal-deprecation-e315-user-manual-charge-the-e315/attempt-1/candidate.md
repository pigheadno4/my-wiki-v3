## Overview

This [[adyen]] **e315** charging page was last modified **2019-04-18** and captured 2026-09-15 under the **terminal-deprecation** manual tree. It records device-specific initial charging and optional gang-charger steps; it does not establish present-day e315 transaction support, device condition, safe use of an unspecified accessory or successful payment. See [[adyen-payment-terminals]] and [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation]].

## Key takeaways

- The historical single-device path says to plug a **VeriFone-certified wall-mount charger** into a wall outlet or powered surge protector and connect the Micro-USB cable to the e315 side port. It also says a computer connection can synchronize data and/or charge the e315. These are different connection options, not evidence that a particular charger, port or device is currently functional. See raw lines 18–22.
- For **initial use**, the page instructs charging the e315 for **eight hours** and **not inserting an iPod or iPhone during initial charging**. It says the e315 must be fully charged before its USB power can charge the attached iPod/iPhone. Preserve that accessory-power condition; it is not a battery-health diagnostic or current accessory-compatibility guarantee. See raw line 24.
- For multiple devices, the dedicated e315 Gang Charger description says **up to six e315 units**: five charging slots plus an option to charge another device through USB. Its steps connect the wall-mount charger to external power and the Gang Charger's **DC IN** port. The “six” capacity relies on the optional USB position; do not describe six physical slots. See raw lines 28–34.
- The separate eVo retirement page lists e315 among models with a historical “No more transactions” date of **29 March 2024**. Charging instructions are not evidence that the terminal remains supported for live transactions. See [[source-adyen-docs-point-of-sale-user-manuals-terminal-deprecation]].
