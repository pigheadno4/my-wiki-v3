## Overview

This 2019-last-modified e315 manual page, captured 2026-09-15 under the terminal-deprecation path, describes mechanically installing an iPod or iPhone into the e315 casing so the combined device can be used for payments. It is a physical assembly guide, not boarding, network setup, a security-inspection checklist, a current support statement, or proof of payment processing. See [[adyen]] and [[adyen-payment-terminals]].

## Key takeaways

- The procedure removes the top screw beside the 2D barcode scanner, lifts the casing from its notch, and notes that a flat-head screwdriver or similar tool may also be used (raw lines 20–26).
- The device is held with the PIN pad facing down. The iPhone/iPod bottom port is aligned with the e315 Lightning connector and pushed down firmly. An iPod needs the flat plastic spacer inserted **before** it to fill the gap for a tight fit (raw lines 28–33).
- The final step replaces the top casing and tightens its screw to secure the device (raw lines 35–36). The page gives no power-off, battery-removal, tamper-inspection, credential, pairing, or boarding instruction; those topics require their own model-specific sources. Mechanical installation alone does not establish an MFI connection or boarded state.
