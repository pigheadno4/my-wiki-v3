## Overview

This 2026-09-15 snapshot maps old Customer Area menu labels and locations to the phased 2026 replacement menu. It is navigation evidence for finding pages, not evidence that a user has a role, that a feature is available on a particular account or environment, or that an action was performed. See [[adyen]] and [[adyen-customer-area-iam]] for the distinct user-access topic.

## Key takeaways

- The guide says the menu update began in July 2026, with renamed/moved sections such as Transactions → Payments, Finance pages → Balances/Reconciliation/Reports, and Insights → Analytics. The old and new page-name tables are the precise lookup authority; for example, old Transactions / Payments becomes Payments / Payment list, while old Transactions / Transfers moves to Balances (raw lines 19–31, 81–95).
- Search accepts both old and new page names. Multiple menu sections can remain expanded while navigating; **Hide all pages** collapses them. These are UI navigation steps, not entitlements (raw lines 43–57).
- The stated rollout progresses from selected-account early access in mid-May, to public preview from 6 July, default new menu from 15 July, and full transition from 15 August when the old menu is no longer available. The dated snapshot describes intended availability and switch-back phases, not a live account observation (raw lines 59–73).
- The guide states that old Customer Area page URLs automatically redirect to new URLs. It maps pages across Payments/Balances, Reconciliation/Reports, Account holders, Risk & disputes/Uplift, Analytics, Devices, Developers, and Settings. Examples include old Developers / Event logs → Webhook event logs and Account / Role groups → Settings / User role groups (raw lines 75–79, 100–271). The tables do not grant access or prove operations at any destination.
