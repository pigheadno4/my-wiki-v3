Q1. What do the e355 LED colors and blink patterns mean in this historical table?
Answer: Green blinking every 4 seconds is Sleep and continuous green is Normal Operation (raw lines 22, 27). Red at 1 Hz/3.7 V is Battery Low and red at 4 Hz/3.6 V is Battery Extremely Low (lines 23–24). Orange at 1 Hz is Battery Charging, continuous orange is Charging Timer Fault, and LEDs are off while charging on a gang charger (lines 25–28). Q2. Does a continuous green LED, a dark LED, or the voltage label prove current support, device safety or a payment outcome?
Answer: No. This e355 page is a 2021-modified status table (raw lines 10, 14–28) under the terminal-deprecation path. It calls continuous green “Normal Operation” (line 27), but says LEDs are off while charging on a gang charger (line 28); the voltage values are listed battery conditions (lines 23–24), not measured evidence for a specific terminal. It gives no current-support, safety or transaction test.
