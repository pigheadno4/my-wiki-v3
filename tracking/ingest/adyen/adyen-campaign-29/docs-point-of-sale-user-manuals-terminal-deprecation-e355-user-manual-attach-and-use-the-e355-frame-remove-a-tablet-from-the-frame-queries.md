Q1. In what order does the historical e355 guide remove a tablet from its frame?
A1. Turn the Removable Module locking screws counter-clockwise and slide the Eject button right; carefully lift the tablet; guide the module's pins back into the frame slots; and turn the screws clockwise (raw lines 21–27). The purpose is removal when the tablet is no longer in use (raw lines 15–17).
