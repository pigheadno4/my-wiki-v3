# Q2 — Does migration or a copied role grant every company action, and what happens to links/APIs?

No. Full company access for a former BPCA admin is conditional on having all company merchant accounts; a Developer without prior admin rights gets merchant-level API/webhook pages until an admin expands permission. A copy-access admin can assign only roles/accounts they already access (pinned raw lines 175–196). BPCA deep links stop after the stated deadline; Customer Area links require a merchant account in the relevant balance platform, with distinct TEST/LIVE examples (lines 210–240). The guide says API integrations remain unchanged (line 208), which does not prove any individual user's role grant or authorization to initiate operations.
