# Retrieval checks

Q1. What does the e355 Bluetooth cleanup page instruct, and when can Unpair work?
Answer: It routes through Admin → Network → Bluetooth → Paired devices, then selects a device and Unpair (raw lines 20–33). The selected device must be disconnected before unpairing (raw line 33). This removes Bluetooth pairings, not documented merchant configuration. Q2. Should the page be read as an unconditional order to remove every pairing or as current e355 payment support?
Answer: No. Its opening says remove other paired devices unless necessary (raw line 16), while the final step says repeat until all devices are removed (raw line 35); the tension is unresolved. The later deprecation source lists 29 March 2024 as e355 “No more transactions,” so a 2026 capture or cleanup cannot prove present payment eligibility.
