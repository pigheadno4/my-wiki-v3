# Q1 — What LED patterns does the historical e315 table identify?

Green blinking every four seconds is Sleep; green continuously on is Normal Operation. Red 1 Hz/50% at 3.7 V is Battery Low and red 4 Hz/50% at 3.6 V is Battery Extremely Low. Orange 1 Hz/50% is Battery Charging and continuously on is Charging Timer Fault (pinned raw lines 20–27). # Q2 — Does an unlit LED mean the e315 is dead or safely charged?

The table's Off row says LEDs are off **when charging on a gang charger** (pinned raw line 28). It does not diagnose all unlit-device causes or certify charging completion. This 2019-modified page is under a deprecated-terminal manual path, so it also does not establish present model support.
