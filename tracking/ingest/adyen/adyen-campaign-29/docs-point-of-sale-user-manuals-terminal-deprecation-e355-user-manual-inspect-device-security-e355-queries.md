Q1. What checks does this e355 manual instruct staff to perform?
A1. It calls for matching the physical device to manual pictures and checking the startup logo, avoiding damaged/stickered devices, verifying repair personnel before access, looking for cameras/skimmers and suspicious unplug/open attempts, and protecting customer PIN entry (raw lines 20–38). It specifies weekly visual integrity checks (raw line 40). This is historical e355 guidance, last modified in 2019 (raw lines 5–16). Q2. What should staff do on suspicion of tampering, and does this page certify a device secure or currently supported?
A2. Report suspicious behavior or tampering/substitution to the manager or security officer, stop using the device when in doubt, and contact Adyen Support (raw lines 36–40). Visual checks do not prove absence of tampering, and the historical e355 manual path and 2019-modified page do not establish current device support or certification (raw lines 5–16).
