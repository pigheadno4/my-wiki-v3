Q1. What did the historical e315 manual require before initial use, and how could it be charged?
A1. It says to charge before use, using a VeriFone-certified wall-mount charger with Micro-USB on the e315 side port; a computer connection is also described for synchronization and/or charging (raw lines 16–22). It specifies eight hours before initial use and no iPod/iPhone inserted during that initial charge; the e315 must be full before USB power charges the attached device (raw line 24). Q2. What is the gang-charger capacity and what support/outcome boundary applies?
A2. The page says up to six units by five slots plus an optional USB-charged device, with external wall power connected to DC IN (raw lines 28–34); it does not describe six slots. The 2019-modified charging procedure cannot prove a working battery, current accessory support, boardable terminal or payment, and the linked eVo-deprecation page lists a later e315 transaction cutoff.
