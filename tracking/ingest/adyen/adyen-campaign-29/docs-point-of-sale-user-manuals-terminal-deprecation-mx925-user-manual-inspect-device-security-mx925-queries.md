# Q1 — What should staff inspect on an MX925, and how often?

The historical MX925 page asks staff to compare the device with manual pictures, check the Adyen/company logo after power-on, inspect for cameras/skimmers regularly, and perform visual integrity inspections weekly (pinned raw lines 20–30, 40). It also calls for verifying third-party maintenance identity and device installation/replacement/return before granting access or moving hardware (lines 28–34). # Q2 — What if damage or tampering is suspected, and what does an inspection prove?

Do not use a damaged/sticker-covered device; report suspicious activity or tamper/substitution signs to appropriate staff, and when in doubt stop use and contact Adyen Support (pinned raw lines 26, 34–40). The page is a 2019-modified MX925 manual under a deprecated-terminal path, so this inspection checklist alone does not certify a particular device or establish present MX925 transaction support; cutoff details belong to the separate deprecation guide.
