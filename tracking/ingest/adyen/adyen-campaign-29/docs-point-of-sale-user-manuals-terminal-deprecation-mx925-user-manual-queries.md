# Q2 — Can this page establish current MX925 availability or successful processing, and what physical warning matters?

No. It expressly says new terminals of this type cannot be ordered (raw line 19). Its “new terminal” quickstart and “ready to process payments” conclusion must be read against the separately captured eVo deprecation table, which lists MX925 **“No more transactions” on 29 March 2024** (deprecation raw lines 32-35). That dated published cutoff is not a live account/device observation; the manual itself does not prove current ordering/support, certification, boarding success or a transaction result (manual raw lines 23-37). A wrong cable/port pairing, including Ethernet in RS232, might severely damage the device (raw lines 27-31). This page identifies no replacement model, current firmware/support status or merchant-account enablement; consult the separately linked deprecation and boarding sources for their own details.
