# C29 Uplift experiments query check

**Q1 — What does this source say an Uplift experiment does, and what must be in place before one starts?** It evaluates changes using real transactions in a live Adyen account, not a simulation (raw lines 19–21). An Uplift-supporting online integration and experiment-specific Customer Area roles are required (lines 23–33). Checkout method experiments require already activated methods, at least three active methods, and exclude primary card methods; dynamic ordering has Personalize/Advanced-flow and enablement gates (lines 32–33, 93–96). Optimization requires a qualifying recommendation and its stated country, method, or feature conditions (lines 49–55, 126–145); risk tests require Risk admin and Protect premium (lines 57–61). **Q2 — How should an operator interpret results and ending an experiment?** Results first appear after about four hours and update regularly; the page recommends waiting for statistical significance, or rerunning longer/larger after an early stop (raw lines 151–167). In an optimization test, **End** keeps the current setting while **End & change setting** changes to the recommended setting; checkout uses **End** (lines 169–176). Variant traffic splitting is described at lines 108–116 and 135–143. No account-specific outcome, uplift, conversion gain, authorization, settlement or live-eligibility proof follows from this guide.
