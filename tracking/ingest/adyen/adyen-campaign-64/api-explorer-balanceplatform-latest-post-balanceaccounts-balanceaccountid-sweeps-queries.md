## Q1

The 2026-11-02 captured Balance Platform API v2 `POST /balanceAccounts/{balanceAccountId}/sweeps` creates a sweep configuration for scheduled or threshold-based pull into, or push out of, a balance account. Its listed server is the TEST base `https://balanceplatform-api-test.adyen.com/bcl/v2`; the page names no specific caller role. Path `balanceAccountId` is required. Body `counterparty`, uppercase three-character `currency` matching a balance currency, and `schedule` are required. Although the counterparty children are individually optional, one of balance account ID, transfer instrument ID, or merchant account is required. Merchant account is a pull source only when processing payments with Adyen; transfer-instrument direct-debit top-up needs the stated pull/merchant combination and Adyen Support enablement. `schedule.type` is required and may be cron, daily, weekly, monthly or balance; `cronExpression` is conditionally required for cron. Optional direction `type` defaults to push, `status` to active, and `triggerAmount` to zero. A present amount object requires currency and minor-unit value; `sweepAmount` and `targetAmount` are mutually exclusive. `category` is required when optional bank-transfer `priorities` are supplied; priority acceptance needs at least one supported and platform-activated option. A successful 200 response includes required sweep `id`, counterparty, currency and schedule, with further optional configuration and disabling-reason fields. [[source-adyen-api-explorer-balanceplatform-latest-post-balanceaccounts-balanceaccountid-sweeps]] ([endpoint](https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/balanceplatform/latest/post/balanceAccounts/(balanceAccountId)/sweeps); verified raw lines 5–48, 113–147).

## Q2

A 200 response establishes successful creation of a sweep configuration and returns its ID; `active` means enabled to be triggered, whereas `inactive` cannot be triggered. The threshold is evaluated by the chosen schedule: pull at balance less than or equal to it, push at balance greater than or equal to it. For a prioritized bank transfer, accepting a request with one valid priority does not guarantee its first choice, transfer execution, booking, settlement or bank receipt. The reference lists 400 request error, 401 authentication required, 403 insufficient permissions, 422 validation error and 500 server failure. Its 422/500 payment-instrument examples are generic and cannot define sweep-specific failures. The page specifies no idempotency, deletion, propagation timing, reversal or version-conflict behavior. The `latest` URL, TEST server and sample active configurations are snapshot evidence rather than proof of live eligibility or money movement.
