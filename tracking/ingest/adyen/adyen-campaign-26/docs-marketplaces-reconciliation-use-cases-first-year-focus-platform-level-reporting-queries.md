Q1. Which Marketplace-level reporting analyses and sources does this guide propose? Answer: Four jobs are monthly profitability, transaction profitability, user profitability, and monthly invoice reconciliation. Monthly work uses liable-account payout totals or value-dated Balance Platform Accounting; transaction work (blended pricing only) joins Payment Accounting Psp Reference to Balance Platform Accounting Payment Psp Reference and retrieves Processing Fee only from Payment Accounting; user work groups account-holder/payment references and uses the Fee Report; invoice work separates transaction, KYC, payout-service and nontransactional scheme fees. Raw lines 19–26, 28–48, 50–95, 97–139, 143–174.

Q2. What limits and conflicts prevent reading these as completed profits or payout proof? Answer: Monthly payout less invoice costs is only an initial indicator; payout arrival may lag initiation/costs. Transaction/user methods exclude nontransactional costs; user effective-rate sentence omits the denominator. Three captured split rows per PSP reference should not be counted as three payments. Grouped Visa sample's 392,30 commission minus 44.65 fee does not equal shown 348.27 profit; suggested allowance has malformed currencies. Report rows and booked bank-transfer counts do not prove bank receipt or accounting sign-off. Raw lines 36–46, 55, 64–80, 87–95, 118–141, 166–174.
