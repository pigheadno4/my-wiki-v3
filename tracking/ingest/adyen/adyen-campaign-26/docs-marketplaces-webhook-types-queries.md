# Q1 — Purpose and taxonomy

The catalog routes asynchronous Marketplace events after resource changes, requests and balance-account movements (raw lines 19–23). A general envelope has `data`, `environment` and `type`, with type-specific data/status (raw lines 33–45). Configuration covers account and payout schedule/execution events, Payment method request events, Transfer request/status events, Balance changes, negative-balance warnings and report readiness (raw lines 79–199). Transfer category/type/counterparty distinguishes payment/capture/refund/chargeback, bank payout, internal transfer and third-party bank transfer (raw lines 143–162). # Q2 — Boundary and inconsistencies

Managed payout execution `succeeded` means initiated with a `transferId`, not bank receipt (raw line 108). The negative-balance warning means a liable-account compensation transfer is **scheduled** after more than 20 negative days, not completed (raw lines 172–178); report-created is ready to download, not reconciliation (raw lines 180–186). Payment webhooks are deprecated in favor of Transfer webhooks (raw lines 188–199). The opening subscription list omits Balance, though a Balance section exists (raw lines 25–31, 164–170), and some managed-payout link labels include spaces after `balancePlatform.` (raw lines 100–110), so check the linked event schema before copying identifiers.
