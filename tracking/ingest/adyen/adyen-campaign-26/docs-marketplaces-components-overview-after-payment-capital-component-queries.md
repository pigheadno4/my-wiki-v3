Q2. Do extra information, signed Terms or a displayed status prove grant credit or repayment?

A2. Route: [[index]] → [[adyen-index]] → [[adyen-platform-components]] → [[source-adyen-docs-marketplaces-components-overview-after-payment-capital-component]] → [[raw/adyen/docs/marketplaces/components-overview/after-payment/capital-component/2026-09-15]]. The >EUR 25,000 extra-information bullet says credit follows submission, but the adjacent Terms bullet requires approval and signed Terms before credit; the Pending label itself says signed but not paid out (raw lines 143–147, 180–184). Preserve that conflict rather than promise immediate funding. The flow is limited to supported countries/regions and individual eligibility; an unsupported-region screen exists (raw lines 153–166). Active, Failed, Revoked and Written off are UI status definitions, not independent account or bank proof. Unscheduled repayments must come from an Adyen-verified transfer instrument, and copying bank details does not execute one (raw lines 153, 180–188).
