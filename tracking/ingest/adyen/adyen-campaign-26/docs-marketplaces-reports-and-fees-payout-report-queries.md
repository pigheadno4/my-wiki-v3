# docs-marketplaces-reports-and-fees-payout-report — worker retrieval checks

Q1. What report is this, who can use it and what period or data does it cover?
A. It is a Balance Platform Payout Report for platforms with scheduled daily, weekly or monthly payout sweeps; the page calls it a daily report, says data appears around 2:00 AM CEST after processing, covers midnight-to-midnight CEST payouts and contributing debit/credit transactions, and generates an empty report if no payouts were processed (raw lines 15–27). Customer Area supports scheduled or manual generation, with Account Holder, Balance Account and Currency filtering (lines 29–31). Q2. What reconciliation and evidence boundaries, plus source ambiguities, matter?
A. Pending refunds/chargebacks can be withheld, leaving a rolling balance; inspect the Accounting Report for booking-before and value-after payout-date transfers (raw lines 33–37). Prose says one line per account/currency, but the sample shows multiple capture rows and a negative booked payout row for each account/currency (lines 31, 49–68). Report timing uses CEST while Payout Date is defined in CET as payout initiation, separate from booking/value dates and their timezone columns (lines 21–25, 89–92, 117). The report is scheduled-sweep/account-ledger evidence; booked transfer and initiation date do not prove bank receipt.
