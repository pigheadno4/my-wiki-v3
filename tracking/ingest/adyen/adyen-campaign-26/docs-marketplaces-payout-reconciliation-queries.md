Q1. Which report or webhook should a Marketplace use for each payout-reconciliation need?
A. The guide maps transaction composition to the Payout report, a full balance-account debit/credit trail to the Accounting report, period activity to the Statement report, and real-time status to payout transfer webhooks (raw lines 21–30). Its matrix permits transaction breakdown for managed and custom scheduled payouts, but not on-demand or CashOut; the other three methods span all four listed payout types (lines 32–41). Q2. How do amount basis and ad hoc payouts limit what a report proves?
A. The default available-balance payout accounts for pending risks and may not match recently settled sales; the described current-balance option uses a reserve account to cover risk (raw lines 43–51). The Payout report maps scheduled payout transfers to contributing payments/refunds/adjustments (lines 57–59). On-demand and CashOut do not appear there: use a unique request reference to find final Booked status and value date in Accounting (lines 61–66), then payout webhooks for status/return updates (lines 68–70). Booked ledger status or a report route alone does not prove beneficiary-bank credit.
