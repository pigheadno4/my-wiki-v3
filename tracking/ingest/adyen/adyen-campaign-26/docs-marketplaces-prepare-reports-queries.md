Q1. What preparation and permissions does Marketplace reconciliation require?
A. Understand balance-platform account structure/money flow, relevant report types and fee types (raw lines 21–27), then align reporting periods/time zones and configure only needed columns (lines 29–40, 102–107). Customer Area column changes require one of Merchant admin, Merchant report, Merchant financial or Merchant POS Report roles and can affect automated ingestion (lines 54–73). Download account and balance-platform reports via their respective guides, and test with complete sample or TEST reports before live use (lines 75–82, 109–116). Q2. Which join-key and scope qualifications prevent a false reconciliation claim?
A. The guide maps Payment/Settlement PSP, merchant and modification references to their Balance Platform Accounting report fields, with per-split Reference appearing there too (raw lines 91–100); unique references are recommended although the source calls them API-optional. Store applies when stores are used, Split payment data aids posting comparison, AMS Booking Date addresses invoice-period boundaries, and Funds Destination applies only during a Classic-to-modern upgrade with parallel reconciliation (lines 42–52). Period/timezone mismatches can break joins, and worked guides show selected rows/columns only (lines 102–116). Correctly configured reports do not prove account-specific booking or bank receipt.
