## Q2 — Capability and outcome boundary

**Query:** Does this dashboard hub establish that a particular user can refund, defend a dispute, download a complete report, or receive a payout?

**Answer:** No. It offers high-level links and descriptions of component areas (raw lines 29–59), without the child guides' role, version, scope or operation prerequisites. A displayed dashboard amount or action is not proof of settlement, bank receipt, dispute result or payment-link payment. Follow each linked child guide for behavior and limits.
