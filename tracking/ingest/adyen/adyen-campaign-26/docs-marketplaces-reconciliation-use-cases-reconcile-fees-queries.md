## Q1 — What fee grains and report sources does this Marketplace reconciliation guide use?

The guide separates per-transaction Interchange/Scheme/Markup/Commission deducted with captured payment funds, daily-total Adyen Processing fees booked to the platform liable balance account, and other fees or adjustments assessed monthly and booked as an overall liable-account adjustment (raw lines 19–25). It routes per-transaction fee data to the Balance Platform Accounting Report, event breakdowns to Payment accounting, month totals/adjustments to the invoice and daily/monthly liable-booking ties to Settlement detail (lines 27–32). The worked EUR 100 example joins Payment accounting, Settlement detail and Balance Platform Accounting (lines 34–44). ## Q2 — What timing and duplication boundaries matter when matching Processing fees?

Payment accounting confirms per-transaction fees at `SentForSettle`, tracks Processing fees on received instructions, and later uses the `Settled` event with `Payable Batch` to explain daily booking timing (raw lines 48–54). Settlement detail shows a daily aggregate `Fee` and its Creation Date/Modification Reference/Batch Number, while its repeated `Settled` transaction fee line is already in Payment accounting (lines 88–107); summing both as independent charges would overcount. BPAR shows per-transaction fee columns for a dedicated fees account and the daily Processing total as `Type: fee` on the default liable account, with booking and value dates (lines 109–132). The report snippets are selected illustrations (line 42), not proof of a platform's complete reconciliation, final settlement or bank receipt.
