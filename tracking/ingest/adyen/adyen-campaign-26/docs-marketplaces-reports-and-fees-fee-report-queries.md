# Retrieval checks — docs-marketplaces-reports-and-fees-fee-report

Q1. What does the Marketplace Balance Platform Fee Report summarize, and what is needed to retrieve and compare it?

A1. It is a monthly report of payment and processing fees incurred on users' transactions, with uncategorizable-to-user fees reported on the liable account (raw lines 15–25). The page says it is available on the first day of the month and can be generated by Customer Area schedule or manually for a specified month; it recommends API/webhook status for individual fees and report-plus-invoice comparison monthly (raw lines 23–41). Q2. Does a row's balance account identify where the fee was billed, or safely establish a fixed schema and transaction-level debit?

A2. No. The page explicitly distinguishes fee incurrence from billing location (raw line 25). The listed standard columns include account holder/account, fee type/name, currency and amount but no transaction ID (raw lines 83–92); generated reports may add columns (raw lines 43–45, 94–105). Even the sample labels `Currency` while the standard table says `Currency Code` (raw lines 49–50, 91), so inspect the exported header.
