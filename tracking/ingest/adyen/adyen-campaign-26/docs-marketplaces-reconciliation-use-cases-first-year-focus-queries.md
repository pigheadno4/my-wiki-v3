Q1. What should a Marketplace prioritize in the first year, and who needs it? Answer: Plan reporting and reconciliation separately: reporting generates statements/summaries, while reconciliation compares record sets for discrepancies. Product and Finance need cost, pricing, revenue/fee and settlement-to-internal-record views; SMB users need daily records and periodic reconciliation. Involve Finance early, with product/engineering as needed, and follow the Marketplace-level and user-level child guides. Raw lines 17–27, 29–64, 66–88.

Q2. What does this hub not prove? Answer: It defines stakeholder tasks and routes, not exact report columns, permissions, time zones, financial formulas, current tax requirements, matching results or bank receipt. Generating a report is distinct from comparing it with internal or bank records; the child guides own detailed methods. Raw lines 19–27, 49–64, 76–88.
