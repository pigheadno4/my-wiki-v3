## Overview

This [[adyen]] Marketplace-path financial-products landing page, last modified 2023-12-12 and captured 2026-09-15, lists optional products a marketplace may offer users alongside processing payments. It belongs under the existing [[adyen-financial-products]] umbrella, with [[adyen-platforms]] as the Marketplace integration context. It is product-family orientation and child-guide navigation, not evidence that a platform or user has any product enabled.

## Key takeaways

- **Business accounts:** The page describes business-related incoming transfers from third-party accounts and outgoing transfers to third-party bank accounts, such as supplier or utility payments. See raw lines 21–23 and 28–31.
- **Capital:** The page says platforms can present Adyen business-financing offers to users without an additional application process, with daily repayment from a fixed percentage of Adyen-processed volume. This is a dated product description; it does not establish a specific user's offer, acceptance, funding, debt, or eligibility. See raw lines 21–24 and 33–36.
- **Issuing:** The page describes virtual and physical Mastercard/Visa debit cards for business use, with platform management of deliveries and card customization. It does not establish card-program approval, issuance, activation or payment authorization. See raw lines 21–25 and 38–41.
- **Payouts:** This umbrella entry describes services to transfer funds to external accounts, including third parties such as suppliers or partners. A listing is not a payout execution or bank receipt. See raw lines 21–26 and 43–46.
