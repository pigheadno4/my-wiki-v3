## Overview

This pinned, **2023-modified** Marketplace page is Adyen's overview of its compliance guidelines for offering **Business accounts, Issuing, Capital, and payment processing with Adyen for Platforms**. It expressly says it **does not constitute legal advice**. Its product/topic matrix is historical source evidence, not proof of current law, product eligibility, Adyen approval or implemented compliance. See [[adyen]] and [[adyen-financial-products]] (raw lines 15–40).

## Key takeaways

- After Adyen approves a financial-product offering, materials requiring pre-approval under the guidelines must be submitted **before publication or distribution**. New marketplaces share materials with their Adyen contact; existing marketplaces submit updates through the specified Adyen review channel. Requested changes may require resubmission. Materials are also supplied annually, for periodic review or on Adyen request. This is conditional on the relevant material's pre-approval requirement, not a blanket claim that every material needs it (raw lines 42–59).
- The matrix covers user application/UI, statements, marketing, support, complaints, pricing/eligibility, reports to Adyen, recordkeeping, and France intermediary registration. Its detailed linked pages own the specific topic rules; the overview alone does not certify implementation (raw lines 61–169).
- The UI row includes not presenting the marketplace as a bank, collecting KYC and consent, giving users terms and product information, and product/region-specific disclosures. A UK/EU payee confirmation notice and EU Depositor Information Sheet are qualified to the named users; the row's payment-processing application is **UK only** (raw lines 63–85).
- Statement templates/data/contact details apply to **Business accounts and Issuing** in this matrix. Most other topic rows include Business accounts, Capital and Issuing, plus **payment processing only in the UK**. The France intermediary row applies only when the platform is registered in France and offers financial products to French users, and lists Business accounts, Capital and Issuing—not UK payment processing (raw lines 86–169).
- The matrix describes complaint acknowledgement/escalation and quarterly complaint reports; proposed user fees/credits and eligibility criteria for Adyen approval; daily fraud/card-status and quarterly complaint reporting; and copies of consent, UI, statements and marketing/communications retained for **at least five years after termination** of the financial-product offering (raw lines 117–160). These are Adyen guideline summaries in a dated source, not legal advice or evidence of actual submissions/retention.
