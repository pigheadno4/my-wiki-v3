## Overview

This 2026-09-15 Marketplace orientation page frames reporting and reconciliation requirements to prioritize during the **first year** of an embedded-payments offering. It distinguishes marketplace product/finance needs from SMB-user needs and routes to separate Marketplace-level and user-level reporting guides. It supplies stakeholder and planning context, not report-column definitions, a reconciliation procedure, or proof that any financial position matches. [[adyen]] [[adyen-reporting]]

## Key takeaways

- The page defines **reporting** as generating statements and summaries of activity, and **reconciliation** as comparing financial record sets to find discrepancies, with bank reconciliation as an example comparing internal cash records to bank records. Merely generating a report does not complete that comparison (raw lines 19–27).
- Marketplace **product** teams may own embedded-payments P&L, commercial strategy, cost structure and pricing-related reporting features. **Finance** handles budgeting, forecasting, accounting/reporting, revenue/fee reconciliation to Adyen reports and invoices, cash-flow monitoring, settlement-to-internal-record checks and audits (raw lines 29–47).
- SMB marketplace users need daily transaction and financial records, discrepancy resolution and metrics, plus periodic month/year reconciliation, statements, bank-record comparisons and tax workflows, sometimes with an external accountant. These are stakeholder tasks, not claims that this page defines current tax requirements or user permissions (raw lines 49–64).
- The guide recommends involving Finance early for requirements and education, with product and engineering according to implementation/granularity. It warns that pricing models, fund flows, currencies and statuses make late reconciliation design difficult, and routes to child guides for Marketplace-level and user-level use cases (raw lines 66–88).
- No report grain, role/access gate, timezone, status formula or financial outcome is established by this hub. Use the child process and report references before inferring booked funds, payout, beneficiary-bank receipt or completed reconciliation (raw lines 17–27, 76–88).
