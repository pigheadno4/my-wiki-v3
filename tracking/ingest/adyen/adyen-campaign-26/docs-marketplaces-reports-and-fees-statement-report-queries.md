# Q1 — Report purpose, scope and retrieval

The daily Balance Platform Statement Report shows starting balance, balance-changing transactions and ending balance for available currencies, and supports daily balance-change and scheduled/on-demand payout tracing (raw lines 17–24). Customer Area generates it automatically on a schedule or manually for a day; platform settings may add columns beyond the sample CSV (raw lines 26–32). Its account-holder and balance-account identifiers are separate; Transfer Id means pending debit/credit and Transaction Id a completed one (raw lines 38–52). # Q2 — Omission, timing and bank boundary

A balance account with opening **and** ending balance zero is omitted (raw line 19), so a missing row cannot establish no intraday movement. Booking Date and Value Date have different meanings and independent time-zone columns (raw lines 59–62). `Amount` is typed Alpha text (line 64); the Reference/Description rows duplicate pending-ID wording (lines 57–58), leaving sign and join assumptions unsupported. The report has no beneficiary bank-credit outcome; even a completed Transaction Id is a balance-account debit/credit grain, not bank receipt (raw line 52).
