Q1. What does this guide explain, and what setup is required?
A1. It traces scheduled pull-sweep and on-demand payment-request top-ups of user balance accounts through Standard, Transfer and Transaction webhook evidence. Integration is Adyen for Platforms; subscribe to the three webhook families and make the server able to receive and accept webhooks (raw lines 19–36). Scheduled events are incoming platformPayment/capture (38–57); on-demand credit is incoming topUp/TopUp while its fee is outgoing platformPayment/PaymentFee (341–361, 666–675). Q2. When is credit or fee deduction supported, and what sample boundary matters?
A2. A received/authorised Transfer event is an earlier stage. The guide associates captured with transactionId and transaction.created booked with the balance-account credit or fee deduction (raw lines 205–337, 523–664, 839–955); it does not show external debit finality or bank receipt. On-demand credit and fee are separate legs per involved account/split (341–350). The fee transaction.created example calls BA...01 although the prose/fee transfer call BA...02, repeats the credit transaction ID, and gives valueDate earlier than bookingDate (345–350, 693–705, 913–976); do not derive a matching rule from that TEST example.
