# docs-marketplaces-components-overview-after-payment-disputes-component — worker retrieval checks

Q1. What do these components do, and what access/integration prerequisites apply?
A. Disputes Overview lists chargebacks, RFIs and fraud alerts by balance account/time, while Management lets eligible users view, accept or defend chargebacks with evidence (raw lines 60–70, 132–167). The guide requires Adyen for Platforms, a Balance Platform API key assigned Disputes Component: Manage, and server-created Session authentication scoped to an account holder and `product: platform`; from library v1.6.0 the role belongs in session policy to enable processing (lines 44–56, 253–270). Action/document access also requires the sole non-liable original-payment split holder and at least one BalanceAccount split to them; refunds are unsupported (lines 52–54). Q2. What boundary prevents a component defense or displayed status from proving recovered funds?
A. Accepting a chargeback means the disputed amount will not return; defense evidence goes to card schemes for review (raw lines 148–167). An RFI does not yet withdraw funds, while a chargeback means funds have been or soon will be withdrawn; Pending means issuer review, not Won, and some disputes cannot be defended under payment-method rules (lines 193–236). The component leaves marketplace split-chargeback configuration unchanged (line 70), and successful component callbacks concern the acceptance/defense request rather than adjudication (lines 380–399). Visibility begins with disputes created in the component on/after May 22, 2025 and actions have split-role gates (lines 52–54).
