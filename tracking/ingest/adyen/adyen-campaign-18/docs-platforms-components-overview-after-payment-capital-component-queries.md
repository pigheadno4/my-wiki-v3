# Fixed retrieval checks — Platforms Capital components

Q1. What do Capital Overview and Capital Offer render, and what does a screen not prove?
- Expected: This 2026-09-15 Adyen for Platforms page describes Capital Overview and Capital Offer components. Overview shows a grant term sheet, status, repayment progress, required actions and active-grant bank details for unscheduled repayments; Offer presents a preliminary term sheet and amount/term selection within a qualifying limit. The guide says components handle offer access, Terms and grant payout steps, but an eligibility message, selected amount, submitted request, signature, Active or Fully repaid screen is not independent proof of current eligibility, accepted financing terms, a booked grant transfer, bank receipt, actual repayment or debt completion. Features vary by library version; this page does not specify a minimum version, session setup or API credential role, and links to a separate integration guide. The Capital product/country context remains separate from the component UI.

Q2. Which disclosures, required actions, status/term meanings and conflicts must be retained?
- Expected: Do not hide or alter component text naming Adyen or its local branches as loan provider or country notices such as US ECOA; the page calls the product grants/business financing too, without resolving legal characterization. It illustrates seven flow stages and an extra-information requirement for requested loans over EUR 25,000, plus Adyen Terms signing. One bullet says amount credited after extra-information submission, while the adjacent Terms bullet requires approval and signature and Pending means signed but not yet paid; submission alone is not a safe credit conclusion. Status labels include Action needed, Active (approved and paid to balance account), Failed, Fully repaid, Revoked and Written off, with distinct meanings. Active-grant UI provides details for unscheduled repayment from an Adyen-verified transfer instrument; viewing/copying details does not execute a bank transfer. The general EUR 500–50,000 equivalent range is subject to individual risk limits; 3/6/12-month terms affect rate/fees. Remaining amount excludes fees, while total repayment includes principal/grant amount and fees; missing a 30-day minimum may prompt further action. Renewal remains eligibility-gated, including before full repayment, while the table says one active grant at a time. These are dated component descriptions, not an individual offer or debt ledger.
