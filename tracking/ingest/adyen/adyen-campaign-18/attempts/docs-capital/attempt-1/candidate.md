## Overview

This 2026-09-15 Adyen Capital landing page describes an embedded business-financing product for eligible users of a platform or marketplace. It says a platform can present Adyen financing through APIs or low-code UI components, with Adyen assessing risk and proactively creating offers. The page offers a request-an-invite link and routes to integration, resources, lifecycle, and reporting guides; it is not an offer, eligibility approval, financing contract or evidence of a funded grant.

## Key takeaways

- Adyen says eligible users can receive funds to a balance account or transfer instrument after a proactive offer, without an additional application process. Eligibility, offer terms, acceptance, capability/verification, grant execution and destination rules are not established for any particular platform or user by this landing page. The account and lifecycle guides own those details.
- The page describes daily repayment as a fixed percentage of a user's processed volume through Adyen and says users pay one fixed fee with no interest or hidden costs. These are historical product descriptions in the 2026-09-15 snapshot, not a live quote, loan agreement, debt-balance calculation or a conclusion about legal characterization or consumer protections.
- The captured supported-location list names Finland, France, Netherlands, Spain, Sweden, the United Kingdom including the Channel Islands and Isle of Man, Canada excluding Saskatchewan, the United States including Puerto Rico, and Australia. It is a dated geography list, not current country, merchant, user or product eligibility proof.
- The next-step links lead to get-started, account/resource, Capital lifecycle, and reporting/reconciliation guides. The landing page's reporting description names grant repayments, webhooks, Balance Platform Accounting Report and payout report, but it does not define their report rows, join keys, grant or repayment outcomes.
