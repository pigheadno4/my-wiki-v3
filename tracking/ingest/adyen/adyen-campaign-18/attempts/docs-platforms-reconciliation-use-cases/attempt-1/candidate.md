## Overview

This [[adyen]] for Platforms page is a **navigation hub** for building reconciliation processes from balance-platform reports. It identifies prerequisite knowledge and routes to first-year priorities, payment reconciliation, fund transfers, fees and balance verification. It does not itself specify a join key, report column, sign convention, accounting treatment, timing window or proof that any reconciliation ran. See [[adyen-reporting]] and [[adyen-platforms]].

## Key takeaways

- Before building reconciliation processes, the page says to understand the payment lifecycle, the balance-platform account structure and fund flow; identify relevant report types; configure, download and combine reports; and understand fee types. The linked guides own those details. A report catalog or process outline does not establish account access, complete data, legal eligibility, final settlement or bank receipt. [Raw: Requirements, lines 21–28]
- The page routes readers to five separate topics: **first-year operational tasks**, **reconcile payments** from payment processing into balance-platform accounts, **reconcile fund transfers** for cash management with the Balance Platform Accounting Report, **reconcile fees**, and **verify balances** across the platform. These are navigation descriptions, not evidence that the child workflows were executed. [Raw: lines 30–48]
