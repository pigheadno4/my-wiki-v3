## Overview

This 2026-09-15 after-payment Platform Experience page is a navigation hub for post-payment dashboards and business-financing UI. It says users can view transactional data and monitor funds moving through balance accounts and toward bank payouts, then links to six component guides. The hub describes possible interface areas; it does not establish that a particular platform has deployed or enabled a component, that a payout reached a bank, or that financing was granted. See [[adyen]], [[adyen-platform-components]] and [[adyen-platforms]].

## Key takeaways

- **After-payment inventory:** The page links Transactions for account/date filtering, exports and detail; Payouts for automatic-payout reports, captured funds, adjustments, net amounts and remaining balances; Reports for daily report filtering/download; Disputes for viewing and defense/acceptance; Pay by Link for store-branded links, expiry and history; and Capital for financing application, qualified-grant display, Terms signature and repayment-progress view. These are brief descriptions and routes, not complete eligibility, role, version or data-scope rules.
- **Operational boundary:** Follow each child guide for authentication/session roles, available products, prerequisites, limits and action lifecycle. A dashboard view, report row, payout amount, payment-link state, dispute control or Capital progress display does not independently prove successful financial execution, beneficiary bank receipt, dispute outcome or a user's actual debt balance.
