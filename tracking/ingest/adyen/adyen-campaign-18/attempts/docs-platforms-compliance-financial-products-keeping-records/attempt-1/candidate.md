## Overview

This 2024-modified, 2026-09-15-captured [[adyen]] for Platforms financial-products compliance-guideline page says the platform must retain detailed service records while offering financial products and for a stated period afterward. It lists examples of user-experience, marketing, communication/complaint and statement records, with additional UK/Ireland vulnerable-customer recording guidance. It expressly disclaims legal advice. See [[adyen-financial-products]] for product-family context; this source is not evidence that any platform kept, delivered or retrieved a record, nor a current legal-retention determination.

## Key takeaways

- The captured page says the platform must keep detailed service records **for at least five years after termination of the financial-products offering**, unless its own retention requirements call for longer. This trigger differs from the separate customer-support/complaints pages' wording about Adyen Service termination and user-account closure; do not merge those periods into one undifferentiated rule. The page itself is dated guidance, not a current retention schedule or legal opinion. [Raw: lines 19–21]
- Listed examples include screenshots of deployed financial-product versions, application flow, user dashboard and support pages; all versions of marketing materials described in the linked communications guide; user communications and complaints, including email/chat/support interactions and related complaint documents; and historical statements generated and made available to users. Listing a statement as a record category does not establish that a particular statement was generated, available or delivered. [Raw: lines 23–33]
- Within the communications/complaints table entry, the captured text additionally identifies UK/Ireland-only vulnerable-customer recording. Systems must be able to categorize signs of vulnerability, with at least health, life events, resilience and capability options; communications from vulnerable users must be accessible, in line with legal requirements, to platform staff responsible for helping them. The table's collapsed formatting makes the regional qualifier easy to miss; retain it and use the raw passage for exact wording. [Raw: lines 29–32]
- The related compliance, UI, statements, marketing, support and complaints guides own the underlying activities. This recordkeeping page specifies categories and the general retention period, but not a storage architecture, delivery channel, audit outcome or proof of actual compliance. [Raw: lines 21–42]
