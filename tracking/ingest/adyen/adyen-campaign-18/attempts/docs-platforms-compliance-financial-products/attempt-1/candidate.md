## Overview

This Adyen for Platforms compliance-guideline hub, last modified 2023-12-11 and captured 2026-09-15, maps platform obligations and child guidance topics for Business accounts, Issuing, Capital and payment processing with Adyen for Platforms (UK-only in its matrix). It expressly disclaims legal advice. The table is a dated overview, not current law, an approved product offering, fulfilled obligation, or a live financial outcome. See [[adyen]], [[adyen-platforms]], and [[adyen-financial-products]].

## Key takeaways

- The page says platforms offering these services should follow Adyen's guidelines for UI, communications and reporting. After an offering is approved, before publishing new financial-product materials for which the guidelines require pre-approval, the platform should submit them for Adyen review, incorporate requested changes and resubmit if applicable. Materials are also provided annually, for periodic review and on Adyen's request (raw lines 19–59). This conditional review path does not itself grant approval.
- The matrix routes to child guides for user application/interface, account statements, marketing, service/support, complaints, pricing/eligibility, reporting, recordkeeping and French intermediary registration. Its applicability column distinguishes Business accounts, Capital, Issuing and **payment processing only in the UK**; account statements are listed for Business accounts and Issuing, while French registration applies to listed financial products under the France/user condition (raw lines 61–169).
- The overview highlights Adyen-provider disclosure and user UI access; Adyen-approved statement templates; approved marketing; support channels; complaint acknowledgement/escalation and quarterly reporting; fees/eligibility submitted for approval; daily fraud/card-status and quarterly complaint reports; and retaining listed records for at least five years after offering termination (raw lines 63–160). These are topic summaries; the linked child pages own implementation and qualification details.
- For a platform registered in France offering financial products to French users, the matrix says register as intermediary, add a website disclaimer and renew annually; that row applies to Business accounts, Capital and Issuing in this snapshot (raw lines 161–169). It does not prove a particular platform's French registration status.
- The overview's pricing row broadly says submit fees/credits and eligibility criteria for approval (raw lines 128–136). The separately captured [[source-adyen-docs-platforms-compliance-financial-products-pricing-and-eligibility]] distinguishes product-specific fee rules and an Adyen-review path for extra criteria; use that child source for detailed application rather than reading the overview as a universal fee rule.
