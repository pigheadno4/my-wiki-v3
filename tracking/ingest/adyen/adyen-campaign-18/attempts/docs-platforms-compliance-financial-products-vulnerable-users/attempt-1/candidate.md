## Overview

This 2026-09-15 Adyen compliance-guideline instruction page is headed “Vulnerable users (UK and Ireland only).” It addresses how platform staff who interact with users should recognize possible vulnerable circumstances, adapt support, document factual disclosures, and follow up. It describes Adyen's partner guidance, expressly not legal advice or a current legal-compliance determination. See [[adyen]], [[adyen-platforms]], and [[adyen-financial-products]].

## Key takeaways

- The page asks platforms to give these instructions to user-facing customer support, sales/marketing staff and managers, and to ensure in-scope staff understand them; unclear points go to Adyen. It also calls for time and support for agents handling distressed users (raw lines 19–39). Its title confines the page to UK and Ireland; its narrative mentions that obligations arise in some countries, without extending these instructions globally.
- Adyen describes vulnerability through health, life events, resilience and capability. In B2B, it can arise particularly for sole proprietors and can change over time; examples and behavioral cues are indicators for sensitive support, not diagnoses or proof of a named user's condition (raw lines 41–69, 77–114).
- Staff guidance uses BRUCE to notice possible decision-making difficulties, TEXAS for a supportive response including explicit consent before saving shared information, and IDEA to understand impact, duration, experience and assistance. Staff should tailor language/channel, offer appropriate referral or escalation, and check understanding (raw lines 114–169).
- Record the vulnerability indicator and support/follow-up on the customer file so users need not repeat disclosures. Notes should contain facts the user shared, not inferred diagnoses or specific conditions absent the user's explicit disclosure; consult internal privacy/legal teams about domestic recording obligations (raw lines 171–200). The example of asking finance whether extra time can be given is conditional, not a promise of an extension (raw lines 186–192).
- Follow-up where needed and periodically review interactions/feedback are recommended steps, not proof that an individual case was resolved (raw lines 202–204).
