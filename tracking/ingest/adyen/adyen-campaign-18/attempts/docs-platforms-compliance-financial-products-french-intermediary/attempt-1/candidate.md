## Overview

This 2026-09-15 Adyen for Platforms compliance-guideline page describes an ORIAS intermediary-registration path for a platform incorporated and registered in France that offers Adyen financial products to French users. It expressly disclaims legal advice. The parent compliance matrix lists the France topic for Business accounts, Capital and Issuing; this page is not proof that any platform must register now, has been accepted, or may offer a product. See [[adyen]], [[adyen-platforms]], and [[adyen-financial-products]].

## Key takeaways

- The page states a conjunctive trigger: offering Adyen to French users **and** platform incorporation/registration in France; its requirements table narrows it to a Platforms integration offering financial products to French users. It describes ORIAS registration as part of Adyen's dated guidelines, not a universal rule for every French customer or a current legal determination (raw lines 19–36).
- For ORIAS registration, the guide calls for professional-capacity proof for at least one director: specified recent work experience, relevant diploma, or approved training, with disqualifying conviction/disciplinary conditions. The exact categories, dates and legal references need current confirmation before acting (raw lines 38–57).
- The guide maps Level I/II/III IOBSP categories to stated NAF codes and describes a usual validation period of less than two months, followed by annual renewal. That timing is an estimate, not approval or entitlement (raw lines 59–67).
- Its procedure calls for a recent kbis extract, director capacity proof, an ORIAS legal-person account, specific IOB activity declarations, Adyen's Attestation de Mandat after supplying SIREN/legal name, upload and payment. After ORIAS email approval it instructs adding a prescribed website disclaimer and renewing annually (raw lines 69–102). The instruction to declare that no funds are entrusted to the intermediary is a registration choice in this guide, not independent proof of a particular platform's money flow.
