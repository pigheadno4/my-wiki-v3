## Overview

This Capital compliance-guidelines overview (last modified 2022-10-26; captured 2026-09-15) says a balance platform offering Capital to its users must follow Adyen's guidelines on interface, communications and reporting. The page expressly says it is not legal advice. Its “must” language describes Adyen's dated guidelines for this product and platform relationship, not current law, a certification of compliance, current Capital access or an approved user offer. See [[adyen]], [[adyen-capital]] and [[adyen-financial-products]].

## Key takeaways

- **Review and pre-approval:** After Adyen approves the financial-product offering, the page says that before publishing or distributing new financial-product material, the platform must submit it for review and pre-approval **where pre-approval of that material is required under the Compliance Guidelines**. A new balance platform shares material with its Adyen contact; an existing platform updating material emails merchantkyc@adyen.com. It describes incorporating requested changes and resubmitting where applicable. It also says materials are provided annually, for periodic review and on Adyen request. This is a conditional review process, not blanket approval of a product, asset or campaign.
- **Topic map:** The overview points to separate Capital guidance for user application/interface (no platform-as-bank presentation, request and KYC flow, terms and consent, access to Capital information); marketing and user communications (collateral, Adyen approval and provider disclosure); support; complaints and escalation/quarterly reporting; pricing and eligibility criteria submitted for Adyen approval; reporting to Adyen; and recordkeeping for at least five years after ending the financial-product offering. The table is a topic-level summary, so consult its linked detail guides for conditions and exact procedures.
- **France-specific branch:** The table says a platform registered in France that offers Capital to French users must register as an intermediary, add a website disclaimer and renew registration annually. This is a dated Adyen guideline with both country and activity conditions; it is not a statement of current French law or proof of registration.
- **Evidence boundary:** The page references Adyen's separate best-practices/templates document for demonstrating compliance, drafting policies and complaint handling. The reference is navigation; this pinned page does not itself prove that any platform submitted material, received Adyen approval, trained support, sent reports, kept records or satisfied legal obligations.
