## Overview

This Adyen for Platforms financial-products compliance-guideline page, last modified 2023-12-12 and captured 2026-09-15, describes user-application and interface disclosures, consent, account access and product/country-specific display requirements. It expressly disclaims legal advice. These are dated Adyen guideline statements, not proof of current law, product availability, configuration, user consent, or an executed financial operation. See [[adyen]], [[adyen-platforms]], and [[adyen-financial-products]].

## Key takeaways

- The page calls for a UI where users request only financial services agreed between the platform and Adyen, supply required KYC information, see agreements/terms and fees, give consent, retain access to accepted agreements, and access applicable account/transaction/fee information free of charge. Hosted onboarding hosts KYC fields, agreements and terms at Adyen; some requirements may be embedded in Adyen UI components. The platform should not present itself as a bank, should identify Adyen as financial service provider, and should not preselect acknowledgement options (raw lines 19–43).
- Product-specific terms and UI content differ for UK-only payment processing, business accounts, Issuing and Capital. The dated table further limits EU business-account depositor information sheets; EU/UK verification/confirmation-of-payee notices; UK BACS Direct Debit mandate, transaction and refund-claim UI; US business-account FDIC and internet-gambling wording; US Issuing/Capital decline and credit disclosures; Canada Capital fee display; and Ireland Capital contract-stage disclosures/consent (raw lines 45–150). An interface prompt or refund-request path is not a completed bank, loan, mandate, refund or transfer operation.
- The attached disclosure/consent procedure distinguishes making information known from freely given, informed, specific agreement. It says only an Authorized Signatory natural person can give consent to Adyen, service-line rules determine required disclosures, and evidence of consent through onboarding forms or APIs must be retained (raw lines 152–186). The source does not establish that any particular signatory was verified or agreed.
