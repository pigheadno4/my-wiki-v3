## Overview

This 2026-09-15 Adyen N.V. EU depositor information sheet presents a dated summary of Dutch statutory deposit-guarantee coverage, limits, aggregation, joint-account treatment, reimbursement and DNB contact details. It is disclosure content, not a platform implementation guide, current legal advice, confirmation that a particular account/deposit qualifies, or evidence of actual reimbursement. See [[adyen]], [[adyen-financial-products]], and [[source-adyen-docs-platforms-compliance-financial-products-user-interface]] for the separate EU Business accounts delivery/consent instruction.

## Key takeaways

- The sheet says Adyen N.V. deposits are protected by the Dutch statutory Deposit Guarantee Scheme, with EUR 100,000 per depositor per credit institution, and deposits at the same institution/trademarks aggregated toward that limit (raw lines 23–25, 34–35). It does not establish that every product or depositor is covered: the sheet itself notes exceptions and says coverage of particular products can be checked with the institution (raw line 32).
- For joint accounts, the sheet states a per-depositor limit but qualifies that deposits of a business partnership, association or similar grouping without legal personality are aggregated and treated as one depositor for the limit (raw lines 26, 36). Do not apply the individual joint-account line to that exception.
- The captured table says euro reimbursement within 10 working days after institution failure; its note says the period will gradually move to seven working days and mentions transitional access for basic needs. These are dated sheet statements, not a current timetable or guarantee that a named depositor will receive funds by a particular date (raw lines 27–28, 37).
- The pinned sheet does **not** specify when or how a platform must deliver it or record user consent. The separately captured [[source-adyen-docs-platforms-compliance-financial-products-user-interface]] states that EU Business accounts users need the PDF before offering and annually thereafter, in the sheet's language, with UI confirmation of acceptance (that source's raw lines 69–75). Keep that obligation attributed to the related dated Adyen guideline, not to this sheet and not as a present legal conclusion.

- Product/depositor exceptions and confirmation on account statements: raw line 32.
