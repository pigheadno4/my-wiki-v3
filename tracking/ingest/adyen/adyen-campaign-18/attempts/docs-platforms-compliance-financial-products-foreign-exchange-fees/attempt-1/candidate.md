## Overview

This 2026-09-15 Adyen financial-products/Platforms compliance-guideline page addresses clear presentation of FX rates and extra fees when a user's transaction converts currencies. It expressly is not legal advice. Its GBP/USD tables are presentation examples, not current tariffs, a conversion quote, a product/region eligibility matrix, actual funds movement or beneficiary-bank receipt. See [[adyen]], [[adyen-platforms]], and [[adyen-financial-products]].

## Key takeaways

- The dated Adyen guideline says users whose transactions include any currency conversion **must** be clearly presented with the FX rate and additional fees both at transaction time and in statements for the relevant period (raw lines 19–23). The page says its examples draw on UK FCA guidance but applies this presentation guidance to platforms in all regions; that is a provider statement, not current law in every region or an EEA-specific role rule (raw line 101).
- A variable-fee GBP/USD example shows £100 paid, 3% (£3) fee, £97 converted at 1.30 and $126.10 received (raw lines 25–36). Fixed-fee examples present upfront amount, conversion value, fee, and recipient amount, with a page statement that the Adyen exchange rate includes a 3% markup in those examples (raw lines 38–72). None establish a live Adyen rate or applicable fee.
- The first “Fixed Fee” table is internally inconsistent: it labels a 1 GBP to 1.26 USD exchange but shows the recipient as £126.10, while £100 × 1.26 would be $126.00 before considering its separately listed £3 total fee (raw lines 40–48). Preserve it as a source example and do not use it to compute an executable quote.
- Third-party-fee examples distinguish an upfront “Pay all fees?” choice from recipient-paid intermediary deductions. The page says intermediary banks may deduct fees and presents an upfront option as intended-amount protection, while another example says the recipient bears third-party charges (raw lines 74–99). Those example claims do not prove a particular bank's fees, actual recipient amount, or settlement.
