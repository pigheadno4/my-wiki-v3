Q1. What is this document's purpose and scope, and what must not be inferred?
A1. The 2026-09-15 page, titled “Vulnerable users (UK and Ireland only),” gives Adyen partner staff practical guidance to recognize and assist platform users in vulnerable circumstances (raw lines 15–29). It explicitly says it is an overview of Adyen compliance guidelines and not legal advice. It does not establish a named user's vulnerability, a diagnosis, current jurisdictional duties outside its stated scope, or that a platform has complied.
