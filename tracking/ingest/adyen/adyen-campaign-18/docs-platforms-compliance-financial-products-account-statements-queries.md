Q2. Which statement, access and regional qualifications govern Business accounts and Issuing?
- Expected: Business-account statements include institution/platform identity, holder and masked account details, statement period, opening/closing balances, transaction/fee data and totals; EU/US bank accounts and UK business accounts have different account and disclosure language, with interest details conditional. For Issuing, the page distinguishes prepaid/debit/credit programs and regional account types; EU may show all required information in UI without a download, while UK requires a downloadable PDF or other durable statement. The later prepaid exception removes a periodic-send requirement but requires on-demand access to all card transactions; UI display invokes screenshot submission for Adyen review/approval and retention as replacement records. Do not impose the EU/UK download rule on US from silence, or apply the prepaid exception to all card programs.
