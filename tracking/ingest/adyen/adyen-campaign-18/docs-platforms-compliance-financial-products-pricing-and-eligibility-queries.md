Q2. What is the central capability, what prerequisites and consequential warnings apply?
A2. As a dated Adyen guideline, the platform's terms of service and fee schedule must clearly outline its implemented fees and terms alongside Adyen-supplied agreements (raw line 28); this is not a current legal conclusion. The page distinguishes EU/US 30-day and UK 60-day written fee-increase notice; Platforms pre-enrollment and FX-rate/fee disclosures; Business accounts/Issuing advance Adyen agreement for platform fees; EU/UK Business accounts equal instant/regular payment pricing; and Capital's no-extra-fee rule with prior-written-approval condition for discounts (raw lines 26–54). It generally disallows extra eligibility criteria beyond Adyen's Business accounts, Capital and Issuing requirements, while describing a review path for any extra request criteria and requiring advance user disclosure/fairness; the UK-only questionnaire is an intake for proposed pricing, not approval (raw lines 56–93). These dated distinctions do not establish current law, actual fee/rate, approved platform criteria or a user's eligibility.
