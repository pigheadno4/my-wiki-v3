Q2. How do fee grain, account destination and matching fields differ across the three reports?
- Expected: Per-transaction Interchange, Scheme, Markup and Commission are deducted when captured funds book to a balance account, assigned to liable or user balance account by split instructions. The Payment accounting report gives event-level fees and an example `SentForSettle` confirmation; `Settled` plus Payable Batch explains later processing-fee timing. Processing fees accumulate per received instruction, then are assessed as a daily total to the liable balance account. Settlement detail has aggregate `Fee` with Creation Date, Modification Reference and Batch Number, and later monthly `InvoiceDeduction`; do not double-count its overlapping `Settled` per-transaction row. Balance Platform Accounting identifies per-transaction fee account/columns and daily-in-arrears liable-account `fee` row with PSP Modification Merchant Reference and distinct Booking/Value dates. Other/volume-adjusted fees are monthly invoice adjustments to the liable account. Match account, reference, report event, currency and dates within the illustrated report grain; raw tables own the exact fields and signs.
