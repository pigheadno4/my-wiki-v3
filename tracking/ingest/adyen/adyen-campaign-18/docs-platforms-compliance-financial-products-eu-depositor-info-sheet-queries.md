Q1. What is this document's purpose and scope, and what must not be inferred?
A1. The 2026-09-15 EU depositor information sheet summarizes the Dutch statutory Deposit Guarantee Scheme for Adyen N.V. deposits, limits, aggregation, exceptions and reimbursement information (raw lines 15–37). It is a dated disclosure sheet, not a platform delivery procedure, current legal advice, proof that a specific product/deposit is covered, or a live reimbursement.
