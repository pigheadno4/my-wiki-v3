Q1. What is this document's purpose and scope, and what must not be inferred?
A1. This 2023-modified, 2026-09-15-captured page is the Adyen Platforms financial-products compliance-guideline hub for Business accounts, Issuing, Capital and UK-only payment processing, pointing to topic-specific child guidance (raw lines 15–40, 59–169). It expressly is not legal advice. Its overview matrix does not prove a current legal rule, approved offering/material, completed review or operational compliance. Q2. What is the central capability, what prerequisites and consequential warnings apply?
A2. The page maps UI, statements, marketing, support, complaints, pricing/eligibility, reports, records and conditional France intermediary registration to the listed products, and states as a dated Adyen requirement that platforms must follow its UI, communications and reporting guidelines and, after an offering is approved, must submit new financial-product materials where pre-approval is required before publishing; it also describes annual/periodic/on-request review (raw lines 29–59, 61–169). Payment-processing rows are UK-only; account-statement rows are Business accounts/Issuing; the France row has its own registered-platform/French-user condition. The overview's broad fees/criteria approval row should be read with [[source-adyen-docs-platforms-compliance-financial-products-pricing-and-eligibility]] for detailed product distinctions. Linked child topics are navigation rather than proof that any platform met them.
