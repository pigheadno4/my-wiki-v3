A1. Q2. What is the central capability, what prerequisites and consequential warnings apply?
A2. The Overview lists chargebacks, RFIs, and fraud alerts by account/period; Management presents detail and can accept or defend eligible chargebacks by sending evidence for review (raw lines 60–91, 132–167). It requires Platforms integration, a Balance Platform API key with Disputes Component: Manage, a server-created account-holder-scoped Session authentication token and client initialization; from library v1.6.0 the role goes in the session policy (raw lines 44–56, 245–299, 355–502). Visibility is limited to disputes created in the component on or after May 22, 2025; action/document access needs the sole non-liable original-payment account holder and at least one BalanceAccount split. Upload limits apply, refunds are unsupported, features vary by library version, acceptance forfeits return of the disputed amount, and Pending means issuer review rather than a win (raw lines 36–38, 52–56, 148–152, 193–236).
