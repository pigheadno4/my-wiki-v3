2. **Overview detail — PASS.** 3DS shares data across issuer, acquirer and interoperability domains and adds checkout identity verification to reduce fraud and help meet SCA. 3DS2 adds device/data transfer for issuer risk assessment; fewer challenges are intended benefits, not guarantees (raw lines 16, 21–27). SCA uses two independent factors from knowledge, possession and inherence; supported methods vary by issuer (32–39). This snapshot says its PSD2 condition requires both issuer and acquirer countries to be regulated (44), not current legal advice. Integration generates a client token, collects payment information, verifies the amount, and may prompt authentication at issuer/local-legislation direction (49–55). A successful or unnecessary authentication supplies a nonce or `authentication_id` for a later transaction/customer/payment-method operation (57–60), not completed authorization or settlement. Its broad liability statement is qualified by the fully read support article: shift is conditional and does not guarantee automatic chargeback representation; merchants still monitor and act (support raw 66–74). 4. **JavaScript v3 advanced detail — PASS.** `verifyCard()` returns liability indicators and a new nonce for a later transaction; client-exposed advanced data is UI-only, not trusted server risk input (17–65). Failed authentication carries card-brand advice to request another method, but a permitting server risk process may proceed with `required: false`; both false means ineligible/no shift, and Amex may revoke a reported shift (57–63). Server nonce/transaction 3DS reporting is separate (65–259). `collectDeviceData` may reduce rejects/challenges (261–274); vaulted cards need a server-generated nonce from the stored token and `customer_id`, followed by client verification (277–331). `challengeRequested: true` requests an issuer challenge (334–336). Domestic Brazil combo-card choice propagates to `accountType` and the corresponding API operation (339–375). Google Pay is eligible only for non-network-tokenized PAN cards, not DPAN; custom UI checks `isNetworkTokenized === false` (377–401). SCA exemptions are issuer-discretionary with merchant liability: low value is below EUR 30 with five-consecutive / EUR 100 accumulation conditions; TRA is below EUR 250, fraud-rate-qualified and not enabled by default (403–418). `Transaction.sale()` exemptions do not override prior 3DS logic; `requestedExemptionType` requests no challenge if conditions hold, else standard authentication (421–435). Data-only is frictionless/no shift, limited to Mastercard/Maestro and Visa on select processors outside PSD2 countries; check Control Panel support, unsupported requests fall back, and Rules Manager still requires `verifyCard()` (438–446). US Visa DCAP is an eligible-transaction route with device ID/IP/billing/email inputs (448–454). Visa DAF establishes an issuer-step-up credential for same instrument/merchant, up to two years; PSD2 requires acquirer TRA, regional exclusions apply, and acquiring-bank confirmation is required (457–463). These authentication options do not authorize, capture or settle payment. Citation: `[[source-braintree-3d-secure-advanced-options-javascript-v3]]`.
