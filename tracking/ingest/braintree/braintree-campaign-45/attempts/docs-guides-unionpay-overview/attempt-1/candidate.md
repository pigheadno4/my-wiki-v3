## Overview

This collected, unversioned Braintree website overview states that the dedicated UnionPay integration is deprecated because UnionPay can now be processed as a credit card through its partnership with Discover. The page routes readers to the credit-card guide and separately describes UnionPay as commonly accepted outside the United States.

## Key takeaways

- The captured page says Braintree SDKs enable merchants to accept UnionPay cards like other credit or debit cards, but it names no SDK, package version, client/server placement, environment, merchant qualification or operational procedure.
- Treat the statement as snapshot documentation of the provider's migration direction, not proof of current support, merchant or card eligibility, enablement, successful authorization, settlement or funding.
- The linked credit-card guide is navigation only for this entry; it was not read as behavioral evidence.
