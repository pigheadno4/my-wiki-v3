4. **AVS/CVV versus velocity / tool-qualified override — PASS.** AVS/CVV uses issuer-returned match codes after issuer approval; configured rules may then gateway-reject and send a void request, which a bank may not recognize immediately (`raw/.../avs-cvv-rules-2026-09-16.md:25-29`). It defaults to first-time rather than recurring/vaulted-card transactions; checking before vaulting requires card verification, and CVV must be recollected (`:63-71`). Its documented exception route is per-API-request selective `skip_avs`/`skip_cvv`, or another payment method (`:110-112`). Risk-threshold rules are velocity checks over repeated values within a window, with Email or Gateway Reject actions; submitted transactions must carry every configured Field the rule relies on (`raw/.../risk-threshold-rules-2026-09-16.md:22-28,75-81,96-156`). Their only documented override is temporarily disabling the rules in the Control Panel, which the guide typically does not recommend (`:161-163`). Google Pay support is method-qualified: Google-account cards support risk thresholds, while device-added Android cards do not support Basic Fraud Tools; the Google Pay page's later underqualified generic sentence must not be generalized (`raw/.../payment-methods/google-pay-2026-09-16.md:87-99`). The overview source correctly treats its umbrella “override” wording as navigation, not one universal mechanism (`source:18-24,38-42`).
