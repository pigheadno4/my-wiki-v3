## Overview

This collected Braintree article is a direct-link portal for merchants told that at least one of their merchant accounts is provisioned by Wells Fargo with IC+ pricing. It routes those merchants to articles tailored to their account nuances; the articles are accessible only by direct links and cannot be found through Braintree's support-site search.

The 2026-09-16 snapshot is Braintree-hosted account navigation, not Wells Flat documentation, independent current Wells Fargo policy, or proof of current eligibility, pricing, payment execution, settlement or funding.

## Key takeaways

- The page says accepted payment types and other aspects of payment processing can vary by banking partner, so generic public support articles can omit important account-specific details.
- According to the page, the partner bank was determined during the application process and depends mostly on Braintree signup timing and the business's domicile.
- A merchant with multiple merchant accounts may have multiple partner banks. Keep each article's merchant-account, banking-partner and pricing-model scope attached rather than transferring its guidance to another account.
- The page recommends bookmarking this portal. Its IC+ pricing link is a navigation route; this overview does not itself state rates, fee treatment or pricing eligibility details.

> [!warning] Keep the Wells IC account scope attached
> Receiving this portal link means the page says at least one merchant account is Wells Fargo-provisioned with IC+ pricing; it does not establish that every account has that processor or pricing model. Do not apply this portal or its linked guidance to Wells Flat or another banking partner without verifying that account's route.
