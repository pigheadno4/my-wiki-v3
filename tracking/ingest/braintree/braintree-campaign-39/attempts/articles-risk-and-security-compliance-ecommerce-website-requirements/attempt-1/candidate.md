## Overview

This collected [[braintree]] article describes website, mobile, contract and invoice disclosures that Braintree says merchants should maintain, along with additional website or mobile-app pages required for accepting PayPal through Braintree. It reports that applicable policy requirements vary with operating location, accepted card brands and business model.

## Key takeaways

- Braintree says it periodically reviews merchant websites and directs merchants to clearly disclose contact information, pricing, refund or cancellation policies, privacy or personally identifiable information policies, and a delivery time frame when physical goods are shipped. The accepted contact-information options are retained in the raw locator.
- Pricing should be clear before payment. When pricing exists only in a custom contract or invoice, customers must agree to it and be able to find contact, privacy and refund/cancellation information there. For members-only pricing, the site must disclose that pricing is available after login; making contact and policy information available to non-members is presented as a recommendation, not a requirement. The article also accepts preset and custom donation amounts for nonprofit organizations.
- A mobile-only merchant must either satisfy the ecommerce requirements inside its mobile app or mobile site, or link from that platform to the requirements on its full site.
- The refund/cancellation policy must appear even when the merchant offers no refunds, and should state whether refunds are offered, eligibility conditions and any refund or cancellation fees. The privacy policy must state what customer information is collected and what the merchant does with it.
- Before accepting PayPal payments through Braintree, the article says the website or mobile app must include privacy-policy and terms-and-conditions pages, whose links are supplied when PayPal credentials are entered in the Braintree Control Panel. Its list of typical terms-and-conditions topics is descriptive guidance rather than a claim that every listed topic is mandatory.

> [!warning] Scope and compliance boundary
> This is a 2026-09-16 snapshot of Braintree-hosted guidance. The article itself says requirements can vary by operating location, accepted card brands and business model; it is not current law, legal advice, proof of compliance, account eligibility, successful credential setup or permission to accept PayPal.
