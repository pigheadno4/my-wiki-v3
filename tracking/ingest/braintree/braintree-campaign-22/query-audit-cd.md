- **Detail question:** “What funding and reconciliation distinctions does this article itself state?” Object/action match: PayPal fund destination/withdrawal is distinguished from reconciliation reports and identifiers. Direct answer: the article says PayPal does not affect credit-card funding. PayPal transactions default to the PayPal Business Account balance; funds can be withdrawn manually or through Settlement Withdrawal, which the page says sends the previous day's transactions to the bank within 2–3 business days and provides a daily report of transactions included in each withdrawal. For reconciliation it recommends Transaction Details and Settlement reports for larger merchants, Monthly Financial Summary for small/medium merchants, and use of those reports with Braintree's Settlement Batch Summary for any size. For a specific transaction, the PayPal console exposes additional fee/net-deposit detail and can be searched by payer email or PayPal Transaction ID. That PayPal ID maps to Braintree's Authorization Unique Transaction ID and differs from the Braintree transaction ID. None of these records alone proves an individual bank deposit. Exact locators: raw lines 20–31, 34–38, and 41–58. **PASS**.
