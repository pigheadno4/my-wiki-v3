# Braintree C23 retrospective

Ten Marketplace pages were ingested through three dynamic worker/reviewer slots. First-review approval was **5/10**; five bounded corrections passed targeted re-review, and the fixed query audit passed **20/20**. Runtime wall-clock closure was **1h 41m 32s** (`2026-09-30T09:15:20Z` to `10:56:52Z`), including dispatch, reviews, local promotion, aggregate updates and query/close audits. The runtime does not record reliable per-stage durations, so this report does not assign exact worker, reviewer or coordinator time.

Three first-pass blocks involved the same known recurring-billing contradiction: the testing/go-live guide discusses recreating settings while the two Marketplace overviews and recurring-billing overview state incompatibility. The other two blocks were a too-broad registered-business required-field claim and omitted funding follow-up/webhook actions. These were material precision issues, not schema or link failures. The sources total 5,669 `wc -w` words, versus 6,264 for C22's different ten-page selection; the lower word count alone does not establish a faster or better method.

The overall elapsed time remains too high for routine ten-page production. The narrowest next improvement is to pass a newly discovered cross-page conflict to other still-running or queued same-topic workers as ordinary dispatch context, and to keep the one-pass subject/condition/action self-check focused on requiredness and operational warnings. Reuse targeted review for bounded fixes. Do not add a new registry, audit layer or validator solely for this campaign.
