## Overview

This 2026-09-16 collected, unversioned [[braintree]] webpage is a directory for enterprise merchants connecting Braintree with named ecommerce and ERP platforms. It lists SAP Commerce Cloud (Hybris), Salesforce Commerce Cloud (Demandware), NetSuite ERP and IBM WebSphere Commerce, with snapshot platform/version labels, integration details, capability inventories and marketplace or Braintree Plugins-team contact routes. It does not identify who develops, owns, maintains or security-reviews each plugin, and it is not current compatibility, endorsement, merchant-enablement or payment-execution evidence.

## Key takeaways

- SAP Commerce Cloud, Salesforce Commerce Cloud and IBM WebSphere Commerce are categorized as ecommerce integrations; NetSuite is categorized as an Enterprise Resource Planning integration. Those labels describe the collected directory, not an independent classification or current product relationship.
- The captured version inventory names Hybris Commerce v6.0-6.7 with B2B and B2C labels, Demandware v18 and IBM WebSphere Commerce v.9. The NetSuite section has no captured `Supported versions` field. Treat all named versions as page-snapshot labels rather than proof of present compatibility or support.
- The SAP, Salesforce and IBM sections describe checkout-interface, transaction/customer-management, PayPal Smart Payment Button and manual phone-order capabilities, with platform-specific payment-method and processing/settlement lists. The NetSuite section instead lists OneWorld/multiple-currency, fraud/L2/L3, payment-method, processing and tokenization-related items. These inventories are directory claims, not proof of secure implementation, merchant configuration, authorization, settlement or funding.
- The page routes readers to the SAP App Center or Salesforce AppExchange where applicable and to a Braintree Plugins-team email for more information or support. A marketplace or contact route does not establish plugin authorship, certification, endorsement, maintenance responsibility, response availability or compatibility with a merchant's environment.

## Material qualifications

> [!warning] Snapshot and responsibility boundary
> The page was collected on 2026-09-16 and provides no current-validation date, security-review statement, maintenance owner or universal compatibility commitment. Verify platform releases, plugin ownership, support terms, security posture and merchant/account eligibility separately.

> [!warning] Capability and payment boundary
> Listed methods and functions are directory inventory, not evidence that a plugin is installed, configured or enabled for a merchant, or that any authorization, settlement or funding succeeded.
