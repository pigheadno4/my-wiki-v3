## Overview

This collected 2026-09-16, unversioned [[braintree|Braintree]] website page routes In-Person hardware and integration issues to support paths for Sandbox or Production and describes the issue information those paths request. It is a support-routing snapshot, not evidence of current service availability, response or resolution times, account eligibility, reader state, or a transaction, authorization, payment, settlement or funding outcome.

## Key takeaways

- For general issues, the page directs the requester to submit a Braintree Help Desk ticket and select **In-Person Payment Support**; it also recommends notifying the assigned Customer Success Manager to help route the request within PayPal.
- The requested diagnostic information depends on the issue rather than forming a universal checklist. Hardware examples cover reader identity/model, observed behavior, reproduction evidence, network setup and Sandbox-versus-Production environment; software/API examples cover request identity and time, available raw request/response, reader/API behavior and errors, reproduction steps, the account's public gateway ID and environment.
- For Sandbox issues, the page directs the requester to the PayPal/Braintree integration team for troubleshooting and also points to the general support-request process.
- For urgent Production hardware issues, escalation is presented as conditional: first contact the requester's internal IT help desk and the Braintree Help Desk, then provide the support ticket number when escalating to the hardware support team. The page scopes that escalation channel to Production card-reader replacement requests and hardware troubleshooting; it does not promise availability, response time or resolution.
- New-hardware orders and onboarding use separate Help Desk issue selections; those routine contact choices remain at the verified raw locators below.
