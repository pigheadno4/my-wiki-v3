## Overview

This collected iOS v7 route is a branding and asset guide for the closed-beta [[braintree-auth|Braintree Auth]] merchant connection experience from [[braintree]]. In the captured snapshot it identifies the merchant-facing brand as PayPal powered by Braintree and provides platform presentation guidance for dashboards, marketing sites or materials, and the Connect with Braintree call to action. Most of the page is platform branding guidance; the iOS-specific item is a downloadable Connect button asset. The snapshot does not establish current availability, account eligibility, SDK support, hosted-modal behavior, payment acceptance or parity with another platform or version.

## Key takeaways

- The page marks Braintree Auth as closed beta and directs interested platforms to contact Braintree; that is a snapshot qualification, not evidence of current access.
- It directs platforms to present the unified Braintree Auth and Connect experience under the PayPal powered by Braintree merchant-facing brand, with dashboard logo assets.
- The payment-method section provides separate marks for cards plus PayPal and for cards only. These presentation assets do not prove current method availability or successful payment acceptance for a platform or merchant.
- The page recommends explanatory copy beside the Connect button. Its examples distinguish a platform intending to create transactions for merchants from one connecting Braintree merchants without running transactions; the wording is recommended example copy, not an implementation or payment-support guarantee.
- The Button section links an iOS asset bundle for the Connect with Braintree button. It does not document an iOS SDK integration, Connect modal behavior or equivalent behavior in another variant.
