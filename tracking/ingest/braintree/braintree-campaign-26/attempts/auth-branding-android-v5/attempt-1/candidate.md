## Overview

This collected Android v5 route is a branding and asset guide for the closed-beta [[braintree-auth|Braintree Auth]] merchant connection experience from [[braintree]]. In the captured snapshot it identifies the merchant-facing brand as PayPal powered by Braintree and gives platform-facing presentation guidance for dashboards, marketing materials and the Connect with Braintree call to action. Most of the page is general platform branding guidance; the Android-specific item is a downloadable Connect button asset. The snapshot does not establish current availability, eligibility, SDK support, hosted-modal behavior or parity with another platform or version.

## Key takeaways

- The page marks Braintree Auth as closed beta and directs interested platforms to contact Braintree; that is a snapshot qualification, not evidence of current access.
- It tells platforms to present the unified Braintree Auth and Connect experience under the PayPal powered by Braintree merchant-facing brand, with logo assets for dashboard use.
- It provides payment-method marks and recommends explanatory copy for the Connect button. The two HTML examples distinguish a platform intending to create transactions for merchants from one linking Braintree merchants without running transactions; they are examples, not a transaction-support guarantee or implementation specification.
- The Button section links an Android asset bundle for the Connect with Braintree button. The page does not document an Android SDK integration or establish equivalent assets and behavior for other variants.
