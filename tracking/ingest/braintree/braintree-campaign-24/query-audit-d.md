2. **What settings, conditions and consequential warnings does this article itself state?** Object/action match: **yes** — answer is limited to retry/proration settings stated by this article. Direct answer: under Recurring Billing Options, merchants can configure automatic retry and proration. A `FAILED` transaction receives three built-in retries (up to two days) before `Past Due`; `PROCESSOR*DECLINED` or `GATEWAY_REJECTED` moves immediately to `Past Due`, after which at least two more retries can use 1–10 day intervals. Final failure can cancel, continue once per later billing cycle, or leave the subscription past due; leaving it past due stops attempts while its balance accrues. A successful manual retry of any amount resets the subscription balance to `$0`; a failed manual retry does not consume an automatic attempt. Some processor decline codes are never automatically retried. Mid-cycle proration is based on days remaining and the account time zone: upgrades/add-ons can charge immediately, while downgrades/discounts credit the subscription balance rather than refunding the payment method; the per-subscription option overrides gateway defaults. A failed upgrade-proration charge can either leave subscription and balance unchanged or keep the higher price and add the failed amount to the balance, depending on the setting.
