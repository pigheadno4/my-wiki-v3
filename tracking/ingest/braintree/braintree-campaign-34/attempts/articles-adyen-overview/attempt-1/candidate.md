## Overview

This collected Braintree article is a direct-link portal for merchants told that at least one of their merchant accounts is provisioned by Adyen. It routes those merchants to articles tailored to account-specific nuances and recommends bookmarking the portal because the articles cannot be found by searching the Braintree support website.

This is Braintree-owned processor and merchant-account navigation, not independent current Adyen authority and not the separate PayPal Orchestration Adyen integration guide.

## Key takeaways

- The page says aspects of payment processing, including accepted payment types, can vary by banking partner; generic public support articles can therefore omit important account-specific details.
- The partner bank was determined during the application process and, according to this snapshot, depends mostly on when the merchant signed up with Braintree and where the business is domiciled.
- A merchant with multiple merchant accounts may have multiple partner banks. Keep processor, account and domicile qualifications attached rather than transferring details between accounts or processors.

> [!warning] Use the account-specific route
> This portal identifies a tailored Braintree documentation route; it does not itself enumerate accepted payment types or establish current account eligibility. Do not substitute independent Adyen documentation or the distinct Orchestration integration route for the linked account-specific Braintree guidance.
