An additional currency requires another merchant account, applicable presentment/settlement guidance, and an integration update specifying its merchant account ID. Customer-bank conversion may add fees and complicate refunds, increasing chargeback risk. Detailed default/Amex setup is at `17-47`, method qualifications at `52-67`, and currency instructions/warnings at `70-80`.
