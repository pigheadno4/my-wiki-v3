# Metronome Campaign 25 Retrospective

## Outcome

- Final content quality: six of six pages approved; the fixed three-page query audit passed 9/9 without expansion.
- First-pass efficiency: three of six pages passed attempt 1; three required one full semantic retry.
- Retry shape: nine worker attempts and nine independent full Sol reviews; zero targeted reviews, failed attempts, or rejected jobs.
- Total elapsed time through promotion, close validation, and final state write: 3,796 seconds (1 hour 3 minutes 16 seconds).
- Coordinator repairs: 1 bounded state-attribution repair; no semantic source repair.

## What worked

The Minimum Sufficient Source contract produced concise query routers across mutation, list/schema, integration, API read, legacy-plan read, and SDK overview pages. Every final source retains exact immutable raw navigation, and the fixed audit passed without expansion. Reviewer-approved concept work was grouped into 16 targets and each target was written once before source promotion, so shared files remained coordinator-owned without worktrees or merge conflicts.

The one-way escalation rule was safe. The workers' complete reads detected material triggers in all three provisional pages, and each page received a distinct complete-source Sol review before promotion.

## Material review findings

- `edit-a-contract` initially omitted finalized-invoice/end-date behavior and immediate threshold-charge effects, and it resolved rather than preserved conflicts around uniqueness-key availability and provider-transition scope.
- `list-seat-balances` initially implied that sibling expanded credits and commits could be attributed to a per-credit-type balance, despite their schemas omitting `credit_type_id` and the example not establishing reconciliation.
- `sdks` initially treated August 2024 event examples as runnable under the snapshot's 34-day ingest window and underweighted the Go sequence whose contract begins after the shown usage and whose metric omits required grouping.

All three defects were query-relevant, so full semantic retries were appropriate.

## Shadow calibration finding

The shadow verdict is `inconclusive`, not a rejection of risk-based review. Metadata classified three pages as provisional, but their complete content contained financial, lifecycle, retry, time-window, propagation, or schema-conflict triggers. The escalation rule caught every case; however, no page remained eligible for simulated release, so this campaign provides no evidence that reviewer sampling would save time safely.

## Bounded coordinator work

The coordinator corrected one worker-attribution field after supplying a malformed CLI assignment, before the worker result was accepted. Canonical source promotion also removed a mechanically introduced trailing newline so each source remained byte-identical to its approved candidate. Neither correction changed reviewed semantics.

No registry, classifier, scheduler feature, state schema, monitoring layer, worktree system, or new validator was added.

## Recommendation

Keep full independent review as the current production rule. If reviewer sampling is revisited, run a separately approved small Metronome pilot whose sample includes genuinely descriptive or navigation-only pages; change only the selection sample, not the scheduler or source contract. Campaign 25 does not authorize sampling for Metronome, Stripe, Adyen, PayPal, Braintree, or another provider.
