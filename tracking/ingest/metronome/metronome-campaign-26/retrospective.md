# Metronome Campaign 26 Retrospective

## Outcome

- Final content quality: five of five pages approved; the fixed three-page query audit passed 9/9 without expansion.
- First-pass efficiency: two of five pages passed attempt 1; database reference, create-products/contracts, and Token Billing required bounded correction.
- Retry shape: nine worker attempts, seven full reviews, and two targeted reviews; no failed or rejected job.
- Total elapsed time through promotion, close validation, and final state write: 2,698 seconds (44 minutes 58 seconds).
- Coordinator repairs: one mechanical result-contract repair; no semantic source repair.

## What worked

Longest and highest-risk pages started first, so the 4,683-line contract mutation and 1,607-line schema reference overlapped shorter work. Dynamic slots switched immediately between workers and reviewers without a batch barrier. All five final sources remained concise query routers with exact current and historical raw navigation where applicable.

The bounded retry rule saved two unnecessary complete reads. Database `cost_basis` and create-products effective-dating defects changed semantic meaning and correctly received full retries. Token Billing's missing umbrella route and quote ranges, plus create-products' unsafe shared-file replacement instruction, used unchanged-hash targeted retries.

## Material review findings

- `database-reference-refresh` initially under-specified that `contracts_commits` documents commit cost basis directly while the broader credit-or-commit balance table retains commit-specific wording.
- `create-products-contracts-refresh` initially narrowed `Starting at` effective dating to usage products; after that semantic correction, a shared suggestion still needed a targeted repair so it would not delete existing subscription and fixed-product facts.
- `token-billing` initially omitted the umbrella usage-based-billing route and used quote ranges too narrow to ground two API-facing suggestions.

## Bounded coordinator work

The final create-products reviewer returned correct reviewed content with two extra top-level identity fields. The coordinator removed only those fields from the temporary result because identity is persisted by the campaign manager; the same result then passed the fixed schema. No reviewed conclusion or canonical content changed.

Approved shared updates were grouped into 15 targets and written once before source promotion. The coordinator then updated company, provider index, provider log, and counts once. No registry, scheduler feature, worktree layer, performance monitor, or new validator was added.

## Recommendation

Keep the current three-slot Sol worker/reviewer pipeline and Minimum Sufficient Source contract. Continue starting the longest pages first and using targeted review only for exact unchanged-hash repairs. Campaign 26 improved on Campaign 25's elapsed time while preserving per-page review, but its 2/5 first-pass rate still argues against removing independent semantic review or scaling page count aggressively.
