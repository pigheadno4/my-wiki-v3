## Overview

Metronome’s documentation home introduces a quickstart path aimed at getting users to their first invoice. It organizes pricing and packaging guidance around pay-as-you-go billing, enterprise commits, subscriptions with usage, and pre-paid credits. The page also provides a “Get started with” section covering contracts, invoicing, and rev-rec.

## Key takeaways

- The Quickstart guide is presented as a path to a first invoice on Metronome.
- Pay-as-you-go billing is described as charging customers in arrears for only what they use.
- Enterprise commits cover prepaid and postpaid commitments, negotiated discounts, one-time charges, and contract renewals.
- Pre-paid credits can be purchased upfront and can use auto-recharge or gated access when credits run out.

## Details

### Pricing and packaging

- The page groups pricing and packaging guidance into pay-as-you-go billing, enterprise commits, subscriptions with usage, and pre-paid credits.
- Subscriptions with usage are described as combining recurring revenue with usage-based components.

### Getting-started destinations

- The page links users to Contracts, Invoicing, and Rev-rec under “Get started with”.
