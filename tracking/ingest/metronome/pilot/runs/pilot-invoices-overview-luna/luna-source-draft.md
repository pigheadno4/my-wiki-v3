## Overview

This page presents Metronome's invoicing overview across Stripe, marketplace, and ERP billing motions and distribution channels. It describes native Stripe invoicing, marketplace invoicing that automates metering and invoice creation for AWS, Azure, and GCP, and out-of-the-box or custom ERP integrations. The page emphasizes optionality and control, allowing organizations to use each capability according to their needs.

## Key takeaways

- Metronome offers Stripe Invoicing, Marketplace Invoicing, and ERP Invoicing solutions.
- Stripe Invoicing natively integrates with Stripe and supports use of Stripe Tax, dunning, and other Stripe product-suite features.
- Marketplace Invoicing automates metering and invoice creation for AWS, Azure, and GCP, supports all Metronome charge types, and requires no third-party integrator.
- ERP Invoicing provides out-of-the-box and custom ERP integrations for transaction synchronization, collection, book closing, and ASC 606 guidelines.
- Metronome describes its invoicing capabilities as optional, so organizations can use simpler integrated solutions or ERP systems according to their requirements.

## Details

### Stripe invoicing

- Metronome natively integrates with Stripe for invoicing needs.
- The Stripe invoicing option is described as a native integration optimized for most billing use cases.
- The page mentions Stripe Tax, dunning, and other features within the Stripe product suite.

### Marketplace invoicing

- Metronome's out-of-the-box marketplace integration automates metering and invoice creation in AWS, Azure, and GCP.
- The integration supports all charge types in Metronome and can make a new pricing plan available through marketplace distribution channels.
- The marketplace option is described as a native metering integration to AWS, Azure, and GCP Marketplaces.

### ERP invoicing

- Metronome provides out-of-the-box and custom integrations to ERPs.
- The ERP option is optimized for clients billing their SLG business or executing revenue workflows inside ERPs.
- The page describes synchronizing transactions for collection and closing books, with reference to ASC 606 guidelines.
- The NetSuite option is described as a native integration for ERP billing and revenue workflows.

### Capability choice

- The page says organizations can choose simpler integrated solutions or ERP systems based on their contracting and revenue-process requirements.
- Metronome presents the invoicing capabilities as options that can be used according to the business's needs.
