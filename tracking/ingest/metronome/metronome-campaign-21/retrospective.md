# Metronome Campaign 21 Retrospective

## Outcome

- Final content quality: five of five pages approved; the fixed three-page query audit passed 9/9 with no expansion.
- First-pass efficiency: zero of five pages approved on attempt 1, below the target of at least four.
- Retry shape: thirteen worker attempts and thirteen reviews — nine full and four targeted. Four full semantic retry cycles were required, above the target of no more than one.
- Coordinator content repairs: zero.
- Time to final reviewer approval: approximately 44 minutes 9 seconds, above the desirable 35-minute target.
- Total campaign elapsed time through promotion and close audit: 3,375 seconds (56 minutes 15 seconds).

## What the archetype playbook improved

The playbook gave workers and reviewers a shared vocabulary for API Read, API List / Schema, API Mutation, Concept / Guide, and Integration Guide pages. Review defects were easier to classify against identity and envelope shape, pagination and examples, mutation requiredness and propagation, lifecycle and worked-example boundaries, and external-provider setup and outcome limits. The final sources are query-oriented indexes rather than exhaustive raw rewrites, retain exact raw deep-dive links, and place durable facts into the relevant concepts.

The independent close audit confirmed that this structure supports factual retrieval, boundary or contradiction retrieval, and exact raw navigation for the fixed sample. It also confirmed all-five raw hashes, approved-candidate equality, catalog entries, and reciprocal concept links.

## Why the pilot did not meet its optimization gate

The archetype guidance did not produce first-pass accuracy or faster closure. Reviewers still found page-specific semantic gaps involving customer-name propagation authority, invoice status terminology, native-invoice field conflicts, AWS listing and dimension lifecycle, cross-source concept placement, and repeated quote-grounding omissions. The final targeted retries were narrow, but they followed several complete rereads and did not recover the failed first-pass target.

The playbook therefore improved review focus and defect attribution, not throughput. It must not be represented as evidence that archetype prompts can replace complete raw reading, independent semantic review, or source-specific cross-authority judgment.

## Decision

Do not promote the Campaign 21 playbook into the Metronome provider production rules as a speed optimization, and do not roll it out across Stripe, Adyen, PayPal, or Braintree yet. Keep it as campaign-local evidence and, if useful, as a lightweight reviewer reference rather than a new schema, registry, routing layer, or mandatory prompt expansion.

Keep the five-page cap for any further experiment. The next optimization decision should reconsider per-page source economics — especially whether detailed API pages can be navigated from accurate overview sources — instead of adding more review layers or archetype machinery.
