## Limits and next gate

The script is serial and single-writer, with one HTTP request per candidate and no extra retry multiplier. Interrupted runs retain attempt/terminal ledgers; automatic resume and targeted network retry are not implemented. Full collection requires a separate approval and must use the corrected inventory above. Preserve failures honestly; do not claim complete coverage based on process exit alone.
